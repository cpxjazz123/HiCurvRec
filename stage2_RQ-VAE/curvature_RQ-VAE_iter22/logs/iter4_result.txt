Iteration 4: behavior-conditioned trainable curvature
Date: 2026-09-24

Mechanism
- Stage1 sentence_t5.npy and its item order were left unchanged.
- Behavior signal uses only Stage0 train.parquet: (last history item, target item) transitions. Valid/test splits are not read.
- Stage2 retains all-item reconstruction and adds an in-batch next-item contrastive loss over per-layer Poincare residual distances.
- Each layer has a bounded trainable curvature scale, initialized from iter1 calibration [0.001, 0.932889, 1.0], with regularization toward that initialization.
- MVG passed on a 640-pair batch. Behavior-loss gradients were nonzero for all three curvature parameters; temporary checkpoint round-trip passed.
