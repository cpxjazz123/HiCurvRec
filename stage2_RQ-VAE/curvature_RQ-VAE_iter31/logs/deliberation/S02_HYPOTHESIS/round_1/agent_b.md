CANONICAL_CANDIDATE_EQUATION_AND_COORDINATES=
For layer l∈{0,1,2}, Quantize.forward returns tangent-coordinate embedding e_l∈T_0(B_{c_l}) represented as a Euclidean vector (including the training STE value), not a Poincaré-ball point. First define the actual layer-curvature ball codeword:
q_l := exp0^{c_l}(e_l) ∈ B_{c_l}.
Map it radially to the common reference curvature c0:
q_l^0 := exp0^{c0}(log0^{c_l}(q_l)) ∈ B_{c0}.
Preserve layer order and right associativity exactly:
h := q_0^0 ⊕_{c0} (q_1^0 ⊕_{c0} q_2^0).
Return the tangent-coordinate decoder input:
z := log0^{c0}(h) ∈ T_0(B_{c0}), with batch×embedding shape and the role expected by the existing decoder.

In the valid, unclipped origin-map domain, log0^{c_l}(exp0^{c_l}(e_l))=e_l and therefore q_l^0=exp0^{c0}(e_l). This is only a coordinate identity for an individual codeword in that domain. It does not say that cross-curvature transfer preserves Möbius sums or that the heterogeneous residual cascade telescopes. Do not apply log0 directly to e_l: e_l is tangent-valued, while log0 takes a ball point. Do not change reverse nesting to left association: Möbius addition is not generally associative.
