## Inherited mechanisms and hidden confounds to retain explicitly

- The parent contains more than “fixed curvature plus a sum”: three per-layer codebooks/quantizers, Sinkhorn assignment, STE, curved distance and curvature-scaled `effective_eps`; Step4 residual subtraction; Step5 cross-curvature transport; reconstruction and behavior-contrastive losses; fixed-curvature regularization call (zero contribution under fixed buffers/weight); training/optimizer/warm-start; and decoder/reconstruction normalization. All are inherited and must remain identical.
- The quantizer's collected `e_l` is a tangent-coordinate selected embedding (training forward value is STE); it is not an ID and not a ball point. The canonical mapping first forms `q_l` before source-curvature log mapping. Directly logging `e_l` or changing representation/call ordering is a contract violation.
- The exact HRA-paper telescope does not transfer by assertion: its cancellation depends on paired residual subtraction and reverse-nested addition at a shared curvature. Iter29 has distinct layer curvatures plus cross-layer residual transport. The contract explicitly disclaims exact telescope, homomorphism, residual inversion, or reconstruction claims. This registered operation is HRA-inspired only.
- Radial origin transfer is not a general Möbius-addition homomorphism. The `exp/log` identity is only valid in the unclipped domain; projection and log clamp can make the transfer differ from its unprojected algebraic simplification. Near-origin embeddings could make the intervention small. Actual effect, clipping incidence, shape/finiteness, and gradient health are not established by this static audit.
- Historical raw-residual/branching provenance is limited: S03 accepts historical method/value provenance but does not provide replay/hash proof. Preserve the exact recorded values and limitation; do not turn an iteration path update into new measurements or normalized-layer-scale substitution.
- Iter29-to-Iter31 is a single-run comparison. Historical point results do not estimate noise or guarantee causal attribution. No performance inference is made here.
