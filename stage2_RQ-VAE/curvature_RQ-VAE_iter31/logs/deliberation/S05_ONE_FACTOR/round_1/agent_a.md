## Decision

The one-factor boundary is defensible **conditionally**. The sole conceptual/model-code delta registered by S02/S04 is replacing Iter29 `modules/rqvae.py::RqVae._step6_sum_embeddings`' Euclidean sum of the three tangent-coordinate quantizer outputs with the exact HRA-STEP6-1 common-reference, right-nested operation below. It returns a tangent-coordinate tensor to the existing decoder; it does not change the decoder or its reconstruction-loss consumer.

For ordered layers L0, L1, L2, preserve exactly:

```text
e_l   = selected tangent-coordinate quantizer embedding
q_l   = exp0^{c_l}(e_l) ∈ B_{c_l}
q_l^0 = exp0^{c0}(log0^{c_l}(q_l)) ∈ B_{c0}
h     = q_0^0 ⊕_{c0} (q_1^0 ⊕_{c0} q_2^0)
z     = log0^{c0}(h)
c0    = c_0 = 1.3660953164241916
```

Only the aggregator output `z_E=e_0+e_1+e_2` is replaced. The source-curvature/log and target-curvature/exp roles, ascending leaves, right nesting, and conditional exp/log identity limits are part of this treatment. Do not log-map `e_l` directly, reorder/reassociate, assert an exact telescope, or claim the cross-curvature transfer is a Möbius homomorphism. The helpers project exp results and clamp log arguments; the registered identity is conditional on an unclipped valid domain. Their actual runtime incidence/effect remains unmeasured.
