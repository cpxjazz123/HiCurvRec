# Direction assessment

**Strongest next direction: a prospective, matched-seed replication of the two already-tested fixed mappings, not a third mapping yet.** The first global review selected one bounded alternative mapping under FCCR-1 and specifically held the iter26 reference protocol constant (`iter28/logs/global_review_after_iter28.md:29-35`; prior S14 Judge lines 24–25). That authorized experiment is now complete, but the apparent advantage is smaller than an unestimated noise band and both observations miss the target. Replication is the highest-information next step because it separates mapping-dependent signal from run variation before another mapping is selected; it preserves the current contract and changes no mechanism. This also follows skill §14: tiny one-run deltas comparable to historical noise are near-parity and should be replicated before any improvement claim.

Concretely, after this S14 decision is canonicalized and synchronized as specified below, register the next iteration as a **matched-seed replication block** for the iter26 mapping versus the iter29 mapping, with three new predeclared paired seeds (43, 44, 45). For each seed pair, hold all inputs, warm start, data identity, Stage2/Stage3 configuration, evaluation count, and runtime protocol fixed; vary only the registered closed-form mapping. Report paired per-seed differences, spread/uncertainty, and each arm's result against the inclusive target. Keep the existing seed-42 pair as prior evidence, not as a substitute for prospective replications; pool it only if the new protocol lock verifies the relevant identities/settings are genuinely compatible. This is a registered replication, not mechanism stacking or a new curvature mechanism. Use the exact S01-verified iter26/iter29 configurations and explicit raw-residual provenance limitation; do not retune either mapping.

A replication does not guarantee reaching `0.065`, and three additional pairs still yield a modest sample, not definitive proof. Its purpose is to resolve whether the only current apparent mapping delta is reproducible and to bound stochastic variation before spending another full iteration on a new equation. If the fixed mappings repeatedly remain near-parity or below target, that is evidence for reconsidering the broader fixed-mapping direction—not an automatic proof the FCCR-1 family failed. Only then should an independently adjudicated between-iteration contract-transition proposal be weighed against further FCCR-1 exploration.

**Why not another mapping now:** FCCR-1 permits it and a carefully preregistered map could still reach target, but after two different maps with one run each a third single-run score would confound map effect and noise again. It offers less discriminating information than replication and risks repeating an ambiguous result. Reconsider another bounded map after the replication if the evidence supports continued FCCR-1 search or identifies a justified, falsifiable next mapping.

**Why not a contract transition now:** learnable/scheduled curvature and optimizer/Sinkhorn/loss changes remain forbidden/deferred by the active contract. Two active but noisy, unpromoted observations do not establish that the broader fixed closed-form hypothesis is false; an immediate transition would change the research question before resolving noise. Any such transition must be a distinct between-iteration 2+1 decision, never smuggled into the replication or treated as authorized by this review alone.
