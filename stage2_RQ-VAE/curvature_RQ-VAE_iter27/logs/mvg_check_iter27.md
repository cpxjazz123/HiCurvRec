## Result: **MVG PASS (mechanism defined) / F-1 partial (mechanism rarely fires)**

The trust-region projection is geometrically defined, the gradient path
is intact, and the projection writes finite corrected codebook tensors
on every step.  However, the canonical `trust_radius_fraction = 0.5`
setting causes `clip_frac = 0` across all 200 MVG steps (and 1000
follow-up diagnostic steps) under the iter18 ckpt regime because the
per-step AdamW displacement (`~0.01`) is much smaller than the trust
radius (`~0.06–0.28`).  The mechanism is therefore **a safety bound that
does not engage** in practice under the iter18 ckpt; iter27 reduces to
iter18 by default.

A scaling sweep confirms `trust_frac = 0.05` keeps the mechanism
geometric while making it active (see diagnostic table below).
