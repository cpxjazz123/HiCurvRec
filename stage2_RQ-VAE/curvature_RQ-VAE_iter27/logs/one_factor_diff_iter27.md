## 3. Inherited mechanisms

All five mechanisms listed under `ACTIVE_MECHANISMS_BEFORE` are
inherited verbatim from iter18.  No loss weight, optimizer
hyperparameter, dataset, or Stage3 trainer setting is changed.

## 4. Optimizer differences

None.  `AdamW(lr=1e-3, β₁=0.9, β₂,l=frozen from c_l(0), eps=1e-8,
wd=1e-4)` is the same group wiring as iter18.  The trust-region is a
**post-step** projection on `layer.embedding.weight` only; it never
touches `optimizer.param_groups`, `optimizer.state`, the encoder,
decoder, or any `c_layer_scale` parameter.

## 5. Loss differences

None.  `reconstruction + rqvae + 0.20·behavior + 0.005·curv_reg` is
unchanged.  No new term is added.

## 6. Stage1 / Stage3 differences

None.  Stage1 reads the same `sentence_t5.npy` + `item_ids.json`.
Stage3 uses `code_path = item_sids.json` (TIGER-compatible 4-token
format) generated by Stage2; the trainer T5 hyperparameters,
beam, n_eval, and launcher env are identical to iter18.

## 7. One-factor statement

The only conceptual change is the **post-optimizer-step hyperbolic
codebook trust-region projection** introduced in iter27.  No second
mechanism is introduced alongside it; no mechanism from iter18 is
removed or weakened.

## 8. Stop conditions

- If `preflight_cao1.py` fails, stop before training.
- If `mvg_check.py` fails, stop before training.
- If `clip_frac ∈ [0.05, 0.95]` is violated for any 1000-step window
  (F-1 or F-2), stop and reclassify as `MECHANISM_INACTIVE` or
  `MECHANISM_DESTRUCTIVE`.
- If `NaN/Inf` appears anywhere in the trust-region summary, stop.
