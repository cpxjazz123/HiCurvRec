# Adversarial mathematics review

Scope: the two operators in `proposals.md` are reviewed as written, against its fixed 32-D / three 256-way codebook / three-token protocol. This is a mathematical review, not an endorsement of task performance or novelty. The literature scan (`literature.md`, especially lines 5, 11, 16–18) is explicitly bounded and abstract-only; it cannot repair an operator defect or establish priority.

## Candidate 1 — ordered-simplex signed area assignment

**Verdict: REVISE (do not join as currently written).** The intended area-magnitude mechanism can be made well-defined, but the advertised signed, orientation-sensitive score is not defined by the stated convention in dimension 32. Minimal repair: choose and declare a fixed ambient orientation (or another explicit, isometry-compatible orientation datum), define signed area relative to it, and weaken the invariance claim to orientation-preserving isometries preserving that datum. If instead the intent is unsigned area, say so and remove the sign-reversal and reflection claims; this changes the mechanism to a symmetric area penalty and requires the registered ablation to validate that version.

### Domain, differentiability, and score

- `proposals.md:13–15` gives valid interior ball points and a valid `Log_p` construction in exact hyperbolic space. `q=Exp_p(Log_p(x))=x` is an identity, not an additional residual construction. For distinct, noncollinear vertices the geodesic triangle and its unsigned area are well-defined. Repeated vertices, a zero edge, and collinear triples have zero area; the distance term remains finite.
- The key defect is in `:15`: orienting the plane by the ordered basis `(u,v)` and integrating the positive area form makes the integral positive by construction. Swapping `q,z` also swaps the chosen plane orientation, so the integral of its positive area form is still positive. It does **not** produce the asserted sign reversal. In 32 dimensions there is no canonical ambient orientation of each 2-plane that fixes this sign. Thus `A_c` as defined is unsigned, while the stated operator is signed. Under the literal convention the score is invariant to swapping `q,z` (both area and distance are symmetric), contrary to the ordered-area story.
- If one supplies an independent orientation, signed area is not a globally canonical scalar under all hyperbolic isometries: orientation-reversing isometries reverse it. This is compatible only with the qualification at `:20`, and requires stating the orientation datum explicitly. An arbitrary per-triangle edge basis is not that datum.
- Away from degeneracy, area and squared distance are smooth. At collinearity, unsigned triangle area behaves locally like a constant times the magnitude of a transverse displacement (`|t|`); it is generally not differentiable there. The zero-gradient convention at `:15` is a permitted chosen subgradient, but not the gradient of a differentiable score. The whole score inherits this kink. Near but not at degeneracy the derivative is direction-dependent; near repeated vertices no uniform smoothness is promised. This matters for the claim of straight-through/soft-assignment gradient flow at `:18`; the stated convention only defines a selected value at the degenerate set.
- Exact interior points, including points arbitrarily close to the boundary, have finite distances and finite triangle area (hyperbolic triangle area is bounded by `π/c`). However numerical triangle-angle evaluation can lose precision near the boundary or near degeneracy. Projection at `:9` bounds the radius and makes the mathematical score finite, but the text must distinguish stable evaluation from mathematical existence. A degenerate base `p` is not a geometric singularity: any `p∈B_c`, including `o`, is valid. At `p=q` or `p=z`, the stated zero rule applies.

### Invariants and controls

- `:20` orientation-preserving-isometry invariance is true for a genuinely signed area whose orientation datum is transported consistently. It is trivially true for unsigned area too, but the claimed swap sign rule fails under the stated self-oriented-plane convention. A counterexample is any noncollinear triangle: compute with ordered basis `(u,v)` and then `(v,u)`; both integrals of the positive induced area form equal the same positive area.
- `:22` has the right qualitative flat control for bounded fixed chart coordinates: curvature-scaled area tends to zero, while hyperbolic distance tends to the Euclidean chart distance (subject to the proposal's coordinate convention). Positive Euclidean nearest-code margin then gives eventual assignment agreement. This does not validate the finite-curvature operator. It is also not a control for a repaired signed-area definition until that definition is specified.
- `:41,49` make the area-zero intervention potentially informative, but do not fix the sign ambiguity. Under the literal operator, this tests an unsigned area penalty, not the claimed signed ordered-simplex term. The registered 10% changed-code condition and task gates remain necessary, not sufficient to establish the intended mechanism.

### Capacity / refuted-form check

No explicit codebook, token, or latent-capacity increase appears (`:5,7,18`). It is not an obvious radius-dominated product assignment, transported-residual frame, role layout, or Euclidean reformulation. But absent a valid signed definition, the claimed order dependence collapses to an unsigned triangle-area penalty; do not present it as the signed operator. Literature context is not decisive: `literature.md:11,16` reports only abstract-level HiHPQ overlap and does not establish either novelty or mathematical validity.

## Candidate 2 — origin-chart ordered composition with tangent gyration

**Verdict: REVISE (operator is largely well-defined, but the proposed ablation/materiality test is not the claimed causal test).** Minimal repair: define the comparison as a same-parameter, same-point counterfactual with only the `γ||δ_k||²` term removed, and separately report a retrained `γ=0` arm if desired. Explicitly declare the Euclidean norm/metric convention in the flat-control equation. Retain strict clamp/task gates.

### Domain, differentiability, and score

- `proposals.md:26–31` is type-consistent: `h,r,v_k∈T_o`, Möbius addition acts on ball points, and `D_oGyr` acts on the tangent space. For all exact interior `p,x,z`, the nested Möbius sums remain interior; `Log_o(m_k)` exists. A degenerate base is not an issue: `p=o` is valid, and radial transport from `o` to itself is identity.
- In exact arithmetic the score is finite and smooth on the open-ball domain. The Poincaré conformal factor has denominator `1-c||a||²`, so formulas and derivatives become ill-conditioned as an input approaches the boundary, but do not have an interior singularity. `Log_o` is smooth at `o` (its apparent radial `atanh(t)/t` quotient has a removable limit); there is no `Log`-at-the-base singularity here. `D_oGyr` is a smooth derivative for interior arguments. The score has no division by a vanishing distance or by `1-c||x||²` at an interior point.
- Projection (`:9,33`) is a separate nonsmooth operation at its clamp threshold. Thus the unprojected score is differentiable, but the complete projected forward map is only piecewise differentiable at the projection boundary; a clamp rate under `0.1%` does not make those individual points differentiable. Hard `argmin` is likewise discontinuous at score ties, as expected for the stated straight-through estimator. The lowest-index tie rule makes the choice defined, not differentiable.
- The phrase “gyrations fix `o`” and derivative at `o` is correct for the stated gyration point map. The radial transport is well-defined and norm preserving but is diagnostic only; its presence does not affect the score.

### Invariants, counterexamples, and controls

- Ordered composition is genuinely noncommutative in general, and `D_oGyr[p,z]` can be nonidentity. But it is not guaranteed to be nontrivial: if `p=o` or `z=o`, the corresponding gyration is identity, so `δ_k=0`; it is also zero for target tangent `r=0` and for vectors fixed by the gyration. These are valid inputs. Thus any blanket claim that the second term always isolates a nonzero order effect is false; the empirical materiality gates at `:42` are essential.
- `:35` flat limit is consistent if all vector norms there use `g_o=4I` in the `c→0` limit. The chart vectors approach twice the ball coordinates, and `Log_o((p⊕z)⊕x)` approaches `h+v+r`. If `||·||` in the displayed Euclidean control instead means ordinary coordinate norm without `g_o`, there is a factor-of-four mismatch. State the convention and compute the control using the same metric. Flat limit is ordered Euclidean composition, not the original quantizer, as the proposal correctly qualifies.
- `:28,33` say transport preserves Riemannian norms and Möbius operations remain in the ball; these are true for exact interior operations. The projection policy at every point operation can perturb the algebra and the derivative. It must not be treated as exact Möbius closure; every such clamp is already counted, and exceeding the threshold kills the run.
- The declared `γ=0` ablation at `:42,50` is suitable as a score-level counterfactual only if it uses the same trained points and codebooks. As written, “paired arm” also says paired arms are trained (`:39`), which permits the codebooks/representations to change and confounds the causal role of the gyration term. A retrained ablation can be reported, but cannot substitute for the fixed-parameter counterfactual if claiming that the term itself changed assignments. Changed-code fraction alone also depends on whether assignments are evaluated on the same latent/code points.

### Capacity / refuted-form check

No extra codes, tokens, or dimensions are introduced (`proposals.md:5,26–31`). The score is not a hidden Euclidean reformulation: at finite curvature its Möbius composition and gyration derivative are genuine hyperbolic operations. Its `c→0` control is Euclidean by design, not evidence of equivalence at the frozen curvature. Nor is the radial transport a transported-residual frame: it is explicitly excluded from the score. Nevertheless, if the `γ` contribution is empirically a no-op, the mechanism reduces to the ordered composition control, exactly as `:42,56` acknowledge. The abstract-only literature scan (`literature.md:11,16`) supports no stronger novelty conclusion.

## Join recommendation

Neither candidate should be approved as currently written. Candidate 1 has a definitional contradiction in its central signed operator. Candidate 2 has a mathematically sound interior score, but needs an unambiguous norm convention and a non-confounded ablation to substantiate mechanism attribution. These are mathematical/experimental-design revisions only; neither verdict relaxes the frozen task gates, dimensions, codebook sizes, or token count.
