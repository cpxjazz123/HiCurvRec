# Role L — verified literature for a new SID-code mechanism

**Scope.** The frozen protocol is unchanged: 32-dimensional latent, three 256-entry codebooks, three integer SID tokens, and the original data/splits/Stage2/Stage3 recipes. This is a literature screen, not a claim that any proposed mechanism will beat the three frozen thresholds. “Overlap” below describes only what the inspected source establishes. `ABSTRACT_ONLY` means the source’s abstract was inspected, not its full paper; no citation is inferred from title alone.

## Candidate mechanism ideas not occupied by the inspected abstracts

These are deliberately hypotheses, not novelty or performance claims. Each places a non-trivial hyperbolic operation in the *selection* of an integer code, rather than changing the frozen recipes. Before implementation, specify all arithmetic and boundary semantics in finite precision, then measure the relevant occurrence rate directly from frozen assignment artifacts (no training). Reject any idea whose operative event is too rare.

1. **Karcher-centroid marginal assignment.** For each candidate code at a level, evaluate the decrease in a bounded-support Fréchet/Karcher objective of the item's fixed neighborhood after assigning it to that code; choose the greatest decrease, with deterministic code-ID tie resolution. This is not ordinary point-to-codeword distance or hyperbolic MLR. Pre-measure how often this neighborhood correction changes the nearest-code assignment. Finite-precision declaration must fix the ball/domain, iteration count, convergence tolerance, and behavior on non-convergence.
2. **Hyperbolic Voronoi-boundary margin.** Select by signed geodesic distance to a candidate code’s decision boundary (not codeword radius/distance), optionally weighted by a fixed, frozen local density estimate. This differs from HyperVQ’s MLR unless implemented as its same hyperbolic decision-hyperplane classifier; that formulation is therefore a collision risk and must be distinguished mathematically, not renamed. Measure the fraction of assignments whose selected code changes versus the frozen rule. Define boundary ties and margin clamps explicitly.
3. **Parallel-transported local-neighborhood agreement.** At code selection, compare a candidate’s tangent-space displacement with the aggregate direction of the item’s frozen nearest-neighbor set, transporting vectors to one declared base point and scoring their agreement. The operation acts on the selection score, not residual reconstruction or a codeword frame. Measure the reassignment rate from frozen embeddings/neighbors first. Specify transport path, curvature/domain, finite precision, zero-vector handling, and comparison tolerance; avoid reducing the score to an identity or duplicating the closed transported-residual family.
4. **Geodesic margin under a frozen triplet constraint.** Among code candidates, prefer the one maximizing the worst-case hyperbolic separation between the item and a frozen positive/negative neighbor pair, with the pair list computed from the original training split only. This is a selection-time operation, not a downstream reranker. Measure how often the constraint changes assignments and its per-level rate before proposing it. Fix empty-pair behavior, distance clamps, finite score tolerance, and tie order in advance.

### Mandatory gate before selecting one

For each idea, provide an executable-from-frozen-artifacts occurrence-rate definition (including denominator and per-level counts) and compute it before training or implementation. For every operator, predeclare: model/domain and valid inputs; dtype; epsilons and clamps; iteration/termination rules if applicable; NaN/Inf and boundary behavior; score comparison tolerance; and deterministic tie-breaking. If the measurable event is negligible, or these semantics cannot be made finite-precision complete, do not advance the candidate. Any eventual claim must be tested against all three frozen strict inequalities: Hard-L1 ER > 16.38953942232631, Stage3 Recall@10 > 0.06406796775709883, and NDCG@10 > 0.03530651701940677.
