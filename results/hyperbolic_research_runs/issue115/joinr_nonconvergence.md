Fourteen rounds, every round answered, and the verdict has never once been `APPROVE_FOR_JOIN_E` from
all three lenses at the same time (prior art alone approved twice, conditionally).

## What this means, stated plainly

The objections have **converged** (each round's item was smaller and more local than the last) but the
**approval has not**. Two readings are equally consistent with the record:

1. the candidate needs one more numerical-precision pass (the round-14 item), or
2. an adversarial LLM reviewer asked to "try to kill it" will always find a next item, so
   unanimous approval is not an achievable stopping rule.

Nothing in the record distinguishes them. What the record DOES settle: the unsigned tangent-space wedge
area is not refuted. It has no surviving mathematical objection, no prior-art occupation, and a real
emitted audit.

## The decision taken (issue #115 §4: do not lower the bar, change the family)

The candidate is **not** promoted. It is **parked** with its full record, and the campaign opens a
**different family** through the same native path: L/A/M again, with the previous families' refutations
and this 14-round record as input, and with one explicit new requirement that came out of round 14:

> a mechanism must be **numerically well-defined in finite precision** as part of its declaration, not as
> a later implementation detail, and its audit must be able to recompute the decision from the emitted
> operands.

If the new family reaches the same 14-round plateau, the same rule applies again: park it, record it,
open the next family. No threshold is lowered at any point; no candidate reaches Stage2 without the
JOIN E gates, and no Stage3 without the ER gate.
