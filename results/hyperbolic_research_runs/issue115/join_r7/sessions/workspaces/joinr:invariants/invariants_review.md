# Adversarial invariants review

## Scope and standard

This review tests the two Role M candidates against the frozen protocol in `proposals.md`: 32-dimensional latent, three 256-entry codebooks, exactly three integer SID tokens, unchanged data/splits/Stage-2 and Stage-3 recipes, and strict conjunctive gates (Hard-L1 ER > 16.38953942232631, Recall@10 > 0.06406796775709883, NDCG@10 > 0.03530651701940677). The stated artifacts are partition statistics, CGV gates, H1-H7 oracle, and frozen ER. Geometry identities alone do not establish a useful partition or a ranking benefit. Nor can an artifact that only summarizes output partitions prove an internal geometric calculation was implemented correctly.

## Candidate 1 — Signed geodesic-triangle-area assignment

**Verdict: FALSIFIABLE, but only in a narrower, operational sense.** Partition statistics can test whether area changes assignments and whether host-conditional usage collapses; frozen ER and the CGV/H1-H7 gates can test outcome. These do not by themselves verify triangle-area numerics, isometry invariance, or Gauss–Bonnet agreement. Those require explicit unit/property tests or logged per-example score components, which are not guaranteed by the named repository artifacts. Treat such claims as unmeasured until those are emitted. “Area contributes half the score variance” is not a sufficient discriminator: variance can be dominated by outliers while almost all argmins remain unchanged.

**Invariant audit.** The listed collinearity, sign reversal, Gauss–Bonnet, and common-isometry properties are useful implementation tests, not efficacy invariants. The 256/three constraint is mandatory but trivially satisfied by any compliant operator. The flat-limit statement is also not enough: the distance term still assigns codes, and zero area naturally leaves a valid distance quantizer. The proposed assignment-agreement falsifier is better, but must compare on identical trained codebooks and report both overall and per-stage agreement; retraining the ablation lets codebooks compensate and can hide dependence.

**Rewrite as discriminating invariants (pre-register on held-out validation items).**

1. For fixed trained encoder and codebooks, compute the exact hard assignment with the declared area score and with the area term set to zero. Report per-stage changed-assignment fraction, paired score margin changes, and bootstrap confidence intervals. Require a pre-registered nontrivial change rate above a declared floor (not merely “not 100% agreement”), and show changes are not confined to numerical ties. Also report area contribution to *argmin-relevant* score differences, e.g. the fraction of examples where area changes the winner and the distribution of winner-vs-runner-up margin changes; variance fraction alone is insufficient.
2. Demonstrate the claimed directional mechanism: among examples where area changes the winner, compare residual direction/host-conditional partition separation against the distance-only assignment, controlling for residual norm/radius. Require a predeclared improvement in a measurable directional-separation statistic, not just nonzero area values.
3. Test a matched flat ablation with the same fixed inputs, codewords, and β, and verify that the area contribution tends to zero as c→0 while the hard-assignment difference from the curved operator disappears. This is a property test, not proof of quality.
4. Keep the count constraint as a protocol assertion (256 codes at each of three stages; exactly three emitted indices), not as evidence for the mechanism.
5. Effectiveness remains the strict triple gate on the frozen evaluation. Report ER and the CGV/H1-H7 outcomes as separate gates; do not let geometric test success substitute for any gate.

**Simplest non-cosmetic control.** Frozen-codebook, same-input **area-off ablation**: set `A_c=0` without retraining, keeping distance term, assignments downstream, and evaluation fixed. Compare changed assignments, partition statistics, frozen ER, and CGV/H1-H7. If the full operator does not alter winners beyond ties, it is cosmetic. If it alters winners but no relevant gate/ER improves, the hypothesized utility is unsupported. A separate retrained area-off run may be useful but is not the minimal causal control.

## Candidate 2 — Non-associative gyro-composition quantization

**Verdict: FALSIFIABLE, with a serious specification/measurement gap.** Partition statistics can reveal whether the score changes assignments; ER and CGV/H1-H7 can decide whether those partitions help. They cannot establish that the changed assignments are caused specifically by gyration, that transport is norm-preserving, or that operations stay in the ball. The candidate's listed algebraic invariants require operation-level tests/logging absent from the named artifact set. More importantly, `G_k` includes composition by the residual after the code, but the written score's gyration difference term compares a gyrated residual with the un-gyrated residual; its scale, sign, and tangent-space identification need a precise implementation contract. The text also defines `u_k` but does not use it. Resolve these before calling an implementation a test of the proposal.

**Invariant audit.** Identity laws and Euclidean limits are generic correctness checks, not evidence of discrimination. Norm preservation of transport is true for correctly implemented parallel transport but says nothing about assignments. Strictly interior points and fixed code/token counts are safety/protocol constraints. “Association orders differ for generic inputs” is an existence test; actual data may lie in degenerate or nearly associative regimes. The meaningful falsifier—gyration near identity on actual pairs or removing its term leaves assignments unchanged—is measurable only if actual pairwise gyration/score components are logged; partition stats alone provide only the latter indirectly through a paired ablation.

**Rewrite as discriminating invariants (pre-register on held-out validation items).**

1. On the actual host/code/residual triples, report the distribution of gyration angle and normalized displacement `||gyr[w]-w||/(||w||+ε)`, plus the fraction above a predeclared meaningful threshold. Report by stage and host-norm strata. A generic random-vector nonzero test does not count.
2. With trained weights/codebooks frozen, remove only the `γ` gyration term. Report per-stage assignment flip fraction, tie-excluded flips, score-margin changes, host-conditional entropy/perplexity, and partition statistics. Require a predeclared nontrivial flip rate. Also run `γ=0` through the *full* `S_k` if the intended causal claim is the explicit penalty; avoid changing composition or other terms simultaneously.
3. Compare curved and flat-limit operators on identical inputs/parameters and verify convergence of gyration displacement and assignment decisions (report near-tie cases separately). A Euclidean-limit match is expected and not itself a positive result.
4. Instrument maximum ball norm and clamp frequency, plus transport norm error, on real training/validation batches; set a predeclared clamp/error ceiling. These safety metrics are not evidence of semantic benefit.
5. Preserve 32 dimensions, three codebooks of 256, and three emitted integer tokens as protocol checks only. Require the strict ER + Recall@10 + NDCG@10 triple gate, with CGV/H1-H7 reported independently.

**Simplest non-cosmetic control.** Frozen-weight/codebook **gyration-off ablation**: set `γ=0` (and leave the composition score, transport, all inputs, and downstream evaluation unchanged), then compare hard assignments and partition statistics against the full score. If the intended claim is broader—that non-associative composition itself matters—add a second matched control replacing the curved composition/gyration calculation with its flat associative limit; do not conflate these two causal claims. If disabling the stated gyration term does not change winners beyond ties, the explicit gyration mechanism is cosmetic.

## Cross-candidate decision rule

Neither proposal earns approval from algebraic identities, nonzero curvature, score variance, changed assignments, or healthy code usage alone. First require a reproducible operation-specific paired ablation with a predeclared nontrivial assignment effect; then require non-collapsed partition diagnostics; finally require all three frozen metrics to strictly exceed their thresholds under unchanged data/splits/recipes. A failed triple gate kills the candidate even if its geometry is implemented perfectly. Conversely, the current named artifacts are insufficient to certify several internal geometric invariants unless instrumentation/unit-test outputs are added; do not infer them from final metrics.

## Role L evidence boundary

`literature.md` reports one abstract-only image-retrieval match (HiHPQ), and explicitly says it does not establish compatibility with this setup or recommendation benefit. It supplies no empirical evidence that either candidate's proposed invariants discriminate or pass the frozen gates. The two candidate reviews above therefore treat literature as motivation only, not validation.
