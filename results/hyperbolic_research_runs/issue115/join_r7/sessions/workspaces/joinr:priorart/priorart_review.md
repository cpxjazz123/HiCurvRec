# Adversarial prior-art review

## Scope and standard

This is a novelty review, not an implementation or performance approval. The protocol remains frozen: 32 latent dimensions, three 256-entry codebooks, three integer SID tokens, original data/splits/recipes, and strict conjunctive gates of ER `> 16.38953942232631`, Recall@10 `> 0.06406796775709883`, and NDCG@10 `> 0.03530651701940677`. Passing those gates would establish utility under this setup, not conceptual novelty. The literature scan itself says it was targeted rather than exhaustive.

## Candidate 1 — Signed geodesic-triangle-area assignment

**Verdict: ADJACENT (novel application is plausible but unverified; not safe to call NOVEL).**

**Closest verified work.** HiHPQ, *Hierarchical Hyperbolic Product Quantization for Unsupervised Image Retrieval*, AAAI 2024, DOI `10.1609/aaai.v38i5.28261`, arXiv `2401.07212` — **ABSTRACT_ONLY**, as recorded in `literature.md`. The verified abstract establishes hyperbolic product quantisation, hyperbolic codebook attention and a hyperbolic product manifold for image retrieval. It does **not** establish use of signed triangle area, host-relative area scoring, or this SID setup. It is a direct occupied-neighbourhood reference for hyperbolic quantisation in retrieval, but not a verified match for the proposed operator. No recommendation-specific discretisation match was verified by the scan.

**Other pipeline location / obviousness.** The proposal puts signed area directly in the hard code assignment and its soft training surrogate. It also uses geodesic distance in that same score as a locality/tie-break term. Triangle area/Gauss–Bonnet is a standard hyperbolic-geometric quantity (recollection; no specific additional paper verified here), and hyperbolic geometry may be used in representation or ranking without occupying this assignment use. Reusing a familiar geometric quantity at assignment is not automatically obvious, but moving geometry from representation/metric learning into quantiser assignment is already adjacent to HiHPQ. The candidate needs to claim the specific signed, host-conditioned area-versus-residual-direction partition, not “hyperbolic quantisation” generally.

**Is it more than a sweep / metric swap / reparameterisation?** In principle, yes. A signed, three-point geodesic-simplex area term is not a rescaled distance, and the `c→0` vanishing-area control would distinguish it from a curvature sweep or norm reparameterisation. However, the proposed score combines area with distance; if area contributes little, or zeroing it preserves assignments, the actual method collapses to a metric-based quantiser. The stated variance condition is necessary evidence, not proof of useful distinctiveness. Also resolve the orientation definition in dimension 32: a triangle has an intrinsic unsigned area, but a signed area requires a coherent orientation convention for its spanning 2-plane. A single ordered frame at `p` does not obviously define a sign for every possible plane while retaining the claimed common-isometry invariance. The invariants should be demonstrated for the implemented convention; otherwise the advertised signed/isometry-invariant operator is underspecified.

**Novelty boundary.** The bounded scan does not verify this exact operator in a recommender, quantiser, or hyperbolic representation paper. That is **UNKNOWN**, not evidence of absence. Closest verified work is HiHPQ (ABSTRACT_ONLY), with hyperbolic product quantisation but no abstract-level evidence of oriented area assignment. A full-text check of HiHPQ and a focused search for hyperbolic triangle-area/angle-defect quantisation are required before upgrading this to NOVEL.

## Candidate 2 — Non-associative gyro-composition quantization

**Verdict: ADJACENT (high risk of being an application-level repackaging; exact assignment use remains unverified).**

**Closest verified work.** Again, HiHPQ (AAAI 2024; DOI `10.1609/aaai.v38i5.28261`, arXiv `2401.07212`) is **ABSTRACT_ONLY** and establishes hyperbolic product quantisation in retrieval, but not Möbius gyration or order-sensitive residual composition. The scan contains no verified recommender-specific assignment paper using this operator. Möbius addition and gyration are standard gyrovector/hyperbolic operations (recollection; no additional identifier verified in the supplied literature file). Their use in hyperbolic representation learning or geometric composition would be prior art for the operations, but would not by itself establish their use as the SID assignment rule proposed here.

**Other pipeline location / obviousness.** This candidate places Möbius composition and a gyration-dependent term in the assignment score, with parallel transport and a learned tangent map feeding the code semantic. Thus geometry is not merely in the encoder or ranking loss. But the ingredients are assembled from standard hyperbolic representation machinery, and HiHPQ already makes hyperbolic quantisation in retrieval an occupied neighbourhood. If Möbius operations or gyrations have already been used in an encoder/decoder/loss, carrying them to assignment is not a wholly unrelated invention; the novelty would have to reside in the particular order-sensitive code/residual decision and its measured effect. Exact prior use at any pipeline stage beyond the verified HiHPQ abstract is **UNKNOWN** here.

**Is it more than a sweep / metric swap / reparameterisation?** The intended distinction is substantive: gyration vanishes in the flat limit, and non-associative composition can distinguish orderings that Euclidean vector addition cannot. If gyration is near identity on the actual pairs, its score contribution negligible, or assignments match the flat/additive control, it is effectively a metric/reparameterisation change and fails its own falsifiers. The score also needs a mathematical audit before implementation: as written, `Exp_p(r)` is a point while the second summand is written as a difference of points and then measured with `g_p`; specify the tangent-space identification/operation that makes this difference well-typed. Likewise ensure the gyration output is compared in the same tangent space and that the stated transport is genuinely norm-preserving under the chosen conventions. Without this, claimed invariants and the ablation are not yet reproducible.

**Novelty boundary.** No exact Möbius-gyration assignment match is verified by `literature.md`; status is **UNKNOWN**, not “none exists.” Closest verified work is HiHPQ, which occupies hyperbolic product quantisation for retrieval at abstract level but does not disclose this mechanism in the abstract. A targeted full-text and citation search for gyrovector quantisation, gyrovector residual/product quantisation, and gyration-based retrieval assignment is needed. The standardness of the component operations makes a broad “first use of gyration” claim especially unsafe.

## Overall decision

Neither candidate merits an unqualified NOVEL verdict from the present evidence. Both are **ADJACENT** to verified hyperbolic retrieval quantisation (HiHPQ, ABSTRACT_ONLY); neither is shown occupied by that paper at the operator level. Candidate 1 has the sharper potentially distinctive assignment statistic, conditional on a coherent high-dimensional sign convention. Candidate 2 combines more familiar gyrovector machinery and therefore faces the stronger obvious-combination objection, as well as a score-definition ambiguity. These are prior-art judgements only; neither candidate is approved on style, and neither can be accepted without the mechanism-specific ablations plus all three frozen thresholds.
