# Measurability review — candidate.md

## Verdict: REVISE

The declared `assignment_audit.emit` artifact is not yet sufficient, as specified, to verify the mechanism's effect without rerunning the assignment. The later schema-3 section improves the proposal by requiring score recomputation from Gram operands and geodesic distances, but it does not fully close the audit contract: it is a prose schema, not an emitted example/schema or implementation, and the earlier emitter signature and schema are left in place without saying which is binding. The review concerns the one declared unsigned tangent-space 2-simplex wedge-area mechanism only.

## What can be verified in principle

If schema 3 is actually emitted with complete, per-example operands for every candidate code, a reviewer can independently recompute `Γ`, `A`, and `S`, compare scores, and derive winners, runner-up margins, ties, and full-versus-area-zero flips. This is substantially stronger than merely reporting aggregate rates. The artifact also expressly keeps the flat-control comparison and partition gate outside its scope; those tests cannot be certified from this artifact alone.

## Missing details needed for decisive falsifiers

1. **Binding schema and executable contract.** Resolve the mismatch between `assignment_audit.emit(...)` in §5 and `emit_v3(...)` in §8. Publish a versioned schema (field names, types, shapes, units, null/NaN policy, ordering) and state that schema 3 supersedes the earlier description. The artifact must identify the producer/code version and frozen configuration, not just assert that it recomputes scores.
2. **Population and denominator.** Include stable example IDs, level/stage identifiers, total eligible examples, and the exact denominator for each rate. Specify whether invalid examples are excluded or fail the run; otherwise a 5% flip rate or 10% tie share can be changed by silent omission. Include per-level numerators and denominators, not just rounded percentages.
3. **Unambiguous ablation and tie semantics.** Specify the exact area-zero score used to obtain `ablated` winners, its tie-breaking key, and whether ties are counted before or after the declared lexicographic rule. Define tie share (among examples, candidates, or assignments) and flip rate (including treatment of tied/invalid cases). Emit the full and ablated score arrays or sufficient operands to independently recompute both, plus the selected keys and tie sets.
4. **Complete recomputation inputs.** Schema 3 names the three Gram entries and geodesic distance, but must specify their dtype/precision and provide them for every example × candidate, together with the actual stored full/ablated scores and winners. Include `ε` and the exact training/evaluation scoring path so the audit can distinguish `A` from `Ã`; state explicitly which score determines evaluation assignments. A hash alone is not a substitute for values needed to recompute.
5. **Numerical and domain checks.** The text says the Gram determinant is computed as a 2×2 Gram determinant “never as a difference of large products,” but the three scalar Gram entries in the artifact cannot establish how it was computed upstream. Define the stable calculation and tolerance, preserve enough precision to reproduce it, and report per-operation counts and denominators for log clamps, code projections, non-finite values, and out-of-ball points. Clarify whether a code projection and a log clamp are separate operations and how repeated operations are counted. The strict clamp failure boundary should have an exact rate formula and exact comparison (`> 0.001`), as §8 promises.
6. **Decision margins.** Define winner/runner-up margin (including zero/one-candidate and tied cases), retain unrounded values, and include enough precision that a reviewer can tell whether near-ties or rounding changed an argmin. The declared tie-share threshold alone does not make a reported winner reproducible.
7. **Integrity and completeness.** Include a manifest with row counts, level/codebook sizes, checksums for the frozen input/split and artifact payload, and a completion marker. This allows detection of truncated, filtered, or mismatched audit files without rerunning the model.

## Decision

Keep the frozen protocol and thresholds unchanged. Before approval, make the schema-3 artifact the sole binding audit format and provide a concrete schema/fixture demonstrating that a reviewer can recompute every reported score and aggregate from the artifact. The flip-rate, tie-share, and clamp falsifiers are promising, but their present prose definitions and unspecified denominators are not yet decisive. The frozen ER/Recall@10/NDCG@10 thresholds remain conjunctive downstream gates; this audit artifact does not by itself establish those metric outcomes.
