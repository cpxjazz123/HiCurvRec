# Measurability review — LOTV

## Verdict: REVISE

The declaration is one mechanism: the unsigned 2-simplex-area candidate is not what is under review. LOTV is the signed ambient Lorentzian 4-by-4 determinant tie-break in `candidate.md`. I find no “second score term” in that document. The stated protocol remains 32-D, three 256-entry codebooks and three integer SID tokens, on the frozen data/splits/recipes; the three outcome cutoffs are conjunctive and strict.

The proposal is substantially more auditable than a prose-only mechanism. In particular, `candidate.md` §AUDIT names `assignment_audit.emit`’s expected operands: vectors after conversion, validity checks, scores and tie tests, determinant terms and accumulated value, A, decision reason, final SID and protocol identifiers. Given a complete, trustworthy row for each comparison, a checker can recompute the local determinant and apply the declared tie rule without running the model implementation.

But the artifact as specified is not enough to verify the *effect* end to end, without rerunning. It describes operands to emit, not a completeness-verifiable, tamper-evident record of the assignment stream and its downstream consequences. The result metrics and hashes are merely reported values; hashes identify inputs/configuration but do not prove that those inputs produced the reported outputs. The following gaps prevent decisive falsification from the artifact alone:

1. **Coverage and completeness.** “For every evaluated item and every candidate triple considered within `tau_score`” does not define a canonical stream boundary, expected item count, candidate-enumeration completeness, or a way to detect omitted rows. Emit every ordinary candidate comparison (including outside-tolerance comparisons), or at minimum a completeness-verifiable per-item summary with the full candidate scores and winner. Include stable item IDs/counts, per-level counts, sequence numbers, and a manifest binding all rows to the split and evaluation run. The current audit operands do not establish the claimed complete stream.
2. **Independent replay of selection.** The row schema should state the exact ordinary winner and all competing triples’ ordinary scores, their deterministic enumeration/tie handling, and the selected triple before and after LOTV. The listed “winning/runner-up values” is ambiguous for multiple candidates. Emit A and the prescribed comparison result for every member of the actual tie set, not just an unspecified pair. This is needed to verify that selection changed only when allowed and that the chosen SID is the declared winner.
3. **Effect and requested thresholds.** The frozen thresholds in the prompt—flip rate `>= 5% per level`, tie share `<= 10%`, and clamp rate `> 0.1%` fails—are not stated or operationalized in `candidate.md`; its `0.1%` condition is instead a non-vacuity minimum for encounters, and it requires at least 100 changed assignments. These are not interchangeable. Define the numerator, denominator, level attribution, handling of invalid/degenerate cases, and strict/inclusive boundary for each requested statistic, and emit sufficient row-level data plus counts to recompute them. “Clamp” is not defined for this operator (which says no clamp); declare the measured clamp event and emit its count, or explicitly establish that the threshold is inapplicable and why. Do not silently substitute candidate.md’s screen for the frozen requested thresholds.
4. **Outcome provenance.** Emit exact evaluator inputs/outputs or a signed evaluator receipt that binds unrounded Hard-L1 ER, Recall@10 and NDCG@10 to the run, split, final SID artifact, and recipe hashes. A printed metric plus hashes is not independently checkable evidence. State metric aggregation and evaluation population, and record the strict comparisons at full precision.
5. **Artifact integrity.** Specify a versioned machine-readable schema, canonical encoding/float representation, row-count and file hashes, and how the emitter’s output is finalized and protected from post-run edits. Include source revision/build and the independent checker version/results. Without this, a reviewer cannot distinguish a complete genuine audit from a selectively edited export.
6. **Non-finite/failure accounting.** Since one invalid/non-finite input fails the run, make the artifact include all attempted items and explicit failure records; otherwise a failing item could disappear from the successful-row set. Include validity and failure counts and ensure a failed run cannot be represented as a passing artifact.

There are also claims not established by the proposed artifact. The byte-for-byte unchanged gradient assertion in invariant 3 needs a defined gradient capture/comparison scope and a before/after digest or equivalent evidence; the current listed operands contain no gradients. The numerical-oracle check needs the deterministic fixture/input corpus (or its reproducible generator and seed) and independent oracle outputs/results, rather than only a statement that 10,000 fixtures were checked. These may be separate pre-training receipts, but must be retained and bound to the run if they are to count as falsifiers.

These are measurability gaps, not a request to revisit the mechanism or the already-refuted families. I do not find grounds here to describe the proposal as a signed 2-simplex area, a second score term, a radius assignment, horospheric level set, transported-residual frame, role-assigned layout, or Euclidean reformulation. Resolve the artifact contract and reconcile the requested thresholds before admission to JOIN_E; otherwise the declared effects and falsifiers cannot be independently verified from the emitted evidence.
