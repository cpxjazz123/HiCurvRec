# Measurability review — candidate v7

## Verdict: REVISE

I reviewed the single mechanism declared in `candidate.md`: the unsigned 2-simplex Gram area, tempered by geodesic distance, with `argmin` assignment. No signed-area or second-score-term mechanism is present in that document; neither is reviewed here.

The proposed audit is a useful start, but **the described JSON is not sufficient to verify the mechanism's effect from that artifact alone**. It says what is written in broad terms, but does not define a schema or enough information to independently recompute the declared falsifiers. A call-site name and stated thresholds do not establish that the emitted values are complete, correctly denominated, or derived from the same evaluation examples.

## What is currently measurable

If correctly implemented, paired full/area-zero winning indices would permit calculating a disagreement rate, while component values and winner/runner-up margins could help diagnose assignments. Counts could support basic numerical-health checks. However, the document does not specify sufficient details for a reviewer to verify those premises from the JSON alone.

## Missing items required for decisive falsifiers

1. **A reproducible, independently checkable record.** Define a versioned JSON schema and include the code/build or commit identifier, audit schema version, configuration (including `c`, `s`, `ε`, clamp rule, dtype and tie tolerance), dataset/split and stage/level identifiers, and hashes for the evaluated example ordering and relevant model/codebook state. Include the number of examples and a stable per-example key (or a privacy-safe deterministic keyed digest). Otherwise one cannot establish provenance, detect omissions/duplicates, or verify that paired assignments refer to the same inputs.
2. **Enough values to recompute winners.** Winners, margins and per-example components are not defined precisely and may be summaries rather than raw data. Specify whether components and margins are recorded for every example and every code, their units/precision, and the exact margin convention. To verify the argmins without rerunning, emit per-code full and ablated score vectors (and the underlying area/distance components), or provide an equivalent lossless sufficient record. Include selected winners and deterministic tie-breaking outcomes as cross-checks. State whether the area-zero score is exactly `1/(1+d)` and how ties are resolved in both arms.
3. **Operational definitions and denominators.** Define flip rate as paired unequal winners divided by the number of valid paired examples, and define tie share (which score/arm, which candidate set, exact or tolerance-based tie, and denominator). Define margin and tie tolerance numerically. Specify whether invalid examples remain in denominators and how they are counted. “Per level” and “per stage” need a complete enumeration and aggregation rule; a missing/empty level must fail, not silently disappear.
4. **Numerical-health accounting.** Give the clamp-rate denominator explicitly (each log evaluation, point, or example), separate clamp counts by operation/level/stage, and define `error_count`. Record out-of-ball checks and non-finite checks/counts at all relevant intermediate and final quantities, including both `A` and `Ã`, scores, and gradients if training stability is part of the claim. Define whether the stated clamp threshold is strict (`>0.1%`) and how rounding is handled.
5. **The remaining falsifiers are not covered by this artifact description.** The flat-limit control needs paired per-example assignment evidence, its exact implementation (`c` value/limit procedure and all otherwise-identical settings), and a pre-registered quantitative rule for “reproduces” (exact equality or a stated rate/tolerance). The partition gate needs per-level code-use counts, distinct-code count, catalogue denominator and largest-cell share, with the precise cell/catalogue definition. The ER/Recall/NDCG gates need their evaluation outputs, denominators, aggregation rules and provenance tied to the same frozen protocol; the audit JSON is not said to contain these. Strict `>` versus `≤` is stated for metrics, but uncertainty/seed handling and the identity of the frozen metric implementation should also be fixed if applicable.
6. **Completeness and integrity.** Emit a declared record count and a final summary calculated from the records; make the artifact append-safe/atomic and fail closed on truncation or missing stages. Include a checksum for the completed artifact (or a manifest referencing it). The review must be able to detect that `emit` was reached for every required evaluation before metric reads, rather than relying on the prose claim alone.

## Required revision

Add a concrete schema/example and precise definitions for the items above, especially the paired population, tie/flip denominators, score/tie behavior, clamp denominator, and flat-control and partition-gate evidence. The audit should contain enough lossless per-example information to independently recompute the assignment comparisons and all stated gates without rerunning model inference. Until that contract exists, the artifact can report numbers but cannot make the falsifiers decisive on its own.

The frozen protocol and strict conjunctive metric thresholds remain as specified in the review brief; this verdict does not propose changing them. The five previously refuted families are out of scope and are not reopened.
