# Adversarial mathematical review

Scope: review of the two operators in `proposals.md` under the frozen 32-D / three 256-way codebook protocol. `literature.md` provides motivation/context but no mathematical validation: its sole verified match is abstract-only and explicitly does not establish compatibility with this setup (literature.md:11,16).

## Mechanism 1 — Signed geodesic-triangle-area assignment

**Verdict: REJECT as written.** The signed area is not defined by the stated orientation prescription in arbitrary dimension; even with a repaired orientation, the score is nonsmooth at common inputs and its claimed flat-limit behavior does not make the complete assignment harmless. Minimal revision: specify an oriented 2-plane/bivector convention that is globally consistent, define area at degenerate triangles by a continuous extension (and accept its nondifferentiability or smooth it), and make the Euclidean control compare the full score/assignment under a stated scaling. Then re-review before joining.

1. **Well-definedness (proposals.md:11–15).** `q=Exp_p(Log_p(x))=x` for α=1, assuming valid interior points and exact maps. Geodesic distance is defined for interior points. But “the unique geodesic 2-simplex” is not enough to define a *signed* area in 32 dimensions: a triangle has a 2-plane, but its orientation must be specified from ordered vertices (or an oriented bivector), not from a single ordered 2-frame at `p` without a rule for resolving that frame against each candidate plane. Projection of the frame can vanish or become ambiguous. In particular, collinear triples have no plane orientation; assigning area zero repairs the value, not differentiability or orientation near the degeneracy. Repeated vertices likewise are degenerate.

   A degenerate base `p=q` makes `Log_p(q)=0`; the proposed plane “spanned by” the two logs is undefined. The explicit collinear-zero convention defines the value but not a unique derivative. Near the ball boundary, formulas involving `λ_p`, `Log`, or `Exp` become ill-conditioned as `1-c||p||²→0`; the open ball excludes the boundary, but quantiser inputs arbitrarily close to it are not uniformly safe. “Clamp only for numerical safety” changes the operator and can break invariance; report rate does not define the mathematical behavior (7, 15).

2. **Score and gradients (proposals.md:13,21).** For interior points, distance and area values are finite. Smoothness fails at degenerate/collinear triangles, coincident points, and wherever the proposed orientation rule changes branch. The angle-defect formula is smooth on nondegenerate triangles but angles/plane orientation are singular at degeneracy. Thus gradients through area are not defined for all inputs the quantiser can see. The softmin is smooth in finite scores, but cannot repair a nonsmooth `S_k`; hard argmin is itself discontinuous at ties. The claim of gradient flow to all listed quantities needs an explicit subgradient/smoothing policy.

3. **Invariants (proposals.md:23).** (i) Zero for collinear triples is stipulated, and swapping `q,z` reverses the vertex orientation only if the sign convention is actually induced by their ordered logs; the fixed-frame wording does not guarantee this. A reflection reverses oriented area, but an isometry with orientation reversal also reverses it, so score invariance requires the “orientation transformed consistently” qualification and is not unconditional. (ii) Gauss–Bonnet angle defect is valid for a nondegenerate geodesic triangle in a constant-curvature plane; it does not settle the sign ambiguity or degenerate derivatives. (iii) The area magnitude and distance are isometry invariant; signed area is invariant only under orientation-preserving isometries (or with a transported orientation). (iv) A_c tends to zero for a bounded, fixed Euclidean-scale triangle as c→0, but this does not imply assignment is harmless: arbitrary β/scaling, codeword parameterization, and near-ties can make a vanishing perturbation flip assignments. More importantly the Euclidean-limit score becomes `β d²`, a nontrivial distance quantizer, not a no-op.

4. **Flat limit/control (proposals.md:17,23,50).** Area has zero flat-limit curvature contribution, but the full score retains `β` times squared Euclidean distance. Consequently the control is ordinary nearest-code assignment, not “area disappears, therefore assignment must demonstrate curvature” without a matched control. Variance fraction alone is not a stable measure of causal contribution: a small area term can flip near-ties, while large variance can be assignment-irrelevant. Require assignment agreement and score perturbation analyses against the same β distance-only control.

5. **Capacity/refuted forms.** The nominal 256 entries and three tokens are preserved (19,23), but no safeguard prevents duplicate/effectively unused codes or host-conditional collapse. It is not a radius-only score as written, but its locality term is squared distance and the area orientation is under-specified. Do not claim it escapes radius/distance confounds until the distance-only control is matched. No obvious hidden Euclidean reparameterisation rescues the area term; conversely the mere use of a hyperbolic area formula does not establish usable curvature signal.

## Mechanism 2 — Non-associative gyro-composition quantization

**Verdict: REJECT as written.** The equations mix incompatible tangent/vector spaces and the second score term subtracts objects that are not in a common vector space. As a result the stated score is not a well-defined scalar operator. Minimal revision: define every vector's tangent space, transport and gyro action consistently, correct the Möbius formula/conventions, and give a dimensionally valid scalar penalty; then test its invariants and re-review.

1. **Well-definedness (proposals.md:29–38).** The displayed Möbius addition formula has a likely denominator error. Standard Poincaré-ball Möbius addition has denominator `1+2c<u,v>+c²||u||²||v||²`, but numerator convention is ` (1+2c<u,v>+c||v||²)u +(1-c||u||²)v`; this agrees as written. For strictly interior `u,v` the denominator is positive. However the input `r=Log_p(x)` is in `T_p`, while `Exp_p(r)` is a point; `v_k` is declared parallel transported into `T_p`, but equation (35) instead uses `Exp_o(T_s Log_o(z))` in the origin chart and combines it with `p` via Möbius addition. Those are not the same construction as `u_k` in (33), and the role of `PT` is discarded. `T_s` is said to act on residual vectors but is applied to a code's origin-log vector (33–35), an unresolved domain/semantics mismatch.

   In (36), `gyr[p, Exp_o(...)]` acts by Möbius gyration on a point/vector convention, then `Exp_p(r)` is a point in the ball. Subtracting that point from itself after a gyration is not a tangent vector at `p`; the resulting Euclidean norm is not the stated `g_p` tangent norm. Also `gyr[a,b]w` is given by a formula whose `⊕` operations require all arguments in the ball; `w` must be a ball point, not an arbitrary tangent vector. The proposal does not specify a valid conversion. `Log_p` of the composed point is defined only if the composition remains in the ball; Möbius addition of interior points does, but applying unbounded/invertible `T_s` before `Exp_o` can saturate numerically near the boundary. “Invertible” alone is not boundedness.

2. **Finiteness/differentiability (proposals.md:35–42).** For interior points, standard `Log`/`Exp` and Möbius addition are smooth, but `Log_p(p)=0` is well-defined (unlike the area-plane issue). Boundary limits are singular/ill-conditioned: denominators `1-c||p||²` and `artanh` at norm 1. At the origin, `Log_o(o)=0` is defined, but derivatives of any chosen gyro/transport convention must be verified. The invalid subtraction/metric in (36) prevents `S_k` from being a well-defined real score, so finite differentiability cannot be claimed. Hard argmin ties are nondifferentiable; softmin does not fix invalid score terms.

3. **Invariants (proposals.md:44).** (i) Identity laws hold for standard Möbius addition with matching conventions, though notation `u⊕0=0⊕u=u` is fine. (ii) Flat limit gives addition and trivial gyration for fixed finite inputs; it does not guarantee assignment equality due to tie flips or scaling. (iii) Gyrations are isometries in the appropriate gyrovector model, but this is not established for the mixed point/tangent action in the proposal; applying an isometry to a point and measuring ordinary coordinate difference is not a tangent-metric-preservation test. (iv) Generic non-collinear inputs do exhibit non-associativity for c>0, but the asserted two association orders are not specified as the same typed inputs in (35–36). (v) Parallel transport preserves Riemannian norm under the exact Levi-Civita transport, but this is a property of `PT`, not proof that the separate learned `T_s` does; arbitrary invertible `T_s` does not preserve norms. (vi) Exact exp/add maps keep points interior, but numerical clamp and unbounded parameters can produce boundary saturation. (vii) Counts are nominally unchanged.

4. **Flat limit/control (proposals.md:44–46,52).** With fixed finite tangent vectors and correctly typed operations, `⊕→+` and gyration→identity. Yet (35)'s composition then yields a sum including code and residual, while (36)'s purported gyration penalty vanishes; the first term remains a nontrivial Euclidean residual-plus-code norm. Thus c→0 is a Euclidean additive residual quantizer, not a harmless null control. Assignment agreement is not implied. The control must compare against that exact additive score with the same code parameterization and `T_s`.

5. **Capacity/refuted forms.** Three 256-entry codebooks and three emitted indices remain on paper (44). But `T_s` can be an arbitrary invertible stage-specific linear map and can absorb substantial codebook reparameterisation; invertibility is not a constraint against learned anisotropic scaling. The first score is a norm of composed residual plus code and can reduce to a Euclidean residual/distance comparison in the flat limit. The “non-associative” distinction only survives if a correctly defined gyration term materially changes assignments. As written the second term cannot establish that, and this proposal risks being a reparameterized residual quantizer rather than a valid new geometric mechanism.

## Join recommendation

Neither candidate is ready for join. Mechanism 1 has an under-specified signed orientation and non-smooth degeneracies; Mechanism 2 has type/domain errors that make its score undefined. These are mathematical blockers, independent of whether either could clear the frozen ER / Recall@10 / NDCG@10 thresholds (proposals.md:3–5,56–62). Do not relax the frozen protocol to address them.
