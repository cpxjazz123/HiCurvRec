IDEA: GROMOV-LCA-MOBIUS-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-tree Gromov products matched by a Möbius-summed curved RQ-VAE code space
MOTIVATION: The frozen semantic-ID pipeline has to place behaviourally adjacent items near each other in code space, yet the plain Euclidean RQ-VAE reaches only Spearman 0.0402 and top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap 0.0124 against the training-split popularity-adjusted transition distance d_beh. The failure is structural: a Euclidean product quantiser cannot represent a tree-like, negatively curved behaviour metric, so co-consumed items fall into unrelated cells even when their text embeddings agree. A behaviour-induced tree, by contrast, embeds in hyperbolic space with arbitrarily low distortion, so the quantiser is given a curved arithmetic and the tree becomes the target the curvature has to realise.
NOVELTY: The earlier rounds moved per-level curvature from prefix entropy (CURVCTRL-SID), ranked items radially by context entropy (BRad-RQ), made codebook updates successor-weighted Fréchet means (poincare-frechet-successor-codebook), and split curvature across branching experts (BRANCHCURV-MOEB-RQ). None of them constructs a behaviour tree, none uses the Gromov product, and none writes the two sides of the objective as the same geometric quantity. This proposal adds two new objects: a spectral bisecting-k-means tree over the popularity-adjusted transition affinity, and an LCA-depth Gromov-product target matched against the Gromov products of Möbius-summed SID points, with the curved residual and geodesic assignment living inside the quantiser rather than being a post-hoc metric or a relabelling of fixed codes.
CHANGE: The RQ residual and the SID point become Möbius operations inside a Poincaré ball (r_l = (−a_l) ⊕ r_{l−1}, x_i = a_1 ⊕ … ⊕ a_L) and every codebook atom is a trainable ball point selected by geodesic distance, so the quantiser is genuinely curved. One extra loss term forces the curved Gromov products of the SID points to reproduce the LCA-depth Gromov products of the behaviour tree, so the same pathway that assigns codes also carries the behaviour gradient into the atoms and the encoder.
SPECIFICATION: Fix curvature c = 1 and working radius R = 1 − 1e−3. The encoder output z is mapped into the ball, atoms are parametrised inside it, RQ runs by Möbius subtraction, assignment is by geodesic distance, and L = L_recon + L_hier, where L_hier is a relative (ranking) Gromov-product match to a frozen behaviour tree. The tree is built once before Stage 2 from the training-split popularity-adjusted transition affinity: top-50 sparse kNN, rank-64 normalised-spectrum embedding, recursive bisecting 2-means to 64 leaves at depth 6. Every constant is structural or numerical (R from the ball boundary, 64 leaves as a documented default of about 384 items per leaf, beta = 1 because both losses are dimensionless, c = 1) and none is swept.
COST: Offline once, before the 3000 epochs: sparse top-50 kNN over 24556 items, a rank-64 randomised SVD of a ~1e6-nnz affinity, six bisecting levels, roughly 3 to 8 minutes on one card, not repeated. Per step at the frozen scale (batch about 1024 of the 24556 training items, 24 steps per epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps): assignments cost L*K*d = 3*256*32 about 25k dot products per sample, about 25 MFLOP per step; three Möbius sums are negligible; the Gromov term evaluates 2*1024 pair distances of dimension 32. Overhead is about 2 to 4 percent of the baseline step, extra memory is one L*K*d atom table, and there is no extra epoch, card or dataloader pass.
HYPOTHESIS: If LCA depth captures the co-consumption hierarchy and the curved code space realises that tree with low distortion, matching Gromov products pulls true successors closer than the Euclidean baseline, visible first as top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap well above 0.0124 (target 0.025 or more) and Spearman well above 0.0402 (target 0.07 or more), then as Stage-3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 under the standing caveat of about 0.013 single-run noise.
RISKS: A six-level tree makes the target nearly binary, so the ordering signal may be too coarse; the behaviour gradient may fight text reconstruction and damage the decoder, showing as worse Stage-3 recall even with a better probe; ball saturation could push atoms to the boundary and make geodesic distances uninformative; the tools-side probe may compute code-space distance as Euclidean on stored parameters rather than as a geodesic, so the Euclidean-on-coordinates variant is reported alongside; sparse transitions make n_ij noisy for tail items and can inflate leaf assignment.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Sequential co-consumption and directed item transitions inside training sequences, converted into the popularity-adjusted symmetric behaviour distance d_beh(i,j) = −log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i pop_j)+1)) and then into a discrete behaviour tree whose LCA depth is the supervision target.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only. n_ij counts training sequences containing both i and j, pop_i counts training sequences containing i, and directed N(i→j) is accumulated from consecutive pairs in the same training sequence. The affinity is W_ij = exp(−d_beh(i,j)) restricted to each item's top-50 neighbours and symmetrised as (W + Wᵀ)/2. The tree is built offline at Stage-2 initialisation by rank-64 truncated SVD of D^(−1/2) W D^(−1/2) followed by recursive bisecting 2-means (deterministic seeding, fixed iteration count) to 64 leaves at depth 6. Each item receives a frozen 6-bit leaf code, and each training transition (i,j) is stored with (leaf(i), leaf(j)).
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Every count, affinity, embedding, leaf code and transition pair is computed exclusively from training-split sequences; validation and test interactions are never read; no Stage-3 signal enters the tree; the tree is frozen before the first Stage-2 epoch, so no validation-driven selection can act on it; and the mechanism needs no early stopping or model selection, so no evaluation split is consulted at any point.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré ball of fixed negative curvature c = 1 with working radius R = 1 − 1e−3, Möbius addition u ⊕ v = ((1 + 2⟨u,v⟩ + |v|²) u + (1 − |u|²) v) / (1 + 2⟨u,v⟩ + |u|²|v|²), geodesic distance d(u,v) = 2 artanh(|(−u) ⊕ v|), and the origin-anchored Gromov product (x·y)_o = 0.5 ( d(x,o) + d(y,o) − d(x,y) ), normalised by G_max = 2 artanh(R).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Curvature acts inside Stage-2 training at four connected places: the encoder-to-ball map, the residual quantiser (Möbius subtraction plus geodesic nearest-atom selection at every level), the SID point formed as a Möbius sum of the selected atoms, and the Gromov product used in the behaviour loss; the atom table is also ball-parametrised, so every atom gradient is a curved-space gradient.
COUPLING PATH: Training transitions give n_ij, pop_i and d_beh, which give the affinity, which gives the frozen tree and the LCA-depth target g_T. In parallel, z is mapped to the ball, quantised by Möbius residuals into atoms and summed by Möbius addition into x_i. Both meet in a single term: removing the behaviour tree zeroes g_T and the loss loses all behaviour content, while removing the curvature replaces the Möbius sum and the Gromov product with a vector sum and Euclidean distance, so each switch changes the gradient that reaches the atoms and the encoder at every step.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_hier = mean_i [ ( ((x_i·x_j)_o − (x_i·x_j')_o) / G_max ) − ( g_T(i,j) − g_T(i,j') ) ]² with x_i = a_{1,k1} ⊕ … ⊕ a_{L,kL} (curved term) and g_T(i,j) = depth(LCA(leaf(i), leaf(j))) / 6 (behaviour term), where j is a stored training successor of i and j' is a uniformly sampled item.
OBSERVABLES: Before Stage 3, the tools-side probe is re-run exactly as specified with d_beh over training transitions only: code-space Spearman against d_beh and top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap, compared with 0.0402 and 0.0124, and the proposal is a candidate only if both improve. During training, mean atom radius, the fraction of atoms within 0.9 R, per-level code usage, reconstruction MSE and the behaviour-term value are logged. After Stage 3, test_recall@10 is reported with the explicit caveat that a single run carries about 0.013 of noise; no early stopping, sweep or seed repetition supports the claim.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEHAVIOUR_ON = False drops the transition pairs and zeroes the behaviour term, leaving the Möbius quantiser, geodesic assignment and curved SID point trained only by text reconstruction. The code is present and correct but this arm is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: GEOMETRY_ON = False replaces Möbius addition with vector addition, the Möbius residual with the ordinary residual, geodesic distance with Euclidean distance, and the ball Gromov product with its Euclidean identity (x·y)_o = 0.5(|x|² + |y|² − |x−y|²), while the same frozen tree and LCA-depth targets stay on. Not trained this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEHAVIOUR_ON = True and GEOMETRY_ON = True, the curved Möbius quantiser plus the behaviour-tree Gromov target, is the single configuration trained in the formal run on the frozen 3000-epoch four-card Stage-2 budget and the frozen 150-epoch Stage-3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: [1] t = tanh(z); p = R·t / sqrt(1 + |t|²); a = R·tanh(θ) / sqrt(1 + |tanh(θ)|²). [2] Möbius u ⊕ v as defined above; inverse −u = −u; d(u,v) = 2 artanh(|(−u) ⊕ v|). [3] r_0 = p; k_l = argmin_k d(r_{l−1}, a_{l,k}); r_l = (−a_{l,k_l}) ⊕ r_{l−1}; q = concat_l(flatten(a_{l,k_l})); L_recon = |Dec(q) − e_text|². [4] x_i = a_{1,k1(i)} ⊕ … ⊕ a_{L,kL(i)}. [5] (x·y)_o = 0.5(d(x,o) + d(y,o) − d(x,y)), d(x,o) = 2 artanh(|x|), G_max = 2 artanh(R). [6] offline: W_ij = exp(−d_beh(i,j)) on top-50 neighbours, symmetrised, rank-64 SVD of D^(−1/2) W D^(−1/2), bisecting 2-means to 64 leaves, g_T(i,j) = common_prefix_len(leaf(i), leaf(j)) / 6. [7] L_hier as in the coupling equation; L = L_recon + 1.0·L_hier. [8] Straight-through: the forward code comes from the argmin, the encoder gradient passes as identity through the selection, and atom gradients are taken directly and through the Möbius sums in x_i.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are a training batch of item ids with their text embeddings, the encoder outputs z, the atom table θ of shape L × K × d, the frozen 6-bit leaf codes, and for each batch item one stored training successor j and one uniform negative j'. Outputs are the L selected code indices per item (the SIDs), the SID ball points x_i, the scalars L_recon and L_hier, and gradients with respect to the atom table, the encoder and the decoder.
SAFEGUARDS: Add 1e−8 to every Möbius denominator, which for c = 1 is bounded below by (1−|u|²)(1−|v|²); clamp the artanh argument to 1 − 1e−6 and radii to R = 1 − 1e−3; keep atoms inside the ball by construction through the smooth R·tanh(θ)/sqrt(1+|tanh(θ)|²) map; use the relative difference-of-pairs form of L_hier so a global contraction of all SID points cannot reduce the loss, which removes the collapse failure mode without a tunable margin; keep reconstruction on unit-normalised text embeddings so beta = 1 is dimensionless rather than tuned; freeze the tree before training so leaf codes cannot co-adapt; skip items with no training successor rather than substituting a random positive; clip gradients only with the existing frozen Stage-2 recipe, adding no new constant.
