IDEA: HYP-GRAPHBOTTLENECK-RQ
TITLE: Curved behaviour-neighbourhood bottleneck inside the RQ-VAE encoder: the quantizer is trained on the Poincare tangent-space mean of an item's training-transition neighbours.
MOTIVATION: The plain Euclidean RQ-VAE places codes by text-embedding proximity only, so items that users actually move between land far apart in code space (probe Spearman 0.0402, top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap 0.0124). Stage 3 then has to predict prefixes whose neighbourhoods are semantically tidy but behaviourally wrong. The fix is not a new distance on finished codes but a change of what Stage 2 is forced to represent: the encoder must survive being averaged with its behaviour neighbours in a curved space before quantisation, so the only codes that reconstruct the item are codes shared with its neighbours.
NOVELTY: Prior rounds moved the codebook (successor-weighted Frechet updates), the radius (behaviour-ranked radial assignment) and the curvature (prefix-transition curvature control). This moves none of those: the codebook, the residual stack, the loss and the decoder are untouched. The coupling sits in the encoder-to-quantizer interface and is a trainable, differentiable geometric operator, not a preprocessing step and not an auxiliary loss. No hyperbolic term is added to the objective; the geometry is inside the forward pass that the existing objective differentiates through.
CHANGE: Every Stage-2 step, each encoder output is mapped into a fixed-curvature Poincare ball, replaced by the equal-weight Frechet (tangent-space) mean of itself and its top-K training-transition neighbours, and mapped back; that curved mean, not the raw encoder output, is what the residual quantizer and the reconstruction loss see.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed c=1 unit Poincare ball. Precompute on the training split only: transition counts n_ij, degree pop_i, d_beh(i,j) = -log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i*pop_j)+1)); neighbour list N(i) = the K smallest-d_beh successors plus predecessors, K = ceil(median out-degree) computed once from the training graph (documented, not tuned). Per step, with encoder output z_i: rescale u_i = clip(s*z_i, r_max), s = 0.8*r_max / q99(||z||) measured on one pass over the training items at epoch 0, r_max = 1/sqrt(c). Ball point p_i = exp_0^c(u_i). Tangent mean m_i = (1/(|N(i)|+1)) * sum_{j in N(i)} log_{p_i}^c(p_j). Smoothed point p_i' = exp_{p_i}^c(m_i), smoothed code input z_i' = log_0^c(p_i')/s. Replace z_i by z_i' everywhere the RQ-VAE forward uses it. Loss, codebook updates, decoder and all hyperparameters unchanged.
COST: 72,000 Stage-2 steps total (24 per epoch x 3000 epochs) on four cards, unchanged. Per step at 24556 items with batch 1024, K neighbours and d=64: about 1024*(K+1)*64 ~ 1.1M exp/log-map evaluations, roughly 10 MFLOP, plus a cached sparse gather of 1.1M floats; that is well under 1 percent of one encoder step. Estimated wall clock +3 to 8 percent, no extra memory beyond a 24556xK cached neighbour index. No extra epochs, no extra Stage-3 forward passes.
HYPOTHESIS: If the frozen Stage-2 objective can only be satisfied when an item and its behaviour neighbours share codes, then Spearman(code-space distance, d_beh) must rise well above the Euclidean baseline 0.0402 and top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap above 0.0124, with a visible drop in level-1 prefix transition entropy while levels 2-3 keep text reconstruction error near baseline. Worth keeping if and only if both probe numbers move up together with level-1 prefix predictability.
RISKS: (a) Over-smoothing: if K is large relative to the codebook, many items collapse to one code, reconstruction of x_i degrades, and the generative model cannot tell items apart, so Stage-3 recall falls even though the probe rises; the self term and the unchanged reconstruction loss are the counterweight, and reconstruction error is logged every epoch as the early warning. (b) Escape route: the encoder can try to arrange neighbours symmetrically around p_i so the tangent mean cancels; in a unit ball with K>=8 this is geometrically expensive, but a low measured ||m_i|| with high reconstruction error would reveal it. (c) The renormalisation s is measured once at epoch 0 and could go stale as the encoder drifts; the clip keeps all points strictly interior so no NaN can occur, but shrinking gradients near the boundary could slow late training.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed transition weight d_beh(i,j) = -log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i*pop_j)+1)) over the training split, used to build the per-item neighbour list.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training interaction sequences, counting directed adjacent pairs i->j into n_ij, with pop_i = out-degree_i + in-degree_i. From these, d_beh is computed for every observed pair and each item keeps the K smallest-d_beh neighbours (successors and predecessors pooled). Computed once before Stage 2; no validation or test sequence is read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: n_ij and pop_i are counted only from training-split sequences; the neighbour list is frozen before Stage-2 epoch 0 and never refreshed, so no gradient, no validation prefix and no test interaction can alter which items are averaged. Stage 2 still sees only the item text embeddings and the training sequences; the probe uses train-split transitions only, so a probe improvement cannot be bought by reading held-out transitions.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1 (unit Poincare ball), with the exponential map exp_0^c, the exponential map at a point exp_p^c, the logarithmic maps log_p^c and log_0^c, and the Mobius addition used inside them.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the Stage-2 forward pass, between the encoder and the first residual quantizer, applied to every batch, every step, differentiably, so its gradients reach the encoder weights and the codebook through the unchanged quantizer.
COUPLING PATH: item text embedding -> encoder -> Poincare point p_i -> tangent-space average over the behaviour neighbour list N(i) -> smoothed point p_i' -> back to Euclidean scale -> residual quantizer -> codebook -> SID. Removing the behaviour signal empties N(i) and the average collapses to the identity; removing the curvature replaces the log/exp average and the ball rescaling by a Euclidean mean. Either change alters the tensor that the quantizer sees, hence the codes and the SID.
COUPLING EQUATION: z_i' = (1/s) * log_0^c( exp_{p_i}^c( (1/(|N(i)|+1)) * sum_{j in N(i)} log_{p_i}^c( exp_0^c(s*z_j) ) ) ), with p_i = exp_0^c(s*z_i); the behaviour term is the neighbour set N(i) built from d_beh, the curved term is the triple of Poincare log/exp maps at curvature c.
OBSERVABLES: (1) Spearman correlation between code-space distance and d_beh, and top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap, computed exactly as in the tools-side probe on training transitions: before = 0.0402 and 0.0124 (plain Euclidean RQ-VAE); after = the single run's measured value, reported as measured. (2) Level-1 prefix transition entropy on training sequences (should drop). (3) Per-level reconstruction MSE (levels 2-3 should stay near baseline; a large rise means over-smoothing). (4) Stage-3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target, with the explicit caveat that one run carries about 0.013 of noise, so the result is a point estimate and not a significance claim.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: use_behaviour=False sets N(i) to the empty set at the operator, so z_i' = z_i exactly (identity map) and Stage 2 is the frozen Euclidean RQ-VAE at the same budget; this arm exists in code and is correct by construction but is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: use_hyperbolic=False replaces the exp/log maps and ball rescaling by the plain Euclidean mean z_i' = (1/(|N(i)|+1)) * sum_{j in N(i) union {i}} z_j with the same neighbour list and the same K, isolating curvature from smoothing; correct in code, not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run, curvature c = 1 plus the d_beh neighbour list, one training run, with no tuning of s, K or c.
SPEC EQUATIONS: c=1; r_max=1.0; s = 0.8*r_max/q99(||z||); u=clip(s*z,-r_max,r_max); p=exp_0(u)=tanh(||u||)*u/||u||; log_0(p)=artanh(||p||)*p/||p||; Mobius p(+)q = ((1+2c<p,q>+c||q||^2)p + (1-c||p||^2)q)/(1+2c<p,q>+c^2||p||^2||q||^2); d_c(p,q)=2/sqrt(c)*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-p)(+)q||); lambda_p=2/(1-c||p||^2); log_p(q)=2/(sqrt(c)*lambda_p)*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-p)(+)q||)*((-p)(+)q)/||(-p)(+)q||; exp_p(v)=p(+)tanh(sqrt(c)*lambda_p*||v||/2)*v/(sqrt(c)*||v||). Pseudocode per step: z=Enc(x_batch); p=exp_0(s*z); m=0; for j in flat(N[batch]): m += log_p(p[batch],p[j]); m/=(deg[batch]+1); p2=exp_p(p,m); z2=log_0(p2)/s; if not use_behaviour: z2=z; if not use_hyperbolic: z2=(z+sum_j z_j)/(deg+1); out=Quantizer(z2); loss=Recon(out,x_batch)+commitment.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: batch of frozen text embeddings x (B x d), encoder output z (B x d), cached neighbour index N (24556 x K, int16) with degrees, the one-time scale s, curvature c and the two boolean switches. Output: a (B x d) tensor z' at the same Euclidean scale as z, differentiable with respect to the encoder weights, used in place of z by the residual quantizer and the reconstruction loss.
SAFEGUARDS: (1) s is taken from the 99th percentile of encoder-output norms measured at epoch 0, so u lies inside the ball with margin, and |s*z| is clipped to r_max. (2) All norms floored at 1e-8 before division, and Mobius-add denominators floored at 1e-8. (3) artanh argument clipped to 1-1e-5. (4) Items with an empty neighbour list use z_i'=z_i. (5) A per-epoch check that no item has ||p_i||>0.999; if one does, log and stop rather than train a degenerate ball. (6) Reconstruction MSE per level is logged; a rise above 2x baseline in levels 2-3 flags over-smoothing. (7) No new scalar enters the loss, so there is nothing to sweep; K and s are measured from the training data and documented once.
END IDEA
