IDEA: DELTA-TREE-STRESS-RQ
TITLE: Curvature measured from the behaviour graph's Gromov delta, spent as a curved stress that pulls the quantized RQ code embedding onto the behaviour graph's ultrametric tree, at every prefix depth.
MOTIVATION: The SID code space is organised by text-embedding reconstruction, not by what users actually do, so two items that users swap for each other constantly can sit in unrelated code regions while two near-duplicate descriptions share a long prefix. The probe already shows this: the Euclidean RQ-VAE reaches only 0.0402 Spearman between code-space distance and the training-split behaviour distance, and 0.0124 top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap. The generative recommender then has to spend its prefix budget on a taxonomy that no user behaviour supports, so its beam search over prefixes drifts away from the items the user would actually take next.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds either changed the metric on frozen codes, or derived curvature from a prefix/transition statistic (per-level prefix-transition score, context entropy, successor profile, behaviour cone). This mechanism derives curvature from a geometric invariant of the behaviour metric itself - the sampled four-point Gromov delta - and it does not use raw graph distance as its target at all: it uses the subdominant ultrametric of the training behaviour metric (MST path-max), which is exactly the kind of metric a negatively curved space represents with low distortion. The curved distance is then applied to the quantized code embedding at every residual prefix depth, weighted by that depth's share of the residual energy, so the coarse SID levels are the ones that must first respect the behaviour tree. No relabelling, no metric swap on fixed codes: the codebook vectors themselves are displaced by a curved stress whose curvature is a measured property of the training behaviour graph.
CHANGE: A single global negative curvature is initialised from the Gromov delta of the training-transition behaviour metric and then learned, and Stage-2 minimises, beside the RQ-VAE loss, a Poincare stress between quantized code embeddings of behaviour-MST-related item pairs whose target is the MST path-max distance; the gradient lands on the codebook entries and on the encoder, so both the codes and the curvature move.
SPECIFICATION: Offline (CPU, once, training split only): compute n_ij directed training transitions and pop_i; form d_beh(i,j) = -log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i*pop_j)+1)); keep the top-K=20 neighbours of each item, symmetrise, take a minimum spanning tree T; store parent pointers and d_T(i,j) = max edge weight on the T path; sample Q=200000 item quadruples to estimate the four-point delta on d_beh, normalise it to delta_tilde and set the initial curvature c_delta = 1/delta_tilde^2. In Stage 2, each step: encode the batch, residual-quantize to get codes k_i^1..k_i^L and the code embedding q_i = sum_l C_l[k_i^l]; for each anchor i sample one near partner j_minus from i's MST children and one far partner j_plus uniformly among items whose normalised tree distance is at least the median; map each prefix embedding into the ball with the curvature-c Poincare exp map; take the Poincare distance at every prefix depth l; regress it on alpha*t_ij with behaviour weights; add lambda times that stress to the RQ-VAE loss. alpha is measured once at the end of epoch 0 and lambda once from the first ten steps' gradient-norm ratio; both are then frozen, as is the curvature's initial value.
COST: Offline precompute is sub-minute CPU and about 2 MB of sparse-graph state (24556 items x 20 neighbours), no GPU. Per step at the frozen scale the extra work is roughly 2*|batch| anchors (|batch| ~ 1023 at 24556 items / 24 steps) times L=3 prefix depths, i.e. about 6000 Poincare distance evaluations in the encoder width plus one additional backward into the codebook: a low single-digit percentage increase in step time over 72000 steps (3000 epochs x 24). The encoder forward, the text encoder passes and the frozen 150-epoch Stage-3 protocol are untouched, so four-card wall clock grows by a few percent, not a factor.
HYPOTHESIS: If the behaviour metric of the training graph is genuinely tree-like at the scale the SID operates on (delta_tilde well below 0.5) then a curvature set by that delta makes the ultrametric embeddable with small distortion, the curved stress makes code-space distance order items the way training transitions do, and prefix levels inherit that order because they carry most of the residual energy. It is worth keeping if the probe's Spearman rises from 0.0402 to clearly above it (order 0.09-0.15) and top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap rises from 0.0124 toward 0.03 or more, and if that transfer survives into Stage 3 as test_recall@10 above 0.065; a single run carries roughly 0.013 of seed noise, so only a margin larger than that is evidence.
RISKS: If delta_tilde is near 0.5 the behaviour metric is not hyperbolic at all and the measured curvature is weak, leaving the stress close to a Euclidean one; the far-pair anchors may be too weak to stop the codebook from contracting; the probe's code-space distance may be a token/prefix distance rather than a distance between code embeddings, in which case the gain arrives only indirectly through prefix alignment; and the frozen alpha and lambda, measured once, could be badly scaled for later epochs.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-transition counts with popularity adjustment, restricted to the Stage-2 training split, turned into d_beh(i,j) = -log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i*pop_j)+1)) and then into an ultrametric tree distance d_T by MST path-max.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training interaction file only: accumulate the directed count matrix n_ij over consecutive item pairs in each training user sequence and pop_i as item i's training interaction count; form d_beh; keep the K=20 smallest-d_beh neighbours per item (K is a documented default, not tuned), symmetrise the resulting sparse graph by averaging the two directions, run Prim to get the MST, precompute the path-max structure through the parent tree; sample Q=200000 quadruples of item indices uniformly to estimate the delta. All of this happens once, before the first Stage-2 step, and is written as a frozen side file.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The count matrix, the popularity vector, the neighbour sets, the MST, the tree distances, the four-point delta, the initial curvature, the alpha scale and the lambda ratio are all functions of the training-split transition file and of nothing else; no validation or test interaction, no held-out user, and no Stage-3 feedback enters any of them. d_T is a property of the item graph, not of any evaluated user, so a test user's sequence cannot influence the SID. The probe itself is recomputed after training with the same training-split d_beh, so probe and mechanism see identical inputs and no test signal leaks into the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: A Poincare ball of trainable negative curvature c = softplus(rho) + 1e-6, initialised to c_delta = 1/delta_tilde^2 from the measured four-point Gromov delta of the behaviour metric, used through the exponential map at the origin and the closed-form Poincare distance.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside Stage-2 training, on the quantized RQ code embedding (not on the encoder's continuous latent alone), at every residual prefix depth; the curvature is a global trainable scalar shared by all depths, so the geometry acts on quantization itself, and its gradient flows back from the code assignments into the codebook.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour transitions -> d_beh -> MST ultrametric target t_ij and pair weights -> Poincare distance between prefix code embeddings under curvature c_delta -> squared stress -> gradient on codebook entries C_l, on the encoder and on rho -> next epoch's code assignment. Removing the behaviour signal removes both the target distances and the pair weights; removing the curvature reduces the same stress to a Euclidean regression on the same targets, changing the metric, the gradient scale and the ball saturation; only the coupled version uses a curvature that the behaviour graph's own tree-likeness determined.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_str = (1/|P|) * sum_l beta_l * sum_{(i,j) in P} w_beh(i,j) * ( d_poincare_c( exp_0^c(q_i^{:l}), exp_0^c(q_j^{:l}) ) - alpha * t_tree(i,j) )^2, with w_beh(i,j) = exp(-d_beh(i,j)/D_b) normalised over P, t_tree(i,j) = d_T(i,j)/D_T, d_poincare_c(u,v) = (2/sqrt(c)) * artanh( sqrt(c) * || (-u) (+) v || ), and c = softplus(rho) initialised from delta_tilde.
OBSERVABLES: The probe Spearman between code-space distance and the training-split d_beh, expected to move from 0.0402 upward; the top-10 behaviour-neighbour overlap, expected to move from 0.0124 upward; the learned curvature c and its drift from c_delta; the frozen alpha and lambda values; the per-depth weights beta_l; the prefix collision rate and the number of distinct level-1 codes as secondary cheap readouts; and finally Stage-3 test_recall@10 against 0.065 with the 0.013 single-run noise caveat stated explicitly.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: use_behavior=False replaces the MST-sampled pairs with uniformly sampled item pairs from the same batch and replaces t_tree by the constant 0.5, leaving the curvature c_delta, the Poincare distance, beta_l, alpha and lambda identical; the term then becomes a curvature-regularised isotropic pair stress with no behavioural content, and the code exists and is checked but this arm is never trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: use_curvature=False replaces d_poincare_c by the Euclidean ||q_i^{:l} - q_j^{:l}|| (the c -> 0 limit of the same stress), keeping the same behaviour pairs, weights, targets, beta_l, alpha and lambda, so only the curved operation changes; the switch exists and is verified but this arm is never trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: use_behavior=True and use_curvature=True, the single training run this round performs: behaviour-derived MST pairs and ultrametric targets under the delta-initialised trainable Poincare metric on the quantized code embedding.
SPEC EQUATIONS:
Precompute: n_ij, pop_i from training transitions; d_beh(i,j) = -log((n_ij+1)/(sqrt(pop_i*pop_j)+1)); clip d_beh to [0, log(1+N_items)].
Sparse graph: E = {(i,j) : j in topK_i d_beh(i,i')} union reversed edges; weight g_ij = mean(d_beh(i,j), d_beh(j,i)) where both directions exist, else the available one.
MST: Prim on (items, E, g) -> parent pi(i), edge weight w_e; d_T(i,j) = max_{e in path_T(i,j)} w_e; D_T = max_e w_e; t_tree = d_T/D_T in [0,1].
Delta: for q in 1..Q draw four distinct items (a,b,c,d); S1 = d_beh(a,b)+d_beh(c,d), S2 = d_beh(a,c)+d_beh(b,d), S3 = d_beh(a,d)+d_beh(b,c); delta_q = (max S - median S)/2; delta_bar = mean delta_q; D_b = mean of the six pairwise d_beh over the same quadruples; delta_tilde = clip(delta_bar/D_b, 0.05, 0.5)  (0.05 is the estimator's resolution floor, documented, not tuned).
Curvature: c_delta = 1/delta_tilde^2; rho = log(expm1(c_delta)); c = softplus(rho) + 1e-6.
Forward: z_i = f_theta(e_i); r^1 = z_i; for l=1..L: k_i^l = argmin_k ||r^l - C_l[k]||^2; r^{l+1} = r^l - C_l[k_i^l]; q_i^{:l} = sum_{m<=l} C_m[k_i^m].
Pairs: P = {(i, j_minus(i)) for i in batch} union {(i, j_plus(i)) for i in batch}, j_minus uniform over MST children of i, j_plus uniform over {j : t_tree(i,j) >= median(t_tree over P_minus)}.
Weights: w_ij = exp(-d_beh(i,j)/D_b) / mean over P of exp(-d_beh/D_b).
Ball map: v_i^{:l} = tanh(sqrt(c) * s_i) * q_i^{:l} / (sqrt(c) * s_i) for s_i = ||q_i^{:l}|| > 0, else 0, with s_i clamped to atanh(0.95)/sqrt(c).
Distance: M = (-v_i) (+) v_j = ( (1 - c||v_i||^2) v_j + (1 - c||v_j||^2) v_i ) / (1 - 2c<v_i,v_j> + c^2||v_i||^2||v_j||^2); d_c = (2/sqrt(c)) * artanh(min(sqrt(c)*||M||, 1-1e-5)).
Energy shares (measured on epoch 0, then frozen): beta_l = mean_i ||C_l[k_i^l]||^2 / sum_m mean_i ||C_m[k_i^m]||^2.
Scale (measured at end of epoch 0, then frozen): alpha = sum_P w_ij d_c(i,j) / sum_P w_ij t_tree(i,j).
Weight (measured on the first ten steps, then frozen): lambda = median_t ||grad_theta L_VAE|| / ||grad_theta L_str||.
Loss: L = L_VAE + lambda * L_str.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the frozen per-item text embeddings e_i, the Stage-2 training transition counts (from which d_beh, the top-K neighbour sets, the MST and delta_tilde are precomputed once), the RQ-VAE encoder f_theta, codebooks C_1..C_L, and the hyperparameters already in the frozen recipe. Outputs are the L code tensors k_i^l for every training item (the SID that Stage 2 emits), the updated codebooks and encoder, the trained scalar curvature c, and a log of alpha, lambda, beta_l and the masked-step fraction; nothing else in the Stage-3 interface changes.
SAFEGUARDS: artanh argument clamped at 1-1e-5 and ball radius limited by clamping the exp-map pre-image norm at atanh(0.95)/sqrt(c); curvature kept positive by softplus(rho)+1e-6; pairs with j == i dropped and pairs with t_tree = 0 skipped; weights normalised so the stress magnitude is independent of batch size; d_beh clipped to [0, log(1+N_items)] so a zero-count pair cannot produce an infinite distance; delta_tilde floored at the estimator resolution so c_delta stays finite when the graph is tree-like; alpha and lambda measured once and frozen so no epoch adapts them to the validation curve; any non-finite stress value masks that step's stress contribution and is counted in the log, and a masked fraction above one percent flags the run as numerically unreliable; the codebook is not re-initialised by the stress, so the RQ-VAE reconstruction term keeps codes separated and the far-pair targets keep the embedding from contracting. Only the both-on configuration is trained: no ablation arm, no sweep over lambda, alpha, c or the clip, and no seed repetition.
