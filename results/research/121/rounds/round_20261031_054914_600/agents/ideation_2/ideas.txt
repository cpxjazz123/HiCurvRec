IDEA: behtree-geocut-sid
TITLE: Behaviour-cut geodesic prefix tree with prefix-local SID ids and hyperbolic soft-assignment (fixed gyrobarycentric anchors)
MOTIVATION: Round one produced 2201 distinct 3-token triples for 24556 items, so ~11 behaviourally different items share one SID and the generative model cannot separate them; the curvature and the prefix structure were also decoupled, and the curved reconstruction objective killed the encoder. The problem addressed is that prefix capacity and prefix membership are allocated by text-reconstruction geometry, not by what users actually do next.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds changed codebook positions (successor-weighted Frechet), per-level curvature, radial rank from entropy/context entropy, cones, per-level boosts and residuals, all on a shared RQ codebook with 256 codes per level. Here the SID is a *tree path in a behaviour-cut geodesic partition*, the token alphabet is prefix-local (the same id at level 2 means different items under different parents), and the fanout of every node is decided by a degree-preserving-null significance test on directed train transitions. So the mechanism changes *which items may share a token and how many distinct triples exist*, i.e. the prefix structure and the SID capacity allocation, not the positions inside a fixed triplet of codebooks. Geometry is not a curvature value (curvature is fixed at c=1 because it is a gauge) but the shape of the separator: geodesic hyperplanes, affine in the Klein chart, sphere arcs in the Poincare model, which cut the item set differently from any Euclidean plane in the Poincare coordinates.
CHANGE: The RQ-VAE assignment is replaced by a descent through a fixed behaviour-built hyperbolic tree: at each of three levels the encoder output competes only among its parent's children, scored by exact Poincare distance to the children's fixed gyrobarycentric anchors, and trained by hierarchical cross-entropy; the tree's fanout and leaves come from directed-train-transition cut tests on geodesic hyperplanes.
SPECIFICATION: Build once from train transitions C: symmetric normalised adjacency, top-32 spectral embedding, lift to the ball as v_i = r_i * g_i/||g_i||. Recursively split each node S by (i) the Fiedler vector of the normalised behaviour Laplacian on S, (ii) the affine (Klein) bisector of the two sides' Klein centroids, (iii) re-assignment by the sign of that affine form until stable, and accept the split only if cut <= E_null[cut] - 2*sigma_null. Recurse to depth 5 per SID level (fanout <= 32) and 3 SID levels. Anchors a_c = Karcher barycentre of {v_i : i in child c}, then frozen. Encoder z = f(x), v = tanh(||z||) z/||z||; logits at level l are -d_P(v, a_c) over the children of the parent chosen at level l-1; loss = text reconstruction (straight-through) + sum_l -log softmax(-d_P). Stage3 unchanged: three tokens per item from a <=32-id-per-level alphabet inside the existing 256-token vocabulary.
COST: Stage2 per-step is not larger than baseline: level-1 distance is 4096x32 anchors, levels 2 and 3 gather only the parent's <=32 children, i.e. 4096*3*32*32 ~ 1.3e7 multiply-adds per step versus the baseline dense 4096x256 codebook matmuls, so expect 0.145 s/epoch to become about 0.14-0.16 s/epoch, i.e. 420-480 s for 3000 epochs at 24 steps/epoch on 4096-item batches. One-off preprocessing: sparse Lanczos top-32 on a ~24.5k x 24.5k transition matrix, ~20-40 s; recursive splits and Klein-bisector iterations over at most 32k nodes, ~2-6 min; ~20 Karcher iterations over 24.5k points in 32 dims, <10 s. Total wall clock +5 to +15 percent, all of it before the first Stage2 step; Stage3 decode cost is identical because the SID length and vocabulary width are unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: The claim is that behaviourally coherent prefixes plus capacity where behaviour is heterogeneous give the Stage3 beam more usable conditional structure than a text-reconstruction partition, and that the extra effective depth (leaves up to 32^3 instead of 2201 triples) is not paid for in prediction difficulty. It is worth keeping if, in the single run, leaves per item count drops well below the round-one 14 items per deepest prefix, the prefix-conditional concentration of the training successor distribution rises (recorded, not gated), Stage2 reconstruction stays finite with no prefix-count collapse (level-1 children used >= 75 percent of allocated children at every level over the last 200 epochs), and Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: Prefix-local ids force one shared token embedding to serve several parent contexts, which can make Stage3 harder to learn and could lose more than the capacity gain; near-unique leaves can destroy the prefix sharing that lets TIGER generalise and may lower recall@10; the 2-sigma null test may accept too few splits on a sparse transition graph, degenerating to a shallow tree and re-creating collisions; tail items with no transitions get degenerate ball coordinates; the hierarchical CE and the text reconstruction can fight, and the straight-through path may let the encoder satisfy CE while the decoder loses text fidelity.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed consecutive-item transitions from the train split only, aggregated as C[i,j] and symmetrised into an undirected behaviour graph W = C + C^T.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: For every session in the train split, count consecutive pairs (i_t, i_{t+1}) into C; row-normalise nothing (raw counts), build the symmetric normalised adjacency A = D_r^{-1/2} (C+C^T) D_c^{-1/2}, take the top m=32 eigenpairs (lambda_k, u_k) by magnitude with Lanczos, set g_i = (lambda_k u_k[i])_{k=1..32}, rho_i = ||g_i||, r_i = r_max * clip(rho_i / q90(rho), r_min/r_max, 1) with r_max = artanh(0.9) and r_min = artanh(0.2), and v_i = r_i g_i/||g_i||, v_i = 0 if rho_i = 0. All of this is computed from train interactions only, before Stage2 starts, and is frozen.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition counts C, the spectral embedding, the ball coordinates, the tree, the fanout, the leaf membership and the anchors are computed in a single pass over the train split interactions, written to a frozen artefact, and never recomputed or updated during Stage2 or Stage3. No validation or test interaction enters C, the SVD, the split criterion, the anchor, or any statistic that affects the SID; validation and test interactions are read only by the frozen Stage3 evaluation after the SIDs are frozen, and the behavioural diagnostics are computed from train-fold transitions only so that they cannot even indirectly select anything.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Constant negative curvature c = 1 (fixed, not tuned, because a tangent rescaling by 1/sqrt(c) reproduces c=1 exactly): the exact Poincare distance d_P(x,y) = arcosh(1 + 2||x-y||^2 / ((1-||x||^2)(1-||y||^2))), the exponential map at the origin exp_0(z) = tanh(||z||) z/||z||, its inverse log_0, the Karcher/gyro-barycentre in the ball, and the geodesic hyperplane represented as an affine hyperplane in the Klein chart k = 2p/(1+||p||^2).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: It acts in SID construction (the partition is made of geodesic hyperplanes and its nodes are gyro-barycentres) and in quantisation every training step (the assignment logits are exact Poincare distances, differentiated back into the encoder). It does not act in Stage3.
COUPLING PATH: train transitions -> symmetrised behaviour graph -> Fiedler coordinate inside each node -> geodesic hyperplane (Klein-affine bisector) -> node fanout and leaf membership -> gyro-barycentric anchor per child -> Poincare-distance logits over that parent's children -> hierarchical cross-entropy -> gradient into the encoder. Removing behaviour changes which hyperplanes exist and which children exist; removing the curved operator replaces sphere-arc separators and Poincare logits by planes and Euclidean norms, which changes both the leaves and the argmax; either removal changes the emitted SIDs.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_sid = -(1/3) sum_{l=1..3} log softmax_{c in Ch(p_{l-1}(i))} ( -d_P( exp_0(f_theta(x_i)), a_c ) ), with a_c = KarcherMean_{B^32}( { v_j : j in S_c } ) and the partition {S_c} defined by argmin over Klein-affine geodesic hyperplanes of cut_W(A,B) subject to cut <= E_null[cut] - 2 sigma_null.
OBSERVABLES: SID assignment: number of leaves, per-level fanout histogram, items per leaf, number of distinct used triples, and the fraction of items whose triple is unique; gradient/parameter update: mean ||z|| over training (must not drift toward 0), gradient norm reaching the encoder through d_P, and per-level child-usage entropy over the last 200 epochs; prefix behaviour predictability: the train-split conditional entropy of the successor's level-1 code given the source item's prefix, and the held-out-train-transition prefix hit rate; Stage3: test_recall@10, recall@5, ndcg@10 over 57439 queries.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: C is replaced by the frozen Stage-1 text kNN graph and v_i by the PCA lift of the frozen text embedding, so the tree, the fanout and the anchors are text-derived; the geodesic hyperplanes, the Poincare distances and the fixed anchors are kept. The SID then has no interaction information at all.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: The same behaviour graph, spectral embedding and ball coordinates are kept, but the separators are ordinary affine hyperplanes in the Poincare coordinates v (not the Klein chart) and the assignment logits are -||v - a_c|| with Euclidean centroid anchors; the split acceptance test is unchanged. Same code path, curved operator replaced by its Euclidean counterpart.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Train-graph spectral ball, geodesic (Klein-affine) separators, gyro-barycentric anchors, Poincare-distance logits, hierarchical cross-entropy, straight-through text reconstruction, 3 SID levels of at most 32 prefix-local ids, 3000 Stage2 epochs on four cards, then the frozen Stage3 protocol. This is the only arm that is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: 1) A = D_r^{-1/2}(C+C^T)D_c^{-1/2}; top-32 eigenpairs; g_i; r_i; v_i = r_i g_i/||g_i||. 2) For a node S: W_S = W[S,S], L_sym = I - D_S^{-1/2} W_S D_S^{-1/2}, f = eigenvector of the second smallest eigenvalue; A = {f<0}, B = S\A. 3) Klein k_x = 2x/(1+||x||^2); kbar_A = mean(k_x, x in A), kbar_B likewise; w = kbar_A - kbar_B, t = 0.5(||kbar_A||^2 - ||kbar_B||^2); repeat assignment x -> A if <k_x,w> > t, B otherwise, with centroid recomputation, at most 5 times, break on a stable sign pattern. 4) Null test: V = sum_{i in S} d_i, m = vol(A)vol(B)/(V-1), s2A = sum_{i in A} d_i^2, s2B likewise, Var = m - s2A s2B/V^2, accept iff cut_W(A,B) <= m - 2 sqrt(max(Var,0)) and cut_W(A,B) >= 1 and |A|,|B| >= 1. 5) Recurse while depth < 5 and |S| >= 2 and the test passes; n_min = max(2, ceil(N/b^3)) = 2 with b = 2^ceil(log2(N^{1/3})) = 32; node children are numbered 0..k-1 and that number is the token id at that level. 6) Anchor: a <- exp_{a}( (1/n) sum_i log_{a}(v_i) ) for 20 iterations from a = mean(v_i), then clamp ||a|| <= 1-1e-5. 7) v = exp_0(f_theta(x)); logits = -d_P(v, a_c); L = L_text(z + sg(sum_l log_0(a_{p_l(i)}) - z)) + L_sid. 8) Forward pass pseudocode: for l in 1..3: Cands = children(parent_{l-1}); s = -d_P(v, a[Cands]); p_l = argmax s; loss += -log softmax(s)[p_l]; token_l = index of p_l among Cands.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the train-split session list, the item index (24556 training items), the frozen Stage-1 text features x_i, the codebook dimension 32, the SID length 3, and the vocabulary width 256. The tree builder consumes (C, v, W) and returns the node table (parent, child index, member set, anchor a_c, depth), which is frozen. The encoder consumes x_i and returns z_i in R^32; the quantiser consumes (z_i, node table) and returns the triple of prefix-local token ids and the softmax probabilities used by the loss; the decoder consumes the straight-through quantised tangent vector and returns the reconstructed text features.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||x||, ||a|| <= 1-1e-5 and denominators 1-||x||^2 >= 1e-6; clamp the arcosh argument to >= 1+1e-7; if ||z|| < 1e-8 set v = 0 and let the logits be -d_P(0, a_c) = -2 artanh(||a_c||), which is finite because anchors are clamped; log mean ||z|| and the per-level child-usage entropy every 50 epochs and treat a monotone decay of ||z|| toward 0 together with a usage entropy toward 0 as the round-one collapse signature (the loss is maximal at the origin, so this should not trigger, but it is the check); nodes with no significant split become leaves and their members share the prefix, which is intended and is not repaired by forcing splits; items with rho_i = 0 get v_i = 0 and are placed by the hyperplane sign with the deterministic tie-break "first child"; empty child sets are pruned before anchor computation; all tree and anchor quantities are recomputed once from the frozen artefact hash so that Stage2 and Stage3 read bit-identical SIDs; no weight, temperature, clip bound or curvature is swept: the only scalar constants are lambda = 1 for the two summed losses (equal-scale documented default), tau = 1 in curvature-normalised units, c = 1 by gauge, r_min/r_max = artanh(0.2)/artanh(0.9), the 2-sigma one-sided test, depth 5 and b = 32, all derived from N, the SID length and the vocabulary width.
END IDEA
