IDEA: CONCMID-GEO-SID
TITLE: Concentration-scaled geodesic step toward each item's behaviour-successor barycentre, taken before level-1 quantization (items move, codebooks do not).
MOTIVATION: The SID is currently determined by where an item's text latent happens to sit in the ball. That geometry knows nothing about what the item is followed by, so at depth three 24,556 items are compressed into 2,201 prefixes (~11 items each) whose members are textually similar but behaviourally unrelated; Stage3 then has to spend prefix tokens discriminating items that the prefix says are the same, or predicting a next item whose prefix is not the prefix that behaviour would have chosen. Issue 126 says the recall-predictive structure is behaviour-CONTRAST (within-group tightness plus between-group separation), not Gromov-delta and not ancestor depth, so the fix has to be a partition change driven by directed behaviour contrast, not another additive loss.
NOVELTY: Different coupling position from everything already tried here. The rejected/prior mechanisms changed (a) codebook centroids via successor-weighted Frechet means, (b) per-level curvature from hard prefix-transition scores, (c) the radial coordinate by context entropy, (d) Lorentz successor-profile smoothing of the codebook, (e) a behaviour cone on prefixes, (f) a successor boost shift. This one leaves the codebooks, the loss and the encoder completely untouched and instead moves the QUANTIZER INPUT: each item is transported along the geodesic toward the tangent-mean of the items that actually follow it, by a step size equal to the measured concentration (Herfindahl) of its own popularity-adjusted transition distribution. There is no origin-minimising objective (the round-one collapse mode), because nothing is optimised: it is a deterministic, closed-form, once-per-run input transformation. No Gromov delta, no ancestor-depth weight, no tree premise. The curvature is not a scale gauge here: the operation is a basepoint-dependent exponential map at each item's own position, which has no flat counterpart at equal step size.
CHANGE: Replace the frozen per-item latent z_i that feeds the RQ chain by b_i = exp_{z_i}( eta_i * sum_j w_ij log_{z_i}(z_j) ), where w_ij are popularity-adjusted directed-transition weights computed from the Stage-2 training split only and eta_i = sum_j w_ij^2 is the measured concentration. Nothing else in Stage2 or Stage3 changes.
SPECIFICATION:
Geometry, fixed curvature c = 1, unit Poincare ball B = {x in R^32 : ||x|| < 1}.
  scale: kappa = 0.9 / max_i ||t_i||  (t_i = frozen Stage-1 text latent, d = 32), computed once, so every point starts inside radius tanh(0.9) = 0.716.
  z_i = exp_0(kappa t_i) = tanh(kappa||t_i||) * t_i/||t_i||.
  log_z(w) = d_B(z,w) * (w (-) z)/||w (-) z||, with Moebius difference (w (-) z) = ((1-||z||^2)w - (1-2<z,w>+||w||^2)z) / (1-2<z,w>+||z||^2||w||^2) and d_B(z,w) = 2*atanh(||w (-) z||).
  exp_z(u) = z (+) tanh(||u||) * u/||u||  (Moebius addition).
Behaviour weights, from the Stage-2 training interaction sequences only:
  count lag-1 pairs (i_t, i_{t+1}) with weight 1.0 and lag-2 pairs (i_t, i_{t+2}) with weight 0.5, over all training sequences truncated at the Stage-2 split boundary;
  p_ij = C_ij / sum_k C_ik ;  popularity p_j = (#occurrences of j)/(#training events);
  adjusted  w_ij = (p_ij / p_j^{1/2}) / sum_k (p_ik / p_k^{1/2}), over {j : C_ij > 0, j != i}.
Step: v_i = sum_j w_ij log_{z_i}(z_j) ;  eta_i = sum_j w_ij^2 ;  b_i = exp_{z_i}(eta_i v_i).
Quantize: feed b_i into the existing curved residual chain (3 levels x 256 codewords, CODEBOOK_DIM 32) and train the codebooks exactly as the baseline does for 3000 epochs.
Switches (all present in code, only both-on trained): BEHAV_SMOOTH in {False,True}; CURV_C in {1.0, 0.0}; both-on = (True, 1.0). BEHAV_SMOOTH=False sets eta_i = 0 for all i, hence b_i = z_i exactly. CURV_C = 0.0 replaces every exp/log by its flat limit, i.e. z_i = kappa t_i, log_z(w) = w - z, b_i = z_i + eta_i sum_j w_ij (z_j - z_i), and the residual chain becomes a flat RQ-VAE; that is the Euclidean counterfactual, with the same weights and the same eta.
COST: One static precompute and zero per-step cost. Precompute: build the lag-1/lag-2 sparse count matrix from the training sequences (order 10^6 nonzeros), row-normalise, popularity-adjust, then one log map per nonzero plus one exp map per item, over d = 32: roughly 1.5e6 x ~150 flops ~ 2e8 flops, a few seconds on one GPU, and a 24,556 x 32 float32 table (3.1 MB). At the frozen scale (24,556 training items, 24 steps/epoch, 4096 global batch, 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps) the added per-step cost is a single row-gather: 0 FLOPs, 0 added communication, no change to DDP bucket sizes. Stage2 wall clock therefore stays at the measured 571 s / 0.145 s per epoch plus about 5 s of precompute (<1%); Stage3 is untouched at 150 epochs.
HYPOTHESIS: What must be true is that (i) items with a concentrated, popularity-adjusted successor distribution are exactly the items whose text latent is a poor guide to their next-item role (serialised content, tightly coupled co-consumption), and (ii) the curved step damps far moves more than a flat blend would, because items already near the ball boundary would otherwise be pushed onto their neighbour's prefix and collapse level-1 usage. If both hold, moving those items onto the geodesic toward their successors' tangent barycentre raises prefix-level predictability (the level-3 token becomes predictable from levels 1-2 and the last item) while the boundary damping keeps the partition spread out. Observed as: level-1 active codewords stay >= 240, distinct L2/L3 prefixes stay >= 1,700, within-prefix behaviour dispersion drops, between-prefix contrast rises, and Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065 in the single frozen run.
RISKS: The concentration step may be tiny for most items (diffuse real-world transition distributions), in which case b_i ~ z_i, the SID is effectively unchanged and the run reproduces round one's 0.054; the eta histogram and the SID-change fraction are recorded for exactly this reason. Popularity adjustment with exponent 1/2 may be too weak, so many items are pulled onto the same hub and level-1/level-3 prefix counts fall. Because the geometry is frozen, the mechanism can only repartition the existing Stage-1 geometry, which round one showed yields a modest ceiling; the step is not allowed to fix a bad text geometry, only to re-aim it. Curvature may turn out to be a near-gauge for the smoothed points (the flat and curved midpoints differ only at second order for small steps), in which case the geometry contributes little; since no geometry-off arm is trained, this is recorded as an untested risk, not an admission gate. Duplicate SIDs could appear where two items share a dominant successor; a deterministic one-pass repair is specified. Finally, the whole mechanism depends on the transition matrix being informative for cold items, which it is not; those keep eta_i = 0.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions with a one-step skip, weighted and popularity-adjusted (a sequential-co-consumption signal), collapsed per item into a normalised successor distribution w_ij and its concentration eta_i.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training sequences only (the interaction sequences used to train the RQ-VAE), truncated at the Stage-2 split boundary: for every position t add 1.0 to C[i_t, i_{t+1}] and 0.5 to C[i_t, i_{t+2}]; drop self-pairs; row-normalise to p_ij; divide by p_j^{1/2} using item frequencies counted on the same training events; renormalise to w_ij; eta_i = sum_j w_ij^2 is the Herfindahl concentration of that row. Items with no outgoing transition in the training split get eta_i = 0 and are counted in a diagnostic.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Every count, every frequency, every row of w and every eta_i is computed from training-split interactions only; validation and test sequences are never opened, and the Stage-3 test queries are not touched at any point of SID construction. The text latents t_i are the frozen Stage-1 outputs built from item metadata, which contain no interaction data at all. No user identifier and no held-out interaction enters the table; the table is built once, before Stage2 starts, and is frozen for the whole 3000 epochs, so no feedback from Stage-2 or Stage-3 loss can reach it.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1 on the unit Poincare ball: the exponential map exp_{z}(.) anchored at the item's own position, the logarithmic map log_{z}(.) to that tangent space, the induced Poincare distance d_B, and the resulting geodesic convex combination exp_{z}(eta * log_z(mu)). Curvature is fixed, not tuned; the mechanism makes no claim about curvature magnitude (issue 126 showed curvature is a gauge), it uses the basepoint-dependent exp/log pair, which is precisely what does not exist at c = 0.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In quantization only, at the input of level 1: the item latents that the residual quantizer partitions are moved in the ball before the first assignment, and because the level-1 assignment, the residual and hence the level-2 and level-3 codes are all functions of that input, the curved step re-partitions all three levels. No change to the encoder, the decoder, the losses, the codebook dimension, the number of codewords or the Stage-3 model.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour counts -> row-normalised, popularity-adjusted weights w_ij and concentration eta_i -> tangent vector v_i = sum_j w_ij log_{z_i}(z_j) -> geodesic step b_i = exp_{z_i}(eta_i v_i) -> level-1 assignment -> residual -> level-2, level-3 codes -> SID tokens -> Stage-3 sequence model. The behaviour term sets both the direction and the length of a curved displacement, and the curved term sets what that displacement means geometrically; setting eta_i = 0 deletes the whole pathway (b_i = z_i, the baseline input), and setting c = 0 turns the same weights into a flat blend, which changes the length and the landing point of every step for every item at radius > 0. Neither removal leaves the core computation intact.
COUPLING EQUATION: b_i = exp_{z_i}( eta_behaviour(i) * sum_j w_behaviour(i,j) * log_{z_i}(z_j) ), with eta_behaviour(i) = sum_j w_behaviour(i,j)^2, all maps taken on the Poincare ball of curvature c = 1; behaviour enters through w and eta, curvature through the basepoint-dependent exp/log at z_i in B_c.
OBSERVABLES: (1) SID assignment: the fraction of items whose level-3 code differs from the code that their unsmoothed z_i would receive under the trained codebooks (computed post hoc, no training) should be large, target > 0.20; (2) prefix structure: level-1 active codewords, distinct L2 and L3 prefix counts must stay in band (L1 >= 240, L2 >= 1,700, L3 >= 1,700, no duplicate SIDs after repair); (3) prefix behaviour predictability: mean total-variation distance between the successor distributions of items sharing a level-3 prefix should fall and the mean between-prefix TV should rise, and the entropy of the level-3 token given (level-1, level-2, last item) on the training sequences should fall; (4) radial structure: the correlation between prefix-group mean radius and prefix-group behaviour dispersion should be more negative than in the text-only partition, matching the group-compactness finding of issue 126; (5) Stage3: test_recall@10 against the 0.065 gate, with recall@5 and ndcg@10 recorded.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEHAV_SMOOTH = False, CURV_C = 1.0: eta_i = 0 for every item, so b_i = z_i and the run is the round-one frozen-geometry curved residual chain exactly; the pipeline executes the same code path with the tangent sum multiplied by zero, so the switch is verifiably wired. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: BEHAV_SMOOTH = True, CURV_C = 0.0: the ball is replaced by flat R^32, z_i = kappa t_i, log_z(w) = w - z, and b_i = z_i + eta_i sum_j w_ij (z_j - z_i), i.e. the identical behaviour weights and the identical concentration applied as an arithmetic blend inside a Euclidean RQ-VAE. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEHAV_SMOOTH = True, CURV_C = 1.0: the single trained arm, b_i = exp_{z_i}(eta_i sum_j w_ij log_{z_i}(z_j)) on the unit ball, followed by the frozen 3000-epoch Stage-2 recipe and the frozen Stage-3 protocol. One run, no tuning, no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: pseudocode for the precompute:
  read training sequences; for each sequence s, for t in range(len(s)-2):
      C[s[t], s[t+1]] += 1.0 ; C[s[t], s[t+2]] += 0.5
  for i: C[i,i] = 0
  freq[j] = column_sum(C)[j] ; p_j = freq[j]/freq.sum()
  for each row i with row_sum(C[i]) > 0:
      p_ij = C[i,j]/row_sum(C[i])
      a_ij = p_ij / sqrt(max(p_j, eps))
      w_ij = a_ij / a_i.sum()
      eta_i = sum_j w_ij^2
  else eta_i = 0
  kappa = 0.9 / max_i ||t_i||
  Z = [exp_0(kappa t_i) for i]                     # unit ball, c=1, radius tanh(0.9) <= 0.717
  for i:
      V[i] = sum_j w_ij * log_{Z[i]}(Z[j])         # tangent at Z[i]
      V[i] = clip_norm(V[i], max_norm = 0.995)     # artanh(0.995) safety
      B[i] = exp_{Z[i]}(eta_i * V[i])
      if ||B[i]|| > 0.995: B[i] = B[i] * 0.995/||B[i]||
  save B as the frozen item input table
  Stage2: for 3000 epochs, 24 steps of global batch 4096, feed B[i] to the existing 3x256 curved RQ-VAE, unchanged loss
  Stage3: frozen protocol, unchanged
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the frozen Stage-1 item text latents t_i in R^32 (one row per training item), the Stage-2 training interaction sequences, the curvature constant c = 1 and the two switches. Outputs: one 24,556 x 32 float32 table B of smoothed ball coordinates, consumed in place of the text latents by the unchanged RQ-VAE; plus diagnostics (eta histogram, per-item support size, count of eta = 0 items). The operator is deterministic and idempotent at fixed inputs; it is computed once before Stage2 and never recomputed.
SAFEGUARDS: (a) atanh argument clipped at 0.9999 and the Moebius denominator floored at 1e-8, so no log map can blow up; (b) the tangent step is norm-clipped to 0.995 and the result re-projected to radius 0.995, so no point reaches the boundary and the exp map never saturates; (c) eta_i is bounded in [1/|S_i|, 1] by construction and is exactly 0 for items with no training transitions, so every step is a contraction toward a neighbour and never an extrapolation past it; (d) popularity adjustment with exponent 1/2 (documented BM25-style default) prevents hub collapse, and level-1 active-codeword count and distinct L2/L3 prefix counts are logged every 100 epochs to expose collapse early; (e) all arithmetic in float32 with the ball radius checked after every exp map; (f) duplicate-SID repair: if two items share a (q1,q2,q3) triple the item with the larger eta_i keeps it and the other item takes the next-nearest level-3 codeword under the same Poincare distance, in a single deterministic pass, with the number of repaired items reported; (g) the table is built once and frozen, so no run-to-run drift and no dependence on the Stage-2 batch order.
END IDEA
