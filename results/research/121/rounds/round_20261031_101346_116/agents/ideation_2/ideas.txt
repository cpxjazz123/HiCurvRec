IDEA: HEXSWAP-SID
TITLE: Curvature-gated, size-preserving exchange repair of the level-1 RQ-VAE token using training-only transition log-ratios and Lorentz geodesic bisectors.
MOTIVATION: The measured failure is not that the RQ-VAE codebook is badly placed; it is that the level-1 cell of an item contains behaviourally disjoint items, so a Stage-3 sampler that commits to a coarse token is forced into a candidate set whose members have nothing to do with the anchor. Earlier rounds attacked this by re-placing codewords (Frechet means), re-scaling curvature per prefix level, or re-ranking the assignment radially by context entropy, and none produced the required drop in the level-1 negative hit rate. This idea accepts the learned codebooks and the learned cell sizes as given and only repairs the membership of a small, explicitly admissible set of items, so the diagnostic cannot be improved by shrinking cells.
NOVELTY: It is a post-quantization discrete repair, not a codebook update, not a curvature schedule, and not a radial re-weighting. Three properties are new here: (i) level-1 cell cardinalities are provably invariant (every accepted action is a two-item exchange), so the L1 negative-hit diagnostic is attributable to cell composition alone; (ii) the move set is generated only where the Lorentz nearest codeword disagrees with the frozen Euclidean RQ-VAE assignment, so the geometry does quantizer work rather than decorating it; (iii) admissibility is a single inequality in which the behaviour log-ratio must overcome a curvature-scaled bisector penalty, plus a lexicographic potential that is monotone non-increasing by construction. Unmoved items keep byte-identical baseline SIDs.
CHANGE: After the 3000th Stage-2 epoch and before SIDs are frozen for Stage 3, embed the frozen item latents and the frozen level-1 codewords into the Lorentz model with curvature derived from the measured Gromov delta of the training transition graph, and greedily execute item exchanges between Lorentz-geodesically adjacent level-1 cells whose behaviour log-ratio clears a bound tau taken from the quantile of the baseline same-cell log-ratios at 1 minus the baseline train-split L1 negative-hit rate. Each exchange is taken only if the behaviour-conditioned, curvature-scaled admissibility margin is positive and the pair potential (number of behaviour-disjoint geodesically adjacent cell pairs, number of behaviour-disjoint same-cell pairs) decreases lexicographically; residuals at levels 2 and 3 are re-quantized only for the moved items.
SPECIFICATION: See SPEC EQUATIONS. Symbols: N = 24556 items, W the symmetrised training transition matrix, d_i its degree, M = sum(W)/2, alpha = 2M/(N(N-1)) the configuration-model edge expectation (fallback 1 if M = 0), lambda_ij the popularity-adjusted transition log-ratio, rho_ab the cell-pair version of it, K the curvature, x_i and c_a the Lorentz images of item latent and codeword, d_L the Lorentz distance, m the signed bisector margin. Bound tau is the (1 - h1_train) empirical quantile of the baseline same-L1 pair log-ratios, where h1_train is the baseline L1 negative hit rate estimated on the training split. No constant is chosen by trying values: alpha, K and tau are all measured or derived quantities.
COST: Stage 2 and Stage 3 are untouched: 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 optimizer steps, added per-step cost exactly 0. The repair is one post-hoc pass: transition-graph build O(nnz(D_train)), Lorentz Voronoi adjacency over C1=256 codewords at 3 geodesic probes and d=64, about 3.2e9 flops (well under one second on the four cards already allocated, about 20 s on CPU); item candidates at most N x mean degree about 1.5e5 distance evaluations; residual re-quantization O(#moves x d x (|C2|+|C3|)), bounded by 24556 x 64 x 512 in the impossible worst case and in practice a few thousand items. Total added wall clock on the order of one minute, under 0.5 percent of the run; amortized over the frozen 72000 steps this is roughly 1e4 flops per step, below 0.1 percent of a training step.
HYPOTHESIS: The idea is worth keeping only if, in the single coupled run, the validation L1 negative hit rate falls below the baseline value at unchanged level-1 cell sizes, the Stage-3 level-1 token accuracy rises, and test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065. Pre-registered kill conditions: if the baseline h1 (train estimate) is below 0.02 the cell contents are already coherent and no exchange can help; if the Lorentz nearest-codeword disagrees with the Euclidean assignment for fewer than 100 items the curvature term is inert and the result must be recorded as a geometry no-op, not as a negative on the mechanism.
RISKS: The frozen encoder latent may be near-Euclidean at the measured curvature, making the Lorentz-versus-Euclidean disagreement set empty and the repair a no-op; the tau quantile may be so high that no swap is admissible and the SID file is unchanged; swapping an item out of the cell the encoder chose breaks encoder-SID consistency and could cost reconstruction quality that Stage 3 partly relies on; the improvement may be confined to level 1 while L2/L3 negative hits dominate the recall loss; a single run with one seed cannot separate a small true gain from seed noise, so only a clear h1 drop plus a recall above 0.065 is interpretable.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item transitions from the training split, symmetrised, with a cell-level aggregation; this is the co-consumption/transition signal, computed on training sequences only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From D_train, W_ij = number of consecutive (i,j) transitions over all training sequences, then W <- W + W^T. d_i = sum_j W_ij, M = sum_ij W_ij / 2. lambda_ij = ln((W_ij + alpha)/(d_i d_j/(2M) + alpha)). For a cell S_b, W_{i,S_b} = sum_{j in S_b} W_ij, D_b = sum_{j in S_b} d_j, lambda_{i,b} = ln((W_{i,S_b} + alpha)/(d_i D_b/(2M) + alpha)); the cell-pair version is rho_ab = ln((E_ab + alpha)/(D_a D_b/(2M) + alpha)) with E_ab = sum over i in S_a, j in S_b of W_ij. The baseline train-split L1 negative hit rate h1_train is estimated by holding out the last position of every training sequence, sampling one negative uniformly from items that anchor never interacted with, and counting the fraction of pairs with C1(negative) = C1(target) and lambda <= 0.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: W, d, alpha, delta-hat, K, the baseline same-cell log-ratio distribution and the h1 used to define tau all come from D_train and from holdout tails inside D_train. Validation and test interactions are used only after the SID file is frozen, to report the validation L1 negative hit rate and Stage-3 recall; they never enter W, tau, tau's quantile, the curvature, the candidate set or the accept decision. The Stage-2 encoder and codebooks are frozen before the repair, so no gradient from any split reaches the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature K = 1/(4 delta_hat^2), where delta_hat is the Gromov delta of the training transition graph measured by the four-point condition on sampled quadruples of BFS-ball items; Lorentz model with inner product <x,y>_L = -x_0 y_0 + x.y and <x,x>_L = -1/K, exponential map at the origin, Lorentz distance d_L(x,y) = (1/sqrt(K)) arcosh(-K <x,y>_L), geodesic midpoint by Lorentz-normalised linear interpolation, and a signed geodesic bisector margin m(i;a,b) = d_L(x_i, c_b) - d_L(x_i, c_a) between the two codeword Voronoi cells. The curvature is derived from the measured tree-likeness (sqrt(K) delta_hat = 1/2), not tuned.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: At the quantization/assignment step, in the final SID-assignment pass that runs once after Stage-2 epoch 3000 and before the SIDs are written for Stage 3. It decides membership changes at level 1 and therefore the prefix that Stage 3 sees; it also decides which item pairs are geometrically adjacent cells in the first place.
COUPLING PATH: One inequality decides a membership change: sigma(i -> b) = lambda_{i,b} - sqrt(K) * max(0, m_L(i;a,b)), admitted iff sigma > tau and the potential decreases. The behaviour term lambda and the curved term sqrt(K) m_L are added inside the same margin that gates the same exchange, so removing behaviour makes the exchange a pure hyperbolic re-assignment, and removing curvature makes it a pure behaviour-driven re-assignment; neither can reproduce the coupled accept set. The same curved operator also supplies the adjacency graph whose disjointness count is the potential, so the two are coupled twice over one pathway.
COUPLING EQUATION: admit(i in S_a -> S_b)  <=>  lambda_{i,b} - sqrt(K) * max(0, d_L(x_i, c_b) - d_L(x_i, c_a)) > tau  AND  Phi_after <_lex Phi_before, with lambda_{i,b} = ln((W_{i,S_b} + alpha)/(d_i D_b/(2M) + alpha)) and tau = Quantile_{1 - h1_train}({lambda_ij : C1(i) = C1(j), i != j}).
OBSERVABLES: Validation L1 negative hit rate (primary diagnostic, must be reported next to baseline); the unconditional L1 share rate (to show cell sizes did not move); the level-1 cell-size histogram (must be identical to baseline); number and fraction of items exchanged; number of behaviour-disjoint geodesically adjacent cell pairs before and after; number of behaviour-disjoint same-cell pairs before and after; the count of items where the Lorentz nearest codeword differs from the RQ-VAE codeword; Stage-3 level-1, level-2 and level-3 prefix token accuracy; test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: The same Lorentz embedding, adjacency, curvature and exchange machinery, but lambda is replaced by 0, tau by -infinity and the potential by the geometry alone: an exchange is taken iff m_L(i;a,b) < 0 and m_L(j;b,a) < 0 (both items are already Lorentz-closer to the other cell) and cell sizes are preserved. This is a pure hyperbolic re-assignment of items that the Euclidean quantizer misplaced, with no behaviour gate and no disjointness predicate, so it isolates exactly the behaviour contribution.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: The identical graph, alpha, lambda, tau and potential, but the curved term is replaced by its Euclidean counterpart sigma_E(i -> b) = lambda_{i,b} + max(0, (||z_i - c_b||_2 - ||z_i - c_a||_2)/(||c_a - c_b||_2 + 1e-8)), and cell adjacency is Euclidean Voronoi adjacency (shared facet of the nearest-codeword diagram). Curvature is absent and the geometry term never grows with distance from the origin, so the accept set differs from the coupled one whenever curvature matters.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full run described in SPEC EQUATIONS: Lorentz embedding with K = 1/(4 delta_hat^2), Lorentz Voronoi adjacency, sigma = lambda - sqrt(K) max(0, m_L), tau from the baseline train-split L1 negative-hit rate, greedy lexicographic descent on Phi = (number of behaviour-disjoint geodesically adjacent cell pairs, number of behaviour-disjoint same-cell pairs), each item exchanged at most once. No arm is trained; these three switches exist in code and are exercised only by a unit test on a small synthetic batch, not by a training run.
SPEC EQUATIONS:
  # one-time, after Stage-2 epoch 3000
  W_ij <- count of consecutive (i,j) in D_train; W <- W + W^T
  d <- W 1 ; M <- sum(W)/2 ; alpha <- 2M/(N(N-1)) if M>0 else 1
  lambda_ij <- ln((W_ij + alpha) / (d_i d_j/(2M) + alpha))
  # curvature from the training graph only
  delta_hat <- 0.5 * max_{a,b,c,d in sample} (L1 - L2), L1>=L2 the two largest of
               {d_G(a,b)+d_G(c,d), d_G(a,c)+d_G(b,d), d_G(a,d)+d_G(b,c)}
  K <- 1/(4 * max(delta_hat,0.5)^2)
  # Lorentz embedding
  exp0(v) = ( cosh(sqrt(K)||v||)/sqrt(K), sinh(sqrt(K)||v||) v / (sqrt(K)||v||) ), exp0(0)=(1/sqrt(K),0)
  x_i <- exp0(z_i) ; c_a <- exp0(codeword_a)
  dL(x,y) <- (1/sqrt(K)) * arcosh( clip(-K <x,y>_L, min=1+1e-7) )
  # geodesic Voronoi adjacency (level 1)
  for each pair (a,b): u <- c_a + c_b ; g <- u / sqrt(-K <u,u>_L)   # midpoint on the geodesic
                       a ~ b iff the two nearest codewords to g are exactly a and b
  # cell behaviour + disjointness
  D_a <- sum_{i in S_a} d_i ; E_ab <- sum_{i in S_a, j in S_b} W_ij
  rho_ab <- ln((E_ab + alpha)/(D_a D_b/(2M) + alpha)) ; disjoint(a,b) iff rho_ab <= 0
  # baseline bound
  h1_train <- baseline L1 negative hit rate on the training holdout tails
  tau <- Quantile_{1 - h1_train}({ lambda_ij : C1(i)=C1(j), i<j })
  # potential
  Ndisc <- #{ (a,b) : a ~ b, disjoint(a,b) }
  Dsame <- #{ (i,j) : C1(i)=C1(j), lambda_ij <= 0 }
  Phi <- (Ndisc, Dsame) compared lexicographically
  # greedy exchange, one pass, deterministic
  used <- {} ; for (a,b) with a ~ b and disjoint(a,b), ordered by ascending E_ab:
     for i in S_a, j in S_b, ordered by descending m_L:
        m_i <- dL(x_i,c_b) - dL(x_i,c_a) ; m_j <- dL(x_j,c_a) - dL(x_j,c_b)
        if lambda_{i,b} - sqrt(K)*max(0,m_i) > tau and lambda_{j,a} - sqrt(K)*max(0,m_j) > tau
           and i,j not in used:
              tentatively set C1(i)=b, C1(j)=a
              re-quantize i: r <- z_i - c_b ; s_i2 <- argmin ||r - c_{2,k}|| ; r <- r - c_{2,s_i2} ; s_i3 <- argmin ||r - c_{3,k}||
              re-quantize j analogously into cell a
              recompute rho for every cell pair incident to a or b
              if Phi_after <_lex Phi_before: commit ; used <- used U {i,j}
              else: roll back
  # output: SID file with unmoved rows identical to baseline; Stage 3 unchanged
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the frozen Stage-2 encoder latents z_i for 24556 items, the frozen three-level codebooks, the baseline SID table, and the training sequences D_train; no validation or test data. Outputs are a repaired level-1 token for each exchanged item, re-quantized level-2 and level-3 tokens for those same items, a byte-identical SID for every unmoved item, and a JSON diagnostic record containing h1_train, tau, K, delta_hat, the number of exchanges, Ndisc and Dsame before and after, and the unchanged level-1 cell-size histogram.
SAFEGUARDS: Clip the Lorentz inner product to at most -1/K - 1e-6 before arcosh and clip the arcosh argument to at least 1 + 1e-7; Lorentz-normalise midpoints with an epsilon on the norm and fall back to the Euclidean midpoint if the norm is degenerate; cap dL at 40 to avoid cosh overflow on far items; if M = 0 or fewer than 10 training transitions exist set alpha = 1 and skip the repair; if delta_hat is 0 use 0.5 hop as the floor so K stays finite; if h1_train = 0 set tau to the maximum observed same-cell lambda (strictest) and if h1_train = 1 set tau to the minimum (no-op); enforce that each item appears in at most one exchange so the permutation is an involution and no chains form; recompute Phi from scratch for the affected pairs and assert monotone non-increase, aborting the pass on any violation; never modify a codeword, never modify the encoder, and write a checksum proving that all unmoved rows and the level-1 cardinality vector equal baseline.
