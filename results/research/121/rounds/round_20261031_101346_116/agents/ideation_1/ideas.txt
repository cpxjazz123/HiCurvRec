IDEA: GROMOVSWAP-L1-RQ
TITLE: Gromov-certified, curvature-gated, occupancy-preserving level-1 repair swaps on an otherwise unchanged Euclidean RQ-VAE SID
MOTIVATION: The measured failure is not that the SID is coarse; it is that behaviour neighbours are split across level-1 cells, so the true next item sits under a prefix the generator cannot reach from the context prefix (high validation L1 negative-hit rate). Four earlier rounds tried to fix this by rebuilding prefixes with curved assignment, curved centroids or curvature-controlled levels, and all failed. This idea stops rebuilding. It keeps the baseline Euclidean quantizer, keeps the level-1 cell structure, and only permits a bounded set of size-preserving swaps that are certified by a curved test and are provably non-increasing in the split-neighbour count.
NOVELTY: Hyperbolicity here is neither the assignment metric, nor a codebook-centroid update, nor a per-level curvature schedule, nor a cone. It is a CERTIFICATE FUNCTION: the four-point (Gromov) deficit measured on the training behaviour embeddings yields delta_hat, and a swap is eligible only when both items lie inside the delta_hat-thin triangle spanned by the two cell Frechet centroids. The behavioural failure count itself calibrates the pair bound (theta = logit of the measured L1 negative-hit rate), so no constant is tuned. The repair is a swap, so cell occupancy and codebook usage are conserved exactly, and the accept test is monotone (strictly decreasing) in the number of split behaviour-neighbour pairs.
CHANGE: At the end of every Stage-2 epoch, after the usual Euclidean residual argmin, a guard recomputes one hyperbolic Frechet centroid per level-1 cell, picks for each split item a destination cell from its behaviour log-ratio candidates by hyperbolic nearest centroid, pairs it with the least-compatible member of that cell, and exchanges the two level-1 codes only if the Gromov certificate holds and the swap strictly reduces split behaviour-neighbour pairs without creating new disjoint same-cell pairs. Nothing else about the quantizer, the encoder, the decoder or levels 2..L changes.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed curvature c=1 Poincare ball. One-time from the Stage-2 train split: directed transition counts, popularity-adjusted PMI behaviour vectors u_i, hyperbolic behaviour embeddings b_i = tanh(||u_i||/tau) u_i/||u_i|| with tau = median ||u_i||, symmetric pair log-ratio A_ij, and delta_hat = median four-point deficit over 1e5 seeded quadruples. Guard off for the first 300 epochs (10% of the frozen 3000), at which point p_neg = train-split level-1 negative-hit rate of that pre-guard partition and theta = log((1-p_neg)/p_neg) are frozen for the rest of the run. From then on, once per epoch: recompute Karcher means m_c (5 iterations), for each split item i take C_i = {code1(j) : A_ij >= theta, j in Nbr(i)}, c* = argmin_{c in C_i} d_H(b_i,m_c), partner k = argmax_{k in cell(c*)} [G(k,a) - G(k,c*)], certify e_i and e_k against delta_hat, compute exact dS and dD over the union of the two cells' neighbour lists, and swap only if dS < 0 and dD <= 0. Swapped items are pinned at level 1 for the next epoch and then released. Everything is deterministic given a seed.
COST: One-time precompute: sparse counts/PMI over 24556 items with at most 128 stored neighbours each (~3.1e6 entries) plus delta_hat over 1e5 quadruples (6e5 Poincare distances) = under 2 minutes of CPU, once. Per epoch: 256 Karcher means x 5 iterations x ~96 members (~8e7 flops) plus a guard pass of |V| <= 24556 items, each doing <=128 neighbour counts and ~96 cell-mate log-ratio lookups for dS/dD (~4.7e6 sparse lookups), vectorised to roughly 0.15-0.5 s per epoch on CPU/GPU, i.e. 8-25 minutes extra across 3000 epochs, +3-8% wall clock on four cards. Amortised per training step at 24 steps per epoch this is 6-20 ms, under 2% of a step. No new trainable parameters, no extra backward pass, no change to the 24556-item codebook update cost.
HYPOTHESIS: The idea is worth keeping only if the level-1 prefix is the bottleneck: then the certified swaps must lower the validation L1 negative-hit rate below the frozen train p_neg and raise the predictability of the true next SID's first token, which should show up as Stage3 test_recall@10 above 0.065. Observation: per-epoch S (split pair count) must be non-increasing by construction, the accepted-swap count must fall to near zero as training converges, and the validation L1 negative-hit rate must move in the same direction as recall.
RISKS: (a) The certificate or the dS<0 rule may certify almost nothing, making the mechanism inert and reproducing the baseline numbers; (b) swaps may damage per-item reconstruction so much that the encoder and the level-1 codebook degrade, hurting prefix quality; (c) p_neg measured at epoch 300 may be dominated by warm-up noise, making theta too lenient and dD almost vacuous; (d) the recall bottleneck may be at levels 2-3 rather than level 1, in which case a level-1-only repair cannot help; (e) one seed only, so a small positive or negative effect is inside seed noise.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed transition PMI between items (the behaviour graph), together with the split-neighbour count S and the disjoint same-cell count D derived from it.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the Stage-2 train sequences: n_ij = count of (item i immediately or within the session window followed by item j), n_i = sum_j n_ij, q_j = sum_i n_ij / Z; u_i[j] = clip(log((n_ij/n_i + 1e-8)/(q_j + 1e-8)), -10, 10), truncated to the 128 largest entries; A_ij = log((n_ij+n_ji+1e-8) Z / ((n_i+n_j) q_i q_j + 1e-8)) precomputed for stored pairs. S and D are recomputed from these lists on the current assignment. p_neg and theta are computed once from the same train transitions at epoch 300 and then frozen.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Every statistic entering the guard (u_i, b_i, A_ij, tau, delta_hat, p_neg, theta, m_c, S, D) is computed from the Stage-2 training split only. The guard's own baseline is the pre-guard partition of the same run at epoch 300, which was produced without any validation or test signal. Validation and test interactions are never read during SID construction and never enter a centroid, a threshold or an accept decision; they are only used to report the L1 negative-hit rate and Stage3 recall as diagnostics.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of fixed negative curvature c=1: d_H(x,y) = arcosh(1 + 2||x-y||^2/((1-||x||^2)(1-||y||^2))), the origin exp/log maps, the Karcher/Frechet mean, and the Gromov four-point deficit delta_hat with the triangle excess e^H = 0.5(d_H(x,y)+d_H(x,z)-d_H(y,z)).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the post-assignment repair step of the quantizer (once per epoch, level 1 only) and in the construction of the behaviour embeddings; never inside the residual argmin, so the Euclidean residual quantizer and levels 2..L are bit-identical to the baseline given the same seed except where a swap was accepted.
COUPLING PATH: p_neg (behaviour, measured) -> theta -> candidate cell set C_i (behaviour log-ratio) -> destination chosen by curved nearest Frechet centroid d_H(b_i,m_c) (geometry) -> partner chosen by behaviour gain G (behaviour) -> Gromov thin-triangle certificate e^H <= delta_hat (geometry) -> monotone accept on dS, dD (behaviour) -> level-1 code exchange -> straight-through gradient into the encoder and the level-1 codebook -> SID tuple -> Stage 3. Removing behaviour empties C_i and the dS/dD test, leaving a pure curved reassignment; removing curvature makes the destination, the centroid and the certificate Euclidean. Both change which items move and where, hence the SID and the gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: c* = argmin_{c in C_i(theta)} d_H(b_i, m_c) with C_i(theta) = { code1(j) : A_ij >= theta, j in Nbr(i) }, theta = log((1-p_neg)/p_neg); accept swap(i,k) iff e^H(b_i; m_a, m_c*) <= delta_hat AND e^H(b_k; m_c*, m_a) <= delta_hat AND dS = S(swap) - S(now) < 0 AND dD = D(swap) - D(now) <= 0, where D counts same-cell pairs with A_ij < theta.
OBSERVABLES: Per epoch: S (split level-1 behaviour-neighbour pairs), D (disjoint same-cell pairs), accepted swaps, cumulative fraction of items whose level-1 code differs from the epoch-300 pre-guard partition, mean reconstruction of swapped items versus non-swapped. Post-training: validation L1 negative-hit rate (the diagnostic that decides whether this is the same failure again), top-10 predictability of the true next SID's first token given the context prefix, and Stage3 test_recall@10 against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: use_behaviour_guard = 0, use_hyperbolic = 1: C_i is every cell, theta and A_ij are unused, the dS/dD accept test is bypassed, and the destination/partner are chosen by the hyperbolic centroid geometry alone. This is a pure curved re-partition and is exactly the falsified path; it isolates whether the behaviour accounting is doing the work. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: use_behaviour_guard = 1, use_hyperbolic = 0: d_H replaced by Euclidean distance, Karcher mean replaced by the arithmetic mean, and delta_hat recomputed as the Euclidean four-point deficit with the identical median-of-quadruples statistic, so the certificate stays self-calibrated and the control still fires. It isolates whether curvature changes the certified set, the centroids and the destination choice. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: use_behaviour_guard = 1, use_hyperbolic = 1, the mechanism exactly as specified, trained once for 3000 Stage-2 epochs on four cards followed by the frozen 150-epoch Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping.
SPEC EQUATIONS:
# ---- once, train split only ----
n_ij = directed transition counts from Stage-2 train sequences, keep top-128 per row
q_j  = sum_i n_ij / Z ;  n_i = sum_j n_ij
u_i[j] = clip( log( (n_ij/n_i + 1e-8) / (q_j + 1e-8) ), -10, 10 )
A_ij = log( (n_ij+n_ji+1e-8)*Z / ((n_i+n_j)*q_i*q_j + 1e-8) )
tau  = median_i ||u_i||_2
b_i  = tanh(||u_i||_2/tau) * u_i / max(||u_i||_2, 1e-12)        # b_i in B^64, c = 1
delta_hat = median over Q = 1e5 seeded quadruples (a,b,c,d) of
            0.5 * ( S2 - S1 ), S1 >= S2 >= S3 the sorted sums
            S1 = d_H(a,b)+d_H(c,d), S2 = d_H(a,c)+d_H(b,d), S3 = d_H(a,d)+d_H(b,c)
# ---- per Stage-2 epoch e ----
codes1 = Euclidean residual argmin, levels 1..L (unchanged baseline quantizer)
if e < 300: continue                                   # warm-up, guard off
if e == 300:
    p_neg = #{train transitions (i->j) : codes1[i] != codes1[j]} / #transitions
    theta = log( (1 - clip(p_neg,1e-6,1-1e-6)) / clip(p_neg,1e-6,1-1e-6) )
    S = #{train pairs (i,j) : codes1[i] != codes1[j]} ; D = #same-cell pairs with A_ij < theta
    freeze theta, S, D
m_c = KarcherMean({b_i : codes1[i]=c}) for the 256 level-1 cells, 5 iterations
for i in seeded order of V = {i : i has a neighbour j with codes1[j] != codes1[i]}:
    if i already swapped this epoch: continue
    a = codes1[i]
    C_i = { codes1[j] : j in Nbr(i), A_ij >= theta, codes1[j] != a }
    if behaviour_on and C_i is empty: continue
    cand  = C_i if behaviour_on else all_level1_cells
    c_star = argmin_{c in cand} d_H(b_i, m_c)
    if behaviour_on:
        k = argmax_{k in cell(c_star)} [ G(k,a) - G(k,c_star) ], G(k,c) = mean_{j in cell(c)} A_kj
    else:
        k = argmax_{k in cell(c_star)} d_H(b_k, m_c_star)
    if use_hyperbolic:
        e_i = 0.5*(d_H(b_i,m_a) + d_H(b_i,m_c_star) - d_H(m_a,m_c_star))
        e_k = 0.5*(d_H(b_k,m_c_star) + d_H(b_k,m_a) - d_H(m_c_star,m_a))
        delta = delta_hat
    else:
        e_i, e_k computed with ||.|| instead of d_H, delta = delta_hat_Euclidean
    if not (e_i <= delta and e_k <= delta): continue
    dS = S_after(i,k) - S ; dD = D_after(i,k) - D            # exact, two-cell neighbourhood only
    if behaviour_on and not (dS < 0 and dD <= 0): continue
    swap codes1[i] and codes1[k]; S += dS; D += dD; pin both at level 1 for epoch e+1
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: Stage-2 train interaction sequences; the current level-1..L code assignment, encoder latents and level-1 codebook vectors of the RQ-VAE; the precomputed behaviour embeddings b_i, pair log-ratios A_ij, tau, delta_hat and the frozen theta. Outputs: a repaired level-1 code assignment (a swap list, at most one swap per item per epoch), the pinned assignments for the next epoch, and the logged values of S, D, accepted swaps, changed-SID fraction and reconstruction of swapped items. No new tensor leaves the module.
SAFEGUARDS: Poincare radii clamped to 1-1e-5; the arcosh argument clamped to >= 1+1e-9; the log map norm at the origin regularised to 1e-12; Karcher iteration step capped at (1-||m||)/2 with fallback to the member minimising the summed distance if the iterate leaves the ball; cells with one member use that member as centroid and empty cells are skipped; theta clipped via p_neg in [1e-6, 1-1e-6]; A_ij, G and u_i clipped to [-10,10]; delta_hat computed from a fixed seeded quadruple sample and frozen; a swap that would raise the epoch-mean reconstruction loss above the epoch median per-item reconstruction error is reverted (measured bound, no tuned constant); cells are never resized and splits are never merged, so codebook usage cannot collapse; the whole guard is a no-op when e < 300 and when |V| = 0.
END IDEA
