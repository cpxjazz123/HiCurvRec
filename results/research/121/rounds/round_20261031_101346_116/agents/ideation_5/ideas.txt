IDEA: TAUSWAP-POINCARE-EXCHANGE
TITLE: Cardinality-preserving Poincare-selected level-1 prefix exchange, admitted only by a measured negative-hit budget
MOTIVATION: The measured failure is not a bad codebook, it is a small set of level-1 prefix cells that hold items whose training behaviour is disjoint; every predicted-prefix collision with a behaviourally unrelated item is a miss the tail tokens cannot repair. Earlier rounds attacked this by re-quantising (new prefixes, Frechet codebook updates, per-level curvature control) and were falsified. This keeps the partition, codebooks, encoder and decoder intact and repairs only the empirical collision set, using curved behaviour geometry to choose which items trade cells.
NOVELTY: The curved operation never touches the quantizer, the residual levels or the training loss; it acts only inside a post-Stage-2 exchange operator. That exchange is (a) cardinality preserving, so the prefix vocabulary, prefix frequencies and cell-size profile of the baseline are exactly unchanged, (b) capped at one move per item, and (c) admitted by exact integer counts on both sides of the partition (disjoint same-cell pairs and split behaviour-neighbour pairs) whose threshold is the baseline's measured negative-hit rate, not a swept constant. Prior candidates changed the assignment rule itself; this changes who sits in which cell, for a measured few items, and nothing else.
CHANGE: After the last Stage-2 epoch, build behaviour points u_i in the unit Poincare ball from PMI-compressed transition profiles, compute each frozen level-1 cell's hyperbolic Frechet mean, then greedily execute 1-for-1 cell exchanges whose behaviour-weighted Poincare gain is positive and whose exact Delta-D / Delta-R counts satisfy the admissibility predicate; only the tail tokens of moved items are re-derived, with the frozen level-2/3 codebooks.
SPECIFICATION: Inputs: frozen RQ-VAE codebooks (levels 1..3), frozen per-item SIDs, training-split directed transition counts C with internal-holdout edges deleted, training-user internal holdout pairs. Steps: (1) r(i,j)=log((C_ij+1)/(T p_i q_j +1)); (2) h0 = measured internal negative-hit rate, tau = h0-quantile of r over all same-level-1 pairs; (3) z_i = 32-dim truncated SVD coordinates of the non-negative PMI row; (4) u_i = tanh(c||z_i||) z_i/||z_i||, c = 1/median||z||; (5) m_b = Karcher mean of cell b; (6) enumerate candidate swaps i in a, k in b where b contains a behaviour neighbour of i; (7) greedy accept in decreasing G under the admissibility predicate and the |S_neg| cap; (8) re-encode tails of moved items; (9) emit SIDs and diagnostics. Output: patched SID table with identical prefix vocabulary and identical cell-size multiset, plus h1, the moved list, and Delta-D / Delta-R ledgers.
COST: Zero added cost in all 72,000 Stage-2 steps (24 steps/epoch x 3000 epochs) and no change to the frozen 150-epoch Stage-3 protocol. One-time repair after epoch 3000: randomized truncated SVD of 24556 x 24556 with about 5e5 nonzeros (dominant term, 1-3 min on one card), 256 Karcher means x 15 iterations x ~96 members x 32 dims = 1.2e7 FLOPs, candidate gains about 5e7 FLOPs, gate counting about 1e7 integer ops, about 3 MB dense plus the sparse graph. Total under 5 minutes, roughly the wall clock of 5-10 Stage-2 epochs; no per-step recurrence.
HYPOTHESIS: If level-1 collisions between behaviourally disjoint items really cause Stage-3 misses, removing them under a one-for-one exchange with no cell-size change must lower the validation L1 negative-hit rate h1 below the baseline h0_val and lift test_recall@10 above 0.065; the gain should show up as a very small moved set (under 1 percent) with a large drop in disjoint co-location, not as a slightly better ER. If h1 does not fall, the mechanism is the same failure again and the round is recorded negative with its numbers.
RISKS: Near the ball's origin Poincare and Euclidean gains may rank swaps almost identically, making geometry-off indistinguishable and the coupling unproven; the Delta-R half of the predicate may reject every swap and turn the operator into a no-op; the internal-holdout h0 may misestimate validation collision structure, giving a tau that is vacuous or that shreds the behaviour graph; tail re-encoding of moved items can create fresh tail collisions outside the level-1 ledger; PMI is noisy for low-degree items; and moved items no longer sit where the encoder put them, so the decoder becomes inconsistent with the emitted SIDs (harmless at Stage 3, but must be documented).
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transition counts from training-split user histories, converted to a popularity-adjusted pointwise-mutual-information log-ratio r(i,j), plus the training-user internal-holdout prefix-collision rate h0 used only to set the disjointness threshold tau.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Training split only. For each training user history (i_1..i_T) add 1 to C[i_t][i_{t+1}] for every adjacent pair, after deleting each user's last transition (that deleted edge is the internal holdout and never enters C). T = number of retained transitions, p_i = rowsum(C)_i/T, q_j = colsum(C)_j/T, r(i,j) = log((C_ij + 1)/(T p_i q_j + 1)). The internal holdout gives, for each training user u with length at least 2, the pair (i_u, j_u) = (second-to-last, last) item; u is an internal negative hit iff P1(i_u) = P1(j_u) and C[i_u][j_u] = 0; h0 = hits / eligible users; tau = the h0-quantile of { r(i,j) : i != j, P1(i) = P1(j) }.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: All construction inputs come from the training split (transition graph, PMI profiles, SVD, Karcher means) and from the frozen baseline SIDs, which Stage 2 produced from the same split. The scalars tau and h0 are measured on training users' internal holdouts only, and each training user's last transition is deleted from C before anything is measured, so it cannot leak back into the graph. The validation split is read exactly once, after the patched SID table is frozen, to compute the diagnostic h1; that number is recorded and never fed into tau, into the candidate ordering, into the cap, or into any acceptance decision. Test interactions are seen only by the frozen Stage-3 evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of dimension d = 32 with fixed negative curvature kappa = 1 (unit ball, documented default); Poincare distance d_P(u,v) = arctanh(||(-u) + v||) with Mobius addition; exponential and logarithmic maps at the origin and at cell means; Karcher (Frechet) mean of a cell under d_P; the behaviour cloud enters the ball by the radial injection u_i = tanh(c||z_i||) z_i/||z_i||.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Entirely inside the post-Stage-2 exchange operator: curved distance to hyperbolic Frechet cell means forms the swap gain, and Karcher means define cell position. Curvature never enters the RQ-VAE loss, the codebook update, the residual chain, or the Stage-3 model.
COUPLING PATH: Behaviour transitions -> C -> PMI log-ratio r -> two uses in a single decision: (i) the radial behaviour profile z_i -> u_i -> Poincare distance to Karcher cell mean m_b, multiplied by the behaviour weight w(i,b); (ii) the threshold tau -> the exact Delta-D and Delta-R counts that admit or veto that same swap. The product w(i,b) * d_P(u_i,m_b)^2 and the counts are the two halves of one accept/reject decision, so deleting the behaviour term turns the gain into a content-geometry gain and disables the gate, while deleting curvature turns the gain into a Euclidean gain on the same points and makes the means arithmetic. Either deletion changes which swaps are found or whether they are admitted.
COUPLING EQUATION: accept(i,k) = 1[G(i,k) > 0] * 1[DeltaD <= 0] * 1[DeltaR <= -DeltaD], with G(i,k) = w(i,b) * ( d_P(u_i, m_a)^2 - d_P(u_i, m_b)^2 ) + w(k,a) * ( d_P(u_k, m_b)^2 - d_P(u_k, m_a)^2 ), w(i,b) = max(0, rho(i,b) - tau), rho(i,b) = mean over j in cell b of r(i,j), and DeltaD, DeltaR the exact changes in disjoint same-cell pairs and in split behaviour-neighbour pairs caused by exchanging i in cell a with k in cell b.
OBSERVABLES: Moved-item set and its size (under 2|S_neg|, expected well under 1 percent of 24556 items); Delta-D and Delta-R ledger per accepted swap; validation L1 negative-hit rate h1 against h0_val; behaviour-neighbour hop-distance and adjacency diagnostics of the patched table; Stage-3 learning curves; and finally test_recall@10 against the 0.065 bar.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Same code, BEHAVIOUR=false: w(i,b) := 1, tau := minus infinity so no pair counts as disjoint, Delta-D and Delta-R are still computed but the predicate is forced true (both switches stay implemented and unit-tested), and u_i is built from the frozen RQ-VAE encoder output e_i instead of z_i; the operator then performs a purely geometric content-space exchange. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Same code, CURVATURE=false: d_P becomes the Euclidean norm in ambient coordinates, Karcher means become arithmetic means, and u_i uses z_i without the tanh radial compression; behaviour weighting, tau and the admissibility counts are unchanged. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full operator as specified: behaviour points from PMI-compressed profiles, Poincare distance to hyperbolic Frechet cell means, behaviour weight w(i,b), and the exact Delta-D / Delta-R predicate with tau from h0. This is the single trained and evaluated run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: pseudocode
  build C from training histories with each user's last edge removed; T=sum(C)
  p=rowsum(C)/T; q=colsum(C)/T; r[i,j]=log((C[i,j]+1)/(T*p[i]*q[j]+1))
  P1 = frozen level-1 SID; h0 = internal negative-hit rate; tau = quantile_{h0}(r over same-P1 pairs)
  X[i,:] = max(0, r[i,j]) for j with r[i,j] >= tau or r[j,i] >= tau
  Z = randomized_truncated_SVD(X, 32); c = 1/median_i ||Z[i,:]||
  u[i] = tanh(c*||Z[i,:]||) * Z[i,:]/||Z[i,:]||
  for each cell b: m[b] = KarcherMean({u[j]: j in b}, iters=15)
  for i, for b in {P1[j] : r[i,j] >= tau} \ {P1[i]}, for k in b:
      a = P1[i]; rho_ib = mean_{j in b, j != k} r[i,j]; rho_ka = mean_{j in a, j != i} r[k,j]
      G = max(0,rho_ib-tau)*(dP(u[i],m[a])^2-dP(u[i],m[b])^2)
        + max(0,rho_ka-tau)*(dP(u[k],m[b])^2-dP(u[k],m[a])^2)
  sort candidates by G desc; count = 0
  for (i,k,G) in order:
      if G <= 0 or moved[i] or moved[k] or count >= |S_neg|: continue
      dD = sum_{j in b\{k}} ([r[i,j]<tau]-[r[k,j]<tau]) + sum_{j in a\{i}} ([r[k,j]<tau]-[r[i,j]<tau])
      dR = sum_{j in b\{k}} ([r[i,j]>=tau]-[r[k,j]>=tau]) + sum_{j in a\{i}} ([r[k,j]>=tau]-[r[i,j]>=tau])
      if dD <= 0 and dR <= -dD: swap P1[i],P1[k]; moved[i]=moved[k]=True; count += 1
  for i in moved: re-encode c2,c3 with frozen level-2/3 codebooks on the new residual
  assert multiset(cell sizes) unchanged; assert set(P1) unchanged; assert count <= |S_neg|
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Takes the frozen per-item three-level SIDs, the frozen codebooks, the training-split directed transition counts C with internal-holdout edges deleted, the training-user internal-holdout pairs, and the precomputed scalars h0 and h0_val. Returns a patched SID table (level-1 assignments exchanged for at most 2|S_neg| items, tails re-derived for exactly those items), the accepted-swap ledger with G, rho, Delta-D and Delta-R per swap, the measured validation diagnostic h1, and the moved-item index list.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp every Poincare norm to 1 - 1e-7 and compute d_P through arctanh of a quantity clipped to [0, 1-1e-7]; if a Karcher step leaves the ball or fails to reduce Frechet variance, fall back to the best member and then to the origin; use alpha = 1 in the PMI denominator (documented default) so no log or division is undefined, and never evaluate r outside the candidate graph; if the h0-quantile is degenerate (h0 = 0 or 1), set tau to the minimum observed r plus a documented epsilon, record the degenerate flag, and let the gate decide; use exact int64 counting for Delta-D and Delta-R; never re-move an item and never change a cell size, with assertions on both; cap accepted swaps at |S_neg| and at N/2, both structural rather than tuned; freeze the Karcher means during the greedy loop so each gate check costs O(cell size); if zero swaps are admissible, emit the baseline SID table unchanged and record count = 0 as the result; if h1 is not below h0_val, record the round as negative with h0, h1, count, Delta-D, Delta-R and test_recall@10, without retuning tau or the predicate.
END IDEA
