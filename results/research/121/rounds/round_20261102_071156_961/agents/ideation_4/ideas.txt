IDEA: CURVCHART-RESGUARD
TITLE: Per-codeword Poincare chart curvature, spent on the assignment metric with the codebook radius left untouched
MOTIVATION: The previous round proved that the standardised training-graph curvature profile does track which SID tables turn out well (Spearman -0.20 over 115 families) but that spending it on the per-level code radius destroys the codebook: kappa_hat [0.00,-8.96,-25.55] -> delta [0.702,0.298,-1.000] -> radius [0.0381,0.0312,0.0163] gave 2/13/56 SIDs, collision 0.998, L1 hit rates 0.982/0.980 and Stage3 test_recall@10 0.041888 against the inherited 0.0446 and the frozen baseline 0.055537 (247/7414/20223). The problem this idea addresses is therefore not "how do we use curvature" but "how do we let curvature reshape which items a codeword accepts, while the codebook keeps every bit of the resolution it had". The mechanism gives every codeword its own local curvature chart, derived from the behaviour of the items that codeword actually serves, and admits residuals through that chart's metric; the codeword vectors, their norms, their count, and their update rule are copied byte-identically from the inherited baseline.
NOVELTY: Different from CURVALLOC-LORENTZ-RQ (which spent curvature on c_l and rho_l, i.e. on the space's radius), from BRad-RQ (which moved items radially using behaviour-ranking), from CURVCTRL-SID (which spent a prefix-transition score on a level learning rate), from poincare-frechet-successor-codebook (curved codebook updates), from LORENTZ-SUCCPROFILE-SMOOTH-RQ / behcone-prefix-rq (conical prefix constraints) and from the Euclidean RQ it inherits. Here curvature is a CODE-LOCAL field living only inside the assignment score, the level-global profile is deliberately made inert (a level-uniform gain cancels in the argmin, which is why round 1 could only express it by moving the radius), and the effect on the partition is bounded by a derived guard band so that the mechanism is provably a bounded perturbation of the baseline Voronoi diagram rather than a rescaling of it. Resolution is protected structurally, not empirically: nothing that determines code scale, code count or code lifetime is a function of curvature.
CHANGE: Each RQ level's assignment score replaces the Euclidean residual norm by a per-codeword Poincare chart distance q_e(r) = artanh(g_e * tanh(1) * ||r-e|| / rho_l), where g_e = exp(-s_e/2) and s_e is the null-standardised mean training-graph Ollivier-Ricci curvature of the items that codeword e covered in the previous epoch; each q_e is then clipped to a [0.5, 2] band around its neutral (g_e = 1) value. Codewords, their radii, their number and their EMA update stay exactly as in the inherited baseline.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Offline, training split only, gradient-free: build the directed item transition graph from consecutive in-session pairs of the training sequences, counts n_ij; node measure m_i = 0.5*delta_i + 0.5*p_i with p_i(j) = n_ij / sum_j n_ij; kappa_OR(i,j) = 1 - W1(m_i, m_j)/dist(i,j) with W1 by Sinkhorn (entropic eps = 1/(2*max_out_degree), 50 iterations), dist the unweighted graph hop distance; kappa_i = mean over out-edges of i. Store as a frozen 24556-vector K. (2) At the end of every epoch (with the previous epoch's hard assignments A_e per level), compute s_e_raw = mean_{i in A_e} K_i and s_e = clip( (s_e_raw - mu_null)/sigma_null, -3, +3 ) where mu_null/sigma_null come from R=8 member shuffles that permute items among codewords preserving each |A_e| exactly (empty codes excluded, they keep s_e = 0). (3) rho_l = RMS of ||r - e|| over the level's residuals at the end of epoch 0, all_reduced over ranks, frozen thereafter. c0_l = tanh(1)^2 / rho_l^2, c_e = c0_l * exp(-s_e). (4) Per step: x = sqrt(c_e)*||r-e||, q_e = artanh(min(x, 1-1e-6)), q_e^0 = artanh(min(tanh(1)*||r-e||/rho_l, 1-1e-6)), q_e <- clip(q_e, 0.5*q_e^0, 2*q_e^0); softmax over -q_e/tau with the baseline's tau unchanged.
COST: One-time offline pass: 24556 nodes, ~1e6 directed edges, Sinkhorn W1 per edge on CPU, roughly 5-15 minutes wall clock, executed independently on every rank (or read from an identical precomputed file on every rank) so there is no rank-0 broadcast. Per-step cost at the frozen scale: 3 levels x (batch x 256) artanh plus a clip and a min/max, i.e. about 786k artanh evaluations per step at batch 1024, roughly 0.2-0.5 ms of GPU time, under 2% of step wall clock over 24 steps/epoch x 3000 epochs = 72000 steps (<25 s total). Per-epoch statistics: one all_reduce of 3x256 floats plus 8 shuffles over 24556 items, about 1 ms per epoch, 3000 epochs -> a few seconds. Memory: one 256-float vector per level, plus a 24556-float curvature vector.
HYPOTHESIS: The mechanism is worth keeping if the code-local curvature field is anti-correlated with successor predictability in the way the graph suggests: codes sitting in tree-like (negative Ollivier) transition regions are exactly the codes whose current Euclidean cells mix several incompatible successor distributions, and giving them a slightly more permissive (concave, larger effective radius at fixed boundary) chart while tightening clique-like codes raises per-code successor purity, which raises next-token predictability of the SID prefix, which Stage 3 converts into Recall@10 above 0.065. Observation: the fraction of items whose argmin changes versus the neutral chart should be non-trivial (expected a few percent), per-code successor entropy should fall on the codes with large |s_e|, the level cardinalities should stay within 0.9-1.1x of 247/7414/20223, and the L1 hit rate should move away from the uninformative 0.982/0.980 of the failed round. If instead fewer than 0.5% of assignments change, the guard band has made the mechanism a no-op and the hypothesis is disconfirmed by the single run itself.
RISKS: s_e is noisy for small cells even after standardisation, so the field may be dominated by high-count codes and act as a near-constant; artanh saturation can make a code reject everything beyond a short radius, which the guard band contains but which can still waste that code's capacity; the lagged EMA creates a feedback loop (assignment -> s_e -> assignment) that may oscillate, mitigated only by the per-epoch update rate and the clip; a 2x bounded gain may simply be too weak to matter at the partition level; and even a cleaner partition may not convert into recall if the Stage 3 transformer already memorises prefix idiosyncrasies. The mechanism also inherits any bias in the transition graph towards popular items.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: The standardised, code-local mean Ollivier-Ricci curvature s_e of the directed training item-transition graph restricted to the items each codeword served in the previous epoch; equivalently a per-code measure of how tree-like (branching, negative curvature) versus clique-like (redundant, positive curvature) the successor structure of that code's cell is.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the TRAINING split only: for each training user sequence, consecutive (i_t, i_t+1) pairs increment n_ij; the item graph has the 24556 training items as nodes and the observed directed pairs as edges; the lazy random-walk measure m_i = 0.5 delta_i + 0.5 p_i is compared to m_j by entropic Wasserstein-1 with eps = 1/(2 max_out_degree), and kappa_OR(i,j) = 1 - W1/dist(i,j). Item curvature kappa_i is the out-edge mean; code curvature is the mean over the code's assigned items, taken from the previous epoch's assignments, standardised against R = 8 size-preserving member shuffles and clipped to +-3. Nothing in this path is differentiable and nothing in it is updated by backpropagation.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The graph, the measures and kappa_i are built exclusively from training-split sequences; the validation and test interactions are never read, never touched during encoding, and never used to fit the transition graph or the per-code means. The standardisation null is a member shuffle of TRAINING items among codes, so it carries no split information either. rho_l is measured from the run's own epoch-0 residuals on training batches. Because the field enters only as a frozen schedule with no gradient, no validation or test signal can propagate into the SID construction, and the per-code means are recomputed from training assignments only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: The Poincare ball distance from the chart origin to the residual, d_P(0,u) = 2 artanh(sqrt(c) ||u||) with trainable-but-frozen negative curvature magnitude c = c_e per codeword, emitted as the dimensionless score q_e = artanh(sqrt(c_e)||r-e||); the per-code curvature is anchored at c0_l = tanh(1)^2/rho_l^2 so that a residual of the measured scale rho_l maps to exactly 1.0 in the neutral chart.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE's assignment step at every level, in the distance that scores residual r against codeword e before the softmax and before the commitment / reconstruction losses; it never enters the codeword update, the codebook radius, the commitment weight, the encoder, or the decoder.
COUPLING PATH: kappa_OR (training graph) -> per-code mean over the previous epoch's cell A_e -> null-standardised s_e (gradient-free) -> gain g_e = exp(-s_e/2) -> curvature c_e = c0_l g_e^2 -> Poincare distance q_e = artanh(sqrt(c_e)||r-e||) -> guard-banded assignment score -> argmin/softmax -> SID tokens. Removing the behaviour term sets s_e = 0 and therefore g_e = 1 for every code, which makes q_e = artanh(tanh(1)||r-e||/rho_l) a single common strictly increasing map of ||r-e||, so the argmin collapses exactly onto the baseline Euclidean assignment; removing the geometry replaces artanh(...) by the identity on ||r-e||/rho_l, so c_e is computed but no longer enters any computation. Both removals change the core computation, and neither touches a radius.
COUPLING EQUATION: q_e(r) = artanh( clip( tanh(1) * exp(-s_e/2) * ||r - e|| / rho_l , 0 , 1-1e-6 ) ), with s_e = clip( ( mean_{i in A_e} kappa_OR(i) - mu_null ) / sigma_null , -3, +3 ) and the guard q_e <- clip( q_e , 0.5 q_e^0 , 2 q_e^0 ), q_e^0 = artanh(tanh(1)||r-e||/rho_l); the behaviour term is exp(-s_e/2), the curved term is artanh.
OBSERVABLES: (a) SID assignment: fraction of items whose hard argmin changes versus the neutral chart, per-level code cardinality, and the deviation of those cardinalities from 247/7414/20223 (must stay inside 0.9-1.1x, this is the resolution certificate); (b) gradient/parameter update: the per-code assignment mass and the codeword EMA trajectories, which must shift only through the changed assignment counts, never through a curvature term in the loss; (c) prefix behaviour predictability: per-code successor-distribution entropy, the level-1 positive/negative hit rates (must move off 0.982/0.980), and the training-split bigram log-likelihood of the SID prefixes; (d) Stage3 test_recall@10, the only judgement, target above 0.065 and in no case below the frozen baseline 0.055537.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEHAVIOUR_OFF=1 sets s_e = 0 for all codes and all levels (kappa is still measured and logged), so every gain is 1 and the assignment reduces provably to the Euclidean argmin; this is the correct switch and it must compile and train, but this round runs no arm with it set.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: GEOMETRY_OFF=1 replaces q_e by the Euclidean ratio ||r - e||/rho_l in the softmax while s_e is still computed and written to the log, so the curvature field is present but the curved operator is absent; no arm is trained with it set.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEHAVIOUR_OFF=0 and GEOMETRY_OFF=0, the only configuration trained this round: the full per-code chart field from the null-standardised training-graph curvature inside the artanh assignment score, with the guard band active.
SPEC EQUATIONS: K = ollivier(training_transition_graph)                         # frozen, no_grad
for epoch in 1..3000:
    s_raw[e] = mean_{i in A_e^{epoch-1}} K[i]                                # per level, A_e from last epoch
    mu, sd   = null_stats(s_raw, R=8, preserve=|A_e|)                        # member shuffle
    s[e]     = clip((s_raw[e]-mu)/sd, -3, 3)
    g[e]     = exp(-s[e]/2)
    c[e]     = tanh(1)^2 / rho_l^2 * g[e]^2
    for step in 1..24:                                                        # per batch, per level
        d2   = ||r - e||^2                                                    # B x 256
        x    = sqrt(c[e]) * sqrt(d2)
        q0   = artanh(clip(tanh(1)*sqrt(d2)/rho_l, 0, 1-1e-6))
        q    = artanh(clip(x, 0, 1-1e-6))
        q    = clip(q, 0.5*q0, 2*q0)
        p    = softmax(-q / tau_baseline, dim=codes)
        k    = argmin_e q                                                      # SID token
    codeword update, commitment loss, reconstruction loss unchanged from baseline
rho_l = all_reduce_sum(||r-e||^2) / all_reduce_sum(count) at end of epoch 0, then frozen.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the residual matrix r (B x D) of a level, the codeword matrix E (K x D) unchanged from the baseline, the frozen per-item curvature vector K (24556), the previous epoch's assignment counts, rho_l (3 floats), tau_baseline. Outputs: the per-code curvature vector c_e (K), the guarded score matrix q (B x K), the soft assignment p (B x K) used by the existing losses, and the hard code index k that becomes the SID token; plus the logged s_e and the cardinality certificate.
SAFEGUARDS: (1) artanh argument clamped to [0, 1-1e-6]; artanh(1-1e-6) ~ 7.25 so no infinity. (2) Empty or tiny codes keep s_e = 0 and their c_e = c0_l, so the field cannot kill a code. (3) The [0.5, 2] guard band bounds every code's effective accepting radius, so no codeword can gain or lose more than a factor two of reach; this is the resolution certificate and is checked every epoch against 0.9-1.1x of 247/7414/20223. (4) sqrt inside sqrt(c_e) is floored at 1e-12 because c_e > 0 by construction; rho_l floored at 1e-6. (5) Curvature is computed under no_grad from a frozen tensor, so it cannot receive or emit gradients. (6) Any per-code statistic that needs more than one rank uses all_reduce with every rank calling it, at epoch boundaries only; the curvature tensor is either recomputed identically on each rank or read from the same file on each rank, never broadcast from rank 0, and the SID export is written independently by each rank to its own shard. (7) A dead-code reset from the baseline is retained unchanged and is curvature-independent. (8) NaN guard: if any q is non-finite the step falls back to q0 for that level and logs a counter.
END IDEA
