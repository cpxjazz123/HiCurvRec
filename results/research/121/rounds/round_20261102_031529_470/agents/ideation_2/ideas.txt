IDEA: ORC-RADIAL-RQ
TITLE: Ollivier-Ricci node potential of the training behaviour quotient graph as the Poincare radial coordinate of every RQ code, with per-level curvature set by the standardised curvature defect.
MOTIVATION: The evidence says the raw curvature of a level's behaviour quotient graph is uninformative, the STANDARDISED curvature is informative (L3 +0.26, L1 -0.16, L1-L3 -0.20) and a flatter profile goes with better Stage3 recall, while the strongest non-geometric predictor is how many codes level 3 actually uses (+0.42) and collision rate (-0.39). Existing SIDs use curvature only as a post-hoc diagnostic or a scalar weight. Here the behaviour graph's curvature becomes a COORDINATE of the code embedding, so the hierarchy depth of each code in the curved space is produced by the training behaviour graph rather than by gradients alone. It also addresses Issue #127 (level-1 collapse to 4 codes, recall 0.0513) because the potential is standardised within each level and therefore cannot coarsen the partition: it only redistributes the K codes along the radius.
NOVELTY: Curvature is not a loss, not a temperature, not a successor-Frechet shift and not a per-level scalar copied from a feedback score. Discrete Ollivier-Ricci node potentials of the quotient graph are mapped by tanh onto the Poincare radial coordinate e_k = exp_0^{c_l}((1/sqrt(c_l)) tanh(phi_hat_k) u_k), so the SID codebook is a quantised embedding of the behaviour graph's curvature field. The per-level curvature then follows a conservation law c_l = c0 exp(-D_l) from the standardised defect D_l, so the curved space supplies exactly the curvature the behaviour partition lacks. No earlier arm used per-code curvature potentials or a curvature-conservation law.
CHANGE: Before each refresh, build the training-split item transition graph, contract it into the current level-l prefix groups, compute edge Ollivier curvatures, standardise them against a size-matched shuffle null, and use the resulting per-node potential to place each code at a fixed hyperbolic radius and the resulting level defect to set the level's negative curvature; assignment then uses the per-level Poincare distance to those radially placed codes.
SPECIFICATION: (1) A = symmetric training co-occurrence from consecutive items, counts only. (2) For level l, node p = the level-l prefix; W_pq = sum_{i in p, j in q} A_ij; keep nodes covering 90% of training transition mass, cap 2048 nodes. (3) d = shortest path on Q_l. (4) kappa(p,q) = 1 - W1(m_p, m_q)/d(p,q), P = (I + D^-1 W)/2, W1 by Sinkhorn on d with eps=0.05, 30 iterations. (5) phi_p = sum_q W_pq kappa(p,q) / sum_q W_pq, standardised over used nodes of degree >= 2: phi_hat_p = clip((phi_p - mean)/sd, -3, 3). (6) Null: B=20 permutations of item-to-prefix labels preserving group sizes, recompute kappa_l; D_l = (kappa_l - mean_B)/sd_B. (7) Geometry: c_l = c0 exp(-clip(D_l,-2,2)), R_l = 1/sqrt(c_l), e_{l,k} = exp_0^{c_l}(R_l tanh(phi_hat_k) u_k), u_k unit trainable, unused code k gets rho = median(phi_hat of used codes). (8) Assignment: d_{H^{c_l}}(z_l, e_{l,k}) with z_l the level latent mapped to the ball at scale tanh(R_l ||z_l||)/||z_l||; residual r_{l+1} = z_l - proj onto e_{l,k} in tangent space, so the baseline RQ machinery is untouched. Refresh every 50 epochs (60 refreshes over 3000), radius table and c_l linearly interpolated between refreshes.
COST: Per step almost nothing: no extra forward or backward pass, only a gather from a K x d radius buffer (3 levels x 256 codes), under 0.1 ms per step at 24556 items and 24 steps per epoch, 72000 steps total, so under 10 s over the run. The refresh is the real cost: per refresh and per level about 2048 Dijkstras on a <=2048-node graph plus 2048-edge Sinkhorn with 256-dim supports, times 20 nulls, order 0.1-0.5 s per level; 60 refreshes x 3 levels is about 1-3 minutes CPU, under 1% of a 3000-epoch four-card run.
HYPOTHESIS: The standardised curvature defect is a computable target, so driving it toward zero while redistributing codes radially should raise the used-code count at level 3 and flatten the profile, and that combination should lift Stage3 test recall@10 above 0.065. Observation: (i) mean |D_l| falls across refreshes; (ii) level-3 distinct codes used rises relative to the frozen init-time count; (iii) prefix-transition predictability of the Stage3 model improves; (iv) final test recall@10 > 0.065 and > the 0.0513 of the Issue #127 collapse arm.
RISKS: phi_hat may be dominated by transition mass rather than shape, so the radial layout reproduces popularity in the ball and suppresses level-3 diversity; the standardised defect may oscillate between refreshes and inject noise; a level whose tangent directions all shrink toward the origin loses angular resolution; the 90%-mass cap may exclude the tail prefixes that carry the discriminative level-3 codes; the correlation evidence is observational, so a causal curvature-to-recall link may not exist.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions counted from training sequences (consecutive item pairs), aggregated into the symmetric co-occurrence graph A and contracted into behaviour quotient graphs at each prefix level; two derived quantities, the per-node Ollivier-Ricci potential phi_hat and the per-level standardised curvature defect D_l.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only. Count (i_t, i_{t+1}) pairs over all training sequences, symmetrise, contract each level's prefix groups (prefixes of the current quantizer, recomputed from the training item-to-SID assignment), build the lazy random walk, compute Ollivier curvature per edge with Sinkhorn over a shortest-path ground metric, aggregate per node by transition mass, standardise per level, and compute D_l against B=20 size-preserving label shuffles.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only training-split sequences, only training-item prefix groups, only shuffles within the training item set. No validation or test sequence is loaded at refresh time; items are restricted to those appearing in the training split, and the refresh function has no argument for a validation or test dataloader, so no validation or test interaction can reach the SID construction. Validation is only read by the frozen Stage3 protocol after the SIDs are frozen.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of negative curvature -c_l (c_l = c0 exp(-clip(D_l,-2,2)), c0 the baseline curvature), the exponential map at the origin exp_0^{c_l}, and the closed-form Poincare distance d_{H^{c_l}}, used both to place codes on their curvature-derived radii and to score the assignment.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the quantizer, as the codebook parameterisation (the radius of every code) and in the nearest-code distance of every RQ level, plus in the per-level latent-to-ball map and the tangent-space residual projection. It acts during training and during SID export, so the exported SIDs are the codes of the curved assignment.
COUPLING PATH: training transitions -> quotient graph Q_l -> Ollivier edge curvature -> (a) node potential phi_hat and (b) level defect D_l -> c_l and R_l -> code radii e_{l,k} -> curved distance in the assignment -> codes -> new prefix groups. Removing behaviour sets phi_hat=0 and D_l=0 (uniform shell at R_0, c_l=c0); removing geometry replaces exp_0, the tanh radius and d_H by a linear radius and L2 distance. Either removal changes the assignment vector and hence the SID.
COUPLING EQUATION: e_{l,k} = exp_0^{c_l}((1/sqrt(c_l)) tanh(phi_hat_{l,k}) u_k) with c_l = c0 exp(-D_l) and assignment score s_{l,k}(z) = -d_{H^{c_l}}(z_l, e_{l,k}), where phi_hat_{l,k} and D_l are the behaviour terms and exp_0, tanh and d_{H^{c_l}} are the curved terms.
OBSERVABLES: SID assignment changes (code histogram per level, used-code counts at level 3, collision rate, prefix counts); the standardised curvature profile of the exported SIDs (mean |D_1|, |D_3|, D_1 - D_3) moves toward the flat target; prefix-transition predictability of the Stage3 model; Stage3 test recall@10 against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: phi_hat := 0 and D_l := 0 with the code switches in place, so all codes sit on one uniform shell at R_0 = 1/sqrt(c0) and every level keeps c_l = c0; same curved machinery, no behaviour input.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp_0 replaced by identity, the tanh radius replaced by rho = phi_hat (linear, unbounded, no curvature radius), d_{H^{c_l}} replaced by L2 distance, c_l := 0; the behaviour potentials are still computed and still set the radii, so only the curvature is removed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: phi_hat and D_l from the training behaviour quotient graph, tanh radius inside the ball of curvature c_l and Poincare distance, exactly as specified; this is the single trained arm and no ablation arm is trained in this round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: for each refresh r at epoch 50*r: A = sum_t (1[i_{t+1}=j] + 1[i_t=j]) over training sequences; for l in 1..3: P_l = prefix_l(sid(i)) for training items i; W_pq = sum_{i in p, j in q} A_ij with the node set = prefixes covering 90% of sum(W), capped at 2048; P = (I + D^{-1}W)/2; kappa(p,q) = 1 - Sinkhorn(d, m_p, m_q)/d(p,q), d = BFS/Dijkstra hops, Sinkhorn eps=0.05, 30 iterations, skip edges with d=0 or degree<2; phi_p = weighted mean of kappa over incident edges; phi_hat = clip((phi - mean)/sd, -3, 3); for b in 1..20: permute item labels within fixed prefix sizes, recompute kappa_l^b; D_l = (kappa_l - mean_b)/sd_b; c_l = c0 * exp(-clip(D_l,-2,2)); R_l = 1/sqrt(c_l); e_k = exp_0(rho_k u_k), rho_k = R_l tanh(phi_hat_k), exp_0(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)/(sqrt(c)||v||) * v; the loss is unchanged (RQ-VAE reconstruction plus commitment) except that the code vectors and the distance are the curved ones; interpolate (c_l, rho) linearly across the next 50 epochs.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: inputs are the training sequences (for A), the training item-to-SID assignment, the codebook size K, the baseline curvature c0, the refresh period (50 epochs) and the null count B=20; outputs are, per level, a curvature scalar c_l, a K-vector of radii rho_{l,k} and a used-code mask, consumed by the quantizer's code parameterisation and by the assignment distance. No gradient flows into the curvature computation: it is a buffer.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||v|| at 1e-8 in exp_0 to avoid 0/0; clamp sqrt(c)||v|| below atanh(1-1e-6) so the image stays inside the ball; floor rho at 0.1 R_l so no code sits at the origin where the Poincare gradient vanishes; phi_hat clipped at 3 sd and D_l at 2 sd (null envelope); nodes with degree<2 or edge distance 0 excluded from the potential and from kappa_l; Sinkhorn falls back to the identity coupling if the marginal error exceeds 1e-3 after 30 iterations; unused codes inherit the median radius; radius and curvature interpolated across the refresh interval to avoid discontinuous jumps; if the used-code count at any level falls below the frozen init-time count the radial spread is not widened by hand, the collapse is recorded as a diagnostic rather than patched.
END IDEA
