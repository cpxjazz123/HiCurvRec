IDEA: CURVPROF-TRANSPORT-RQ
TITLE: Depth-monotone behaviour-curvature profile that sets the per-level curvature of a hyperbolic residual transport inside the RQ-VAE quantizer
MOTIVATION: The forensic evidence says the recall is not explained by the raw curvature of the behaviour quotient graph but by its standardised profile: level-3 curvature is positively associated with recall (+0.34 over mechanism families), level-1 negatively (-0.16), and the difference L1 minus L3 is negative (-0.20, p=0.0095). Existing SID quantizers cannot act on that, because the partition's curvature is only ever measured after the fact, and because the strongest non-geometric predictor, level-3 code usage (+0.42), collapses when a quantizer is forced toward a coarser behavioural partition (Issue #127: level 1 fell to 4 codes, recall 0.0513). We need a quantizer whose geometry is driven by the measured curvature profile while keeping item-level resolution.
NOVELTY: Earlier arms here changed codebook positions (successor-weighted Frechet), changed radial placement by behaviour entropy, or ran a per-level curvature controller on hard transition scores. This idea does none of those: code positions, codebook size, radii and entropy are untouched. The behaviour curvature sets only the negative curvature c_l of the Poincare ball in which the Möbius log map is taken at the current prefix point, so curvature changes the direction of the residual and therefore which code the next level sees. Removing the behaviour term or replacing the ball by its flat limit changes the code choice at levels 2 and 3, not just a loss value.
CHANGE: For each prefix level, the training-transition quotient graph's standardised Ollivier curvature is turned into a depth-monotone target profile and then into a per-level curvature magnitude c_l; the RQ residual is the curvature-c_l Möbius logarithm of the item point at its accumulated prefix point, so c_l rotates and rescales the residual before nearest-code selection.
SPECIFICATION: Quotient and curvature are computed as below; the quantizer is a hyperbolic RQ-VAE whose only curvature input is c_l from the profile target; per level the assignment is argmin_k ||r_l - e_{l,k}|| with r_l the transported Möbius log at p_{l-1}; the prefix step is a geodesic step of the chosen code. Provenance of every constant: m comes from the measured spread, the sign from the L1<L3 evidence, the clip bounds are two standardised units, the codebook occupancy floor is K/2, the recomputation cadence is a cost budget stated as 61 computations over 3000 epochs.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: inputs are the training split sequences T, the current SID table (item -> 3 prefixes), and a batch of item latents h in the tangent space at the origin, dimension d=64. Outputs are, every 50 epochs, z*_l and c_l per level; per step, the selected codes k_1,k_2,k_3, the residuals r_l, and the reconstruction/commitment gradients.
SPEC EQUATIONS: (1) Q_l: nodes are distinct length-l prefixes; w_l(p,q)=W_l(p,q)+W_l(q,l p) with W_l(p,q)=#{(u->v) in T : pref_l(u)=p, pref_l(v)=q}; s_l(p)=#{(u->v): pref_l(u)=pref_l(v)=p}; d_l(p)=sum_q w_l(p,q)+2 s_l(p). (2) lazy walk m_p = 0.5*delta_p + 0.5*sum_q (w_l(p,q)/sum_q w_l(p,q)) delta_q. (3) Ollivier kappa_l(p,q)=1 - W1(m_p,m_q)/dist_{Q_l}(p,q). (4) kbar_l = sum_{p<q} w_l(p,q) kappa_l(p,q) / sum_{p<q} w_l(p,q). (5) null: 16 shuffles of the item->prefix label vector preserving group sizes, same computation, giving mu_l, sigma_l; z_l=(kbar_l-mu_l)/max(sigma_l,1e-6). (6) zbar=(z_1+z_2+z_3)/3; m=min(1, |z_1-z_3|/2); z*_l=clip(zbar+(l-2)*m, -2, 2); c_l=exp(-z*_l). (7) ball B_c={x: c||x||^2<1}, lambda_x=2/(1-c||x||^2), a(+)b=((1+2c<a,b>+c||b||^2)a+(1-c||a||^2)b)/(1+2c<a,b>+c^2||a||^2||b||^2). (8) exp_0(v)=tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) v/(sqrt(c)||v||). (9) log_p(x)= (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)||(-p)(+)x||) * ((-p)(+)x)/||(-p)(+)x||. (10) transport to origin PT_{p->0}(v)=(lambda_p/2) v. (11) r_l=(lambda_{p_{l-1}}/2) log^{c_l}_{p_{l-1}}(x), with x=exp^{c_l}_0(h). (12) k_l=argmin_k ||r_l-e_{l,k}||. (13) p_l=exp^{c_l}_{p_{l-1}}((2/lambda_{p_{l-1}}) e_{l,k_l}); p_0=0. (14) L = sum_l d_{c_l}(x, p_l)^2 + ||sg(r_l)-e_{l,k_l}||^2 + 0.25 ||r_l-sg(e_{l,k_l})||^2, d_c(x,y)=(2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)(+)y||). (15) flavour: canonical prefix point maintained in B_1 and mapped into B_{c_l} by x -> sqrt(c_l) x.
COST: Baseline frozen scale is 24556 training items, 24 steps per epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps. Per step the added work is, for batch 1024 and d=64, two Möbius additions, one artanh, one normalisation and one exp map, about 0.5-1 MFLOP, i.e. roughly +5 to +10 percent of a step; codebook and model size unchanged. Curvature is recomputed 61 times (epoch 1, every 50 epochs, final): each recomputation is one bincount pass over the transition edge list (order 10^6 ints, tens of milliseconds) plus entropic W1 on at most 50000 quotient edges with support capped at 64 neighbours, about 1-3 s. Total curvature overhead about 2-4 minutes, under 5 percent of the 3000-epoch wall clock; memory +O(E) ints and 3 scalars.
HYPOTHESIS: If the signed curvature profile is causal rather than a correlate of code usage, then imposing z_3 > z_1 through the ball curvature should make level-3 cells behaviourally tighter while level 1 stays branchy, and should show up as (a) unchanged or larger level-3 used-code count, (b) z_3 - z_1 > 0 measured on the final SID, (c) Stage3 test recall@10 above 0.065 in the single run.
RISKS: The association may be confounded by level-3 code usage, so the mechanism could raise z_3 while recall stays flat; the target could overshoot and saturate distances so that few codes win, which the occupancy guard only catches after the fact; the recomputation lag of 50 epochs may make c_l stale as the partition moves; in the nearly flat regime the transport may be near-identity and the effect may be small; the profile target is derived from a null that is itself noisy at level 3 where many groups are singletons.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Edge-mass-weighted mean Ollivier curvature of the lazy random walk on the training-transition quotient graph induced by each prefix level, standardised against a group-size-preserving member-shuffle null; the resulting three-level profile is converted into the depth-monotone target z*_l.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only: consecutive item pairs in each training sequence give directed counts; prefixes come from the current SID table (which itself is built only on training items); for each level l contract items into prefix groups, build the undirected weighted quotient with within-group mass kept as self-loops, build the 1/2-lazy walk, compute W1 by entropic optimal transport on the neighbour support using shortest-path distances inside Q_l, average kappa by edge mass, and standardise against 16 label shuffles that preserve group sizes; all of this is a function of the training graph and the training-time partition only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition graph is built once from training sequences before any SID training, and the quotient is a contraction of that frozen graph by the current training-time code assignment; no validation or test sequence, no validation/test co-occurrence, and no held-out recall enters the curvature, the target, the clip bounds or the guard thresholds. Validation and test items are never used to set c_l, and the only test quantity read is the final Stage3 test_recall@10.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball with trainable-free, behaviour-derived negative curvature magnitude c_l = exp(-z*_l), clipped to [e^-2, e^2]: Möbius addition, exponential and logarithmic maps, parallel transport to the origin with conformal factor lambda_p, and geodesic distance d_c.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE quantizer's forward pass and its straight-through gradient path, at every level l in {1,2,3}: the encoder point is exp-mapped with c_l, the residual is the Möbius log at the accumulated prefix point, and the prefix step is an exp map; the reconstruction term is a geodesic distance.
COUPLING PATH: transition graph -> quotient Q_l -> kbar_l -> z_l -> target z*_l -> c_l -> Möbius log at p_{l-1} -> residual direction -> nearest code k_l -> next prefix point -> next level's residual. Behaviour and curvature meet in the same operator (the log map's Möbius term), so removing behaviour fixes c_l=1 and changes the residual direction, and removing curvature replaces the log map by subtraction and changes the residual direction too.
COUPLING EQUATION: r_l = (lambda^{c_l}_{p_{l-1}} / 2) * log^{c_l}_{p_{l-1}}(x), with c_l = exp(-clip(zbar + (l-2) min(1, |z_1-z_3|/2), -2, 2)) and lambda^{c}_{p}=2/(1-c||p||^2); the behaviour term is z_l inside c_l, the curved term is the Möbius logarithm.
OBSERVABLES: per-level code occupancy and its perplexity, prefix collision rate, the measured standardised profile z_1,z_2,z_3 of the final SID's quotient graphs, per-level gradient norms of the codebook and encoder, and Stage3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: z_l set to 0 for all levels, so z*_l=0 and c_l=1; the hyperbolic machinery stays exactly as specified (unit curvature ball), the graph and null are still computed and logged, but no behaviour quantity enters the geometry.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c_l=1 and every curved operation replaced by its flat limit, log_p(x)=x-p, exp=addition, transport=identity, distance=Euclidean, while the behaviour term survives only as the per-level residual scale exp(z*_l/2) multiplying (x-p), so the behaviour switch remains live in the Euclidean arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run, c_l from z*_l, curved log/transport/exp/distance active; this is the single trained candidate.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp the artanh argument to 1-1e-7 and ||p|| to (1-1e-6)/sqrt(c) before every log/exp; assert c in [e^-2,e^2] and skip the update on NaN/Inf keeping the previous c_l; cap W1 support at the top 64 neighbours per node and cap sampled quotient edges at 50000 by edge weight, renorma-lising the walk; occupancy guard reverts c_1 if fewer than K/2 level-1 codes (K = codebook size, about 256, i.e. 128 codes) hold at least 1 percent of training items, and reverts c_3 if level-3 used codes fall below half of their epoch-1 value; no item is merged or reassigned to a coarser partition, so Issue #127-style resolution loss cannot come from the mechanism itself; the curvature null uses a fixed 16 draws and a fixed seed so the target is reproducible within the single run.
END IDEA
