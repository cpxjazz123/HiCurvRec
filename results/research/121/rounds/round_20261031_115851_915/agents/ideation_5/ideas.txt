IDEA: GROMOV4-MARGIN-GATE-SID
TITLE: Measured-δ hyperbolic margin gate plus a training-demand gate for precision-first relabeling of the deepest RQ level
MOTIVATION: The frozen Stage-2 RQ-VAE resolves the first L-1 tokens well but leaves the deepest token carrying almost no behavioural information: items whose training next-item demand differs sharply sit in the same (prefix, level-L) cell, so Stage 3 must memorise the tail of the SID instead of reading it off the tree. The earlier certificate round tried to fix this by moving one third of the catalogue on a predicate that was necessary but not sufficient, which redistributed the deepest codes without changing what Stage 3 actually has to emit and raised the validation L1 negative hit rate. This idea replaces coverage-first movement with a two-sided certificate whose tolerance is a *measured* hyperbolicity of the training transition graph and whose admissibility is additionally gated by training-split next-item demand, so that a move is only accepted when it changes the token Stage 3 must produce in a region where that token is genuinely uncertain.
NOVELTY: The occupied slots in this repository's history are: curved codebook update (Poincaré–Fréchet successor codebook), per-level curvature from a prefix-transition score (CURVCTRL-SID), radial behaviour-ranked assignment (BRad-RQ), Lorentz successor profiles, behaviour cones, geodesic boosts, MoE branch curvature, fibre mixtures. This idea touches none of them: both codebooks and levels 1..L-1 are frozen and bit-identical, the curvature is a single fixed value derived from the measured Gromov four-point defect of the training transition graph (not per level, not trained, not a radial coordinate), and the novel object is that the *same measured δ is re-expressed as a radius-dependent local hyperbolic resolution* δ_loc(i) which becomes the margin a discrete move must beat. The second novelty is the admissibility condition itself: a per-prefix deepest-token entropy gate and a per-item next-item-mass condition, both computed on the training split, which separate "satisfies the tree certificate" from "reduces what Stage 3 has to guess".
CHANGE: After the frozen Stage-2 run, each training item gets a hyperbolic point (exp map of its encoder embedding under a curvature fixed by the measured δ_G of the training transition graph), smoothed by a weighted Karcher mean over its training transition neighbours; each (prefix, deepest-code) cell gets a curved centroid. An item's deepest code is then reassigned inside its own prefix only if five inequalities hold simultaneously: a hyperbolic margin of one local δ, a transition-share advantage, a demand advantage, an entropy-eligible prefix, and a capacity cap taken from the frozen Stage-2 cell sizes. Stage 3 then runs unchanged on the relabelled SIDs.
SPECIFICATION: All quantities below are computed once after Stage 2, from the training split only, with a fixed seed 20261031; no gradient flows into SID construction; Stage 3 is then the single frozen 150-epoch run.
(1) Symmetrised transition matrix W_ij = N_ij + N_ji from ordered training sessions; unweighted graph A = 1[W>0], giant weak component G.
(2) Hop metric: BFS from a deterministic 2048-item sample S of G; δ_G = max over 262144 sampled quadruples (a,b,c,d) of (largest − second largest)/2 of {d_ab+d_cd, d_ac+d_bd, d_ad+d_bc}. Curvature c = 1/max(δ_G, 1.0), κ = −c².
(3) 16 farthest-point-sampled anchors (seeded at the max-degree node); tangent vector v_i = s·(z_i − mean z) with s = median‖D_i‖ / median‖z_i − mean z‖ (D_i = the 16 hop distances). x_i = exp_0^κ(v_i) = tanh(c‖v_i‖)·v_i/(c‖v_i‖).
(4) y_i = Riemannian weighted Karcher mean (20 fixed steps, step 1.0, retraction by exp) of {x_j : j ∈ {i} ∪ N32(i)} with ω_ii = 1 and ω_ij = W_ij/max_k W_ik.
(5) Cell = (π_i, c_L(i)) with π_i = (c_1..c_{L-1})(i); m_cell = Karcher mean of {y_j : cell(j)=cell}.
(6) Move predicate and greedy acceptance exactly as in SPEC EQUATIONS.
COST: Zero added cost inside training: 0 extra FLOPs on each of the 72,000 Stage-2 steps (24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs) and 0 on every Stage-3 step, because nothing is added to either loop — the mechanism is a one-shot post-Stage-2 construction whose output is a new deepest-token table. One-time precompute ≈ 2–4 CPU-minutes and <1 GB: 2048 BFS over ≈24.5k nodes and a few 10^5 edges (≈10^9 edge traversals, the dominant term), 262,144 quadruple evaluations (constant time each), 24,556 exp maps plus 24,556×20 Karcher retractions, a few thousand cell means, and a sparse T over at most 32 neighbours per item. Peak memory: 2048² float32 = 16 MB for the distance matrix, plus 24,556×64 float32 for the points. No GPU time, no retraining.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if three things hold in the single run: the moved fraction is materially smaller than the previous one third while still non-trivial (a few percent), the deepest-token conditional entropy falls under the entropy-eligible prefixes (the moves land where uncertainty was, not in already-well-predicted regions), and Stage-3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065. Observation is by direct comparison of the recorded before/after tables and the single test number; no arm is trained, so the case must be carried by the both-on run plus the pre-registered diagnostics.
RISKS: The training transition graph may be far from tree-like, giving a large δ_G, a small κ and a geometry almost indistinguishable from Euclidean; the behaviour graph may be too sparse to give most items a non-empty 32-neighbourhood, so almost nothing is admissible and the mechanism is inert; hyperbolic Karcher means shrink points toward the origin, which shortens all distances and could make the margin test too permissive; the median entropy gate could select prefixes that are high-entropy only because their cells are tiny; Stage 3 may be nearly insensitive to the deepest token (short SID, strong memorisation), in which case even a correct relabel cannot move recall; and the cap, anchored to frozen Stage-2 cell sizes, may block exactly the moves that matter when one cell is already dominant.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions (symmetrised to W_ij = N_ij + N_ji) from ordered training sessions, plus the derived training next-item demand m_i (number of training next-item events whose target is i).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training split only. For each training session (ordered item list) and each consecutive pair (a,b) in that session, N[a,b] += 1. W_ij = N_ij + N_ji. m_i = column sum of the training next-item event counts (equivalently Σ_j N_ji plus session-terminal targets, counted exactly as Stage 3's target construction does on the training split). Derived quantities: per-prefix demand p(c|π) = Σ_{i∈cell(π,c)} m_i / Σ_{i:π_i=π} m_i, per-prefix deepest-token entropy H(π) = −Σ_c p(c|π) log p(c|π), per-item cell mass u(π,c) = (Σ_{i∈cell(π,c)} m_i)/(|cell(π,c)|·Σ_{i:π_i=π} m_i), median gate Hmed over prefixes with at least two non-empty cells, and the hop metric on the giant component of A.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Every input to the move predicate — N, W, m_i, H(π), Hmed, u, the 2048-item BFS sample, the 16 anchors, δ_G, κ, s, and the frozen Stage-2 cell sizes — is computed from the training split and frozen Stage-2 artifacts only. Validation is touched only after the relabel, read-only, to produce the before/after diagnostics; it never enters a threshold, a sort key or a stopping rule, and because levels 1..L-1 are bit-identical the validation L1 negative hit rate is required to come out exactly unchanged (asserted in code as a falsification check, not used to choose anything). Test is untouched until the single frozen Stage-3 evaluation. No early stopping, no selection among variants, no seed repetition.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré ball of constant negative curvature κ = −c² with c = 1/max(δ_G, 1.0) (δ_G in hop units, so c has units 1/hop), with exp_0^κ(v) = tanh(c‖v‖)·v/(c‖v‖), d_P(x,y) = (1/c)·arcosh(1 + 2c²‖x−y‖²/((1−c²‖x‖²)(1−c²‖y‖²))), Riemannian Karcher means under d_P, and a radius-dependent local resolution δ_loc(i) = d_P(exp_0^κ(v_i), exp_0^κ(v_i + δ_G·n_i)) with n_i a fixed unit vector orthogonal to v_i. Curvature is fixed, not trained. Distances carry the standard factor-2 convention relative to tangent norms (d_P(0, exp_0(v)) = 2‖v‖); every inequality below uses the same d_P, so the factor never has to be calibrated.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: At SID construction after the frozen Stage-2 quantization: the encoder embedding is mapped into the ball, the behaviour smoothing is a curved mean, cell centroids are curved means, and the accept/reject margin of each discrete deepest-code move is a curved distance increment. Nothing is added to the Stage-2 or Stage-3 training loops.
COUPLING PATH: TRAIN sessions → symme trised counts W → unweighted transition graph → BFS hop metric → measured four-point defect δ_G → curvature c = 1/max(δ_G,1) → exp map of the encoder embedding into the ball → weighted Karcher smoothing whose neighbourhood and weights are W → curved cell centroids → the transfer statistic T and the margin d_P + δ_loc → accept/reject of the deepest-code move → new SIDs → frozen Stage 3. Removing behaviour deletes the neighbourhood, the weights, δ_G (hence κ) and the demand gate, so the computation becomes plain Euclidean-to-ball nearest-centroid; removing curvature deletes d_P, the exp map and the Karcher mean, so the same move set is decided by chordal distances and the tolerance collapses to a Euclidean δ. Either way the accepted move set changes, which is why the coupling is real rather than decorative.
COUPLING EQUATION: accept(i → c′) iff
  H(π_i) > Hmed
  and u(π_i,c′) > u(π_i,c_L(i))
  and T(i,c′) > T(i,c_L(i)),  T(i,cell) = Σ_{j∈cell} W_ij·exp(−(d_P(y_i,y_j;κ) − dmin_i)) / Σ_{j:W_ij>0} W_ij·exp(−(d_P(y_i,y_j;κ) − dmin_i))
  and d_P(y_i, m_{π_i,c′}; κ) + δ_loc(i) < d_P(y_i, m_{π_i,c_L(i)}; κ)
  and |cell(π_i,c′)| < cap(π_i) = max_c |cell(π_i,c)| at frozen Stage 2,
with the behaviour term W_ij and the curved term d_P(·;κ) appearing multiplicatively inside the same statistic and the same decision.
OBSERVABLES: Moved fraction of the 24,556 training items (must be far below the previous one third). Per-prefix deepest-token entropy H(π) before and after, and per-item deepest-code conditional entropy H(π_i) before and after, reported as distributions. Validation L1 negative hit rate before and after (must be exactly equal, by bit-identity of levels 1..L-1, and is asserted). Validation (L−1)-prefix negative hit rate before and after (free to change; expected to fall). Number of distinct (prefix, code) cells and the maximum cell size. Stage-3 test_recall@10 for the single frozen run, judged against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Neighbourhoods are emptied (N32(i) = ∅), so y_i = x_i, all weights are set to zero so T is replaced by the curved-nearest-cell indicator, and the H-gate and u-condition are disabled (Hmed := −∞). The result is a pure curved nearest-centroid reassignment under the same δ_loc margin and the same capacity cap. The switch exists and is unit-tested on a 200-item dry run which must produce a different move set; no arm is trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: The c = 0 branch is taken: exp_0 and log_0 become the identity, d_P becomes the Euclidean chord distance, Karcher means become arithmetic weighted means, and δ_loc(i) becomes the Euclidean chordal image of a δ_G tangent step. The behaviour graph, W, δ_G-as-a-number, the H-gate, the u-condition and the cap are all unchanged. The switch exists and is unit-tested; no arm is trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full pathway above, run once: frozen Stage-2 (3000 epochs, four cards) → the one-pass relabel with δ_G-derived curvature and training-demand gating → frozen Stage-3 (150 epochs) → test_recall@10. This single run is the candidate; any diagnostic that would normally be used to pick a setting is reported only.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Pseudocode, everything once, seed 20261031.
  for (a,b) in consecutive pairs of each TRAIN session: N[a,b] += 1
  W = N + N.T ; A = (W > 0) ; G = giant weak component of A
  S = 2048 nodes of G sampled deterministically ; D_S = BFS hop distances (inf if unreachable)
  Q = 262144 quadruples from S (seed) ; δ_G = max_Q (s1 − s2)/2, s = descending sort of {d_ab+d_cd, d_ac+d_bd, d_ad+d_bc}
  c = 1/max(δ_G, 1.0) ; κ = −c²
  A16 = farthest-point sample of 16 nodes of G seeded at argmax degree ; D_i = hop distances to A16, inf clipped to the max finite value
  zc = z − mean(z) ; s_scale = median‖D_i‖ / median‖zc_i‖
  v_i = s_scale·zc_i ; x_i = tanh(c‖v_i‖)·v_i / (c‖v_i‖)    [v_i = 0 → x_i = 0]
  ω_ij = W_ij / max_{k∈N32(i)} W_ik , ω_ii = 1 , N32 = top-32 neighbours by W (ties by index)
  y_i = Karcher({x_j, ω_ij}, init x_i, 20 steps of step 1.0): grad = −2Σ_j ω_ij log_{y}(x_j)/Σ_j ω_ij ; y ← exp_y(−grad)
  for each cell: m_cell = Karcher({y_j}, uniform, init = mean)
  dmin_i = min_{j:W_ij>0} d_P(y_i,y_j) ; T(i,cell) = Σ_{j∈cell} W_ij e^{−(d_P−dmin_i)} / Σ_j W_ij e^{−(d_P−dmin_i)}   [log-sum-exp form]
  v_i^t = s_scale·zc_i ; n_i = normalize(any fixed vector ⊥ v_i) ; δ_loc(i) = d_P(exp_0(v_i^t), exp_0(v_i^t + δ_G·n_i))
  Hmed = median{H(π) : π has ≥ 2 non-empty cells}
  cand = all (i, c′) with π(c′) = π_i, c′ ≠ c_L(i)
  keep cand if H(π_i) > Hmed, u(π_i,c′) > u(π_i,c_L(i)), |cell(π_i,c′)| < cap(π_i),
              T(i,c′) > T(i,c_L(i)),
              d_P(y_i, m_{π_i,c′}) + δ_loc(i) < d_P(y_i, m_{π_i,c_L(i)})
  sort by (T(i,c′) − T(i,c_L(i))) descending, then (i, c′) ascending
  accept greedily if the source cell keeps ≥ 1 item and the target cell is under cap; one pass, no re-scoring
  write c_L′(i) ; assert c_1..c_{L-1} unchanged for every item and both codebooks unchanged (hash the tensors before/after)
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the encoder embedding z_i of every training item (dimension d, e.g. 64), the frozen level assignments (c_1..c_L)(i), the two/three codebooks, the training session sequences, the codebook size K at level L, the number of levels L, the fixed seed 20261031. Outputs: a new deepest-level assignment c_L′(i) for a subset of the 24,556 training items (levels 1..L-1 and all codebook tensors bit-identical, verified by a hash assertion); and a diagnostic bundle containing the moved fraction, per-prefix and per-item deepest-code conditional entropy before/after, validation L1 and (L−1)-prefix negative hit rates before/after, cell-count and max-cell-size before/after, δ_G, c, and the number of candidates rejected by each of the five conditions.
SAFEGUARDS: arcosh argument clamped to ≥ 1 + 1e-12; ball coordinates clamped to ‖x‖ ≤ (1 − 1e-5)/c; a c < 1e-6 branch runs the Euclidean formulas to avoid division by c; δ_G = 0 is floored to 1 hop so κ stays finite and the margin becomes strict rather than infinite; log-sum-exp with the per-item minimum distance prevents exp underflow when d_P is large; items with an empty neighbourhood never move (T undefined → reject); unreachable pairs in the BFS metric are dropped from the four-point sample and clipped on the anchor-vector path; Karcher iterations have a fixed count with a divergence check on the step norm (break if it grows twice in a row) and return the initial point on failure; a source cell may never fall below one item and a target cell may never exceed the frozen max cell size, so no empty prefix code and no concentration; deterministic tie-breaking by (score, item index, code index) makes the relabel reproducible; a pre-registered assertion that the validation L1 negative hit rate is bitwise identical before and after relabels is raised as a hard failure if violated; the moved-fraction ceiling is an output, never a target, so no constant is chosen to hit a coverage number.
