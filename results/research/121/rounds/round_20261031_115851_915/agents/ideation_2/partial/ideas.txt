IDEA: HORO-LEAF-CERT
TITLE: Horospherical deepest-level SID re-assignment certified by Gromov delta: a precision-first leaf move rule for the frozen RQ-VAE
MOTIVATION: The previous certificate-guarded split/merge moved about one third of the catalogue because its admissible predicate was necessary but not sufficient: every item that satisfied it still had several "equally acceptable" destinations, so the tie-break, not the evidence, decided the SID. The leaf level is where that damage lands: it is the token that disambiguates two items sharing levels 1..L-1, so a mass leaf move both creates new collisions and destroys the leaf-token conditional entropy a generative recommender needs. This idea replaces the tie-break with a distortion-budget margin measured on the frozen Stage-2 geometry, so a leaf item moves only when the geometry resolves the destination beyond its own noise floor.
NOVELTY: No mechanism here uses horospherical / Busemann (ideal-boundary) geometry; prior work used Poincare distance, radii, Frechet centroids, per-level curvature feedback and cone/prefix variants. The secondary novelty is the sufficiency test: a unique Busemann argmax whose margin exceeds 2*delta_p, where delta_p is the Gromov delta of the frozen Poincare embedding of the item's training behaviour neighbourhood. delta is measured, not chosen, so the rule needs no swept constant, and ties resolve to "do not move" instead of to a coverage-increasing tie-break.
CHANGE: After Stage 2 only c_L(x) may change, and only inside x's own prefix group. A move x -> k is applied iff a two-sided Gromov-delta certificate holds AND k is the unique argmax of a Busemann alignment score by more than twice the group's measured distortion budget AND the total leaf-slot Gromov delta of the partition strictly falls.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Freeze Stage-2 encoder embeddings u_x, level-L centres e_k, SIDs s(x); compute sigma = median_x ||u_x - mean(u)|| and map z_x = tanh(||u_x-mean(u)||/sigma)*unit(u_x-mean(u)) into the unit ball, curvature kappa = -1/sigma^2. (2) From TRAIN sessions only build directed counts w(x,y); behavioural star N(x) = top-mass successors + top-mass predecessors + {x}. (3) Compute delta(S) by the four-point Gromov product in the curved metric; set delta_p = median over the prefix group of delta(N(x)).
