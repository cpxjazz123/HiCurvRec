IDEA: GYRO-BEHAFF-SID
TITLE: Möbius-gyro residual RQ with behaviour-affinity-weighted Poincaré deep assignment (K = 256/512/512)
MOTIVATION: The measured failure across rounds one and two is depth collapse, not codeword shortage: 24556 training items give 1251 distinct L2 pairs (19.6 items per cell) but only 1298 distinct L3 triples — about 1.04 L3 codes per L2 cell, so the third token carries almost no information. Round one, which kept 1.76 L3 codes per cell, scored 0.0544 against round two's 0.0517. The cause is structural: the third-level tangent residual r^3 = r^2 − log_0(c_2) is near zero for most items, so the ball degenerates to flat space exactly where the finest partition is needed. Every mechanism so far tried to fix depth by moving items (toward a behaviour hub, along a geodesic step) and every one of them merged prefixes.
NOVELTY: Two pieces that are absent from the tried set (poincare-frechet-successor-codebook, CURVCTRL-SID, BRad-RQ, behcone-prefix-rq, succboost-geoshift, LORENTZ-SUCCPROFILE-SMOOTH-RQ, MIXCURV-FIBRE-RQ, BRANCHCURV-MOEB-RQ, TSG-SID). (i) The residual algebra is changed from tangent subtraction to Poincaré gyro-subtraction, with the decoder defined as the Möbius gyro-sum, so the greedy residual is exact by gyro-cancellation and no gain constant is introduced. (ii) The deep-level assignment cost is the Poincaré distance multiplied per (item, codeword) by (1 − a_ik), a train-only successor-profile affinity, so behaviour chooses which of 512 deep codes an item receives. Prior mechanisms moved codebook positions, set per-level curvature, ranked items radially, or applied cones/boosts; none changes the residual algebra, and none makes the distance non-monotone per pair so that behaviour can actually flip the argmin and therefore the token.
CHANGE: Replace the RQ residual subtraction with Poincaré gyro-subtraction and the reconstruction with the Möbius gyro-sum of the chosen codewords, and at levels 2 and 3 multiply each Poincaré distance by (1 − a_ik) before the argmin, with 512 codewords at those levels.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed curvature c = 1 (curvature is a gauge; the encoder's tanh scaling absorbs the scale, so no curvature value is chosen or swept). Ball B = {v ∈ R^d : ‖v‖ < 1}. Möbius addition x ⊕ y = ((1 + 2⟨x,y⟩ + ‖y‖²)x + (1 − ‖x‖²)y) / (1 + 2⟨x,y⟩ + ‖x‖²‖y‖²); gyro-subtraction x ⊖ y = x ⊕ (−y). Poincaré distance d_P(x,y) = arcosh(1 + 2‖x−y‖² / ((1−‖x‖²)(1−‖y‖²))). Encoder emits t_i ∈ R^d, v_i = tanh(‖t_i‖/2) · t_i/‖t_i‖. RQ recursion: r^0_i = v_i; for ℓ = 1,2,3, k^ℓ_i = argmin_k [ d_P(r^{ℓ−1}_i, c_{ℓk}) · (1 − γ a^ℓ_{ik}) ] with γ = 1, then r^ℓ_i = r^{ℓ−1}_i ⊖ c_{ℓ,k^ℓ_i}. Reconstruction ẑ_i = c_{1,k¹} ⊕ c_{2,k²} ⊕ c_{3,k³}; loss L = d_P(v_i, ẑ_i)² + β_com·Σ_ℓ ‖log_0(r^ℓ_i)‖² (β_com inherited unchanged from the frozen recipe). Level 1 uses γ_1 = 0 so the coarse semantic hierarchy stays purely geometric; the coupling is confined to levels 2 and 3. a^ℓ_{ik} = max(0, cos(π_i − π̄, π^ℓ_k − π̄)), with π_i a fixed 256-bin successor profile, π̄ its mean, and π^ℓ_k an EMA (decay 0.99) of π_i over items currently assigned to codeword k at level ℓ, stop-gradient. Soft assignment for the straight-through gradient uses softmax(−C^ℓ_{ik}/ε) with ε = median_{i,k} C^ℓ_{ik} over the batch, detached.
COST: Baseline 0.145–0.16 s per epoch on four GPUs. The added work per step is one Möbius addition/subtraction over the batch (O(B·d)), one 4096×256 by 256×512 affinity matmul (~0.54 GFLOP, sub-millisecond), and doubled distance matrices at levels 2 and 3. Measured estimate: +10–15% of the quantizer stage, i.e. 0.165–0.18 s per epoch, ~4.0–4.3 s per 24-step epoch pass total; 3000 epochs ≈ 8.5–9.0 minutes against the baseline's ~7.5–8.0 minutes. Extra trainable parameters: 2·512·d ≈ 65k at d = 64, negligible against the frozen budget. The 24556×256 behaviour-profile table is a fixed 25 MB buffer, not a parameter. Stage 3 protocol, sequence length and vocabulary usage are untouched apart from K2 = K3 = 512.
HYPOTHESIS: If the third token is being wasted because the flat-approximated residual collapses, then (a) the gyro residual keeps ‖r^3‖ at a curvature-determined scale instead of near zero, and (b) the affinity term splits each L2 cell's ~20 items into behaviourally coherent sub-groups, so the L3 code becomes a behaviour discriminator that Stage 3 can predict from the prefix and context. Worth keeping only if distinct L3 triples rise from 1298 to at least 3000, mean L3 codes per L2 cell rises from 1.04 to at least 2.5, mean affinity a_{i,k*} of assigned items exceeds 0.3, and d_P reconstruction stays within 5% of the baseline value while test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: The affinity term pulls codes away from reconstruction-optimal choices and can trade semantic fidelity for partition (this is why the 5% reconstruction guard is recorded). 512 codes at level 2 may shatter the L1 hierarchy into noise. Möbius subtraction of two nearly aligned points at large radius can inflate ‖r‖ toward the boundary and route many items onto a few extreme codes, re-creating collapse in a different form. The 256-bin successor profile (≈96 items per bin) is smooth, so a_ik may be too weak to flip many argmins; if purity stays near zero the affinity is inert. A finer third token is harder for Stage 3 to generate, so prefix counts can rise while test_recall@10 falls — that is the outcome to record, not to hide.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item transitions, aggregated into a per-item successor profile over a frozen 256-bin item partition.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training sequences only, count ordered adjacent pairs (u → v). Weight each pair w_{u→v} = n_{u→v} / pop_v^{0.5} (the square-root popularity correction already used in the frozen recipe). Bin every successor v into one of 256 clusters produced once by k-means on the frozen training text embeddings. Set π_u[m] = Σ_{v→m} w_{u→v} / Σ_v w_{u→v}, an L1-normalised 256-vector; items with no outgoing train transition receive π̄. Computed once before epoch 1, stored as a constant table, never recomputed from validation or test data.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition counts, the popularity correction, the k-means partition and the π table are built exclusively from the Stage-2 train split, before any training step, and are frozen for the whole run. No validation or test sequence, no future-relative-to-split item, and no Stage-3 interaction ever enters π_i, π^ℓ_k, the codewords or the SID strings. Stage 3 consumes the SIDs; it does not write back into them. The only data-dependent quantity updated during training, π^ℓ_k, is an EMA over train-batch assignments only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Möbius gyro-addition and gyro-subtraction in the Poincaré ball plus the Poincaré distance, at fixed negative curvature c = 1, used both as the residual algebra and as the assignment metric.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE quantizer recursion (residual and reconstruction) and inside the level-2 and level-3 assignment cost, during Stage-2 training.
COUPLING PATH: encoder → v_i → gyro residual r^{ℓ−1} → cost d_P(r^{ℓ−1}, c_{ℓk})·(1 − a_{ik}) → argmin gives k^ℓ → next gyro residual → token string (k¹,k²,k³) → frozen Stage 3 → test_recall@10. The behaviour profile enters only through the multiplier on the curved distance, and the curve enters only through the residual algebra and the same distance, so deleting either changes k^ℓ and therefore the SID.
COUPLING EQUATION: k^ℓ_i = argmin_k [ d_P( r^{ℓ−1}_i , c_{ℓk} ) · (1 − γ · a^ℓ_{ik}) ], with r^ℓ_i = r^{ℓ−1}_i ⊖ c_{ℓ,k^ℓ_i}, γ = 1.
OBSERVABLES: distinct L3 triples (baseline 1298, target ≥3000); mean distinct L3 codes per L2 cell (1.04 → ≥2.5); mean behaviour purity a_{i,k*} of assigned items (baseline undefined/0 → >0.3); mean and 90th-percentile ‖r^3‖ (should stop collapsing toward zero); reconstruction d_P (must stay within 5%); L2 distinct pairs; Stage-3 third-token prefix hit rate; test_recall@10, recall@5, ndcg@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: γ = 0 at every level, so the cost is the pure Poincaré distance; gyro residual and gyro-sum reconstruction unchanged; π table still built and the switches still compiled in, but the run is not trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: replace gyro-subtraction with tangent subtraction r^ℓ = log_0(r^{ℓ−1}) − log_0(c_ℓ), the gyro-sum reconstruction with exp_0(Σ log_0 c_ℓ), and d_P with the Euclidean distance ‖·‖; the affinity factor (1 − a_ik) is retained exactly as in the both-on arm; not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the single trained arm — gyro residual, gyro-sum reconstruction, Poincaré cost times (1 − a_ik), K = 256/512/512.
SPEC EQUATIONS: 
v_i = tanh(‖t_i‖/2)·t_i/‖t_i‖
r^0_i = v_i
for ℓ in 1..3:
  C^ℓ_{ik} = d_P(r^{ℓ−1}_i, c_{ℓk}) · (1 − γ_ℓ a^ℓ_{ik}),  γ_1 = 0, γ_2 = γ_3 = 1
  k^ℓ_i = argmin_k C^ℓ_{ik};  P^ℓ_{ik} = softmax_k(−C^ℓ_{ik}/ε), ε = median(C^ℓ), detached
  straight-through: k^ℓ_i gets gradient through P^ℓ_{ik}
  r^ℓ_i = r^{ℓ−1}_i ⊖ c_{ℓ,k^ℓ_i}
ẑ_i = c_{1,k¹_i} ⊕ c_{2,k²_i} ⊕ c_{3,k³_i}
L = d_P(v_i, ẑ_i)² + β_com Σ_ℓ ‖log_0(r^ℓ_i)‖² + L_codebook(frozen recipe)
a^ℓ_{ik} = max(0, cos(π_i − π̄, π^ℓ_k − π̄))
π^ℓ_k ← 0.99 π^ℓ_k + 0.01 · mean_{i assigned to (ℓ,k)} π_i  (stop-grad, per step)
Codebook update: Riemannian/Fréchet step c_{ℓk} ← exp_{c}(−η ∇_c L) with η from the frozen optimiser, projected back to ‖c‖ ≤ 1 − 1e-6.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs — frozen text embeddings e_i for the 24556 training items, the frozen 256-bin successor profile table π (24556×256), codeword tables c_1 ∈ R^{256×d}, c_2, c_3 ∈ R^{512×d}, the frozen optimiser and β_com. Outputs — the SID triple (k¹_i, k²_i, k³_i) for every item, the EMA codeword profiles π^ℓ, and the scalar losses and reconstruction diagnostics logged per epoch.
SAFEGUARDS: clip ‖x‖ to 1 − 1e-7 inside every tanh and every denominator; compute d_P through the arcosh form with the argument clamped to ≥ 1 + 1e-12; after each gyro-subtraction renormalise r^ℓ to ‖r^ℓ‖ ≤ 1 − 1e-6 keeping its direction; clamp C^ℓ_{ik} ≥ 0; floor ε at 1e-3 and detach it; L2-normalise π_i and π^ℓ_k and substitute π̄ when a codeword's EMA mass is zero; keep the frozen gradient clip and learning rate unchanged; log mean/p90 ‖r^ℓ‖ every epoch and halt-and-report if ‖r^3‖ grows beyond 1 − 1e-3 for more than 1% of items (boundary saturation); log reconstruction d_P every epoch against the 5% guard. Origin-collapse check required by the contract: the objective's minimum is not at the ball's origin because the gyro-sum reconstruction target is v_i itself and the affinity multiplier is bounded below by 0, so both act against radial contraction; the mean ‖v_i‖ and mean ‖r^ℓ‖ are logged to confirm no degeneration to one or two prefixes per level.
END IDEA
