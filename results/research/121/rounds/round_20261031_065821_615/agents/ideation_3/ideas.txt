IDEA: BGSQ-SID
TITLE: Behaviour-weighted principal-geodesic 4-way split of the deepest RQ level: a 256→1024 composite SID alphabet that can only refine, never merge
MOTIVATION: Both negative rounds lost recall exactly when they lost deep prefix capacity (2201 L3 prefixes → 0.05439; 1298 → 0.05165), and both lost it by aggregation: round two pulled 99.1% of items toward a popularity-adjusted successor barycentre, and the 3-level 256-codeword level three is already saturated (~24556 items collapse onto ~2201 distinct (l1,l2,l3) triples, i.e. ~11 items share every deep prefix). The recommendation problem is therefore not "make the deep partition more semantic by moving items" but "make it finer where successor behaviour actually differs". A behaviour-contrast construction inside a single parent cell is the only place where a refine can happen without paying a merge somewhere else.
NOVELTY: Every mechanism in this repository moves things: a curved residual chain (round one), a concentration-scaled geodesic step toward successor barycentres (round two), per-level curvature driven by prefix-transition score (CURVCTRL-SID), radial ranking by behaviour entropy (BRad-RQ), successor-weighted Fréchet codebook updates (poincare-frechet-successor-codebook). All of them consume capacity. BGSQ-SID moves nothing across a parent boundary: it keeps the frozen 256-codeword parent partition bit-for-bit and expands only the *symbol set* at the deepest level, with the expansion produced by a frozen curved bisection whose normal is a behaviour-contrast second moment. SID length stays 3, so the frozen Stage-3 sequence format and decoding trie are unchanged; the only architecture delta is the position-three alphabet.
CHANGE: Level 3 becomes a composite symbol 4*k + side (256 parents × 4 sides = 1024 symbols), where the 4 sides are the quadrants of the top-2 directions of the behaviour-weighted residual second moment of parent cell k; the side test is a geodesic hyperplane through the *trained* parent codeword in the Poincaré residual ball and the four child codewords sit off-centre at ±s along those geodesic directions, so the behaviour statistics and the curved geometry enter the emitted token and the reconstruction vector in the same expression.
SPECIFICATION: One deterministic precompute from the train split and the frozen text geometry; then a 4-way composite level-3 quantizer used unchanged for all 3000 epochs. Precompute: (1) transition rows p_i (row-normalised consecutive-item counts, train split only) and cell means pbar_k; (2) one forward pass of the recipe's RQ-VAE at its initial parameters on the frozen text embeddings, giving parent ids k_i and level-3 residual points r_i; (3) t_i = log_0(r_i), w_i = JS(p_i || pbar_k)/ln2 ∈ [0,1], M_k = Σ_{i∈C_k} w_i t_i t_i^T, u_{k,1},u_{k,2} = top-2 orthonormal eigenvectors with deterministic sign, s_{k,r} = max(median_i |⟨t_i,u_{k,r}⟩|, 1e-3·median_i ||t_i||); store u,s as frozen buffers. Every step: recompute the level-3 residual of the current encoder output, side b = 1[⟨t,u_1⟩≥0] + 2[⟨t,u_2⟩≥0], SID_3 = 4k + b, level-3 reconstruction vector c_k ⊕ exp_0((2b1−1)s_1u_1 + (2b2−1)s_2u_2). Three wired switches: behaviour off sets w_i ≡ 1; geometry off sends c→0 (Euclidean + , identity exp/log, Euclidean hyperplane); both on is the run.
COST: Zero added parameters; u,s are buffers of 2201×2×(d+1) floats (~0.3M at d=64, under 0.1% of the encoder). One-off precompute: sparse JS against 2201 cell means plus 2201 eigendecompositions of d×d matrices (~0.6 GFLOP, seconds on one GPU, minutes end-to-end including the initial RQ pass). Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs, global batch 4096): two dot products of dim d and one extra Möbius add plus one exp_0 per item, and a per-parent lookup of (u,s); measured-equivalent overhead ≈ +2–4% of the quantizer, so Stage 2 stays at ~0.15 s/epoch on four cards. Stage 3 gains only a larger position-three softmax (256→1024, ~+0.2M head parameters), 150 epochs unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: Three things must hold. (i) Items inside one parent cell differ systematically in successor distribution and that difference has a non-zero projection onto the level-3 residual tangent — otherwise s_k collapses to its floor and the split degenerates into a near-duplicate 2-bit tag (collision relief only, no semantic gain). (ii) The two side bits are predictable from the user's SID history, i.e. prefix-conditional entropy of the side bits is low, otherwise a 4× larger position-three alphabet costs the generator more than the finer partition buys. (iii) The 4× reduction in deep-prefix collisions outweighs the harder position-three prediction. All three are observable: realised between-side JS of successor distributions inside a cell, side-bit conditional entropy on train, distinct (l1,l2,l3) triple count, full-SID collision rate, and finally Stage-3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 in the single both-on run.
RISKS: The level-3 residual after three quantization steps may be mostly text noise, so the behaviour-weighted axis may not separate successors at all (graceful: s floors out, only the token capacity remains). A 1024-symbol deepest level may be under-trained by Stage 3 in 150 epochs, diluting rather than sharpening prefixes. Freezing the axes at epoch 0 means the trained encoder can drift away from the precomputed directions, which would make the split progressively less behaviour-aligned. TIGER-style trie-constrained decoding over 1024 deep symbols can mis-calibrate beam search. Single seed, no ablation arm: any of these fails as a negative number, recorded.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only directed item-transition rows p_i over consecutive-item pairs of training sequences, and the derived per-item behaviour contrast w_i = JS(p_i || pbar_{k(i)})/ln2 against the mean successor distribution of the item's own level-3 parent cell.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only: count n_ij over consecutive pairs (u_t, u_{t+1}) in each training user's item sequence (no user/item filtering, no popularity rescaling so the contrast is not a popularity surrogate), row-normalise to p_i; items with no outgoing pair get w_i = 0 (neutral, they never steer an axis). Cell means pbar_k = (1/|C_k|)Σ_{j∈C_k} p_j, and JS with the standard bounded form, divided by ln2 so w ∈ [0,1] with no scale constant. Computed once, before Stage 2, from the frozen text geometry's initial quantization.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition matrix, the cell means, the weights, the axes u and the offsets s are all computed from training sequences and from a deterministic initial forward pass of the RQ-VAE; no validation or test interaction is read, and the SID vocabulary is frozen before Stage 2 and before Stage 3, so no validation/test signal can enter the token construction. Stage 3 keeps its frozen protocol and only consumes pre-built SIDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré ball with fixed unit negative curvature c = 1 (gauge-fixed, so no curvature sweep), with exp_0(t) = tanh(||t||)t/||t||, log_0(v) = artanh(||v||)v/||v||, Möbius addition ⊕, its inverse Möbius subtraction ⊖, and a geodesic hyperplane through the parent codeword c_k defined as the zero set of ⟨log_0((⊖c_k) ⊕ v), u⟩ for a unit tangent u ∈ T_0 of the residual ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In quantization, at the deepest RQ level: the side assignment is a geodesic half-space test in the residual ball and the four child codewords are placed by exp_0 of ±s·u inside the tangent space at the parent codeword; the assignment is recomputed every step from the current trained codeword, so the curvature acts throughout the 3000 epochs and not only at initialisation.
COUPLING PATH: train transitions → pbar_k, w_i → behaviour-weighted second moment M_k → top-2 tangent directions u_k → geodesic hyperplane through the trained codeword c_k → side bits b → composite SID symbol 4k+b and reconstruction vector c_k ⊕ exp_0(Σ ±s u). Removing the behaviour term changes u (unweighted moment) and therefore every deep token; removing the curved term changes ⊕/exp/hyperplane into +/identity/affine and therefore every deep token and every child codeword. Neither can be dropped without changing the emitted SID.
COUPLING EQUATION: SID_3(i) = 4·k_i + Σ_{r=1..2} 1[ ⟨log_0( (⊖c_{k_i}) ⊕ v_i^{(2)} ), u_{k_i,r}(w) ⟩ ≥ 0 ] · 2^{r−1}, with u_{k,r}(w) = eig_r( Σ_{i∈C_k} w_i(p_i, pbar_k) · t_i t_i^T ), w_i = JS(p_i‖pbar_k)/ln2, and the child codeword c_{k,b} = c_k ⊕ exp_0( Σ_r (2b_r−1) s_{k,r} u_{k,r} ), s_{k,r} = median_i |⟨t_i, u_{k,r}⟩|.
OBSERVABLES: distinct (l1,l2,l3) triples (recorded; today 2201 of 24556 items); full-SID collision rate; realised between-side JS of successor distributions inside each parent cell (did the split actually separate behaviour); side-bit conditional entropy given the prefix on train (prefix behaviour predictability); the effective number of used position-three symbols; and Stage-3 test_recall@10, recall@5, ndcg@10. Geometry on vs Euclidean should also change the *distribution* of s across radius — the deployment check that curvature is doing work is that central, high-traffic cells get larger relative tangent offsets than boundary cells.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: identical code path with w_i ≡ 1, so M_k is the plain second moment of the level-3 residual and the four sides are unrelated to successor behaviour. Exists and is wired; not trained this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: identical code path with c → 0: r_i and t_i become plain Euclidean residuals, ⊕ becomes +, exp_0/log_0 become identity, the side test becomes a linear hyperplane through c_k, child codewords become c_k ± s u. Exists and is wired; not trained this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the coupled run: behaviour-weighted second moment, geodesic hyperplane, exp_0-placed children, composite 1024-symbol level 3. This is the single trained arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS: precompute: p_i = n_i / Σ_j n_ij; pbar_k = mean_{j∈C_k} p_j; w_i = JS(p_i‖pbar_k)/ln2; t_i = log_0(r_i) with r_i = (⊖c^{(3)}_{k_i}) ⊕ v_i^{(2)} from the initial pass; M_k = Σ_{i∈C_k} w_i t_i t_i^T; [u_{k,1},u_{k,2}] = top-2 eigvec(M_k), sign fixed so the largest-|·| coordinate is positive, u_2 replaced by the lowest-index unit vector orthogonal to u_1 if λ_2/λ_1 < 1e-6; s_{k,r} = clip(median_{i∈C_k}|⟨t_i,u_{k,r}⟩|, 1e-3·ρ_k, ρ_k) with ρ_k = median_{i∈C_k}||t_i||; if |C_k| < 4 set s = 1e-3·ρ_k and u_r = e_r. per step: t_i ← log_0((⊖c_{k_i}^{(3)}) ⊕ v_i^{(2)}) (detached for the sign, live for the reconstruction); b_{i,r} ← 1[⟨t_i,u_{k_i,r}⟩ ≥ 0]; y3_i ← 4k_i + b_{i,1} + 2b_{i,2}; v̂_i^{(2)} ← c_{k_i}^{(3)} ⊕ exp_0( (2b_{i,1}−1)s_{k_i,1}u_{k_i,1} + (2b_{i,2}−1)s_{k_i,2}u_{k_i,2} ); the rest of the RQ loss is the frozen recipe's, unchanged.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen text embeddings x_i, encoder output v_i ∈ B, parent id k_i and parent codeword c_k, frozen buffers (u_{k,1},u_{k,2},s_{k,1},s_{k,2}), train transition rows p_i. Outputs: composite deepest SID symbol y3_i ∈ {0..1023}, child codeword v̂_i^{(2)} ∈ B used in the reconstruction term, and (for diagnostics only) the side bits and realised between-side JS.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||t|| ≤ artanh(1−1e-5) and renormalise any t with ||t|| < 1e-12 to avoid 0/0 in log_0; floor and cap s_k as above so a cell can never place a child on the boundary or produce a zero-radius duplicate; deterministic eigenvector signs and a deterministic orthogonal fallback for degenerate spectra so the buffers are reproducible across the four ranks; compute the precompute once on rank 0 and broadcast, so all DDP ranks emit identical tokens; items with empty successor rows get w_i = 0 rather than a fabricated uniform profile; assert after initialisation that every parent cell has 4 distinct child vectors up to the floor; and because both the axis (eigenvector of a measured moment) and the offset (a measured median) are non-parametric, no objective in the mechanism is minimised at the ball origin, so the round-one encoder-collapse failure mode cannot be reached by construction.
