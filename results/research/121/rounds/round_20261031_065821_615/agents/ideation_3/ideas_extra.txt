IDEA: BISECT-L4-ASYM
TITLE: Directed-transition asymmetry carved into a per-cell 4-symbol fourth SID digit by hyperbolic quantile bisection
MOTIVATION: The frozen chain is depth-starved: 24556 items share only 2201 distinct level-3 triples (about 1.24 level-3 codes per level-2 cell), so the deepest frozen level is nearly redundant, and every mechanism tried so far reduced that count further (1298 in round two) and lost recall. Behaviour information that separates items by how they are consumed predicts recall, but round two injected it by displacing item points toward a behaviour hub, which merged prefixes. The problem: put behaviour into the deepest tokens while increasing, not decreasing, the number of distinct deep cells.
NOVELTY: No global codebook is re-weighted, no input point is moved, no successor barycentre, no entropy/radial ranking, no per-level curvature scalar. A hierarchical product digit is attached per level-3 cell: each cell owns four child prototypes that are hyperbolic Fréchet centroids of the cell's directed-transition ASYMMETRY quantiles (forward-successor distribution versus backward-predecessor distribution), and the child is chosen by a two-anchor weighted Poincaré score whose second term is scaled by the item's own asymmetry. None of per-cell shared-alphabet refinement, forward/backward asymmetry, or a two-anchor weighted Poincaré assignment exists in the repository, and the two-term sum is precisely what keeps the curvature from being a pure gauge.
CHANGE: Add a fourth SID token from a shared alphabet {0,1,2,3}, keyed by (l1,l2,l3,child); child vectors are trainable but born at frozen hyperbolic asymmetry-quantile centroids, and each assignment is the argmin over j of d_c(xi,u_kj) + b_i·d_c(xi,v_kj).
SPECIFICATION: Offline on the training split, build the first-order transition count matrix F and the per-item support-shrunk forward/backward Jensen-Shannon asymmetry b_i in [0,1]. Fix c = (1/(1.2R))^2 from the measured initial latent radius R. At the end of epoch 1, cut every level-3 cell's items into four equal-mass b-quantiles, compute each quantile's hyperbolic Fréchet centroid v_kj, and clone it into a trainable u_kj. Every step the frozen levels 1-3 run unchanged; level 4 is the argmin of the two-anchor score; the quantized latent is q1+q2+q3+u_k[l4] with a straight-through residual; the baseline reconstruction and commitment losses now include level 4.
COST: +1024 trainable and +1024 frozen child vectors (d=64: about 131k parameters, under 1% of the encoder); per step 4096 items x 4 children x 2 anchors = 32768 Poincaré distances at d=64 (about 4M FLOP, under 3% of an encoder step) plus 4096 four-way argmins. At the frozen scale (24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs, 0.145-0.16 s/epoch) the projected overhead is at most +0.004 s/epoch, i.e. at most +12 s over the whole Stage-2 run; the one-off quantile/centroid pass is O(N log N) over 24556 items and costs seconds.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if the fourth digit actually spreads items, i.e. distinct four-token SIDs rise from about 2201 to at least 6000 with no level-3 cell collapsing onto a single child, and child index tracks the asymmetry quantile the child was born from. If both hold, Stage 3 sees a finer, behaviour-labelled deep partition without any input displacement and test_recall@10 should exceed 0.065. If the digit collapses (all items of a cell to one child) the mechanism is a no-op and the merge lesson predicts no gain.
RISKS: (1) A fourth token lengthens Stage-3 generation by one step and may cost precision even with a finer SID. (2) Transition asymmetry may be sparse: items with fewer than 5 transitions fall back to the smoothed default and their children degrade toward text quartiles, so behaviour stops reaching the tokens. (3) Freezing v at the end of epoch 1 may lock a bad geometry if the encoder drifts; an EMA would introduce a non-derivable constant, so it is deliberately not used. (4) Curvature is a gauge, so any measured gain must come from the two-anchor sum and the per-cell prototypes, not from the value of c. (5) Child usage imbalance could make the 4-symbol alphabet effectively binary.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-transition asymmetry per item, i.e. how different the items that follow an item are from the items that precede it (entry versus terminal character), with support-based shrinkage, restricted to the training split.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training sessions only, count F[i,j] = number of times item i is immediately followed by item j. Row-normalise F[i,:] into p_succ(i,·) and column-normalise F[:,i] into p_pred(i,·); b_i = s_i · JSD(p_succ(i,·), p_pred(i,·)) / log 2 in [0,1], with s_i = n_i/(n_i+5) and n_i = sum_j F[i,j] + sum_j F[j,i]; if n_i < 5 then b_i is set to the median of b over items with n_i >= 5 (a documented fallback, not a tuned value). b is computed once before Stage 2 and stored per item.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: F, b, the quantile groups and the frozen prototypes v are computed exclusively from training sequences and training-item latents; validation and test sequences are read only by the frozen Stage-3 protocol. Prototypes are frozen at the end of epoch 1 from training items only, and the SID table is built from training items only; validation/test items are encoded by the frozen Stage-2 encoder and given digits by the same argmin rule using only frozen prototypes and frozen b, so no validation or test interaction can enter the SID construction. No test statistic chooses c, the smoothing prior, or the number of children.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré ball of curvature -c with c = (1/(1.2R))^2 derived from the measured initial latent radius R; Möbius addition (+); Poincaré distance d_c(x,y) = (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)·||(-x)(+)y||); and the hyperbolic Fréchet centroid via a fixed-point log/exp mean.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Only in the level-4 (deepest) quantizer: it builds the frozen child prototypes from the asymmetry quantiles and supplies both distance terms of the assignment; levels 1-3 keep the frozen Euclidean RQ path and the decoder keeps its Euclidean reconstruction, so curved and Euclidean pieces meet inside one operator.
COUPLING PATH: b_i gives the asymmetry quantile groups of the level-3 cell; those define the frozen hyperbolic centroids v_kj and also supply the per-item weight; both enter the two-anchor Poincaré score; the argmin emits l4; the SID becomes the Stage-3 token. Removing the behaviour term changes both the anchors' ordering and the weight, hence l4; removing the curved operation changes the centroid construction and both distances, hence l4.
COUPLING EQUATION: l4(i) = argmin_{j in {0,1,2,3}} [ d_c(xi_i, u_{k,j}) + b_i · d_c(xi_i, v_{k,j}) ] with k = (l1(i), l2(i), l3(i)) and v_{k,j} = Fréchet_c({xi : item in asymmetry quantile j of cell k}).
OBSERVABLES: distinct four-token SID count and per-cell child entropy (expect at least 6000 distinct and non-degenerate mass); correlation between child index and the asymmetry quantile the child was born from; prefix-conditioned next-token accuracy at position 4 versus positions 1-3; level-4 token usage in the Stage-3 generator; final Stage-3 test_recall@10 against 0.065 and against the 0.05439 and 0.05165 references.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: b_i set to the constant 0.5 for every item and the four groups formed by text-only quartiles of the cell's latent along its first principal direction, so no transition statistic enters anchors or weights; the curved operator is kept.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c treated as Euclidean, d_c replaced by the Euclidean distance, Fréchet centroids replaced by arithmetic means for both prototypes and assignment; b and the two-anchor weighting are kept.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full operator as specified, prototypes from asymmetry quantiles under Poincaré centroids and the assignment using the weighted sum of two Poincaré distances.
SPEC EQUATIONS: (1) F, p_succ, p_pred as above with +1e-9 smoothing; b_i = s_i·JSD(p_succ(i,·),p_pred(i,·))/log 2; s_i = n_i/(n_i+5). (2) c = (1/(1.2R))^2 with R = max over the 24556 initial training latents of ||xi||. (3) End of epoch 1, for each level-3 cell k: sort S_k by b ascending (ties by item id), cut into four equal-mass groups Q_k0..Q_k3 (if |S_k| < 8 replicate one group four times); v_kj = Fréchet_c(Q_kj) by m = mean(Q_kj), then 5 times m = exp_m(mean_{x in Q_kj} log_m(x)), and if sqrt(c)||m|| > 0.95 then m = m·0.95/(sqrt(c)||m||); set u_kj = v_kj. (4) Every step: compute l1,l2,l3 with the frozen RQ path; r4 = xi - (c_l1+c_l2+c_l3); score_j = d_c(r4, u_kj) + b_i·d_c(r4, v_kj); l4 = argmin_j; q = c_l1+c_l2+c_l3+u_k[l4]+sg(r4-u_k[l4]); loss = ||dec(q)-dec_target||^2 + ||sg(q)-xi||^2 + beta·||q-sg(xi)||^2 with beta as in the frozen recipe, u_k[l4] updated by the gradient through q. (5) Deterministic dead-child reseed at the ends of epochs 10, 30, 100 and 300 (fixed fractions of the 3000-epoch budget): any child whose EMA mass in its cell is below 0.02 is moved to the cell item farthest in d_c from the cell's assigned set; the EMA is 1/(1+t) so no decay constant is chosen.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the current RQ latent xi (dim d), its level-1..3 codes, the frozen per-item scalar b_i in [0,1], the frozen prototype table v (256 cells x 4 x d) and the trainable child table u (same shape). Output is the integer l4 in {0,1,2,3} plus the quantized vector q in R^d; the index is stop-gradient and only the chosen child vector is differentiable.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp sqrt(c)||x|| to <= 1-1e-5 before every artanh and clamp the artanh argument to <= 1-1e-6; replace any non-finite b_i or centroid with the cell mean; cells with fewer than 8 items use four replicated children and no reseed; the dead-child reseed prevents alphabet collapse to one symbol; the epoch-1 freeze and the reseed epochs are the only schedule constants and are fixed fractions of the 3000-epoch budget, with no value chosen by trying alternatives; per-cell child entropy is logged every 100 epochs so the round-one collapse failure mode is visible rather than silent.
END IDEA
