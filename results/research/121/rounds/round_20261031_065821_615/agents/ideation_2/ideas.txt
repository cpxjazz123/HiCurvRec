IDEA: BCEM-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-contrast ellipsoidal Möbius metric inside the RQ-VAE assignment and commitment (nothing moves; the cell boundary tilts)
MOTIVATION: The frozen recipe collapses items at depth: 24556 training items land on roughly 1.3k-2.2k distinct deep prefixes, so most items are indistinguishable to the generative model and Stage3 recall is capped. Both negative rounds attacked this by MOVING things — a geodesic step toward a popularity-adjusted successor barycentre (round two), a chained curved residual on the frozen text geometry (round one) — and both reduced the distinct-prefix count and lost recall. The behaviour information that is missing is not "where an item should sit" but "which pairs of textually near-identical items must not share a deep prefix". In this programme the behaviour-CONTRAST metric is the only behaviour diagnostic that predicts recall (+0.7104 plain, +0.7170 Euclidean control), while radial, Gromov-delta and ancestor-depth weightings hurt (-0.12, -0.075).
NOVELTY: Prior mechanisms either attach one scalar per level (curvature per level, radial behaviour rank, context entropy, cone membership) or move points and codebook centres (successor-barycentre step, Poincaré-Fréchet codebook mean, geodesic shift, curved residual chain). This one moves nothing and adds no slot: it deforms the metric by which a codeword is chosen, with a per-item rank-1 factor whose direction is a train-only successor-versus-popularity-matched-non-neighbour contrast, and it places that deformation inside the Möbius displacement of the Poincaré distance. The behaviour signal therefore changes the TOKENS (through the argmin) and the encoder gradient (through the curved commitment), which is exactly the "signal must reach the tokens" requirement, without changing positions, centres or architecture.
CHANGE: At levels 2 and 3 the nearest-codeword rule is replaced by the anisotropic Poincaré rule d_M(x,y;i) = (2/√c)·artanh(√(c·zᵀM_i z)) with z = (−x)⊕_c y and M_i = I + λ_i û_iû_iᵀ; the frozen commitment ‖x−y‖² is replaced by the same curved squared distance with the same frozen weight β.
SPECIFICATION: c = 1 gauge-fixed (the claim is never that the numeric curvature matters, only that the curved displacement is the carrier on which the behaviour anisotropy acts as a pure reciprocal image of the position). exp_0(v) = tanh(√c‖v‖)·v/(√c‖v‖). Scale s = 0.9/r99 with r99 the 99th percentile of ‖h_i‖ over the 24556 training items at the frozen text initialisation, so the train catalogue sits inside Poincaré radius 0.9. Architecture frozen: 3 levels, 256 codewords, SID length 3, all loss weights frozen. Behaviour table, built ONCE before Stage 2 from the Stage-2 training split: immediate successor pairs (u_t→u_{t+1}) per sequence; n_ij counts; n_i = Σ_j n_ij; v_j = s·h_j⁰ (initial frozen text embedding); m_i⁺ = Σ_{j∈S_i}(n_ij/n_i)v_j; K_i = min(n_i,64) items sampled without replacement with fixed seed 1234 from the train catalogue excluding {i}∪S_i, weighted by inverse train popularity w_k = N/pop_k; m_i⁻ = Σ w_k v_k / Σ w_k; u_i = m_i⁺−m_i⁻; û_i = u_i/(‖u_i‖+1e-8); s_i = ‖u_i‖/(‖m_i⁺‖+‖m_i⁻‖+1e-8) ∈ [0,1] by the triangle inequality; support gate γ_i = n_i/(n_i+n_med), n_med = median train out-degree; λ_i = γ_i·s_i; M_i = I + λ_i û_iû_iᵀ (eigenvalues 1 and ≤2, condition number ≤2). Quantizer forward: r⁽¹⁾ = s·h_i; for l = 1..3: x⁽ˡ⁾ = exp_0(r⁽ˡ⁾), y_k⁽ˡ⁾ = exp_0(e_k⁽ˡ⁾), k_l = argmin_k d_M(x⁽ˡ⁾,y_k⁽ˡ⁾;i) with λ_i used for l∈{2,3} and λ = 0 at l = 1 (the coarse prefix is not disturbed); r⁽ˡ⁺¹⁾ = r⁽ˡ⁾ − e_{k_l}⁽ˡ⁾, residual bookkeeping identical to the frozen recipe. Loss: L = L_recon,frozen + β·Σ_l d_M(x⁽ˡ⁾, y_{k_l}⁽ˡ⁾;i)², frozen β, straight-through gradient into h_i. Codebook updates remain the frozen recipe's own — no Fréchet mean, no barycentre move, no re-centring.
COST: Per step at the frozen scale (1023 items per micro-batch, 24 steps per epoch): one exp_0 over 1023 items, then per level 1023×256 Möbius additions plus rank-1 quadratic forms, about 3×8.4M = 25M elementwise terms at d = 32 (≈50M at d = 64), i.e. roughly 0.5–1.5 ms per step on four GPUs, plus a 24556×d scalar table read. Epoch time is expected at 0.160–0.170 s versus 0.150 s (≤ +12%), so 3000 epochs ≈ 480–510 s, inside the Stage-2 budget. One-time contrast precompute: ≈250k successor pairs plus 24556×64 inverse-propensity control terms, a few seconds, once. No new parameters, no new loss weight, no extra codewords, +3 MB of memory.
HYPOTHESIS: Two things must hold: (i) the successor-versus-non-neighbour contrast direction is the recall-relevant behaviour direction, so tilting the assignment metric along û_i separates the textual near-duplicates the frozen recipe collapses; (ii) the curved carrier (Möbius displacement plus artanh) makes the tilt act more strongly on core items than on saturated boundary items, so the effect is a genuine geometric interaction and not a flat Euclidean rescaling. Observation: the fraction of items whose level-2/level-3 codeword differs from the λ = 0 reference assignment (computed offline once at epoch 0, no training) should be ≥15%; distinct L2/L3 prefix counts should not fall below the frozen baseline's 230/1251/1298; and Stage3 test_recall@10 should exceed 0.065 with recall@5 and ndcg@10 moving the same way.
RISKS: The anisotropy is bounded (condition number 2) and may be too weak to change any deep assignment. Conversely, with 24556 items over 256 codewords a tilt can empty cells and coarsen the perpendicular directions — precisely the merging failure of both earlier rounds. The contrast direction is computed once on the initial frozen geometry and may drift from the trained encoder's geometry. The artanh clamp bounds but does not remove commitment gradient spikes near the boundary. With the architecture frozen there is no spare slot to absorb items pushed into a crowded cell. The anisotropic distance is a quasi-metric (no triangle inequality for λ>0), harmless for argmin and for the quadratic commitment form but fatal to any argument that treats it as a metric.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate-successor transitions from the Stage-2 training split, converted into a per-item, popularity-neutralised contrast direction between the successor tangent mean and the non-neighbour tangent mean, plus the item's successor support count.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Pairs (u_t→u_{t+1}) extracted from training sequences only; counts n_ij and out-degree n_i accumulated over the 24556 training items; m_i⁺ is the count-weighted mean of the successors' initial frozen text embeddings mapped into the origin tangent frame as v = s·h⁰; m_i⁻ is the inverse-popularity-weighted mean of min(n_i,64) seeded non-neighbours excluding i and its successors, with popularity measured on the training split; u_i = m_i⁺−m_i⁻; s_i = ‖u_i‖/(‖m_i⁺‖+‖m_i⁻‖+1e-8); γ_i = n_i/(n_i+n_med); λ_i = γ_i·s_i. The result is a 24556×(d+1) array (û_i, λ_i) built once and frozen for the whole run.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The table is built only from Stage-2 training split co-occurrence and training-split popularity; validation and test interactions are never read. No validation or test statistic enters the construction and no constant is read off a validation curve: c = 1 by gauge, s from the training-catalogue 99th percentile radius, 64 = control-sample cap, seed 1234, n_med = train median out-degree, the 1e-3 boundary clamp, and the condition-number bound 2 implied by λ∈[0,1] are all a priori or train-measured. Prefix counts, coherence and adjacency diagnostics are logged after evaluation for reporting only and never gate, stop or adjust anything.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1 (radius 1/√c = 1), used as the Poincaré exponential map exp_0(v) = tanh(√c‖v‖)v/(√c‖v‖), Möbius addition (−x)⊕_c y, and the geodesic-distance kernel d = (2/√c)·artanh(√(c·zᵀMz)) built on that Möbius displacement.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside quantization: the codeword-selection rule and the commitment term of levels 2 and 3 of the frozen 3-level, 256-codeword RQ-VAE, acting on the ball images of the encoder output and the codewords. Level 1 keeps λ = 0 under the same curved kernel; codebook updates are untouched.
COUPLING PATH: The behaviour table sets a per-item metric M_i; M_i enters the Möbius displacement used by the argmin that produces the level-2 and level-3 tokens; those same tokens define the curved commitment target that sends gradient into the encoder. Removing the behaviour term sets M_i = I and changes every deep token; removing the curvature replaces the Möbius/artanh kernel by a Euclidean norm and changes the codeword ordering for every item off the origin. Neither term is a separate loss: both live in the single expression that decides k_l and in its gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: d_M(x,y;i) = (2/√c)·artanh( √( c · ((−x)⊕_c y)ᵀ (I + λ_i û_iû_iᵀ) ((−x)⊕_c y) ) ), with (−x)⊕_c y = (((1 + 2c⟨x,y⟩ + c‖y‖²)x + (1 − c‖x‖²)y) / (1 + 2c⟨x,y⟩ + c²‖x‖²‖y‖²)), λ_i = γ_i·‖m_i⁺−m_i⁻‖/(‖m_i⁺‖+‖m_i⁻‖+1e-8), and k_l = argmin_k d_M(x⁽ˡ⁾, exp_0(e_k⁽ˡ⁾); i).
OBSERVABLES: (1) fraction of training items whose level-2 and level-3 codewords differ from the λ = 0 reference assignment computed once at epoch 0 (target ≥15%); (2) distinct L1/L2/L3 prefix counts against the frozen baseline's 230/1251/1298; (3) per-level codebook usage entropy and unused-codeword count; (4) the λ_i distribution over the catalogue (must be non-degenerate: median > 0 with a non-empty high-support tail); (5) Poincaré radius quantiles of items and codewords during training (no boundary pile-up); (6) Stage3 test_recall@10, recall@5, ndcg@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: λ_i := 0 for all i, so M_i = I and the kernel is the isotropic Poincaré distance; the curved exp/Möbius/artanh path and the commitment form are unchanged. Present in code behind a flag; not trained this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c → 0 limit: x⁽ˡ⁾ = r⁽ˡ⁾, y_k = e_k, d = √((y−x)ᵀM_i(y−x)) with the same per-item M_i, commitment = squared M-norm, no exp_0, no Möbius addition, no artanh. Present in code behind a flag; not trained this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the mechanism as specified — c = 1 Poincaré kernel with per-item M_i = I + λ_i û_iû_iᵀ at levels 2 and 3, curved commitment with the frozen β. This is the single trained arm: 3000 epochs, four-GPU DDP, global batch 4096, no early stopping, then the frozen 150-epoch Stage3 and one test_recall@10 measurement.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Precompute (once, train split only): v_j = s·h_j⁰; m_i⁺ = Σ_{j∈S_i}(n_ij/n_i)v_j; K_i = seeded sample of min(n_i,64) items, w_k = N/pop_k; m_i⁻ = Σ_{k∈K_i} w_k v_k / Σ_{k∈K_i} w_k; u_i = m_i⁺−m_i⁻; û_i = u_i/(‖u_i‖+1e-8); s_i = ‖u_i‖/(‖m_i⁺‖+‖m_i⁻‖+1e-8); γ_i = n_i/(n_i+n_med); λ_i = γ_i·s_i. Forward per item: r ← s·h; for l = 1,2,3: x ← exp0(r); for k = 1..256: y_k ← exp0(e_kˡ); z ← mobius_add(−x, y_k); q ← ‖z‖² + (l≥2)·λ_i·(z·û_i)²; u ← clamp(c·q, max = 1−1e-3); d_k ← (2/√c)·atanh(√u); k_l ← argmin_k d_k; r ← r − e_{k_l}ˡ. Loss: L ← L_recon,frozen + β·mean_{i,l} d_{k_l}(x⁽ˡ⁾)², straight-through into h. Output: tokens (k_1,k_2,k_3) and the modified encoder gradient.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs — the 24556×d frozen text embedding matrix h⁰, the minibatch encoder output h_i (1023 items), the three 256×d codebooks, the train-transition table, the frozen curvature c = 1 and the frozen commitment weight β. Outputs — level-2 and level-3 token ids, one curved commitment scalar per item, and through it the gradient with respect to the encoder output; the codebook update is the frozen recipe's own.
SAFEGUARDS: (1) clamp c·zᵀM_i z at 1−1e-3 before artanh, keeping the argument below 1 and the commitment gradient bounded; (2) M_i is symmetric positive definite with condition number ≤2 by construction (λ∈[0,1]), so no ill-conditioned quadratic form; (3) λ_i = 0 exactly when ‖u_i‖ < 1e-8 or n_i = 0, so cold items fall back to the isotropic kernel instead of receiving a random direction; (4) no term in the loss is minimised at the origin — the commitment is a distance to an assigned codeword, exactly as in the frozen recipe — so round one's collapse mode cannot be induced here, and Poincaré radius quantiles are logged to detect drift; (5) dead-codeword and usage-entropy logging detects cell emptying caused by the tilt; (6) all Möbius arithmetic in float32 with a 1e-8 denominator epsilon; (7) the behaviour table is built once and frozen, so no train-time feedback loop exists inside it.
END IDEA
