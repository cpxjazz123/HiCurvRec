IDEA: HOLO-TILT-L3
TITLE: Behaviour-contrast holonomy tilt of the deepest residual codebook in a Lorentz RQ-VAE
MOTIVATION: Both negative rounds moved item points toward a behaviour hub, which is algebraically a linear tilt of the assignment target and provably merges prefixes (L3 2201 -> 1298 cost 0.0027 recall). The frozen baseline (~0.0679) keeps a fine deep partition. What is missing is resolution in the direction the behaviour signal actually lives: the deepest level must be re-angled toward each item's successor-contrast direction, because the round-three diagnostics show the radial direction is group compactness, not hierarchy.
NOVELTY: An ISOMETRY OF THE TANGENT FRAME instead of a movement of points. No radial objective (so the round-one origin collapse is structurally absent), no successor Fréchet codebook, no per-level curvature control, no radial behaviour ranking, no branching MoE. The curved object is the holonomy (geodesic-triangle area) of the Lorentz model used as a rotation angle; the behaviour object is the per-item successor-contrast direction used only as a rotation plane. Capacity is added exactly where the evidence says it was lost: levels 2 and 3 go from 256 to 512 codewords, with the 3-token SID length and the frozen Stage-3 protocol untouched.
CHANGE: At level 3 only, each item's residual tangent vector is rotated by an angle equal to the hyperbolic area of the triangle (origin, prefix point, unit contrast probe), inside the plane spanned by the radial direction and that item's successor-contrast direction; the rotated query is matched against the ordinary residual codebook, which stays in one canonical frame.
SPECIFICATION: c gauge-fixed to 1. Levels [256,512,512]. Levels 1-2 and all losses are the frozen recipe. Level 3: k* = argmax_k [ <Rot_i r_i, w_k>_p - ||w_k||_p^2/2 ], with r_i = log_p(x_i), w_k = log_p(e_k), p the level-2 prefix point; Rot_i is the SO(2) rotation of angle theta_i = pi/2 - alpha - beta in the orthonormal pair (rho, u_perp), alpha = atan(tanh(b)/sinh(a_i)), beta = atan(tanh(a_i)/sinh(b)), a_i = d_L(o,x_i), b = median_i d_L(o,x_i) measured once after epoch 1 and then frozen. The frame (theta_i, rho, u_perp) is detached from autograd.
COST: Precompute of u_i once (seconds). Per step one O(d) rotation plus one Gram-Schmidt per item: ~1e6 flops per step at 24 steps/epoch and 3000 epochs, under 1% of the measured 0.145-0.16 s/epoch; plus 2 x 256 x d codebook parameters (~16k floats). Estimated 0.15-0.155 s/epoch, inside the four-GPU budget.
HYPOTHESIS: If the deep fan is tilted toward successor-contrast, the extra 512-codeword level-3 capacity acts as discriminative tokens, distinct L3 prefixes exceed the round-one 2201 and Stage-3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065; if the tilt is only assignment noise, prefixes stay flat and recall falls below the frozen baseline.
RISKS: The reconstruction objective can shrink the level-3 residual until the tilt is inert. Behaviour is item-specific while the codebook is shared, so the codebook cannot rotate for all items and reconstruction loss rises. theta reaching ~45-50 degrees at depth can turn the deepest token into an unusable extra bit. Items with no outgoing transitions get no tilt, splitting the population. Stage 3 may find a behaviour-tilted L3 harder to generate than a reconstruction residual.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Successor-contrast direction per training item: the mean frozen text embedding of its high-mass directed successors minus the mean of popularity-matched non-successors, mapped into the latent tangent at the origin.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Stage-2 TRAIN split only. m_i is the smallest prefix of i's outgoing transitions covering at least 50% of i's total outgoing mass (a derived count, not tuned); Succ(i) = those m_i successors; Control(i) = m_i items drawn deterministically by a hash of (i, m_i) from the pool with train popularity within +-20% of the mean successor popularity and zero transition count from i; u_i = normalize(P(mean_{Succ} t_j - mean_{Control} t_j)), with P the frozen text-to-latent linear map and t_j the frozen text embeddings. Items with no outgoing transitions get u_i = 0. Computed once before Stage 2 and frozen.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only train-split transitions, train-split popularity and frozen text embeddings enter u_i; validation and test items, interactions and metrics are never read; every codebook and the SID vocabulary are fitted on train items only, so no validation or test interaction can reach the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1 on the Lorentz hyperboloid (a gauge fixing, since curvature is a gauge) and the holonomy angle of a geodesic triangle, equal to its hyperbolic area pi/2 - alpha - beta, acting as an SO(2) rotation of T_p.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the level-3 quantization step of Stage-2 training, between the encoder residual and the codebook match; not in the loss, not in the codebook parameterisation, not in Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: u_i (train-only behaviour) -> rotation plane in T_p -> holonomy angle theta_i from the curvature-1 triangle -> Rot_i applied to r_i -> level-3 code index k* -> 3-token SID -> Stage 3. Setting u_i = 0 makes theta_i = 0 and Rot_i the identity; taking c -> 0 makes the triangle area zero and Rot_i the identity under a Euclidean metric. Only the both-on path changes the deep partition.
COUPLING EQUATION: k*_3(i) = argmin_k d_L( x_i , exp_p( Rot( log_p(e_k) ; theta_i, rho, u_i^perp ) ) ) = argmax_k [ <Rot_i r_i, w_k>_p - ||w_k||_p^2/2 ], the behaviour term supplying u_i^perp and the curved term supplying theta_i and the metric.
OBSERVABLES: Level-3 indices change for every item with u_i != 0 (expected >90% of items); distinct L3 prefixes and level-3 usage entropy rise above the round-one 2201 (and round-two 1298); mean level-3 residual norm stays above 0.7x the round-one value (mechanism not inert); behaviour-conditioned adjacency and transfer-predictability diagnostics are recorded but not gated; final judgement is Stage-3 test_recall@10 against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: u_i = 0 for all items, so theta_i = 0 and Rot_i = I; curvature 1, the Lorentz metric and the [256,512,512] capacity are kept; the level-3 partition is the plain shared-codebook partition.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c -> 0, i.e. the standard Euclidean RQ-VAE with the same [256,512,512] capacity, Euclidean assignment and Euclidean residual; the triangle area and hence the tilt vanish identically.
ABLATION BOTH ON: curvature-1 Lorentz assignment plus the successor-contrast holonomy tilt on every item with u_i != 0, capacity [256,512,512], frozen losses and frozen Stage-3 protocol. This is the only arm trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: <x,y>_L = -x0 y0 + x.y, <x,x>_L = -1, o = (1,0,...,0), d_L(x,y) = arccosh(-<x,y>_L). log_p(y) = (d/|y - cosh(d) p|)(y - cosh(d) p); exp_p(v) = cosh(|v|) p + sinh(|v|) v/|v|. Radial unit tangent: rho = sinh(D) o + coth(D)(p - p0 o), D = arccosh(p0). Transport: PT_{o->p}(v) = v + (<p,v>_L/(1 - <o,p>_L))(p + o). v_i = PT(u_i); u_perp = v_i - <v_i,rho>_L rho; if ||u_perp||_L < 1e-8 then Rot = I. a_i = clip(d_L(o,x_i), 1e-4, 30); b = clip(median_i d_L(o,x_i), 1e-4, 30); alpha = atan(tanh(b)/sinh(a_i)); beta = atan(tanh(a_i)/sinh(b)); theta_i = clip(pi/2 - alpha - beta, 0, 1.5). w_par = <w,rho> rho + <w,u_perp> u_perp; Rot(w) = (w - w_par) + cos(theta) w_par + sin(theta)(<w,rho> u_perp - <w,u_perp> rho). q_i = Rot_i^{-1}(r_i) with the frame detached; k* = argmin_k ||q_i - w_k||_p^2; reconstruction point exp_p(Rot_i^{-1}(w_k*)) with the frozen straight-through estimator.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: train transition counts, train popularity, frozen text embeddings and map P, the level-2 prefix point p, the encoder point x_i, the 512-entry level-3 codebook, the measured b. Outputs: level-3 code index k* (the third SID token), the rotated reconstruction point for the STE, and per-epoch diagnostics (theta histogram, mean |r_3|, distinct L3 prefix count, level-3 usage entropy).
SAFEGUARDS: theta is in [0, pi/2) by construction and clipped to [0, 1.5]; Rot = I whenever u_i = 0, a_i < 1e-6 or u_perp degenerates, so the fallback is exactly the baseline assignment; a_i and b are clamped to [1e-4, 30] to keep sinh/tanh finite; the frame is detached, so the rotation is a constant isometry that cannot amplify gradients or be cancelled by the encoder learning; no objective term is minimised at the ball's origin, so the round-one collapse mode is structurally absent; distinct L1 prefix count (~250 healthy) and mean level-3 residual norm are monitored as collapse/inertness checks; all diagnostics are recorded, never used as admission gates.
END IDEA
