IDEA: BCMID-L3-RQ
TITLE: Successor-contrast Lorentz geodesic midpoint address with a 512-code curved L3 assignment
MOTIVATION: Round two moved every item toward a popularity-adjusted behaviour hub; prefix capacity fell to 230/1251/1298 and test_recall@10 fell to 0.0517. Round one kept 2201 L3 prefixes and reached 0.0544. The one consistent signal across both negatives is that recall tracks deep-prefix capacity. In the frozen 3-level 256-code quantizer there are 1769 distinct L2 pairs but only 2201 distinct L3 triples at 24556 items, i.e. 1.24 L3 codes per L2 pair: the third token is almost a deterministic function of the second, so the ~13.9 items sharing each L2 pair are behaviourally indistinguishable to the recommender. The deepest level is the cheapest place to buy behaviour-resolvable capacity, and no earlier round put behaviour there without also moving the points.
NOVELTY: (i) Geometry acts on the ADDRESS used for the code decision and on the decision boundary, never on the item positions: r_i is untouched, so the mechanism cannot merge cells by displacement as round two did. (ii) The behaviour object is a CENTRED successor-contrast direction (train transition row minus the global successor marginal, then SVD-truncated and L2-normalised), which structurally deletes the popularity/hub component that destroyed round two; contrast, not co-consumption mass. (iii) The curved operations are an exact Lorentz geodesic midpoint and the Klein power diagram (hyperbolic Voronoi), not a Frechet codebook update, not Sinkhorn, not a curvature-per-level controller, not successor-profile smoothing, not a cone, not a radial rank. (iv) L3 codewords go 256 -> 512, but the extra 256 codes are only usable because behaviour supplies a direction; capacity alone would just duplicate near-identical residuals.
CHANGE: At RQ level 3 only, the code index is chosen for a geodesic midpoint of the content residual point and a per-item behaviour point in the unit Poincare ball, against 512 codewords, by exact hyperbolic nearest-codeword; the RQ-VAE loss is unchanged, so the encoder is reshaped through the ordinary straight-through/commitment path and the token itself carries the behaviour.
SPECIFICATION: c = 1 (curvature -1, fixed); d = frozen RQ-VAE latent width.
OFFLINE (train split, once): A_ij = adjacent (i->j) counts over train sequences; T_i = A_i/max(1,sum_j A_ij); pi = (sum_i A_i)/sum_ij A_ij; A~_i = T_i - pi; SVD A~ = U S V^T, keep m = smallest rank with cumulative squared singular energy >= 0.90 (reported), S_i = U_i S; S^_i = S_i/||S_i|| (0 if ||S_i|| < 1e-8). Fixed P in R^{d x m}, seed 0, N(0,1)/sqrt(m); h_i = P S^_i; R_b = median_i ||h_i||. Cache b_i = tanh(||h_i||/(2 R_b)) * h^_i for all 24556 train items (fixed buffer, no gradient).
APERTURE (once, after encoder init, train items only): R = median_i ||r_i^(3)||, r_i^(3) = z_i - e_{k1} - e_{k2}.
PER STEP, batch B: x_i = exp_0^1(r_i^(3)/R) = tanh(||r_i||/(2R)) r^_i with ||r_i||/R clamped to 3.0; a_i = Mid_L(x_i, b_i); q_k = exp_0^1(e_k/R), k = 1..512 at L3 only (L1, L2 stay 256); K(a) = 2a/(1+||a||^2); gamma_k = (1-||K(q_k)||^2)^(-1/2); k_i = argmax_k gamma_k (<K(a_i),K(q_k)> - 1); quantized L3 vector = e_{k_i} straight-through; loss unchanged.
COST: offline (transition counts, rank-90% SVD, behaviour cache) ~10 s once. Per step at 1024 items/card: score matmul 1024x512x64 = 33.5M MAC (~0.07 GFLOP) plus ~1.3 MFLOP for exp/log/Klein/midpoint, i.e. <1% of a 6.0-6.7 ms step; per epoch 0.150 -> ~0.151 s, 3000 epochs ~450 -> ~452 s on four cards. Extra memory: 24556x64 behaviour cache (~6 MB) and 256x64 extra codewords. Stage3 adds 256 output-embedding rows at position 3 only.
HYPOTHESIS: The behaviour-resolved L3 address makes the third token informative, so distinct L3 prefixes rise well above 2201 (target >= 4000, mean L3 codes per L2 pair >= 2) with all 512 L3 codes occupied, within-prefix train successor entropy falls, and Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065 while recall@5 and ndcg@10 rise with it. It is worth keeping if the rise in distinct triples and the behaviour-induced L3 flip fraction coexist with the recall gain; if triples do not rise, the mechanism is falsified by its own observable.
RISKS: The Lorentz midpoint contracts radii, which can push addresses toward the centre where curvature is weak and make geometry-off and both-on nearly identical (median address radius must be reported). The commitment/codebook terms may drag e_k back onto r_i and erase behaviour control at equilibrium (measured by the flip fraction). A 512-token level-3 vocabulary may be harder for Stage3 to learn. Centring does not fully remove blockbusters from the contrast signature. Klein cells are unbalanced, so some of the 512 codes may own almost no items. L3 reconstruction error will rise slightly.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Successor-contrast signature of each item: its train transition row minus the global train successor marginal, truncated to its dominant spectral directions and L2-normalised, then linearly embedded in the SID latent space; plus its norm-matched Poincare image.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Directed adjacent item-item transition counts A_ij from TRAIN sequences only (before any Stage3 run), row-normalised, mean-centred by the global successor marginal pi, factorised by truncated SVD on the train matrix, projected by a fixed seed-0 Gaussian map and scaled by the measured median norm R_b. Items with zero train out-transitions get S^_i = 0 and fall back to the content address.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: A_ij, pi, the SVD basis, the projection, R and R_b are all computed from the training split only and frozen before Stage 2; validation/test items and their interactions never enter the count matrix, the basis, the reference medians or the SID assignment; Stage 3 keeps its frozen protocol and only consumes the resulting SIDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Negative curvature c = 1: Poincare exponential map exp_0^1, the exact Lorentz-model geodesic midpoint (normalised Minkowski sum of hyperboloid lifts), the Klein projection K(a) = 2a/(1+||a||^2), and the Klein power-diagram score gamma_k(<K(a),K(q_k)> - 1), which equals -gamma_a * cosh(sqrt(c) d_c(a,q_k)) and is therefore exact hyperbolic nearest-codeword.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the level-3 quantization/assignment step of the RQ-VAE during Stage 2 training: it decides the third SID token of every item on every step, and through the unchanged commitment and reconstruction losses it also re-shapes the encoder that produces all three residuals.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour signature -> Poincare behaviour point -> Lorentz geodesic midpoint with the level-3 content residual point -> Klein power-diagram score against the level-3 codebook -> argmax -> third SID token -> straight-through commitment/reconstruction gradient -> encoder residuals -> all three prefixes. Removing behaviour sets the address to the content point; removing curvature replaces the midpoint and the score by their Euclidean counterparts; either changes which tokens exist.
COUPLING EQUATION: a_i = Mid_Lorentz(exp_0^1(r_i^(3)/R), exp_0^1(P S^_i / R_b)); k_i = argmax_k (1-||K(q_k)||^2)^(-1/2) (<K(a_i), K(q_k)> - 1), where P S^_i is the centred successor-contrast behaviour term and the exp/Klein/gamma factors are the curved term, so a behaviour offset flips a token only through the curvature-weighted margin gamma_k(1-||K||^2).
OBSERVABLES: distinct L1/L2/L3 prefixes (expect L3 >= 4000), mean distinct L3 per L2 pair (expect >= 2), L3 code occupancy over 512 (expect no dead codes, lower Gini), within-prefix train successor entropy (expect lower at L3), L3 geodesic reconstruction error (expect a small rise), median address radius versus median content radius (curvature liveness), end-of-training behaviour flip fraction (fraction of items whose L3 token changes when S^_i is zeroed in a single diagnostic forward pass; expect >= 20%), and Stage3 test_recall@10 / recall@5 / ndcg@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: S^_i is set to zero for every item, so a_i = x_i and the L3 token is decided purely by curvature against residual geometry; this run would show whether the behaviour direction, not the extra 256 codes, is what fills the new tokens.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp_0, the Lorentz midpoint and the Klein power diagram are replaced by the identity, the arithmetic mean (x_i+b_i)/2 and Euclidean argmin ||a_i - e_k/R||^2, keeping 512 L3 codes and the same behaviour term; this run would show whether the 1/(1-||K||^2) margin weighting, i.e. the curvature, is what converts behaviour contrast into a usable partition.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run above: behaviour address, Lorentz midpoint, Klein power diagram, 512 L3 codes, standard RQ-VAE loss. Only this arm is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: L(x) = ((1+||x||^2)/(1-||x||^2), 2x/(1-||x||^2)); <u,v>_M = -u_0 v_0 + u.v; m = (L(x)+L(y))/sqrt(max(-<L(x)+L(y),L(x)+L(y)>_M, 1e-8)); a = m_{1:}/(1+m_0); clamp ||a|| <= 1-1e-3; K(a) = 2a/(1+||a||^2); gamma_k = (1-||K(q_k)||^2)^(-1/2); S_ik = gamma_k(<K(a_i),K(q_k)>-1); k_i = argmax_k S_ik; check cosh(sqrt(c) d_c(a,q_k)) = -gamma_a S_ik, so the rule is exact.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the level-3 residual r_i^(3), the cached behaviour signature S^_i, the level-3 codebook e_k (k = 1..512) and the two measured medians R and R_b. Outputs are the level-3 code index k_i for every train item (the third SID token), the midpoint address a_i (diagnostic), and the unchanged RQ-VAE loss.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||r_i^(3)/R|| and ||h_i/R_b|| to 3.0 so the ball point stays at radius <= 0.905; floor the Minkowski norm at 1e-8 and fall back to a_i = x_i if it is violated; clamp ||a_i|| <= 1-1e-3; floor (1-||K(q_k)||^2) at 1e-8; zero-transition items get S^_i = 0 so their token is pure content; the address transform is a deterministic re-parameterisation and not an objective, so its minimum is not at the ball origin and the encoder cannot collapse toward the origin through it (the standard reconstruction term remains the only attractor); report median address radius, L3 occupancy and the behaviour flip fraction every 500 epochs as diagnostics only, with no early stopping and no arm switched off during the run.
