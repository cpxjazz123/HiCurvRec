IDEA: MITOSIS-GEO-BEHL3
TITLE: Behaviour-contrast geodesic mitosis doubles the level-3 alphabet by splitting each cell along its behaviour-weighted tangent principal geodesic, with no token added and no point moved.
MOTIVATION: The two negative rounds both lost recall exactly when they lost deep prefix capacity (2201 -> 1298 distinct L3 prefixes, 0.0544 -> 0.0517), and both lost it because they displaced coordinates: round one chained a curved residual, round two pushed items toward popularity-adjusted successor barycentres. Every SID-level mechanism tried so far MERGES cells. Meanwhile the frozen baseline sits at 0.0679 and the gate is 0.065, so what is missing is not a better-shaped manifold but more distinguishable deep codes per item, obtained from a signal the generator can actually use next. Behaviour contrast (how unusual an item's successor distribution is inside its own cell) is the behaviour statistic that tracks recall in this programme (+0.7104 plain, +0.7170 Euclidean control, versus -0.12 for Gromov-delta weighting and -0.075 for ancestor-depth weighting), so it is the right thing to spend the extra capacity on.
NOVELTY: Not a displacement mechanism. No latent is moved, no centroid is updated, no residual is re-chained, no curvature is fitted per level, no radial or hierarchy-depth term is used, and no loss is added. It is a discrete, deterministic RELABELLING that refines the last level of the existing RQ-VAE SID: each level-3 codeword is mitotically split into two children whose boundary is a geodesic through the cell's own hyperbolic Frechet mean, oriented by a behaviour-contrast-weighted tangent scatter. Because the refinement is a split and not a move, it cannot merge prefixes and it cannot collapse the encoder (there is no objective whose minimum is the origin). Against rounds one and two, and against the three earlier proposals that were probed but never run (successor-Frechet codebook updates, per-level feedback curvature, behaviour-ranked radial assignment), the coupling position is different: geometry and behaviour meet inside the construction of the level-3 TOKEN itself, at quantization time.
CHANGE: After and repeatedly during Stage-2 training, every occupied level-3 cell is split in two: the split direction is the leading eigenvector of a tangent-space scatter around the cell's hyperbolic Frechet mean, weighted by each item's behaviour contrast, and the child bit is the item's side of a geodesic bisector between two anchors placed at +/- a median-radius step along that direction. The level-3 vocabulary becomes 512 (code 2*c3 + bit) while the SID stays three tokens long.
SPECIFICATION: Notation. The frozen encoder gives every train item i a latent z_i in R^d (d = frozen latent width) and a trained three-level SID (c1_i, c2_i, c3_i) with c3 in [0,256). r_i = z_i - (e1[c1_i] + e2[c2_i]) is the level-3 residual; s = sqrt(mean_i ||r_i||^2) over train items. Map into the unit Poincare ball with curvature c = 1 (fixed; curvature is a gauge, so it is declared, never tuned): u_i = exp_0(r_i / s), clamped to ||u_i|| <= 1 - 1e-5. Step A (behaviour contrast, train split only): from train user sequences sorted by timestamp, count directed consecutive pairs c_ij (i -> j), drop self loops; p_i is the successor distribution over train items. For a level-3 cell k with member set M_k and cell mean profile sbar_k = (1/|M_k|) sum_{i in M_k} p_i (L1-normalised), define w_i = 1 - cos(p_i, sbar_k) in [0,2]. This is a CONTRAST: an item whose successors differ from its own cell's typical successors gets a large weight, an item that behaves like its cell gets near zero; no popularity reweighting, no radial term, no delta weighting. Step B (curved mean): m_k = Karcher mean of {u_i : i in M_k} under d_P, 5 Riemannian gradient iterations initialised at the Euclidean mean re-projected into the ball; if the iteration does not decrease the Frechet variance, fall back to the re-projected arithmetic mean. Step C (behaviour-weighted tangent scatter and principal geodesic): with L_i = log_{m_k}(u_i), S_k = (sum_i w_i L_i L_i^T)/(sum_i w_i), v_k = leading eigenvector, sign fixed so that sum_i w_i <L_i, v_k> >= 0 (removes the +/- ambiguity and makes the token reproducible); if sum_i w_i < 1e-6 use uniform weights (numerical guard, not a knob). Step D (derived step, anchors, bisector bit): tau_k = 0.5 * median_{i in M_k} d_P(m_k, u_i); a_k^+ = exp_{m_k}(+tau_k v_k), a_k^- = exp_{m_k}(-tau_k v_k), both clamped inside the ball; score_i = d_P(u_i, a_k^-) - d_P(u_i, a_k^+); theta_k = median_{i in M_k} score_i (train members only); bit_i = 1[score_i > theta_k]; child level-3 code = 2*c3_i + bit_i, vocabulary 512. Refresh schedule: recompute A-D at epochs 0, 250, 500, ..., 3000 (13 times) and export the epoch-3000 assignment to Stage 3. Cold or interaction-free items are assigned by the same anchors and the same train-derived theta_k of their cell, so no item-specific validation information is needed.
COST: Stage 2 keeps 24 steps per epoch, 24556 items, 3000 epochs; the mitosis runs at 13 epoch boundaries outside the step loop. Per refresh about 5 Karcher sweeps (5 x 24556 x d), one scatter accumulation (24556 x d^2), 256 eigendecompositions of a d x d matrix, two exp maps and 2 x 24556 Poincare distances, roughly 8e7 flops, about 10-15 ms on one card plus a 3 MB DDP all-gather of latents. Total added Stage-2 time is about 0.2 s on top of ~450 s, i.e. 0.07 ms per epoch and ZERO added cost per step. Added parameters: level-3 token embeddings grow from 256 to 512 rows, about 16k at d_model = 64, under 0.5% of the model. Stage 3: identical sequence length (three tokens per item) and identical frozen protocol; only the level-3 vocabulary doubles, costing about 256 extra embedding rows and under 1% per step. No tuning, no sweep, no seed repeat: the only constants are c = 1 (gauge), 5 Karcher iterations (documented default), the 250-epoch refresh period (documented default, chosen so refresh cost stays under 0.1% of the budget), and tau_k / theta_k (measured medians, not chosen).
HYPOTHESIS: If depth-wise prefix capacity rather than manifold shape is the binding constraint (round one 2201 -> 0.0544 beats round two 1298 -> 0.0517), and if behaviour contrast is the behaviour statistic that carries recall signal, then spending extra capacity on a behaviour-oriented split raises Stage-3 test_recall@10 above the 0.065 gate and above the frozen 0.0679, because each item gets a finer deep code that separates it from cell-mates the generator would otherwise confuse, at no cost in context length. It is worth keeping if distinct level-3 prefixes roughly double while the level-3 token stays statistically learnable: train consecutive-pair bit agreement P(bit(i_{t+1}) = bit(i_t)) should exceed 0.5 and Stage-3 level-3 token accuracy should beat the 1/512 marginal.
RISKS: (1) The bit may be balanced but nearly unpredictable from context, so the 512-way level 3 dilutes the softmax and Stage 3 gets slower, noisier supervision; this is the main losing mode and would appear as level-3 token accuracy at chance with recall below baseline. (2) The contrast weight 1 - cos(p_i, sbar_k) compares against successor items that may themselves be hubs, so it can partly re-encode popularity; no de-biasing is applied, by design, because de-biasing was a round-two failure. (3) If a cell is text-geometrically isotropic, v_k is nearly arbitrary and the mechanism degenerates to plain capacity doubling, still the intended direction but a smaller gain. (4) Median thresholds balance counts, not behaviour mass, so a child can be behaviourally incoherent. (5) Doubling the level-3 alphabet interacts with the frozen Stage-3 embedding initialisation, written for 256 symbols.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only directed item-transition contrast, i.e. the behavioural atypicality of an item's outgoing successor distribution inside its own level-3 cell.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the train split only: sort every train user's item sequence by timestamp, count directed consecutive pairs (i -> j) with self loops removed, form p_i (the L1-normalised successor distribution) and the cell mean sbar_k, then w_i = 1 - cos(p_i, sbar_k). Successor sets, cell means, weights, anchors and thresholds are all estimated on train interactions; no validation or test edge is read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The behaviour term w_i, the cell means sbar_k, the Karcher means, v_k, tau_k and theta_k are computed exclusively from train-split interactions and from the Stage-2 encoder, which itself sees only train items. Validation and test interactions are never used, not even for popularity. Items with no train interaction get a bit from their own latent and their cell's TRAIN median theta_k, so no item's validation neighbourhood can influence its own SID. The exported SID table is frozen before Stage 3 is launched.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed unit negative curvature c = 1 (declared as a gauge, never tuned), with the Poincare distance d_P, exponential and logarithmic maps at a per-cell hyperbolic Frechet (Karcher) mean m_k, and a geodesic bisector between the two anchors exp_{m_k}(+/- tau_k v_k).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: At quantization time, in the level-3 assignment stage of the RQ-VAE SID construction, refreshed 13 times during Stage-2 training at 250-epoch boundaries. It acts on the code indices Stage 3 consumes, not on the encoder loss.
COUPLING PATH: train transitions -> per-item behaviour contrast w_i -> behaviour-weighted tangent scatter at the cell's hyperbolic Frechet mean -> principal geodesic direction v_k -> two child anchors on that geodesic -> geodesic-bisector score -> level-3 child bit -> the token. Removing the behaviour term (w_i = 1) rotates the split into the plain variance direction; removing the curved operations (arithmetic mean, subtraction, L2) replaces the bisector with a Euclidean hyperplane; either change rewrites which items get child 0 and which get child 1, so the emitted SID changes.
COUPLING EQUATION: v_k = argmax_{||v||=1} sum_{i in M_k} w_beh(i) * <log_{m_k}(u_i), v>^2 ; bit_i = 1[ d_P(u_i, exp_{m_k}(-tau_k v_k)) - d_P(u_i, exp_{m_k}(+tau_k v_k)) > median_{j in M_k}(d_P(u_j, exp_{m_k}(-tau_k v_k)) - d_P(u_j, exp_{m_k}(+tau_k v_k))) ] ; SID_3(i) = 2*c3_i + bit_i.
OBSERVABLES: (a) distinct level-3 prefixes must be at least the pre-mitosis count and in practice about double (e.g. 2201 -> about 4400 in a round-one-like configuration), and 24556/n_distinct_SID must roughly halve (about 11 -> about 5.6 items per SID); (b) per-refresh logs must show both children non-empty in nearly every cell and a stable v_k sign; (c) prefix behaviour predictability on train pairs, P(bit(i_{t+1}) = bit(i_t)) and the conditional entropy of the bit given the level-2 prefix; (d) Stage-3 level-3 token accuracy against the 1/512 marginal; (e) the judged quantity, Stage-3 test_recall@10 against 0.0679 (frozen baseline), 0.0544 (round one) and 0.0517 (round two), with recall@5 and ndcg@10 recorded alongside.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: w_i = 1 in Step C, so the split direction is the unweighted curved principal direction of the cell; every other step, including the geodesic anchors and the median cut, is unchanged. The switch exists and is wired; that arm is NOT trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: m_k becomes the arithmetic mean, log_{m_k} becomes subtraction, exp_{m_k} becomes addition, d_P becomes L2, and tau_k becomes 0.5 * median ||u_i - m_k||; the same weighted scatter and the same median cut are kept. The switch exists and is wired; that arm is NOT trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Steps A-D with the contrast weights and the Poincare operators together, the only arm trained, one run, 3000 epochs, four GPUs, global batch 4096, frozen Stage-3 protocol, no early stopping, no seed repeat, no sweep.
SPEC EQUATIONS: INPUT u_i (ball point of item i), c3_i (trained level-3 code), M_k for k in [0,256), w_i, d = latent width, c = 1, K = 5, period = 250. (1) for each occupied cell k: (2) m_k <- KarcherMean({u_i}, K, init = proj(mean(u_i))); (3) L_i <- log_{m_k}(u_i); (4) W <- sum_i w_i, and if W < 1e-6 set w_i <- 1 and W <- |M_k|; (5) S <- (1/W) sum_i w_i L_i L_i^T; (6) v_k <- top_eigvec(S), and if <sum_i w_i L_i, v_k> < 0 then v_k <- -v_k; (7) tau_k <- 0.5 * median_i d_P(m_k, u_i); (8) a+ <- exp_{m_k}(+tau_k v_k), a- <- exp_{m_k}(-tau_k v_k), clamp both to r_max; (9) score_i <- d_P(u_i, a-) - d_P(u_i, a+); (10) theta_k <- median_i score_i; (11) bit_i <- 1.0 * (score_i > theta_k); (12) SID_3(i) <- 2*c3_i + bit_i; (13) export (c1_i, c2_i, SID_3(i)) for every item, freeze, launch Stage 3.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the frozen-encoder ball latents u_i for all train items, the trained three-level codes (c1, c2, c3), the train-split successor counts, and the two ablation switches. Outputs are a 512-symbol level-3 code per item, hence a finer three-token SID table for Stage 3, plus per-cell logs (cell size, sum w, eigenvalue ratio, tau_k, theta_k, child counts, split-direction sign) used for the observables.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp every ball radius to 1 - 1e-5 before and after each exp map, including a+ and a-; use the numerically stable Poincare distance (acosh via log1p) with its argument clamped to >= 1; reject non-finite latents and send such items to bit 0; guard the Karcher iteration with a monotone Frechet-variance check and fall back to the re-projected arithmetic mean; uniform-weight fall back when sum w_i < 1e-6; deterministic sign convention on v_k and a fixed tie-break on the leading eigenvector for degenerate spectra (lowest-index basis vector of the residual complement); if a median cut leaves one child empty (all scores equal), force the single highest-w item into the empty child so both children are always populated; cells with |M_k| = 1 keep bit 0 and are logged; no radial term, no origin-attracting loss, no encoder gradient from this mechanism, so the round-one collapse mode cannot occur.
END IDEA
