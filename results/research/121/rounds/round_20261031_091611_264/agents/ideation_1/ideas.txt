IDEA: TOLLCAP-SID
TITLE: Behaviour-funded hyperbolic toll gate on a capacity-frozen SID transport (shape A: partition provably untouched, only cell membership of noise-floor items changes).
MOTIVATION: All three negative rounds lost recall by moving the objects the quantizer owns: round one bent the residual chain, round two displaced 99.1% of items off the text geometry, round three added a fourth argmin-pinned level. Recall tracked distance-from-text-geometry, not prefix capacity. So the remaining degree of freedom that has NOT been tested is cell MEMBERSHIP: the SID vocabulary, the codebooks and every prefix count can be held bit-identical while the assignment of a small set of boundary items to those existing cells is re-decided. This proposal takes shape (A) explicitly: it acts at quantization time, after the frozen Stage-2 RQ-VAE, as a capacity-frozen optimal transport inside each level-1 group in which a curved distance sets the toll of a relabel and the training-only successor PPMI decides whether the toll is worth paying.
NOVELTY: No earlier proposal here prices a relabel with a hyperbolic distance. Existing mechanisms move embeddings (geodesic shift to successor barycentres), move codebooks (Poincare-Frechet successor updates), change radial coordinates (context-entropy radial assignment), add cones, add levels, or set per-level curvature from transition scores. TOLLCAP-SID changes none of those: alpha, the codebooks, the cell set, the cell sizes and the token vocabulary are frozen and asserted equal to the baseline; the only things that can change are which existing cell a noise-floor item is named by. Curvature is load-bearing in exactly the one way the round's own measurements say is real - the radius/compactness term - because the Poincare chord factor 1/(1-||m_s||^2) makes entering a fine, boundary cell cost more than entering a central cell, so the curved metric acts as a price on the transport rather than as a displacement. It therefore cannot repeat prefix merging (counts frozen exactly), moving the input off the text geometry (codebooks and embeddings untouched, per-item toll bounded by the quantizer's own RMS error), or a fourth argmin level (no new level, no new token, identical SID vocabulary).
CHANGE: Replace the plain nearest-cell rule for a bounded set of boundary items by a capacity-frozen transport inside each level-1 group, where a move's cost is its Poincare distance increase minus lambda times its successor-PPMI advantage, and lambda = eps_rec/sigma_A is derived, not tuned.
SPECIFICATION:
(1) Freeze everything: text embeddings x_i, level codebooks C1..C3, baseline triple t(i)=(a,b,d), cell set S, and per-cell sizes n_s. All of these are read-only.
(2) alpha = 1/max_i ||x_i||; phi(x) = tanh(alpha||x||) * x/||x|| (unit Poincare ball, curvature -1 fixed, scale derived from the data, no curvature knob).
(3) d_P(p,q) = arcosh(1 + 2||p-q||^2/((1-||p||^2)(1-||q||^2))), evaluated in float64.
(4) Cell centroid m_s = phi(c1_a + c2_b + c3_d) for s=(a,b,d), fixed for the whole pass.
(5) eps_rec = sqrt(mean_i d_P(phi(x_i), m_t(i))^2), the quantizer's own noise floor.
(6) Training-only behaviour: A(i,s) = log((p(s|i)+eps_p)/(pi(s)+eps_p)) from directed consecutive training transitions, p(s|i)=sum_{j in s} p(j|i), pi(s)=sum_{j in s} q_j with q the global incoming share, eps_p = 1/(total transitions).
(7) lambda = eps_rec / sigma_A, sigma_A = std of {A(i,s): s in the same L1 group, s != t(i)}.
(8) Cost and admissibility, per item i and candidate cell s in the SAME level-1 group:
    toll(i,s) = d_P(phi(x_i), m_s) - d_P(phi(x_i), m_t(i));
    Delta(i,s) = toll(i,s) - lambda*(A(i,s) - A(i,t(i))); Delta(i,t(i)) = 0;
    admissible iff toll(i,s) <= eps_rec; items with zero outgoing training transitions get A=0 everywhere and never move.
(9) Per level-1 group: expand each cell into n_s slots (so capacities are exactly the baseline sizes), build the square matrix M[i,slot]=Delta(i,s)+1e-9*1[s != t(i)], solve with scipy linear_sum_assignment in float64, decompose the item permutation into cycles, keep cycles in ascending order of max toll while the cumulative moved count stays <= 0.05*N, and set t'(i)=t(sigma(i)). Dropping whole cycles keeps every capacity exact.
(10) Emit t'(i) plus a record: rho, per-cycle tolls, prefix counts at levels 1/2/3 before and after, behaviour contrast before and after.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: 24556 frozen text embeddings, frozen three codebooks and baseline triples (251/1769/2201 distinct prefixes), training-split directed transition counts, alpha-derived Poincare map, eps_rec, sigma_A. Outputs: repaired triples t'(i) (the SID sequence Stage 3 consumes), plus diagnostics rho, max toll, exact prefix-count assertions, behaviour-contrast delta.
SAFEGUARDS: float64 throughout; clamp 1-||p||^2 and 1-||q||^2 at 1-tanh^2(1)=0.4199 (guaranteed by alpha) so no log or arcosh overflow; 1+2||p-q||^2/... clamped at 1+1e-12 before arcosh; identity tie-break 1e-9 so behaviour-off is bit-exactly the baseline; hard per-item toll cap eps_rec; 5% moved-cycle cap; assertions that the SID vocabulary, the per-cell size vector and the level 1/2/3 distinct-prefix counts are identical to the baseline, and abort to the baseline SIDs if rho=0.
COST: Zero added work per Stage-2 step (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps, unchanged 0.145-0.16 s/epoch) and zero added Stage-3 work. One pass: one sparse pass over the training transition pairs (seconds), 24556 hyperbolic distances plus ~700 per level-1 group, and 251 linear_sum_assignment calls of size ~98x98 (about 7.5e7 flops). Total well under one minute once, i.e. ~0.3-0.8 ms amortized per Stage-2 step, under 1% of the frozen Stage-2 wall clock.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping if moving boundary items into behaviourally coherent existing cells raises prefix behaviour predictability and therefore Stage-3 recall, because the text-geometry signal the baseline relies on is preserved at the quantizer's own resolution. Observation: rho small but non-zero, prefix counts and SID vocabulary identical, training-split behaviour contrast strictly higher than baseline, and test_recall@10 above 0.0679. Falsified if rho>0 and contrast rises while recall does not move, or if rho=0 (the shell is empty) and the SIDs are identical to the baseline.
RISKS: The admissible shell may be empty because the PPMI advantage rarely pays an eps_rec toll, giving a null result equal to the baseline. Or the PPMI advantage is dominated by popularity so moves concentrate in hub cells, which the cycle cap limits but does not eliminate. Or behaviour contrast rises on the training transition graph yet does not transfer to the beam-search predictor. Also, a relabel can move an item to a cell whose Stage-3 token statistics are already saturated by 11-item collisions, diluting the benefit.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted, directed successor PPMI between a training item i and an existing SID cell s, computed as p(s|i) over the global incoming share pi(s) of that cell.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training interaction split only: for consecutive (i -> j) pairs in training sequences build n_ij, row-normalize to p(j|i), aggregate to p(s|i) by summing over items j whose baseline triple is s, aggregate the global incoming share q_j into pi(s), and form A(i,s)=log((p(s|i)+eps_p)/(pi(s)+eps_p)) with add-one smoothing eps_p=1/(total transitions). Items with no outgoing training transitions are assigned A=0.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transitions, pi(s), sigma_A, eps_rec and alpha are computed exclusively from the Stage-2 training split before Stage 3 exists. The pass takes no validation or test interaction, reads no Stage-3 statistic, and its output is frozen before Stage 3 starts; the frozen Stage-3 protocol (150 epochs, seed 42, beam 20) sees only t'. The frozen Stage-2 checkpoint and codebooks are read-only inputs, so no test signal can re-enter the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed curvature -1 Poincare ball: phi(v)=tanh(alpha||v||)v/||v|| with alpha=1/max||x_i||, and distance d_P(p,q)=arcosh(1+2||p-q||^2/((1-||p||^2)(1-||q||^2))), whose radius factor 1/(1-||m_s||^2) is the group-compactness term.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Quantization time, in the cost of the capacity-frozen transport that decides cell membership for boundary items; it never touches the codebook update, the encoder, the assignment metric used by Stage 2, or any token.
COUPLING PATH: The curved distance and the successor PPMI meet as the two terms of a single move cost Delta(i,s) which is the objective of one optimal-transport solve per level-1 group; the transport output is the SID. Removing behaviour (lambda=0) collapses Delta to a non-negative quantity so the transport returns the identity and the SIDs are the baseline; removing geometry (Euclidean chord toll) changes which moves are admissible and which are profitable, so the SID set changes; both-on is the only arm with a curved, behaviour-funded move set.
COUPLING EQUATION: Delta(i,s) = [d_P(phi(x_i), m_s) - d_P(phi(x_i), m_t(i))] - (eps_rec/sigma_A) * [A(i,s) - A(i,t(i))], admissible only when the first bracket <= eps_rec, minimized over a transport whose cell capacities are exactly the baseline cell sizes.
OBSERVABLES: rho (fraction of items whose triple changes), distribution of hyperbolic tolls per moved item, max toll versus eps_rec, behaviour contrast before and after, level 1/2/3 distinct-prefix counts (must be identical), Stage-3 prefix accuracy at levels 1 and 2 (diagnostic only), and Stage-3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: lambda is set to 0 with the identical code path, Poincare map, eps_rec, admissibility and solver; every Delta is >= 0 with a 1e-9 identity tie-break, so the solver returns the identity permutation, rho=0 and the Stage-3 labels are bit-identical to the frozen baseline. Not trained this round, only wired and unit-checked against the baseline SID file.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: every d_P is replaced by the Euclidean chord ||p-q|| in the same Poincare ball, with eps_rec and sigma_A recomputed under the chord so the tolerance keeps the same meaning and lambda keeps the same derivation; alpha and the ball are unchanged so only the radius-dependent price disappears. Not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full curved, behaviour-funded, capacity-frozen transport above; this is the single trained arm producing the reported test_recall@10.
SPEC EQUATIONS: phi(x)=tanh(alpha||x||)x/||x||, alpha=1/max_i||x_i||; m_s=phi(c1_a+c2_b+c3_d); d_P as above; eps_rec=sqrt(mean_i d_P(phi(x_i),m_t(i))^2); p(j|i)=n_ij/sum_k n_ik; p(s|i)=sum_{j in s} p(j|i); pi(s)=sum_{j in s} q_j; A(i,s)=log((p(s|i)+eps_p)/(pi(s)+eps_p)); sigma_A=std{A(i,s)}; lambda=eps_rec/sigma_A; toll=d_P(phi(x_i),m_s)-d_P(phi(x_i),m_t(i)); Delta=toll-lambda(A(i,s)-A(i,t(i))); solve min sum_i Delta(i,sigma(i)) over bijections sigma that fill every cell to exactly n_s slots; keep cycles by ascending max toll up to 0.05N; t'=t o sigma.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: In: 24556 text embeddings, frozen C1,C2,C3, baseline triples and cell sizes, training-split transition counts. Out: 24556 repaired triples plus rho, toll statistics, contrast delta, and hard assertions that SID vocabulary, cell sizes and all three prefix counts are unchanged.
SAFEGUARDS: float64 with denominators clamped at 0.4199 (guaranteed by alpha), arcosh argument clamped at 1+1e-12, identity tie-break 1e-9, per-item toll cap eps_rec, 5% cycle cap, cold items never move, add-one smoothing eps_p=1/total transitions, and a rollback to the baseline SIDs if any capacity, vocabulary or prefix-count assertion fails.
END IDEA
