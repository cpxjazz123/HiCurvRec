IDEA: BEHTIE-SNAP
TITLE: Curvature-scaled hyperbolic behaviour-contrast snap of one existing RQ level inside a measured quantisation-error tie band.
MOTIVATION: Three rounds lost recall by moving item codes (curved residual, successor barycentre, PPMI fourth level), and the loss tracks deviation from the frozen text geometry rather than prefix count; meanwhile the programme's own 107-pair measurement says behaviour CONTRAST is the positive predictor of recall (+0.7104). The missing mechanism is one that raises the behaviour contrast of the SID assignment while moving nothing off the text geometry beyond the quantiser's own error scale, and that adds no level, deletes no code and creates no residual cascade.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms here changed the quantiser input (curved residual chain), shifted the input toward a popularity-adjusted successor barycentre, added an argmin-pinned fourth level, ranked items radially by context entropy, or updated codebooks with successor-weighted Frechet means. This candidate freezes the trained encoder and every codebook, runs once after Stage 2 as a deterministic 24,556-item pass, and edits exactly ONE coordinate of ONE existing level for the items inside a tie band as wide as that level's own median squared quantisation error. The curved part is a Poincare-ball geodesic comparison of popularity-adjusted transition profiles, with curvature fixed by the measured radial scale of those profiles, so radial (peakedness/popularity) differences are compressed and only behaviour DIRECTION separates codes. No new codebook, no new level, no removed code, no gradient term.
CHANGE: For each item, form the admissible set of existing level-l codes whose squared text distance to the item's encoder output is within rho_l of its current best code, then pick the code minimising a two-term dimensionless objective: normalised text distortion (weight 0.5) minus hyperbolic behaviour-contrast similarity (weight 0.5). Deeper codes are kept stale (their residual chain is not recomputed), so the edit is a single-coordinate relabelling with no cascade.
SPECIFICATION: (1) From the Stage-2 TRAIN split only, count directed transitions C_ij = adjacent pairs (i then j) in training sessions; p_ij = C_ij / sum_j' C_ij'; q_j = column share of C; PPMI_ij = max(0, log(p_ij/q_j)); x_i = L2-normalised PPMI row truncated to its top M = 20 successors (20 is the frozen Stage-3 beam width, a documented default, not tuned). (2) b_i = V_64^T x_i with V_64 the top-64 right singular vectors of the PPMI matrix from a truncated SVD with a fixed start vector (deterministic, no seed sweep); items with no outgoing train transitions get b_i = 0. (3) Curvature from data: R = 95th percentile of ||b_i||, kappa = artanh(0.9)/R, c = kappa^2, so the ball has fixed curvature -c and the 95th-percentile profile sits at Poincare radius 0.9; b~_i = exp_0^c(b_i) = tanh(kappa||b_i||) b_i/(kappa||b_i||); code behaviour centroid mu_k = mean_{i in code k} b_i (tangent barycentre), c~_k = exp_0^c(mu_k). (4) rho_l = median over train items of ||z_i - c_{k(i)}||^2 at level l; A_i = { k : ||z_i - c_k||^2 <= ||z_i - c_{k(i)}||^2 + rho_l }, capped to the 32 nearest codes (documented default). (5) sigma_k = mean_{i in k} d_P(b~_i, c~_k); S_i(k) = exp(-d_P(b~_i,c~_k)/sigma_k) in (0,1]. (6) J_i(k) = 0.5*||z_i - c_k||^2/rho_l - 0.5*S_i(k); k*(i) = argmin_{k in A_i} J_i(k), ties keep k(i). (7) The edited level is l = argmax over l >= 2 of the measured behaviour impurity 1 - mean_k mean_{i in k} cos(x_i, xbar_k), so level 1 can never be edited.
COST: zero added FLOPs inside the 72,000 Stage-2 steps (24 steps/epoch x 3000 epochs), and Stage 3 is untouched. One post-hoc pass: counting train transitions O(E) over the train interaction stream; one rank-64 truncated SVD of the 24,556 x 24,556 sparse PPMI matrix (order 0.5M non-zeros) taking minutes on one CPU node; centroids and geodesics O(N*K_l*d_b) ~ 24,556 x 2,201 x 64 ~ 3.5 GFLOP, under a second on one GPU; the snap itself O(N*32*(d_b+d)) ~ 75 MFLOP. Total added wall clock a few minutes, under 2 percent of the Stage-2 run; per-step cost at the frozen scale is exactly 0.
HYPOTHESIS: If items that share a prefix but lead to the same popularity-adjusted successors make one SID coordinate predictable from that prefix in Stage 3, then reassigning items only among near-tied text codes raises that coordinate's behaviour contrast, lowers Stage-3 conditional entropy at that position, and lifts test_recall@10 above 0.065 without the deviation penalty. Worth keeping only if intra-code contrast of the snapped level rises AND text-distortion inflation stays within one median quantisation error AND the Stage-3 entropy at that position falls; at parity the mechanism is inert and the band occupancy is the evidence.
RISKS: the tie band may be nearly empty at the chosen level, making the arm bit-identical to the baseline (recorded parity, with the occupancy as the measurement); the geodesic may prefer radially extreme codes and create singleton deep prefixes (counted and reported); the stale residual makes the reconstruction inconsistent for moved items although Stage 3 never reads it; the contrast gain may not survive seed noise; the positive contrast correlation may be cross-sectional rather than causal.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: popularity-adjusted directed item-to-item transition profiles (positive-PMI successor sets) per item, from training sessions only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: from the Stage-2 training split, all sessions of the train portion; C_ij counts adjacent (i,j) orderings; each row becomes PPMI against the item's marginal transition share q_j; rows are L2-normalised and truncated to the top 20 successors; a rank-64 truncated SVD with fixed start vector gives b_i. Nodes are the 24,556 training items, so every item, including validation and test items, has a profile built from train edges only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: edges come only from train-split sessions; validation and test interactions are never read to build C, x_i, b_i, rho_l, sigma_k, the impurity score or the level choice. Text embeddings are item metadata fixed before Stage 2. The snap is a deterministic function of (z_i, b_i, codebooks) computed after Stage 2 and before Stage 3 starts, and the frozen Stage-3 protocol, beam width and epoch budget are untouched.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance d_P(u,v) = (1/sqrt(c)) * arcosh(1 + 2c||u-v||^2 / ((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||v||^2))) with fixed negative curvature -c, c = (artanh(0.9)/q95(||b||))^2, plus the exponential map exp_0^c used to place behaviour vectors and code behaviour centroids in the ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: at quantisation time only, inside the code-selection step of one existing level; it acts on the discrete SID coordinate produced after Stage-2 training, not on Stage-2 gradients and not on Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: (train transitions) -> PPMI rows -> rank-64 SVD -> b_i -> exp_0^c -> b~_i, and (frozen Stage 2) -> z_i and {c_k} -> rho_l and A_i; the two meet in one expression, because A_i is filtered by Euclidean text distortion, the survivors are scored by geodesic behaviour distance to their curved centroids c~_k, and the winner is written into the SID coordinate. Behaviour off zeroes the curved term, making A_i irrelevant so k* = k(i) for every item. Geometry off keeps A_i and the same placements but replaces d_P by the Euclidean norm on the same ball coordinates, which changes the winner because the Poincare metric compresses radial (popularity/peakedness) differences and preserves angular (which-successor) differences.
COUPLING EQUATION: k*(i) = argmin_{k in A_i} [ 0.5 * ||z_i - c_k||^2 / rho_l  -  0.5 * exp( - d_{P,c}( b~_i , c~_k ) / sigma_k ) ] with A_i = { k : ||z_i - c_k||^2 <= ||z_i - c_{k(i)}||^2 + rho_l }; w_behaviour(i,k) = exp(-d_{P,c}(b~_i,c~_k)/sigma_k) is the behaviour term and d_{P,c} is the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: moved-item count and fraction and band occupancy per level; level-1 assignment change count (must be exactly 0); level-1..l-1 prefix counts (must be exactly unchanged); depth-l and full-SID counts versus baseline (reported, not gated); mean intra-code behaviour contrast of the snapped level before and after; per-position conditional entropy of Stage-3 sequences at the snapped coordinate; test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEH=0 sets w_behaviour(i,k) = 0 for all i,k, so J_i(k) is pure normalised text distortion and every item keeps its Stage-2 code; the partition and the SID table are bit-identical to the baseline and the mechanism provably does nothing. Wired as a boolean that zeroes the curved similarity before the argmin.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: GEO=0 keeps the identical admissible set A_i and the identical b~_i placements but replaces d_{P,c} with ||b~_i - c~_k|| and sigma_k with the corresponding Euclidean mean, comparing the same information without curvature; the selected codes, and therefore the SIDs, generally differ from the both-on arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEH=1 and GEO=1, the coupled argmin above; this is the single arm trained and evaluated in this round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: C from train sessions; p_ij = C_ij / sum_j C_ij; q_j = colsum(C)_j / sum(C); PPMI_ij = max(0, ln(p_ij/q_j)); x_i = normalise(top20(PPMI_i)); V = svds(PPMI, k=64, v0=fixed) right vectors; b_i = V^T x_i; kappa = artanh(0.9)/quantile95(||b||); c = kappa^2; exp_0^c(v) = tanh(kappa||v||) v/(kappa||v||) with exp_0^c(0)=0; mu_k = mean_{i in k} b_i; c~_k = exp_0^c(mu_k); sigma_k = mean_{i in k} d_P(b~_i,c~_k); rho_l = median_i ||z_i - c_{k(i)}||^2; l = argmax_{l>=2} [1 - mean_k mean_{i in k} cos(x_i, xbar_k)]; for each i: A_i = the 32 nearest codes by ||z_i - c_k||^2 whose distortion is within rho_l of the minimum; J_i(k) = 0.5*||z_i-c_k||^2/rho_l - 0.5*exp(-d_P(b~_i,c~_k)/sigma_k); k*(i) = argmin J_i(k); set SID_i[l] = k*(i) and leave all other coordinates untouched; freeze the SID table and run the frozen Stage 3.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: inputs are the trained Stage-2 encoder outputs z_i for the 24,556 training items, the level-l codebook {c_k}, the train-split session stream, the three switches BEH, GEO and the level selector; output is a SID table in which at most one coordinate per item differs from the Stage-2 table, plus a diagnostic record (band occupancy, moved count, prefix counts, contrast, geodesics used).
SAFEGUARDS: the ball is scaled so no mapped point exceeds radius 0.9, hence 1 - c||u||^2 >= 0.19 > 0 and the geodesic denominator cannot vanish; the arcosh argument is clamped to at least 1 + 1e-8; ||b_i|| = 0 or sigma_k < 1e-6 makes S_i constant so those items and codes never move; rho_l is clamped to a positive floor; A_i is capped at 32 codes so a flat distance profile cannot move an item far; ties keep the Stage-2 code; geodesics are computed in float64 and the argmin uses float64 reductions; the SVD start vector is fixed so the pass is deterministic. Every constant is either measured on the train split (rho_l, sigma_k, kappa, l) or a documented default tied to the frozen protocol (M = 20 from beam 20, 32 nearest, rank 64, radius 0.9, equal 0.5/0.5 split), so nothing is chosen by trying alternatives.
END IDEA
