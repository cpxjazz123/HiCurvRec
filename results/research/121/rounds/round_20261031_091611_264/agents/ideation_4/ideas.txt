IDEA: BPLX-LEAF-EXCHANGE
TITLE: Behaviour-contrast Poincare leaf exchange: a count-preserving relabelling of the deepest SID digit inside one shared parent
MOTIVATION: SHAPE (A). All three prior losses came from moving the encoder input off the text geometry, merging/reshaping prefixes, or pinning a new level. This mechanism touches none of those: it leaves the encoder, the codebook vectors and every level-1/level-2/level-3 count bit-identical, and changes only which item carries which existing level-3 token, inside the same level-2 parent. The problem it addresses is leaf ambiguity in beam search: at a fixed 2201-leaf budget, some leaves mix items with incompatible training predecessor contexts, so beam mass is split between leaf tokens that the history cannot discriminate. The prior 107-pair evidence says behaviour-contrast statistics, not Gromov-delta or ancestor depth, track recall; this mechanism optimises exactly that contrast statistic, with the radial group-compactness (Poincare distance to the leaf centroid) as the feasibility guard.
NOVELTY: Not a codebook update (no Frechet mean of successors, unlike poincare-frechet-successor-codebook), not an item move (unlike the concentration-scaled geodesic step), not per-level curvature (unlike CURVCTRL-SID), not radius/entropy ranking (unlike BRad-RQ), not a new fourth level (unlike round three). It is a discrete pairwise exchange of existing leaf labels, whose feasibility set is defined by a fixed-curvature Poincare reconstruction budget and whose direction is defined by the between/within behaviour-contrast (Fisher) ratio of leaf groups; the two enter multiplicatively in one acceptance score.
CHANGE: After Stage-2 training and before SID export, run one greedy exchange pass over candidate pairs that sit in sibling level-3 leaves under the same level-2 parent; accept a swap only if it raises the behaviour-contrast ratio of the two leaves, with the gain damped by exp(-Delta d_P / delta), where Delta d_P is the change in Poincare reconstruction and delta is a measured scale. Prefix counts cannot move because a pairwise exchange is a permutation within a parent.
SPECIFICATION: (1) From the training split only, build the directed predecessor profile q_i (row-normalised counts of the item that immediately precedes i, top-100 entries) and the PPMI successor matrix. (2) bc(i,j) = 1 - cos(q_i, q_j). (3) For each level-2 parent g with leaf set A_g and member sets M_a, W = mean bc within leaves, B = mean bc between leaves, CR = B / (W + eps). (4) Candidate pairs: for each item i, its top-5 successors by training PPMI that lie in a different leaf under the same parent g. (5) Delta CR(i<->j) is the exact change of CR in g when i and j are exchanged (only four sums change; update means incrementally). (6) Map x_i and leaf codeword e_a to the ball: u = tanh(sqrt(c)*||x||/2) * x/(sqrt(c)||x||), c=1, with x first divided by the measured 95th-percentile training norm s95 (gauge normalisation, reported). (7) d_P(u,v) = (2/sqrt(c)) arcosh(1 + 2c||u-v||^2 / ((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||v||^2))), c=1. (8) Delta d_P(i<->j) = [d_P(x_i,e_b)+d_P(x_j,e_a)] - [d_P(x_i,e_a)+d_P(x_j,e_b)]. (9) delta = median(|Delta d_P|) over all candidate pairs, computed once on training data. (10) score(i<->j) = Delta CR(i<->j) * exp(-max(0,Delta d_P(i<->j))/delta). (11) Sort by score descending; accept greedily if both items are still unswapped and Delta CR > 0; stop when the accepted count reaches N_max = min(0.05*N, #{pairs : Delta CR > 75th percentile of the training candidate Delta CR distribution}). (12) Recompute leaf centroids; if total Poincare reconstruction R_P = sum_i d_P(x_i, e_leaf(i)) rises by more than 1% of its baseline value, undo accepted swaps in decreasing Delta d_P until it does not. (13) Export SIDs.
COST: Zero extra training steps: the 24 steps/epoch x 3000 epochs four-GPU Stage-2 run is unchanged, as is the frozen 150-epoch Stage-3 run. One pass over 24556 items x 5 candidates = 122,780 pairs, each 4 Poincare distances in the 64-d text space plus 2 cosine similarities on 100-d truncated profiles, about 5.6e7 flops: under 2 s single-CPU, under 0.5% of the roughly 450 s Stage-2 wall clock, and no GPU time.
HYPOTHESIS: If beam-20 recall is limited by leaf ambiguity rather than by capacity, then exchanging at most 5% of leaf labels so that items sharing a leaf have compatible predecessor contexts should raise the between/within behaviour-contrast ratio and Stage-3 validation prefix accuracy, and end above the 0.0679 baseline with test_recall@10 > 0.065, while all L1/L2/L3 counts stay identical. If test recall stays at or below baseline with identical counts and a materially raised contrast ratio, then 3-level partition content is exhausted and the leaf-relabel direction is closed.
RISKS: The measured exchange set may be tiny (PPMI is sparse for tail items), giving no effect; the frozen Stage-3 model may not exploit a leaf relabelling it never trained on; the 1% Poincare budget may reject most candidate swaps; a post-hoc relabel can hurt if the Stage-2 decoder was co-adapted to the old leaf contents; the contrast criterion may be dominated by a few high-degree items.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only directed predecessor profiles q_i and successor PPMI over item pairs; leaf-group behaviour-contrast (Fisher) ratio CR.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training-split sequences only: count immediate predecessor->item transitions, row-normalise to q_i, truncate to top-100; compute PPMI over directed successor pairs with the standard smoothing; bc(i,j) = 1 - cos(q_i,q_j); CR per level-2 parent from the current baseline leaf membership.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only training-split transitions enter q_i, PPMI, the candidate lists, delta and the 75th-percentile budget. No validation or test interaction is read; no recall number enters any threshold; the exchange pass runs after Stage-2 and before Stage-3, so the frozen test protocol never influences SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball distance with fixed negative curvature c = 1 (curvature -1), after a gauge normalisation of the encoder output by the measured 95th-percentile training norm s95 so the ball is used; measured on training data, not tuned.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the quantization / SID-export stage, as the feasibility term of the leaf-assignment refinement; the encoder loss and the codebook update are untouched.
COUPLING PATH: One acceptance decision per candidate pair: the behaviour-contrast gain of the exchange multiplies a curvature-controlled damping of the Poincare reconstruction change. Removing behaviour makes the gain identically one and selects purely geometry-reducing swaps; removing curvature swaps d_P for d_E and changes delta, hence a different accepted set.
COUPLING EQUATION: s(i<->j) = Delta CR_behaviour(i<->j) * exp( - max(0, [d_P(x_i,e_b)+d_P(x_j,e_a) - d_P(x_i,e_a)-d_P(x_j,e_b)]) / delta ), with d_P the c=1 Poincare distance and Delta CR the between/within behaviour-contrast ratio change.
OBSERVABLES: Fraction of items exchanged; mean Delta CR; mean and total Delta d_P; R_P increase in percent; exact equality of L1, L2 and L3 prefix-count multisets against the baseline (asserted, reported); between/within contrast ratio before and after; Stage-3 validation prefix accuracy; final test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: use_behaviour=False sets Delta CR := 1 for every candidate pair, so the pass accepts the swaps that most reduce the Poincare reconstruction (pure geometry); everything else, including delta and the guards, is identical.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: curvature=False replaces d_P by the Euclidean distance and recomputes delta from the Euclidean Delta d, so the same behaviour gain is gated by a flat feasibility set.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full rule above with behaviour gain and c=1 Poincare damping; this is the one arm that is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: q_i = normalise(count of immediate predecessors); bc(i,j)=1-cos(q_i,q_j); CR_g = B_g/(W_g+eps); Delta d_P as in the coupling equation; s = Delta CR * exp(-max(0,Delta d_P)/delta); delta = median|Delta d_P|; N_max = min(0.05N, #{Delta CR > P75}); accept greedily with both items free and Delta CR>0; roll back while R_P > 1.01 R_P(baseline).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen Stage-2 item embeddings x_i (N x 64), frozen leaf codewords e_a, baseline SID table, training predecessor/successor counts. Outputs: a permutation of the level-3 token per item within each level-2 parent (the item->leaf map), identical L1/L2/L3 counts, and the reported diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp ||u|| to (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c) before every arcosh and exp map; arcosh argument clamped to >=1+1e-12; eps=1e-8 in CR denominators; profiles with fewer than 5 training occurrences are excluded from candidate generation; enforce one swap per item; assert prefix-count equality and the 1% R_P budget; if the accepted set is empty, fall back to the unmodified baseline SIDs and record it.
END IDEA
