IDEA: CURV-RESID-CONTRAST
TITLE: Curved residual chain with a behaviour-contrast anisotropic metric: the RQ-VAE quantizes in a Poincaré ball so that the training-only successor-vs-matched-null contrast direction absorbs code capacity before the deeper tokens do
MOTIVATION: In RQ-VAE the SID is a residual chain: level-0 explains most of the item, and the later tokens only explain what level-0 left over. For a generative next-item recommender the later tokens must be predictable from the prefix, i.e. they should carry behaviour-predictable variation, not encoder noise. The measured evidence here says what predicts Recall@10 is a behaviour-contrast quantity (successor-vs-null distance contrast), not hierarchy and not curvature; here that contrast is made the thing the residual chain spends code on, inside a curved metric where the same tangent residual moves the codeword point by a state-dependent amount.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds put behaviour into the codebook update (successor-weighted Fréchet means), into per-level curvature (CURVCTRL, which the gauge evidence undermines), into radial ranking (BRad-RQ) or into prefix/subtree boosting. This idea never sets curvature from data, never uses a Fréchet mean, and never uses a radius rank: curvature is a declared gauge (c=1), and behaviour enters as an anisotropic direction plus a gain INSIDE the residual-chain assignment rule, multiplied by the conformal factor of the ball. The coupling lives in the quantizer's argmin and in the exponential-map composition, not in an auxiliary loss beside the quantizer.
CHANGE: Each level's codeword is chosen by minimizing a curved, contrast-weighted quadratic form of the transported residual (gain alpha_i along the behaviour direction u_i, uniform orthogonal), and the prefix point advances by an exponential map instead of a vector addition.
SPECIFICATION: Map frozen item text embedding z_i to x_i = exp_0(s z_i) with c=1, s = 0.9/max_i||z_i||. Precompute once, from the training split only, alpha_i and unit u_i (see BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION). Quantize x_i by the greedy curved chain of SPEC EQUATIONS. Train the encoder and the codebooks with that same per-level objective plus the standard commitment term; the codebooks receive the gradient of the curved objective, so their positions are shaped by the curved metric rather than by a Euclidean mean.
COST: No extra epochs. One preprocessing pass: 24556 items x (32 successor draws + 32 null draws), two log maps each, O(n*d) memory (about 12-25 MB at d=64-128); minutes once, off the training clock. Per optimisation step at the frozen scale (global batch 4096, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs): L x K x 4096 closed-form ball distances (L=3, K=256 gives 3.1M), each O(d) plus one scalar conformal factor and one rank-1 transport correction; roughly 2-3x the baseline nearest-neighbour quantizer cost, which is a minor fraction of a step because the text/ID encoder dominates. Expected Stage2 wall-clock overhead 3-6 percent, Stage3 cost unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: If the contrast direction computed from training transitions marks variation the generator can actually predict, then absorbing that direction earlier in the chain makes prefix-conditioned next-token prediction easier, and Stage3 test_recall@10 rises above 0.065 while the code-space within-successor / within-null distance ratio falls. Observed by per-level token-flip rate against the baseline SIDs (small at level 0, large at levels 1-2), the code-space contrast ratio, hyperbolic residual norms per level, and finally test_recall@10 from the single frozen Stage3 run.
RISKS: The contrast direction may be dominated by popularity even after null matching, drifting codes toward popular items; the curved chain may saturate near the boundary and raise reconstruction error; greedy per-level curves only bite from level 1 (level 0 sits at the origin); and better code-space contrast may not become token-level predictability, which is exactly what the one run must reveal.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item directed successor contrast against a popularity-matched null, computed in the hyperbolic tangent space at the item's own point.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training-split sequential interactions only: S_i = multiset of next items observed after item i inside training sessions, capped at the 32 highest-count successors; N_i = 32 items drawn with probability proportional to training popularity from the same popularity decile as S_i, excluding i, its 1-hop neighbours and its successors, with a seed keyed deterministically on the item id. Then u_i = mean_{j in S_i} log_{x_i}(x_j) - mean_{j in N_i} log_{x_i}(x_j), all log maps in the ball of curvature 1, and the gain is a dimensionless ratio of hyperbolic norms (so it survives any uniform radial rescaling of the chart).
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: u_i and alpha_i are computed once before Stage2 from training interactions and the frozen training item text embeddings, stored as constants, never recomputed from validation or test interactions; the null sampler sees only training popularity; Stage3 never feeds back into SID construction; no item outside the 24556 training item set contributes to any SID.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré ball of fixed curvature c=1, gauge-fixed by the data-derived radial scale s = 0.9/max||z||; operations are exp_0, log_p, exp_p, parallel transport Pi_{0->p}, and the conformal factor lambda(p) = 2/(1-c||p||^2) that defines the curved norm of a tangent residual.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the quantizer, at codeword selection and at prefix accumulation, and in the gradient that reaches the encoder and the codebooks; not in the decoder's reconstruction loss and not in any standalone regularizer.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour direction u_i and gain alpha_i -> transported residual decomposition in the level-l objective -> argmin over codewords -> prefix p_{l+1} through exp_p -> next level's transport and conformal factor -> SID tokens. Removing behaviour makes the form isotropic (argmin returns the plain nearest codeword of the curved chain); removing the curve makes the chain additive Euclidean with lambda=1; either change alters the chosen tokens.
COUPLING EQUATION: J_l(k) = lambda(p_l)^{-2} * [ alpha_i * <r_l - Pi(e_{l,k}), Pi(u_i)>^2 + || (r_l - Pi(e_{l,k}))_perp ||^2 ], k* = argmin_k J_l(k), with r_l = log_{p_l}(x_i), Pi = Pi_{0->p_l}, lambda(p) = 2/(1-c||p||^2), c = 1.
OBSERVABLES: per-level SID token flip rate against the baseline SID; code-space contrast ratio = mean d_P(prefix pair inside a successor set) / mean d_P(prefix pair to the null set); per-level hyperbolic residual norm; Stage3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: alpha_i set to 1 and u_i set to the zero vector for every item, leaving the curved chain, the transport and lambda exactly as in the full run.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c treated as 0 for the chain: p advances by plain vector addition, Pi is the identity, lambda = 1, so the quantizer is the usual Euclidean residual chain with the behaviour gain retained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the single trained run, with alpha_i and u_i from training transitions and the c=1 curved chain both active.
SPEC EQUATIONS: exp_0(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)/(sqrt(c)||v||) * v; for the Mobius form use w = ((1-c||p||^2)v - (1-c||v||^2)p)/(1 - 2c<p,v> + c^2||p||^2||v||^2), log_p(v) = (2/(c(1-c||p||^2))) * artanh(sqrt(c)||w||)/(sqrt(c)||w||) * w; exp_p(r) = p (+) tanh(sqrt(c)||r||)/(sqrt(c)||r||) r; Pi_{0->p}(v) = (lambda(0)/lambda(p)) * (v - c<p,v>/(1+sqrt(1-c||p||^2)) p); lambda(p) = 2/(1-c||p||^2); ||v||_p = lambda(p)||v||; u_hat_i = u_i/||u_i||_{x_i}; alpha_i = clip(||u_i||_{x_i} / median_j ||u_j||_{x_j}, 0.5, 2.0). Quantize: p_0 = 0; for l in 0..L-1: r_l = log_{p_l}(x_i); for each k: g_{l,k} = Pi_{0->p_l}(e_{l,k}), a = <r_l - g_{l,k}, Pi_{0->p_l}(u_hat_i)>, b = ||r_l - g_{l,k}||^2 - a^2, J = lambda(p_l)^{-2}(alpha_i a^2 + b); k* = argmin J; token_l = k*; p_{l+1} = exp_{p_l}(r_l - g_{l,k*}). Loss = sum_l lambda(p_l)^{-2}(alpha_i a_l^2 + b_l) + beta*||sg[e] - r||^2 with the frozen baseline beta, decoder term unchanged.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen item text embeddings z (24556 x d), training transition counts, codebooks e (L x K x d), gain alpha (24556,), direction u_hat (24556 x d). Outputs: SID tokens (24556 x L), updated encoder parameters, updated codebooks, per-level prefix points, and the recorded diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||p|| to 0.995 inside every exp_p and log_p denominator; clamp the artanh argument to 1-1e-7; if ||u_i||_{x_i} < 1e-6 or |S_i| < 2, set alpha_i = 1 and u_hat_i = 0 (that item silently falls back to isotropic); transport preserves the tangent norm up to lambda(0)/lambda(p), so rescale the residual by lambda(p) before the commitment term to keep the frozen beta meaningful; alpha is clipped to [0.5, 2.0] by a budget argument (no item may claim more than twice the median residual budget), not by search; the radial gauge s comes from max||z|| and c is never fitted, so no curvature estimate is claimed.
END IDEA
