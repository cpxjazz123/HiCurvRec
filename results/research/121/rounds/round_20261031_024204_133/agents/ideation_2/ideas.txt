IDEA: GYROFRAME-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-pole gyroframe residual quantization: RQ codes are emitted, assigned and composed inside the moving Lorentz frame of each item's train-only successor-contrast direction, with the pole at a constant one-curvature-unit radius.
MOTIVATION: Content-only RQ-VAE SIDs group items by what they are, but a generative next-item recommender needs prefixes that already carry "what tends to follow this" information; the repository measurements show the predictor of Recall is a behaviour CONTRAST quantity, not hierarchy and not curvature magnitude, so the contrast has to enter the code assignment itself rather than decorate it. An item whose successor contrast points one way and an item whose successor contrast points another way should not receive the same level-1 token just because their text embeddings are close.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms here changed the curvature per prefix level, ranked each item's radius by behaviour entropy, or replaced the codebook update with a curved Fréchet mean. This one keeps the curvature fixed at c=1 (the gauge is not estimated), keeps a single constant pole radius for every item (no behaviour statistic ranks items radially), and instead uses the behaviour contrast to choose the DIRECTION of a Lorentz frame change: the quantizer's residual is taken at a behaviour-defined base point and each code is parallel-transported into that base point's tangent space, so the item-specific gyration (a pure curvature effect with no Euclidean analogue at the same cost) is what mixes behaviour into the code indices.
CHANGE: The RQ target point is the item's content point, but the composition starts at a behaviour pole p_i = exp_o(1 * u_hat_i) instead of the origin, and every codebook vector is parallel-transported from T_o into T_{z_{l-1}} before it is compared with the residual; the encoder output is projected orthogonal to u_hat_i so the encoder cannot undo the behaviour shift.
SPECIFICATION: Lorentz model, curvature c=1 fixed, signature (-,+,...,+), <x,x>_L = -1. Pole: u_i = sum_{j in succ(i)} A_ij t_j - (sum_j A_ij) t_pop, A_ij = max(0, log(N*n_ij/(n_i*n_j))) clipped at log N, t_pop = popularity-weighted mean text direction; u_hat_i = u_i/||u_i||, p_i = exp_o(u_hat_i) (geodesic radius exactly 1, Poincaré radius tanh(1)=0.762, below the measured saturation point). Encoder: a_i = f_phi(e_i) projected orthogonal to u_hat_i and clipped to ||a_i||<=1. Item point x_i = exp_o(a_i). Quantization: z_0 = p_i; for l=1..L, r_l = log_{z_{l-1}}(x_i), k_l = argmin_k || r_l - PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k}) ||^2, z_l = exp_{z_{l-1}}( PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k_l}) ). SID(i) = (k_1,...,k_L). The decoder keeps the baseline content reconstruction from d_i = log_o(z_L). Loss = baseline reconstruction + sum_l [ ||r_l - c~_{l,k_l}||^2 + ||r_l - sg(c~)||^2 + beta ||sg(r_l) - c~||^2 ] with c~ = PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k}).
COST: Per step at the frozen scale (batch 4096, L=4, d=64): 9 closed-form Lorentz maps per item (5 log/exp for the residual-and-compose recursion, 4 transports, each O(d), no matrices) = about 18 kflops per item, about 74 MFLOP forward and about 0.2 GFLOP forward+backward per step; 24 steps per epoch gives about 5 GFLOP per epoch and about 16 TFLOP over 3000 epochs, i.e. well under 1 percent of a 4-GPU transformer step. One-off offline preprocessing: one pass over the training interactions to build n_ij, n_i, n_j (minutes on CPU), one N-by-d pole table (24556 by 64 float32 = 6.3 MB) held as a non-trainable buffer and gathered by item id, so no per-step graph and no extra parameter. Expected wall clock within 1 percent of the baseline 3000-epoch Stage 2 run.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if (a) the item-specific transport rotation is large enough to move code assignment (the mean gyration angle of PT_{o->p_i} on the orthogonal complement is of order the geodesic distance, i.e. O(1) rad at radius 1, recorded once at initialisation, and the level-1 code histogram differs from the Euclidean-shift variant by a large fraction of items), and (b) the resulting SIDs are more successor-predictive, observed as a drop in the train-only prefix successor entropy and, finally, as Stage 3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 in the single frozen run. If instead the encoder drives the achievable SID set to a relabelling of the baseline's, the run lands at baseline recall and the idea is dead.
RISKS: The encoder still has second-order freedom to compensate the frame shift, so the coupling can be partially absorbed (diagnostic: record ||<a_i,u_hat_i>|| and the mean hyperbolic displacement ||log_{p_i}(x_i)|| per epoch). Items with noisy one-transition successor sets get a full-strength direction because the radius is constant, which may inject PMI noise into level-1 codes (no shrinkage by design, to stay out of the radial-ranking slot). The displacement target has norm up to 2 at radius 1, so the L-level budget is stretched and reconstruction can degrade, which would make SIDs less informative for Stage 3 even if they are more behaviour-aligned. Fixed-radius poles for rare items may also increase SID collisions where successor contrast is uninformative; collision count and distinct-SID count are recorded but are not gates.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed sequential co-consumption contrast: for every training item, the popularity-adjusted (PMI) successor direction from its outgoing session transitions, centred on the popularity-background content direction.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training interactions only, within sessions, count ordered pairs (i -> j) giving n_ij, outgoing counts n_i, incoming counts n_j, total transitions N. Compute A_ij = max(0, log(N*n_ij/(n_i*n_j))) clipped at log N (the analytic maximum, since n_ij <= min(n_i,n_j)). Project successor content directions t_j (the frozen text/metadata embeddings already used by the RQ-VAE input, dimension d) with these weights, subtract the background term (sum_j A_ij) * t_pop where t_pop = sum_j (n_j/sum n) t_j, and L2-normalise. Items with no outgoing train transition get u_i = 0 and are routed to the identity frame.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The pole table is built once, before Stage 2 starts, from the training split interactions only; validation and test interactions are never read, no Stage 3 history or Stage 3 model output is consulted, and the table is frozen (a plain buffer, no gradient, no online update) so nothing from an evaluation pass can flow back into SID construction. The t_j and t_pop used are the same frozen content embeddings the baseline RQ-VAE already consumes, so no new external signal enters.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed-curvature (c=1) Lorentz operations: exponential and logarithm maps at a data-defined base point, and parallel transport PT_{x->y}(v) = v + (<y,v>_L/(1 - <x,y>_L))(x + y), all evaluated on the hyperboloid (the chart that stays numerically sound where the Poincaré chart saturates); the residual comparison uses the transported tangent vectors.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the residual quantizer, at every level: the base point of the composition (z_0 = p_i), the residual extraction (r_l = log_{z_{l-1}}(x_i)) and the code comparison (transported code PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k}) instead of the raw code). It acts during Stage 2 training of the RQ-VAE, not in Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: successor PMI contrast -> pole direction u_hat_i -> base point p_i on the hyperboloid -> residual r_1 = log_{p_i}(x_i) and transported codes PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c) -> argmin code choice -> codebook/commitment gradients -> SID prefix tokens -> Stage 3 next-token prediction. Removing the behaviour (p_i = o) collapses the frame to the origin and removes all item-specific rotation; removing the geometry (Euclidean shift by u_hat_i, plain addition) keeps the behaviour shift but removes the gyration, so the assignment changes.
COUPLING EQUATION: r_1(i) = log^c_{p_i}(x_i) with p_i = exp^c_o(u_hat_i) and u_hat_i = unit( sum_j max(0, PMI_ij) t_j - (sum_j max(0, PMI_ij)) t_pop ); assignment k_1(i) = argmin_k || r_1(i) - PT^c_{o->p_i}(c_{1,k}) ||^2. The behaviour term is u_hat_i (through p_i), the curved terms are exp^c_o, log^c_{p_i} and PT^c; neither can be dropped without changing r_1 and the argmin.
OBSERVABLES: (1) level-1 code histogram and the fraction of items whose level-1 token differs from the Euclidean-shift variant evaluated once at initialisation (static, no training arm); (2) mean gyration angle of PT_{o->p_i} on the orthogonal complement, recorded at init; (3) per-epoch mean hyperbolic displacement ||log_{p_i}(x_i)|| and ||<a_i,u_hat_i>||, which must stay near zero; (4) train-only prefix behaviour predictability: mean top-1 successor hit rate of a prefix bucket versus a popularity baseline, and prefix conditional successor entropy; (5) SID collisions and distinct-SID count; (6) Stage 3 test_recall@10, the only judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: flag BEHAV=0 sets u_hat_i = 0 for every item, hence p_i = o, r_1 = a_i and PT_{o->o} = identity, so the code is the plain Lorentz RQ on the content latent and codes are chosen without any item-specific frame; wired as a single branch on the pole table, never trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: flag GEOM=0 keeps the same pole but replaces every Lorentz map with its Euclidean counterpart (p_i stored as the tangent vector u_hat_i, z_0 = u_hat_i, z_l = z_{l-1} + c_{l,k}, r_l = x_i - z_{l-1}, no transport), so the behaviour enters only as a translation and the curvature-induced rotation is gone; wired as a branch that swaps the operator table, never trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the single trained configuration: BEHAV=1 and GEOM=1, i.e. p_i = exp_o(u_hat_i), residuals by Lorentz log at a moving base point, codes transported by PT before the argmin, one 3000-epoch four-GPU Stage 2 run then the frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping, no extra seeds, no sweep of any constant.
SPEC EQUATIONS: exp^c_o(v) = cosh(||v||)*o + sinh(||v||)/||v||*v in (time, space) coordinates with ||v|| the Euclidean tangent norm and c=1; log^c_o(x) = arcosh(-<x,o>_L) * (x + <x,o>_L o)/||x + <x,o>_L o||; log^c_z(x) = d * (x + <z,x>_L z)/||x + <z,x>_L z|| with d = arcosh(-<z,x>_L); PT^c_{x->y}(v) = v + (<y,v>_L/(1 - <x,y>_L))(x + y); composition z_l = exp_{z_{l-1}}(PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k_l})); assignment k_l = argmin_k ||log_{z_{l-1}}(x_i) - PT_{o->z_{l-1}}(c_{l,k})||^2; losses L_rec = sum_l ||r_l - c~_{l,k_l}||^2, L_cb = sum_l ||r_l - sg(c~)||^2, L_cm = beta sum_l ||sg(r_l) - c~||^2 with beta the baseline commitment weight unchanged, plus the baseline content reconstruction and decoder terms; SID = (k_1..k_L); pole u_i = sum_j A_ij t_j - (sum_j A_ij) t_pop, A_ij = min(log N, max(0, log(N n_ij/(n_i n_j)))), u_hat_i = u_i/max(||u_i||, 1e-8), p_i = exp_o(u_hat_i) if ||u_i|| > 1e-8 else p_i = o; a_i <- (a_i - <a_i,u_hat_i> u_hat_i) * min(1, 1/||a_i - <a_i,u_hat_i> u_hat_i||).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item id, its frozen content embedding e_i, the frozen pole row u_hat_i (or zero), the L codebooks c_{l,k} in T_o, the baseline commitment weight beta. Outputs: the L-tuple SID, the reconstructed tangent d_i = log_o(z_L) for the decoder, and the three RQ losses; the codebooks receive gradients through the transported codes and through the moving-frame residuals.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp the arcosh argument to max(1 + 1e-7, -<z,x>_L); clamp the ||u_i|| division at 1e-8 and route zero-norm poles to the origin frame; hard-clip the encoder tangent to ||a_i||<=1 (one curvature unit, matching the pole radius, so the Poincaré radius stays at or below tanh(1)=0.762, well below the c~2 saturation the repository measured) after projecting orthogonal to u_hat_i (project, then clip, then re-project once); compute every map in the Lorentz chart and convert to Poincaré only for logging; clamp the transport denominator 1 - <x,y>_L to at least 1e-6 by bounding the geodesic step at 2 curvature units (the maximum displacement reachable at this radius); renormalise z_l to <z,z>_L = -1 after each composition to stop float drift; skip the behaviour term (identity frame) for items with no train successor so no pseudo-direction is invented; no constant is fitted: c=1 is the gauge, the pole radius is one curvature unit, the PMI clip is the analytic maximum log N, the tangent bound equals the pole radius, and beta is inherited from the baseline recipe.
END IDEA
