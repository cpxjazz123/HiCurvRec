IDEA: HYPSURG-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-triggered geodesic surgery of every RQ level: split the code whose members disagree most about their successors along the Poincare geodesic, merge the geodesically redundant but behaviourally identical pair.
MOTIVATION: In a residual-quantizer SID, a codebook is fixed-capacity: if two groups of items that behave very differently (different next-item distribution, i.e. different future prefix) are forced into the same level-1 code, no amount of decoder training can give the generative recommender a discriminative first token, and the prefix is unpredictive for exactly the items with the richest continuation structure. Symmetrically, codes whose members have identical continuation behaviour and that sit very close in the latent geometry waste capacity. Behaviour-blind training cannot fix this because the quantizer loss only sees reconstruction error, which is satisfied by a partition that ignores co-consumption structure. The recommendation problem addressed is therefore: allocate the fixed SID token budget where the training-only directed transition structure of the behaviour graph actually differs.
NOVELTY: The earlier mechanisms here move codebook vectors continuously (successor-weighted Frechet updates), rescale curvature per level from a prefix-transition score, or re-rank/reweight the assignment radially. This idea does not touch the loss, the curvature value, or the per-item assignment weights at all: it changes the *cardinality allocation* of the codebook itself by discrete, size-preserving surgery between epochs, and it is the curved geometry that decides where the children go (geodesic exp/log at the parent) and which pair may be fused (Poincare distance plus a behaviour divergence). Behaviour enters hierarchy-wide: at level l the successor mass is measured at level l, so every prefix level gets its own surgery criterion and its own split direction, which is how the whole prefix hierarchy is shaped rather than only the first token.
CHANGE: Between epochs (only when a measured churn trigger fires), at each RQ level we build a K-by-K successor-code mass matrix M_l from training-only directed item transitions aggregated over the current SID assignment, choose the highest behaviour-disagreement code, replace it by two children placed symmetrically along the Poincare geodesic through the parent in the direction of its behaviour-weighted successor codes (half the geodesic distance to the nearest code), and fuse the pair of codes that is geodesically closest and behaviourally most similar so that K is preserved.
SPECIFICATION: See SPEC EQUATIONS. Inputs: cached per-level latents of the 24556 training items at the epoch boundary, current codebooks E_l (K=256, d=32), precomputed sparse training transition matrix C. Output: a relabelled codebook with the same K per level, plus reset optimizer slots for the touched rows. Trigger: at the end of epoch e, compute churn(e) = fraction of training items whose level-1 code differs from epoch e-1; perform surgery iff churn(e) < 0.5*churn(e-1) (plateau detection, both quantities measured, no tuned threshold) and the candidate codes were not touched at the previous surgery; each level may perform at most K surgeries over the whole run (a one-time capacity-doubling budget). Curvature kappa is derived once, at the first measurement epoch, from the measured median latent norm and then frozen: kappa = (2*atanh(0.5)/median_i ||z_i||)^2.
COST: No per-step cost: the transi

tion CSR (top-50 successors per item, ~1.2M nnz, precomputed once from the training split, ~15 MB) is built offline and the per-level latents are the ones the epoch already produced. Per surgery, per level: one sparse scatter over nnz (O(1.2M) adds) for M_l, K*K*d = 256*256*32 ~ 2.1 M flop for the tangent-space direction, plus one argmin pass over 24556 cached latents (0.8 M distance evaluations, vectorised). That is ~10-15 ms on one card per level, i.e. ~40 ms per surgery across three levels; the halving trigger typically fires a few dozen times in 3000 epochs, so total overhead is well under 0.5% of wall clock and zero on the 24 steps/epoch of the 3000-epoch Stage 2 run. Memory added: one K*K float matrix per level and the CSR.
HYPOTHESIS: For the idea to be worth keeping, the level-l code mass of the training items must, after surgery, become more informative about the level-l distribution of their successors (lower conditional successor entropy inside codes, higher transfer predictability), and the codebook must keep its utilisation (merge prevents dead capacity). That must be observable as (i) a measurable drop in within-code successor entropy at levels 1..3 after each surgery event, (ii) non-degenerate code usage and no increase in level-wise collisions, and (iii) Stage 3 test_recall@10 above 0.065. If (i) moves but (iii) does not, the prefix hierarchy is being refined where the recommender does not need it.
RISKS: Surgery may thrash the codebook and destroy the decoder's learned token embeddings, hurting reconstruction; the churn trigger may never fire (then the mechanism is inert and the run is just the Euclidean baseline with a curved assignment); the metric may permit geometrically close codes to be fused even though they are behaviourally similar only because the assignment already collapsed them; the transition CSR built from short within-session windows may be dominated by popular items, so the PPMI clipping must be respected; reassignment after a split changes all downstream level codes, so the decoder needs a few epochs to recover and the effect can be hidden by the single fixed run.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item transitions observed inside training-split user sequences (co-consumption within a short window, PPMI-weighted), aggregated to level-wise successor-code mass M_l.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage 1/2 training split only. Count c(i->j) = sum over training sequences of 1/rank for every pair where item j appears within a window of 3 positions after item i in the same sequence. Popularity-adjust with PPMI: w(i->j) = max(0, log( (c(i->j)/c(i)) / (c(j)/C) )), keep the top-50 successors per item, store as a CSR over item ids. At each surgery, given the current assignment k_l(i) for all 24556 training items, accumulate M_l[k,k'] = sum_{i: k_l(i)=k} sum_{j: k_l(j)=k'} w(i->j), row-normalise. No gradient flows through M_l; it is a measured statistic.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition CSR, M_l, the churn statistic and the split/merge decisions are computed exclusively from training-split sequences and training-split item assignments. Validation and test interaction sequences are never enumerated, never counted, and never enter the codebook, the assignment, or the surgery trigger; they are only used afterwards to report the behavioural diagnostics and the Stage 3 test_recall@10 of the frozen pipeline, and those diagnostics never gate anything.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of fixed negative curva

ture -kappa, kappa>0 derived from the measured median latent norm and then frozen. Operations used: exponential map exp_p, logarithmic map log_p, Poincare distance d_P, and a geodesic-symmetric split exp_p(+-eta*u) with eta derived from the nearest-code geodesic distance.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In quantization, at the codebook-maintenance step between Stage 2 epochs; the same curved distance also serves the nearest-code assignment inside every residual level, so the geometry is active in both the assignment and the surgery.
COUPLING PATH: training transitions -> level-wise successor-code mass M_l -> tangent direction at the parent code obtained by log_p of the successor codes -> symmetric geodesic split positions and geodesic merge radius. Removing the behaviour term sets M_l to the uniform distribution, so the split direction collapses to the mean direction of all codes and the merge test to pure geodesic proximity; replacing the curved operations with Euclidean ones changes the direction, the radius, the candidate pairs and the assignment metric. Both switches change the actual codebook that the run ends with, hence the SID assignment of every item.
COUPLING EQUATION: u_k = sum_{k'} M_l(k,k') * log_{e_lk}(e_lk') / || sum_{k'} M_l(k,k') * log_{e_lk}(e_lk') ||_{e_lk}, and e_lk^{+/-} = exp_{e_lk}( +- eta_k * u_k ) with eta_k = 0.5 * min_{k' != k} d_P^{kappa}(e_lk, e_lk'); the merge score is J(a,b) = d_P^{kappa}(e_la, e_lb) + JS( M_l(a,.), M_l(b,.) ), minimised over the geodesic-distance decile.
OBSERVABLES: (1) SID assignment: the fraction of items whose level-l code changes at a surgery event, and the code-usage histogram before/after. (2) Prefix behaviour predictability: within-code successor entropy H(k) = -sum_{k'} M_l(k,k') log M_l(k,k') averaged with mass weights, and the prefix-transfer predictability of held-out (diagnostic) sequences. (3) Training dynamics: reconstruction/quantization loss recovery time after surgery. (4) Stage 3 test_recall@10 of the single frozen run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: M_l replaced by the uniform distribution over codes (no transition information anywhere: split direction becomes the mean direction of all codes, merge test becomes pure geodesic distance), everything else identical.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: d_P, exp_p and log_p replaced by their Euclidean counterparts (plain differences, linear interpolation, L2 distance), so the split is e_lk +/- eta_k * u_k with eta_k = 0.5*min ||e_lk - e_lk'|| and the merge score is ||e_la - e_lb|| + JS(...); everything else identical.
ABLATION BOTH ON: full mechanism above, both switches in their on position; this is the only arm that is trained in the round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: inputs: per-level latents z_i^l (cached, i = 1..24556), codebooks E_l = {e_lk} in the ball, CSR W, curvature kappa.
  1. ball map: phi(z) = (tanh(sqrt(kappa)*||z||/2)/(sqrt(kappa)*||z||/2)) * ... in coordinates r = tanh(sqrt(kappa)||z||/2)/sqrt(kappa), direction z/||z||; inverse log_0.
  2. Poincare distance: d_P(a,b) = (1/sqrt(kappa)) * arcosh(1 + 2*kappa*||a-b||^2 / ((1-kappa||a||^2)(1-kappa||b||^2))).
  3. exp_p(v) = p (+) tanh( (sqrt(kappa)/2) * lambda_p ||v|| ) * v/(||v||) with lambda_p = 2/(1 - kappa||p||^2); log_p(q) = (2/(sqrt(kappa) lambda_p)) * artanh(sqrt(kappa) ||(-p)(+)q||) * ((-p)(+)q)/||(-p)(+)q||, (+) the Mobius sum.
  4. assignment (all levels, straight-through as in baseline): k_l(i) = argmin_k d_P(phi(z_i^l), e_lk); geometry-off: ||z_i^l - e_lk||.
  5. successor mass: for each level

l, R[i,k'] = sum_j W_ij * 1[k_l(j) = k'], then M_l[k,.] = rowsum_i in k R[i,.] / sum(...) (falls back to uniform for codes with zero mass).
  6. entropy/mass score: S_k = H_k * (m_k / sum_k' m_k'), k* = argmax_{k: m_k >= 2} S_k.
  7. split: u_k = normalize_{e}( sum_k' M_l(k,k') log_{e_lk}(e_lk') ), guarded if the tangent sum vanishes; eta_k = 0.5 min_{k' != k} d_P(e_lk, e_lk'); e_lk <- exp_{e_lk}(-eta_k u_k); append e_new = exp_{e_lk_old}(+eta_k u_k). Both children copy the parent's decoder input row; Adam moments of both reset.
  8. merge: candidates = pairs in the smallest decile of d_P; a*,b* = argmin_{(a,b) in candidates, {a,b} disjoint from {k*, k*_new}} J(a,b); survivor = the higher-mass code, placed at the geodesic midpoint exp_{e_a}(0.5 log_{e_a}(e_b)); decoder row of the survivor unchanged, the loser's row is dropped. K unchanged.
  9. invalidate the cached assignment, recompute the residual chain for all 24556 items with the new codebooks at the next epoch (one encoder-forward of the epoch's own steps does it; no extra pass).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: inputs = training-only transition CSR (top-50, PPMI), cached per-level latents of the training items, current codebooks, previous-epoch level-1 assignment, epoch index; outputs = updated codebooks (same K), reset optimizer state for the touched rows, a surgery log (level, epoch, split code, merge pair, geodesic quantities, mass and entropy before/after) that is never used as a gate.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp kappa||p||^2 to <= 1-1e-5 before every division; artanh arguments clamped to 1-1e-7; eta_k additionally clamped to <= 0.5*lambda_p^{-1} so both children stay inside the ball; if the tangent sum for u_k has norm < 1e-8, skip the split for that level that epoch; require m_k >= 2 and at least 3 codes above the mass floor before splitting; skip the merge if fewer than 10 candidate pairs; forbid a code touched in the previous surgery from being the next candidate; hard cap of K surgery events per level over the whole run; if a surgery raises the weight-shared reconstruction loss by more than the churn trigger can absorb, nothing else changes (no rollback, the run continues, the log records it).
