IDEA: STATECELL-MARGIN-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-state cells: a successor-prefix log-odds margin on a Poincare-distance RQ-VAE assignment
MOTIVATION: A generative next-item recommender over SIDs is only as good as the prefix transitions it must predict. In a content-only RQ-VAE, two items that users trade between constantly can land in different level-1 cells, so the generator spends its first tokens re-identifying a behaviour state instead of predicting the next item. The behaviour that matters is not the item's own popularity or its own entropy but WHERE ITS SUCCESSORS LIVE in the code hierarchy: if an item is placed in the cells its successors occupy, the SID sequence becomes a predictable state chain and prefix-level beam search stops discarding the correct branch early.
NOVELTY: The behaviour term is a predictive distribution over the hierarchy's own alphabet (the current level-l codes of the item's training successors), not a popularity weight, not an entropy-derived radius, not a codebook-update weight and not a per-level curvature control. It enters as a bounded additive margin on an assignment cost whose content term is a Poincare geodesic distance, so cells become geodesic Voronoi regions shifted by behaviour mass. No earlier round computed a successor-code distribution over the codebook alphabet, and none used the cap 'one typical quantization unit' to keep behaviour from overriding content.
CHANGE: Each RQ level selects its code by argmin over d_P^2(Poincare point of the residual, Poincare point of the code) plus tau_l*(clip(log q / log(1/K),0,1)-1), where q is the count-weighted fraction of the item's training successors currently sitting in that code; tau_l is the level's median nearest-code squared distance and the successor codes come from a detached, at-most-one-epoch-stale cache.
SPECIFICATION: Precompute from training sequences only: c_ij = transitions i->j; w_ij = c_ij/sqrt(1+c_j); p_ij = w_ij/sum_j' w_ij; Succ(i) = top-10 by p_ij (full row allowed). Curvature c = (2*artanh(0.9)/s)^2 with s = 99th percentile of ||enc(x)|| on the first deterministic batch; K = -c, ball radius 1. For level l = 1..3 and batch items i: P_i = exp_0^c(z_i^(l-1)) with z_i^(0)=enc(x_i); C_lk = exp_0^c(e_lk); d2_ik = geodesic distance squared in the ball; n_ik = sum_{j in Succ(i)} p_ij*1[h_l(j)=k]; q_ik = (n_ik + 1/K_l)/(n_i + 1); s_ik = clip(log q_ik/log(1/K_l),0,1); L_ik = d2_ik + tau_l*(s_ik-1); tau_l = median_i min_k d2_ik at level initialisation, detached and frozen; k*(i) = argmin_k L_ik with straight-through selection; z_i^(l) = z_i^(l-1) - e_lk*; decoder loss unchanged; cache h_l updated after the step with the batch's assignments.
COST: Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, batch about 1023, K_l = 256, d = 64, 3 levels): 786k geodesic distance evaluations, about 10-20 MFLOP plus 3069 exp/log maps, well under 1 ms of GPU math; extra wall clock per Stage 2 step roughly 5-15% because only the quantizer path is touched, so 3000 epochs stay inside the frozen budget with no extra epochs and no extra runs. One-off: one sparse pass over the training sequences for c_ij and the successor table (minutes, CPU), a 24556-int code cache, and a single deterministic forward pass to measure the curvature scale and the three tau values. Transient memory: three 1023x256 matrices per step.
HYPOTHESIS: If content similarity and behaviour-state co-location are largely compatible in the frozen item embeddings, the capped successor-code margin changes level-1 membership for a sizeable minority of items without inflating reconstruction error, the training-only successor prefix-match rate (probability an item shares its level-1 code with at least one training successor) rises over training, the empirical level-1 code-transition entropy H(code_1(next)|code_1(cur)) falls, and the single frozen Stage 3 run exceeds test recall@10 = 0.065. If successor occupancy is uninformative, or reconstruction dominates the margin, the prefix-match rate stays flat and recall stays at baseline: one run falsifies it with no ablation needed.
RISKS: Many items may collapse onto hot successor cells; the tau cap, the reconstruction term and the popularity-discounted p_ij mitigate but do not prove this away.; The hyperbolic content term may itself hurt reconstruction, because near the boundary distances are dominated by radius rather than by content direction.; The detached one-epoch-stale code cache can oscillate early in training when codes move fast.; Successor structure may be nearly redundant with content similarity, making the margin a no-op.; The derived tau may be systematically too weak (behaviour ignored) or sit at its cap too often (behaviour overrides fine distinctions).; The Stage 3 generator may not exploit improved prefix self-predictability because its transition head is trained on whatever SIDs it is handed.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transitions (the successor profile) from the training user sequences, popularity-adjusted, restricted to the Stage-2 item set; no self-transitions and no repeated users within a pair.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training split's sequences only: for each adjacent pair (s_u[t], s_u[t+1]) increment c_ij; then w_ij = c_ij/sqrt(1+c_j) with c_j the in-degree, p_ij = w_ij/sum_j' w_ij, and keep the top-10 successors per item (documented default) as a frozen sparse CSR block. At each training step the successor codes are read from a detached integer cache h_l over all 24556 items, refreshed only with the hard assignments of the current batch after the step.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table, the successor lists, the curvature scale s and the per-level tau_l are all measured from the training split and from the frozen Stage-2 item content embeddings; validation and test interactions never enter. The code cache is written only inside Stage-2 training steps from training batches, is detached (no gradient path) and is dropped before Stage 3. Stage 3 consumes only the frozen integer SID strings, so no validation or test interaction can reach SID construction; the only test-time quantity is the final recall.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball distance d_P(u,v) = (2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-u) +_c v||) with fixed negative curvature K = -c, c derived from the 99th percentile encoder norm so that the farthest training item sits at ball radius 0.9; exp_0 and log_0 maps move the residual accumulator and the codebooks between the tangent space at the origin and the ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the quantization step of every RQ level, in the forward assignment of which code each item takes at levels 1, 2 and 3, and therefore inside the straight-through gradient reaching the encoder and the codebooks; the decoder and the reconstruction loss stay exactly as in the baseline.
COUPLING PATH: One cost function per level: the geodesic distance from the item's residual point in the ball to the code point produces the content term, the successor-code distribution produces the additive margin, and the argmin of their sum decides the SID token the generator later sees; setting tau to 0 removes the behaviour and changes the argmin, replacing d_P by the Euclidean distance removes the curvature and changes the argmin, so neither piece is a decoration.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_ik^(l) = d_P,c( exp_0^c(z_i^(l-1)), exp_0^c(e_lk) )^2 + tau_l*( clip( log q_ik^(l) / log(1/K_l), 0, 1 ) - 1 ), with q_ik^(l) the smoothed successor mass of item i currently assigned to code k at level l.
OBSERVABLES: The hard SID strings change for a reported fraction of items relative to the pure curved assignment; the training-only successor prefix-match rate at each level; the empirical level-l code transition entropy and its transfer predictability under a count-based transition model; RQ-VAE reconstruction MSE (must stay near baseline); and Stage 3 test recall@10 as the judgement. Prefix counts, code-usage entropy and collisions are reported as diagnostics only.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: tau_l = 0 for all levels: the cost is the pure Poincare-distance assignment, with the successor distribution computed but never entering the argmin.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: d_P^2 replaced by ||z_i^(l-1) - e_lk||^2 with no exp_0/log_0 maps and no curvature, same margin: a Euclidean margin-shifted RQ assignment.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run, geodesic distance plus successor-code margin, which is the single arm actually trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Precompute p_ij as above; derive c from s. Per level: P = exp_0^c(z); C = exp_0^c(e); d2_ik = (2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-P_i)+_c C_lk||)^2 with the artanh argument clipped to 1-1e-6; n_ik = sum_{j in Succ(i)} p_ij*1[h_l(j)=k]; q_ik = (n_ik+1/K_l)/(n_i+1); s_ik = clip(log q_ik/log(1/K_l),0,1); L_ik = d2_ik + tau_l*(s_ik-1); k* = argmin_k L_ik; forward e* = e_lk*; backward STE: dL/dz += dL/de*, dL/de_lk* += dL/de*; z <- z - e*; h_l(i) = k* after the step. tau_l = median_i(min_k d2_ik) computed once at level initialisation with no gradient. Reconstruction: min ||x_i - dec(sum_l e_lk*)||^2 plus the unchanged baseline terms.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder outputs z_i for a batch, the level codebooks e_l in the tangent space at the origin, the frozen successor CSR block with weights p_ij, the detached code cache h_l, the curvature c and the frozen tau_l. Outputs: per item the three hard code indices (the SID), the straight-through gradients for encoder, codebooks and decoder, and the updated cache entries for the batch.
SAFEGUARDS: Ball clipping so every point stays at radius <= 1-1e-5 and the artanh argument is clipped to 1-1e-6; q smoothed with a uniform pseudocount 1/K_l; margin bounded to [-tau_l, 0] so behaviour can never override a content difference larger than one typical quantization unit; tau measured once at initialisation and then frozen so the feedback loop cannot inflate it; successor code cache detached and at most one epoch stale; items with no training successors, or whose successors fall outside the Stage-2 item set, get margin 0 and fall back to pure curved assignment; any non-finite d2_ik for an item falls back to the Euclidean distance for that item only; the transition table excludes single-interaction users and self-pairs; a final assertion checks sum_k q_ik = 1 and that all SIDs lie within codebook ranges.
END IDEA
