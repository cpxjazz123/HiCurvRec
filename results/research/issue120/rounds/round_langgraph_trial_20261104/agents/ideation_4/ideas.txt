IDEA: SPPS-RQ
TITLE: Successor-Profile Poincare Sinkhorn RQ: curvature-aware optimal-transport assignment whose ground cost is a training-only sequential co-consumption divergence, applied at every RQ prefix level.
MOTIVATION: A generative recommender's RQ-VAE assigns codes by geometric closeness of content embeddings, so two items that are consumed by the same users in the same order can land in different prefixes while two content-similar but behaviourally unrelated items share a prefix. The Stage-3 generator then has to spend prefix capacity predicting transitions that the SID hierarchy does not support. This idea makes the transport plan that builds the whole prefix hierarchy itself behaviour-aware, so every level of the 3-level code is chosen both by where the item is in a negatively curved latent and by what it actually leads to in training sequences.
NOVELTY: Prior mechanisms here moved the codebook centroids (successor-weighted Frechet means), set curvature per RQ level from prefix-transition scores, ranked radius per item by behaviour entropy, smoothed Lorentzian successor profiles, or constrained codes to behaviour cones - all of them still decide the code by nearest-centroid on a fixed cost. This idea leaves the cost-to-centroid as the only geometric term but replaces the argmin/nearest-neighbour assignment with a doubly-stochastic optimal-transport plan computed in hyperbolic space, and adds a bounded sequential-co-consumption divergence between the item's training successor distribution and the live successor profile of each codeword. Behaviour therefore reallocates codeword capacity (mass) rather than nudging positions, and curvature changes the transport ground metric itself.
CHANGE: The level-ell code for item i is drawn from a Sinkhorn transport plan over a cost matrix whose entries are Poincare distances plus a scaled L1 divergence between the item's training successor distribution and the codeword's exponentially-updated successor profile; the same plan supplies the codeword gradients and the encoder gradients, and the residual passed to level ell+1 is the log-map residual at the chosen codeword.
SPECIFICATION: (1) u=exp_0(z), r_1=u. (2) For each level ell=1..3: v_ell=log_{c_ell}(r_ell); r_{ell+1}=exp_0(v_ell). (3) Cost S_ik = d_P(r_ell,c_ellk) + lambda * J_ik with J_ik = 0.5*||p_i - q_ellk||_1, where p_i is the L1-normalised training successor row of item i and q_ellk the L1-normalised successor profile of codeword k at level ell. (4) P_ell = Sinkhorn(exp(-S/tau), 5 iterations, log-domain) with tau measured per batch as mean_i(s_2(i)-s_1(i)) (two smallest costs), clamped to [1e-3, D_ref]. (5) lambda = D_ref (D_ref = mean Poincare distance between items and their nearest codeword measured once on a fixed 4096-item training sample at initialisation). (6) Codeword update c_ellk <- exp_{c_ellk}(-eta * clip(g_k, 0.5*(1-||c_ellk||^2))) with g_k = sum_i P_ik d/dc d_P(r_ell,c_ellk). (7) Profile update q_ellk <- normalise((n_k q_ellk + sum_i P_ik w_i.)/(n_k + sum_i P_ik)), n_k a step counter. Switches: COUPLING_BEHAVIOUR (lambda=0 when off), COUPLING_CURVATURE (d_P->||r-c||, log/exp residuals -> r-c, retraction -> clamp when off).
COST: One extra Sinkhorn (3 levels x 5 iterations on a 256x256 cost matrix), one sparse-dense product p_i (B x |V| sparse) @ Q^T (|V| x 256) per level, and Poincare exp/log evaluations. Per step at the frozen scale (batch 256, 24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch): a

bout 1e6 Sinkhorn flops + about 8e6 sparse flops + O(B*K) elemental transcendental ops per level, i.e. roughly +10-20 ms of matmul on a modern GPU against a baseline RQ-VAE step of order 100 ms, so ~+15-20% Stage-2 step time and roughly +1.5-2 h over 3000 epochs on four cards. Extra memory: 3 levels x 256 x 24556 float32 successor profiles = about 76 MB. One-off preprocessing: one pass over the training sequences to build the sparse successor matrix (24556 rows, mean degree ~40), under a minute.
HYPOTHESIS: If successor distributions are predictable from content and a codeword can only host a bounded mass, then routing mass by co-consumption divergence should make each prefix level more predictive of the next item, which the Stage-3 generator should convert into test_recall@10 > 0.065. Observable: level-1 and level-2 next-token accuracy of the frozen Stage-3 model must rise relative to the same-budget baseline, the codeword successor profiles q_k must become more concentrated (lower mean entropy) over training, and the mean Poincare radius of codewords at level 3 must spread rather than collapse.
RISKS: Successor profiles are sparse and long-tailed, so most J_ik may be near its uninformative value 0.5 and the coupling degenerates to plain hyperbolic Sinkhorn; the Sinkhorn plan with a balanced-marginal constraint may fight genuine popularity imbalance and give hub items no dedicated codeword; the measured tau can collapse to the 1e-3 floor in late training and saturate the plan; the behaviour term can push prefixes apart in a way the generator likes less (shorter, noisier effective prefix vocabulary); a single run cannot separate these, so a negative result is ambiguous.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed sequential co-consumption counts from the training split: for every ordered pair (i,j) of items in the same training sequence with j within the next W positions of i, a count w_ij; row-normalised into the successor distribution p_i. Aggregate "behaviour-conditioned assignment weight" per codeword is q_ellk.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training sequences only (the exact split used to fit the RQ-VAE): sliding window of length W=5 over each sequence, increment w_{i_t, i_{t+1..t+W}} with discount 1/(position offset); build the sparse row-normalised matrix P in [0,1]^{24556 x 24556}; codeword profiles Q_ell (256 x 24556 per level) start from the level-ell zero-shot assignment at initialisation and are then updated with the count-based EMA in step (7) using only the current training batch.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only Stage-2 training sequences are read; validation and test interactions are never opened during RQ-VAE fitting, profile updates or lambda/tau measurement, and the SID table is frozen before Stage 3 starts. Items with no training-successor row get p_i = 0, hence J_ik = 0.5*||q_k||_1 = 0.5 for every k, a constant shift that Sinkhorn's row normalisation removes, so unseen items fall back exactly to pure geometry instead of to any statistic computed on evaluation data.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Constant negative curvature -1 (i.e. c=1) Poincare ball distance d_P(x,y)=arcosh(1+2||x-y||^2/((1-||x||^2)(1-||y||^2))), with exp_0, log_0 and the log map at a codeword log_c(x) = (2/(1-||c||^2)) * artanh(||(-c)+x||) * ((-c)+x)/||(-c)+x|| for the curved residual, and the geodesic retraction exp_c(g) for the codebook update.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the quantizer of the RQ-VAE at every level: in the ground metric of the assignment transport plan, in

the residual chain that defines the input to the next level, and in the codebook retraction.
COUPLING PATH: One pathway: p_i (behaviour) -> J_ik -> cost S_ik <- d_P(r_ell,c_ellk) (curvature) -> Sinkhorn plan P_ell -> {codeword geodesic update, encoder gradient, residual r_{ell+1}} -> SIDs of all prefixes -> Stage-3 training. Removing the behaviour term changes the plan, hence the codeword moves and the residual; removing curvature changes the ground metric and the residual geometry, hence the plan. Neither can be dropped without changing P_ell.
COUPLING EQUATION: S_ik = d_P(r_ell, c_ellk) + lambda * 0.5 * ||p_i - q_ellk||_1, with P_ell = Sinkhorn_5(exp(-S/tau)) and lambda = D_ref.
OBSERVABLES: The transport plan P_ell (its effective per-codeword mass and its row entropy), the fraction of items whose level-ell code changed against an initialisation snapshot, mean entropy of codeword successor profiles q_k, mean Poincare radius per level, Stage-3 level-1/level-2 next-token accuracy on validation, and finally Stage-3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: lambda = 0; the cost is pure d_P(r_ell,c_ellk), the plan is hyperbolic Sinkhorn on geometry only and q_k is still maintained but unused, so the transport plan and hence the SID hierarchy no longer see any transition statistic.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: identical code with d_P(x,y)=||x-y||, log_c(x)=x-c, exp_c(g)=c+g, residual r_{ell+1}=r_ell-c_ell and retraction replaced by a norm clamp; the behaviour divergence lambda*J_ik and the Sinkhorn plan are unchanged, so the effect of curvature is isolated at the same coupling weight.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full run above, one training run, frozen budget (3000 Stage-2 epochs, four cards, frozen Stage-3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep, no second seed).
SPEC EQUATIONS:
u = exp_0(z) = tanh(||z||) z/||z||
r_1 = u ; v_ell = log_{c_ell}(r_ell) ; r_{ell+1} = exp_0(v_ell) ; r_4 unused
S_ik = d_P(r_ell, c_ellk) + lambda * 0.5 * ||p_i - q_ellk||_1
tau_b = mean_i( s_2(i) - s_1(i) ), tau = clamp(tau_b, 1e-3, D_ref)
A = exp(-S/tau) ; for t in 1..5: A = A / A.sum(dim=0, keepdim=True) ; A = A / A.sum(dim=1, keepdim=True)
P_ell = A ; code_ell(i) = argmax_k P_ell[i,k] (SIDs stored from argmax; the continuous P is used for gradients)
g_k = sum_i P_ik * d/dc d_P(r_ell, c_ellk) ; g_tan = g_k * (1-||c_ellk||^2)^2/4 (Riemannian correction)
c_ellk <- exp_{c_ellk}( -eta * clip(g_tan, 0.5*(1-||c_ellk||^2)) )
n_k <- n_k + sum_i P_ik ; q_ellk <- normalise( (n_k_old*q_ellk + sum_i P_ik w_i.) / n_k_new )
loss_ell = sum_ik P_ik * S_ik + ||sg[r_ell] - r_ell||^2 straight-through to the encoder
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: batch of content embeddings z in R^{B x d} (B=256), the sparse row-normalised successor matrix rows w_i for the batch, the three codebooks as tangent-space parameters mapped by exp_0, the profile matrices Q_ell, and the measured constant D_ref. Outputs: the integer SID triple (k_1,k_2,k_3) per item, the continuous plans P_ell for backpropagation, updated codebooks and updated profiles.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||u|| <= 1-1e-5 and ||c|| <= 1-1e-5 before every arcosh/artanh; clamp the arcosh argument to >= 1+1e-8 and the artanh argument to <= 1-1e-7; Sinkhorn done in log-domain with a row-uniform fallback if any row sum underflows; tau floored at 1e-3 and capped at D_ref; lambda fixed to D_ref measured once, never tuned; geodesic gradient clipped by the scale-relative bound 0.5*(1-||c||^2); items with empty successor rows automatically receiv

e the constant 0.5 divergence so they reduce to pure geometry; profile matrices renormalised with an epsilon to avoid division by zero for empty codewords; all constants (W=5, 5 Sinkhorn iterations, 0.5 clip factor, 1e-5/1e-8 floors) documented defaults, none swept.
