IDEA: HOLO-DETOUR-RQ
TITLE: Geodesic-holonomy residual rotation: the behaviour successor mean bends the RQ tangent frame by the curvature angle defect
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE prefixes are learned purely from reconstruction, so two items with the same prefix can have completely different training-time next items. A generative recommender then emits a prefix whose continuation distribution is behaviourally incoherent. The problem is that nothing in the quantizer knows where the prefix actually leads. This idea makes the prefix's own training-transition successors define a second, curved path from the origin to the prefix, and lets the curvature-induced mismatch between the direct path and the behaviour-detour path rotate the residual that selects the next code. Prefixes therefore become frames aligned with their behavioural future rather than only with reconstruction.
NOVELTY: The operator is a pure curvature invariant, the Gauss-Bonnet angle defect (holonomy) of the geodesic triangle (origin, prefix point, behaviour successor mean), applied as an SO(2) rotation of the residual tangent vector before the nearest-code search. Earlier work here coupled behaviour through codebook centroids (Frechet), per-level curvature scalars, radial/rank assignment, cones, and first-order embedding shifts; none used a frame/holonomy term, and none sits at the level of the residual's tangent frame. The rotation is exactly zero both in flat space and when the behaviour point collapses onto the prefix, so the behaviour term and the curvature term are not two additive losses but a single multiplicative interaction that exists only when both are on.
CHANGE: At every RQ level the residual r_l = log_{p_l}(z) is rotated by R(delta_l), where delta_l is the angle defect of the geodesic triangle O-p_l-m(p_l) under curvature -c and m(p_l) is the training-transition successor mean of the items currently sharing prefix p_l. The rotated residual, not the raw one, is compared with the level codebook, so the chosen code (and therefore the whole downstream prefix) depends jointly on behaviour and curvature.
SPECIFICATION: Poincare ball B^d_c with fixed c=1 (embeddings projected to ||x|| <= (1-eps)/sqrt(c)); exp/log/Mobius-add as usual. For each level l: p_0 = g = Frechet mean of training item embeddings; r_l = log_{p_l}(z); m(p_l) = exp_0( sum_{i in C(p_l)} w_i log_0(m_i) / sum_i w_i ); compute a=d_c(p_l,m), b=d_c(O,m), d=d_c(O,p_l); angles by the curvature -c law of cosines; delta_l = pi - (alpha_O + alpha_P + alpha_M), principal value clamped to [0, pi/2]; build the orthonormal 2-frame u = log_0(p_l)/||log_0(p_l)||, w = orthonormalised part of log_0(m) orthogonal to u; r'_l = R(delta_l) r_l with R = I + sin(delta)(w u^T - u w^T) + (cos(delta)-1)(u u^T + w w^T); code k_l = argmin_k d_c(exp_{p_l}(r'_l), e_{l,k}); p_{l+1} = p_l (+) e_{l,k_l}. All delta and m values are stop-gradient; gradients flow through r_l in z and p_l.
COST: The frozen scale is 24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch (about 1023 items/step), 3000 epochs, four cards, one run. Per step the extra work is about 1023 x 4 levels x (3 distances + 3 angle evaluations + one 64-dim 2-plane rotation) ~ 8e6 flops, roughly 1-3 percent of a transformer step, plus a small autograd node count because of the exp/log maps. The per-epoch prefix-cluster successor refresh is 24556 log/exp evaluations plus 4 aggregations, under a second. Per-item successor means are precomputed once at epoch 0 and refreshed every 100 Stage-2 epochs from detached embeddings (about 7e5 exp/log each refresh, tens of seconds total). Expected wall-clock overhead 2-5 percent; the 3000-epoch budget, the frozen Stage-3 protocol and the single run are unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: If the RQ tangent frame at a prefix is rotated toward the direction of that prefix's training-transition successors, deeper codes should become more behaviour-predictable and the generative recommender should place the true next item inside its top-10 beam more often. The keep condition is that on the single run the Stage-3 test Recall@10 exceeds 0.065, with the recorded diagnostics showing (i) a measurable churn in level>=1 code assignments, (ii) an increase in prefix-to-successor top-1 predictability on training prefixes, and (iii) no collapse of codebook usage. If recall is flat while delta_l is empirically near zero for most prefixes, the behaviour means are too close to the prefix and the idea is not doing anything.
RISKS: (a) delta_l may be near zero for most prefixes, making the rotation a no-op; (b) delta_l near pi/2 for a minority could over-rotate and scramble otherwise good codes; (c) the successor mean is dominated by globally popular continuations unless the count normalisation suppresses them; (d) rotating the target on its geodesic sphere changes only assignment, not the reconstruction target, so the codebook may keep its old geometry and the gain may be limited; (e) adding a behaviour statistic per epoch adds a moving target that can slow convergence inside the fixed 3000 epochs.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transitions inside training sequences (the immediate next items within a forward window of 5 positions), turned into a normalised transition weight and then into a per-prefix successor mean point in the Poincare ball.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only, build a sparse table count(i -> y) over next-item pairs inside a window of 5 in each training sequence. Set w(i,y) = count(i,y) / sqrt(pop(i) * pop(y)) with pop(x) the total training occurrence count (a cosine/popularity normalisation with no free parameter). The per-item successor point is m_i = argmin_{m in B} sum_y w(i,y) d_c(m, e_y)^2, solved by K=3 Karcher fixed-point iterations m <- exp_m( sum_y w(i,y) log_m(e_y) / sum_y w(i,y) ) from m = e_i, using the current detached item embeddings. The prefix successor point is m(p) = exp_0( sum_{i in C(p)} w_i log_0(m_i) / sum_i w_i ) with w_i = out-degree of i in the training transition graph and C(p) the items assigned prefix p at the previous epoch's snapshot. Items with no training successors take m_i = e_i; prefixes with fewer than two members or with m(p) within 1e-3 of p take delta = 0.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table, the popularity counts, the per-item successor means and the per-prefix means are built exclusively from the training split's user sequences; validation and test interactions are never read, and the Stage-3 protocol only consumes the frozen SID table produced by Stage 2. No validation or test user, item or transition enters the curvature, the behaviour means, the frame construction or the code assignment. The per-epoch refresh uses current model parameters and the same frozen training transition weights, so it is a training-time statistic, not a data leak.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball model of constant negative curvature -c with c = 1 fixed by the unit-ball normalisation (no curvature sweep). Curved operations used: Mobius addition, exp_p / log_p maps, geodesic distance d_c, the curvature -c hyperbolic law of cosines for triangle angles, the Gauss-Bonnet angle defect delta = pi - (alpha_O + alpha_P + alpha_M), and the resulting SO(2) holonomy rotation R(delta).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the residual quantizer, at every RQ level, after the residual r_l = log_{p_l}(z) is formed and before the nearest-code search; the rotation changes the code index and therefore the straight-through gradient and the SID prefix, and it also runs in the forward pass of the reconstruction loss.
COUPLING PATH: z -> p_l -> successor mean m(p_l) taken from the training transition table -> geodesic triangle (O, p_l, m(p_l)) -> angle defect delta_l under curvature c -> rotation R(delta_l) -> rotated residual r'_l -> nearest code k_l -> SID prefix p_{l+1} -> Stage-3 generation. Removing behaviour sets m(p_l) = p_l, the triangle degenerates and delta_l = 0 so R = I. Removing geometry sets c = 0, so the defect is c * area = 0 and R = I as well; the same removal also replaces exp/log/d_c with their Euclidean counterparts. The rotation therefore exists only when both factors are present, and one connected pathway carries both into the code index.
COUPLING EQUATION: r'_l = R( delta_c( O, p_l, m(p_l) ) ) * log_{p_l}(z), with delta_c = pi - ( alpha_O(c, a, b, d) + alpha_P(c, a, b, d) + alpha_M(c, a, b, d) ), a = d_c(p_l, m(p_l)), b = d_c(O, m(p_l)), d = d_c(O, p_l); behaviour enters only through m(p_l) from the training transition table, curvature enters only through the sqrt(c)-scaled law of cosines and the metric d_c, and the product R(delta) * log_{p_l}(z) is the single term in which they meet.
OBSERVABLES: (i) per-level SID churn: fraction of items whose code at level l changes relative to the uncoupled quantizer, expected to be near zero at level 0 only if the root collapses and clearly nonzero at levels 1 and above; (ii) prefix-to-successor top-1 predictability of training prefixes under a held-out-transition probe, which should rise if the rotation aligns frames with behaviour; (iii) codebook usage entropy and collision rate as health checks; (iv) the primary judgement, Stage-3 test Recall@10 against the 0.065 target on the single frozen-protocol run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: The same code with the transition table disabled: m(p_l) := p_l for every prefix, so every geodesic triangle is degenerate, delta_l = 0, R = I, and the quantizer reduces to the plain hyperbolic RQ. No other change.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: The same code with c = 0: exp/log become identity, d_c becomes Euclidean squared distance, the law of cosines is replaced by the flat one giving zero defect, and delta_l := 0 with R = I. Behaviour statistics are still computed and still attached, they simply cannot act.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run exactly as specified: training-only successor means build m(p_l), the curved triangle gives delta_l, and R(delta_l) rotates the residual at every RQ level. Nothing is ablated and nothing is tuned in this run; the switches exist in the code for later diagnosis but only this arm is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: c=1. x (+) y = ((1+2c<x,y>+c||y||^2)x + (1-c||x||^2)y) / (1+2c<x,y>+c^2||x||^2||y||^2). exp_0(v)=tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) v/(sqrt(c)||v||); log_0(x)=artanh(sqrt(c)||x||) x/(sqrt(c)||x||). exp_p(v)=p (+)( tanh(sqrt(c) lambda_p ||v||/2) v/(sqrt(c)||v||) ), lambda_p=2/(1-c||p||^2). log_p(x)=(2/(sqrt(c) lambda_p)) artanh(sqrt(c)||(-p)(+)x||) ((-p)(+)x)/||(-p)(+)x||. d_c(x,y)=(2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)(+)y||). Law of cosines: cos alpha_O = (cosh(sqrt(c)b)cosh(sqrt(c)d) - cosh(sqrt(c)a)) / (sinh(sqrt(c)b)sinh(sqrt(c)d)), and cyclically for alpha_P and alpha_M with the opposite side in the first factor replaced appropriately. delta = pi - (alpha_O+alpha_P+alpha_M); delta <- min(delta, pi-delta); delta <- clip(delta, 0, pi/2). u = log_0(p_l)/||log_0(p_l)||; wperp = log_0(m) - <log_0(m),u>u; w = wperp/||wperp||; R = I + sin(delta)(w u^T - u w^T) + (cos(delta)-1)(u u^T + w w^T). r_l = log_{p_l}(z); r'_l = R r_l; k_l = argmin_k d_c(exp_{p_l}(r'_l), e_{l,k}); p_{l+1} = p_l (+) e_{l,k_l}. Successor means: w(i,y)=count(i,y)/sqrt(pop(i)pop(y)); m_i from K=3 iterations m <- exp_m( sum_y w(i,y) log_m(e_y) / sum_y w(i,y) ); m(p)=exp_0( sum_{i in C(p)} w_i log_0(m_i) / sum_i w_i ), w_i = out-degree(i). Total loss unchanged (RQ reconstruction + commitment + codebook) except that codes and prefixes are the rotated ones.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs per item per level: the item embedding z in B^d_c, the current prefix point p_l in B^d_c, the prefix successor mean m(p_l) in B^d_c (or p_l itself when the behaviour switch is off), and the level codebook {e_{l,k}} in B^d_c. Outputs: the scalar holonomy angle delta_l, the rotated tangent vector r'_l in R^d (same hyperbolic norm as r_l), the selected code index k_l, and the next prefix point p_{l+1}; and, when the switch is off, delta_l = 0 and r'_l = r_l.
SAFEGUARDS: project every encoder output and every codebook entry to ||x|| <= (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c) after each update; clamp artanh arguments to 1-1e-7 and cosh arguments to at most 20; set delta = 0 whenever any of sinh(sqrt(c)a), sinh(sqrt(c)b), sinh(sqrt(c)d) is below 1e-6, whenever ||log_0(p_l)|| < 1e-3, whenever ||wperp|| < 1e-3, or whenever the prefix cluster has fewer than two members; take the principal defect min(delta, pi-delta) and clamp to [0, pi/2] so the rotation never exceeds a quarter turn; assert | ||r'_l|| - ||r_l|| | < 1e-6 because R is orthogonal in the conformal coordinate frame and the Poincare metric factor is isotropic; stop-gradient on delta_l and on m(p_l) so no gradient is taken through acosh or through the transition statistics; fall back to m_i = e_i for items with zero training successors; if a NaN appears in delta, reset it to 0 for that step and continue rather than aborting the run.
END IDEA
