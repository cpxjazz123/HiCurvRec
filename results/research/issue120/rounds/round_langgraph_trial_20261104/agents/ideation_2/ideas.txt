IDEA: GESTSID-HYPRQ
TITLE: Behaviour-gestated hyperbolic codebook growth: successor-entropy decides which SID prefix splits, negative curvature decides where the new codeword is born.
MOTIVATION: In an RQ-VAE SID hierarchy the code capacity per level is fixed a priori and shared by every prefix, so prefixes whose users actually branch into several successor interests are forced to share a codeword with unrelated items while quiet prefixes hoard unused codewords. The result is SID prefixes whose next-token distribution is behaviourally ambiguous, which is exactly what the Stage 3 generative recommender must then model. Fixed capacity is the problem; the shape of the latent (curved) is the resource that should be spent where behaviour branches.
NOVELTY: Previous rounds touched the assignment rule (radial/cone/weighted), the codebook averaging rule (Frechet, successor-weighted), the per-level curvature value (CURVCTRL), the successor profile smoothing and the codebook target shift. None of them changed the CARDINALITY/TOPOLOGY of the hierarchy. Here nothing about the assignment score and nothing about the codebook update rule changes: the mechanism is a growth operator that inserts codewords at behaviourally branching prefixes, with the insertion point and its spacing produced by the Poincare exp map at the parent codeword and a curvature-derived doubling radius. Behaviour supplies the WHICH (successor entropy) and the DIRECTION (successor Einstein barycentre); geometry supplies the POSITION and the SPACING. Removing either leaves a different operator, so the coupling is in the operator itself, not in a loss term beside it.
CHANGE: Every 250 Stage 2 epochs, for each level-l prefix whose training-only successor distribution is significantly more than binary, insert ONE new level-(l+1) codeword at exp_{c_{l,k}}( s(rho) * u ), where u points from the parent codeword toward the Einstein barycentre of that prefix's empirical successors and s(rho) is the curvature-implied doubling radius around the parent's own item cloud.
SPECIFICATION: Lorentz/Poincare ball B_c^n, curvature -c, c=1 fixed (documented default, NOT trained, NOT swept). Standard curved RQ: v = encoder(item) in B_c^n; r_0 = v; level l: k_l = argmin_k d_c(r_{l-1}, C_l[k]), r_l = exp_0( log_{C_l[k_l]}( r_{l-1} ) ). SID = (k_1..k_L). Growth operator G (see SPEC EQUATIONS) runs at epochs E = {250,500,...,3000} (12 passes); it reads only cached training transition counts C and cached item assignments, appends codewords to C_2..C_L, refreshes the SID table, and never touches Stage 3. Growth threshold log(2) nats and mass floor 1/|C_l| are derived, not tuned; curvature c=1 and doubling factor 2 (one split -> two children) are documented defaults.
COST: Baseline 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 steps. Growth overhead: 12 passes x (one sparse aggregation over the frozen transition matrix, ~5e5 nonzeros, plus a 24556-item Einstein-midpoint reduction, plus a 24556 x L SID refresh) = O(12 * (5e5 + 7e4)) ops, a few milliseconds per pass, < 0.1% of wall clock. Per-step cost at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs) is unchanged except the level-l nearest-codeword matmul grows by at most the insertion count: entropy-gated growth adds <= ~12% codewords per level, i.e. <= ~12% on that microsecond-scale matmul, well under 1% of a step. No extra forward/backward pass is added.
HYPOTHESIS: Behaviourally branching prefixes are the ones that cap Stage 3 prefix predictability, and a co

deword placed at the curvature-correct distance from the parent is the right amount of new resolution there (too close = no separation, too far = hijacks other regions). If true, the conditional successor entropy of the sharpened prefixes drops after growth, the level-(l+1) usage mass of the new codewords is dominated by their parent prefix's items, and Stage 3 test_recall@10 moves above the 0.065 target in the single frozen run.
RISKS: Growth may be too slow to matter (12 passes, entropy threshold rarely exceeded after the first passes); new codewords may be dead on arrival if the encoder's residual distribution is concentrated away from the parent ball; level-(l+1) codes shared across prefixes could let unrelated items steal a new codeword, blurring the prefix; curvature-derived radius could exceed the useful residual scale and be clipped constantly; Stage 2 codebook non-stationarity may destabilise the commitment loss in late epochs; single-run seed noise may swamp a real but small gain.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed training-item transitions aggregated to prefix level: the successor entropy H(p) of every level-l SID prefix p, together with the successor mass distribution pi_p over level-l prefix clusters that supplies the barycentre direction (behavioural branching / user-interest branching).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the TRAINING split only, before Stage 2: build sparse C[i,i'] = number of directed consecutive pairs (i followed by i' within a training session window) over the 24556 training items, symmetrised optional-free (directed kept as is). Frozen thereafter. At each growth pass, using the current item->prefix assignment (k_1..k_l) available from the epoch's own forward passes (cached, no extra pass): for each prefix p, S_p = items with that prefix, pi_p(j) = (sum_{i in S_p} sum_{i' in S_j} C[i,i']) / Z_p with Z_p the total outgoing mass of S_p, H(p) = -sum_j pi_p(j) log pi_p(j) in nats.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: C is built only from training-split sequences; no validation or test interaction, no test item, no test session is ever read during SID construction. SID refresh passes use only training-item assignments from Stage 2 forward passes on training batches. The Stage 3 generator sees only the final exported codebooks and SID table; nothing in the growth operator conditions on Stage 3 data, and the validation set is used only for the frozen protocol's logging/none of the mechanism.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp map at the parent codeword, exp_{c_p}: T_{c_p}B_c -> B_c, plus its inverse log_{c_p}, plus the Einstein midpoint (hyperbolic barycentre) and the curvature-implied sphere-area doubling radius sqrt-c * s with sinh(sqrt-c * s) = 2^{1/(n-1)} * sinh(sqrt-c * rho); curvature c = 1 fixed.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the quantization itself, as a discrete topological update of the RQ codebooks between Stage 2 epochs (a curved codeword-birth operator acting on the hierarchy levels 1..L-1), not as a loss.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour entropy decides whether prefix p splits -> the same behaviour mass pi_p defines the barycentre b_p and therefore the tangent direction u_p at the parent codeword -> the curvature-derived radius s(rho_p) converts that direction into an actual point in the ball -> that point becomes a codeword of level l+1 -> which changes which residual each item is assigned to and hence every downstream SID prefix. Behaviour without geometry has no position; geometry without behaviour has no target prefix.
COUPLING EQUATION:

H(p) = -sum_j pi_p(j) log pi_p(j) > log 2  =>  c_new = exp_{c_{l,k}} ( s(rho_p) * u_p ), u_p = log_{c_{l,k}}(b_p)/||log_{c_{l,k}}(b_p)||_L, s(rho_p) = arcsinh( 2^{1/(n-1)} sinh rho_p ), b_p = EinsteinMidpoint( {e_{i'} : i' successor of S_p}, weights pi_p ), rho_p = median_{i in S_p} d_c(v_i, c_{l,k}).
OBSERVABLES: (a) SID assignment: number of codewords per level and the fraction of items whose level-(l+1) index changes after a growth pass; (b) prefix behaviour predictability: mean successor entropy H(p) over level-l prefixes before/after, and the parent-prefix purity of the new codewords' item mass; (c) parameter update: the new codewords' gradients and usage counts in the epochs after insertion; (d) Stage 3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: pi_p replaced by the uniform distribution over successor clusters and H(p) replaced by the residual-variance criterion (split the highest-residual-variance prefix of each level, same insertion budget, same curved radius, same geometry), so the growth still happens but is not behaviour-selected and has no behaviour direction (the direction falls back to the top principal tangent direction of the parent's residual cloud).
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: identical behaviour path (same H(p), same b_p, same prefixes chosen) but the insertion is Euclidean: c_new = c_{l,k} + s_E * (b_p - c_{l,k})/||b_p - c_{l,k}|| with s_E = 2^{1/(n-1)} * rho_p on the uncurved coordinates, no exp/log, radius linear instead of sinh.
ABLATION BOTH ON: H(p) > log 2 selects the prefix, pi_p supplies b_p, and c_new = exp_{c_{l,k}}( arcsinh(2^{1/(n-1)} sinh rho_p) * u_p ) is inserted into level l+1; this is the arm that is trained (the only arm trained in this round).
SPEC EQUATIONS: pass at epoch e in E: for l = 1..L-1: for each prefix p at level l with |S_p| >= 2 and Z_p > 0: pi_p(j) = mass(S_p -> S_j)/Z_p; H_p = -sum_j pi_p(j) log pi_p(j); support_ok = (at least two j have pi_p(j) >= 1/|C_l|); if H_p > log 2 and support_ok: rho_p = clip(median_{i in S_p} d_c(v_i, c_{l,k}), 1e-3, 0.5*R_max); u_p = log_{c_{l,k}}(b_p)/max(||log_{c_{l,k}}(b_p)||_L, 1e-8); s_p = arcsinh( 2^{1/(n-1)} * sinh(rho_p) ); s_p = min(s_p, 0.5*R_max); cand = exp_{c_{l,k}}( s_p * u_p ); if ||cand||_P >= 1/(1+1e-5): cand = cand*( (1/(1+1e-5)) / ||cand||_P ); threshold check: if d_c(cand, any existing C_{l+1}[k]) > 1e-3: append cand to C_{l+1}, reset its optimizer state, mark it protected for one pass from usage death; then recompute the SID table for all 24556 training items with the curved RQ assignment rule and continue training. Per-pass insertion cap: at most |C_l| per level (<= doubling), which the entropy gate never realistically reaches.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen sparse training transition matrix C (24556 x 24556, ~2e6 nnz cap), item embeddings v_i of the training items in B_c^n, current codebooks C_1..C_L, current item->prefix assignment. Outputs: grown codebooks C_1..C_L, an updated SID table (24556 x L), and a per-pass log (prefix p, H_p, rho_p, s_p, parent index, new index) for the diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: all distances clamp arcosh argument to >= 1+1e-8; Poincare norms clamped to 1/(1+1e-5) after every exp map; rho_p and s_p clipped to [1e-3, 0.5*R_max] with R_max the 99.9th percentile of the epoch's residual distances (measured, scale-relative, not tuned); duplicate-codeword rejection at 1e-3 curvature distance; entropy computed with pi floor 1e-12 and renormalised; prefixes with |S_p| < 2, zero outgoing mass or

a single dominant successor (pi_max > 1 - 1/|C_l|) are never split; new codewords protected from usage-based retirement for one pass; growth is idempotent-safe if a pass finds nothing to split; the whole operator is skipped if C is empty (behaviour-off switch); no gradient flows through the growth operator (detached embeddings), so Stage 2's loss is untouched.
END IDEA
