IDEA: BARYGAUGE-RQ
TITLE: Successor-barycentre tangent charts: the RQ-VAE residual chain is quantized in a per-item Poincare tangent chart whose basepoint is the hyperbolic centroid of the item's training-only successor distribution, and that basepoint slides toward the behavioural future level by level.
MOTIVATION: In a flat multi-level RQ-VAE every item lives in one shared chart anchored at the origin, so the coarse digit must describe both what the item is and what it leads to, and the encoder has no way to spend its first prefix on the behavioural future. Items with many, diffuse or strongly skewed next-item distributions get the same geometry as leaf-like items, so their prefixes carry no transition information and Stage 3 generation drifts to globally popular but behaviourally wrong successors.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds changed the codebook (successor-weighted Frechet centre updates), the curvature per level (prefix-transition-driven curvature), or the radial gain of the assignment. Here the codebook, the curvature and the radius are untouched: what changes is the COORDINATE CHART (tangent-space basepoint) in which the residual chain is quantized and decoded, and the chart moves along the geodesic toward the item's successors as the level deepens. The behaviour signal therefore changes the metric origin of every digit, not the dictionary and not the distance.
CHANGE: Replace z -> q1 -> r1 -> q2 -> ... with t1 = log_{b_i^(1)}(z_i), k_l = argmin_k d_P(exp_{b_i^(l)}(t_l), C_k), t_{l+1} = t_l - log_{b_i^(l)}(C_{k_l}), where b_i^(l) = exp_0(lambda_l * log_0(m_i)) and m_i is the Lorentzian centroid of the EMA embeddings of i's top-K training successors. Decoding is z_hat = exp_{b_i^(L)}(sum_l log_{b_i^(l)}(C_{k_l})), and the reconstruction distance is measured in the same chart.
SPECIFICATION: See SPEC EQUATIONS. Constants are fixed, not tuned: curvature c = 1 (unit Poincare ball, documented default), depth schedule lambda_l = 1 - 2^(-l) for l = 1..L (forces the chart to open at the midpoint toward the future and to converge to it), K = 20 successors (documented default, truncated by training transition count), EMA momentum 0.9 (documented default). Switches BEHAV in {on, off} and GEOM in {poincare, euclid} exist in the code; the single run uses BEHAV=on, GEOM=poincare.
COST: Transition table built once from the Stage 2 training split: sparse counts over 24556 items, truncated to 24556 x 20 = 491k entries, O(nnz) memory (~4 MB at fp32 with indices), built in seconds. The basepoint table m_i (24556 x d) is recomputed once per epoch: 24556 x 20 lifts + weighted Minkowski sums + projections, about 16 MFLOP at d = 64, i.e. under 0.1 s per epoch, 3000 epochs = about 5 minutes total. Per step (24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps) the extra work is a table lookup plus B x K lifts and one centroid projection for the batch, about B*K*d = 1024*20*64 = 1.3 MFLOP, plus 2L exp/log maps per item (6 maps at B = 1024 -> 6144 artanh/tanh) - well under 2% of the encoder+decoder step, so wall clock is roughly +2 to +4% over the frozen Stage 2 budget with no extra epochs.
HYPOTHESIS: If the first digit is allowed to encode the behavioural future rather than the item identity, then (i) SIDs of items with sharp successor distributions move toward their dominant successor in the ball while diffuse items stay near the origin, (ii) prefix-conditioned successor predictability computed on training transitions from the top-1 prefix rises relative to the origin

-chart baseline, and (iii) Stage 3 test recall@10 exceeds 0.065 in the single frozen run. It would be observed as higher train-only adjacency/coherence diagnostics for high-branching items and as the recall number itself; both are recorded, recall judges.
RISKS: The EMA buffer lags early in training so m_i is initially a stale centroid (mitigated by initialising the buffer from the first forward pass and detaching it); the level schedule can push basepoints so far out that the coarse prefix becomes item-specific and prefix reuse collapses; the tangent-space residual subtraction is exact only in the small-residual regime, so large-radius items can accumulate reconstruction error; low-count transitions can produce noisy centroids; the chart change could simply be absorbed by the encoder and leave Stage 3 unchanged.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transitions within sessions (sequential co-consumption), restricted to the Stage 2 training split, giving per item a successor distribution p(j|i).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over training sessions only: for every consecutive pair (i_t, i_{t+1}) with i_t != i_{t+1}, increment N[i_t, i_{t+1}] by 1 (user-id-level dedup so a user counts once per pair). Normalise p(j|i) = N[i,j] / sum_j N[i,j], truncate to the top-K = 20 successors, and treat items with no outgoing transitions as having m_i = 0 (origin). The centroid uses an item-representation buffer e_i that is an EMA (0.9) of the tanh-mapped encoder outputs produced for training items, always detached, so no gradient flows through the behaviour side.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition counts, the top-K successor lists, the EMA buffer and every basepoint are computed exclusively from Stage 2 training-split sessions (plus representations of training items produced by the model itself). Validation and test interactions are never read: the tables are frozen before Stage 3, the Stage 3 prefix-tree/beam protocol consumes only the learned codebooks and the chart of the *context* item built from training transitions, and no validation- or test-derived statistic enters the SID assignment, the basepoints or the codebooks.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of fixed negative curvature c = 1 (metric factor lambda_x = 2/(1-||x||^2)); explicit curved operations: Moebius addition, exponential map exp_x, logarithmic map log_x, Poincare distance d_P, and the Lorentzian (hyperboloid) weighted centroid used as the barycentre (a closed-form Frechet mean), all at c = 1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the residual quantizer, at every level l of the RQ chain: the chart basepoint exp_0(lambda_l log_0 m_i) defines where the level is quantized and decoded, and the reconstruction/commitment distances d_P are measured there. It also propagates back through the encoder gradient (the log/exp maps are differentiable), so the parameter update is curved.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour -> basepoint -> chart -> assignment -> residual -> reconstruction loss -> encoder and codebook gradient. The behaviour distribution produces the centroid m_i; the centroid is transported by exp_0 into the ball to become the chart basepoint b_i^(l); b_i^(l) is the origin of the log map that produces the level-1 tangent vector, so the same vector z_i is compared with a different codebook entry (and produces a different residual) than under the origin chart, and the gradient of the reconstruction loss flows through log_{b} and hence through m_i. Setting BEHAV=off collapses b to 0 and the whole chain becom

es the flat origin-chart RQ; setting GEOM=euclid replaces Moebius/exp/log/d_P by Euclidean mean, identity maps and L2 distance while keeping the same basepoint table, so removing either one demonstrably changes the assignment, the residual and the gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: b_i^(l) = exp_0( lambda_l * log_0( m_i ) ),  m_i = LorentzCentroid( { e_j : j in TopK(p(.|i)) }, p(j|i) ),  k_l = argmin_k d_P( exp_{ b_i^(l) }( t_l ), C_k ),  t_{l+1} = t_l - log_{ b_i^(l) }( C_{k_l} ).
OBSERVABLES: SID digit l of an item changes (assignment flips) as a monotone function of its successor concentration (1 - normalised entropy of p(.|i)) and of its radius; the fraction of items whose digit-1 code is shared with at least one of their training successors increases; train-only prefix-to-successor predictability rises; Stage 3 test recall@10 is the single judged number and must exceed 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: m_i := 0 for all i, so every basepoint is the origin and the chain reduces to the origin-chart curved RQ (curvature, distance and maps unchanged, successor table computed but unused). Code path: BEHAV=off short-circuits basepoint construction before the centroid lookup.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: the same successor centroids and level schedule are used, but m_i is a weighted Euclidean mean, exp/log are the identity, Moebius addition is +, and d_P is the Euclidean distance: the chain becomes a behaviour-anchored Euclidean residual quantizer. Code path: GEOM=euclid dispatches to flat_ops.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEHAV=on and GEOM=poincare, i.e. successor Lorentzian centroid -> exp_0(lambda_l log_0 .) basepoint -> log_b / exp_b quantizer. This is the only arm trained in the round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Poincare ball D = {x in R^d : ||x|| < 1}, c = 1, lambda_x = 2/(1-||x||^2).
Moebius: x (+) y = [ (1 + 2<x,y> + ||y||^2) x + (1 - ||x||^2) y ] / (1 + 2<x,y> + ||x||^2 ||y||^2).
exp_x(v) = x (+) ( tanh(||v||/2) * v / ||v|| ) for v != 0, else x.
log_x(y) = 2 * artanh(||(-x) (+) y||) * ((-x) (+) y) / ||(-x) (+) y||, 0 if y = x.
d_P(x,y) = 2 * artanh( ||(-x) (+) y|| ).
Lift: xi(x) = ( (1+||x||^2)/(1-||x||^2), 2x/(1-||x||^2) ) in R^{d+1}, Minkowski form <a,b>_M = -a0 b0 + sum_{i>=1} a_i b_i.
LorentzCentroid({x_j}, w_j): y = sum_j w_j xi(x_j) (sum_j w_j = 1); y <- y / sqrt(-<y,y>_M); return y_{1:} / (y_0 + 1).
Basepoint: u_i = log_0(m_i) = 2 artanh(||m_i||) m_i/||m_i|| (0 if m_i = 0); b_i^(l) = exp_0(lambda_l u_i), lambda_l = 1 - 2^(-l), l = 1..L.
Encoder output z_i = r_max * tanh(W h_i) with r_max = 1 - 1e-3, so z_i is inside the ball by construction.
Quantizer (per item i, levels l = 1..L, L = 3):
  t_1 = log_{b_i^(1)}(z_i)
  for l = 1..L:  k_l = argmin_k d_P( exp_{b_i^(l)}(t_l), C_l[k] )
                 a_l = log_{b_i^(l)}( C_l[k_l] )
                 t_{l+1} = t_l - a_l
  z_hat = exp_{b_i^(L)}( sum_{l=1..L} a_l )
Loss: L_rec = d_P(z_hat, z_i)^2 ; L_commit = d_P(z_i, exp_{b_i^(1)}( stopgrad(t_1) ))^2 + sum_l d_P( stopgrad(exp_{b_i^(l)}(t_l)), exp_{b_i^(l)}(a_l) )^2 weighted as in the baseline beta; codebooks updated by the baseline EMA rule in ball coordinates on the assigned (exp_{b}(t_l)) points.
Successor table: N[i,j] from training sessions; p(j|i) = N[i,j]/sum_j N[i,j]; TopK 20.
Buffers: e_i <- 0.9 e_i + 0.1 tanh(W h_i).detach(), initialised on the first epoch's forward passes.
Pseudocode:
  build_transitions(train_sessions) -> (idx, p)
  each epoch: m = LorentzCentroidTable(e, idx, p, K) ; b[l] = exp0(lambda_l * log0(m))
  each step: z = r_max*tanh(enc(x)); b_i

= b[l][batch]; run charted residual chain above; loss; backward; EMA codebook; EMA buffer.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latent z_i in the open unit ball (d dims), the frozen training successor table (indices + probabilities, K = 20), the EMA item buffer e (24556 x d), the level codebooks C_l (Kc x d in the ball), depth L and the fixed lambda_l schedule. Outputs: the discrete SID tuple (k_1,...,k_L), the reconstructed z_hat in the ball, the reconstruction/commitment losses, and gradients w.r.t. encoder parameters and C_l. At inference the same basepoint is built for the *context* item from training transitions only, and beam search over (k_1..k_L) uses the same chart.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||m_i|| to 1 - 1e-3 and z to r_max = 1 - 1e-3 before any log/artanh; clip the artanh argument to 1 - 1e-6; return 0 for log_x(x) and use a 1e-8 norm floor on every v/||v||; detach the EMA buffer and the successor table from the graph (no gradient through behaviour counts); fall back to b = 0 for items with zero outgoing transitions or empty buffers; renormalise the Lorentz centroid if -<y,y>_M <= 1e-8; keep codebook entries inside the ball by the same clamp after each EMA update; guard against NaN by skipping the EMA write for a code whose assigned count is 0 in the step; raise the level schedule only after the first epoch so the buffer is warm; deterministic top-K tie-breaking by item index so the chart is reproducible.
