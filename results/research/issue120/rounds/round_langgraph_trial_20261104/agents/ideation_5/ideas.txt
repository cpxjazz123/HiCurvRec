IDEA: BRANCHFLOW-PB-TRANSPORT
TITLE: Branching-conditioned geodesic transport of the encoder latent before the RQ-VAE quantizer
MOTIVATION: Content-only latents place an item next to items that look alike, not next to items a user actually consumes after it, so a generative recommender that emits prefix codes has to spend early prefix bits on content similarity and cannot express "this item leads to that item". Items whose continuation is ambiguous (branching users) and items whose continuation is deterministic need different geometric treatment, but a Euclidean quantizer has one global scale for both.
NOVELTY: The previous rounds moved the codebook (Fréchet successor means), moved per-level curvature (feedback curvature control) and re-ranked radial assignment (BRad-RQ). None of them transformed the latent that is fed into the quantizer. Here the training-only transition table acts as a vector field, the encoder point is moved along the geodesic joining it to its successors' Fréchet consensus, and the transported point - mapped back to tangent space - is what all L residual levels quantize. The step length is a behaviour-only scalar (branching concentration x transition support) and the path is a behaviour-free object, so the contributions are separable while the computation is one shot.
CHANGE: Insert, between the encoder and the residual quantizer, a Poincaré-ball transport step x' = exp_x(w * log_x(s)) whose direction s is the hyperbolically averaged full-prefix code of the item's training successors and whose scalar weight w is the item's successor branching concentration times a measured support shrinkage. The quantizer then sees R * log_0(x').
SPECIFICATION: Poincaré ball D_c = {x in R^d : c||x||^2 < 1}, c = 1 fixed (curvature -1), metric g_x = (2/(1-c||x||^2))^2 I, lambda_x = 2/(1-c||x||^2), exp_0(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) v/(sqrt(c)||v||), log_0(x) = artanh(sqrt(c)||x||) x/(sqrt(c)||x||), exp_x(v) = x (+) tanh(sqrt(c) lambda_x ||v||/2) v/(sqrt(c)||v||) with Möbius addition (+), log_x(y) = 2/(sqrt(c) lambda_x) artanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)(+)y||) ((-x)(+)y)/||(-x)(+)y||. Per item j and per epoch: x_j = exp_0(z_j / R) with R = median_batch ||z|| measured once at epoch 0 and frozen; s_j = 3-step weighted Fréchet mean in D_c of {x_k : k in Succ_K(j)} (detached); w_j = conc_j * m_j/(m_j + m_med); x'_j = exp_{x_j}( w_j * log_{x_j}(s_j) ); v'_j = R * log_0(x'_j); the RQ-VAE consumes v'_j and reconstructs z_j. Switch BEHAVIOUR_OFF sets conc_j = 0 (hence w_j = 0, hence x'_j = x_j and v'_j = z_j exactly); switch GEOMETRY_OFF replaces the last two lines by v'_j = R*((1-w_j)(z_j/R) + w_j log_0(s_j)) (straight tangent-space chord, no lambda rescaling, no exp/log on the path); switch BOTH_ON is the code above.
COST: Adds one exp, one log_x, one exp_x, one log_0 per item (4 elementwise passes over d) plus one sparse gather at the epoch refresh: N*K*d MACs per fixed-point iteration = 24556*20*64 = 31.4M MACs, x3 iterations = 94M MACs per epoch, i.e. 3.9M MACs per step at 24 steps/epoch, under 0.2 percent of a 10M-parameter encoder step at batch 256. Refresh wall clock about 10-20 ms on one GPU once per epoch (3000 refreshes total), transport under 1 ms per step; memory 24556x20 int32 indices (2.0 MB) plus 24556x20 float32 weights (2.0 MB) plus three N-vectors of scalars. Overall under 1 percent wall clock, no change to the Stage 3 protocol.
HYPOTHESIS: Successor pairs must actually become close in latent space after transport, i.e. the transport must reduce, not increase, the distance between a training item and its successor consensus while leaving content reconstruction intact. Observable: the fraction of test-window adjacent pairs sharing the level-1 SID and the expected matching prefix length must rise relative to behaviour-off, reconstruction loss must rise by less than a fixed small fraction, and Stage3 test_recall@10 must exceed 0.065. If reconstruction loss rises materially while prefix agreement does not, the encoder is cancelling the pull and the idea is dead.
RISKS: (1) The encoder can learn to invert the transport, making the pathway a no-op; applying the same deterministic map at SID-assignment time limits but does not remove this. (2) Popularity-adjusted P still concentrates mass on head items, so many items are dragged toward a handful of attractors and level-1 codes collapse. (3) The detached per-epoch consensus lags the codebook and can oscillate. (4) Fixed c = 1 mismatches the latent scale if the encoder's norm distribution drifts, since R is frozen. (5) lambda_x blows up near the boundary and can produce large gradients.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Successor branching concentration combined with a measured outgoing-transition support, computed per item from directed consecutive-item transitions in the Stage 2 training split only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From every training sequence, count directed pairs (u_t = j, u_{t+1} = k) into N_jk; n_k = training interaction count of k; adjust Ntilde_jk = N_jk / n_k; keep the top K = 20 successors by Ntilde, renormalize to P_j; conc_j = max(0, 1 - H(P_j)/log K) when j has at least 2 kept successors and 0 otherwise; m_j = sum_k N_jk; m_med = median of m_j over items with m_j > 0. All of N, m_med, P, conc are computed once before Stage 2 and frozen.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: N_jk, n_k, m_med and the successor lists come only from the Stage 2 training split; the successor-latent consensus s_j is built from the current Stage 2 codebook and from the same training-only x_k, never from validation or test items; validation and test appear only in the Stage 2 selection metric and the frozen Stage 3 protocol, neither of which writes back into the table, the consensus or the transported latent. Items absent from training have conc_j = 0 and receive no transport.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Exponential and logarithmic maps of the Poincaré ball with fixed negative curvature c = 1 (curvature -1), including the position-dependent conformal factor lambda_x and a three-step weighted Fréchet mean, used as the geodesic interpolation exp_x(w log_x(s)).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside Stage 2 training, on the forward path between the encoder output and the first residual level of the RQ-VAE, so every one of the L quantization levels and therefore every prefix position is computed on the transported latent; the same map is applied unchanged when SIDs are assigned.
COUPLING PATH: z -> v = z/R -> x = exp_0(v) -> [s = Fréchet(P, code embeddings), w = conc*m/(m+m_med)] -> x' = exp_x(w log_x(s)) -> v' = R log_0(x') -> RQ levels 1..L -> decoder -> reconstruction loss -> gradient into the encoder. The behaviour scalars (P, conc, m, m_med) enter only through s and w, the curved operations enter only through exp/log and the geodesic, and they meet in the single expression exp_x(w log_x(s)): setting w = 0 removes the behaviour entirely, replacing exp/log by identity makes the same expression a Euclidean chord.
COUPLING EQUATION: v'_j = R * log_0( exp_{x_j}( w_j * log_{x_j}( s_j ) ) ), with w_j = conc_j * m_j/(m_j + m_med) from the training transition graph and s_j = FrechetMean_{D_c}({x_k}_{k in Succ_K(j)}; P_j), so the transported step length is exactly w_behaviour(j) * d_geodesic(x_j, s_j).
OBSERVABLES: (a) mean Euclidean distance between x_j and s_j over training items (must fall relative to behaviour-off at the same epoch); (b) fraction of SID level-1 codes shared by training adjacent pairs and by held-out adjacent pairs; (c) churn of level-1 and level-2 assignments between epoch 0 and the last epoch; (d) reconstruction MSE trend; (e) Stage3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target, with the behaviour-off, geometry-off and both-on switches present in the config but only both-on trained.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: conc_j = 0 for all j so w_j = 0; then x'_j = x_j and v'_j = z_j bit-exactly, the pipeline reduces to the plain RQ-VAE and the SIDs equal the baseline ones. The switch exists and is unit-tested for exact identity; it is not trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: v'_j = R*((1 - w_j) z_j/R + w_j log_0(s_j)), the same behaviour weights and successor consensus but a flat chord in tangent coordinates with the metric factor removed; this tests whether the curved path, not merely the behaviour pull, carries the gain.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full geodesic transport, the arm actually trained once for 3000 Stage 2 epochs on four cards.
SPEC EQUATIONS: PRE: N_jk from training pairs; P_j = topK(N_jk/n_k) renormalized; m_j = sum_k N_jk; m_med = median(m_j | m_j>0); conc_j = clamp(1 - H(P_j)/log K, 0, 1). R = median_batch ||z|| at epoch 0 (frozen). EPOCH START (torch.no_grad): e_k = sum_{l=1..L} Codebook_l[SID_l(k)]; x_k = exp_0(e_k / R); for each j: s^(0) = x_j; repeat 3 times: s^(t+1) = exp_{s^(t)}( sum_k P_jk log_{s^(t)}( x_k ) ); store s_j, w_j = conc_j*m_j/(m_j+m_med). FORWARD per batch: r = clamp(||z||/R, 1e-6, r_max); v = z/R; x = tanh(r)*v/r; d = log_x(s_j); beta = w_j * d; x' = x (+) tanh(sqrt(c) lambda_x ||beta||/2) * beta/(sqrt(c)||beta||); v' = R * artanh(clamp(sqrt(c)||x'||, 0, 1-1e-3)) * x'/(sqrt(c)||x'||); feed v' to RQ. LOSS unchanged: ||z - Dec(zq(v'))||^2 + commitment + codebook terms. Switch BEHAVIOUR_OFF: w_j = 0. Switch GEOMETRY_OFF: v' = R*((1-w) z/R + w log_0(s_j)).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder output z (B x d), frozen tables {P_jk, conc_j, m_j}, m_med, R, current codebooks and SID assignment, fixed c = 1. Outputs: transported latent v' (B x d) with the same shape and scale as z for the quantizer, plus the per-item (s_j, w_j) cache refreshed once per epoch. No change to the decoder, the RQ-VAE loss or the downstream generative model interface.
SAFEGUARDS: clip all radii with rho_max = (1 - 1e-3)/sqrt(c) before every exp/log and inside Möbius addition; floor 1 - c||x||^2 at 1e-6 in lambda_x and in every denominator; apply artanh to inputs clipped at 1 - 1e-6; when ||x - s||_g < 1e-8 return x (log gives 0); when an item has fewer than 2 kept successors or m_j = 0, set conc_j = 0 and s_j = x_j so the transport is the exact identity; detach s_j and w_j (no gradient through the transition table or the consensus) and recompute them only at epoch boundaries to avoid oscillation; clamp w_j <= 1 (guaranteed since m_j/(m_j+m_med) < 1); if the epoch-level mean ||v' - z||/||z|| exceeds 1.0, scale all w_j by that ratio for the epoch (a measured, self-limiting normalization, not a tuned constant); keep the RQ codebook and commitment terms byte-identical to the baseline; run the whole transport in float32 even under mixed precision.
END IDEA
