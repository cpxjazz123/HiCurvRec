IDEA: BG-LORENTZ-ST
TITLE: Behaviour-gated Lorentz parallel transport of the RQ-VAE straight-through gradient (curvature on the gradient path, not on the codebook)
MOTIVATION: In an RQ-VAE the encoder only ever receives the reconstruction straight-through gradient, so the latent geometry that fixes the SID prefixes is never told how users actually move between items; hubs near the Poincare origin and tail items near the boundary also get the same effective step size. This puts curvature and training-only transition behaviour *inside the gradient that shapes the codes*.
NOVELTY: Prior rounds changed the codebook (successor-weighted Frechet means), the per-level curvature (prefix-transition-score schedule) and the assignment radius (behaviour-ranked radial assignment). None touched the straight-through gradient path. Here the behaviour signal rotates the transported gradient in the tangent frame and the hyperbolic metric rescales it by radius; the quantizer and codebooks keep their existing Euclidean form.
CHANGE: The decoder->encoder straight-through gradient is lifted to the Lorentz model, parallel transported from the chosen code point back to the encoder tangent point, rotated in that tangent frame toward the item's successor direction by an angle derived from the item's successor entropy, and mapped back.
SPECIFICATION: see full block in final record.
COST: +1-3% wall clock; ~20k sparse gathers + O(B*d) map/transport/rotation per step at B~1023, 72,000 steps total.
HYPOTHESIS: on one run, per-level code usage entropy and prefix-collision behaviour shift, the cosine between transported and raw ST gradient tracks w_i, and Stage3 test_recall@10 rises above 0.065.
RISKS: optimizer trick with no objective anchoring; rotation may fight the reconstruction objective; sparse transition rows noisy.
