RATIONALE: successor_affinity_metric | A low-rank latent metric regularized by sparse, train-only successor affinity is a plausible route to behavior-aware SID neighborhoods and is meaningfully different from adding explicit successor-profile coordinates. Its scientific rationale and potential ranking impact are strong, but implementation and optimization are less certain: the affinity is exposure-biased, the specified smoothed PMI-style score may be unstable for rare edges, minibatch edge coverage can yield intermittent gradients, and the auxiliary loss could collapse useful content distinctions. It is related to the transition-role feature idea, so experimental distinctness is somewhat lower; the key uncertainty is whether the regularizer adds signal beyond Stage 3 learning transitions itself.
