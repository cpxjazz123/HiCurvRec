IDEA: transition_role_residual
TITLE: Quantize a train-only directed transition-role residual
MOTIVATION: Items with similar text can play different roles in user trajectories: an item often used as a bridge into one family of next items may not be interchangeable with a visually similar item that is usually an endpoint or repeat. Text-only reconstruction can collapse these behaviourally distinct roles and hurt next-item ranking.
NOVELTY: This adds a compact directed successor-role signal to the input geometry before ordinary global RQ-VAE quantization. It is not level reordering, graph denoising of embedding residuals, popularity/demand weighting, or separate successor code branches: every item still receives one global SID from one residual quantizer.
CHANGE: Estimate each training item's smoothed distribution over coarse successor-text clusters from adjacent train-only events, compress that distribution into a small vector, and concatenate a low-weight normalized version to its text embedding before RQ-VAE. Freeze this feature before the 3000-epoch optimization; all items, including items without observed transitions, use the same global feature map and quantizer.
SPECIFICATION: Fit K=64 spherical k-means centroids c_k on train-item embeddings, and assign each item j to g(j)=argmin_k ||e_j-c_k||². From each row with nonempty history, count directed adjacency (history[-1]=i,target=j) into A[i,g(j)]. Define p_i=(A[i,:]+alpha*u)/(sum_k A[i,k]+alpha), where u_k is the global train successor-cluster frequency normalized to sum 1 and alpha=5. Let q_i=log(p_i/u+1e-6), center each dimension over train items and divide by its train standard deviation (floor 1e-3), then clip to [-3,3]. Set x_i=[e_i, lambda*q_i] with lambda=0.1*sqrt(mean(e²)/mean(q²)); train existing RQ-VAE on x_i, and export its hard indices for all items. Use zero-count prior p_i=u for unseen items. The extra coordinates are auxiliary geometry only; retain existing collision extension to ensure unique global IDs.
COST: One offline pass over train adjacencies plus k-means on 24,556 vectors; negligible relative to training. Per-step cost is the baseline RQ-VAE plus first-layer work for 64 extra inputs (under 0.1% for the 512-wide encoder), with no added per-step graph operation. At 24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps, the added computation is effectively constant-factor-negligible; feature construction is paid once.
HYPOTHESIS: Within-text-neighborhood variation in directed successor roles is predictive of the next item beyond text similarity. Keep it if held-out Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.06335 and train-only diagnostics show nontrivial successor-profile variation among nearby text items; the strongest supporting observation is improved recall among items sharing a text cluster but differing in successor distributions.
RISKS: Sparse item transitions make profiles noisy despite smoothing; coarse successor clusters may erase useful distinctions; concatenation can distort the reconstruction scale or spend scarce code capacity on noise. The frozen Stage3 model may also fail to exploit the modified SID geometry even if the profiles are informative.
END IDEA
