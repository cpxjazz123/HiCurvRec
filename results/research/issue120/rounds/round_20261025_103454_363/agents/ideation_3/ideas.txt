IDEA: successor_affinity_metric
TITLE: Tilt quantization toward observed next-item neighborhoods
MOTIVATION: Items that occur as plausible next steps in similar training histories can be recommendation substitutes even when their text embeddings are far apart; a reconstruction-only tokenizer can therefore spend SID distinctions on wording rather than on behaviorally useful neighborhoods.
NOVELTY: Keep one global, deterministic SID per item and the ordinary residual quantizer, but learn a small behavior-derived metric on its encoder space. This does not add context-dependent IDs, extra tokens, Sinkhorn balancing, or a transition-graph denoising pass over the quantizer inputs.
CHANGE: Estimate sparse item-to-item successor affinity from training sequences, then train the encoder to make high-affinity item pairs close under a low-rank Mahalanobis metric while retaining the baseline reconstruction and quantization losses. At export, use the same learned encoder and ordinary nearest-codeword assignments for every item.
SPECIFICATION: From train sequences only, count directed adjacent pairs n_ij and outgoing totals n_i. Define a symmetric affinity a_ij = 0.5*[log((n_ij+α)/(n_i+αV) / p_j) + log((n_ji+α)/(n_j+αV) / p_i)] for observed edges, clamp below at zero, retain each item's top m=8 neighbors, and row-normalize to w_ij. Let h_i=U z_i, where z_i is the existing encoder output and U is a learned r×d matrix initialized to the first r rows of identity (r=16). Add L_succ = (1/|E_B|) Σ_(i,j)∈E_B w_ij ||h_i-h_j||², with α=1 and E_B the retained edges whose endpoints occur in the current minibatch (use cached sampled edges if none); optimize L_RQ + λ L_succ, λ=0.05, and do not apply the auxiliary term to validation/test interactions. Maintain the existing assignment and collision-resolution export unchanged.
COST: Precompute successor counts/top-8 edges once in O(number of training transitions); store at most 8 edges per training item. Per update, use at most 8 sampled in-batch edge pairs and one rank-16 projection per participating item, O(B·16d + 8·16), versus the baseline's O(B·3Kd) codebook distance work. At the frozen scale (24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps), the only recurring extra work is this small projection/pair loss; no extra full-catalog pass or additional quantizer lookup is required per step.
HYPOTHESIS: Train transitions contain stable successor neighborhoods that are not already recoverable from text, and making those neighborhoods share nearby latent representations improves the grouping/prefixes learned by Stage 2. Keep it only if a fixed Stage 3 comparison improves test_R@10 over 0.06335 and train-only diagnostics show stronger retained-edge affinity in latent/SID neighborhoods without a material rise in reconstruction error or ID collisions.
RISKS: Sequential adjacency is noisy and exposure/popularity-biased; over-regularization may collapse distinct items or hurt content generalization. Sparse minibatch coverage may make the auxiliary gradient intermittent, and the recommendation benefit may be small if Stage 3 already learns these transitions independently of the SIDs.
END IDEA
