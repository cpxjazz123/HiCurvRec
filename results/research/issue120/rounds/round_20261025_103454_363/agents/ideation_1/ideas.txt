IDEA: target_axis_metric
TITLE: Weight content axes by train-only next-item utility
MOTIVATION: A content embedding may devote much of its capacity to distinctions that do not help predict what users choose next; uniform reconstruction can spend early SID capacity on those axes and blur distinctions useful for next-item ranking.
NOVELTY: Unlike item-level demand signatures, transition residuals, or changing the quantizer geometry, this keeps each item represented by its existing content vector and learns a single diagonal metric from train-only next-item prediction, applying it only to reconstruction/quantization error.
CHANGE: Estimate which embedding dimensions carry train-only next-item signal, freeze those weights before RQ-VAE training, and measure reconstruction in the weighted metric. The encoder, codebooks, residual assignment, and one-global-SID-per-item contract otherwise remain unchanged.
SPECIFICATION: From chronological training sequences form pairs (i,j) of consecutive items; fit ridge logistic regression p(j|i) ∝ exp(b_j + x_i^T W x_j) on training pairs only, with fixed regularization and a held-out train-only split for fitting. Let s_d = mean over sampled pairs of |(x_i)_d (W x_j)_d|; set w_d = clip((s_d+ε)^α / mean_k((s_k+ε)^α), w_min, w_max), then renormalize mean(w)=1. Freeze w and replace each embedding-space squared error ||a-b||² in the RQ-VAE reconstruction loss with Σ_d w_d(a_d-b_d)²; quantizer internals are unchanged. If fitting W is impractical, use a diagonal ridge probe and the same formula.
COST: One-time train-pair probe fit is O(P·D) per ridge iteration for P sampled transitions and embedding dimension D; it does not run each training step. The per-step loss adds O(B·D) multiplies for batch size B, negligible beside the baseline encoder/decoder and codebook distance calculations; at 24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps, no additional model forward or assignment is required.
HYPOTHESIS: The train-only next-item signal has a concentrated, repeatable set of content axes, and preserving those axes better produces SIDs whose prefixes distinguish items that matter for user choices. Keep it only if repeated training-sequence splits yield stable weights and Stage3 test_R@10 improves over 0.06335 without relying on validation/test interactions to derive weights.
RISKS: The probe can overfit sparse transitions or encode popularity rather than transferable preference; diagonal weights miss useful cross-axis combinations; train/test distribution shift can invalidate the estimated utility; poorly chosen clipping or regularization can make the metric effectively uniform or collapse dimensions.
END IDEA
