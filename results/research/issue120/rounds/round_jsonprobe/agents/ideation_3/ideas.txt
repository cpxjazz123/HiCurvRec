IDEA: beh-rapidity-expstep-rq
TITLE: Behaviour-rapidity geodesic stepping of every RQ residual query, with rapidity capped by the measured quantization cell radius
MOTIVATION: A generative recommender decodes SID prefixes autoregressively from the user history, so what makes the early tokens useful is the direction in which an item's code deviates from its content neighbours toward the items users actually go to next. A content-only RQ-VAE spends all of its levels on reconstruction, so level-1 and level-2 inclusive prefixes under-determine the next item and Stage 3 recall stalls near the baseline. Items that share a successor distribution should share a prefix; nothing in a plain Euclidean RQ-VAE enforces that.
NOVELTY: Distinct from every mechanism already tried in this programme: it is not a successor-weighted Fréchet codebook refit (poincare-frechet-successor-codebook), not a per-level curvature field driven by prefix-transition scores (CURVCTRL-SID), not a radial ranking of codes by context entropy (BRad-RQ), not successor-profile smoothing, behaviour cones, branching-curvature MoE or a successor shift of the embedding. The codebook, the curvature, the losses and the level structure are all untouched. The single new object is an isometric exponential-map step inserted into the residual-query recursion: because it is a Lorentz isometry it preserves the query's hyperbolic norm, so it steers code assignment without inflating the reconstruction residual (a shift cannot do this), and because it is applied before every level's nearest-code search the behaviour signal recurses through the whole prefix hierarchy instead of touching one level. Its rapidity is a product of two measured quantities (transition top-2 margin, hyperbolic radial depth) capped by a measured quantization scale, so no constant is tuned.
CHANGE: At each RQ level l the query is replaced by r~ = exp_r(eta_i^(l) * u_i^(l)), one geodesic step of length eta in the tangent direction that points at the item's training-successor aggregate; codes are then chosen by Lorentz geodesic distance, while the residual carried to level l+1 is taken from the unstepped query so the reconstruction scale is unchanged.
SPECIFICATION: precompute once from training transitions -> per-item unit tangent u_i and margin m_i; measure median cell radius s_l per level at init; in every forward pass, for l=1..L: project u_i onto the tangent space at the current residual query, set eta = m_i * min(rho_i, s_l), step the query into exp_r(eta*u), pick k_l = argmax_k (-<r~, c_k>_L), emit token l, advance the Euclidean residual with the unstepped query. All eta, m, u, rho are detached; gradients flow through exp and exp_r into the encoder.
COST: At the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs, batch 256, M=256 codes, d=64, L=3): extra per step is ~3*256 tangent projections (64 flops each) + 2 cosh/sinh per item per level + one extra time component in the existing B*M*d code comparison, i.e. about 1.2 MFLOP, under 1% of the step's matmul cost; measured cost expectation +1% to +3% wall clock. One-off: sparse successor aggregate (nnz ~ 20*24556 times d ~ 31 MFLOP) and one 4096-item median per level at initialisation, i.e. seconds. No extra encoder forward pass, no extra parameters, no extra memory beyond three (n_items x d) frozen matrices.
HYPOTHESIS: Behaviour steering of code assignment is only worth keeping i

f it re-assigns prefixes without destroying reconstruction. It must therefore be true that level-1 and level-2 code usage entropy stay within roughly 10% of the behaviour-off value and per-level reconstruction loss degrades by less than roughly 10% relative, while the behaviour-alignment diagnostic (share of items whose top successor sits in the same level-1 code) rises clearly above its behaviour-off value and Stage3 test_recall@10 lands above 0.065. If reconstruction degrades much more than that, the step is damaging cells rather than steering them and recall should not improve; that single run decides it.
RISKS: ["The step could rotate queries out of their own cell, raising reconstruction loss and starving rarely used codes, which would cost recall instead of gaining it.", "If successor directions are noisy (sparse transitions), m_i is near zero for most items and the mechanism silently reduces to the plain hyperbolic RQ baseline, i.e. no effect.", "With only 3000 epochs on four cards one run cannot separate a real gain from seed noise, so the effect size must be large enough to be visible in a single run.", "Applying the step before every level could compound displacements so that deep levels encode behaviour direction instead of residual content, hurting fine reconstruction.", "The measured cell radius s_l is fixed after initialisation, so a codebook that moves a lot during training may leave the cap mismatched to the current cell size."]
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transitions of the training split, summarised per item as a unit successor direction plus a transition concentration (top-2 margin); both are training-only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-1/2 training interaction sequences only: build the sparse count matrix N with n_ij = number of training sessions in which item j is the successor of item i (window 1); normalise rows to p_ij = n_ij / sum_j n_ij; with the frozen pre-Stage-2 content embeddings Z (n_items x d) compute b_i = sum_j p_ij (z_j - z_i), u_i = PT_{o->x_i}([0, b_i]) (a Lorentz tangent vector at the item's lifted point), and m_i = (n_(1) - n_(2)) / (n_(1) + n_(2)) from the two largest outgoing counts (n_(2) = 0 if the item has one successor; m_i = 0 if the item has no training successor). Computed once before Stage 2 and frozen.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: N is counted only over training sequences; Z is the frozen content embedding of training items plus item metadata; u_i, m_i and s_l are functions of those and of training items only. No validation or test interaction, and no validation-derived statistic, enters the SID construction. Validation/test quantities appear only in post-hoc diagnostics and Stage 3 scoring, never in the operator, the codebook or the cap.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed unit negative curvature (kappa = 1) Lorentz hyperboloid; the operator is the exponential map exp_r(eta*u) = r*cosh(eta) + u*sinh(eta) along a unit tangent u at r (a geodesic translation, an isometry of the hyperboloid), and code selection uses the geodesic distance in the stable form d_L(a,b) = 2*asinh(||a-b||_M / 2).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the Stage-2 residual quantizer, at the assignment step of every RQ level, applied to the residual query immediately before the nearest-code search; through the straight-through estimator it also acts on the gradient that reaches the RQ-VAE encoder.
COUPLING PATH: residual query r_(l-1) -> behaviour tangent u_i and rapidity eta_i^(l) = m_i * min(rho_i, s_l) -> hyperbolic exponenti

al step r~ = exp_r(eta*u) -> Lorentz nearest-code argmin -> code k_l -> SID token l -> and (via the unstepped query) the Euclidean residual that feeds level l+1, plus the straight-through gradient into the encoder. Removing behaviour (m_i = 0) makes the step the identity and the pathway becomes plain hyperbolic RQ; removing curvature (Euclidean addition and Euclidean distance) changes both the step and the argmin. Neither term is decoration: each changes the computed code sequence.
COUPLING EQUATION: r~_i^(l) = exp_{r_i^(l-1)}( m_i * min(rho_i, s_l) * u_i^(l) ), k_l = argmin_k d_L(r~_i^(l), c_k), with rho_i = arcosh(<x_i, e_0>_L) the radial depth of the item latent and u_i^(l) the behaviour successor direction orthogonalised against the current query.
OBSERVABLES: level-wise code usage entropy; behaviour-alignment rate (fraction of items whose most frequent training successor shares the level-1 code) as a diagnostic; held-out prefix-transition log-likelihood as a diagnostic; the ratio of encoder gradient norms for high-margin versus zero-margin items (should scale like cosh(eta)); per-level reconstruction loss; Stage3 test_recall@10 as the only judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: m_i = 0 for every item (successor matrix zeroed at load time), with Lorentz distances, the measured caps s_l, the codebook and all other heads unchanged, so eta = 0, the step is exactly the identity and the run is the hyperbolic RQ baseline.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp_r(eta*u) replaced by the tangent-space linear step r + eta*u and d_L replaced by the Euclidean distance, while eta and u keep their full behaviour dependence, so behaviour still steers but curvature is gone.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run described here, trained once on the frozen budget with no early stopping and no other arm run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Let x = exp_o([0, z]) = (cosh rho, sinh rho * zhat), rho = ||z||, zhat = z/rho, e_0 = (1,0), <a,b>_L = -a_0 b_0 + a.b. SID levels l = 1..L with Lorentz codes c_k = exp_o([0, w_k]). Residual stream v_0 = z, query r_l = exp_o(v_l). For each level: (1) transport the frozen successor direction u_i from x_i to the query: u <- PT(r_l) and project, u_perp = u + <u, r_l>_L * r_l, u_hat = u_perp / sqrt(1 + <u, r_l>_L^2); (2) eta_i^(l) = m_i * min(rho_i, s_l); (3) r~ = r_l*cosh(eta) + u_hat*sinh(eta); (4) k_l = argmax_k (-<r~, c_k>_L); (5) v_{l+1} = v_l - w_{k_l} (Euclidean residual from the unstepped query, so the commit/reconstruction losses are unchanged in scale); (6) SID(i) = (k_1, ..., k_L). Caps: s_l = median over a frozen 4096-item training sample at initialisation of d_L(exp_o(v_{l-1}), c_{k_l}) taken with the behaviour-off selection. All of eta, m_i, u_i, rho_i and s_l are detached from the graph (torch.no_grad for the statistics); gradients pass through exp_o, exp_o(.) and exp_r only.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the encoder output z_i (d-dim), the Lorentz codebook {c_k} kept in tangent coordinates w_k, the frozen per-item arrays u_i (d-dim tangent components) and m_i (scalar), and the measured per-level caps s_l. Outputs: the discrete code sequence k_1..k_L that becomes the item's SID tuple, the per-level Lorentz distances for the usage/entropy diagnostics, and the Euclidean residual stream for the unchanged reconstruction and commit losses.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp eta to [0, s_l] and floor s_l at 1e-3; if ||b_i|| < 1e-8 or the item has no training successor set m_i = 0 and eta = 0 (exact identity, cold items fall back to plain RQ); compute d_L in the

2*asinh(||a-b||_M/2) form and clamp the arcosh argument used for rho_i to at least 1+1e-7 to avoid NaN gradients; normalise u_hat with the closed-form 1/sqrt(1+a^2) so it stays a unit tangent up to float error; use -<r~,c_k>_L for the argmin (no arcosh in the hot loop) and score-tie-break by the smaller code index for determinism; guard cosh/sinh against overflow by the eta <= s_l clip (s_l is a median of small geodesic lengths, so eta stays O(1)); keep the behaviour statistics in float32 outside the autograd graph and freeze the successor matrix after the one-off build; rebuild nothing from validation data; log level-wise usage entropy every epoch so a collapsed level is visible.
END IDEA
