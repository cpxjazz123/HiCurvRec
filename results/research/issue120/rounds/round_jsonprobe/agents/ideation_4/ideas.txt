IDEA: COCON-GEODESIC-RQ
TITLE: Co-consumption graph as the hyperbolic aggregation operator: curved Karcher item points feeding a geodesic residual RQ-VAE.
MOTIVATION: An item's RQ-VAE point comes only from its own encoder row, so two items that users consume together can land in unrelated regions of the ball and receive unrelated SID prefixes; the generator then has to memorise the pairing instead of predicting a shared prefix. The defect is item-side: the point that gets quantized ignores the popularity-adjusted co-consumption neighbourhood.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms act on the codebook (successor-weighted Karcher/Frechet code mass), on the curvature schedule per RQ level (measured hard prefix-transition score) and on the item radial coordinate (transition-context entropy). None changes the aggregation OPERATOR that builds the item point, and none uses constructor co-consumption. Here negative curvature is the metric in which the behaviour neighbourhood is averaged (a Riemannian Karcher mean over Poincare log maps), and the same metric then defines the RQ residual transport, so behaviour enters as edge weights of one equation and curvature as the operator and distance in that same equation.
CHANGE: The item point becomes a 2-step geodesic Karcher mean of its own embedding and its popularity-adjusted co-consumption neighbours in the Poincare ball; the quantizer then assigns codes by geodesic distance and carries the residual to the next level by parallel transport to the origin instead of Euclidean subtraction.
SPECIFICATION: Build the symmetric popularity-adjusted co-consumption graph from training sequences only: pmi_ij = log(n_ij * N / (n_i n_j)), keep neighbours with pmi_ij > log 2, cap 16 by pmi, row-normalise with self weight 1. Point: x_0 = v_i; for t in 1..2 compute g = sum_j w_ij log_x(v_j) and set x = exp_x(g). Quantize z_i = x_2 with geodesic argmin per level, residual r = log_{c_l}(u_l), next query u_{l+1} = exp_0(r / (1 - c|c_l|^2)). Loss = baseline reconstruction plus commitment rewritten with squared geodesic distance. c = 1 fixed, no sweep.
COST: One-time graph build over training sequences (24556 items, at most 16 stored neighbours, about 1.6 MB). Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps per epoch, batch about 1023 items, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps): about 34 log maps plus 2 exp maps for aggregation and about 9 manifold ops for the 3-level residual chain per item, roughly 45 transcendental 64-dim vector ops per item, about 46k per step, under 10 percent of a step's wall clock; total Stage 2 wall clock +8 to +15 percent, no extra epochs, no extra passes.
HYPOTHESIS: If co-consumed items become geodesically close before quantization, they should share RQ prefixes more often and the generator should predict a prefix that transfers to unseen items, showing up as Stage 3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 in the single coupled run, together with a lower prefix-collision rate for high-lift co-consumption pairs than the baseline Euclidean RQ-VAE records.
RISKS: Long-tail items have too few lift>=2 partners, so z_i stays near v_i and the mechanism is inert exactly where SIDs matter.; Hyperbolic averaging can over-smooth and collapse distinct items onto one code, raising collisions and possibly hurting recall.; 2 Karcher iterations may leave a convergence bias that appears as inflated commitment loss and slightly shifted code usage.; If the generator already fits prefixes without item-side structure, the recall gain may sit inside single-seed noise.; The geometry-off and behaviour-off comparisons exist only as code switches, so the causal claim rests on one trained run.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted co-consumption neighbours: pointwise-mutual-information-weighted symmetric item pairs formed inside user training sequences (sequential co-consumption, popularity corrected by PMI).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage 1 and Stage 2 training sequences only. For each training sequence count every unordered item pair present (no time-window constant is introduced: the whole training sequence of a user defines co-consumption). n_i, n_j are the numbers of training sequences containing the item and N the number of training sequences; pmi_ij = log(n_ij N / (n_i n_j)). Keep neighbours with pmi_ij > log 2 (lift at least 2, a rule-based significance-style threshold), rank by pmi and keep at most 16 (compute cap at 64 dims). Rows are normalised to sum to one and the self weight is fixed to 1.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Pair counts, PMI, neighbour lists and row weights are computed from the frozen training split before Stage 2 starts and are never recomputed during Stage 3 or from validation or test interactions. No validation or test item, sequence or co-occurrence is read at any point of SID construction, and the final SID assignment pass uses only item identity plus its training-derived weight row, so an unseen test interaction cannot alter a code.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of fixed negative curvature c = 1 (unit curvature, the canonical scale of the ball, not tuned): Moebius log and exp maps, geodesic distance d_c(u,v) = (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c) |(-u) (+) v|), the Karcher mean built from log maps, and parallel transport of the level residual to the origin.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Both in training (inside the encoder, where the item point that will be quantized is produced, and inside the quantizer's argmin and residual chain of every RQ level, hence in the gradient reaching the encoder) and in the final SID assignment pass, which applies the same operator to all 24556 items.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour weights -> geodesic Karcher aggregation -> z_i -> geodesic RQ assignment and transport -> SID prefix. The behaviour weights are the coefficients of the log maps defining z_i and curvature is the metric in which those log maps, the Karcher update and the code distance are computed, so dropping the edges changes z_i and dropping curvature changes the operator that both produces and quantizes z_i. There is no separate behaviour loss and no separate geometric loss.
COUPLING EQUATION: SID_i = RQ_c(z_i), z_i = argmin_{x in B_c} sum_{j in {i} U N(i)} w_ij^behaviour * d_c(x, v_j)^2, solved as x <- exp_x^c( sum_j w_ij log_x^c(v_j) ); per level k_l = argmin_k d_c(u_l, c_{l,k}) with u_{l+1} = exp_0^c( log_{c_{l,k}}^c(u_l) / (1 - c |c_{l,k}|^2) ).
OBSERVABLES: Share rate of SID prefixes for high-lift co-consumption pairs at level 1 and level 2; the distribution of geodesic radii of item points; per-level code usage and collision counts; the commitment term expressed in geodesic distance; the prefix-transition predictability diagnostic (recorded, never gated); and finally Stage 3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Switch BEHAV_COCON = 0 exists in the code: neighbour rows are replaced by the identity row, so the Karcher update degenerates to the item alone (z_i = v_i) while the curved quantizer stays active. Not trained in this round; it exists so the behaviour contribution is well defined.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Switch GEOM_CURV = 0 exists in the code and takes c -> 0, where log, exp and parallel transport become the identity, the Karcher mean becomes the weighted Euclidean mean of the same neighbours and d_c becomes the Euclidean distance, i.e. exactly a Euclidean RQ-VAE with the same behaviour graph. Not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Switches BEHAV_COCON = 1 and GEOM_CURV = 1, the single trained candidate reported as the round result: full coupling, one run, 3000 Stage 2 epochs on four cards, frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep, no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: lambda_x = 2/(1 - c|x|^2). u (+) v = ((1 + 2c<u,v> + c|v|^2)u + (1 - c|u|^2)v) / (1 + 2c<u,v> + c^2|u|^2|v|^2). d_c(u,v) = (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)|(-u)(+)v|). exp_x(v) = x (+) (tanh(sqrt(c) lambda_x |v|/2) v/(sqrt(c)|v|)). log_x(y) = (2/(sqrt(c) lambda_x)) artanh(sqrt(c)|(-x)(+)y|) ((-x)(+)y)/|(-x)(+)y|. PT_{0<-c}(r) = lambda_c r / 2 = r/(1 - c|c|^2). Aggregation: a_ii = 1, a_ij = pmi_ij / sum_{j' in N(i)} pmi_ij', w_ij = a_ij / sum_j a_ij; x_0 = v_i; for t = 1,2: g_t = sum_j w_ij log_{x_{t-1}}(v_j); x_t = exp_{x_{t-1}}(g_t); z_i = x_2. Quantization: u_1 = z_i; for l = 1..L: k_l = argmin_k d_c(u_l, c_{l,k}); u_{l+1} = exp_0( log_{c_{l,k_l}}(u_l) / (1 - c|c_{l,k_l}|^2) ). Loss: L = L_recon(z_i) + beta sum_l [ d_c(u_l, c_{l,k_l})^2 + d_c(sg[u_l], c_{l,k_l})^2 ] with the baseline beta, baseline L_recon, and 3 levels x 256 codes inherited unchanged. Constants: c = 1, lift threshold log 2, k <= 16, T = 2, radius clamp 0.9/sqrt(c), all derived rules or documented defaults, none swept.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: per item the encoder embedding v_i in the ball (64 dims), the training-only row (neighbour ids, normalised PMI weights) of at most 16 entries, the level codebooks, and the fixed curvature c. Outputs: the aggregated point z_i, the L prefix code indices forming the item SID, and the geodesic commitment loss whose gradient reaches both the encoder and the codebooks.
SAFEGUARDS: Project every ball point and every exp-map output to radius at most 0.9/sqrt(c) to bound lambda_x; clamp tangent norms to 1/sqrt(c) before exp; evaluate artanh with its argument clipped to 1 - 1e-7 and add epsilon inside all norms; add 1e-8 to counts before the PMI ratio and drop pairs with n_ij < 2 to avoid single-sequence PMI spikes; cap stored neighbours at 16 and floor-normalise rows so no single neighbour exceeds 0.9 of the row mass; break argmin ties deterministically by code index so assigned SIDs are reproducible; if a row has no kept neighbour use the self weight alone (identity fallback) so no division by zero occurs; skip the Karcher update for items already within 1e-4 of the radius clamp; log the count of items left with identity rows as a diagnostic only.
END IDEA
