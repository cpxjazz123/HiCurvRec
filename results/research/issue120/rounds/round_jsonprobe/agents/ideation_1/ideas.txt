IDEA: BML-OT-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-marginal Lorentzian optimal-transport quantization of the RQ-VAE SID prefix hierarchy
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE Semantic IDs are fitted by Euclidean nearest neighbour with a uniform softmax, so every training item counts the same and no transition structure enters the assignment. Coarse level-1 prefixes drift towards content-similar but behaviourally unrelated items, codes no user ever traverses keep receiving gradient, and the Stage-3 generator spends its small prefix vocabulary on catalogue regions that carry almost no training traffic.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds here moved the codebook centroids (successor-weighted Frechet means), the per-level curvature (prefix transition score), or the item radius (context entropy). This proposal moves none of those: it replaces the ASSIGNMENT OPERATOR of every RQ level with an entropic optimal-transport plan whose row marginals are training-transition masses and whose row temperature is the item's successor concentration, while the transport cost is the squared Lorentz geodesic distance on a hyperboloid whose negative curvature is measured from the data. The entropic scale is the batch median transport cost, so no constant is swept. Nesting comes from a Mobius-subtraction residual chain, so behaviour mass flows from the coarse prefix down to the fine levels.
CHANGE: The per-level softmax/nearest-neighbour assignment becomes a 20-iteration log-domain Sinkhorn plan over a behaviour-mass marginal and a successor-concentration-scaled Lorentz cost, and the residual handed to the next level is the Mobius difference between the item's Poincare point and the chosen code's Poincare point instead of a linear subtraction.
SPECIFICATION: Offline (training sequences only): N_ij = count of directed adjacent transitions i->j; P_ij = N_ij / sum_j' N_ij'; behaviour mass m_i = (indegree_i + 1) / sum_i' (indegree_i' + 1); successor concentration w_i = max_j P_ij (fallback: mean w over items with a successor). Geometry: R = median_i ||z_i||_2 measured once from the frozen initial encoder at epoch 0, c = 1/R^2, frozen. Codes are trainable tangent vectors u_lk with hyperboloid points E(u) = (cosh(sqrt(c)||u||), sinh(sqrt(c)||u||) u/(sqrt(c)||u||)). Per step, per level l = 1..L: x_i = E(z_i^l), z_i^1 = encoder output; C_ik = w_i * d_L(x_i, E(u_lk))^2 with d_L(x,y) = (1/sqrt(c)) arccosh(clamp(-c<x,y>_L, 1+1e-7, inf)); eps = stopgrad(median_ik C_ik); K_ik = exp(-C_ik/eps); log-domain Sinkhorn, 20 iterations, marginals a = m/sum_batch(m) and r = 1/Kc; row-conditional plan gamma_ik; soft code cbar_i = sum_k gamma_ik u_lk / sum_k' gamma_ik'; hard code k*_i = argmax_k gamma_ik straight-through to decoder and SID token; baseline RQ losses unchanged with cbar_i; curved residual p_i = tanh(sqrt(c)||z_i^l||/2) z_i^l/||z_i^l||, p'_i = p_i (+) (-q_i) with q_i the chosen code's Poincare point, z_i^{l+1} = (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)||p'_i||) p'_i/||p'_i||.
COST: At the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps per epoch = about 1023 items per step, Kc = 256 codes per level, L = 3, 20 Sinkhorn iterations, latent dim about 32) each step adds one 1023x256 Lorentz cost matrix per level (about 26 MFLOP and 0.8 M arccosh) and about 31 M exponentials plus 120 reductions for Sinkhorn: roughly 3-6 ms on one modern GPU against a 20-60 ms baseline step, i.e. 5-15 percent step overhead, about 4-7 minutes of extra GPU time over all 72000 steps of 3000 epochs, with about 2 MB transient memory per level. The one-off precompute is a single sparse counting pass over the training sequences plus one encoder pass at epoch 0 to fix R; no per-epoch precompute and no SVD.
HYPOTHESIS: If the coupling is real, the level-1 plan is measurably sharper for high-successor-concentration items than for branching items (row entropy of gamma monotonically decreasing across w_i deciles), code usage stays near log(Kc) because of the uniform column marginal, and Stage-3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065, with the level-1 token alone narrowing the candidate set far below the catalogue.
RISKS: Sinkhorn divergence or NaN on degenerate rows; straight-through argmax disagreeing with the soft plan and starving the decoder of coherent codes; popularity-mass marginals concentrating the codebook on head items and hurting tail SIDs; the measured curvature R making the ball too flat so the Lorentz cost is nearly Euclidean; over-soft assignment delaying convergence inside the fixed 3000 epochs; the changed plan effectively altering the codebook learning-rate behaviour
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions of the training split, summarised as successor concentration w_i (row-max of the row-normalised transition matrix) and behaviour mass m_i (add-one successor in-degree, batch-normalised).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training-split sequences only, counting adjacent directed pairs (item at position t to item at t+1) into N_ij, row-normalising to P_ij, and forming m_i from the column sums of N; both become fixed length-24556 tensors gathered by item id during training.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: N_ij and m_i are built exclusively from training-split sequences; the SID codebooks are fitted with those fixed tensors and neither validation nor test sequences are read at any point of SID construction, so no evaluation interaction can reach the codebooks, the marginals or the residual chain.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Lorentz geodesic distance on the hyperboloid of constant curvature -c, c = 1/R^2 with R the epoch-0 median latent norm, used as optimal-transport cost; plus Mobius addition with additive inverse in the Poincare ball of the same curvature for the residual chain, and the exp/log maps at the origin between tangent space and hyperboloid/ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside quantisation, at every RQ level: the Lorentz distance defines the transport cost of the assignment plan and the Mobius subtraction defines the residual the next level receives; both act in the Stage-2 forward/backward pass and therefore shape the SID tokens consumed later.
COUPLING PATH: Behaviour tensors (w_i, m_i) enter the transport-cost row scale and the row marginal of the Sinkhorn plan; the plan's row-conditional weights assign the item to hyperboloid codes and the plan's hard argmax code is Mobius-subtracted from the item's Poincare point to give the next-level residual, which is re-costed by the same behaviour-scaled Lorentz distance at the next level; removing w_i and m_i (uniform marginals, unit scale) or replacing the Lorentz cost and Mobius residual by Euclidean ones changes the plan, the codes and every downstream residual, so both factors are load-bearing in one computation.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ik = w_i * d_L(x_i, e_lk)^2 with row marginal a_i = m_i / sum_batch(m) and column marginal r = 1/Kc; plan gamma = diag(u) exp(-C/eps) diag(v), eps = median(C); residual z^{l+1} = log_0( exp_0(z^l) (+) (- exp_0(u_{l k*})) ).
OBSERVABLES: Monotone decrease of level-1 mean row entropy of gamma across w_i deciles; per-level code-usage entropy staying near log(Kc) under the uniform column marginal; the behavioural diagnostics (SID coherence, transition adjacency rate, prefix transfer predictability) recorded only; per-level residual norms after Mobius subtraction; Stage-3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Same code with w_i set to 1 and a_i set to the uniform batch marginal, so Sinkhorn runs with isotropic Lorentz cost and no transition information; present behind a flag and never trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Same code with C_ik = ||z_i^l - u_lk||^2 Euclidean, entropic scale the Euclidean batch median, and the residual chain as plain linear subtraction; present behind a flag and never trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run: behaviour-scaled Lorentz cost, behaviour-mass row marginal, Sinkhorn plan, Mobius residual, one training run on the frozen budget with no early stopping and no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: N_ij from adjacent training transitions; P_ij = N_ij / sum_j' N_ij'; w_i = max_j P_ij; m_i = (sum_j N_ji + 1)/sum_i'(sum_j N_ji' + 1); c = 1/R^2, R = median_i ||z_i||_2 at epoch 0 frozen; E(u) = (cosh(sqrt(c)||u||), sinh(sqrt(c)||u||) u/(sqrt(c)||u||)); <x,y>_L = -x_0 y_0 + sum_j x_j y_j; d_L(x,y) = (1/sqrt(c)) arccosh(clamp(-c<x,y>_L, 1+1e-7, inf)); C_ik = w_i d_L(x_i, E(u_lk))^2; eps = sg(median_ik C_ik); L_sink(u,v) = sum_ik exp(-C_ik/eps + u_i + v_k) - sum_i a_i u_i - sum_k r_k v_k with 20 alternating exact minimisations in log domain; gamma_ik = exp(-C_ik/eps + u_i + v_k); cbar_i = sum_k gamma_ik u_lk / sum_k' gamma_ik'; k*_i = argmax_k gamma_ik; p = (tanh(sqrt(c)||v||/2)/||v||) v; log_0(p) = (2/sqrt(c)) artanh(sqrt(c)||p||) p/||p||; p (+) q = ((1 + 2c p.q + c||q||^2) p + (1 - c||p||^2) q) / (1 + 2c p.q + c^2 ||p||^2 ||q||^2); z^{l+1} = log_0( p(z^l) (+) (-p(u_{l k*})) ).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latent z_i, trainable code tangent vectors u_lk of level l, frozen per-item scalars w_i and m_i, measured R, flags behaviour_on and geometry_on. Outputs: row-conditional plan gamma_i, soft code cbar_i for the loss, hard code indices k*_i for the discrete SID token and decoder, and the next-level residual z_i^{l+1}.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp the Lorentz arccosh argument to at least 1+1e-7 and at most 1e18; run Sinkhorn fully in log domain with logsumexp stabilisation, stop early at max row-marginal deviation 1e-3 else 20 iterations, and fall back to the hard nearest code if any output is non-finite; clamp the Poincare norm to 1/sqrt(c) - 1e-5 before every exp/log and Mobius operation; use eps = median cost plus a floor of 1e-6 times the mean cost so a degenerate batch cannot divide by zero; gather m_i and w_i with an add-one floor so unseen or successor-less items get the documented fallback (uniform marginal, w = mean w); apply the straight-through estimator so the discrete SID seen by the decoder is always a real code; log per-step Sinkhorn residual, plan entropy and residual norm, and fall back to nearest-neighbour for that step only if the plan has negative entries beyond 1e-6; all constants are the measured curvature scale R, the median-cost entropic scale, the add-one floor and the 20-iteration cap, all documented defaults, none swept.
END IDEA
