IDEA: BRANCHCAP-HYPFANOUT
TITLE: Behaviour-branching hyperbolic capacity allocation: the number of SID slots each prefix subtree owns is derived from training transition mass and hyperbolic successor spread
MOTIVATION: An RQ-VAE SID gives every first-level prefix exactly the same number of child codewords. User behaviour is not uniform: a few prefixes are transition hubs whose continuations fan out broadly, while many prefixes lead to a narrow, almost deterministic next item. Uniform fanout spends most of the second/third-level capacity on prefixes with almost no future traffic and starves the hubs, so quantization error concentrates exactly where the generative recommender samples next tokens most often.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds kept the fanout schedule fixed and changed codebook update weights (Poincare-Frechet successors), per-level curvature (CURVCTRL-SID), per-item radius (BRad-RQ), Lorentz profile smoothing, behaviour cones, geodesic shifts or branch-curvature MoE. Here the structure of the code changes: the per-prefix code budget N_p is a derived quantity and each prefix owns a disjoint contiguous slice of the level codebook, so prefixes get unequal numbers of SID children. Nothing in the repository allocates codebook capacity, and nothing derives a capacity from a hyperbolic spread of successors.
CHANGE: At every level l>=2 the total slot budget N is split across prefixes by the high-rate quantization rule N_p proportional to (m_p * sigma_p^2)^(D/(D+2)), where m_p is the training transition mass leaving the prefix and sigma_p is the hyperbolic (Lorentz) Frechet spread of that prefix's training successors; the level-l assignment argmin is then restricted to prefix p's own slice of N_p codewords.
SPECIFICATION: Build W (top-M row-normalised directed transition counts) and m_p from training sequences only; measure s = median latent norm at init and fix K = -1/s^2; at every C epochs map the EMA latent table to the Lorentz model, compute for each prefix p the weighted Riemannian Frechet mean and spread with 5 fixed gradient steps, allocate N_p by largest-remainder rounding of (m_p sigma_p^2)^(D/(D+2)) with integer floor 1 and clipping of sigma_p to measured q05/q95, rebuild each prefix's codebook slice by 5-step warm-started k-means, and keep the standard EMA codeword update between refreshes.
COST: Negligible relative to baseline. Encoder/decoder/Stage 3 cost unchanged; the level-level argmin is the same total size (every item still scans N codewords, only within its own prefix slice). Added work is one allocation refresh every C=100 epochs: 30 refreshes over 3000 epochs, each 24556*8 = 196448 successor pairs, 5 Frechet iterations and 5 k-means iterations in ambient dim D+1 (D=32), about 1.6e7 flops per refresh and about 5e8 flops in total. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch) the added cost is one slice-offset lookup, below 0.5% wall clock; the transition matrix is built once before training.
HYPOTHESIS: If capacity follows behaviour branching in curved latent space, then high-mass/high-spread prefixes receive visibly more level-2 slots (spread of N_p across prefixes clearly above 1), the fraction of training transitions whose successor shares the item's level-2 prefix rises, and Stage 3 test recall@10 exceeds 0.065 in the single both-on run; if N_p is nearly uniform and recall stays at or below 0.065, the idea is discarded.
RISKS: sigma_p may be nearly constant across prefixes, so the both-on allocation collapses to the uniform schedule and the run degenerates into a baseline run.; Capacity concentration can starve low-mass prefixes and raise collisions for long-tail next-item targets.; The C=100 warm-start reallocation could oscillate and destabilise the EMA codebooks.; The exponent D/(D+2) close to 1 makes the allocation nearly proportional to mass, so the geometric term may be too weak to change any argmin.; The generative recommender's per-level vocabulary becomes level-dependent in size, which requires a careful deterministic token-space remap.; Behaviour mass is dominated by popular items, so the allocation may simply reproduce a popularity prior at the SID level.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: User-interest branching expressed as directed item transitions aggregated per SID prefix: the transition mass m_p and the successor multiset of each prefix.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One restricted pass over Stage 2 training sequences only. For each consecutive pair (i_t, i_t+1), c[i_t, i_t+1] += 1. Rows are normalised, the top M=8 successors per item kept (documented default) and renormalised, and m_i = sum_j c_ij stored with log1p. For a prefix p, m_p = sum over i in S_p of m_i, and the successor multiset is the union of the kept rows of all i in S_p with summed weights.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: W, m_p, the successor multisets and the median norm scale s come exclusively from the training-split sequences, and sigma_p from encoder latents of training items. Validation and test sequences are never read during SID construction, allocation or codebook refresh, and the item catalogue is the frozen training catalogue, so no validation or test interaction can enter a SID token.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz model of constant negative curvature K = -1/s^2 with radius s = median training latent norm measured once at initialisation (a measured scale, not tuned). Operations: exp_0^K, the Lorentz distance d_K(a,b) = s*arccosh(-<a,b>_L/s^2) with signature (-,+), and a weighted Riemannian (Karcher) Frechet mean computed by fixed-step gradient descent.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In quantization capacity allocation: at every C-th epoch boundary, inside the RQ-VAE codebook-budget schedule, before the level-l slice-restricted nearest-codeword assignment and the matching k-means slice rebuild.
COUPLING PATH: training transition graph -> per-prefix behaviour mass m_p and successor multiset -> per-prefix curved spread sigma_p around the Lorentz Frechet mean -> derived slot count N_p -> the prefix's codebook slice and its offsets -> slice-restricted argmin -> level-l SID token -> Stage 3 prompt tokens. Removing behaviour makes m_p and the successor multiset uninformative (uniform allocation); removing curvature changes the mean and spread (Euclidean variance gives a different N_p); either change moves the argmin and hence the emitted token.
COUPLING EQUATION: N_p = max(1, LR_round( N * (m_p * sigma_p^2)^(D/(D+2)) / sum_q (m_q * sigma_q^2)^(D/(D+2)) )) with sigma_p^2 = sum_j w_j d_K(m_p^*, x_j)^2 / sum_j w_j and m_p^* = argmin_x sum_j w_j d_K(x, x_j)^2, where the w_j are behaviour transition weights and d_K is the Lorentz distance of curvature K = -1/s^2.
OBSERVABLES: Distribution (min/median/max and entropy) of N_p at levels 2 and 3; per-level code usage entropy; successor prefix predictability, i.e. the fraction of training transitions whose successor shares the source item's level-1 and level-2 prefix; SID collision count and prefix counts; the allocation computed statically for the behaviour-off and geometry-off configurations without any extra training; and Stage 3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: m_p and the successor multiset replaced by the prefix's own item count and its own items, i.e. w_j = 1 for j in S_p, so the allocation sees no transitions. Exists in code behind BEHAVIOUR_ON=False, is unit-checked, is computed once as a static diagnostic and is never trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: BEHAVIOUR_ON=True but K set to 0 in the allocation branch, so the Frechet mean becomes the weighted Euclidean mean and sigma_p the weighted Euclidean standard deviation, giving a different N_p. Exists behind GEOMETRY_ON=False, unit-checked, static diagnostic only, never trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The single trained configuration: transition weights AND Lorentz curvature K=-1/s^2 spread both active, producing N_p and the slice-restricted level assignments. One training run, 3000 Stage 2 epochs, frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping, no tuning sweep, no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: (1) c_ij = count of consecutive (i,j) in training sequences; W_ij = c_ij / sum_j' c_ij', top-M=8 kept and renormalised; m_i = sum_j c_ij. (2) s = median_i ||mu_i||_2 at init; K = -1/s^2; x_i = exp_0^K(mu_i). (3) d_K(a,b) = s*arccosh(-<a,b>_L/s^2). (4) Per prefix p with aggregated weights w_j and mass m_p = sum_j w_j: x_p^(0) = exp_0^K( sum_j w_j mu_j / m_p ); for t=0..4: g = -2 sum_j w_j log_{x_p^(t)}(x_j) / m_p and x_p^(t+1) = exp_{x_p^(t)}(eta_t g) with eta_t = s/(2 sqrt(t+1)); m_p^* = x_p^(5). (5) sigma_p^2 = sum_j w_j d_K(m_p^*, x_j)^2 / m_p, with sigma_p clipped to the measured [q05,q95] quantiles over the same epoch's prefixes. (6) alpha = D/(D+2); a_p = (m_p sigma_p^2)^alpha; N_p = max(1, floor(N a_p / sum a_p)) with the deficit distributed by largest remainder so sum_p N_p = N exactly. (7) o_p = cumulative sum of N_p; level-l assignment for item i with prefix p: k_l(i) = argmin_{k in [o_p, o_p + N_p)} || r_l(i) - C_l[k] ||_2^2, with the slice initialised by 5-step warm-started k-means on the residuals of S_p and then EMA-updated as in the baseline. (8) Refresh at epoch boundaries only, every C = 100 epochs, 30 refreshes in total.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training-only transition counts, the EMA latent table over 24556 items, the prefix assignment from the previous epoch, the level slot budget N, the residual dimension D and the measured scale s. Outputs: integer per-prefix slot counts N_p, contiguous slice offsets o_p, per-prefix initialised codebook rows, and the slice-restricted codeword indices that become the SID tokens.
SAFEGUARDS: sigma_p clipped to measured q05/q95 quantiles of the same epoch so no degenerate prefix owns the whole budget; N_p floored at 1 and capped at N-(P-1) so every prefix keeps at least one child; largest-remainder rounding guarantees sum N_p = N exactly; cosh and arccosh arguments clamped (>= 1 + 1e-7) and norm denominators floored at 1e-8; the Frechet gradient step is capped at radius s so no exp map leaves the chart; allocation refreshes happen only at epoch boundaries with warm-started k-means so the EMA codebooks cannot oscillate; the transition graph is frozen before training so no validation/test interaction can leak in; the level token vocabulary is remapped deterministically from the global slot index so the generative recommender sees a stable token space.
END IDEA
