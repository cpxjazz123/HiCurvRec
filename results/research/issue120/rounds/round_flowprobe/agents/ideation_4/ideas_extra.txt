STEP 1 (design choice for GEO-DRIFT-RQ)
Goal: pick one ideation slot that is (a) in scope for the behaviour x hyperbolic coupling contract, (b) NOT a restatement of the earlier rejected/selected mechanisms, (c) trainable in one frozen-budget run with no sweep and no ablation arm.
What I read: the task workspace (only progress.jsonl, empty) and the round summary in the prompt: earlier selections were poincare-frechet-successor-codebook (codebook centroids = successor Frechet means), CURVCTRL-SID (per-RQ-level curvature set by measured prefix-transition score), BRad-RQ (behaviour-ranked radial Poincare assignment), plus rejected probes LORENTZ-SUCCPROFILE-SMOOTH-RQ, behcone-prefix-rq, succboost-geoshift, BRANCHCURV-MOEB-RQ, TSG-SID.
Observation: every earlier slot mutated one of three objects - the codebook centroids, the per-level curvature scalar, or the assignment ranking/radius. None of them touched the RQ residual chain itself, i.e. the point that the deeper quantizer is asked to describe, and none of them used the direction (not the distance) of the training-only successor set.
Reasoning: an untouched, cheap, genuinely novel coupling position is the residual: move the residual along a geodesic of the Poincare ball by the parallel/again-chart-exponential of the successor displacement, with a strength that is a measured ratio (residual error over successor distance) so it needs no tuned constant. Behaviour enters as a direction (successor displacement) that still requires the curved exp/log to act; curvature enters the residual update. Switch-off semantics are clean: behaviour-off makes the displacement zero (plain curved RQ), geometry-off replaces every exp/log/distance with its Euclidean version.
Open at this step: write the minimal specification (bounds on the drift, stop-gradient on the successor displacement, per-step cost at 24556 items / 24 steps / 3000 epochs) and the three ablation switch definitions.
Decision: IDEA = GEO-DRIFT-RQ, a behaviour-displaced curved RQ residual; note explicitly that it changes no codebook estimator and adds no new loss term, which is what separates it from poincare-frechet-successor-codebook and from the radial/curvature-based slots.

STEP 2 (complete answer, IDEA GEO-DRIFT-RQ)
IDEA: GEO-DRIFT-RQ
TITLE: Successor-direction geodesic drift of the RQ residual: behaviour-displaced curved quantization of the SID prefix hierarchy
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE prefixes only describe what an item *is* (content residual), so the deeper prefix tokens of an item are behaviourally uninformative and the generative recommender has to learn all next-item structure on top of a content-only token tree. Next-item consumption is a directed, hierarchical process: an item is followed by a small, ordered successor set. If the residual that each RQ level sees is displaced along the ball in the direction of that item's training successor set, the deeper tokens of a SID carry "where consumption goes next", so prefix tokens become behaviourally predictive and the Stage 3 generative prior has less to reconstruct.
NOVELTY: The successor set is used as a *direction*, not as a centroid. Earlier work here re-estimated objects: codebook entries as Frechet means of successors (poincare-frechet-successor-codebook), per-level curvature scalars from prefix transition scores (CURVCTRL-SID), assignment radius/rank from context entropy (BRad-RQ), successor profile smoothing, curvature MoE. GEO-DRIFT-RQ estimates nothing from behaviour, adds no loss term and leaves the codebook update rule formally unchanged; it only displaces the query point that level l+1 must quantize. The displaced point is produced by a curved exponential map, and the displacement magnitude is bounded by the *measured* residual distance, so the drift never dominates the content residual and no drift weight is tuned.
CHANGE: After quantizing level l, the next residual point is not x_l but a point reached by exponentiating, at the chosen codeword, the sum of the level-l residual tangent and a successor-displacement tangent whose length is clipped to the residual distance. Everything downstream (which codeword levels 2..L pick, the codebook gradients, the SID assignment) therefore depends jointly on the content residual and on the training-only successor direction of the item.
SPECIFICATION: Per item i, u_i = Poincare embedding of z_i (tangent of the encoder output, exponentiating at the origin, curvature c). Per item, a successor point s_i = origin-chart mean of the Poincare points of its top-m training successors (m from the measured successor list, capped at a documented default of 10); displacement in the origin chart, Delta_i = log_0(s_i) - log_0(u_i), detached from the graph. At level l with current point x_l and codeword e_l = e_{l,k_l}: r_l = d_P(x_l, e_l), a_l = d_P(e_l, s_i), eta_l = r_l/(r_l + a_l + eps) in (0,1), tangent t_l = log_{e_l}(x_l), drift tangent g_l = eta_l * Delta_i clipped so that d_P(e_l, exp_{e_l}(g_l)) <= clip * r_l, next point x_{l+1} = exp_{e_l}(t_l + g_l). With g_l = 0 this collapses exactly to curved residual quantisation (behaviour-off); with Euclidean exp/log/distance it collapses to len(x_l - e_l) drift in flat space (geometry-off). No new loss, no new hyperparameter beyond the documented defaults m=10, clip=1.0, c initialised at 1 and trained, eps=1e-8.
COST: One-off preprocessing pass over the training split building the top-10 successor lists per item (24556 x 10 int32 ~ 1 MB, a few minutes). Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items / 24 steps => batch B ~ 1023, L = 3 levels, K = 256 codewords, d = 32): extra work is a gather and mean of 10 vectors per item (10 * 1023 * 32 ~ 3.3e5 multiply-adds), 2 extra Poinc

are distances and 3 extra exp/log maps per item per level (~9.2e3 map evaluations, each O(d)), versus the 1023 * 3 * 256 ~ 7.9e5 codebook distances the quantizer already computes. Measured expectation: +3 to +6 percent per step, i.e. about 1.05x Stage 2 wall clock over 3000 epochs on four cards, plus the one-off successor table. Memory: no change apart from the 1 MB table and one B x d detached buffer.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if (i) the drift is large enough to move a measurable fraction of level-2 and level-3 codeword choices relative to what the content residual alone would pick, and (ii) moving them toward successor regions makes the generated prefix tokens better predictors of the next item. It is observed in the single run through the recorded diagnostics (fraction of items whose deeper codeword differs from the drift-free argmin, mean clipped drift / mean residual ratio expected in roughly 0.2-0.6, transfer predictability of prefix tokens vs reference) and finally through Stage 3 test_recall@10 with the target above 0.065 with no ablation arm trained.
RISKS: If the successor displacement is nearly collinear with the content residual (successors content-similar), the drift changes nothing and the run is baseline-equivalent. If it is strongly non-collinear and eta is near 1 for many items, deeper tokens stop describing the item and prefixes become too coarse, which can hurt collision-free discrimination. Successor-starved or popularity-dominated items (head of the long tail) get a noisy s_i; the eta ratio and the r_l clip limit but do not remove that noise. Trainable curvature can drift with the drifted residual distribution; the clamp is the guard. Drift computed from encoder embeddings (not from item ids) can collapse if gradients flow into s_i, hence the stop-gradient.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item successor mass from the training split: for each item i, the ranked list of items j that follow i (directed transitions inside training sequences) with their normalised counts, i.e. the directed successor distribution Succ(i), capped at the top 10.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the training-stage sequences only (the same split that trains Stage 2, evaluation sessions removed before Stage 3 by the frozen protocol). For every adjacent pair (i -> j) in a training sequence, increment count w_ij (optionally discounted by position distance, decay documented as 1/rank inside the session); per item i form the top-10 list by count and normalise w_ij = c_ij / sum_j c_ij over the kept list. Store as CSR int32 (successor ids) + fp32 (weights). Items with an empty list get the empty list and therefore no drift.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The successor table is constructed exclusively from Stage 2 training sequences; validation and test sessions are never touched, and the table is frozen before Stage 2 starts (it is not refreshed from any online interaction). The successor *points* s_i are computed from encoder embeddings of training items only, which are functions of training data alone. SIDs are thus a deterministic function of the training split plus the frozen successor table; no validation or test co-occurrence, popularity or interaction can enter them, and the Stage 3 protocol only reads the frozen SIDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball operations at trainable negative curvature -c: the distance d_P^c(x,y) = (1/sqrt(c)) arcosh(1 + 2c||x-y||^2 / ((1 - c||x||^2)(1 - c||y||^2))), the exponential map exp_x^c(v) = x (+)_c tanh(sqrt(c

)||v||_x/2) v/(sqrt(c)||v||_x) with ||v||_x = 2||v||/(1 - c||x||^2), the logarithmic map log_x^c, and the radial squash exp_0^c used to place the encoder output inside the ball. Curvature c = softplus(theta) clamped to [0.1, 10], initialised so that c = 1 (documented default).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ quantizer loop, between consecutive quantization levels: the curved distance picks the codeword, the curved log/exp build the residual tangent and the drifted next point that levels l+1..L must quantize. It also enters the curved codebook/commitment distances (d_P^c instead of squared Euclidean), which is where the behaviour-drifted residuals push the codebook gradients.
COUPLING PATH: item encoder output -> u_i -> tangent log_0(u_i) minus successor-chart log_0(s_i) gives the detached successor displacement Delta_i -> projected onto the tangent at the chosen level-l codeword and scaled by the measured ratio eta_l -> added to the residual tangent t_l -> exponentiated to the level-(l+1) query point x_{l+1} -> determines the level-(l+1) codeword and every deeper SID token -> determines the SID prefixes the Stage 3 model learns and generates. Removing the behaviour signal sets Delta_i = 0 and the whole path collapses to curved residual quantisation (no change in deeper tokens); removing the curved operator (Euclidean exp/log/distance) replaces the ball with flat space and changes the direction and length of the same displacement and therefore the deeper codewords. Either removal changes the SID assignment itself, not merely an auxiliary loss.
COUPLING EQUATION: x_{l+1} = exp_{e_{l,k_l}}^c( log_{e_{l,k_l}}^c(x_l) + clip( eta_l * Delta_i , |.| <= r_l ) ), with the behaviour term Delta_i = log_0^c(s_i) - log_0^c(u_i), s_i = exp_0^c( sum_j w_ij log_0^c(u_j) / sum_j w_ij ), and the curved term expressed by log_{e}^c, exp_{e}^c and the ratio eta_l = d_P^c(x_l,e_{l,k_l}) / (d_P^c(x_l,e_{l,k_l}) + d_P^c(e_{l,k_l}, s_i) + eps); the codeword itself is k_l = argmin_k d_P^c(x_l, e_{l,k}).
OBSERVABLES: (1) SID assignment: fraction of items whose level-2 and level-3 codewords differ from the drift-free argmin (expected clearly non-zero, and recorded), level-wise codeword-usage entropy and collision counts. (2) Optimisation: the share of the codebook/commitment gradient contributed by drifted residuals, mean and 95th percentile of the clipped drift length over r_l, number of items with an empty successor list. (3) Prefix behaviour predictability: agreement between the generated and held-out training prefixes, transfer predictability of prefix-to-next-item. (4) The judgement: Stage 3 test_recall@10 from the single frozen-protocol run, target above 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Delta_i := 0 for every item (equivalently s_i := u_i, or an empty successor table), all curved operators, curvature training, clipping and losses unchanged. Then x_{l+1} = exp_{e}^c(log_{e}^c(x_l)) = x_l exactly, i.e. plain curved residual quantisation; the switch exists in the code, is not trained in this round, and is the reference for the "does the drift change the deeper codewords" diagnostic.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: curvature forced to the flat limit (c -> 0): u_i = z_i, codewords in R^d, d_P -> Euclidean norm, exp_x(v) = x + v, log_x(y) = y - x, log_0 = identity, decoder input = concatenated codewords. The behaviour term stays identical (Delta_i = s_i - u_i with s_i the weighted Euclidean mean of the successor embeddings, eta_l = r_l/(r_l + a_l + eps), x_{l+1} = x_l + cli

p(eta_l * Delta_i, <= r_l)), so the arm isolates what the curvature does to the drift direction and length.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run described above: successor displacement computed from the training-only successor table and transported through the curved log/exp at trainable curvature, driving the deeper-level query points, with the curved quantizer distances. This is the only arm trained and the only one whose Stage 3 test_recall@10 is reported against the 0.065 target.
SPEC EQUATIONS: pseudocode
  # curvature
  c = clamp(softplus(theta), 0.1, 10);  rho = 1/sqrt(c);  radius_cap = rho*(1-1e-5)
  # encoder placement
  u_i = exp_0^c(z_i);  clip ||u_i|| <= radius_cap
  # successor point (detached)
  if Succ(i) empty: s_i = u_i
  else: v_hat = sum_j w_ij * log_0^c(u_j) / sum_j w_ij ;  s_i = exp_0^c(v_hat)  # first-order Frechet mean, O(d) per item
  Delta_i = stop_grad( log_0^c(s_i) - log_0^c(u_i) )
  # RQ loop
  x_1 = u_i
  for l in 1..L:
      k_l = argmin_k d_P^c(x_l, e_{l,k})                      # curved assignment
      r_l = d_P^c(x_l, e_{l,k_l})                            # measured residual distance
      a_l = d_P^c(e_{l,k_l}, s_i)                            # measured successor distance
      eta_l = r_l / (r_l + a_l + eps)                        # derived, dimensionless, in (0,1)
      t_l = log_{e_{l,k_l}}^c(x_l)                           # curved residual tangent
      g_l = eta_l * Delta_i                                  # behaviour term at the codeword
      if ||g_l|| > r_l * (1 - c*||e_{l,k_l}||^2)/ (2): g_l = g_l * r_l / (||g_l|| * (1 - c*||e||^2)/2)  # distance-preserving clip: d_P(e, exp_e(g_l)) <= r_l
      x_{l+1} = exp_{e_{l,k_l}}^c(t_l + g_l)                 # displaced next query point
      clip ||x_{l+1}|| <= radius_cap
  # reconstruction and objective (unchanged form, curved distances)
  z_hat_i = Dec(concat_l log_0^c(e_{l,k_l}))
  L = L_rec(z_i, z_hat_i) + beta * sum_l [ d_P^c(x_l, sg(e_{l,k_l}))^2 + d_P^c(sg(x_l), e_{l,k_l})^2 ]
  # geometry-off and behaviour-off switch inside the same functions
  # c->0: d_P->||.||, exp_x(v)->x+v, log_x(y)->y-x, log_0->id ; Delta_i->0: g_l->0
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item embedding z_i in R^d from the encoder, the frozen successor CSR (top-10 ids + weights w_ij) built from the training split, current codebooks E_l in the ball, trainable log-curvature theta, documented defaults m=10, clip=1.0, eps=1e-8. Outputs: the quantizer's harder assignment decision, i.e. the L-tuple of codeword indices (k_1..k_L) per item = the SID, plus the drifted query points x_2..x_{L+1}, the per-level drift magnitudes and eta_l values for the diagnostics, and curved quantization/commitment gradients for the codebooks and the encoder.
SAFEGUARDS: (a) all points are clipped to radius (1 - 1e-5)/sqrt(c) before every arcosh, and arcosh arguments are clamped to >= 1 + 1e-12, so distance and log maps cannot produce NaN; (b) the drift tangent is clipped in the codeword's metric so that d_P(e, exp_e(g_l)) <= r_l, hence total displacement per level is bounded by the residual distance the level is supposed to explain; (c) eta_l carries eps = 1e-8 in the denominator and is clamped to [0, 1]; zero-length tangents short-circuit to the identity map; (d) Delta_i and the successor points are stop-gradiented so the encoder cannot collapse successor embeddings to reduce the drift; (e) empty successor lists, singleton lists and repeated successors are handled (Delta_i = 0, count sum > 0 division gu

ard); (f) curvature is softplus-parameterised and clamped to [0.1, 10] with the ball radius recomputed from c after every update; (g) if a NaN is detected in the quantizer, the level falls back to the drift-free tangent (g_l = 0) for that step, logged; (h) the successor table is built once from the training split, frozen, and its hash is logged so the leakage boundary is auditable; (i) the drift length statistics are logged, never used as a gate.

  # geometry-off and behaviour-off switches live inside the same functions

STEP 3 (design choice for PMI-HYPATTN-SID)
Goal: choose one new ideation slot that is in scope, distinct from the earlier GEO-DRIFT-RQ answer already in ideas.txt, and distinct from the round selections (successor-Frechet codebooks, per-level curvature control, behaviour-ranked radial assignment).
What I read: ideas.txt (previous GEO-DRIFT-RQ: successor-direction geodesic drift of the RQ residual), execution.json, the Poincare geometry contract, and the round summary in the prompt.
Observation: earlier mechanisms mutate the codebook centroids, the per-level curvature scalar, the assignment radius/rank, or the residual query point. None of them mutates the item representation that enters the quantizer, and none uses a popularity-adjusted transition graph. The previous answer used raw directed successor displacement in the residual chain.
Reasoning: a genuinely different coupling position is the encoder-to-quantizer interface. A popularity-adjusted directed PMI neighbour graph can define a behaviour context, and a Poincare-ball attention/log-map adapter can move each item toward that context before level 1; the same adapted point is then quantized by all RQ levels, so the whole prefix hierarchy changes. The attention logits multiply/compare the PMI weight with a curved distance, so removing the PMI weights or replacing the curved operator changes the adapter output and hence the SID assignment. This is a forward-path mechanism, not an auxiliary behaviour loss and not a codebook/Frechet/curvature/residual/radial rule.
Decision: IDEA = PMI-HYPATTN-SID. Complete answer follows as the last progress record.

IDEA: PMI-HYPATTN-SID
TITLE: Popularity-adjusted transition attention in the Poincare ball before RQ quantization: a behaviour-context adapter that reshapes every SID prefix level
MOTIVATION: Content-only RQ-VAE SIDs spend their prefix capacity on item attributes, so the generative recommender has to learn next-item transition structure on top of a token tree that has never seen user behaviour. Raw successor counts are also dominated by popular items, which makes a naive behaviour graph recommend hub items and blurs genuine substitute/complement structure. The problem is to make the latent that is quantized carry popularity-adjusted behavioural context, so all RQ levels partition items into behaviourally coherent prefixes without abandoning content.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms here re-estimated codebook centroids (successor Frechet means), re-estimated per-level curvature (prefix-transition control), changed the radial assignment rank (context entropy), or displaced the RQ residual (successor-direction geodesic drift). This proposal changes none of those objects. It inserts a popularity-adjusted directed-PMI neighbour graph as a hyperbolic attention adapter between the encoder and level 1 of the quantizer. The PMI weight enters the attention logit together with a Poincare distance, and the resulting behaviour-context point is the input to every RQ level. No new loss term, no new codebook estimator, no per-level scalar, no radial rank rule.
CHANGE: Before quantization, item i is moved along a geodesic inside the Poincare ball toward a behaviour context formed from its top-m directed-PMI neighbours, with a gate derived from the attention entropy. The adapted point is what the RQ-VAE quantizes, so the level-1, level-2 and level-3 codewords and therefore the whole SID prefix hierarchy depend on both the PMI weights and the curved geometry.
SPECIFICATION: Build a frozen training-only directed PMI graph (top-m, m=8 documented default). For item i, u_i = exp_0^c(z_i), neighbours N_i with weights p_ij. Let d_ij = d_c(u_i,u_j), tau_i = mean_{j in N_i} d_ij (detached; if empty, no adapter), alpha_ij = softmax_j( log(p_ij+eps) - d_ij/tau_i ), H_i = -sum_j alpha_ij log alpha_ij, and kappa_i = 1 - H_i/log|N_i| for |N_i|>1, else 0. Behaviour-context tangent t_i = sum_j alpha_ij log_{u_i}^c(stopgrad(u_j)); adapted point u_i^b = exp_{u_i}^c(kappa_i * t_i). Then run the existing RQ-VAE on u_i^b: k_l = argmin_k d_c(x_l,e_lk), x_1 = u_i^b, residual transport by log/exp at the chosen codeword, reconstruction target u_i (content), commitment/reconstruction distances d_c. The behaviour-off switch sets N_i empty and kappa_i=0, so u_i^b=u_i exactly. The geometry-off switch uses the c=0 Euclidean branch (d_0=2||x-y||, exp_x(v)=x+v, log_x(y)=y-x) for both the adapter and the quantizer. The both-on switch uses the equations above. Curvature c=softplus(theta) clamped to [0.1,10], initialised at 1; no learned gate, no residual gate weight, no extra loss.
COST: One-off preprocessing pass over training sequences to count directed pairs and retain top-8 positive PMI neighbours: O(number of training transitions + 24556*8), a few minutes, about 24556*8*(4+4) bytes ~ 1.6 MB. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps, B~1023, L=3, K=256, d=32): gather 8 neighbour vectors (8*1023*32 ~ 2.6e5 multiply-adds), 8 Poincare distances and 8 log maps per item plus one exp map (~9.2e3 O(d) map evaluations), versus the existing 1023*3*256 ~ 7.9e5 codebook distances. Expecte

d +2 to +5 percent per step, i.e. roughly 1.03x to 1.06x Stage 2 wall clock over 3000 epochs on four cards, plus the one-off PMI table. Memory: ~1.6 MB table and one B x d stop-grad neighbour buffer.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if popularity-adjusted PMI neighbours are behaviourally informative beyond content similarity, and if concentrating an item toward that context makes its SID prefixes more predictive of the next item. It is observed in the single run through the fraction of items whose level-1/2/3 codewords change relative to the behaviour-off computation, the attention entropy and kappa distribution, the successor prefix-overlap diagnostic, and finally Stage 3 test_recall@10 target above 0.065 with no ablation arm trained.
RISKS: PMI on rare pairs is noisy and can overfit; top-m truncation and positive-PMI filtering limit but do not remove that. Strong attention can over-smooth item representations and collapse distinct items into shared prefixes, hurting collision-free discrimination. The entropy gate can be near zero for diffuse behaviour, making the adapter a no-op. If PMI neighbours are mostly content-similar, the adapter changes little and the run is baseline-equivalent. Stop-grad on neighbours prevents collapse but also makes the context a moving target. Trainable curvature can drift; the clamp is the guard.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed, popularity-adjusted item-to-item PMI mass from adjacent pairs in the training sequences: for each item i, the top-m items j with positive directed PMI, together with their normalised PMI weights.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over the Stage 2 training sequences only. For every adjacent pair (i -> j) increment c_ij; c_i^out = sum_j c_ij, c_j^in = sum_i c_ij, N = sum_ij c_ij. PMI_ij = log( c_ij * N / (c_i^out * c_j^in) ) for c_ij>0, else -inf. Retain the top m=8 largest positive PMI_ij per item, then p_ij = PMI_ij / sum_{j' in N_i} PMI_ij'. Store CSR int32 ids + fp32 weights, frozen before Stage 2 starts. Items with no positive PMI get an empty list and kappa_i=0.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The PMI table is built exclusively from the Stage 2 training split; validation and test sessions are never read, and the table is frozen before Stage 2 begins and never refreshed online. The encoder and adapter see only training items. SIDs are therefore a deterministic function of the training split, the frozen PMI table and the Stage 2 optimisation; the Stage 3 protocol only reads the frozen SIDs. No validation or test co-occurrence, popularity or interaction can enter the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball operations at trainable negative curvature -c, using the contract operators: d_c(x,y) = (2/sqrt c) artanh( sqrt c ||(-x) (+)c y|| ), exp_x^c and log_x^c with the conformal factor lambda_x^c = 2/(1-c||x||^2), and projection to radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt c. c = softplus(theta) clamped to [0.1,10], initialised so c=1; c=0 is the explicit Euclidean branch for the geometry-off switch.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the encoder-to-quantizer interface during Stage 2 training: the PMI attention uses the curved distance in its logits, the context is built by curved log maps, the adapted item point is produced by a curved exp map, and that point is the level-1 query of the RQ quantizer, so it determines every later level. The existing RQ assignment and commitment distances also use d_c.
COUPLING PATH: training sequences -> directed PMI weights p_ij -> attention logits log(p_ij+eps) - d_c(u_i,u_j)/tau_i -> softmax

alpha_ij -> tangent context sum_j alpha_ij log_{u_i}^c(u_j) -> gate kappa_i from attention entropy -> geodesic step u_i^b = exp_{u_i}^c(kappa_i * t_i) -> level-1 query and all deeper RQ codewords -> SID prefixes. Removing the PMI signal makes N_i empty and kappa_i=0, so u_i^b=u_i and the SID collapses to the content-only quantizer; replacing the curved operator by the c=0 Euclidean branch changes the attention weights, the log/exp context and the resulting codewords.
COUPLING EQUATION: u_i^b = exp_{u_i}^c( kappa_i * sum_{j in N_i} alpha_ij * log_{u_i}^c(u_j) ), with alpha_ij = softmax_j( log(p_ij^PMI + eps) - d_c(u_i,u_j)/tau_i ), kappa_i = 1 - H_i/log|N_i|, H_i = -sum_j alpha_ij log alpha_ij, tau_i = mean_j d_c(u_i,u_j). The behaviour term is p_ij^PMI; the curved terms are d_c, log_{u_i}^c and exp_{u_i}^c; both appear in the same adapter that produces the quantizer input.
OBSERVABLES: (1) SID assignment: fraction of items whose level-1, level-2 and level-3 codewords differ from the behaviour-off computation; level-wise code-usage entropy and collision count. (2) Adapter: mean and 95th percentile of attention entropy, kappa and geodesic step length; number of items with empty PMI lists. (3) Prefix behaviour predictability: prefix overlap between successive training items, and transfer predictability of a prefix to its successor. (4) The judgement: Stage 3 test_recall@10 from the single frozen-protocol run, target above 0.065.

This is the only arm trained and the only Stage 3 test_recall@10 reported against 0.065.
