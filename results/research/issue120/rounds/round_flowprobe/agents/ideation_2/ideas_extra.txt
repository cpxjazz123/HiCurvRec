IDEA: GYROCONS-RQ
TITLE: Successor-mass gyro-consolidation of the encoder point feeding a Poincare-distance RQ at every prefix level
MOTIVATION: In a text/content RQ-VAE the vector handed to the quantizer knows nothing about what users actually do with the item, so two items that are near-duplicates in content but sit on opposite sides of a user transition get pushed into the same code while strongly co-transitioned items get split across unrelated prefixes. The generative recommender then emits prefixes whose continuations are not the ones users actually take. The fix must be in the point that is quantized, not only in the codebook, because the residual chain at every level is cut from that same point.
NOVELTY: The behaviour signal is not put on the assignment weights, on the codebook update, on a per-level curvature schedule, on a radial rank or on a residual transport; it is put into the geometric position of the quantizer input via a single Moebius/hyperbolic-tangent consolidation step over training-only directed item transitions, and the resulting consolidated ball point is what all L RQ levels see, with the association distance being the Poincare distance and the residual subtraction carried in the origin tangent chart. Removing the transitions collapses the consolidation to the identity; removing the curvature collapses every curved operator to its Euclidean counterpart, which is exactly the baseline quantizer. The two ingredients therefore meet inside one equation, not in two added losses.
CHANGE: Before quantization, the encoder latent e_i is projected into a Poincare ball of trainable negative curvature and moved halfway along the geodesic toward the hyperbolic tangent-space barycentre of its successor-mass-weighted training neighbours; all L RQ levels then assign by Poincare distance to that moved point and subtract code directions in the tangent chart at the ball origin.
SPECIFICATION: kappa_bar = softplus(kappa_raw) > 0 (curvature -kappa_bar), kappa_raw initialised 0, floor kappa_bar >= 1e-2. Ball projection Pi(x) = (R/||x||) tanh(||x||/R) x with R = 0.9/sqrt(kappa_bar).
Step 1 (behaviour, training split only): build C_ij = count of directed item->item transitions inside training sequences. For item i, let N_M(i) be the shortest successor prefix (sorted by C_ij) covering 90% of outgoing transition mass, truncated at the 95th percentile of |N_M| over all items (both quantities measured once on the training graph, then frozen). a_ij = C_ij / sqrt(Dout_i * Din_j); w_ij = a_ij / sum_{j' in N_M(i)} a_ij'.
Step 2 (curved consolidation): u_i = Pi(e_i); t_i = sum_{j in N_M(i)} w_ij * log^kappa_{u_i}(u_j); v_i = exp^kappa_{u_i}(0.5 * t_i); if N_M(i) is empty then v_i = u_i.
Step 3 (curved quantization, l = 1..L): z^(0)_i = v_i; for each level k_l(i) = argmin_k d_kappa(z^(l-1)_i, c_{l,k}); S_i += log^kappa_0(c_{l,k_l(i)}); z^(l)_i = Pi( log^kappa_0(z^(l-1)_i) - log^kappa_0(c_{l,k_l(i)}) ).
Step 4: decoder receives S_i and reconstructs x_i as in the baseline; codebook and commitment losses are taken in the tangent chart: L_cb = sum_l || sg[log^kappa_0 z^(l-1)_i] - log^kappa_0 c_{l,k_l} ||^2, L_commit = sum_l || log^kappa_0 z^(l-1)_i - sg[log^kappa_0 c_{l,k_l}] ||^2, with the baseline reconstruction and usage terms unchanged. Full loss L = ||x_i - dec(S_i)||^2 + L_cb + beta * L_commit (+ baseline usage term).
