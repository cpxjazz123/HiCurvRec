IDEA: SUCC-TANSLERP-RQ
TITLE: Successor-prefix tangent-direction rotation of the quantization target inside a Poincare RQ-VAE: behaviour turns the residual direction at constant geodesic radius before every level
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE semantic IDs are trained to reconstruct an item embedding, so level-2 and level-3 tokens spend their capacity on residual detail of the item itself and carry almost no information about which item the user consumes next; the generative recommender must then learn transition structure from the token sequence alone. Rotating the quantization target, level by level, toward where the item's successors sit in the prefix tree makes the whole prefix hierarchy behaviour-predictive instead of merely reconstructive.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms in this programme moved the codebook (successor-weighted Frechet centroid updates), changed curvature per level (measured prefix-transition score), or ranked items by radius (transition-entropy rank). This idea leaves codebooks, curvature (-1 fixed) and radii untouched and instead rotates the quantization TARGET direction inside the tangent frame of the current curved prefix, at constant geodesic radius, separately at every level, with a per-item coupling weight derived from transition counts, and composes the prefix path by Mobius addition so the hierarchy bends. It is an angular coupling, not a translational geodesic shift and not a centroid update.
CHANGE: Before each RQ level the item residual direction log_p(x) is slerped toward the successor-prefix tangent field and the chosen code is the one whose Mobius step lands nearest that rotated target; the RQ-VAE therefore encodes transition-consistent residuals into the level-2 and level-3 tokens.
SPECIFICATION: Poincare ball of fixed curvature -1; rho_max=0.9; ball embedding x = rho_max*z/(kappa+||z||) with kappa the running RMS of ||z|| measured over the first 200 Stage-2 steps; code radius bound derived by equal geodesic budget d(0,u^l_k) <= d_max/L with d_max=2*atanh(rho_max), L=3, K=256; per level: r=log_{p}(x), s=sum_j w_ij*log_{p}(P_l[j]) with P_l the detached prefix buffer, lam_i=n_i/(n_i+nbar) capped at 0.9, target x~=exp_p(||r||*normalize((1-lam)r_hat+lam*s_hat)); assignment k_l=argmin_k d_P(p(+)u^l_k, x~); prefix p <- p(+)u^l_{k_l}; loss d_P(p_L,x)^2 + beta*commitment (beta=0.25) + geodesic codebook term, straight-through estimator; SIDs frozen and handed to the unchanged Stage 3 protocol.
COST: Stage 2 only, at the frozen scale: 24556 training items / 24 steps = 1023 items per step. Per item per level the assignment evaluates 256 Mobius steps plus geodesic distances (~150 flops at D=32) = ~38k flops, so ~1.2e8 flops per step forward and the same backward, against ~2.5e7 flops for the baseline Euclidean assignment: about +1e8 flops per step, 7.2e10 per epoch, 2.2e14 over 3000 epochs, i.e. roughly +15-25% Stage-2 step time and under +2% end-to-end wall clock. Successor aggregation costs one index lookup and a degree-weighted 32-dim sum per item per level (<6k flops/item/level); the detached prefix buffer is N*L*D floats = 9.4 MB. Stage 3 unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if (a) the mean derived coupling weight over items with training transitions does not collapse (report mean lam; if below 0.05 the run is declared behaviour-inert) and (b) the single frozen Stage 3 run reports test_recall@10 above 0.065 and above the programme baseline. The assignment must visibly change: level-2/3 token histograms and SID identity differ for high-lam items relative to what the behaviour-off configuration would produce, and prefix transition predictability at levels 2-3 rises while geodesic reconstruction error may rise slightly.
RISKS: The successor aggregate is coarse, so the rotated target can leave the item's own manifold and hurt reconstruction, hence token likelihood; Constraining all three code steps inside the ball radius cap limits expressivity of the last level; The detached successor prefix buffer is stale by up to one epoch and can oscillate or feed back into itself; Most items may have very few training transitions, driving lam toward 0 and making the run an ordinary curved RQ-VAE; Near-boundary Poincare arithmetic can blow up without clipping and projection; The Stage 3 generator may not exploit prefix coherence, in which case recall stays flat despite the coupling
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions from the training sequences, aggregated per item into a successor-prefix tangent field, plus the derived per-item reliability weight n_i/(n_i+nbar).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only: n_ij = number of times item j immediately follows item i across training sequences, w_ij=n_ij/sum_j n_ij, n_i=sum_j n_ij, nbar = median of n_i over items with n_i>0, all computed once before Stage 2 and frozen. The successor field at level l is s_l(i)=sum_j w_ij*log_{p_l(i)}(P_l[j]), where P_l is a detached buffer of each item's ball point (level 1) or prefix points (levels 2..L) written during Stage-2 training batches.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transitions, counts, nbar and the prefix buffer are computed exclusively from training-sequence interactions and from Stage-2 training batches; SIDs are built once and frozen before Stage 3. No validation or test sequence, interaction or score ever enters the counts, the buffer, the target rotation or the codebooks, so no information about held-out next items can reach the IDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball with fixed negative curvature c=-1: Mobius addition for the curved residual composition, geodesic distance d_P for assignment and reconstruction, and exp/log maps that define the tangent frame at each prefix point; code radius bound derived by equal geodesic budget d_max/L rather than chosen.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside quantization: in the forward pass that turns an item embedding into its L-token SID, once per level, and in the Stage-2 reconstruction and codebook losses; nothing is added to Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: One connected pathway: encoder output -> ball point -> per-level prefix p_l -> tangent frame at p_l -> (successor prefix field at the same depth) rotated target x~ -> Mobius-step candidate points p_l(+)u^l_k -> geodesic distance coding choice k_l -> level-l prefix token -> prefix of level l+1. The behaviour signal enters only through the rotation of the target and the geometry only through exp/log/Mobius/d_P at p_l; with lam=0 the rotation is the identity (behaviour inert), with Euclidean exp/log/Mobius/d_P the rotation is a flat vector blend and the composition plain addition (curvature inert), so removing either changes the produced codes.
COUPLING EQUATION: x~_l = exp_{p_l}( ||r_l|| * normalize( (1 - lam_i)*r_hat_l + lam_i*s_hat_l ) ), with r_l = log_{p_l}(x_i), s_l = sum_j w_ij * log_{p_l}(P_l[j]), lam_i = n_i/(n_i+nbar); k_l = argmin_k d_P^{c=-1}( p_{l-1} (+) u^l_k , x~_{l-1} ).
OBSERVABLES: Mean and quartiles of lam; fraction of items with lam=0; per-level code-usage entropy; SID identity change rate relative to the behaviour-off configuration (assignment observable); geodesic reconstruction error; prefix transition predictability (fraction of test-sequence prefixes whose next token lies in the successor set of the prefix) recorded as a diagnostic only; frozen Stage 3 test_recall@10 as the sole judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Config use_behaviour=False: lam_i:=0 and s_l is never read, so x~_l = x_i and the run is a plain curved Mobius-composed RQ-VAE. The switch exists and is correct; no arm is trained with it in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Config use_hyperbolic=False: exp/log/Mobius/d_P replaced by identity, weighted mean and L2, giving x~_l = p_l + ||r_l||*normalize((1-lam_i)r_hat_l + lam_i*s_hat_l) and additive residual composition; behaviour survives as a flat blend, curvature disappears. The switch exists and is correct; no arm is trained with it.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The single trained candidate: use_behaviour=True and use_hyperbolic=True, one seed, 3000 Stage-2 epochs on four cards, frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep over lam, bound, temperature or any other constant, no extra arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS: c=1 (curvature -1). Ball embedding x = rho_max*z/(kappa+||z||), kappa = running RMS of ||z|| over the first 200 steps, rho_max=0.9. Derived code bound: d_max=2*atanh(rho_max); per level u^l_k <- u^l_k * min(1, tanh((d_max/L)/2)/max(||u^l_k||,1e-8)). Mobius: a(+)b = ((1+2<a,b>+||b||^2)a + (1-||a||^2)b)/(1+2<a,b>+||a||^2||b||^2). Distance: d_P(a,b)=2*atanh(min(||(-a)(+)b||, 1-1e-6)). log_p(x) = (2/lambda_p)*atanh(min(||(-p)(+)x||,1-1e-6))*((-p)(+)x)/||(-p)(+)x||, lambda_p=1-||p||^2. exp_p(v) = p (+) ( tanh(lambda_p*||v||/2)*v/||v|| ). Forward per item: p_0=0; for l=1..L: r=log_{p_{l-1}}(x); s=sum_j w_ij log_{p_{l-1}}(P_l[j]); lam=0 if ||s||<1e-6 else min(0.9, n_i/(n_i+nbar)); d_hat=normalize((1-lam)*r/max(||r||,1e-8) + lam*s/||s||); x~=exp_{p_{l-1}}(||r||*d_hat); k_l=argmin_k d_P(p_{l-1}(+)u^l_k, x~) with straight-through (hard index, soft gradient to the selected u); p_l=p_{l-1}(+)u^l_{k_l}; write p_l into detached buffer slot (i,l). Loss L = d_P(p_L,x)^2 + beta*mean_l d_P(p_{l-1}(+)u^l_{k_l}, sg[x~_l])^2 + geodesic codebook term, beta=0.25 default, straight-through estimator. SIDs=argmin indices per level, frozen, fed to the unchanged Stage 3 protocol.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder output z in R^D, transition counts n_ij with derived w_ij and nbar, detached prefix buffer P in R^{N x L x D}, codebooks U^l in R^{K x D} inside the ball. Outputs: the L-token SID (k_1..k_L) per item, the geodesic reconstruction loss with gradients for encoder and codebooks, and the diagnostics (lam distribution, per-level usage entropy, geodesic error).
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp every ball norm to rho_max=0.9 before any Mobius operation and re-project to <=0.9 after each addition; epsilon 1e-8 in every denominator; clamp the atanh argument to 1-1e-6; a zero-radius code or zero residual skips exp/log and returns p unchanged; lam capped at 0.9 and forced to 0 when the successor field is missing (sparse-transition items, unstored buffer slots) so no item is dragged toward the origin by a phantom direction; the code-radius projection runs after the optimizer step, not inside the graph; the successor buffer is written with detached values only and never back-propagated; stale entries are refreshed lazily on appearance and unseen successors fall back to lam=0; loss is NaN-checked every 50 steps with a floor of 1e-6 on the Mobius denominator (no constant is changed); nothing derived is re-tuned if the mean lam turns out low - the run is reported as behaviour-inert.
END IDEA
