IDEA: GEO-SUCCBARY-SINKHORN-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-pulled curved assignment targets: geodesic interpolation toward the Lorentz barycenter of an item's successors, resolved by a Poincare-distance Sinkhorn plan inside the RQ-VAE
MOTIVATION: A generative recommender learns to emit SID prefixes; it only generalises to a target item if that item's coarse prefix is a region the prefix generator already visits after the user's history. Plain Euclidean RQ-VAE assigns codes from item identity alone, so two items that are behaviourally interchangeable (one reliably follows the other) can receive unrelated roots and the generator must memorise each transition separately. The fix is to make the quantizer's ASSIGNMENT TARGET, not merely its codebook, depend on where the item's training-successors already sit, and to make that target a curved object so that pulling an item toward a bundle of successors follows the true geometry of the latent rather than a chord through the ball.
NOVELTY: Different coupling position from the discarded rounds: not curved codebook updates (Fréchet successor codebooks), not per-level curvature control (CURVCTRL-SID), not radial behaviour ranking of codes (BRad-RQ), not entailment cones over prefixes, not Lorentz profile smoothing, not geodesic residual booster. Here the behaviour signal defines a per-item DESTINATION in the manifold and the geometry defines both that destination (closed-form Lorentzian weighted centroid of the cached successor points) and the transport used to choose the code (Poincare cost matrix plus Sinkhorn with a uniform column marginal). Nothing is added as a side loss; the behaviour and the curvature meet inside the single assignment cost matrix, and the residual recursion continues from the resulting curved codeword, so both switches bite the same tensor.
CHANGE: At every RQ level the query point is first geodesically interpolated, by a per-item factor derived from its training successor distribution and the level's residual scale, toward the Lorentz-model weighted barycenter of that item's training successors; the resulting point is matched to codewords by a Sinkhorn-transported soft assignment whose cost is the squared Poincare distance with a measured (not swept) entropic scale and a uniform column marginal.
SPECIFICATION: Encoder gives z (Euclidean). p_1 = exp_0(z) = tanh(||z||) * z/||z|| on the Poincare ball of curvature K=-1. For level l = 1..L: (1) fetch cached tangent-at-origin vectors of the top-M training successors of each batch item, map them to the hyperboloid, take the closed-form weighted Lorentzian centroid, project back to the ball to get g_l[i]; (2) shift the live query point p_l[i] geodesically toward g_l[i] by the derived fraction lambda_i^(l) = lambda_i * ||log_0(p_l[i])|| / (||log_0(p_1[i])|| + 1e-8), with lambda_i = max_j P(i->j) the item's top-1 training successor probability (0 if the item has no outgoing training transition); (3) build M[i,k] = d_P(p~_l[i], c_l[k])^2, entropic scale eps = mean(M) clipped to [1e-3, 1e2]; (4) run 30 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations with row marginal 1/B and column marginal 1/K to get plan T; take code k_l[i] = argmax_k T[i,k] with straight-through soft assignment T*K in the backward pass; (5) update the codebook by EMA on the detached soft counts; (6) residual tangent t = log_0(p_l[i] (-) c_l[k_l[i]]) (Moebius subtraction), p_{l+1}[i] = exp_0(t). Every step also writes p_l[i] into the tangent cache with momentum 0.9 (stop-grad). Loss = standard RQ-VAE reconstruction + commitment; no extra loss term is introduced. Behaviour-off sets lambda_i = 0 (p~ = p, identical arithmetic); geometry-off replaces exp/log/d_P/the Lorentz centroid by identity maps, the weighted Euclidean mean and squared L2 (Sinkhorn structure unchanged); both-on is the full mechanism. One training run, no arm is trained off.
COST: About +35-50% wall clock on the Stage 2 quantizer path, well under a full epoch of extra work. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch, batch B ~ 1023, L = 4 levels, K = configured codebook size per level, say 256): 4 level loops, each with B*M = 1023*8 = 8184 cache gathers, one closed-form Lorentz centroid (8184 x 33 add-multiply), one B*K = 262k squared Poincare distance matrix (a few 1e6 FLOPs) and 30 Sinkhorn iterations over a 1023x256 matrix (~30*262k*2 = 1.6e7 FLOPs); total ~7e7 extra FLOPs per step and ~5e12 over 3000 epochs (72k steps), which is small beside the transformer encoder. Extra memory: one tangent cache of 24556 x L x d floats (12.6 MB at d = 32) plus the read-only successor index (24556 x 8 ints, 0.8 MB). No extra backward pass, no extra forward of the encoder.
HYPOTHESIS: If behaviour-guided curved assignment targets are real, then in the single both-on run (i) per-level code usage entropy rises and stays high (uniform column marginal plus successor pulling prevents collapse), (ii) the fraction of training successor pairs that share the level-1 prefix rises materially over what an unshifted Euclidean RQ gives, (iii) a prefix-only next-item predictor computed on held-out training transitions from the frozen Stage 3 generator scores higher top-10 hit rate, and (iv) Stage 3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065. If recall stays at or below 0.065 while usage entropy and successor prefix sharing do rise, the hypothesis that behaviour-guided curved assignment is the binding constraint is falsified for this budget.
RISKS: Successor pulling collapses many behaviourally overlapping items onto the same coarse prefix, so the generator cannot tell them apart and top-10 recall drops even though prefix predictability rises.; The tangent cache lags (momentum 0.9) while the encoder moves fast in early epochs, so the Lorentz barycenters are stale targets and inject noise.; Sinkhorn with a uniform column marginal may over-smooth: with B ~ 1023 rows and K columns the plan spreads mass across codes and argmax becomes unstable early in training.; Entropic scale eps = mean(M) is scale-relative but not curvature-relative; if the learned point cloud shrinks toward the origin, eps shrinks and the plan hardens abruptly.; Items with sparse training transitions get lambda_i near 0, so the mechanism only acts on the head of the transition graph and may not generalise to the long tail that dominates test recall.; Extra per-step cost on the quantizer path may force shorter effective training within the frozen 3000-epoch budget if wall-clock is capped.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed training-only item-to-item transitions, reduced to two per-item quantities: the normalised successor distribution P(i->j) over the item's top-M successors, and its commitment lambda_i = max_j P(i->j).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Single pass over the TRAINING split sequences only: for each consecutive pair (u_t, u_{t+1}) with a gap of at most 1 within a session, increment C[i,j]. Then P(i->.) = C[i,:] / (sum_j C[i,j] + eps) (no popularity normalisation, no test data, no validation data), keep the top-M = 8 successors after a fixed, deterministic tie-break by item id, and store lambda_i = max_j P(i->j) with lambda_i = 0 when the outgoing count is zero. The tensor is frozen before Stage 2 starts and is never recomputed from any other split.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The behaviour tensor is built exclusively from sequences of the training split that Stage 2 already uses; validation and test interactions are never read by the SID construction path. lambda_i and P(i->.) are frozen file artefacts computed once before training, so no target item of a Stage 3 evaluation episode can influence any code assignment, the codebook, the curvature, or the tangent cache. The tangent cache is written only from encoder outputs on Stage 2 training batches, and Stage 3 evaluation is read-only with respect to it.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature K = -1 on the Poincare ball (exp_0, log_0, log_p, Moebius subtraction, Poincare distance d_P) together with the closed-form weighted Lorentzian centroid on the hyperboloid of the same curvature (ambient weighted mean followed by re-projection onto the hyperboloid). Curvature is fixed, not tuned; only the point positions move.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE quantizer, at every one of the L residual-quantisation levels: it defines the behaviour target (Lorentz centroid), the geodesic interpolation of the live query point, the Sinkhorn cost (squared Poincare distance), and the residual recursion (log_0 of the Moebius residual). It acts during Stage 2 training only; Stage 3 sees only the resulting integer SIDs.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour tensor -> lambda_i and successor set -> Lorentz barycenter g_l[i] of the cached successor points -> geodesic shift p~_l[i] = exp_{p_l}(lambda_i^(l) * log_{p_l}(g_l[i])) -> Poincare cost matrix M[i,k] -> Sinkhorn plan T -> code k_l[i] -> residual p_{l+1}[i]. Removing the behaviour sets lambda_i = 0 so p~ = p, which changes every cost entry, every plan and every code; removing the curvature replaces the barycenter, the interpolation and the cost by their Euclidean counterparts, which changes the same tensor. Both switches therefore act on one tensor, not on two independent losses.
COUPLING EQUATION: p~_l[i] = exp_{p_l[i]}( lambda_i^(l) * log_{p_l[i]}( g_l[i] ) ),  lambda_i^(l) = max_j P(i->j) * ||log_0(p_l[i])|| / (||log_0(p_1[i])|| + 1e-8),  g_l[i] = pi_P( sum_j w_ij x_j^l / sqrt(|<sum_j w_ij x_j^l, sum_j w_ij x_j^l>_eta|) ),  then T = Sinkhorn( exp( -d_P(p~_l[i], c_l[k])^2 / eps ) ; 1/B, 1/K ) and k_l[i] = argmax_k T[i,k].
OBSERVABLES: SID assignment: per-level code histograms, usage entropy and collision counts of the frozen SID table; gradient/parameter update: encoder norms and codebook EMA drift per level, plus the mean shift magnitude ||lambda_i^(l) log_{p_l}(g_l)|| tracked per epoch; prefix behaviour predictability: top-10 hit rate of a prefix-only successor predictor on held-out training transitions and the fraction of training successor pairs sharing level-1 and level-1-2 prefixes; Stage 3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 bar. All diagnostics are recorded, none gates the run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: lambda_i set to 0 for every item, so p~_l = p_l and the pipeline reduces to a fixed-curvature hyperbolic RQ with Sinkhorn assignment; the switch exists in code (a boolean that zeroes the behaviour tensor) and is correct but is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp/log/Moebius/d_P/Lorentz centroid replaced by identity maps, the weighted Euclidean successor mean and squared L2 cost, keeping lambda_i, the Sinkhorn structure, the residuals and all constants identical; one flag selects the Euclidean branch and it is exercised only by unit tests, not by a training arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run: derived lambda_i, Lorentzian successor barycenter from the tangent cache, geodesic interpolation at the live query point, squared Poincare cost, Sinkhorn with uniform column marginal, Moebius residual recursion. This is the single trained candidate of the round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Notation: B batch, K codes/level, L levels, M = 8 successors, ball radius bound 1 - 1e-3, curvature K_c = -1. 1. p_1 = exp_0(z) = tanh(||z||) * z/max(||z||, 1e-12); clamp ||p_1|| <= 1 - 1e-3. 2. Cache: cache[i, l, :] is tangent-at-origin log_0(p_l[i]) written with momentum: cache <- 0.9*cache + 0.1*detach(log_0(p_l)). 3. Barycenter: v_j = cache[j, l, :]; x_j = [sqrt(1 + ||v_j||^2), v_j] in R^{d+1}; w_ij = P(i->j)/sum_{j in TopM(i)} P(i->j); xbar = sum_j w_ij x_j; s = |<xbar, xbar>_eta| with eta = diag(-1, 1, ..., 1); xg = xbar / sqrt(max(s, 1e-12)); g = xg[1:] / (xg[0] + 1) (Poincare ball). If ItemHasNoSuccessor(i): g = p_l[i], lambda_i = 0. 4. Derived strength: lambda_i^(l) = lambda_i * ||log_0(p_l[i])|| / (||log_0(p_1[i])|| + 1e-8). 5. Shift: p~ = exp_{p}(lambda^(l) * log_p(g)), log_p(g) = 2 arctanh(||(-p (+) g)||) * (-p (+) g)/||(-p (+) g)||, exp_p(u) = p (+) (tanh(||u||/2) * u/||u||). Clamp ||p~|| <= 1 - 1e-3. 6. Cost: M[i,k] = (2 arctanh(||(-p~_i) (+) c_l[k]||))^2; eps = clip(mean(M), 1e-3, 1e2); Kmat = exp(-M / eps). 7. Sinkhorn (log domain, 30 iterations): f = 0, gv = 0; repeat: f = -eps * logsumexp_k( (-M[i,k] + gv[k]) / eps ) + eps*log(1/B); gv = -eps * logsumexp_i( (-M[i,k] + f[i]) / eps ) + eps*log(1/K). Plan T = exp( (-M + f[:,None] + gv[None,:]) / eps ). 8. Assignment: A = T / T.sum(dim=1, keepdim=True); hard = onehot(argmax_k A); soft = A; q = hard + soft - soft.detach() (straight-through). Code index k_l[i] = argmax_k A[i,k]. 9. Codebook: EMA update with counts = q.sum(dim=0).detach(), decay 0.99, Laplace smoothing 1e-5. 10. Residual: t = log_0(p_l (-) c_l[k_l]); p_{l+1} = exp_0(t). Loop l = 1..L. 11. Loss: standard RQ-VAE reconstruction + commitment (beta from the repository default); the mechanism adds no loss term, only the assignment path. 12. Switches: behaviour-off sets lambda_i = 0; geometry-off replaces arctanh distances by ||.||^2 and step 3 by the Euclidean weighted mean of cache[j, l, :], and steps 1/5/10 by identity maps.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder output z in R^{B x d} for the batch; the frozen behaviour artefact (top-M successor indices and probabilities per item, lambda_i per item); the frozen tangent cache of size N x L x d; the current per-level codebooks c_l in R^{K x d}. Outputs: per-level hard code indices k_l in {0..K-1}^B (concatenated into the item's SID tuple), the straight-through soft assignment q for the encoder backward pass and codebook EMA, the updated tangent cache rows for the batch, and diagnostics (usage counts, mean shift magnitude, mean Poincare cost) for the progress log.
SAFEGUARDS: All exp/log maps use the clamped norm min(max(||.||, 1e-12), 1 - 1e-3) and 2*arctanh is evaluated as log((1+r)/(1-r)) for stability; the Lorentz re-projection uses |eta(xbar,xbar)| with a 1e-12 floor so a null or negative norm cannot produce NaN; the barycenter is fully detached (stop-grad) so no second-order graph runs through the cache; the entropic scale is the measured batch mean cost clipped to [1e-3, 1e2], and both Sinkhorn marginals are floors (1/B, 1/K) with 30 log-domain iterations so no division by zero occurs; empty code rows keep their previous values through Laplace smoothing 1e-5; items with no outgoing training transition and all items on the first step fall back exactly to the unshifted hyperbolic assignment so the mechanism can never make the objective ill-defined; a skipping rule never updates the cache from a batch containing a NaN loss; gradient clipping (repository default) applies to the straight-through assignment; no constant is swept: lambda_i comes from counts, the level strength from the residual-norm ratio, eps from the measured cost, the column marginal from 1/K, and the cache momentum 0.9, Sinkhorn iterations 30, M = 8 and K_c = -1 are documented defaults fixed before the run.
END IDEA
