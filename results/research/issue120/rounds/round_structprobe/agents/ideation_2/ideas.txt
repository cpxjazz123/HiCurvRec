IDEA: BHOT-SINKORN-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-tilted hyperbolic optimal-transport assignment: a Sinkhorn plan over a Poincare cost, tilted by a training-only successor-set prior, chooses every RQ-SID token
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE SIDs are built by a per-item nearest-neighbour argmin in a flat tangent space. Nothing in that rule knows that item i is usually followed by item j, and nothing in it allocates codeword capacity to the items the generator must actually emit; the resulting prefix hierarchy is neither behaviourally coherent nor usage-balanced, which makes prefix-level generation for next-item recommendation harder than it must be. The problem addressed is that the assignment operator itself, not the codebook contents, decides whether a prefix means anything to the sequence model.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms in this programme changed codebook means (successor-weighted Frechet/Karcher), set the curvature value from a prefix-transition score, ranked items radially by behaviour entropy, built behaviour cones, smoothed Lorentz successor profiles, shifted prefixes behaviourally, mixed curvature per branch, used a token-side graph, or transported a behaviour vector along the residual chain. None of them replaced the assignment operator. This idea leaves codebook contents, the curvature value, the radii and the residual chain exactly as in the baseline and changes only how a codeword is selected at each level: the per-item argmin becomes one batch entropic optimal-transport plan whose cost is a Poincare distance (geometry) minus a successor-overlap log prior (behaviour), constrained by usage balance. The SID token is the row argmax of that plan, so the whole prefix hierarchy is the output of a transport problem that neither the behaviour term nor the curvature term can produce alone.
CHANGE: At every level the current Euclidean residual and every codeword are mapped into the Poincare ball of curvature -c; a cost matrix d_Poincare^2 minus sigma^2*log(item-to-codeword-member successor overlap) is turned into a doubly-stochastic plan by 20 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations, and the SID token becomes the argmax of that plan instead of the argmin of the Euclidean distance. Reconstruction, decoder, token vocabulary, sequence length and the Stage 3 pipeline are byte-identical to baseline.
SPECIFICATION: Per level l with Euclidean residual r_i in R^d: x_i = exp^c_0(r_i), q_k = exp^c_0(C_l[k]); A_ik = sum_j P_ij m_jk + eps with P the row-normalized top-50 training transition matrix and m the detached running-average plan of level l, eps = 1e-8; Ct_ik = d_c(x_i,q_k)^2 - sigma^2*log A_ik; G = exp(-Ct/sigma^2); 20 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations u = 1/(G v), v = 1/(G^T u) with marginals 1/B and 1/K; Pplan = diag(u) G diag(v); token k_l = argmax_k Pplan_ik; the baseline quantization and commitment losses are then applied to C_l[k_l] with a straight-through estimator exactly as before, so no new loss weight appears. Constants are measured, not chosen: c = 1/(4*median_i||r_{1,i}||^2) from the frozen initial encoder over the 24556 training items, sigma^2 = median_i min_k d_c(exp^c_0(r_{1,i}), exp^c_0(C_1[k]))^2 after k-means codebook init, m a plain cumulative average (no rate constant), 20 Sinkhorn iterations as the documented default.
COST: B ~ 1023 items per step (24556/24). Cost matrix 1023x256x32 per level ~ 8.4M MAC; 20 Sinkhorn iterations ~ 5.2M MAC per level; successor tilt sparse gather 1023x50x256 ~ 13M MAC per level; about 1.1e8 MAC per step, roughly 5-7 percent of step time, plus ~100 MB host memory for the four 24556x256 running-average plans (fp32). Estimated 1.5 extra hours over the 72000-step, 3000-epoch Stage 2 run on four cards; zero extra Stage 3 cost because vocabulary, sequence length and prefix trie are unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: If the token-choice operator rather than the codebook contents is the binding constraint on prefix quality, then the coupled run should show higher training-transition prefix adjacency and higher level-wise usage entropy than the recorded baseline while keeping reconstruction distortion within 3 percent of baseline, and Stage 3 test_recall@10 should clear 0.065. If recall is flat while adjacency and usage entropy rise, the assignment change is not what the generator needs; if recall falls while prefix mass concentrates, the successor tilt is collapsing level-1 discriminability.
RISKS: Batch-level plans make token assignments mildly batch-composition dependent, so token noise between steps may dominate a single-run verdict.; The successor tilt can pull many items onto codewords whose members are popular successors, destroying level-1 discriminability (high adjacency, worse recall).; The balanced usage marginal fights the reconstruction objective on skewed item popularity, raising distortion for head items.; The Poincare cost can be nearly rank-equivalent to the Euclidean cost if codeword norms stay uniform, making the geometry arm almost identical and the coupling nominally present but inert.; The detached running-average plan can lag behind moving codebooks and feed stale successor statistics.; Top-50 successor rows miss long-tail items, which fall back to the pure curved cost and concentrate any gain in head items.; A single run with no ablation means the judgement rests on one recall number; behavioural diagnostics cannot rescue it.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transition prior from training sequences (row-normalized top-50 successor distribution per item, self-loops removed), converted into a successor-set overlap between an item and the current member set of each codeword.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Scan the Stage-2 training split sequences only; count directed co-occurrences C_ij of item i followed by item j within a window of 2; set C_ii = 0; keep the 50 largest counts per row; row-normalize to P. At each training step, the level-l plan is accumulated into the detached running average m (24556 x K per level), and A_ik = sum_j P_ij m_jk + eps is the expected member mass of codeword k among item i's training successors.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: P, m, c and sigma^2 are built only from Stage-2 training sequences and training forward passes. No validation or test interaction enters the transition counts, the plans, the codebooks, the curvature or the temperature. The item-to-token table is produced by the same procedure as the baseline, from training items only, with the identical vocabulary size, so Stage 3 sees exactly the information it saw before; behavioural diagnostics are computed on training transitions, never on validation or test pairs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of fixed negative curvature -c with c = 1/(4*median||r_1||^2); exponential map exp^c_0(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)/(sqrt(c)||v||) * v; distance d_c(x,q) = (2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)||(-x) (+) q||) with Mobius addition (+). The squared Poincare distance is the transport cost, and its Riemannian scale, not a tuned constant, sets the entropic temperature.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ quantizer, at the assignment step of every level; codebook contents, codebook updates, the residual chain, the reconstruction and the decoder are untouched.
COUPLING PATH: residual r_i -> exp^c_0 -> Poincare distance to exp^c_0(C_l[k]) -> cost matrix -> Sinkhorn plan under a usage-balance constraint -> row argmax gives token k_l -> that token drives the baseline quantization and commitment losses and the next residual. The behaviour log-prior enters the same cost matrix, and the plan is literally exp(-d_Poincare^2/sigma^2) multiplied by A_ik, so zeroing A_ik (behaviour off) or setting c = 0 (geometry off) each changes the plan, hence the token, hence the gradient that shapes the whole prefix hierarchy.
COUPLING EQUATION: Pplan = Sinkhorn_20( exp( -( d_{P,c}( exp^c_0(r_i), exp^c_0(C_l[k]) )^2 - sigma^2 * log( sum_j P_ij m_jk + eps ) ) / sigma^2 ) ), and k_l = argmax_k Pplan_ik.
OBSERVABLES: Training-transition prefix adjacency (fraction of consecutive training pairs sharing the first token, and sharing the first two tokens); mean row entropy of the plan and its drift over epochs; level-wise token usage entropy and Gini; correlation between token mass and item demand; reconstruction distortion relative to baseline; the plan's total transport cost per epoch; Stage 3 test_recall@10 as the only judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: The tilt is removed, Ct_ik = d_c(exp^c_0(r_i), exp^c_0(C_l[k]))^2, so the plan is a purely geometric balanced OT assignment carrying no successor information; the BEH_OFF path runs the same Sinkhorn on the same codebooks and the same constants. Implemented and correct, not trained this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c is set to 0, so exp^c_0 is the identity and d_c becomes the Euclidean distance; Ct_ik = ||r_i - C_l[k]||^2 - sigma^2*log A_ik and the plan is a flat behaviour-tilted balanced assignment. The GEO_OFF path runs through the same functions with the manifold calls skipped. Implemented and correct, not trained this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The default and only trained run: c from the initial-encoder residual median, sigma^2 from the median nearest-codeword Poincare distance after k-means init, P from Stage-2 training transitions, running-average plans with one uniform warm-up epoch, 20 Sinkhorn iterations, 3000 Stage 2 epochs on four cards at 24 steps per epoch, then the frozen Stage 3 protocol. No sweep, no seed repetition, no early stopping.
SPEC EQUATIONS: c = 1/(4*median_i||r_{1,i}||^2); sigma^2 = median_i min_k d_c(exp^c_0(r_{1,i}), exp^c_0(C_1[k]))^2; C_ij = sum over training sequences of 1[j follows i within window 2], C_ii = 0, P = rownorm(keeptop50(C)). For level l = 1..L: x = exp^c_0(r); q_k = exp^c_0(C_l[k]); S_ik = sum_j P_ij m_jk + 1e-8; Ct_ik = d_c(x,q_k)^2 - sigma^2*log S_ik; G_ik = exp(-Ct_ik/sigma^2); log-domain Sinkhorn 20 iterations alternating row scaling to 1/B and column scaling to 1/K; k_l = argmax_k Pplan_ik; m <- (t*m + batchmean(Pplan))/(t+1) with m detached; residual update, reconstruction and losses identical to the baseline RQ-VAE with C_l[k_l] and a straight-through estimator.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: latent z and its per-level Euclidean residual r (d=32), codebooks C_l (K x d per level, L=4), sparse P (24556 x 24556, 50 per row), running-average plans m_l (24556 x K, detached), and the two measured constants c and sigma^2. Outputs: the SID token tuple (k_1..k_L), the plan Pplan of every level for diagnostics and for the m update, and the unchanged quantization and commitment losses with their unchanged weights.
SAFEGUARDS: Log-domain Sinkhorn with 1e-8 added to G, fixed 20 iterations, row and column renormalisation after each half-step; clip sqrt(c)||r|| and sqrt(c)||C_l[k]|| to at most 1.0 so the Poincare radius stays at or below tanh(1) ~ 0.76 and the metric factor cannot blow up; clamp the Mobius sum norm to 1-1e-6 before artanh and use a Taylor branch of artanh for arguments below 1e-4; add 1e-8 to every norm before division; empty successor rows give S_ik = 1e-8 only, so those items are assigned by the pure curved cost; deterministic tie-breaking on the lowest codebook index; m is a detached buffer, warm-started at uniform 1/K for one epoch, never receives gradient and never leaves the training split; c and sigma^2 are frozen after measurement so no constant drifts into a tuned value; codebooks, vocabulary size, sequence length and prefix trie stay identical to baseline, and SID collisions are handled exactly as before, so Stage 3 and its feeding code need no modification.
END IDEA
