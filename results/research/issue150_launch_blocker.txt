Issue #150: why the formal Stage2 has not started (documented blocker)

Four dispatches of the single formal run were attempted and each died before the
first epoch printed a line. The chain of evidence, in the order it was obtained:

1. dispatch 1 — DDP wrap aborted: "No backend type associated with device type
   cpu". Cause: target_marginal is a registered (non-persistent) buffer, DDP
   broadcasts every buffer, and it had been created on the CPU after the model
   moved to CUDA. Fixed in bac21302 (buffer built on the codebook's device).

2. dispatch 2 — "Cannot center a constant distance matrix" on ranks 1 and 3.
   Cause: the proxy centres every level, and MixedVQLevel.center_distance raises
   on a matrix with no spread because the baseline only centres one it is about to
   balance. Fixed in 34d82de7 (centred_cost: zero on a flat matrix, raise with the
   range on a non-finite one).

3. dispatch 3 — "the cost matrix is not finite: max=nan min=nan" on all ranks.
   Instrumented in 28e51322: the message names the call site.

4. dispatch 4 — "the cost matrix is not finite at level 1: max=nan min=nan
   finite_rows=0.0 shape=(1024, 256)", then, with the tensor guard of 2b7b2c10,
   "the encoder output is not finite: finite share 0.0 shape (1024, 32)".

So in the launched run the encoder produces NaN for the whole batch at step 0,
which means its parameters (or their broadcast) are NaN, since the inputs are
finite: the embedding matrix is finite with |x| <= 0.59 and 24556 TRAIN rows, all
non-zero.

What does *not* reproduce it, all run inside this repository:

* one process, batch 1024, the trainer's own main(): one epoch completes,
  collision 0.360 at epoch 1 (results recorded in the round's notes);
* one process, batch 4096: one epoch completes (issue150_smoke.txt);
* four NCCL ranks replaying the trainer's exact order — attach the prior,
  initialise the weights with set_seed(SEED+rank), wrap in DDP, initialise the
  codebooks on the full TRAIN set, forward 1024 shuffled rows per rank — every
  level finite, 256 distinct codes per level, ball gap 0.91, residual norm 0.30
  (results/research/issue150/four_rank_step0.json).

The difference is therefore confined to the launch environment or to a part of
main() that the replay does not execute, not to the mechanism, the data or the
arithmetic. The next step is to print, inside the launch, whether the encoder's
parameters themselves are finite before the first forward, and to bisect the
launcher's env (NCCL/OMP/TORCH_NCCL_BLOCKING_WAIT) against the replay. Until that
is settled the arm has no checkpoint, no item_sids.json and no collision number,
so nothing downstream of it can run.
