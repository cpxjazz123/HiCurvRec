IDEA: poincare_residual_direction_sinkhorn_v1
TITLE: Poincare Conformal Residual-Direction Sinkhorn: train-only transition-direction bias on levels 2-3 under a curvature set by the measured residual scale
MOTIVATION: The rejected round proved coarse layers can be re-drawn by behaviour (L1 Hard ER 16.39->6.36, all 256 L1 codes used) but that doing so eats fine-layer resolution: only 955 distinct L2 prefixes against 7414 in the frozen baseline, weighted 3-level collision 0.771 against 0.275, refused before Stage3 because the coarse partition had already consumed the behaviour cohesion and left residuals small, magnitude-dominated and mutually aligned. The recommendation problem this attacks is exactly that: the first Semantic-ID token decides the coarse regime, but the second and third tokens, which are what makes a 3-level SID address 24556 items instead of 256, lose their discriminative power. The fix must add fine-layer separability without re-partitioning the coarse layer.
NOVELTY: Different from curvature-stratum-sinkhorn-v1 in four coupled ways: (1) the behaviour quantity is a vector, not a scalar rank - a popularity-adjusted successor centroid in latent space rotated onto the residual tangent - so it changes the *direction* of a residual, not the level it belongs to; (2) it acts only at levels 2 and 3, with the L1 cost matrix provably bit-identical to the frozen baseline, so the coarse occupancy budget is untouched; (3) the curvature is not a stratification rho_i against a stratum centre, it is a conformal metric on residual directions with kappa_l = -1/R_l^2 where R_l is the measured training median residual norm of that level, i.e. the geometry is derived from the level's own residual scale; (4) the behaviour bias is applied as an angle in the ball, so it cannot influence the radial coordinate and therefore cannot pull items onto a shared fine code. No existing mechanism here couples a directed-transition latent centroid with a conformal Poincare residual metric inside the balanced Sinkhorn cost.
CHANGE: At levels 2 and 3, replace the Euclidean residual-to-codeword cost inside the baseline Sinkhorn plan by a Poincare-ball distance between (a) the item residual lifted by tanh(||r||/2R_l) along a behaviour-rotated unit direction and (b) the codebook vector lifted by the same radial map; the assignment rule, its uniform marginals and its balancing are unchanged, only the cost matrix changes.
SPECIFICATION: Notation: z_i encoder output (decode-side latent) for training item i, c^1_{k1(i)} its frozen-baseline level-1 codeword, r_i^l the level-l residual (r^2 = z - c^1, r^3 = z - c^1 - c^2). Level 1 is untouched: C^1 is the baseline cost and the baseline plan is reused verbatim. Training-only behaviour: PPMI-clipped directed transition successors. For each level l in {2,3} and each epoch: (1) measure R_l = median_i ||r_i^l|| over the 24556 training items (detached, no gradient) and set kappa_l = -1/R_l^2; (2) build the successor centroid h_i = sum_j w_ij sg(z_j), w_ij the row-normalized positive-PPMI weights, capped at 32 successors, sg = stop-grad; (3) unit residual rhat_i = r_i/||r_i||, behaviour tangent b_i = normalize(h_i - z_i), tangential part u_i = b_i - (b_i . rhat_i) rhat_i (zero if the item has no positive-PPMI successor or ||r_i|| < 1e-6); (4) behaviour-rotated direction v_i = normalize(rhat_i + u_i); (5) radial coordinate a_i = tanh(||r_i|| / (2 R_l)); (6) lifted points p_i = a_i v_i and e_k = tanh(||c_k|| / (2 R_l)) * normalize(c_k); (7) cost C^l_{ik} = (1/sqrt(|kappa_l|)) * arcosh(1 + 2|kappa_l| ||p_i - e_k||^2 / ((1 - |kappa_l| ||p_i||^2)(1 - |kappa_l| ||e_k||^2))); (8) Sinkhorn with the baseline rule and the baseline marginals on C^l, temperature rescaled scale-relatively as eps_l = eps_base * mean(C^l) / mean(C^l_euclid) computed detached once per epoch, and take the plan's argmax column as the level-l index; (9) codebook and encoder gradients flow through the plan and through C^l, hence through the behaviour direction, so the behaviour changes parameter updates, not only indices. Collapse guards are structural, not tuned: behaviour never enters the radius a_i, never enters level 1, and never enters the marginals.
COST: Under 5 percent wall clock for the single frozen 3000-epoch, four-card run, and no extra parameters. Per-step at the frozen scale (batch 1024, 24 steps per epoch, 24556 training items): two extra 1024x256 cost matrices with an arcosh each, about 0.04 GFLOP per step at d=64, well under 1 percent of a transformer step, plus two Sinkhorn plans of the size the baseline already solves at every level. Per-epoch: one sparse successor-centroid table, at most 24556x32 non-zeros times d, about 0.05 GFLOP against roughly 240 GFLOP of training steps per epoch; two medians over 24556 values. Memory: one 24556 x d behaviour table (about 6 MB at d=64) and two 256 x d ball-embedded codebooks.
HYPOTHESIS: If the coupling is real then, at fixed Sinkhorn balance and with L1 bit-identical to the baseline, the level-2 and level-3 residual directions of behaviourally distinct items become separable under the conformal metric, so codes are reused across coarse regimes instead of inside one: distinct L2 prefixes must stay at or above the frozen baseline's 7414 (and weighted 3-level collision at or below 0.275) instead of collapsing toward the rejected round's 955 and 0.771, level-2 codebook usage must stay broad, and the one allowed Stage3 run must give test Recall@10 above 0.065. If L2 prefix counts stay flat while Stage3 recall also stays flat, the behaviour direction carried no fine-layer information and the idea is dead on one run, no ablation needed.
RISKS: The behaviour direction for low-degree or cold training items is noisy or zero; if a large fraction of items has no positive-PPMI successor, the effectively coupled subset is small and the fine-layer gain never reaches Stage3.; Conformal amplification near the ball boundary can over-penalize large-residual items and push their L2 indices into a narrow angular sector, which would trade the old collapse for a new one.; The Sinkhorn balance fights an anisotropic cost: marginals stay uniform but the plan may saturate, and the entropy temperature rescaling could make the plan nearly hard and destabilize codebook updates.; tanh radial compression loses resolution among the very small residuals that dominate inside a well-separated L1 code, so level-3 could lose rather than gain prefix variety.; Behaviour built from training transitions reinforces popularity head behaviour and can homogenize the fine codes of long-tail items that actually carry much of Recall@10.; A single run with seed noise: if the effect is smaller than run-to-run recall variance, the mechanism cannot be distinguished from noise at this budget.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item transitions from the training split, turned into a per-item latent successor centroid whose tangential component defines the residual direction bias.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only, count directed adjacent (i to j) co-occurrence inside each training user sequence. Form PMI_ij = log( count_ij * T / (count_i * count_j) ) with T the total training pair count, clip negative values to zero, keep at most 32 successors per item by descending PMI, and row-normalize to weights w_ij. Multiply the stop-gradient encode-side latents of the successors: h_i = sum_j w_ij sg(z_j), then b_i = normalize(h_i - z_i). Everything is recomputed once per epoch from the current encoder latents with the transition counts fixed; no validation or test sequence is ever counted.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: SID construction reads only training-split sequences and the encoder latents of training items. The transition counts, the PPMI weights, the successor centroids, the medians R_l and the temperature scale ratios are all measured on the 24556 training items before any validation or test pass, and the SIDs are exported once from that training-only quantizer; Stage 3 then uses them as fixed strings. No validation or test item, sequence, prefix or statistic enters the cost matrix, so the curvature and the behaviour direction carry zero test information and cannot overfit the evaluation split by construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball distance with fixed negative curvature kappa_l = -1/R_l^2, R_l the measured training median residual norm of level l: d(x,y) = (1/sqrt(|kappa_l|)) * arcosh(1 + 2|kappa_l| ||x-y||^2 / ((1 - |kappa_l| ||x||^2)(1 - |kappa_l| ||y||^2))), together with the radial exp_0 map a = tanh(||r|| / (2 R_l)) that places each residual at a curvature-consistent radius.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the residual quantizer at levels 2 and 3, in the cost matrix of the balanced Sinkhorn assignment, both at every training step (the code indices used for reconstruction and the gradients they carry) and at the final SID export; level 1 is left on the baseline Euclidean cost.
COUPLING PATH: One connected path: training transitions -> PPMI successor centroid h_i -> tangential behaviour direction u_i -> behaviour-rotated unit residual v_i -> lifted ball point p_i = tanh(||r_i||/2R_l) * v_i -> Poincare cost C^l(i,k) against the ball-lifted codewords -> baseline Sinkhorn plan -> level-l code index and the reconstruction gradient on codebooks and encoder. Remove the behaviour (u_i = 0) and p_i becomes the plain radial lift, so every cost entry, plan entry and gradient changes; remove the curvature (Euclidean cost on r_i + ||r_i|| u_i) and the conformal anisotropy disappears, so the same plan and gradients change again.
COUPLING EQUATION: C^l_{ik} = (1/sqrt(|kappa_l|)) * arcosh( 1 + 2|kappa_l| ||p_i - e_k||^2 / ((1-|kappa_l|||p_i||^2)(1-|kappa_l|||e_k||^2)) ) with p_i = tanh(||r_i^l|| / (2 R_l)) * normalize( rhat_i^l + normalize(h_i - z_i)_tangential ), i.e. the curved term is d_poincare_kappa( . , . ) and the behaviour term is the tangential successor-centroid rotation normalize(rhat + u), both inside the same cost entry that feeds the baseline Sinkhorn plan.
OBSERVABLES: Distinct L2 prefix count and weighted 3-level collision rate recorded every epoch (diagnostic, not a gate); level-2 and level-3 codebook usage histograms; the fraction of items whose level-2 index differs from the behaviour-off cost; level-2 reconstruction loss; the Sinkhorn marginals and the L1 plan checked bit-identical to the baseline; finally Stage3 test Recall@10 above 0.065 on the single run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Same code with the behaviour bias zeroed (u_i = 0 everywhere), so the cost is the pure curvature-scaled Poincare residual distance; the plan and the gradients still change because the tangential term is gone. Exists in the code, never trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Same code with the curved distance replaced by the flat Euclidean cost || r_i + ||r_i|| u_i - c_k ||^2 while keeping the behaviour-rotated residual, so the conformal anisotropy is gone but the behaviour direction survives. Exists in the code, never trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run: behaviour-rotated residual direction inside the curvature-scaled Poincare cost at levels 2 and 3, baseline Sinkhorn balance and baseline level 1, trained once for 3000 epochs on four cards, judged by Stage3 test Recall@10.
SPEC EQUATIONS: 1) w = row_normalize( top32( max(0, log( count_ij * T / (count_i * count_j) ) ) ) ) over training adjacent pairs. 2) h_i = sum_j w_ij sg(z_j); b_i = normalize(h_i - z_i); rhat_i = r_i / (||r_i|| + 1e-8); u_i = b_i - (b_i . rhat_i) rhat_i, zeroed if no successor. 3) v_i = normalize(rhat_i + u_i); a_i = tanh( clip(||r_i|| / (2 R_l), 0, 3) ); p_i = a_i v_i. 4) e_k = tanh( clip(||c_k|| / (2 R_l), 0, 3) ) * normalize(c_k). 5) d_ik = (1/sqrt(|kappa_l|)) * arcosh( clamp(1 + 2|kappa_l| ||p_i - e_k||^2 / ( max(1 - |kappa_l|||p_i||^2, 1e-6) * max(1 - |kappa_l|||e_k||^2, 1e-6) ), min=1+1e-7) ). 6) C^l = d; eps_l = eps_base * mean(C^l) / mean(C^l_euclid) (detached, once per epoch). 7) Pi = sinkhorn(C^l, uniform marginals, eps_l, baseline iterations); index_l(i) = argmax_k Pi_ik. 8) level 1: C^1 and Pi^1 identical to the frozen baseline.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latents z_i of the training batch, the item residuals r_i^l at levels 2 and 3, the current codewords c_k^l, the fixed sparse PPMI successor matrix W (24556 x 24556, at most 32 non-zeros per row), the per-epoch stop-gradient latent matrix Z (24556 x d), and the baseline Sinkhorn configuration (marginals, iterations, eps_base). Outputs: a behaviour vector h_i and tangential direction u_i per item, curvature-scaled cost matrices C^2, C^3 of shape batch x 256, balanced transport plans Pi^2, Pi^3, integer level-2 and level-3 indices, and the differentiable graph that carries the reconstruction gradient back into the codewords and the encoder; at export, the same routine returns the frozen 3-level SID strings.
SAFEGUARDS: Radial argument clipped to [0,3] before tanh so a_i <= 1 - 1e-9 and the conformal denominator is floored at 1e-6; arcosh argument clamped at 1+1e-7; normalization epsilon 1e-8; u_i set to zero when the item has no positive-PPMI successor or when ||r_i|| < 1e-6; R_l floored at 1e-6 and recomputed as a detached training median once per epoch; eps_l rescaled scale-relatively and never swept; stop-grad on successor latents, on R_l and on the temperature ratio so behaviour cannot be gamed by shrinking latents; the level-1 cost matrix, marginals and plan are asserted bit-identical to the baseline by a unit check, and Sinkhorn marginals are asserted uniform after every plan, so the balancing that keeps codebook usage even is provably the baseline's; per-epoch distinct-prefix and collision counters are logged as diagnostics only, never used as an admission gate.
END IDEA
