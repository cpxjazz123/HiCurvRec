IDEA: cohort_anchor_32
TITLE: Transition-cohort anchors for a compact hyperbolic first level
MOTIVATION: The beam loses targets when early prefixes branch too widely, yet a useful first token must group items likely to follow similar training histories.
NOVELTY: Learn first-level anchors from behavior-conditioned cohort prototypes, rather than applying a behavior penalty to a fixed Euclidean quantizer. Cohort evidence moves the curved anchors themselves; the first-level budget is explicitly 32 groups.
CHANGE: Build each item's smoothed distribution over its training-only predecessor cohorts, map cohort prototypes into the Poincare ball, and optimize 32 first-level anchors against both item embeddings and those prototypes using one geodesic assignment cost. Residual Euclidean RQ-VAE stages encode remaining item detail.
SPECIFICATION: For each training item i, let q_i be its normalized smoothed distribution over predecessor-item clusters (clusters obtained by spherical k-means on frozen training item embeddings; include only directed transitions in train). Let a_k be trainable Poincare anchors, and p_g fixed Poincare embeddings of predecessor clusters. Define b_ik = sum_g q_ig d_c(a_k,p_g)^2 / (sum_g q_ig + eps). Assign z_i=argmin_k [d_c(x_i,a_k)^2 + lambda b_ik], with 32 anchors; train the encoder and anchors by the same softmin expected cost plus standard VQ commitment/reconstruction losses. Quantize residuals at L2/L3 with the baseline Euclidean residual quantizer; T4 remains collision extension only. Budget: T1 at most log2(32)=5 bits, T2/T3 share the remaining discriminative budget (target combined 8–10 nats), T4 target <0.1 nats. At the frozen scale, precompute q and prototypes once; assignment work is O(24*24556*32*G) only if dense, so store q sparse top-8 and calculate O(24*24556*32*8) scalar distances; roughly 151M distance terms/epoch and 453B over 3000 epochs, but only the first-layer warmup/assignment pass (e.g. evaluate b once per epoch and cache) reduces this to 151M terms total per epoch plus negligible anchor updates. This should be benchmarked; cache b_ik per epoch and refresh once per epoch.
COST: Precompute the transition/cohort table once; per epoch cached behavior cost is O(N*32*8) (~6.3M scalar distance contributions), plus baseline training. The uncached upper bound is ~151M contributions/epoch; at 24 steps, compute b only on the item minibatch and cache detached costs for the epoch. Expected modest memory for N*32 float costs (~3.1M values, 12 MB); no extra Stage3 cost.
HYPOTHESIS: Similar predecessor cohorts have compact neighborhoods in negative curvature, and 32 curved anchors can improve first-prefix predictability without erasing useful distinctions. Keep only if canonical 52,855-pair/10-fixed-negative L1 Hard ER remains informative, gold-prefix survival through depths 1–3 rises, T4 NLL remains near constant, and frozen-protocol Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: Predecessor clusters may be weak proxies for next-item intent; popular transitions can dominate prototypes; coarse 32-way assignments can overmerge unrelated items; behavior costs can overwhelm content geometry. Sparse transition coverage needs smoothing and a no-history fallback.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate item transitions, summarized as predecessor-cluster distributions for each target item.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From train.parquet only, count consecutive (previous item,target item) pairs within each user's training sequence; map previous items to fixed embedding clusters and form q_i,g=(count(i,g)+alpha)/(sum_g count(i,g)+alpha G). Never read validation or test rows.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct transitions and q only from train interactions; item embeddings/clusters are frozen Stage1 content artifacts, not derived from validation/test interactions. Validation/test are used only after SID freezing for evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Poincare distance d_c^2 at fixed negative curvature -c (c=1), with expmap0 for anchor/prototype parameterization and projection to radius (1-eps)/sqrt(c).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First-level anchor learning and assignment during Stage2 quantization; residual levels remain baseline Euclidean and T4 remains deterministic collision extension.
COUPLING PATH: Each target's train-derived cohort distribution weights geodesic distance to cohort prototypes, changing the argmin over trainable curved first-level anchors; removing q or replacing d_c changes the same assignment.
COUPLING EQUATION: z_i=argmin_{k in [32]} {d_c(x_i,a_k)^2 + lambda sum_g q_i,g d_c(a_k,p_g)^2}; q_i,g is the behavior term and d_c is the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on the canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives; gold-prefix survival at depths 1,2,3,4; T4 NLL; Stage3 test_R@10. Capacity target: T1 ≤5 bits, T2/T3 carry item discrimination, T4 <0.1 nats. Predict L1 ER lower than 256-way baseline but nonzero, depth-1 survival higher, depth-2/3 survival higher, T4 NLL approximately unchanged, and Recall@10 >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 while retaining 32 Poincare anchors and identical optimizer/data/training schedule.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace every first-level d_c squared distance with squared Euclidean distance, retain q, lambda, 32 anchors and matched parameter count.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only q-weighted Poincare assignment with fixed c=1 and 32 anchors; compare all three arms under identical 3000-epoch Stage2 and frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: q_i,g as above; map Euclidean item vector u_i to x_i=exp_0^c(u_i); parameterize anchor raw vectors v_k and prototypes r_g, map with exp_0^c; compute C_ik=d_c(x_i,a_k)^2+lambda sum_g q_i,g d_c(a_k,p_g)^2; hard SID token argmin_k C_ik, with soft assignment during training. Use standard residual quantization on x_i after subtracting the selected first-level reconstruction in the existing baseline space.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen item embeddings, train-only sequences, cluster map, and Stage2 minibatch. Output is one of 32 T1 tokens per item, updated encoder/anchors, and unchanged baseline T2/T3 plus deterministic T4.
SAFEGUARDS: Use alpha smoothing, q top-8 renormalization, epsilon denominators, Poincare projection, float32 distance accumulation, clamp sinh/arcosh arguments, gradient clipping, and a fixed no-history prior. Cache behavior costs once per epoch; deterministic seeds and fixed negatives across arms.
END IDEA

IDEA: branchwise_curvature_router
TITLE: Branching-adaptive curvature routes successor cohorts through the first-level ball
MOTIVATION: A single global curvature cannot simultaneously keep predictable high-confidence successor branches compact and give ambiguous histories enough angular separation.
NOVELTY: Unlike a behavior loss added to a fixed-geometry quantizer or a global curvature schedule, this sets a per-item curvature from train-only successor uncertainty and uses that curvature directly in first-level codeword ranking. Retain 64 effective first-level groups to cap branching.
CHANGE: Estimate each item's successor entropy from training transitions, map it to a bounded negative curvature, and assign its embedding to one of 64 first-level codewords by the corresponding Poincare distance. The uncertainty-derived curvature is therefore an input to the very distance that selects T1, not a side objective.
SPECIFICATION: From train-only transitions define p(j|i)=(n_ij+alpha)/(n_i+alpha V_i) over observed successors plus a fixed unknown bucket, and h_i=H(p(.|i))/log(max(2, support_i+1)). Let c_i=c_min+(c_max-c_min)h_i, with c_min=0.25,c_max=2. For raw embedding u_i and codeword v_k, x_i=exp_0^{c_i}(u_i), a_ik=exp_0^{c_i}(v_k), C_ik=d_{c_i}(x_i,a_ik)^2; assign argmin among 64. Train using softmin(C_i) plus baseline reconstruction/commitment losses, and residual-quantize at T2/T3. Capacity budget: T1 ≤6 bits; T2/T3 target combined ≥8 nats; T4 <0.1 nats. Entropy and curvature are itemwise precomputed; per minibatch cost is 24*24556*64 = 37.7M pair distances per epoch if all items scanned once per step; at the frozen 24 steps this is 37.7M per epoch, 113B across 3000 epochs. The additional cost is one curvature scalar per item and distance scaling; cache exp maps per item where encoder is frozen or recompute for the minibatch, roughly 5–15% overhead depending on distance kernel.
COST: One transition pass O(number of train transitions); 24*24556*64 (~37.7M) first-level distances/epoch and ~113B over the full 3000 epochs, approximately 5–15% over baseline (kernel-dependent), no added Stage3 compute. Use vectorized batched distances and cached h_i; avoid materializing the full item-code matrix outside each minibatch.
HYPOTHESIS: Successor uncertainty should determine how much negative curvature is useful: low entropy histories get tighter, easier-to-predict prefixes, while high entropy histories preserve separation. Worth keeping only if this conditional allocation increases depth-1/2/3 gold survival and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 crosses 0.065 without T4 becoming informative.
RISKS: Entropy may mostly encode item popularity or sparse-count noise; curvature changes distance scale and could create an unintended assignment bias; item-dependent metrics may fragment shared prefixes; clipping the curvature range may remove the effect.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Normalized entropy of each item's outgoing next-item distribution (successor uncertainty).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count adjacent directed transitions (i,j) within users' train split only; apply additive alpha smoothing over observed successors and an unknown bucket, then compute normalized entropy per source item. Items with no train outgoing transitions receive the global train entropy median.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transition counts and entropy are built solely from train.parquet, without validation/test sequences or popularity counts from those splits; freeze h_i and all generated SIDs before Stage3 validation/test evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp map and distance at per-item negative curvature -c_i, c_i in [0.25,2].
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The first-level codeword distance used for both training soft assignments and final hard T1 quantization; not used as an auxiliary loss.
COUPLING PATH: Train-derived successor entropy determines c_i, which parameterizes the exp maps and geodesic distance that ranks the 64 T1 codewords; removing behavior fixes c_i, and geometry-off replaces the ranking metric.
COUPLING EQUATION: c_i=0.25+1.75h_i; z_i=argmin_{k in [64]} d_{c_i}(exp_0^{c_i}(u_i),exp_0^{c_i}(v_k))^2; h_i is the behavior term and d_{c_i} the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: L1 Hard ER on canonical pairs/ten fixed negatives; gold-prefix survival depths 1–4; T4 NLL; Stage3 test_R@10. Capacity target: T1 ≤6 bits, T2/T3 carry most remaining SID information, T4 <0.1 nats. Predict modestly lower L1 ER than a 256-way T1, better depth-1 survival and sustained depth-2/3 survival, unchanged near-constant T4 NLL, and >0.065 Recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set all c_i to the train-item mean curvature while keeping Poincare distance, 64 codewords, and all other settings fixed.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Use Euclidean squared distance with the same entropy-conditioned scalar as a matched radial temperature on the assignment logits, so the behavior signal remains but curved exp/log and geodesic ranking are removed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use item-specific c_i from train successor entropy inside Poincare exp maps and distance for the 64-way T1 assignment.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Compute h_i=H(p(.|i))/log(max(2,|support_i|+1)); c_i=c_min+(c_max-c_min)h_i. Project raw u_i,v_k to radius (1-eps)/sqrt(c_i), calculate d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)⊕_c y||), and train with softmax(-C_ik/tau) assignment plus standard RQ-VAE terms. T2/T3 use baseline residuals; T4 is deterministic collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen item embeddings, train-only transition counts, train minibatches, and 64 trainable first-level codewords. Outputs are item-specific curvature values and four-token SIDs with the first token selected by the curved assignment.
SAFEGUARDS: Smooth counts; cap normalized entropy and c_i; assign median curvature for no-history items; project strictly inside each ball; clamp atanh inputs to 1-eps; use float32 distance accumulation, gradient clipping, fixed seeds and fixed evaluation negatives. Curvature is bounded and fixed from train statistics, not jointly inferred from validation/test.
END IDEA
