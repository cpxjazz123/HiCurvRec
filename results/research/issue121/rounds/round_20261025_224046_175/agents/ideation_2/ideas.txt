IDEA: tangent_successor_residual
TITLE: Transition-directed tangent residuals preserve predictable coarse prefixes
MOTIVATION: The first generated token must be predictable from a user's recent item while later tokens retain item-specific distinctions; the available training transitions can supervise the residual direction without making the first level high-branching.
NOVELTY: Unlike cohort-anchor placement, behavior-conditioned curvature routing, or a behavior gate on first-level distance, this applies a directed transition signal to the *residual update between levels*: successor evidence bends the residual along a curved geodesic, rather than moving first-level anchors or adding a separate transition loss. T1 has 32 groups; T2/T3 retain most discrimination and T4 is collision extension only.
CHANGE: At each training item, use the train-only distribution of its observed successors to form a tangent-space direction at its selected T1 codeword, and subtract a bounded geodesic step in that direction before T2/T3 quantization. This changes the actual residual presented to subsequent quantizers; without transitions the step is zero, and with Euclidean geometry the parallel transport/log-exp construction is replaced by ordinary vector residual subtraction.
SPECIFICATION: Freeze train transition counts n_ij and p(j|i)=(n_ij+alpha)/(sum_j n_ij+alpha |S_i|) over observed successors S_i. Let m_i be the normalized weighted mean of frozen Stage1 embeddings of successors j (weights p(j|i)); for T1 anchor a_k and item latent x_i in the Poincare ball of curvature -c, form v_i=Log_{a_k}(Exp_0(m_i)) in T_{a_k}B_c, clip its norm to R, and set r_i=PT_{a_k->0}(Log_{a_k}(x_i)-eta v_i). Quantize r_i at L2/L3 using the baseline residual quantizer; hard T1 uses the baseline 32-way curved assignment. Capacity budget: T1 ≤ log(32)=3.47 nats; T2/T3 jointly carry the discriminative information (target 8–10 nats); T4 target <0.1 nats and remains deterministic collision extension. Cost: one precomputed successor prototype per item, then per Stage2 item in the batch one Log, vector subtraction, parallel transport and Exp-equivalent residual operation, O(Bd) beyond baseline; at 24 steps this is 24*B such operations per epoch, versus 24*24556*d scalar-scale operations if all items are covered once (about 600k item-ops/epoch, 3000 epochs), typically <10% arithmetic overhead and no Stage3 cost.
COST: Precompute train successor means in O(E_train*d), store one d-vector and count per item. Per minibatch add O(Bd) tangent/log/transport work (about 24*24556 item-vectors per epoch at full coverage; 3000 epochs), expected <10% Stage2 wall-time overhead subject to manifold-kernel cost; no added Stage3 cost.
HYPOTHESIS: Successor direction is predictable enough from a target item's training transition context that a small geodesic residual correction makes T2/T3 continuations easier to predict from the user's prefix, while T1 stays low-branching. Keep only if depth-1 survival does not fall, depth-2/3 survival rises, T4 NLL remains near constant and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: Item-level successor averages can be multimodal and popularity dominated; a single direction may erase useful semantic residuals or leak source/target identity through a shortcut. If an item has few or no successors the estimate is noisy, and tangent transport may add instability.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate next-item successor distribution for each item, with a popularity-corrected weighted successor embedding prototype.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From train.parquet only, count consecutive within-user transitions i→j. Set raw edge weight w_ij=(n_ij+alpha)/(n_i+alpha|S_i|) * (log(1+N_train)/(log(2+pop_train(j)))); normalize over observed successors, then m_i=sum_j w_ij e_j / sum_j w_ij using frozen Stage1 item embeddings. Use the train global embedding mean for items with no outgoing train edge. All counts and m_i are frozen before Stage2 SID export.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build edges, item popularity, successor sets and prototypes exclusively from train.parquet; Stage1 embeddings are content-only frozen artifacts. No validation/test interactions enter statistics, residual transforms, codebook fitting or SID assignment; evaluate only after the full SID file is frozen.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball Log/Exp maps at fixed negative curvature -c (c=1) and parallel transport between tangent spaces, with geodesic residual correction.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Within Stage2 after hard T1 selection and before L2/L3 residual quantization; the same transformed residual feeds training and exported hard SIDs. T4 remains the existing deterministic collision-extension token.
COUPLING PATH: The train transition prototype determines the tangent vector at the selected curved T1 anchor; the hyperbolic log map and parallel transport convert that behavior vector into the residual correction that directly determines T2/T3 assignments. Removing either changes those assignments.
COUPLING EQUATION: r_i=PT_{a_{k_i}->0}[Log_{a_{k_i}}(x_i)-eta clip_R(Log_{a_{k_i}}(Exp_0(m_i)))]; m_i=sum_j w_ij e_j is the behavior term and Log/Exp/PT on B_c are the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: On canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives report L1 Hard ER, gold-prefix survival depths 1–4, T4 NLL and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Predict L1 ER broadly unchanged from its matched 32-way control, depth-1 survival maintained or improved and depth-2/3 survival improved, T4 NLL unchanged (<0.1 nats target), and test_R@10 >0.065. Capacity budget: T1 ≤3.47 nats; T2/T3 carry item discrimination; T4 <0.1 nats.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set eta=0 and bypass all successor prototypes; retain 32-way T1, identical Poincare residual quantization, optimization, training steps and fixed seeds.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Log/Exp/parallel transport with Euclidean residual r_i=x_i-eta clip_R(m_i), retaining the identical train-only prototypes, eta, 32-way T1 and all training settings.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only popularity-corrected successor prototypes inside the fixed-curvature Poincare tangent residual update, followed by matched T2/T3 quantization and deterministic T4.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Given latent x_i, choose k_i=argmin_{k≤32} d_c(x_i,a_k)^2. Compute v_i=Log_{a_{k_i}}(Exp_0(m_i)); r_i=PT_{a_{k_i}->0}(Log_{a_{k_i}}(x_i)-eta min(1,R/(||v_i||+eps))v_i). Feed r_i to the baseline L2/L3 residual quantizer and reconstruction/commitment objective. For behavior-off use eta=0; for geometry-off use r_i=x_i-eta clip_R(m_i). Do not append learned behavior tokens; preserve fixed width and collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen item embeddings, train-only transition table/prototypes, Stage2 latent minibatch, and 32 first-level anchors. Outputs: unchanged-capacity T1 plus behavior-adjusted T2/T3 assignments and existing deterministic T4; gradients flow through latent, anchor, and downstream quantizer paths, not through the frozen transition table.
SAFEGUARDS: Add alpha smoothing and popularity floor; require minimum edge count or shrink m_i toward global train mean; clip prototype norm and tangent correction, eta≤0.2 with warmup; clamp ball norms below (1-eps)/sqrt(c), use stable atanh/log implementations, float32 manifold arithmetic, eps denominators, finite-value checks and gradient clipping. Cache m_i; fixed deterministic seeds and frozen evaluation negatives.
END IDEA

IDEA: curved_transport_prefix
TITLE: Successor-aware capacity transport assigns compact hyperbolic prefix cells
MOTIVATION: Prefix branching can exhaust a width-20 beam even if the first token is semantically plausible; a capacity-constrained assignment can prevent a few popular or ambiguous items from consuming too many first-level cells while using transition similarity to decide which items share each cell.
NOVELTY: This couples directed successor-neighborhood compatibility to the *transport cost matrix* of a balanced first-level assignment, rather than placing cohort anchors, conditioning curvature, or adding a transition regularizer to independent nearest-neighbor quantization. A small 32-cell first level has an explicit beam-friendly capacity; subsequent Euclidean residual levels preserve distinctions.
CHANGE: Compute a train-only transition-neighborhood vector per item and blend its cosine dissimilarity with Poincare geodesic distance inside an entropic Sinkhorn assignment to 32 first-level anchors with equal item-mass capacity. The behavior term changes the cost whose transport plan produces T1 assignments; geometry-off substitutes Euclidean distance in that same matrix.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i let b_i be its normalized, popularity-adjusted vector of incoming predecessor IDs from train transitions; reduce dimension with a fixed train-only signed hash into 128 bins and L2 normalize. Let x_i,a_k be Poincare points at curvature -c, c=1. Define C_ik=d_c(x_i,a_k)^2 + lambda(1-cos(b_i,mu_k)), where mu_k is a trainable normalized behavior centroid updated from transport assignments (zero vector if empty). Per minibatch solve 10 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations with row marginals 1/B and column marginals 1/32; logits -C/tau, tau=0.1. Use the soft plan for straight-through T1 training and argmax(plan) for export; residual quantize at L2/L3, then deterministic T4. Capacity budget: T1 exactly 32 effective groups (≤3.47 nats, near equal mass); T2/T3 target 8–10 nats combined; T4 <0.1 nats. Cost at frozen scale: 24 steps/epoch, each batch B items and 32 codes, cost matrix O(24*B*32) distance and cosine entries per epoch (for full item coverage ≈18.9M entries/epoch, 56.7B over 3000 epochs); 10 Sinkhorn updates O(10*24*B*32), small vs encoder and generally 5–15% wall time. Store 128-d hashed b_i (about 12.6MB float32 for 24,556 items); no full dense pair matrix.
COST: One train transition pass O(E_train), cached 24,556x128 behavior vectors (~12.6 MB). Each of 24 steps adds Bx32 costs and 10 Sinkhorn row/column normalizations; approximately 18.9M pair costs and 189M normalization entries per epoch if batches cover the dataset, 3000 epochs, expected 5–15% Stage2 overhead; Stage3 unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: The capacity constraint keeps beam-facing first-level branching small, while behavior-compatible items compete for the same cells under genuine geodesic distance; the residual levels can then encode distinctions the prefix does not need to carry. Keep only if L1 ER is non-degenerate, depth-1 survival rises and depth-2/3 survival is retained or rises, T4 NLL stays near constant and test_R@10 >0.065.
RISKS: Equal capacities may conflict with the true train item distribution and force unrelated items together; hashed behavior vectors can collide; Sinkhorn softness may make train assignments mismatch hard export argmax; balancing can improve utilization without improving next-item prediction. Curvature may be too weak to change ranking.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted incoming predecessor neighborhood for each target item, derived from directed train transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From train.parquet only, count within-user adjacent transitions j→i. Set a_ji=n_ji/(sqrt((pop_train(j)+1)(pop_train(i)+1))); accumulate a_ji into a fixed-seed signed-hash vector b_i in 128 bins, L2 normalize. No validation/test transitions or popularity are used. Learn per-cell behavior centroids mu_k from the Sinkhorn-weighted minibatch assignments, with EMA decay 0.9, as Stage2 parameters/statistics.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: All interaction edges and popularity values are computed from train.parquet alone; behavior vectors are frozen before RQ-VAE fitting. Centroids are learned only from those frozen vectors and Stage2 train item batches. Export/freeze all SIDs before any validation or test evaluation; those interactions cannot affect the transport cost or assignment.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Poincare geodesic distance d_c^2 at fixed negative curvature -c, c=1, used directly in the Sinkhorn cost matrix; points are projected to the open ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First-level Stage2 assignment: the curved distance is one component of the cost matrix whose entropy-regularized transport plan assigns items to 32 prefix cells. T2/T3 use the baseline residual quantizer; T4 remains deterministic collision extension.
COUPLING PATH: For every item-code pair, the train-derived behavior neighborhood dissimilarity and Poincare geodesic distance are summed into one transport cost; that cost determines the Sinkhorn plan and hence the T1 token, so neither term is an auxiliary disconnected loss.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ik=d_c(x_i,a_k)^2 + lambda[1-cos(b_i,mu_k)]; P=Sinkhorn(exp(-C/tau), row=1/B, col=1/32), with b_i the behavior term and d_c the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives; gold-prefix survival depths 1–4; T4 NLL; Stage3 frozen-protocol test_R@10. Predict depth-1 survival higher than unconstrained branching, depth-2/3 at least maintained or improved, T4 NLL approximately unchanged, and test_R@10 >0.065. Capacity target: T1 ≤3.47 nats / 32 groups; T2/T3 jointly carry 8–10 nats; T4 <0.1 nats. L1 Hard ER should be nonzero and informative rather than forced to 1.0 by a single shared prefix.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 and remove behavior-centroid updates while retaining 32-way capacity-constrained Sinkhorn, Poincare distance, identical marginals and training schedule.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace d_c(x_i,a_k)^2 with ||x_i-a_k||_2^2 in the same cost matrix, keeping behavior dissimilarity, Sinkhorn temperature, marginals, 32 groups and optimizer fixed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use C_ik=d_c(x_i,a_k)^2+lambda[1-cos(b_i,mu_k)] with train-only b_i and 32-way balanced Sinkhorn assignments; preserve residual quantization and collision extension.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Normalize K_ik=-C_ik/tau. Initialize logP=K; repeat 10 times: logP += log(row_marginal)-logsumexp(logP,dim=1); logP += log(col_marginal)-logsumexp(logP,dim=0). P=exp(logP); train with straight-through hard argmax onehot(P) plus soft-plan gradients and baseline reconstruction/commitment terms. Use row marginal 1/B, column 1/32; export hard argmax from the same frozen cost and then quantize residuals. The behavior-off and geometry-off variants alter only their named term.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are train-only edge counts/popularity, fixed-hash behavior vectors b_i, current item latents, 32 Poincare anchors, behavior centroids and minibatch size. Output is a soft transport plan, hard T1 assignments, updated Stage2 parameters/centroids, and four-token SIDs after unchanged T2/T3 and deterministic T4.
SAFEGUARDS: Signed hash uses a committed seed; normalize vectors with eps and set zero-neighborhood vectors to zero; cap lambda and anneal from 0 to its fixed value over first 100 epochs; log-domain Sinkhorn with cost centering, finite checks and 10 iterations; clip/project Poincare points to (1-eps)/sqrt(c); float32 distance and plan arithmetic; clip gradients; use EMA centroid updates only from train batches. If hard export occupancy deviates materially from 32-cell capacity, report it rather than silently changing assignments.
END IDEA
