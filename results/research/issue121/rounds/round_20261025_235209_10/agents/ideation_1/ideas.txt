IDEA: successor-geodesic-coarse
TITLE: Successor-geodesic coarse partition with a small first-level alphabet
MOTIVATION: A target that follows an anchor in training histories should remain reachable through an early beam prefix, while the first level must avoid scattering likely successors across a large alphabet. Make the coarse split follow directed successor neighborhoods, reserving T2/T3 for item discrimination and T4 for collision extension.
NOVELTY: Unlike adding a transition bias to an otherwise unchanged Euclidean quantizer or changing codebook size alone, trainable first-level prototypes are positioned by a directed, popularity-corrected successor objective in the Poincare ball, and assignment uses that same curved objective. Use K1=32 (not a four-level repartition); keep total SID width and remaining-level codebook sizes fixed.
CHANGE: Precompute a compact successor-neighbor list from training events. Optimize 32 Poincare prototypes and assign each item to the prototype minimizing its embedding distance plus a neighbor-consensus geodesic term; residual quantization at T2/T3 supplies the remaining semantic detail.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i, make S_i the top 16 successors by count c_ij/sqrt((c_i+1)(c_j+1)), over adjacent events of each training user only. Let x_i=exp^c_0(W e_i), and prototypes p_k lie in the curvature -c ball. For a minibatch, soft assignment a_ik=softmax_k(-[d_c(x_i,p_k)^2 + lambda*sum_{j in S_i} q_ij d_c(p_k,x_j)^2]/tau), with q normalized popularity-corrected weights. Train via soft-to-hard codebook/commitment loss and straight-through hard argmin; export hard k as T1. Re-encode residuals and use the fixed baseline Euclidean RQ levels at T2/T3; force T4 to the deterministic collision token. Clip gradients and retain the baseline reconstruction objective.
COST: One-time graph construction O(E log 16) time and O(24556*16) storage. At frozen scale each step has B≈1023 items, 32 prototypes, 16 neighbors: ~0.52M pairwise curved-distance evaluations, versus ~32.8k item-prototype distances (about 16x first-level distance work); batched vectorization keeps this modest. 24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 updates; no extra updates, estimated step wall time +5–15% overall (measure). Four-card DDP retains the same 24 steps/epoch.
HYPOTHESIS: Corrected successor neighborhoods have locally coherent coarse destinations, so both-on improves depth-1/2 gold-prefix survival and width-20 inclusion enough to lift test_R@10 above 0.065. Keep only if the change appears on canonical train/valid pair ranking and frozen Stage3 evaluation, not merely reconstruction loss.
RISKS: High branching or sparse histories make successor consensus noisy; geometric prototypes can collapse or overfit frequent hubs; the latent embedding may not align with item transitions. lambda/tau require a predeclared train/valid-only choice, not test tuning.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed adjacent-item successor transitions, popularity-corrected.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Sort each training split's events by user and timestamp when available; count adjacent (source,target) pairs; retain top 16 per source with weight c_ij/sqrt((c_i+1)(c_j+1)), then normalize. The split is Stage2 training parquet only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build the table before Stage2 optimization using only Stage2 training events; do not load/merge validation or test histories. Item embeddings and IDs are frozen as in baseline; unseen-to-training items receive an empty S_i. SID export applies the learned encoder/prototypes only, with no interaction-dependent lookup.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp map and squared distance d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c)*atanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x)⊕_c y||), curvature -c with c=1 fixed initially.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First-level training assignment score and its codebook/commitment gradients; only T1 uses this operator, preserving fixed T2/T3 RQ and deterministic T4.
COUPLING PATH: Each source's training successor distribution weights the prototype-to-successor geodesic term inside the same per-item T1 assignment cost that also measures the item-to-prototype geodesic; altering either q or d_c changes T1 assignments and gradients.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ik=d_c(x_i,p_k)^2 + lambda*sum_{j in S_i}q_ij*d_c(p_k,x_j)^2; a_ik=softmax_k(-C_ik/tau), k_i=argmin_k C_ik.
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on the canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives; width-20 gold-prefix survival at depths 1,2,3,4; teacher-forced T4 NLL; and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Predicted both-on vs behavior-off and geometry-off: L1 ER should rise modestly above its 1-code trivial baseline if 32 codes are occupied (but remain interpreted with the fixed alphabet); depth-1 survival may be lower than a single-code arm, while depth-2/3 survival and complete-path survival should rise; T4 NLL should stay near baseline/low and should not carry the gain; target test_R@10 >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 while keeping K1=32, curvature, initialization, optimizer, losses, and all other levels unchanged; this is curved item-only T1 assignment.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace exp/log and d_c everywhere in C_ik with identity and squared Euclidean distance, preserving the same q_ij, lambda, K1, data, and optimization schedule.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only q_ij and the Poincare curved C_ik above; evaluate with frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: q_ij=normalize_j[c_ij/sqrt((c_i+1)(c_j+1))]; x_i=exp^c_0(W e_i); C_ik=d_c(x_i,p_k)^2+lambda*sum_j q_ij*d_c(p_k,x_j)^2; k_i=argmin_k C_ik. Use softmax(-C/tau) for gradients and straight-through hard assignment; optimize baseline RQ-VAE reconstruction + commitment/codebook losses. Export [k_i,T2_i,T3_i,T4_collision_i].
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen Stage1 embedding e_i; train-only sparse successor lists/weights; 32 trainable prototypes; baseline encoder/decoder and fixed RQ levels. Output per item: four integer SID tokens, with T1=k_i and T4 determined only by collision resolution.
SAFEGUARDS: Project ball vectors to norm <=(1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp atanh argument to 1-1e-6; compute distances/softmax in FP32; clamp tau>=0.03 and gradient norm; add epsilon to counts and normalizers; empty neighbor list means zero neighbor term; deterministic tie-breaking; monitor prototype occupancy and NaN/Inf; collision token must not depend on validation/test.
END IDEA

IDEA: branching-tangent-residual
TITLE: Successor-branch tangent residuals quantized by a hyperbolic fine partition
MOTIVATION: An item's outgoing training transitions can branch in multiple directions, so a single coarse representation may conceal which successor family is relevant. Keep a compact T1 anchor partition, then make T2 encode the behavior-conditioned residual direction; T3 resolves item detail and T4 remains the collision token.
NOVELTY: This couples a training-only branching signal to the residual vector before fine quantization, rather than adding an independent transition loss or using transition-derived radii to bias the first-level code assignment. The curved residual is itself the quantizer input at T2; turning off either the branching transform or curvature changes the residual assignments.
CHANGE: For each item, estimate two to four successor-family tangent directions from its training-only outgoing edges. Subtract a bounded mixture of those directions from the Euclidean RQ residual in the tangent space, map the result into the Poincare ball, and assign T2 by geodesic nearest-code scoring; subsequent levels quantize the inverse-mapped remainder with the unchanged baseline rule.
SPECIFICATION: For source i, cluster its outgoing successor embeddings into at most M=4 weighted spherical/tangent means u_im (if fewer than 2 edges, use one zero direction); weights pi_im are normalized popularity-corrected edge counts. At T2 residual r_i=z_i-Q1(z_i), obtain branch probabilities alpha_im=softmax_m(-d_c(exp_0(r_i), exp_0(u_im))^2/t_b). Define t_i=sum_m alpha_im u_im and r'_i=r_i-beta*tanh(||r_i||/s)*t_i. Quantize y_i=exp^c_0(r'_i) against a 32-entry Poincare T2 codebook using d_c^2. Decode selected code c2_i via log^c_0 and continue ordinary T3 on r'_i-log^c_0(c2_i); T4 stays collision-only. Train alpha and code assignment through straight-through soft nearest-code weights and the baseline reconstruction/commitment losses.
COST: Precompute outgoing edge directions and up to four weighted means once, O(E*D) plus O(24556*4*D) memory/compute depending on stored dtype. At each frozen-scale step B≈1023, M=4, K2=32: ~131k branch comparisons plus ~32.8k curved code distances (plus exp/log maps), roughly +5–10% Stage2 step time; 72,000 updates total (24×3000), no schedule extension. Four-card setup unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: Multi-branch sources have distinct successor clusters that align with useful residual directions; removing predictable branch components from the fine residual makes T2 prefixes more likely to match observed target paths without pushing rare distinctions into T4. Evidence: better depth-2/3 gold-prefix survival and test_R@10 >0.065, with T4 NLL essentially unchanged.
RISKS: Successor clusters may be too sparse or not semantically aligned; excessive beta erases useful content; branch probability depends on embedding geometry and may collapse to one mode. The method may hurt items with high transition entropy or cold-start items.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-source multi-modal directed successor branch distribution from adjacent user events.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: On Stage2 training events only, sort by user/time, count directed adjacent transitions, popularity-correct each edge by c_ij/sqrt((c_i+1)(c_j+1)), and cluster each source's successor Stage1 vectors into up to four weighted means. No validation/test transition is used.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Branch means and mixture weights are constructed exclusively from the Stage2 training event frame and frozen before SID export. Validation/test interaction rows are excluded from graph construction, clustering, hyperparameter selection, and codebook assignment; unknown sources get t_i=0.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp/log at origin and geodesic squared distance, with fixed negative curvature -c (c=1); curved nearest-code T2 assignment.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The behavior-conditioned T2 residual is mapped to the ball and scored against the T2 codebook; this is inside RQ-VAE quantization, not a detached regularizer.
COUPLING PATH: Training successor cluster directions determine t_i, which changes the residual r'_i that is mapped into the curved space; the Poincare distance to T2 code vectors then determines the hard token and gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: r'_i=r_i-beta*tanh(||r_i||/s)*sum_m alpha_im u_im; y_i=exp^c_0(r'_i); k2_i=argmin_k d_c(y_i,c2_k)^2, where alpha_im is computed from training branch m using d_c(exp_0(r_i),exp_0(u_im))^2.
OBSERVABLES: Record canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER with ten fixed negatives; width-20 target gold-prefix survival for depths 1-4; teacher-forced T4 NLL; and Stage3 test_R@10. Expected L1 ER roughly unchanged because T1 is held fixed; depth-1 unchanged, depth-2/3 and full SID survival improve; T4 NLL remains at baseline (not the source of gains); Stage3 test_R@10 should exceed 0.065. Compare all metrics across all three arms.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set beta=0 and remove the successor-derived t_i while keeping curved T2 code assignment, codebooks, and training protocol fixed; the T2 input returns to the ordinary residual.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the same behavior-conditioned r'_i but replace exp/log maps and T2 geodesic scoring with identity and squared Euclidean distance; preserve codebook dimensions and training schedule.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the successor-cluster tangent mixture, behavior-conditioned residual, and curved T2 assignment as specified.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Let r_i=z_i-Q1(z_i); alpha_im=softmax_m(-d_c(exp^c_0(r_i),exp^c_0(u_im))^2/t_b); t_i=sum_m alpha_im*u_im; r'_i=r_i-beta*tanh(||r_i||/s)t_i; y_i=exp^c_0(r'_i); k2_i=argmin_k d_c(y_i,c2_k)^2. Straight-through soft assignments supply gradients; T3 uses log^c_0(c2_k) to form the residual; T4 is the deterministic collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoded item z_i, first-level reconstruction Q1(z_i), training-derived branch means u_im and weights, Poincare T2 codebook. Outputs: behavior-adjusted T2 token, ordinary T3 token, collision-only T4 token, and the fixed T1 token.
SAFEGUARDS: Normalize/clip branch means to bounded tangent norm; cap beta in [0,0.5], clamp branch softmax temperature, and set zero direction for absent/one-edge sources. Project all ball points inside radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), clamp inverse-hyperbolic arguments, use FP32 for distance/log/exp, protect norms with epsilon, clip gradients, deterministic ties. Never train a learned behavior table from validation/test edges.
END IDEA
