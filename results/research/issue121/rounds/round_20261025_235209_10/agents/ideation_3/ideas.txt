IDEA: train-successor-geodesic-coarse
TITLE: Successor-geodesic attraction for a small first-level alphabet
MOTIVATION: Width-20 beam survival is helped when a training-observed next item shares a rankable coarse prefix with its anchor, while an oversized first level can discard the target immediately.
NOVELTY: Unlike a transition residual or a branch-entropy radius, this uses directed successor edges to directly deform the first-level assignment cost through pairwise hyperbolic geodesic distance. The alphabet is explicitly capped at 32; T2/T3 retain the fine distinctions and T4 remains collision extension only.
CHANGE: Add a train-only, popularity-corrected directed successor attraction to the first-level curved assignment objective. Quantize to K=32 first-level prototypes on the Poincare ball, then perform the existing residual quantization for T2/T3 and deterministic T4 extension.
SPECIFICATION: Let E_i be frozen Stage1 vectors mapped by exp_0 to ball points x_i, and let P_ij be normalized train-only successor counts after subtracting a small popularity expectation. Optimize first-level prototype points c_k and assignment logits a_ik with L = L_RQ + lambda sum_ij P_ij sum_k a_ik a_jk d_c(x_i,x_j)^2 + tau sum_ik a_ik d_c(x_i,c_k)^2. Use hard argmin at inference; train a_ik with temperature-soft assignments. The edge term rewards behaviorally linked items whose curved representations are near, and hence tends to put linked items in common coarse cells.
COST: Store at most the observed sparse training edges; once per minibatch, edge penalty costs O(B_e K) for sampled edges with endpoint assignment vectors, plus O(BK) curved point-to-prototype distances. At frozen scale (24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs), roughly 72,000 optimizer steps; sample at most 4 edges per item per step (up to 98,224 endpoint-edge evaluations/step) and expect about 1.2–1.5x baseline Stage2 wall-clock, no added Stage3 cost.
HYPOTHESIS: If training successors are useful coarse-prefix neighbors and the curved metric places their prototypes more faithfully than Euclidean distance, the full arm will raise depth-1/2 gold-prefix survival and Stage3 test_R@10 above 0.065. Measure canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER with ten fixed negatives, width-20 gold-prefix survival at depths 1–4, teacher-forced T4 NLL, and test_R@10; desired pattern is improved early survival without increased T4 NLL serving as the source of gains.
RISKS: Excessive edge weight can collapse popular successor hubs into one code, hurting discrimination; popularity correction and assignment-entropy monitoring are needed. The sparse train graph may not cover validation/test transitions.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transition counts P_ij, popularity-adjusted and row-normalized.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From adjacent item pairs in training histories only, count i->j; subtract expected mass proportional to train out-count(i)*in-count(j)/total, clamp at zero, and normalize each nonempty source row. Never use valid/test pairs.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: E_i are the frozen Stage1 item vectors; all graph counts and edge samples are computed from the Stage2 training split only. Validation is used only for the frozen prescribed checkpoint selection/evaluation protocol, and test interactions never enter fitting, assignment, or hyperparameter selection.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Poincare geodesic distance d_c(x,y)^2 at fixed negative curvature -c, c=1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Both the item-to-first-level-prototype assignment cost and the train-successor attraction term during Stage2 training; first-level hard assignment at SID extraction uses the same curved distance.
COUPLING PATH: The train-only successor edge weights connect the two endpoint assignment distributions; their edge penalty is computed from Poincare-geodesic item distances and gradients update the shared item encoder/prototypes, changing the same first-level partition used to emit T1.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_edge = lambda sum_(i,j in E_train) P_ij sum_(k=1..32) a_ik a_jk d_c(x_i,x_j)^2, with a_ik = softmax_k(-d_c(x_i,c_k)^2 / temperature).
OBSERVABLES: Compare L1 Hard ER, depth 1–4 gold-prefix survival, T4 teacher-forced NLL, and Stage3 test_R@10. Predict more shared T1 among positive successor pairs, higher depth-1/2 survival, approximately unchanged or lower T4 NLL, and higher Recall@10; report all metrics even if ER falls because ER is not the admission criterion.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set P_ij=0 while retaining identical curved assignment, K=32, and optimization schedule; prediction: fewer successor positives share T1 and lower depth-1/2 survival than both-on.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace every d_c(x,y) with Euclidean norm while retaining the same P_ij, graph samples, K, and schedule; prediction: changed T1 partition and weaker early survival/Recall than both-on if negative-curvature neighborhoods fit branching structure.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Train with P_ij and Poincare distances; compare against both matched ablations on identical data split, fixed negative set, frozen beam-20 Stage3 protocol, and no test-time tuning.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Normalize embeddings u_i=E_i/||E_i|| with bounded norm, x_i=exp_0^c(r u_i), r<1/sqrt(c). For sparse train edges sample (i,j) proportional to P_ij. a_i=softmax(-d_c(x_i,c_k)^2/t); update encoder and 32 prototypes by L_RQ + lambda*mean_edges[P_ij sum_k a_ik a_jk d_c(x_i,x_j)^2] + tau*mean_items sum_k a_ik d_c(x_i,c_k)^2. At extraction assign T1=argmin_k d_c(x_i,c_k); quantize residuals into T2/T3 with baseline method; produce T4 only via deterministic collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen Stage1 vectors, training-only ordered histories, K=32, c=1, lambda/tau/temperature fixed from training-side pilot only. Outputs: first-level prototypes, modified item representations/assignments, four-token SIDs and standard Stage2 diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp ball norms to (1-epsilon)/sqrt(c), use stable atanh/log1p distance evaluation with epsilon floor, clamp squared distances and logits, normalize edge weights and cap per-source sampling, use gradient clipping, and monitor active-code count and assignment entropy to detect collapse. Keep lambda fixed across ablations.
END IDEA

IDEA: branch-radial-lorentz-split
TITLE: User-branch radial hierarchy in Lorentz space
MOTIVATION: Items that lead into several distinct next-item branches need a coarse code that preserves the shared interest trunk, while branch-specific discrimination can be deferred to T2/T3 where beam ranking can exploit it.
NOVELTY: Instead of making branching entropy a radius/gate on an otherwise unchanged quantizer, this creates a Lorentz-space hierarchical split: train-only user-conditioned branch prototypes define a behavior-dependent tangent direction, and the first-level assignment is the hyperbolic projection onto a small shared trunk alphabet. It is distinct from transition residual optimization because the behavior signal controls the coarse hierarchy itself.
CHANGE: Compute per-item successor-branch distributions from training users, pool their branch prototypes in the Lorentz tangent space, and use a branch-conditioned Lorentz geodesic assignment to K=16 coarse anchors. Use the branch-conditioned residual only to initialize/refine T2/T3; T4 remains deterministic collision extension.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i, form q_i over successor categories (successor item clusters from a fixed training-only partition) using smoothed user-history successor counts; define a behavior tangent b_i = sum_m q_im log_o^L(p_m), where p_m are trainable Lorentz branch prototypes. Map Stage1 vector z_i to Lorentz point x_i. Score coarse prototype k as s_ik = d_L(x_i,c_k)^2 + alpha d_L(exp_{x_i}(eta b_i),c_k)^2; assign T1 by argmin. The second term shifts the item along its behavior tangent before curved assignment, coupling behavior and geometry directly.
COST: Precompute sparse q_i and b_i once; training adds one tangent aggregation per item and two Lorentz distances to each of 16 prototypes. At frozen scale this is about 2x the first-level distance arithmetic, O(24,556*16) score terms per full item pass (sampling minibatches keeps per-step cost O(B*16)); estimate 1.15–1.4x baseline wall-clock over 72,000 steps, negligible storage for sparse behavior vectors/prototypes.
HYPOTHESIS: Items with compatible successor branches will share a coarse trunk code more often, while branch-specific evidence survives in T2/T3; this should improve beam coverage at depths 1–3 and test_R@10 > 0.065 without making T4 NLL the gain mechanism. Observe L1 Hard ER on 52,855 canonical pairs/ten negatives, depth-wise width-20 gold-prefix survival, T4 teacher-forced NLL, and Stage3 test_R@10.
RISKS: The successor-cluster vocabulary can be noisy, and tangent averaging can cancel multimodal interests; smoothing, per-user normalization, and norm caps reduce but do not eliminate this. The branch displacement may overfit frequent users or starve rare coarse codes.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: User-conditioned distribution over an item's next-item branch categories.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training split sequences only, assign each successor item to one of M fixed clusters learned from Stage1 embeddings on training items; for source item i count successor-cluster events separately per user, normalize each user's counts, average over users, add Dirichlet smoothing, and normalize to q_i. No validation or test sequences contribute.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The branch partition, q_i, and all learned p_m are built only from Stage1 vectors and training histories. No valid/test transition is read by SID construction; validation/test are reserved for the unchanged evaluation protocol.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz-model log/exp maps and Lorentz geodesic distance at fixed curvature -1; exp_{x_i}(eta b_i) is the curved behavior displacement.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage2 first-level score construction and assignment, before residual quantization; the same Lorentz score is used in SID extraction.
COUPLING PATH: q_i forms a branch tangent through Lorentz log maps; exp at the item's Lorentz point creates a behavior-shifted point, whose Lorentz distance to each coarse code determines T1. Turning behavior off removes the displacement, and replacing the Lorentz maps/distance with Euclidean operations changes this assignment computation.
COUPLING EQUATION: score_ik = d_L(x_i,c_k)^2 + alpha*d_L(exp^L_{x_i}(eta*sum_m q_im log^L_o(p_m)),c_k)^2.
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER (may decrease as small K intentionally merges more pairs), canonical-pair gold-prefix survival at depths 1–4 under beam 20, T4 NLL and Stage3 test_R@10. Predict T1 survival increase, T2/T3 survival improved or maintained, T4 NLL stable, and full-arm Recall exceeding both ablations if branch structure generalizes.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Use q_i=0 and hence zero branch displacement; preserve Lorentz assignment, K=16, and all other settings. Expected lower early prefix survival than both-on.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Lorentz log/exp/geodesic operations with Euclidean vector addition and Euclidean distances, retaining the same q_i and branch prototypes; expected altered coarse groups and lower beam survival/Recall than both-on.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Enable train-only q_i displacement and Lorentz geometry together; run the fixed 3000-epoch Stage2 and frozen Stage3 protocol with no early stop or test tuning.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Let q_i in simplex(M), p_m on Lorentz hyperboloid H^d_{-1}, and b_i=sum_m q_im log^L_o(p_m). Let x_i=map_L(z_i) and y_i=exp^L_{x_i}(eta b_i). Set T1(i)=argmin_{k<=16}{d_L(x_i,c_k)^2+alpha*d_L(y_i,c_k)^2}; optimize this score with softmax temperature assignments plus baseline reconstruction/commitment losses. Feed the residual after T1 to ordinary T2/T3 quantizers; preserve deterministic T4 collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen Stage1 vectors, training-only user sequences, M training-only successor clusters, K=16, curvature -1, alpha/eta. Outputs: Lorentz branch prototypes and coarse prototypes, four-token item SIDs, and evaluation diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: Project all Lorentz points back to the hyperboloid after updates; enforce positive time coordinate, clamp Minkowski inner products to at most -1-epsilon before acosh, cap tangent norms and eta, use float32/64 stable acosh, normalize per-user counts and smooth q_i, clip gradients, and monitor code occupancy. Fix alpha/eta across matched ablations.
END IDEA
