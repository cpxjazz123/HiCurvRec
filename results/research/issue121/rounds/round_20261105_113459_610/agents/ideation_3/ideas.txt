IDEA: LORENTZ-SUCC-MID-RQ
TITLE: Curvature that only chooses the shallow SID digit: a Lorentz geodesic midpoint between the item and its popularity-adjusted successor prototype selects level-0, while the residual handoff and levels>=2 stay exactly Euclidean baseline.
MOTIVATION: All three measured arms failed for the same structural reason: curvature sat on the reconstruction/deep residual path, so re-partitioning level 0 deformed the residuals that levels 1..L quantize and deep code usage collapsed (l3_collision 0.549 / 0.943 / 0.960; level2_distinct_prefixes 4236 / 784 / 720). Yet the shallow digit is exactly where the recommender needs separation (level-1 prefix predictability, hit rate, valid_l1_hard_er). The open problem is to let curvature buy shallow successor alignment without touching the geometry the deep digits see.
NOVELTY: (a) The curved operator is SELECTOR-ONLY: the digit is chosen in the hyperboloid, but the vector subtracted to build the residual is the unchanged baseline Euclidean codeword, so the deep stack sees a permutation of the same codebook, not a warped space. (b) The behaviour coupling is not a per-codeword curvature field (OCCURV), not a demand radius (GEOCAP), not successor-CODE centroids with Sinkhorn (strataot): it is the Lorentz geodesic MIDPOINT between the item lift and the lift of its PMI-weighted successor prototype, matched to the lifted anchors. No Sinkhorn, no balancing, no per-layer curvature, no cross-map transport, no tuned constant (R measured, temperature = R, midpoint weights equal).
CHANGE: Level-0 argmin_k ||z - e_k|| becomes argmax_k of a straight-through softmax over Lorentz distances d_L(m_i, h(e_k^0)), with m_i the Lorentz geodesic midpoint of h(z_i) and h(b_i), b_i the popularity-adjusted training-successor prototype; the chosen digit is quantized with the unchanged Euclidean e_{k*}^0 and levels 1..L keep the unmodified Euclidean argmin residual recursion.
SPECIFICATION:
Derived constants only: R = sqrt(mean ||f(x_i)||^2) over a fixed 4096-item training block at initialization, frozen (curvature -1/R^2); tau = R; successor cap 50 per item (cost bound, documented default); running-mean table with step 1/count (no free eta). Level 0 only. z_i = f(x_i); b_i = sum_j w_ij E_j / sum_j w_ij (b_i = z_i if the row is empty); h(v) = (sqrt(R^2+||v||^2), v); m_i = R (h(z_i)+h(b_i)) / ||h(z_i)+h(b_i)||_L with ||s||_L = sqrt(max(-<s,s>_L, 1e-12)); d_ik = R arccosh(clamp((m_i0 a_k0 - m_i.a_k)/R^2, 1+1e-7, 1e7)) with a_k = h(e_k^0); p = softmax(-d/tau); k* = argmax p; q = onehot(k*) + p - stopgrad(p); zhat_0 = sum_k q_k e_k^0 (forward value e_{k*}^0); r_1 = z - zhat_0, then baseline. Loss unchanged, no extra term.
COST: 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72,000 steps on four cards. Extra per step: one (B x K0) Lorentz distance table (baseline already pays one such table at level 0), O(B d) for two lifts, one Minkowski sum and one arccosh, a gather over <=50 successors per item, one running-mean update. No new parameters; +1.6 MB (d=16) to 6.3 MB (d=64) table and 2.5 MB weights; expected +3 to +5 percent step time, under +10 MB memory; schedule and Stage 3 protocol untouched.
HYPOTHESIS: Midpointing the selector toward the successor prototype groups items with shared successor mass into the same first digit, so the true next item more often shares the level-1 prefix (valid_l1_hit >= 0.12, valid_l1_hard_er <= 4), while the untouched Euclidean reco

nstruction code and deep recursion keep l3_collision <= 0.35 and level2_distinct_prefixes of the order of the baseline 7414; the case is made by one run on Stage3 test_recall@10 > 0.065 plus the recorded curved-vs-Euclidean selector disagreement rate.
RISKS: Level-0 concentration narrowing the level-1 residual distribution (distinct prefixes could still fall); hub domination of the prototype despite PMI adjustment; a fixed temperature selecting codes the deeper levels cannot accommodate; R measured on an untrained encoder drifting out of scale; a stale table early in training weakening the pull for rare items. All are visible in the layered diagnostics of the single run; none is fixable by a sweep.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item-transition weights from the training split, condensed to one successor prototype vector per item (training-only popularity-adjusted graph-neighbour signal).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From consecutive (u_t -> u_{t+1}) pairs of the training split only, count n_ij; with m_j = sum_i n_ij, N = sum n_ij, w_ij = max(0, log2((n_ij/row_i)/(m_j/N))), keep the 50 largest per row, row-normalise. Maintain E in R^{N x d} as the running mean (step 1/count, detached) of each training item's encoder output; b_i = sum_j w_ij E_j, b_i = z_i when the row is empty.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Counts, PMI weights, the successor cap, the E table and R come only from the training split (R from a fixed training block at initialization). SIDs are frozen after Stage 2 and Stage 3 only consumes them, so no validation or test interaction enters the SID construction; evaluation uses the frozen Stage 3 protocol and candidate set.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz/hyperboloid model with fixed negative curvature -1/R^2: lift h(v) = (sqrt(R^2+||v||^2), v), Minkowski geodesic midpoint m = R (h(u)+h(v))/||h(u)+h(v)||_L, Lorentz distance d_L = R arccosh(-<m,h(e_k)>_L/R^2), and a straight-through softmax over those distances with temperature tau = R.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In quantization, level-0 assignment only; it decides the stored first digit and carries the straight-through gradient to the level-0 codebook and encoder, and never enters the residual recursion, distances or assignment of levels >= 1.
COUPLING PATH: training transitions -> PMI weights w_ij -> successor prototype b_i via E -> Lorentz midpoint m_i = mid_L(h(z_i), h(b_i)) -> Lorentz distances to h(e_k^0) -> softmax/argmax -> digit k*. behaviour_off sets b_i := z_i (pure hyperbolic nearest-anchor); geometry_off makes the midpoint arithmetic and the distance Euclidean (behaviour pull without curvature). Both switches move the same argmax, so it is one computation, not two losses.
COUPLING EQUATION: score_k(i) = -d_L( mid_L( h(z_i), h( sum_j w_ij E_j / sum_j w_ij ) ), h(e_k^0) ), k* = argmax_k score_k, with reconstruction by e_{k*}^0 and levels >= 1 Euclidean.
OBSERVABLES: valid_l1_hit (>= 0.12), valid_l1_hard_er (<= 4), l3_collision (<= 0.35), level2_distinct_prefixes (~7414), level-0 usage entropy, curved-vs-Euclidean selector disagreement rate, longest matching SID prefix with the ground-truth next item, Stage3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: behaviour_off=True: b_i := z_i, so m_i = h(z_i) and the selector is pure hyperbolic nearest-anchor against the lifted baseline codebook; curvature, Lorentz distance, straight-through path and the Euclidean deep stack unchanged.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: geometry_off=True: m_i := (z_i + b_i)/2, d_ik := ||m_i - e_k^0||_2, plain softmax at the

same scale; the behaviour route is unchanged, only curvature is removed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: behaviour_off=False, geometry_off=False: Lorentz midpoint of item and successor-prototype lifts, Lorentz distance to lifted anchors, straight-through argmax, Euclidean reconstruction code, baseline deep stack. Both switches off reproduces the baseline level-0 argmin exactly and is the reference point of the single run.
SPEC EQUATIONS:
1) R = sqrt(mean_{4096-item train block} ||f(x_i)||^2) once at init; curvature -1/R^2; tau = R.
2) w_ij = max(0, log2((n_ij/row_i)/(m_j/N))), top 50 per row, row-normalise.
3) E_j <- E_j + (z_j - E_j)/count_j, detached.
4) b_i = sum_j w_ij E_j, else z_i when row_i = 0.
5) h(v) = concat(sqrt(R^2+||v||^2), v).
6) s_i = h(z_i)+h(b_i); m_i = R s_i / sqrt(max(-<s_i,s_i>_L, 1e-12)).
7) arg_ik = clamp((m_i0 a_k0 - m_i.a_k)/R^2, 1+1e-7, 1e7); d_ik = R arccosh(arg_ik).
8) p_ik = softmax_k(-d_ik/tau); k*_i = argmax_k p_ik; q_ik = p_ik + onehot(k*_i) - p_ik.detach().
9) zhat_0,i = sum_k q_ik e_k^0 (forward = e_{k*}^0); r_1,i = z_i - zhat_0,i; levels 1..L baseline, Euclidean, no Sinkhorn.
10) Loss = baseline loss unchanged; W, E, R stop-gradient.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: In: z (B x d), baseline level-0 Euclidean codebook e^0 (K0 x d), sparse PMI successor matrix W (N x N, 50 nnz/row), running-mean table E (N x d), frozen radius R. Out: level-0 digit k* per item (first SID token), straight-through soft assignment q, Euclidean quantization zhat_0 = e_{k*}^0 feeding the unchanged deeper RQ stack; side outputs b_i and d_ik for diagnostics.
SAFEGUARDS: Minkowski norm floored at 1e-12 before dividing (b_i = z_i makes identical lifts the common case; must not produce NaN); arccosh argument clamped to [1+1e-7, 1e7]; logits max-shifted before exp; tau = R fixed so logits are dimensionless and no sweep is needed; zero successor rows fall back to b_i = z_i; E initialised at zero and written only for training items; R stored in the checkpoint so Stage 2 and any later encode share the same curvature; negative PMI floors at zero and rows without a positive successor are skipped; the curved selector governs only the stored digit and the straight-through gradient while the reconstruction code is always the Euclidean e_k^0, so assignment and reconstruction can never disagree on the codeword position.
END IDEA
