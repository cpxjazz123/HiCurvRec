IDEA: HORO-FLOW-SID
TITLE: Behaviour-flow horocyclic level-1 quantizer with a learned Poincare boundary anchor
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE Semantic IDs spend their first token on a Euclidean k-means cell that is blind to how users actually move between items, so the generative model's first prefix decision is only weakly predictable from session context. We make the first SID level a coordinate along the dominant directed user-transition flow, measured on horospheres of the Poincare ball, so that the coarsest token is the macro direction in which real sessions travel.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds used successor-set rank matching, tangent-space transport, fork angles, cones, radial shells, per-branch curvature and prefix reweighting, all of which re-score or transport existing cell geometry. This idea does not touch pair geometry at all: it replaces the level-1 partition itself with a horocyclic foliation (level sets of a Busemann function) whose boundary anchor is fitted to a training-only behaviour flow coordinate. The curved object is a horosphere, the behaviour object is the leading out-flow mode of the directed transition matrix, and they meet in one regression that positions the anchor, which then bins every item.
CHANGE: The level-1 codeword of every item becomes the index of the horocyclic band containing its Poincare latent, where bands are behaviour-mass quantiles of the Busemann coordinate and the boundary anchor of the Busemann function is trained to reproduce each item's training transition-flow rank; levels 2..L stay residual RQ-VAE.
SPECIFICATION: Compute r_i and m_i from training transitions only. Map encoder output to the Poincare ball, b_i = Busemann_{xi}(z_i). Add L_align = sum_i m_i (bhat_i - rtilde_i)^2 / sum_i m_i with bhat z-scored by running stats and rtilde the z-scored flow rank. Backpropagate L_align into xi and the encoder. Once per epoch recompute K1-1 band edges as m-weighted quantiles of b over training items and freeze them. token1_i = bin(b_i); tokens 2..L = standard residual RQ on (z_i - e1[token1_i]).
COST: Extra per step O(B*d) for the Busemann value and its gradient (B=batch, d=latent dim), roughly 3*B*d FLOPs, under 0.1 percent of a baseline RQ-VAE step. At the frozen scale: 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 steps, 24556 training items, so about 72000 * 2e5 = 1.4e10 extra FLOPs in total plus one sparse power-iteration SVD of the 24556 x 24556 transition matrix (about 20 matvecs) and one 24556-element sort per epoch (72000 sorts overall, seconds). Expected wall-clock overhead 1-3 percent, no extra parameters beyond the d-dim anchor.
HYPOTHESIS: The first SID token is predictable from session context only if it encodes a behaviourally meaningful macro direction. If true, held-out successor entropy of token1 given the user prefix drops relative to the baseline quantizer while residual levels stay comparably reconstructing, and Stage3 test Recall@10 moves above 0.065. It is falsified by token1 entropy at or above baseline or by reconstruction error rising without a recall gain.
RISKS: The dominant out-flow mode may be dominated by a few blockbuster items, collapsing the flow rank into a popularity proxy. A single scalar coordinate may under-separate items so levels 2..L must carry almost all information, raising residual error. Band edges recomputed per epoch can churn tokens and destabilise the generative vocabulary. The alignment term may compete with reconstruction and pull latents off the manifold.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transitions aggregated into a row-stochastic matrix, combined with per-item departure mass; the signal is the item's rank along the leading out-flow singular mode, weighted by departure mass.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training sequences only, count every consecutive pair (item_t -> item_{t+1}) inside a user sequence into a sparse matrix C (self-loops removed). m_i = row sum of C, normalised to sum to one over items with at least one outgoing transition (zero otherwise). P = row-normalised C with add-one smoothing. Take the top singular triple (u1, s1, v1) of P by power iteration on P^T P. r_i = (P v1)_i, and rtilde_i = (r_i - mean)/std over items with m_i > 0, 0 otherwise. Both rtilde and m are frozen constants during Stage 2.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: C, m and rtilde are built exclusively from training-split sequences, before any Stage-2 or Stage-3 evaluation, and are frozen for the whole run. Band edges are quantiles of training-item coordinates only. No validation or test sequence, no test item and no Stage-3 prediction ever enters C, rtilde, the anchor regression or the band edges, so the SID construction cannot see the evaluation interaction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Busemann function on the Poincare ball of fixed unit curvature kappa = 1, b_xi(z) = log( (1 - |z|^2) / |z - xi|^2 ), with xi constrained to the boundary sphere |xi| = 1; its level sets are horospheres, i.e. the curved analogue of parallel hyperplanes.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: It acts in the quantizer's level-1 assignment and in the anchor-training objective; the encoder reaches the ball through the origin exp map exp_0(e) = tanh(sqrt(kappa)|e|/2) * e / (sqrt(kappa)|e|).
COUPLING PATH: behaviour flow rank rtilde_i and mass m_i -> anchor regression that trains the boundary point xi -> Busemann coordinate b_xi(z_i) -> level-1 horocyclic band edges -> token1 -> residual levels and the generative prefix. Deleting the behaviour term makes xi an arbitrary fixed boundary direction and the bands behaviour-blind; replacing the Busemann coordinate by a Euclidean projection makes the anchor regression and the bands operate on a straight-line coordinate. Both change token1 for the same items, so the coupling is load-bearing.
COUPLING EQUATION: token1(i) = Band_q( b_{xi*}( z_i ) ), with xi* = argmin_{|xi|=1} [ sum_i m_i * ( bhat_xi(z_i) - rtilde_i )^2 / sum_i m_i ] and b_xi(z) = log( (1-|z|^2) / |z-xi|^2 ); here m_i and rtilde_i are the behaviour terms and b_xi with the boundary constraint is the curved term, and q are the m-weighted quantiles of b over training items.
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 token histogram and its concentration; held-out successor entropy and top-1 predictability of token1 given the user prefix; per-level residual reconstruction error; geodesic spread of items inside the same token1; Stage3 test Recall@10 against the 0.065 target; drift of xi across epochs.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: rtilde_i = 0, m_i = 1/N (uniform), xi frozen at the canonical boundary point e1/sqrt(kappa), and band edges become plain item-count quantiles of b_{e1}; everything else identical, same curvature, same compute.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: kappa -> 0 limit, i.e. b is replaced by the linear projection <z, xi>, the tanh exp map by the identity, the boundary constraint by a unit-norm vector; the behaviour term, the mass weights and the band construction are unchanged.
ABLATION BOTH ON: full run with behaviour-derived rtilde and m, the trained boundary anchor xi, and the horocyclic Busemann banding; this is the single trained candidate.
SPEC EQUATIONS: z_i = exp_0^kappa(E_phi(x_i)); b_i = log((1-|z_i|^2)/|z_i - xi|^2); bhat_i = (b_i - mu_b)/sigma_b with mu_b, sigma_b running (detached); L = L_RQVAE + lambda * sum_i m_i (bhat_i - rtilde_i)^2 / sum_i m_i; lambda = 1 because both terms are unit-variance standardised, which makes the weight dimensionless and derived rather than tuned; q = weighted_quantiles({b_i}, {m_i}, K1-1); token1_i = sum_k [b_i > q_k]; e1 = Codebook1[token1_i]; residual r_i^(1) = z_i - e1; levels 2..L quantise r^(l) with the baseline residual RQ and straight-through gradients; each epoch: recompute mu_b, sigma_b, q from the training set, freeze, continue.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Input: batch of encoder outputs e (B x d), the frozen constants rtilde (N) and m (N), the current anchor parameter w (d), codebooks 1..L. Output: token1 (B integers in [0, K1-1]), the align loss scalar, gradient wrt w and wrt the encoder, and the residual input for levels 2..L.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp |z| <= 1 - 1e-3 after the exp map; clamp |z - xi|^2 >= 1e-6 before the log; renormalise xi = w/||w|| every step and reject ||w|| < 1e-8; standardise b with running statistics and clamp bhat to [-10, 10] to stop the alignment term dominating early; skip items with m_i = 0 in L_align; keep band edges monotone by sorting and dropping duplicates (if fewer than K1-1 distinct edges, fall back to the previous epoch's edges); stop gradient through band edges and through the bin index; keep the Busemann and exp/log computations in float32 with a float64 fallback for the log denominator on the first epoch; guard the power iteration against a zero transition matrix by falling back to the uniform vector.
END IDEA
