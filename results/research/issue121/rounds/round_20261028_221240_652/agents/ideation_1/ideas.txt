IDEA: BEH-SINKHORN-CURVED-MARGINAL
TITLE: Behaviour-derived column marginals on a Poincare-cost Sinkhorn assignment for RQ-VAE Semantic IDs
MOTIVATION: In RQ-VAE the level codebooks are fit by nearest-neighbour plus a usage/commitment heuristic, so an item falls into whichever code is closest in a flat latent, and rare-but-branchy items end up sharing codes with popular hubs. The generative recommender then inherits prefixes whose first token does not separate "many plausible next items" from "one dominant next item", which is exactly the distinction a next-item generator needs to spend probability mass correctly.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds here pushed successor, fork, cone and shell information into an additive loss, a rank objective, a shell regulariser or a tangent-space transport of successor directions. This proposal puts behaviour inside the assignment constraint matrix itself: the Sinkhorn column marginal is a frozen training-only branching mass, the ground cost is a Poincare geodesic, and the two meet only inside the transport plan that produces the SID token. No new loss term is added to the RQ-VAE objective; the quantizer's argmax is replaced by the argmax of a doubly-constrained curved plan.
CHANGE: At every RQ-VAE level, replace nearest-neighbour code selection with the argmax of a Sinkhorn plan P* = argmin <P,C> - eps H(P) subject to P1 = a and P^T 1 = b, where C is the Poincare distance between the radius-squashed latent and the codebook points and b is a behaviour-derived column marginal built from the training-only successor branching of the items each code serves.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Precompute frozen per-item branching score g_i and per-code marginal b_k from the TRAIN split only. (2) Per level l, squash the residual h into the open ball with s = 1/(2*sqrt(d)), then build the cost C[b,k] = arcosh(1 + 2||z_b - e_k||^2/((1-||z_b||^2)(1-||e_k||^2))) at fixed curvature c = 1. (3) Row marginal a = 1/B; column marginal b = normalise(n_k + alpha*g_k) with alpha = mean(g)/(mean(g)+mean(n)) fixed before Stage 2. (4) Run 30 log-domain Sinkhorn iterations with eps = median(C) measured at initialisation. (5) Forward code is argmax_k P[b,k]; residual becomes r - e_code; gradient flows straight-through via the soft plan. The RQ-VAE reconstruction and commitment objective is unchanged.
COST: Three Sinkhorn solves of a B x K matrix per step (B about 1024, K = 256, 30 iterations) plus roughly 786k Poincare distance evaluations per step, about 25M extra flops and 2-4 ms per step against a full encoder/decoder pass, so +2-4% wall clock. At the frozen scale that is 24 x 3000 = 72000 steps with no budget change; the behaviour table costs one extra pass over the training transitions, computed once before Stage 2.
HYPOTHESIS: If branching-heavy items are genuinely under-served by plain nearest-neighbour codes, then giving them a larger column budget inside a curved cost space will raise per-level codebook usage entropy modestly, raise Stage-2 held-out first-token prefix predictability, and lift Stage-3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 in the single coupled run. If none of the three observables moves, the coupling is inert and the idea should be dropped.
RISKS: The marginal pressure may homogenise codes and erase the fine distinctions the decoder needs; the branching score is noisy for low-degree items at 24556 items; the Poincare cost is flat near the origin so the plan may degenerate toward uniform; the derived eps = median(C) may over- or under-smooth and cannot be corrected without tuning, which the programme forbids.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted successor branching per item, aggregated into a per-codebook-entry mass.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From TRAIN interactions only, build directed transition counts T[i,j]. For item i let S_i = {j : T[i,j] > 0} and g_i = (1/|S_i|) * sum_{j in S_i} 1/log(1 + pop_j), with pop_j the TRAIN item frequency and g_i = 0 when |S_i| = 0. Normalise g over items, then for level l and code k set g_k = sum_i w_{i,k} g_i / sum_i w_{i,k} where w_{i,k} is the fraction of levels at which item i chose code k in the previous epoch, detached.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: g_i uses only TRAIN transitions and TRAIN popularity, computed once before Stage 2 and frozen. n_k is recomputed each epoch from codes assigned to TRAIN items only. Validation is read exclusively to log prefix predictability and never enters b, eps, alpha or any codebook update; the test split is untouched until the final Stage-3 evaluation, so no validation or test interaction can shape a SID.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball distance at fixed negative curvature c = 1, arcosh(1 + 2||x-y||^2/((1-||x||^2)(1-||y||^2))), acting as the ground cost of the assignment transport.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE quantisation step at every level, as the cost matrix of the Sinkhorn assignment that produces each SID token, and through it as the curvature of the gradient that reaches the codebook and the encoder latent.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour -> column marginal b -> constrained optimal transport -> plan P* -> argmax token and straight-through gradient -> codebook position and encoder latent. The marginal is a constraint of the same optimisation whose cost is the geodesic, so deleting b makes the plan uniform-column and deleting the curvature makes the plan Euclidean; neither factor survives as an independent term.
COUPLING EQUATION: P* = argmin_{P in U(a,b)} sum_{b,k} P[b,k] * d_Poincare(z_b, e_k; c=1) - eps * H(P), with b_k proportional to n_k + alpha * g_k, where g_k is the popularity-adjusted successor-branching mass of code k and alpha = mean(g)/(mean(g)+mean(n)).
OBSERVABLES: per-level codebook usage entropy, share of high-branching items landing in the top-usage codes, Stage-2 held-out first-token prefix accuracy, effective rank and collision count of the SID set, and finally Stage-3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: b_k is replaced by the uniform marginal 1/K (equivalently alpha = 0 and n_k dropped) while curvature and Sinkhorn settings stay identical. The switch exists as BEHAV_SINKHORN=False and is never trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: C[b,k] = ||z_b - e_k||^2 on the same radius-squashed latents with the same marginals and Sinkhorn settings. The switch exists as GEOM_POINCARE=False and is never trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full plan with b_k proportional to n_k + alpha*g_k and C the Poincare distance; this is the single trained candidate.
SPEC EQUATIONS: precompute g_i, g_k, b_k, alpha, and eps = median(C_0). For each level: r_0 = h; for l in 1..L: z = squash(r_{l-1}); C[b,k] = arcosh(1 + 2||z_b - e_k||^2/((1-||z_b||^2)(1-||e_k||^2))); K = exp(-C/eps); iterate u = a/(K v), v = b/(K^T u) thirty times in log space; P = diag(u) K diag(v); k* = argmax_k P[b,k]; token_l = k*; r_l = r_{l-1} - sg(e_{k*}) + (P_row - sg(P_row)) as the straight-through residual. Objective unchanged: reconstruction plus commitment; e_k updated by the gradient flowing through the soft plan.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are the encoder latent h (B x d), the codebook points e (K x d) per level, the frozen behaviour marginal b (K), the uniform row marginal a (B), eps, and the fixed curvature c = 1. Outputs are the SID token per level per item plus a straight-through gradient into both the codebook and the encoder.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||z|| <= 1 - 1e-5 and ||e|| <= 1 - 1e-5; clamp the arcosh argument to >= 1 + 1e-7; floor b_k at 1e-6/K before normalising; floor K at 1e-30 in the log domain; cap Sinkhorn at 30 iterations with a residual check; if a plan row is tied within 1e-8 fall back to nearest-neighbour for that item; a NaN guard reverts that level to nearest-neighbour for the step and increments a logged counter.
END IDEA
