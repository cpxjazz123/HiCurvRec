IDEA: CURV_COH_PREFIX_SINKHORN
TITLE: Behaviour-graph local curvature (triangle cohesion rank) as a curvature anchor for level-1 prefix grouping, carried inside the balanced hyperbolic Sinkhorn cost
MOTIVATION: The baseline RQ-VAE SIDs are geometrically and behaviourally blind: level-1 prefixes are learned from reconstruction alone, so coarse codes mix items whose behavioural neighbourhoods have very different local curvature (triangle-dense hub clusters versus chain-like fringes). A generative recommender then spends prefix capacity memorising heterogeneity instead of predicting a coherent coarse intent. If the coarsest prefix is homogeneous in behavioural curvature, the coarse token becomes a predictable behavioural cluster and the fine tokens only refine locally, which is what a next-item generator needs.
NOVELTY: Not a behaviour-weighted distance and not a standalone hyperbolic regulariser. Each level-1 codebook entry carries a geometric curvature anchor a_k = tanh(sqrt(c)||e_k||), its Poincare depth, and each item carries a bounded behavioural curvature coordinate s_i = 2*rank(coh(i))/N - 1 from training-graph triangle cohesion. The two meet twice on one path: (i) as the offset -lambda_b*s_i*a_k inside the Sinkhorn cost matrix whose distance term is the Poincare distance, and (ii) as a centred curvature-assignment alignment loss on the Sinkhorn soft assignment P whose weights are curvature similarities (w_ij - wbar). No new parameters, no extra head, no second codebook, no plain-argmin assignment.
CHANGE: Replace the Euclidean distance inside the existing Sinkhorn cost matrix by d_c(exp_0^c(z_i), exp_0^c(e_k)) and subtract the behaviour-geometry offset lambda_b*s_i*a_k, keep the baseline Sinkhorn balancing and hard-assignment rule unchanged, and add one differentiable alignment term on the resulting soft level-1 assignment that rewards sharing a coarse prefix between curvature-similar items and penalises it between curvature-dissimilar items.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Cohesion from the training split only: undirected co-occurrence graph G, edges (i,j) weighted by co-occurrence count over training sequences; N(i) = top-M neighbours by count (M = 50, documented default); triangle count T_ij = |N(i) intersect N(j)| per edge; bounded per-item statistic coh(i) = mean over incident edges of log1p(T_ij). (2) Rank-normalise s_i = 2*((rank of coh(i) among the 24556 training items, ties averaged) - 0.5)/24556 - 1, so s in (-1,1), mean(s) = 0; precomputed once and frozen as a 24556-vector. (3) Geometry: fixed c = 1.0 (R_c = 1); item point x_i = exp_0^c(z_i), code point q_k = exp_0^c(e_k), exp_0^c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)*v/(sqrt(c)||v||); curvature anchor a_k = tanh(sqrt(c)||e_k||) in (0,1). (4) Cost matrix, level 1 only: C_ik = d_c(x_i,q_k) - lambda_b*s_i*a_k. (5) Assignment: P = Sinkhorn(-C/tau) with the baseline's exact iterations, temperature, marginals and balancing rule; the hard level-1 index is the baseline's balanced choice (row argmax of P as in the baseline code), never an unconstrained argmin of C. Levels 2 and 3 keep the baseline rule with C^(l)_ik = d_c(exp_0^c(r_i^(l-1)), exp_0^c(e_k^(l))), geometry on, behaviour offset only at level 1 where the prefix identity is decided. (6) Alignment loss on P over a deterministic stride subsample S of m = 128 batch rows: L_curv = (1/m^2) sum_{i,j in S} (w_ij - wbar) * <P_i,P_j>, w_ij = 1 - 2|s_i - s_j| in [-1,1], wbar the mean over S; minimising the covariance makes prefix sharing track curvature similarity. (7) L = L_recon + L_commit + lambda_curv*L_curv, with lambda_b = rho_b * median_{i,k} d_c(x_i,q_k) / (median_i|s_i| * median_k a_k + 1e-8), rho_b = 0.1, and lambda_curv = rho_c * sg(L_recon) / (sg(|L_curv|) + 1e-8), rho_c = 0.1, both computed from the current mini-batch, no history and no sweep; sg = stop-gradient so the term contributes a fixed 10 percent of the reconstruction scale. (8) Reconstruction and residual recursion use the un-lifted codewords e_k exactly as in the baseline; decoders see z, not x. STE, 3 levels, dimension, codebook sizes, optimiser, epochs, batch and seed are unchanged.
COST: One training run only. Extra per step: B*K1*d MACs for the Poincare distance (B = 1024, K1 = 256, d as baseline, about 17M MACs at d = 64), m^2*K1 = 4.2M MACs for L_curv, and the same number of Sinkhorn iterations as the baseline, only on different numbers. Cohesion is a one-off CPU precompute over the training split (minutes). At the frozen scale of 24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps, overhead is about +9 +/- 3 ms/step, under 5 percent of Stage 2 wall clock (roughly 11 to 15 extra minutes on four cards); Stage 3 cost unchanged because SIDs are assigned once. Memory: one 24556-vector and one B*K1 distance buffer, no extra parameters.
HYPOTHESIS: If behaviour-graph curvature is the right coarse variable for a prefix, the level-1 prefix groups of the coupled run must be measurably more curvature-homogeneous than random pairing (within-prefix share of |s_i - s_j| below the 10 percent quantile band of random pairs, reported against a random-pair control), level-1 codebook usage must stay even (max level-1 code share near the reference, nowhere near the 31 percent collapse seen with argmin), and the single coupled run's Stage 3 test Recall@10 must exceed 0.065. If homogeneity moves but Recall stays at or below 0.065, the coarse variable is not the one the generator needs.
RISKS: Hub items with raw triangle means near 700 could dominate the cohesion vector if it were not rank-bounded; log1p plus rank transform mitigates this but residual hub concentration could still skew wbar.; The alignment term could push the encoder into a bimodal curvature split and hurt reconstruction; the stop-gradient scale matching and the 10 percent cap bound this but do not remove it.; With c = 1 and an origin exp map the ball is filled only through tanh, so large ||z|| saturates a_k and the offset becomes constant for deep codes, mitigated by the radius shrink but not eliminated.; Median-based lambda_b is per-batch and therefore noisy once the stratum subsample reduces the effective sample.; A single run with one seed means seed noise sits inside the verdict.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Edge-level triangle cohesion of the training co-occurrence graph, aggregated per item and rank-transformed into a bounded behavioural curvature coordinate s_i: the second-order (neighbour-of-neighbour) cohesion degree of an item in its own behavioural neighbourhood, not raw popularity and not sequential order.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Built from the training split only. Pass 1 over training sequences: undirected co-occurrence counts n_ij (distinct-sequence support, self-pairs dropped) and item degrees; keep top-M = 50 neighbours per item by n_ij to form N(i). Pass 2 over the retained edge list: T_ij = |N(i) intersect N(j)| by sparse sorted-set intersection, coh(i) = mean over incident edges of log1p(T_ij) (isolated items get coh = 0). Then rank-normalise coh over all 24556 training items into s_i in (-1,1), zero-mean. No validation or test sequence is read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: coh and s are frozen before Stage 2 starts and are built from training sequences only; the SID construction, the Sinkhorn cost and L_curv never call the validation or test loaders, and the reported homogeneity and Stage 3 metrics are computed after assignment from held-out data that enters no gradient. Because s is a precomputed constant vector, no evaluation interaction can leak into the codebook through Stage 2 gradient descent.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1.0 (ball radius R_c = 1): origin exponential map exp_0^c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)*v/(sqrt(c)||v||) applied to the Euclidean latent and to the codewords; Poincare distance d_c(x,y) = (2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)(+)y||); and codeword curvature anchor a_k = tanh(sqrt(c)||e_k||), the hyperbolic depth of a code.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside quantization, in the level-1 Sinkhorn cost matrix (distance term) and, through a_k, in the behaviour offset of that same matrix; and in the level-1 soft assignment P produced by the balanced Sinkhorn, where the curvature-homogeneity alignment loss is evaluated. Levels 2 and 3 use the curved distance only.
COUPLING PATH: coh -> s_i (frozen training vector) and e_k -> a_k (curved codeword depth) -> cost C_ik = d_c(x_i,q_k) - lambda_b*s_i*a_k -> Sinkhorn(-C/tau) -> P -> (a) the hard level-1 prefix, which changes the SID tokens and hence the Stage 3 prefix sequence, and (b) L_curv on P, whose weights are curvature similarities and whose gradient flows back into z_i and e_k, hence into the encoder and the level-1 codebook. Behaviour off (s = 0) removes the offset and zeroes every weight w_ij - wbar; geometry off (c = 0) turns d_c Euclidean and a_k into a bounded Euclidean depth; both change C and L_curv. There is no other path.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ik = d_c(exp_0^c(z_i), exp_0^c(e_k)) - lambda_b * s_i * tanh(sqrt(c)||e_k||); L_curv = (1/m^2) sum_{i,j in S} ((1 - 2|s_i - s_j|) - wbar) * <Sinkhorn(-C/tau)_i, Sinkhorn(-C/tau)_j>
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 hard error rate, number of distinct level-2 prefixes, level-3 collision rate, level-3 refinement gain, per-level codebook usage histogram (max code share, used level-1 codes against the reference 241 of 256), Sinkhorn marginal residual (must stay at baseline level, proving balancing is intact), curvature homogeneity of level-1 groups (within-prefix share of |s_i - s_j| below the 10 percent random-pair band versus the random-pair control), the magnitude ratio lambda_b*|s_i|*a_k over median d_c (near 0.1 by construction), and Stage 3 test Recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: s_i := 0 for all items at every step, so the offset vanishes identically, w_ij - wbar := 0 so L_curv is exactly zero, and the cost matrix is the pure Poincare cost; Sinkhorn balancing, geometry, codebook sizes, weights and schedule unchanged. Exists in code behind one flag; not trained this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c := 0, so d_c becomes the Euclidean distance, a_k := tanh(||e_k||) (bounded Euclidean depth) and the exp map becomes the identity; the behaviour offset and L_curv stay exactly as specified. This is the same-capacity Euclidean control, reported as a separate arm in its own round (directory plus the same layered numbers), not as an in-round ablation.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run of this round: Poincare cost with the behaviour-curvature offset inside the unchanged balanced Sinkhorn, plus L_curv on the level-1 soft assignment, trained once on the frozen 3000-epoch Stage 2 and the frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping.
SPEC EQUATIONS: exp_0^c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) * v / (sqrt(c)*||v|| + 1e-12); a_k = tanh(sqrt(c)*||e_k||); u_ik = (-x_i)(+)q_k via Mobius addition; d_c(x_i,q_k) = (2/sqrt(c)) * artanh(clamp(sqrt(c)*||u_ik||, 0, 1-1e-5)); C_ik = d_c(x_i,q_k) - lambda_b*s_i*a_k; P = Sinkhorn(-C/tau) with the baseline iteration count J, temperature tau and marginals, identical call site with only C substituted; k1_i = baseline balanced hard index (row argmax of P, never a bare argmin of C); w_ij = 1 - 2|s_i - s_j|; wbar = mean_{i,j in S} w_ij; G_ij = <P_i,P_j>; L_curv = mean_{i,j in S} (w_ij - wbar)*G_ij; lambda_b = 0.1 * median_{i,k} d_c(x_i,q_k) / (median_i|s_i| * median_k a_k + 1e-8); lambda_curv = 0.1 * sg(L_recon) / (sg(|L_curv|) + 1e-8); L = L_recon + L_commit + lambda_curv*L_curv. STE: forward uses the balanced one-hot of P and the baseline residual recursion on un-lifted e_k; backward passes dL/dC = dL/dP * dP/dC through the Sinkhorn recursion and the clipped artanh, so z_i and e_k both receive gradient from the behaviour term.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: minibatch Euclidean latents z in R^{B x d}, un-lifted level-1 codewords e in R^{K1 x d}, frozen behaviour vector s in R^24556 restricted to the minibatch, curvature c = 1.0, temperature tau and Sinkhorn settings from the baseline, deterministic stride subsample S of size m = 128, rho_b = 0.1, rho_c = 0.1. Outputs: level-1 hard indices k1 (and level-2/3 indices as in the baseline), the loss scalar L, and diagnostic scalars L_curv, lambda_b, lambda_curv, the Sinkhorn marginal residual and the per-code usage histogram.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp the artanh argument to 1-1e-5 and ||u|| to (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); add 1e-12 to every norm denominator; bound ||z_i|| and ||e_k|| by shrinking the usable ball radius to (1-1e-3)/sqrt(c) with a differentiable rescale z <- z*min(1, a_r/(||z||+1e-12)), so the origin exp map never reaches the boundary; s_i in (-1,1) with mean 0; both lambdas stop-gradient and scale-matched so neither dominates; L_curv on centred weights w - wbar so it cannot drift into global repulsion; if |L_curv| < 1e-8 or the batch has fewer than 4 distinct s bands the term is set to zero and a counter is logged instead of blowing up; Sinkhorn in log domain with max-subtraction and the baseline iteration count so the marginal residual stays at baseline level; codebook usage histogram logged every epoch to catch the 42/256, 31 percent collapse mode; the assignment is never written as an unconstrained argmin over the level index.
END IDEA
