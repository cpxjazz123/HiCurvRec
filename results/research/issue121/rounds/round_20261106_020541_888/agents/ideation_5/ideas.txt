IDEA: scc-l1-curvcoherence
TITLE: Curvature-coherent prefix grouping: a training-only triangle-coherence curvature coordinate offsets the level-1 Poincare Sinkhorn cost while the baseline balanced assignment is left intact.
MOTIVATION: The first RQ-VAE level is the coarsest prefix: everything below it is conditioned on which root code an item receives. The baseline assigns roots purely by embedding proximity, so one root can mix items whose behaviour neighbourhoods have completely different local shape (tight co-consumed clusters versus sparse, chain-like, tree-like fan-out). Such a root forces levels 2-3 to serve two incompatible kinds of residual, wastes prefix capacity and makes the generative prior's first token less informative. The proposal makes the root grouping homogeneous in an explicitly geometric, behaviour-derived quantity: the local curvature of each item's co-consumption neighbourhood.
NOVELTY: Not a behaviour suffix on an unchanged Euclidean quantiser and not a standalone hyperbolic loss. The behaviour signal is not a co-occurrence count or a transition count either: it is a bounded local-curvature proxy (log-compressed triangle density of the training co-occurrence graph, rank-normalised to [-1,1]), and it enters as a per-item/per-codeword offset INSIDE the same cost matrix that carries the Poincare distance, before the baseline Sinkhorn. A second differentiable pass (Sinkhorn of the offset cost) makes codeword curvature centres b_k depend on the curved plan itself, so behaviour, geometry and the balanced assignment form one loop rather than three parallel terms. Unlike popularity reweighting, the quantity being homogenised is geometric (tree-like versus clustered), so the ablation that matters is exactly the curvature-versus-Euclidean one.
CHANGE: Level-1 cost becomes d_c(u_i,c_k)/median(d_c) + 0.25*|kappa_i - b_k|, where kappa_i is the frozen training-only triangle-coherence curvature rank of item i and b_k is the curvature centre of codeword k inferred from the curved level-1 Sinkhorn plan; the baseline balanced Sinkhorn is then re-run on this cost and its row-argmax is the level-1 code. A curvature-homogeneity term on the resulting balanced plan is added to the loss at the commitment weight.
SPECIFICATION: OFFLINE (train split only, once): build undirected co-occurrence graph G from training sequences with window W=5; keep top-M neighbours per item by co-occurrence count with M=ceil(sqrt(n_items))=157 (union-symmetrised); triangle counts t_ij=|N(i) cap N(j)| by sorted neighbour-list intersection; coh(i)=mean_{j in N(i)} log1p(t_ij); kappa_i=2*rankavg(coh)_i/(n-1)-1 in [-1,1]; freeze kappa as a table. Compute c0=1/median_i(||r_i||_2)^2 on the step-0 probe batch. PER STEP (level 1 only): 1. u_i = exp_0^c(r_i) with c=clamp(softplus(rho), c0/4, 4c0); c_k=exp_0^c(e_k) (e_k stay unlifted for the residual). 2. D_ik = d_c(u_i,c_k) / stopgrad(median_{i,k} d_c). 3. P0 = Sinkhorn(D/eps_base, r, c_marg) - baseline routine, marginals and epsilon unchanged. 4. Buffers m_k (mass) and s_k (mass-weighted kappa), detached, accumulated over the run; b_k = (s_k^old + sum_i P0_ik kappa_i)/(m_k^old + sum_i P0_ik + 1e-6). 5. C1_ik = D_ik + 0.25*|kappa_i - b_k|; P1 = Sinkhorn(C1/eps_base, r, c_marg) - SAME balanced rule, never an argmin. 6. k1_i = row-argmax of P1 (baseline hard rule); e1_i = sum_k P1_ik e_k soft for reconstruction, STE to hard e_{k1} at eval; r2 = r1 - e_{k1} with unlifted codewords. 7. Levels 2 and 3 and the residual recursion: baseline, unchanged. 8. L = L_recon + beta*L_commit + beta*L_curv/Var_batch(kappa), with L_curv = sum_k sum_i P1_ik (kappa_i-b_k)^2 / sum_k sum_i P1_ik.
COST: One extra Sinkhorn pass on a 1023x256 matrix at level 1 (20 iterations) plus two 1023x256 elementwise passes: about 1e8 extra FLOPs per step, roughly +2 to +4 percent Stage-2 step time at the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps per epoch, 3000 epochs, four cards); Stage 3 unchanged. Offline triangle counting on 24556 items with M=157 (~3.9M edge intersections) costs a few minutes of single-core CPU once and is memoised, amortised over 72000 steps.
HYPOTHESIS: If local triangle coherence carries usable hierarchy information, then the both-on run should (i) reduce within-level-1-prefix curvature dispersion relative to random pairing (homogeneity ratio H well below 1.0), (ii) keep level-1 code usage near the reference arm because the balanced assignment is untouched (order 240 of 256 codes used, largest code far from 31 percent of items, level-3 collision far from 0.703), and (iii) lift Stage3 test Recall@10 above 0.065 in one run. If post-training H is indistinguishable from random pairing the signal is rejected regardless of recall.
RISKS: Triangle density is popularity-confounded; rank normalisation and log1p bound the range but hub items may still occupy the extreme ranks.; The derived offset cap (half the batch median curved distance) may be too weak to move any assignment, leaving the both-on run numerically close to behaviour-off: the mechanism would be inert.; Forcing curvature-homogeneous roots may cost reconstruction and cancel the recall gain.; Batch-median normalisation of the cost changes the effective entropy strength relative to the reference arm; identical in all three switch states but the raw medians must be reported.; The trainable curvature may drift toward zero (mitigated by the measured clamp band) or the extra L_curv term may fight commitment.; Single run, single seed: differences of a few thousandths of recall sit inside seed noise, so only the joint reading of all three observables is interpretable.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only item-level local curvature coordinate kappa_i derived from triangle coherence of the undirected co-consumption graph: a bounded, rank-normalised proxy of local clustering, hence of local geometric curvature (zero triangles means tree-like, negatively curved neighbourhoods; dense triangles mean clustered, flat patches).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training interaction sequences only: undirected edges for item pairs co-occurring inside a window of W=5 positions; edge weight = co-occurrence count; per-node top-M truncation with M=ceil(sqrt(n_items))=157, M derived from the item-count scale, never swept; triangle count per retained edge t_ij=|N(i) cap N(j)| via sorted-list intersection; coh(i)=mean over retained neighbours of log1p(t_ij); kappa_i=2*rankavg(coh)_i/(n-1)-1. Computed before Stage 2, frozen, stored with the SID artefacts.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: kappa is a deterministic function of the training split's sequences only; the graph build reads no validation or test interaction and the table is frozen before the first optimiser step, so Stage-3 interactions cannot influence it. At inference the level-1 code of an unseen item needs only its own embedding, the codebooks and the frozen training-derived buffers b_k; no test-time behaviour is read. The homogeneity diagnostic is computed post hoc from the frozen kappa and the emitted SIDs and is never an admission gate.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball distance d_c(x,y)=(2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x) xor y||) on the ball of radius R_c=1/sqrt(c), with exponential map exp_0^c(v)=tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)*v/(sqrt(c)||v||) and negative curvature -c; c=softplus(rho) trainable but clamped to [c0/4, 4c0] with c0=1/median||r||^2, so the ball radius is tied to the measured embedding-norm scale. Tangent vectors carry the factor-2 Riemannian norm ||v||_{0,c}=2||v||, documented as a convention; the optimiser learning rate is not rescaled.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the level-1 quantisation step: the curved distance is one of the two summands of the cost matrix of the baseline balanced Sinkhorn, and the curvature-homogeneity term acts on that Sinkhorn's output plan. Levels 2-3, the residual recursion, the encoder and the generative model stay Euclidean and use unlifted codewords.
COUPLING PATH: encoder embedding r_i -> lift exp_0^c -> curved distance matrix D -> first Sinkhorn plan P0 -> codeword curvature centres b_k -> offset cost C1 = D + lambda|kappa_i - b_k| -> second Sinkhorn plan P1 -> level-1 code and quantised vector -> reconstruction loss and L_curv. Removing the behaviour term zeroes |kappa_i-b_k|, collapses C1 to D and changes P1 and every downstream gradient; setting c to zero turns D into squared Euclidean distance, changing P0, hence b_k, hence C1. Neither side can be removed without altering the assignment actually used for quantisation.
COUPLING EQUATION: C1_ik = d_c(exp_0^c(r_i), exp_0^c(e_k)) / median_{i,k} d_c + lambda * |kappa_i - b_k(P0)|, with b_k(P0)=sum_i P0_ik kappa_i / sum_i P0_ik, P0=Sinkhorn_k(d_c/eps), P1=Sinkhorn_k(C1/eps), code(i)=argmax_k P1_ik; lambda = 1/2 divided by the range of |kappa_i-b_k| (=2), so the behaviour offset is capped at half the batch median curved distance (a bound derived from measured scales), and L_curv = sum_k sum_i P1_ik (kappa_i-b_k)^2 / sum_k sum_i P1_ik.
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 hard entropy ratio (codes used of 256, largest-code share), level-2 prefix count, level-3 collision rate, level-3 refinement gain, Stage3 test Recall@10, and the curvature-homogeneity ratio H = mean over prefixes of mean_i|kappa_i - kappa_prefix| divided by the mean over random item pairs of |kappa_i - kappa_j| (H well below 1 means the coupling homogenised roots; H near 1 means the behaviour signal was inert).
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEHAVIOUR_CURVATURE=0 in the config: lambda=0 and the L_curv weight is zero, so the cost is the purely curved, batch-median-normalised distance and the balanced Sinkhorn, levels 2-3, STE and schedule are identical; the switch exists and is covered by a one-batch unit test, but this arm is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: HYPERBOLIC=0 in the config: c is forced to 0 and the level-1 cost becomes squared Euclidean, ||r_i-e_k||^2 divided by its batch median (same-capacity Euclidean quantiser, no lifting, same offset lambda|kappa_i-b_k| and same L_curv). This is the family's Euclidean control arm, opened as its own separate round under directory arm_euclid_<round_id>; this round trains only the both-on arm and the mechanism report cites that directory with the same stratified numbers (L1 hard ER, L2 prefix count, L3 collision, L3 refinement gain, Stage3 test Recall@10, homogeneity ratio).
ABLATION BOTH ON: HYPERBOLIC=1 and BEHAVIOUR_CURVATURE=1: curved cost plus behaviour curvature offset inside the same level-1 balanced Sinkhorn, plus the homogeneity term; the single arm trained this round, one run, 3000 Stage-2 epochs on four cards, frozen Stage-3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep, no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Offline: t_ij=|N(i) cap N(j)|; coh(i)=(1/|N(i)|) sum_{j in N(i)} log1p(t_ij); kappa_i=2*rankavg(coh)_i/(n-1)-1 in [-1,1]; M=ceil(sqrt(n))=157; W=5; c0=1/median||r||^2; c=clamp(softplus(rho), c0/4, 4c0). u_i=exp_0^c(r_i)=tanh(sqrt(c)||r_i||)*r_i/(sqrt(c)||r_i||) (limit r_i as ||r_i||->0); c_k=exp_0^c(e_k); d_c(u,c_k)=(2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)||(-u) xor c_k||). D_ik = d_c(u_i,c_k)/stopgrad(median_ik d_c). P0 = Sinkhorn(-D/eps, r_marg, c_marg)  [baseline routine, baseline eps and marginals]. b_k = (s_k + sum_i P0_ik kappa_i)/(m_k + sum_i P0_ik + 1e-6)  [s_k, m_k detached cumulative buffers]. C1_ik = D_ik + 0.25*|kappa_i - b_k|. P1 = Sinkhorn(-C1/eps, r_marg, c_marg)  [SAME balanced rule, plan doubly stochastic up to eps]. k1_i = argmax_k P1_ik; e1_i = sum_k P1_ik e_k (soft), STE to e_{k1} at eval; r2 = r1 - e_{k1}. L = ||x - dec(z_q)||^2 + beta*||r1 - stopgrad(e1)||^2 + beta*[sum_k sum_i P1_ik (kappa_i-b_k)^2 / sum_k sum_i P1_ik] / (Var_batch(kappa)+1e-6).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: the batch of encoder embeddings r_i (1023 x d per step), the level-1 codebook {e_k} (256 x d, unlifted), the frozen curvature table kappa (24556 floats in [-1,1]), the trainable curvature parameter rho, the detached cumulative buffers (m_k, s_k) and the batch median of d_c. Outputs: the level-1 balanced plan P1 (1023 x 256), hard level-1 codes k1, the soft quantised vector e1, the scalar curvature-homogeneity loss and updated curvature centres b_k; differentiable w.r.t. r_i, e_k and rho through both Sinkhorn passes.
SAFEGUARDS: artanh argument clipped at 1-1e-7 and lifted points projected back to radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c) before every distance evaluation; exp_0 returns 0 cleanly when ||r||=0; cost matrices normalised by a detached batch median (epsilon 1e-8) so curvature changes cannot blow up Sinkhorn; Sinkhorn keeps the baseline iteration count, log-domain stabilisation and epsilon, with a 1e-12 floor on row and column sums; b_k smoothed by its cumulative detached denominator and used only for codes with mass above 1e-6 (others keep b_k=0, i.e. neutral); c clamped by construction to [c0/4, 4c0] so curvature can neither collapse to Euclidean nor explode, with c0 measured not chosen; the offset coefficient 0.25 is derived from the explicit cap of half the batch median curved distance, not tuned; reconstruction and the residual recursion use only unlifted codewords so no Riemannian metric leaks into the decoder; the curvature table is frozen and hashed into the run artefact so a split mismatch is detectable; STE forwards the hard code but backprops the soft plan, keeping the behaviour-geometry gradient path alive; all three switch states are config booleans asserted at startup and covered by a one-batch unit test, and no switched-off arm is trained.
END IDEA
