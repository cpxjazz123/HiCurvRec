IDEA: cohort_hyperbolic_prefix_budget
TITLE: Train-cohort geodesic routing with a narrow first-token budget
MOTIVATION: A first SID token that branches across many weakly predictable alternatives is often pruned before useful L2/L3 evidence is reached; yet a single uninformative token wastes the prefix. User cohorts with similar next-item choices can provide a training-only organizing signal for a deliberately narrow but useful first level.
NOVELTY: Unlike pairwise transition smoothing of codewords, curvature conditioned on entropy, or post-quantization behavior tags, this learns a small set of first-token destinations by routing each item toward its train-only cohort's hyperbolic centroid. The number of destinations is explicitly capped at 32, while residual levels retain the fine distinctions.
CHANGE: Compute item cohort distributions from training users' immediate successor choices, embed those distributions in a fixed-curvature Poincare ball, and include cohort-centroid geodesic distance in the first-level code assignment cost. Use exactly 32 first-level codewords; the behavior-geodesic term participates in the same argmin and gradient path as reconstruction, and T4 is reserved for deterministic collision extension rather than learned discrimination.
SPECIFICATION: From train-only adjacent transitions count C_ij. Let q_i(j)=(C_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(sum_l C_il+alpha), pi_j the smoothed train destination marginal. Map q_i to a compact tangent feature h_i by a fixed train-only projection, and x_i=exp_0^c(Wz_i), b_i=exp_0^c(Vh_i) with c=0.5. Maintain 32 trainable cohort anchors a_g in the ball; for candidate group g use J_i(g)=d_c(x_i,a_g)^2 + lambda*d_c(b_i,a_g)^2. Assign g_i=argmin_g J_i(g), and train encoder/projection/anchors with straight-through quantization and reconstruction plus the selected J term. Then run ordinary residual quantization in L2/L3; do not learn T4 (export uses the repository's deterministic collision-extension token). Capacity budget: T1 has at most 32 groups (5 bits); T2 and T3 carry remaining discrimination under the unchanged residual codebooks; T4 must have near-zero teacher-forced information (target median <0.1 nat and no systematic increase over matched baseline), not become the rescue discriminant. Compare per-position information and beam survival rather than forcing exact entropy targets.
COST: One sparse transition pass O(E_train), one projection/cache of successor distributions O(N*d_h), and first-level candidate scores O(B*32*D) versus O(B*K1*D) at the original K1=256. At frozen scale there are 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs = 72,000 steps and 73,668,000 item presentations; the curved first-level scoring adds approximately 73,668,000*32*D distance-coordinate work, generally less than the original 256-way first-level scan, plus one transition-feature cache lookup per item. Four-token decoder and Stage3 costs are unchanged; benchmark wall clock against matched controls.
HYPOTHESIS: A modest first-token branching budget, when its groups align with what users tend to consume next, lets Stage3 retain and rank useful paths instead of losing them at depth 1 or postponing all discrimination to T4. Keep only if both-on beats behavior-off and geometry-off, changes prefix structure, and Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065. Required measurements: canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER should move away from the degenerate 1.000 and be informative but not collapse toward all-positive/all-negative; gold-prefix survival should be non-decreasing at depth 1 versus the matched 256-code baseline and higher at depths 2-3, with depth 4 not being the sole recovery; T4 teacher-forced NLL should remain near-constant/low rather than rise; test_R@10 target >0.065.
RISKS: Successor distributions are sparse and may chiefly encode popularity; 32 groups may be too few or cohorts may not transfer to Stage3 history representations. A behavior term can dominate content reconstruction, and fixed projected distribution features could be noisy. The predicted prefix and NLL directions are hypotheses, not guarantees; the full Stage3 result is decisive.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Smoothed per-item distribution over immediate next items in directed user sequences.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: On TRAIN only, order each user's events by the supplied sequence/timestamp, count adjacent i->j transitions without crossing users, compute pi_j from training destinations, then q_i(j)=(C_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(sum_l C_il+alpha). Freeze sparse top-M q_i and its fixed-dimensional projection before RQ-VAE fitting; all dimensions and smoothing constants are declared before evaluation.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transition counts, destination marginal, projection inputs, cohort anchors, and item assignments use only TRAIN events and the fixed item inventory/embeddings. No valid/test interactions or metrics enter the cached q, fitting, assignment, hyperparameter choice, or SID export. Validation/test are used only by the frozen downstream evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp map and squared geodesic distance d_c with fixed negative curvature -c=-0.5; all projected points and anchors are ball points.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First-level candidate assignment cost and its straight-through encoder/anchor gradient during Stage2, before residual L2/L3 quantization.
COUPLING PATH: Train transition counts -> q_i -> projected behavior point b_i -> Poincare cohort distance d_c(b_i,a_g) inside the same 32-way first-token assignment cost as the mapped content point -> selected group, gradients, and SID prefix. Replacing q_i with a constant changes the route cost; replacing the curved maps/distance changes that same route and assignment.
COUPLING EQUATION: g_i=argmin_{g in 1..32}[d_c(exp_0^c(Wz_i),a_g)^2 + lambda*d_c(exp_0^c(Vh(q_i)),a_g)^2]. Here q_i/h(q_i) is the train-only behavior term and exp_0^c,d_c are the curved terms in one connected assignment.
OBSERVABLES: Record first-token changes and occupancy; canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER with ten fixed negatives (predict non-degenerate and changed versus controls); width-20 gold-prefix survival at depths 1,2,3,4 (expect depth-1 retention at least baseline and gains at 2-3); teacher-forced T4 NLL (expect low/near constant); and Stage3 test_R@10 (must exceed 0.065 to meet target). Also inspect whether the information moves into useful L2/L3 rather than T4.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Replace q_i/h(q_i) for every item by the same train-set mean behavior vector, keeping 32 groups, curvature, training schedule, and seed fixed. Measure the same ER, depth-1..4 survival, T4 NLL, and Stage3 test_R@10; this tests whether cohort evidence changes prefix structure.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the exact train-derived q_i, 32-group capacity, and behavior pathway, but replace exp/log and Poincare distance with identity maps and matched squared Euclidean costs; hold initialization, reconstruction scaling, schedule, and Stage3 fixed. Measure the same four required outcomes.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the real frozen q_i, 32 first-level groups, and the specified Poincare coupled assignment; produce all four-token SIDs and evaluate the same canonical ER, width-20 survival at depths 1-4, T4 NLL, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10.
SPEC EQUATIONS: C_ij=sum_{u,t in TRAIN}1[i_{u,t}=i,i_{u,t+1}=j]; pi_j=(sum_i C_ij+eps)/(sum_{i,j}C_ij+N*eps); q_ij=(C_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(sum_l C_il+alpha). Let h_i=Proj(topM(q_i)); x_i=exp_0^c(Wz_i), y_i=exp_0^c(Vh_i). For g=1..32, J_i(g)=d_c(x_i,a_g)^2+lambda*d_c(y_i,a_g)^2; g_i=argmin_g J_i(g). Apply the baseline straight-through quantizer/reconstruction update to selected a_g and add gamma*J_i(g_i), then quantize residuals in L2/L3 using baseline rules; T4 is deterministic collision extension only. Log per-level NLL, prefix survival and assignments, without tuning on test outcomes.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: train-only ordered user interaction sequences, frozen item IDs/embeddings, encoder output z_i, 32 first-level anchors, fixed projection and curvature c, alpha/eps/M/lambda/gamma. Outputs: cached q_i/h_i, first-level assignments and quantized vectors, baseline L2/L3 residual assignments, deterministic T4 extension, and the global item-to-SID map.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute counts/probabilities in float64 or stable float32; smooth zero-count rows to pi, clip and renormalize top-M distributions, and use a fixed projection with bounded norm. Use float32 Poincare operations, project norms below (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), clamp atanh arguments below 1-1e-5 and denominators away from zero, check finite losses/gradients, and clip gradients. Match behavior/geometric cost scales on TRAIN batches only; deterministic tie breaks and fixed initialization across arms. Never use T4 learned loss to hide prefix failures; if T4 exceeds the stated near-constant diagnostic, report the hypothesis contradicted rather than tuning using test data.
END IDEA
