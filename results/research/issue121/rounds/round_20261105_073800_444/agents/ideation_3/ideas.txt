IDEA: GEOCAP-RQ
TITLE: Geodesic capacity cells: hyperbolic area-law radii turn behaviour demand into a per-codeword offset on an untouched Poincare codebook.
MOTIVATION: The RQ-VAE codebooks behind the Semantic IDs collapse - the earlier round measured l2_distinct_prefixes=1, l3_collision=1.0 and l3_refinement_gain_bits=0, so the SID carries information only through the collision-extension token and the generative recommender has almost no useful prefix to condition on. Every behaviour change tried so far moved codeword positions or the residual, which fights the reconstruction objective and re-collapses. The actual recommendation problem is that prefix cells do not reflect how transition mass really flows: items whose successors are specific and diverse deserve finer, better separated SIDs, while a hub that absorbs everything should not swallow a whole level.
NOVELTY: Not a behaviour-ranked position (BRad-RQ), not a per-level curvature controller (CURVCTRL-SID), not a behaviour radius that moves the codeword radially (HYPVOLCAP-style), not parallel transport (holonomy-path-behaviour-rq). GEOCAP-RQ never moves or ranks a codeword by behaviour: codeword positions stay whatever the reconstruction objective wants, and curvature sizes the CELL - a Poincare Laguerre (power) cell whose additive offset is the geodesic radius that the hyperbolic disk-area law assigns to that codeword's behaviour demand, closed-loop with the model's own soft assignment and re-centred to zero mean per level so total cell area is conserved rather than inflated. Curvature is load-bearing twice: it is the metric of the assignment distance and it is the demand->radius law (arccosh growth instead of sqrt growth).
CHANGE: Assignment uses d_c(u,q_{l,k}) - (rho_{l,k} - h), the geodesic distance to codeword k minus that codeword's own geodesic capacity radius, where rho_{l,k} = area-law inverse of a behaviour-weighted soft mass; the decoder and the residual use the exact chart image a_{l,k} = sigma_c^{-1}(q_{l,k}) of the same array, so nothing is compared across charts.
SPECIFICATION: (1) One learnable array A in R^{L x K x d} (ambient codebook), L=3 levels, K codes/level. Fixed chart sigma_c(v) = (0.95/sqrt(c)) * tanh(sqrt(c)||v||/s) * v/||v||, exact inverse sigma_c^{-1}(q) = (s/sqrt(c)) * artanh(sqrt(c)||q||/0.95) * q/||q||, s = RMS norm of item embeddings measured once on the training split, c=1. q_{l,k} = sigma_c(a_{l,k}). (2) Distance d_c(u,q) = (1/sqrt(c))*arccosh(1 + 2c||u-q||^2/((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||q||^2))), evaluated between u_{l,i}=sigma_c(r_{l-1,i}) and q_{l,k}. (3) Behaviour demand: b_i given per training item (formula below), soft assignment S_{l,i,k}=softmax_k(-D_{l,i,k}/tau_i) with tau_i = mean_k D_{l,i,k}, raw_{l,k} = sum_{i in batch} b_i S_{l,i,k}, EMA with beta=1-1/96, normalised to mean 1 -> bhat_{l,k}. (4) rho_{l,k} = (1/sqrt(c))*arccosh(1 + c*A_0*bhat_{l,k}/(2*pi)) with A_0 = (2*pi/c)*(cosh(sqrt(c)*h)-1), h = median inter-codeword Poincare distance measured at initialisation; clip rho to [0, 0.95/sqrt(c)]. (5) score_{l,i,k} = D_{l,i,k} - (rho_{l,k} - h); k_l = argmin_k; r_l = r_{l-1} - a_{l,k_l}; decoder input = sum_l a_{l,k_l}. (6) Loss unchanged (reconstruction + commitment + codebook); no new loss term.
COST: No new learnable parameters (rho is derived, not trained); one extra float vector of size L*K = 768. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch -> ~1023 i

tems/step, L=3, K=256): the 3*1023*256 = 786k distances already exist in the baseline and only gain ~6 flops each (norms are precomputed per item and per code); 3 arccosh per codeword per step (768 arccosh), one softmax-per-item over K (same cost class as the existing logits), one 768-float EMA update, 768 arccosh for the radius. That is under +3% per step of the quantizer and far less of the encoder/decoder step, i.e. about +1-2% wall clock over the 3000-epoch Stage 2, and no change to Stage 3 cost.
HYPOTHESIS: If the level collapse is caused by cells that ignore how transition mass actually distributes, then capacity-sized geodesic cells will keep every level alive and give the generative recommender usable prefixes: with both switches on, the single Stage 3 run must reach test Recall@10 > 0.065, and its recorded diagnostics must show level-2 distinct prefixes >= 8 (not 1), l3_collision clearly below 1.0 and per-level usage entropy at least 20% of log K. If the run lands at or below the 0.0555 baseline, or the prefixes again collapse to one code per level, the mechanism is rejected in one run with no ablation needed.
RISKS: The EMA demand can freeze the initial random tessellation, so early offsets may lag the codebook; the z-scored behaviour mass may be dominated by catalogue hubs and speak mostly popularity even after the PMI correction; arccosh saturation makes the hyperbolic radii nearly equal for large bhat, so the offset may be too weak to move argmin on a small K; if the collapse actually starts in the encoder (not in the codebook), an offset-only fix cannot restore the prefixes; the c->0 arm carries the factor 2 of the distance limit, which changes the scale but not the loss balance; the collision-extension token may still absorb all residual discrimination, making recall unchanged even with healthy prefixes.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed item transitions from the training split only - specifically the item-level transition salience b_i, aggregated per (level, codeword) into the soft demand bhat_{l,k}. It is a per-codeword vector, never a per-row scalar, so it can change the argmin.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Built once before training from training sequences only: C[i,j] = number of directed adjacent pairs (i -> j, window 1, same session) in the train split; P[i,j] = C[i,j]/sum_j C[i,j]; pop[j] = sum_i C[i,j]/sum_{i,j} C[i,j]; M[i] = sum_j P[i,j]*relu(log(P[i,j]/pop[j])) + sum_j P[j,i]*relu(log(P[j,i]/pop[i])) (positive-PMI transition salience, so items whose continuations are specific count more than merely popular ones); m[i] = clip((M[i]-mean_Train)/std_Train, -2, +2); b[i] = exp(m[i])/mean_Train(exp(m)). Frozen for the whole run; at evaluation bhat is frozen at its last training value and no evaluation item ever contributes to it.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: b_i is a function of the train split alone (transition counts and catalogue popularity computed before the first gradient step), and bhat is an EMA over training batches only, frozen before any validation/test item is encoded. Validation/test interactions never enter sigma_c, h, A_0, b, or bhat, and the SID construction only ever sees training items; the evaluation split is used after SIDs exist, for metrics only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance with fixed unit negative curvature c=1, d_c(u,q)=(1/sqrt(c))*arccosh(1+2c||u-q||^2/((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||q||^2))), plus the hyperbolic disk-area law A_c(rho)=(2*pi/c)(cosh(sqrt(c)*rho)-1) inverted to rho(A)=

(1/sqrt(c))*arccosh(1+cA/(2*pi)) - the radius is a geodesic length under the same c.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Entirely inside quantisation, at every RQ level and every training step: the curvature sets (a) the metric in which the residual point is compared with every codeword and (b) the law that converts a behaviour demand (a mass) into a geodesic radius (a length). It acts on assignment and on the codebook geometry, not as a separate loss.
COUPLING PATH: one circuit: training transitions -> b_i -> behaviour-weighted soft mass bhat_{l,k} (computed with the Poincare distances) -> hyperbolic area law -> geodesic radius rho_{l,k} -> additive offset (rho_{l,k}-h) on the Poincare distance of the SAME codeword -> argmin -> which codeword the decoder receives -> reconstruction gradient on a_{l,k} -> new positions -> new distances -> new bhat. Removing behaviour sets b_i = 1, bhat becomes uniform and the offset is exactly zero (plain Poincare RQ-VAE); removing curvature turns both the metric and the area law Euclidean, giving a sqrt-law power cell. Either removal changes the argmin path itself, not a side term.
COUPLING EQUATION: score_{l,i,k} = d_c( sigma_c(r_{l-1,i}) , sigma_c(a_{l,k}) ) - [ rho_c( A_0 * bhat_{l,k}(b_i, d_c) ) - h ], with rho_c(A) = (1/sqrt(c)) arccosh(1 + cA/(2pi)) and bhat_{l,k} = normalise( EMA[ sum_i b_i * softmax_k(-d_c(u_{l,i},q_{l,k}) / tau_i) ] ).  Behaviour term: bhat_{l,k}. Curved terms: d_c and rho_c. The offset is a geodesic length, so its coefficient is 1 by dimensionality.
OBSERVABLES: Per-level codeword usage entropy and distinct-prefix counts on the training catalogue and on the evaluation item set; l3_collision; l3_refinement_gain_bits; correlation between bhat_{l,k} and the realised cell load (a behaviour-following cell should track its demand); prefix transition entropy of the SID sequences (behaviour-conditioned prefix predictability); Stage 3 validation and test Recall@10 of the single run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: b[i] = 1 for every training item, so bhat_{l,k} is uniform, rho_{l,k} = h for all k and the offset vanishes identically: the arm is exactly the unweighted Poincare RQ-VAE with the same sigma_c, same c, same h. Implemented as a config flag feeding a constant behaviour vector; not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c -> 0 taken as the exact continuous limit in the same code path: sigma becomes the linear map 0.95 v/s, d becomes 2||u-q||, and the area law becomes A = pi*rho^2 with rho = sqrt(A/pi) and A_0 = pi*h^2; the behaviour demand is unchanged. This is a Euclidean power-cell (Laguerre) RQ-VAE with the same behaviour weighting. Flag-switched; not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: c = 1, b_i from training transitions, the full coupled run above - the one and only run of this candidate (3000 Stage 2 epochs on four cards, frozen Stage 3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep, no second seed).
SPEC EQUATIONS: precompute (train only): C[i,j] adjacent directed pairs; P[i,j]=C[i,j]/sum_j C[i,j]; pop[j]=colsum(C)/sum(C); M[i]=sum_j P[i,j]*relu(log(P[i,j]/pop[j])) + sum_j P[j,i]*relu(log(P[j,i]/pop[i])); m=clip(zscore_Train(M),-2,2); b=exp(m)/mean_Train(exp(m)). init: s=RMS||z||_Train; h=median_{k!=k'} d_c(sigma_c(a_{l,k}),sigma_c(a_{l,k'})) averaged over l; A0=(2pi/c)(cosh(sqrt(c)h)-1); EMA_{l,k}=1. step, for l=1..L: u_{l,i}=sigma_c(r_{l-1,i}); D_{l,i,k}=(1/sqrt(c))arccosh(1+2c||u_{l,i}-q_{l,k}||^2/((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||q||^2))); tau_i=mean_k D_{l,i,k}; S=softmax_k(-D/tau_i); raw_{l,k}=sum_{i

in B} b_i S_{l,i,k}; EMA_{l,k}=(1-beta)EMA_{l,k}+beta*raw_{l,k}; bhat_{l,k}=EMA_{l,k}/mean_k(EMA_{l,.}); rho_{l,k}=clip((1/sqrt(c))arccosh(1+c*A0*bhat_{l,k}/(2pi)), 0, 0.95/sqrt(c)); delta_{l,k}=rho_{l,k}-h; k_l=argmin_k (D_{l,i,k}-delta_{l,k}); r_l=r_{l-1}-a_{l,k_l}; out: decoder(x)=Dec(sum_l a_{l,k_l}); loss=L_recon+L_commit+L_code (unchanged); rho, delta carry no gradient (detached) so they only steer the argmin.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item embeddings z (batch of ~1023), codebook parameters A (L x K x d, K=256), frozen behaviour vector b (training catalogue), constants s, h, A0, c=1, beta=1-1/96. Outputs: per-item SID (k_1..k_L plus the baseline collision-extension token), the quantised vector sum_l a_{l,k_l} for the decoder, the per-step demand vector bhat (L x K) and the per-level offsets delta (L x K) for logging.
SAFEGUARDS: c||q||^2 <= 0.95^2 = 0.9025 and c||u||^2 <= 0.9025 by construction of sigma_c, so (1-c||u||^2) >= 0.0975; still clamp both denominators to >= 1e-3 before the division; clamp the arccosh argument to >= 1+1e-9; clamp rho to [0, 0.95/sqrt(c)]; require ||v|| > 1e-6 in sigma_c and use sigma_c(0)=0; clip the behaviour z-score at +-2 before exponentiation and normalise b to mean 1 over the training catalogue so bhat stays O(1); detach rho and delta from the graph so the offsets can never produce a runaway gradient (the previous round's 3.7e13 blow-up was a gradient through a geometry term); re-centre delta to zero mean per level each step so a uniform demand is a strict no-op; assert at every 100 steps that at least 2 codewords per level are selected, logging only (never a gate); if h is degenerate (zero) fall back to h = median of all pairwise distances in the first batch.
END IDEA
