IDEA: flow_barycenter_geodesic_bottleneck
TITLE: Successor-flow barycentres anchor a hyperbolic coarse-code bottleneck
MOTIVATION: The useful regime appears to require many items to share a coarse first token while retaining item identity in the final token. Items with overlapping training-only next-item destinations should therefore share a behavior-defined coarse anchor, but content geometry should still decide which anchor a latent actually uses.
NOVELTY: Unlike the other ideas' fixed graph partitions, trees, shells, or behavior-restricted candidate sets, this uses each item's soft successor-flow barycentre as a per-item hyperbolic translation of its latent before one unrestricted global first-level quantizer. The behavior signal continuously moves the point being quantized; it does not prescribe a discrete parent or add a separate behavior loss.
CHANGE: Form a smoothed successor distribution for each training item, map that distribution to the same latent space as item vectors, and use it as a Poincare tangent displacement before first-level assignment. Compute levels two and three from the original latent minus the selected coarse code vector, so the coarse behavior displacement cannot erase the identity-bearing residual input.
SPECIFICATION: From training transitions define p_i(j)=(n_ij+q_j)/(out_i+1), where q_j is the training-split item unigram probability. Let v_i be the normalized weighted mean of frozen input embeddings x_j under p_i, with weight p_i(j); center v_i by the training-item mean and scale it by the ratio of median encoder-latent norm to median nonzero centered-v norm. Let b_i be that scaled tangent vector. With curvature -c, project encoder output z_i and trainable first codewords e_k into the tangent ball and form y_i=exp_0^c(z_i ⊕_c b_i), where ⊕_c is Mobius addition. Choose q1(i)=argmin_k d_c(y_i,exp_0^c(e_k))^2, then quantize r_i=z_i-e_q1(i) with the unchanged Euclidean second and third RQ levels. The first-level codebook remains the existing size; all coarse structure arises from translated points aggregating around behavior-derived successor barycentres.
COST: One sparse transition aggregation and one weighted-embedding barycentre per item are computed once. Training adds a barycentre lookup, one Mobius addition and exp-map, and the existing first-level Poincare codebook comparisons per item; no extra optimizer step or all-pairs operation. At the frozen scale this is 24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps over 24,556 items (about 1,023 items/step), with constant-factor overhead limited to first-level geometry.
HYPOTHESIS: If training successor-flow barycentres align with useful continuation roles, the translated points will make first-level assignments coarser and more behavior-predictive while original-latent residuals preserve the third digit. Keep only if the sole frozen Stage3 run has test Recall@10 > 0.065; record assignment changes, prefix predictability, and third-level distinct triples as diagnostics only.
RISKS: Successor barycentres may mostly encode popularity or be noisy for rare items; embedding averages can cancel when destinations are heterogeneous; translation may push points near the ball boundary or dominate content; unchanged residual quantization may not recover identity if first-code reconstruction gradients become poorly calibrated.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item smoothed directed next-item destination distribution, represented by its weighted successor-embedding barycentre.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count adjacent i→j transitions separately within each user's Stage2 training sequence. Set n_ij to the aggregated training count, q_j to the training-only destination unigram probability, and p_i(j)=(n_ij+q_j)/(sum_j n_ij+1). Compute v_i=sum_j p_i(j)x_j; center over training items. Zero-outdegree items use q_j directly. No validation or test events enter these quantities.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transition counts, destination frequencies, barycentres, centering/scaling statistics, and all exported SIDs are constructed from the Stage2 training split and frozen training item embeddings only. No validation/test interactions or metrics are read during this construction or Stage2 optimization; evaluation follows the frozen Stage3 protocol.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Mobius addition ⊕_c, origin exponential map exp_0^c, and Poincare geodesic distance d_c at negative curvature -c; c is derived as the inverse mean squared norm of projected training latents, with a positive numerical floor.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The first-level forward assignment path: behavior barycentre translates each latent in the curved ball, and geodesic distance selects its first SID token. The residual sent to levels 2–3 is computed from the unshifted latent to retain identity information.
COUPLING PATH: Training-only successor counts determine b_i; b_i Mobius-translates z_i into y_i; Poincare distance from y_i to each first-level codeword determines q1, its quantization gradient, and hence the first prefix. Removing behavior sets b_i=0; removing geometry replaces Mobius translation and Poincare distance by ordinary addition and squared Euclidean distance, changing this same assignment computation.
COUPLING EQUATION: p_i(j)=(n_ij+q_j)/(out_i+1); b_i=s(v_i-mean(v)); y_i=exp_0^c(z_i ⊕_c b_i); q1(i)=argmin_k d_c(y_i,exp_0^c(e_k))^2. The behavior term is b_i from the training successor distribution; the curved terms are ⊕_c, exp_0^c, and d_c.
OBSERVABLES: First-level assignments and their gradients should change, with items sharing successor barycentres more often sharing a coarse code; the original-latent residual path should keep third-level triples informative. Record first-level assignment/occupancy, training-only next-item predictability, and third-level distinct triples, but judge solely by Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set b_i=0 for every item while retaining the Poincare first-level assignment and all residual levels. This counterfactual switch is implemented but not trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace y_i=exp_0^c(z_i ⊕_c b_i) and the Poincare distance with y_i=z_i+b_i and squared Euclidean distance to e_k; preserve the same behavior barycentres and residual path. This counterfactual switch is implemented but not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-derived successor barycentres as Mobius translations and Poincare geodesic first-level assignment; this is the only candidate run, trained once under the frozen budget.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=sum_{u,t in train}1[i_{u,t}=i,i_{u,t+1}=j]; q_j=(destination_count_j+1)/(total_destination_count+M); p_i(j)=(n_ij+q_j)/(out_i+1); v_i=sum_j p_i(j)x_j; b_i=s(v_i-mean_train(v)); s=median_train(||z||)/(median_train_nonzero(||v_i-mean_train(v)||)+epsilon); c=1/(mean_train||z||^2+epsilon); y_i=exp_0^c(z_i ⊕_c b_i); q1=argmin_k d_c(y_i,exp_0^c(e_k))^2; r_i=z_i-e_q1; (q2,q3)=baseline_RQ(r_i). Use the baseline RQ-VAE reconstruction/commitment objective with its first-level assignment error measured on y_i; do not add or tune a behavioral loss coefficient.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training-only ordered interactions, frozen item embeddings x_j, Stage2 encoder latents z_i, and the existing three-level RQ-VAE codebooks. Outputs are frozen successor barycentres/scaling, learned first-level assignments, original-latent residual assignments at levels two and three, and full SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Use epsilon=1e-8 in count and scale denominators; use float32 for barycentre reductions and float64 for scale statistics. Center barycentres before scaling; if their median norm is zero, set all b_i=0 deterministically. Derive c and s from training-latent statistics only; cap tangent vectors and ball points to norm at most (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), stabilize Mobius denominators and clamp acosh arguments to at least 1+1e-7. Use baseline gradient clipping, finite checks, deterministic lowest-index ties, and preserve original z_i for later residual quantization.
END IDEA
