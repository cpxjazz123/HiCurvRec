IDEA: mobius-nextstep-drift
TITLE: Successor-conditioned Möbius drift in the quantized latent
MOTIVATION: An item’s useful identity for next-item prediction includes the direction of its training-observed successors, not only its content or the number of successors it has.
NOVELTY: Rather than placing successor-set hierarchy on a radial coordinate or contrasting successor-sharing items, this trains the item representation to predict the *tangent direction* of its outgoing transitions using a curved displacement. The direction is computed from training transitions and is coupled directly to a Poincaré displacement loss.
CHANGE: Map encoder latents into a Poincaré ball and learn a small tangent-space transition predictor. For each item, regress its predicted tangent vector toward the mean log-map displacement to its observed successors; use the predicted vector to define a successor-directed curved residual that also enters that item’s quantization input.
SPECIFICATION: For source item i, let S(i) be its training-only successors and m_i their mean tangent displacement at z_i. Predict v_i = f(z_i); set q_i = exp_{z_i}(v_i). Form the quantizer input as r_i = log_{z_i}(q_i) + log_{z_i}(z_i) = v_i, with the assignment score d_P(q_i,c_k)^2 + d_P(exp_{z_i}(m_i),c_k)^2 averaged over successors. More practically, use the single per-item score D(i,k)=d_P(exp_{z_i}(v_i),c_k)^2 + mean_{j∈S(i)}d_P(exp_{z_i}(m_i),c_k)^2; assign by argmin D. Train encoder/predictor/codebooks with the usual reconstruction and commitment terms plus this assignment-path loss. Switches: behavior-off sets m_i=0 and removes the successor average; geometry-off replaces exp/log and d_P by Euclidean addition and distance; both-on uses the stated equations. No switch arm is trained.
COST: One training run at 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs. Precompute successor means once; per step adds O(B·d) tangent prediction and O(B·K·d) curved distances if folded into the existing B-by-K assignment matrix. No extra epochs or runs; likely modest wall-clock increase over baseline assignment.
HYPOTHESIS: Items with similar successor direction but distinct successor sets become easier for the generative model to continue from, improving Stage3 test Recall@10. Keep only if the single frozen-protocol run exceeds baseline Recall@10=0.055537; record transition-direction prediction and Stage3 recall as diagnostics, not gates.
RISKS: Sparse or noisy successor sets give unstable means; averaging tangent vectors may hide multimodal successors; quantizer assignment may overfit frequent transitions; curved distance computation may raise Stage2 cost.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive item pairs (i,j) within each user’s training sequence only. For each i with successors, compute m_i = mean_j log^P_{z_i}(z_j), weighting each distinct successor equally to avoid letting repeated popular transitions dominate.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build transitions from the training split only, before Stage2 fitting. Do not read validation/test sequences or their derived statistics when constructing S(i), m_i, assignments, or IDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré log map, exp map, and geodesic distance at fixed negative curvature −c, where c is the documented baseline curvature; use the same c for all items.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the Stage2 encoder-to-quantizer pathway: successor displacements are measured at the encoded item point, and the resulting curved successor target contributes to the codeword assignment score.
COUPLING PATH: Training transitions determine m_i; m_i is mapped through exp_{z_i} to a curved successor target, whose geodesic distance to each codeword changes the item’s assignment and its encoder/codebook gradients.
COUPLING EQUATION: D(i,k)=d_P(exp_{z_i}(f(z_i)),c_k)^2 + β_i·mean_{j∈S(i)}d_P(exp_{z_i}(m_i),c_k)^2, where β_i=1 if S(i) is nonempty and 0 otherwise; β_i is a data-availability mask, not a tuned weight.
OBSERVABLES: On the full coupled run, inspect changed SID assignments and transition-direction prediction diagnostics; the decision observable is Stage3 test Recall@10. Behaviour-off removes successor-conditioned assignment changes; geometry-off replaces the curved targets and distances with Euclidean ones; both-on is the proposed run.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: In code, set the transition target to zero and omit the successor-target distance term, leaving the same predictor/quantizer path otherwise; do not train this arm.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: In code, use Euclidean displacement, addition, and squared distance in place of Poincaré log/exp/distance; do not train this arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-derived m_i, Poincaré log/exp/distance, and D(i,k) as specified; train this sole candidate.
SPEC EQUATIONS: z_i=proj_B(exp_0^P(h_i)); m_i=mean_{j∈S(i)}log^P_{z_i}(z_j); v_i=f_θ(z_i); q_i=exp^P_{z_i}(v_i); D(i,k)=d_P(q_i,c_k)^2+β_i mean_{j∈S(i)}d_P(exp^P_{z_i}(m_i),c_k)^2; k_i*=argmin_k D(i,k). Backpropagate the existing RQ-VAE losses through the selected assignments and this score.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are item encoder output h_i, training-only successor adjacency S(i), curvature c, and existing codebook C. Output is the assignment index (and thus the SID) plus gradients for encoder, transition predictor, and codebook.
SAFEGUARDS: Project points to radius at most (1−ε)/√c; clamp distance denominators and arcosh arguments; skip empty successor means; average unique successors; normalize or norm-clip m_i using a scale derived from the minibatch median nonzero tangent norm, with the clip bound fixed from that measured scale; use the documented baseline c and no searched constants.
END IDEA

IDEA: branch-tangent-barycenter
TITLE: Hyperbolic branch barycentres for user-interest branching
MOTIVATION: A user can follow different next-item branches from the same recent context. A single averaged Euclidean transition target can blur those alternatives, weakening the prefix representations a generative recommender must continue.
NOVELTY: This uses per-item successor *branch structure* to construct a negatively curved barycentre and trains codeword assignments against branch-specific curved targets. It differs from successor-count radial hierarchy and positive-pair angular coherence: the target is a set of branch prototypes, and their curved barycentre directly participates in quantization.
CHANGE: Cluster each training item’s successor embeddings into a small, deterministic set of branch prototypes, then compute their Poincaré Fréchet barycentre. Condition the item’s assignment score on its distance to each branch prototype, with branch weights derived from training transition counts.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i, deterministically partition its distinct training successors into at most min(4, number of successors) groups by spherical k-means on their initial encoder embeddings (fixed item-ID ordering for initialization; no tuning). Let prototypes p_ib be the Poincaré Fréchet means of each group, and weights π_ib be the group’s share of distinct successors. Score D(i,k)=d_P(z_i,c_k)^2 + Σ_b π_ib d_P(c_k,p_ib)^2. The behaviour term changes per-codeword scores and therefore assignments; when behavior is off omit the second term, when geometry is off use Euclidean means/distances, and both-on uses Poincaré means/distances. These are code switches only; train the both-on candidate once.
COST: Precompute successor groups and Fréchet means once from the training graph and initial embeddings. Per Stage2 step adds O(B·K·G·d) distance work for G≤4 branch prototypes; 24 steps/epoch × 3,000 epochs on 24,556 items, with no extra training run. Memory is O(N·G·d) for cached prototypes.
HYPOTHESIS: Codewords become useful identifiers for distinct continuation branches, allowing Stage3 prefix decoding to preserve alternative next-item directions. The sole keep/drop criterion is whether the frozen Stage3 run exceeds Recall@10=0.055537; log assignment and branch diagnostics only.
RISKS: Arbitrary grouping can split semantically similar successors or merge distinct branches; rare items have noisy prototypes; the unweighted codeword-to-branch term may compete with reconstruction; branch prototypes from initial embeddings may be poor.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: User-interest branching represented by distinct successors of an item.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From consecutive item pairs in training user sequences, gather distinct successors per source item; partition each source’s successors deterministically into up to four groups, and use group successor proportions as π_ib.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The graph and successor groups use training interactions only. Validation/test interactions are excluded from graph construction, clustering, prototype computation, and Stage2 assignment; Stage3 test is used only for final Recall@10.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré geodesic distance and iterative Fréchet barycentre under fixed negative curvature −c.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage2 codeword assignment: branch barycentres are curved targets and their geodesic distances to candidate codewords alter the assignment score.
COUPLING PATH: Training-only successor branches yield weighted Poincaré barycentres; candidate codewords are scored by geodesic distance to those barycentres, so behavior and curvature jointly change assignments and downstream gradients.
COUPLING EQUATION: D(i,k)=d_P(z_i,c_k)^2 + Σ_b π_ib·d_P(c_k, μ^P(S_ib))^2, where S_ib is a training successor branch and μ^P its Poincaré Fréchet mean.
OBSERVABLES: Inspect whether assignments differ under the in-code behavior-off and Euclidean controls; record branch coverage and prefix continuation diagnostics. Judge the both-on candidate only by frozen Stage3 test Recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Omit branch prototypes and use D(i,k)=d_P(z_i,c_k)^2; code path exists but this arm is not trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Compute Euclidean group means and Euclidean distances in the same score; code path exists but this arm is not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only branch groups, their Poincaré Fréchet means, and the stated geodesic assignment score; this is the sole trained run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: S(i)=distinct training successors; partition S(i)=⋃_b S_ib; π_ib=|S_ib|/|S(i)|; μ_ib^P=argmin_{x∈B_c}Σ_{j∈S_ib}d_P(x,z_j)^2; D(i,k)=d_P(z_i,c_k)^2+Σ_bπ_ib d_P(c_k,μ_ib^P)^2; k_i*=argmin_k D(i,k). Compute μ by a fixed number of log-map mean updates, with iteration count set by the existing baseline implementation’s standard numerical tolerance/limit.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training successor lists, initial item embeddings, quantizer codebooks, fixed curvature c, and encoded z_i. Outputs are cached branch weights/prototypes and per-item codeword assignments.
SAFEGUARDS: Project points into the open ball with radius margin ε; clamp norms before log/exp and clamp distance arguments; skip items without successors; use deterministic clustering initialization and stable item ordering; use a fixed convergence tolerance and maximum iterations documented in code; cap G at four as a computational bound, not a sweep.
END IDEA
