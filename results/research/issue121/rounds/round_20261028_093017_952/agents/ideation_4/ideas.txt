IDEA: successor_transport_curvature
TITLE: Curvature-aware transport of successor distributions into the item latent
MOTIVATION: An item may have several plausible next items; encoding only its visual/content reconstruction can erase which successors are likely, while a single successor centroid can collapse branching.
NOVELTY: Unlike a radial successor-hierarchy plus angular sibling-coherence objective, this uses the full directed successor distribution as a target measure and couples its mass directly to hyperbolic distances from the current item to candidate successor representations. It does not append behaviour to an unchanged quantizer.
CHANGE: Build a training-only, popularity-corrected transition distribution per item. Train the encoder so its Poincare representation induces a temperature-scaled geodesic successor distribution matching that target; the same representation is consumed by the unchanged RQ-VAE quantizer after mapping to its baseline latent interface.
SPECIFICATION: From training-only consecutive item pairs, let n_ij be transition counts and d_j the destination occurrence count. Define q(j|i) proportional to (n_ij + alpha * p0(j)) / sqrt(1+d_j), restricted to observed destinations and normalized; alpha=1 and p0 is the empirical training destination distribution. For a minibatch source i, sample/retain up to K=all observed successors if at most 32, otherwise the 32 largest q values and renormalize. Map encoder output z_i to x_i=exp_0^(-1)(?); concretely use x_i=exp_0(s*z_i) in curvature -1 ball, with s=1/sqrt(mean_train ||z||^2) measured once from the initial training encoder pass, and project norm below 1-1e-5. For candidate successor j use y_j from the same encoder. p_H(j|i)=softmax_j(-d_H(x_i,y_j)^2/tau), tau=median of positive pairwise squared distances in the first training batch (stop-gradient, floor 1e-6). Minimize KL(q(.|i)||p_H(.|i)) alongside baseline reconstruction/quantization losses; each batch's candidate set is the union of positives and an equal number of training-only in-batch non-successors. The geometry-off switch replaces d_H by squared Euclidean distance on tangent vectors log_0(x), log_0(y); behaviour-off sets this KL term to zero.
COST: Transition table preprocessing O(number of training transitions), stored sparse. Per step at frozen scale (24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs): encoder already processes the 24,556-item training set as baseline; at most 32 successor and 32 negative comparisons per source item, O(B*64*D) distances and one small softmax/KL, no extra encoder pass if candidate representations are cached per epoch with gradients refreshed only for current minibatch candidates. Expected modest overhead, approximately one additional batched distance-and-softmax operation per step; total 72,000 steps.
HYPOTHESIS: A successor distribution shaped by geodesic neighbourhoods will make the quantized item representation retain branching transition structure useful to Stage3 next-item decoding. Keep only if the single frozen Stage3 run has test Recall@10 > 0.065; record transition predictability and Recall, with no diagnostic admission threshold.
RISKS: Sparse or highly popular transitions can make q noisy; sampled candidates may omit useful alternatives; representation distances may overfit transition rather than content; the fixed 32 cap can truncate long tails.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate item-to-item transition counts, popularity-adjusted.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only, count adjacent items within each user's chronologically ordered training sequence; estimate destination popularity from those same training interactions and normalize the smoothed inverse-square-root-popularity counts into q(j|i). No validation or test sequences are read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Freeze q before training from training-split interactions only. Cache contains source/destination training item IDs and counts, never future interactions; validation/test are used only by the frozen Stage3 evaluator and cannot affect SID construction, candidate sampling, normalization, or constants.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance squared d_{-1}(x,y)^2, curvature c=-1, with exp_0 projection and norm clipping.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage2 encoder representation training, inside the behaviour-conditioned successor-distribution logits, before the standard RQ-VAE quantization and SID export.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition counts define q, q supervises logits computed from geodesic distances between encoded source and successor items, and those same trained encoder outputs feed RQ-VAE quantization; removing the behaviour target removes the distributional gradient, while replacing the metric changes the logits and gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_succ(i)=KL(q_train(.|i) || softmax_{j in C_i}[-d_{-1}(x_i,y_j)^2/tau]); x_i=exp_0(s z_i). Behaviour-off: L_succ=0. Geometry-off: d_{-1}(exp_0(sz_i),exp_0(sz_j)) replaced by ||log_0(x_i)-log_0(y_j)||_2. Both-on: use the displayed equation.
OBSERVABLES: Encoder gradients and updates become directed-successor-distribution sensitive; encoded successor ranking/predictability should change; the sole outcome judgement is Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: In-code switch disables q and its KL contribution, leaving the baseline RQ-VAE encoder/quantizer objective; defined for comparison only, not trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: In-code switch uses tangent-space Euclidean distance for the same q, candidate set, temperature, and loss; defined for comparison only, not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: One candidate run uses training-only q and the curvature -1 Poincare distance together in successor logits; no ablation arm is run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=count_train(i->j); q_ij=normalize_j[(n_ij+p0(j))/sqrt(1+d_j)]. x_i=Proj_{||x||<1}(exp_0(s z_i)); d_{-1}(x,y)=arcosh(1+2||x-y||^2/((1-||x||^2)(1-||y||^2))). L=L_RQVAE+KL(q_i||softmax_{j in C_i}(-d_{-1}(x_i,y_j)^2/tau)). Use at most 32 observed successors and an equal number of training in-batch non-successors; derive s from initial-pass latent RMS and tau from first-batch positive distance median, then freeze both.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are current minibatch latent vectors, cached training-only sparse transition table, training item frequencies, and candidate item vectors. Output is scalar successor KL added to baseline Stage2 loss plus unchanged RQ-VAE SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp squared norms to at most 1-1e-5 before distance evaluation; clamp arcosh argument to at least 1+1e-7; compute arcosh stably; tau floor 1e-6; detach the one-time scale/temperature estimates; ignore sources with no observed outgoing transition; use finite-value checks and gradient clipping already present in baseline.
END IDEA

IDEA: branch_tangent_parallel_transport
TITLE: Successor-branch tangent alignment by Poincare parallel transport
MOTIVATION: When a training item has distinct successor branches, a useful identifier should preserve their directional organization around the source, not merely put all successors close to one centre.
NOVELTY: This encodes branch arrangement through transported tangent vectors and pairwise signed angular relations at each source, rather than successor radial depth or global sibling contrastive coherence. The behavioural branch relation is computed with an explicit curved transport operation that affects the encoder gradients before quantization.
CHANGE: For each source with multiple observed successors, obtain source-to-successor logarithmic tangent vectors in the Poincare ball, parallel-transport them to the source tangent space, and align their pairwise cosine Gram matrix with a training-derived successor-branch similarity matrix. Thus two successors sharing downstream continuation are encouraged to form a related direction, while unrelated branches need not collapse.
SPECIFICATION: Build train-only transition graph counts. For each source i with at least two successors, define normalized successor weights q(j|i)=n_ij/sum_k n_ik. Define branch similarity B_i(j,k)=cosine of smoothed outgoing transition profiles of j and k, using one-step destination count vectors over training items with additive empirical-prior smoothing (one pseudo-count distributed by empirical destination frequency); clamp B to [0,1] and set diagonal to zero. For encoded x_i,x_j in curvature -1 Poincare ball, v_ij=PT_{x_j->x_i}(log_{x_j}(x_i)) with sign reversed as needed to represent the direction from i toward j; use the implementation convention v_ij=PT_{x_j->x_i}(log_{x_j}(x_i)) multiplied by -1. Normalize v with epsilon. Minimize weighted squared error between off-diagonal cosines of transported vectors and B, with pair weight q(j|i)q(k|i). To avoid ungrounded scaling, coefficient is one relative to this explicit normalized Gram loss; no tuned weight. Geometry-off replaces log/transport by Euclidean differences z_j-z_i. Behaviour-off sets B off-diagonal to zero and disables the term. Use a bounded random subset of at most 16 successor pairs per source, selected deterministically by highest q, to limit cost.
COST: Sparse graph/profile preprocessing O(training transitions plus nonzero profile intersections), cached once. At each step compute at most 16 successor log maps/transports and 120 pairwise cosines per qualifying source (B=number of source items in batch); O(B*16*D + B*120*D), with no extra network pass. Across 72,000 steps, one small geometry/Gram objective per minibatch; expected higher than a simple distance loss but bounded by the fixed 16-successor cap.
HYPOTHESIS: Branch continuation similarity has directional structure that next-item generation can exploit; success requires changed encoder gradients and better Stage3 generalization, judged solely by one frozen Stage3 test Recall@10 above 0.065. Record branch-structure prediction only as a diagnostic.
RISKS: One-hop successor profiles may be too sparse or fail to reflect semantic branches; transport gradients can become unstable near the ball boundary; branch similarity may impose a false smoothness prior; high-fanout sources are truncated.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Similarity of successor branches, derived from each successor item's own training-only outgoing transition profile.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: For training-only consecutive transitions count n_ij. Build each item's smoothed outgoing probability vector r_j over training destination IDs; B_i(j,k)=cosine(r_j,r_k), clamped to [0,1], for pairs of observed successors j,k of i. This uses the training split only and does not incorporate validation/test behaviour.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition counts, empirical prior, and all B matrices are built once from the training split and frozen. No validation/test user, item-transition, or held-out frequency enters preprocessing, minibatch pair selection, scale, gradients, or SID generation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare logarithmic map and parallel transport along the unique geodesic in curvature c=-1; pairwise cosine is computed between transported tangent vectors at the same point.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Stage2 encoder loss, after mapping each item latent into the Poincare ball and before RQ-VAE quantization; gradients update the exact representation quantized into SIDs.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition graph determines B_i(j,k) and successor weights; the target B is matched by pairwise inner products of hyperbolically logged and parallel-transported source-to-successor directions, thereby changing the shared item encoder representation used by the quantizer.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_branch(i)=sum_{j<k} q(j|i)q(k|i)[ <u_ij,u_ik> - B_i(j,k) ]^2, where u_ij=normalize(-PT_{x_j->x_i}(log_{x_j}(x_i))) and x_l=exp_0(s z_l). Behaviour-off: term disabled (or B=0 switch definition); geometry-off uses normalized Euclidean z_j-z_i directions. Both-on uses train-only B and hyperbolic transported directions.
OBSERVABLES: The encoder's directional gradient and item embeddings should reflect successor-branch relationships; measure branch-similarity prediction and parameter-update checks, but decide the candidate only by Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: In-code switch disables the branch loss (equivalently removes the train-only B target); baseline RQ-VAE objective remains. No such run is trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: In-code switch computes directions from Euclidean latent differences without log maps or transport, leaving the exact same B target and pair weighting. No such run is trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only branch similarities and Poincare log/parallel transport in the single candidate run; no ablation arm is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: r_j=normalize(n_{j*}+p0); B_i(j,k)=clip(cos(r_j,r_k),0,1), j!=k. x_l=Proj(exp_0(s z_l)); v_ij=-PT_{x_j->x_i}(log_{x_j}(x_i)); u_ij=v_ij/(||v_ij||+eps). L_branch=sum_i sum_{j<k}q_ij q_ik(<u_ij,u_ik>-B_i(j,k))^2. L_total=L_RQVAE+L_branch. Geometry-off: u_ij=normalize(z_j-z_i); behaviour-off: omit L_branch. s is inverse latent RMS measured on the initial training pass and frozen; no coefficient sweep.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are minibatch latents, train-only transition adjacency/counts, smoothed outgoing profiles, and candidate successors. Outputs are a scalar branch-geometry loss and gradients to encoder latents; standard RQ-VAE quantization emits the SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Project ball vectors to norm <=1-1e-5; clamp conformal denominators; use stable log/transport formulas with small-norm series branches; normalize tangent vectors with epsilon 1e-8; skip sources with fewer than two observed successors; clamp B to [0,1]; detach sparse targets; use at most 16 successors chosen by fixed count order; assert finite loss/gradients. Do not select parameters by validation or diagnostic results.
END IDEA
