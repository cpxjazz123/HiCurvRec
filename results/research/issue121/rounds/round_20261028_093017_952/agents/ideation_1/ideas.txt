IDEA: successor-shell-angle
TITLE: Successor branching shells with geodesic purchase-sibling contrast
MOTIVATION: Items with a narrow predictable continuation set should occupy a different radial scale from items with diffuse continuations, while items that lead to the same purchased successors should remain close in the representation used by the quantizer.
NOVELTY: This couples two complementary training-only views directly into the encoder representation read by RQ-VAE: a scale-free radial ordering signal and a geodesic angular positive-pair signal. It is not a behavior-biased code assignment or a standalone auxiliary loss on unrelated coordinates; both constraints act on one mapped latent before quantization.
CHANGE: Map each encoder latent to the curvature -1 Poincare ball, derive per-item continuation entropy from training-only purchase transitions, and train radial distances to correlate with that entropy. In the same ball representation, contrast each item against a real co-successor purchase sibling using Poincare geodesic distances; quantization and the ordinary reconstruction/VQ objective remain otherwise unchanged.
SPECIFICATION: From training histories only, count consecutive item transitions n_ij. Let p_ij=n_ij/sum_j n_ij, H_i=-sum_j p_ij log(p_ij+eps), and h_i=H_i/log(max(2,d_i)), where d_i is the number of distinct successors (set h_i=0 for d_i<2). For encoder z_i, x_i=exp^(-1)_0(z_i), r_i=d_P(0,x_i). Add L_rad=1-corr(r,h) over items with defined h, using centered vectors and eps-stabilized norms. Sample positive j for i with probability proportional to n_ij+n_ji among unordered pairs that share a successor (some k with n_ik>0 and n_jk>0), using only training transitions; negatives are other batch items. Add L_sib= -log[exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j)^2/tau)/(exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j)^2/tau)+sum_{m in N_i}exp(-d_P(x_i,x_m)^2/tau))]. Optimize L_base+L_rad+L_sib, with each auxiliary coefficient fixed to 1 after normalizing each loss by its own batch mean scale on the initial training batch (documented deterministic calibration, no sweep). Output standard RQ-VAE SIDs from x_i mapped back with log_0 before the unchanged Euclidean quantizer.
COST: One O(E_train) transition/sibling preprocessing pass and O(B^2 D) geodesic pair distances per step for batch size B≈24556/24≈1024; sparse positive lookup is O(B), and radial correlation is O(B). Across 24*3000=72,000 steps, the added pair-distance work is O(72,000*B^2*D), likely materially slower than baseline; implement chunked pairwise distance to bound memory, retaining exact batch objective.
HYPOTHESIS: Continuation entropy contains useful information about how an item should be represented, and shared-purchase-successor siblings identify predictive functional similarity. Keep only if the single frozen Stage3 test Recall@10 exceeds 0.065; log radius/entropy correlation and sibling geodesic separation as diagnostics, never as admission gates.
RISKS: Entropy may mostly encode popularity or noisy sparse tails; sibling positives can be false semantic neighbors; the radial statistic may conflict with reconstruction; full pairwise geodesics may exceed the wall-clock budget. A single run leaves seed uncertainty.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only normalized entropy of an item's directed successor distribution, plus shared-purchase-successor sibling identity.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Build counts n_ij from adjacent items in training-split user sequences only. Compute normalized outgoing entropy h_i as specified; enumerate or sample siblings i,j when their outgoing successor sets intersect, weighted by symmetric transition counts. Do not use validation/test histories.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table, entropy, and positive-pair pool are frozen from the training split before Stage2. Validation and test interactions are inaccessible to preprocessing, batching, loss computation, and SID export.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp_0/log_0 maps and geodesic distance d_P at fixed sectional curvature -1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Encoder-latent mapping before RQ-VAE quantization; both radial entropy correlation and sibling contrastive distances act on x_i in the same ball, then log_0(x_i) feeds the baseline quantizer.
COUPLING PATH: Training transitions produce h_i and sibling pairs; these directly supervise radial distance and pairwise geodesic distance of the exact mapped encoder vectors passed to quantization. Removing behavior removes both targets/pairs; replacing the ball with Euclidean vectors changes both radial and contrastive computations.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_coupled = [1-corr(d_P(0,exp_0(z_i)), h_i)] + E_(i,j+,N_i)[-log exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j+)^2/tau)/(exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j+)^2/tau)+sum_m exp(-d_P(x_i,x_m)^2/tau))], where h_i and j+ come only from training behavior and x=exp_0(z).
OBSERVABLES: With the mechanism on, the representation's radial/branching correlation and sibling-vs-negative geodesic margin should change; gradients update the encoder and hence potentially the emitted SIDs. Final judgment is Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set h_i to the batch mean and replace sibling positives with ordinary random batch positives, retaining the same curved maps, losses, optimizer, and schedule; this removes training-behavior information.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace exp/log maps, origin distance, and every d_P by their Euclidean counterparts (norm and Euclidean pair distance), retaining the exact same training-derived h_i and sibling pairs; this is the geometry-off comparison.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the training-derived entropy and real shared-successor siblings with Poincare exp/log maps and curvature -1 distances as specified. These are code switches for comparison only; the prescribed candidate is trained once with both on, with no ablation arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=count_train(i->j); p_ij=n_ij/(sum_k n_ik+eps); H_i=-sum_j p_ij log(p_ij+eps); h_i=H_i/log(max(2,d_i)); x_i=exp_0(z_i); r_i=d_P(0,x_i); L_rad=1-corr(r,h). Define S_i={k:n_ik>0}; eligible positive j satisfies S_i∩S_j≠empty, sampled with weight n_ij+n_ji+eps. For batch negatives N_i, L_sib=mean_i -log[ exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j+)²/tau)/(exp(-d_P(x_i,x_j+)²/tau)+sum_m exp(-d_P(x_i,x_m)²/tau)) ]. L=L_base+L_rad+L_sib. Derive tau as the median nonzero squared geodesic pair distance in the first deterministic training batch; freeze it, with a positive epsilon floor. This uses a measured scale, not a sweep.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training-only transition counts, batch item IDs, encoder latents z, and the baseline RQ-VAE. Outputs: scalar coupled loss and gradients to encoder; SIDs remain the usual hard residual-quantizer tokens from log_0(x).
SAFEGUARDS: Compute geometry in float32; project norms below (1-1e-5); clamp atanh arguments to 1-1e-6; use eps=1e-8 for probabilities, norms, and zero-count handling. Exclude items without enough transitions from correlation; return zero radial loss if batch variance is zero. If no shared-successor positive exists, skip that anchor rather than inventing a positive. Chunk pairwise distance calculations; use stable log-sum-exp; fix all random sampling seeds under the frozen run seed. Log finite losses/gradients and fall back to zero auxiliary term for degenerate batches.
END IDEA

IDEA: branch-geodesic-rank
TITLE: Match successor-set overlap ranks to Poincare geodesic ranks
MOTIVATION: Directed next-item behavior often gives a richer signal than a binary similar/dissimilar label: items whose successor distributions overlap strongly should be closer than pairs with weak overlap, without forcing all successors into one cluster.
NOVELTY: Instead of assigning items to behavior-defined clusters or imposing a radial hierarchy, this uses a training-only pairwise successor-overlap ranking as the target of geodesic distance learning on the quantizer's input representation. The behavior-derived order and the curved metric participate in the same ranking loss.
CHANGE: For each training batch, sample item pairs from a cached sparse successor graph and rank their Poincare distances according to popularity-corrected successor-distribution overlap. Apply this loss to exp-mapped encoder vectors before the unchanged RQ-VAE quantizer, so ranking gradients can alter both latents and resulting SIDs.
SPECIFICATION: Count training transitions n_ij and define p_i(j)=n_ij/sum_k n_ik. Compute pair affinity a_ij=sum_k min(p_i(k),p_j(k)) / (sqrt((1+log(1+pop_i))(1+log(1+pop_j)))) with pop_i=sum_k n_ki; retain sparse pairs with common successor. In each batch sample positive pair (i,j) proportional to a_ij and a lower-affinity pair (i,k) from the same batch using a deterministic rank-based sampler. Map x=exp_0(z) into curvature -1 ball and use L_rank=softplus((d_P(x_i,x_j)^2-d_P(x_i,x_k)^2)/s), where s is the median squared distance over the first deterministic batch, then frozen. Add L_rank with coefficient 1 to the baseline objective after dividing by its first-batch mean (scale calibration fixed, no tuning).
COST: Precompute sparse normalized successor rows in O(E_train); create sparse candidate overlaps using inverted successor lists, capped at the naturally observed batch-pair count without dense N-by-N storage. Per step sample O(B) triplets and evaluate O(BD) distances; across 72,000 steps this is O(72,000*B*D), a small-to-moderate overhead relative to baseline. Preprocessing cost depends on common-successor fanout but is performed once.
HYPOTHESIS: Popularity-adjusted overlap of future purchase distributions is predictive of useful semantic similarity, and a relative ranking is robust to varying pairwise overlap magnitudes. Observe geodesic rank correlation and SID changes diagnostically; retain only if the single frozen Stage3 test Recall@10 is above 0.065.
RISKS: Sparse behavior creates few reliable shared-successor pairs; popularity correction may over-discount genuinely popular useful continuations; pair rank targets can be noisy and compete with reconstruction; one-run variance remains.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted overlap of directed successor distributions from training-only adjacent item transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Construct n_ij from consecutive items within each training user sequence; normalize each outgoing row and compute sparse min-overlap for item pairs sharing successors, correcting by the square root of incoming-popularity factors. All pair sampling and affinity values are fixed before training.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only training sequences contribute to n_ij, pop_i, pair overlap, and the frozen pair pool. No validation or test event is read for graph construction, pair selection, hyperparameter calibration, or SID generation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exponential map and squared geodesic distance at fixed negative curvature -1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Pre-quantization encoder latent space; the triplet ranking objective measures distances between mapped representations that are subsequently logarithm-mapped into the baseline residual quantizer.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition distributions determine pair ordering and sample weights; that ordering is enforced by Poincare distances between the same encoder representations that feed quantization. Behavior-off eliminates the ordering, while geometry-off replaces the distances and maps, changing the loss's pair ranking gradients.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_rank=E_(i,j+,k-)[softplus((d_P(exp_0(z_i),exp_0(z_j+))²-d_P(exp_0(z_i),exp_0(z_k-))²)/s)], with pair selection and j+/k- ordering determined by training-only affinity a_ij.
OBSERVABLES: The pairwise geometry should align more strongly with successor-overlap rank, producing encoder gradients and potentially changed SIDs; final criterion is Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065, not the diagnostic rank correlation.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Permute training affinity ranks among sampled pairs while preserving triplet count and loss, removing the meaningful behavior ordering but retaining hyperbolic geometry and compute.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace exp_0/log_0 and d_P with Euclidean identity maps and squared Euclidean distances, keeping pair identities, rank order, scales, and objective structure fixed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use true training-only popularity-adjusted successor overlaps and curvature -1 Poincare maps/geodesics for the ranking loss. These switches are implemented as valid controls, but no control arm is trained in this round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p_i(j)=n_ij/(sum_l n_il+eps); pop_i=sum_l n_li; a_ij=[sum_l min(p_i(l),p_j(l))]/sqrt((1+log(1+pop_i))(1+log(1+pop_j))). Sample j+ from higher-affinity pool and k- from lower-affinity pool for anchor i, requiring a_i,j+>a_i,k-. x_i=exp_0(z_i), d_ij=d_P(x_i,x_j); L_rank=mean softplus((d_ij+²-d_ik-²)/s). Set s to median positive squared Poincare distance on the first deterministic batch, floor at eps and freeze. Train with L=L_base+L_rank after first-batch scale normalization.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen sparse training transition matrix/overlap pair sampler, batch item IDs, encoder latents, and baseline RQ-VAE. Outputs: baseline SIDs plus ranking-loss gradients to the encoder; no change to decoder or token vocabulary.
SAFEGUARDS: Use float32 geometry, project ball norms to at most 1-1e-5, clamp inverse-hyperbolic arguments, and add eps=1e-8 to row normalizers/popularity. Sample only valid strict-rank triplets; skip anchors without both pools. Use stable softplus and sparse inverted indexes; cap preprocessing fanout using a deterministic top-count rule derived from available memory, not validation performance. Freeze distance scale after the initial deterministic batch; check finite losses and gradients.
END IDEA
