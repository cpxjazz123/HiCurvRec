IDEA: prefix_successor_barycenter
TITLE: Prefix-conditioned geodesic successor consensus for the third code
MOTIVATION: The measured bottleneck is that T3 must be predictable after T1,T2; for a source item, its training successors often occupy a compact behavioral family even when their embedding residuals do not.
NOVELTY: Unlike successor-geodesic mechanisms acting on a coarse first code, or a successor penalty detached from level-three assignment, this makes the level-3 prototype decision a prefix-conditioned geodesic consensus of observed successor residuals. T1 remains the ordinary cheap 26-way coarse code and level 4 remains the deterministic collision extension.
CHANGE: At each training step, construct a stop-gradient successor consensus for the source's assigned T1,T2 prefix in a Poincare ball, then add the consensus distance directly to each candidate T3 residual assignment cost; thus the geodesic term can change emitted T3 IDs.
SPECIFICATION: From train-only directed counts n_ij, store for each source i up to 8 successors j sampled deterministically by largest n_ij, with normalized weights p_ij=(n_ij+0.1*pi_j)/(sum_k n_ik+0.1), pi_j train popularity. At a step, encode current batch items and obtain hard prefix (q1,q2); for each source i retrieve successors that share that prefix (fall back to all stored successors if fewer than 2), map their level-3 residuals u_j to the ball, and compute one weighted Karcher mean m_i by 3 updates m <- exp_m(sum_j p_ij log_m(u_j)); detach m. For source residual r_i^(3), assign k3=argmin_k [d_c(r_i^(3),c_3k)^2 + lambda (1-H(p_i)/log(max(2,d_i))) d_c(c_3k,m_i)^2]. Use curvature c=0.5, lambda=0.15, train decoder/codebooks with normal RQ-VAE loss and straight-through selected code. This acts at levels 2 and 3: the T2 hard prefix selects which successor cohort defines m; the curved T2 assignment distance is also calibrated by the prefix-conditioned m via an auxiliary candidate term at T2, lambda2=0.03, using the same cohort mean mapped to the T2 residual ball. No behavior or curve is used at level 1.
COST: Precompute capped successor lists once from 24,556 training items. Per minibatch, at most 8 successors per source and three 32-D Karcher updates; candidate costs are Bx256 at levels 2-3. Expected <0.02 s extra per epoch (24 steps; well below 0.13 s/epoch baseline), <15% total overhead across 3000 epochs/four cards; do not materialize all item pairs.
HYPOTHESIS: Within a T1,T2 prefix, successor residuals have a concentrated curved mean that ordinary reconstruction fails to capture. Keep only if T3 teacher-forced top-1 rises above 0.53 toward 0.7, with T4 remaining high, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065; verify with the exact measured contract below rather than SID uniqueness alone.
RISKS: Prefix gating can be sparse or self-reinforcing early in training; a mean can erase genuinely multimodal successors; popularity smoothing can overweight hubs; the additional T2 term may destabilize the cheap coarse partition.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only directed item successor counts and the normalized successor distribution for each source.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Sort each user's train.parquet target sequence in its stored event order, count consecutive directed pairs i->j, compute train popularity pi_j, and form p_ij as specified; cap to eight successors for step-time lookup. No validation/test rows are read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build and freeze successor tables solely from train.parquet before Stage2; validation/test are used only by the frozen downstream evaluation and never queried for counts, prototypes, neighbor lists, or hyperparameters.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball exp/log maps and squared geodesic distance d_c with fixed negative curvature -c=-0.5; Karcher iteration uses these maps.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The Karcher successor prototype is computed in the level-3 residual ball, and its geodesic candidate cost changes level-3 quantization; its prefix-selected cohort also supplies the smaller level-2 candidate cost.
COUPLING PATH: Train successor counts determine the prefix-cohort weights and geodesic Karcher target; that target enters the actual per-code argmin at T2/T3. Removing either the successor table or the curved operator changes candidate rankings and potentially emitted codes.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_l(i,k)=||r_i^(l)-c_lk||^2 + lambda_l B_i d_c(c_lk,m_i)^2, l in {2,3}; B_i=1-H(p_i)/log(max(2,d_i)), m_i=Karcher_c({r_j^(3)}_{j in Succ_train(i), prefix(j)=prefix(i)}).
OBSERVABLES: Log per-level code changes relative to baseline, L1 Hard ER on the canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives (predicted slightly up or flat, not the target), gold-prefix survival depths 1-4 (L1 near unchanged; L2/L3 up; L4 at least maintained), teacher-forced T3 top-1 (up, target >0.53 and aspirational 0.7-0.8), T4 top-1 (maintain near baseline resample 0.977), and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 (up, target >0.065).
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Use identical Poincare distances and Karcher computation but replace successor weights/cohort with uniform random in-batch residuals; this tests whether train-only directed behavior is necessary.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep identical successors, weights, prefix cohort and loss, but replace exp/log/Karcher and d_c by Euclidean weighted mean and squared Euclidean distances; this tests curvature necessity.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only directed successors, current hard T1,T2 prefix cohort, Poincare Karcher mean and curved T2/T3 assignment costs as specified.
SPEC EQUATIONS: x_i=Enc(e_i), r_i^(l)=x_i-sum_{h<l}c_{h,q_h}. Map residual vectors into the radius-(1-sqrt(eps)) ball using exp_0^c; d_c(u,v)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||(-u)⊕_c v||). m_i^(0)=u_{j*} (highest weight); repeat m_i^(t+1)=exp_{m_i^t}(sum_j p_ij log_{m_i^t}(u_j)), t=0..2. Then q_l=argmin_k C_l(i,k), with B_i as above. Clip/renormalize p after successor filtering; if no successor survives prefix filter, use all capped successors.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are batch item IDs, encoder residuals, hard prefix codes, per-item train-only capped successor IDs/counts, and codebooks; output is selected level-2 and level-3 code indices plus differentiable RQ-VAE assignment losses. Successor prototype is stop-gradient; codebook and encoder receive gradients through selected geodesic costs/straight-through path.
SAFEGUARDS: Float32 geometry; project every ball point to norm <=(1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp atanh arguments to 1-1e-6; clamp squared norms and log-map denominators to 1e-8; normalize nonnegative weights with 1e-8 floor; fallback for empty cohorts; cap successors at eight and Karcher updates at three; detach prototype so unstable iterative gradients cannot explode; clip model gradient norm to existing 1.0.
END IDEA

IDEA: successor_triplet_margin
TITLE: Train-only successor ranking triplets with a curved T2/T3 residual margin
MOTIVATION: Even after T1,T2, standard reconstruction can spend T3 codes separating content-near items rather than grouping items that function as plausible next steps in training sequences; directly aligning third-level residual neighborhoods with successor behavior could make the third token a useful predictable decision.
NOVELTY: This is a sparse edge-triplet ranking objective on the residual-code geometry, not a successor-derived quantizer radius, coarse-token renumbering, entropy penalty, or successor geodesic assignment. Curved distance ranks positive and negative successor residuals and its differentiable loss updates the encoder/codebooks used by the T2/T3 quantizer.
CHANGE: Sample source-positive-negative triples from train-only directed successors and popularity-adjusted non-successors, and minimize a Poincare geodesic margin ranking loss on their residual code representations after the shared T1,T2 path; apply a smaller matched ranking term at T2 so both assignments receive coupled gradients.
SPECIFICATION: For each source i, choose positive j from n_ij counts and negative k from items not observed after i, sampled proportional to (pop(k)+1)^0.25; retain only triples with current hard T1,T2 prefix agreement, and if none, use the nearest train-only non-successor by embedding cosine. For l in {2,3}, form residuals r_i^(l)=x_i-sum_{h<l}c_{h,q_h}, project them into the Poincare ball, and compute L_l=softplus(d_c(r_i^(l),r_j^(l))-d_c(r_i^(l),r_k^(l))+m), m=0.2. Add 0.05*(L_3+0.2*L_2) to ordinary reconstruction/VQ loss; gradients through residuals/codebooks are straight-through, and hard assignments use the same curved costs d_c(r,c_lk)^2 at levels 2 and 3, ensuring geometry changes emitted IDs, not only an auxiliary representation.
COST: Precompute sparse positive adjacency and train popularity once. Sample 64 triples per minibatch (fixed 32,768 triplets/step across the four ranks, at most 786,432/epoch); compute two 64x64? pair lists as elementwise 32-D distances only, O(64*32), plus unchanged 256-way curved assignments at levels 2-3. Expected <0.01 s/epoch additional (<8% of 0.13 s baseline) and feasible over 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs/four cards.
HYPOTHESIS: Successor-consistent residual triples can encourage T3 codes to predict behavioral continuation conditional on prefix without collapsing level 1. Keep only if T3 top-1 exceeds 0.53 (aim 0.7+) and Stage3 frozen test_R@10 >0.065 while T4 accuracy/prefix survival remain close to resample; compare all three prescribed arms.
RISKS: A successor edge does not imply the destination item's own residual should be near the source residual; negatives can be false negatives; popularity correction may overcorrect; same-prefix filtering may starve early batches; triplets can cause collapse or hurt semantic reconstruction if weighted too heavily.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed successor edges from consecutive target items in training user histories, with train popularity for negative sampling.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Group train.parquet by user preserving event order, count consecutive target transitions; sample positive destination proportional to smoothed counts, and sample negative from the complement of that source's observed successors using train popularity^0.25. All filtering and fallback nearest-neighbor search use only train item embeddings/IDs.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Triplet adjacency, popularity and candidate negatives are constructed only from train.parquet; SID generation never reads validation/test interaction rows and no test-driven tuning changes margins or weights.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball squared geodesic distance d_c^2 at fixed negative curvature -0.5, used both for T2/T3 codeword assignment and positive-vs-negative ranking.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Residual-level T2 and T3 quantization costs use Poincare distances; sampled train successor triplets rank these same residual representations during Stage2 updates.
COUPLING PATH: The directed train edge chooses the positive item and the popularity-adjusted negative; their current residuals are scored with the curved metric that also selects level-2/3 codewords, and the triplet gradient updates the encoder/codebooks that emit those codes.
COUPLING EQUATION: L= sum_(i,j,k) softplus(d_c(r_i^(3),r_j^(3))-d_c(r_i^(3),r_k^(3))+m) + 0.2 L_trip^(2); (q_2,q_3)=argmin_{k2,k3} [d_c(r^(2),c_2k2)^2,d_c(r^(3),c_3k3)^2], where (i,j)~E_train and k~popularity-adjusted nonSucc_train(i).
OBSERVABLES: Record L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives (predicted flat/slightly worse), gold-prefix survival depths 1-4 (L1 near unchanged, L2/L3 improve; L4 maintained), teacher-forced T3 top-1 (must exceed 0.53 and ideally approach 0.7-0.8), T4 top-1 (maintain near 0.977 resample), and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 (target >0.065). Also log fraction of minibatch triplets surviving prefix filter and code assignment changes to verify operative coupling.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Keep hyperbolic residual assignment and sample positive/negative items uniformly without directed edges or popularity; same number of triples and optimization steps.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the exact directed triples, sampling, prefix filter, margin, weights and candidate assignments, replacing every Poincare distance with squared Euclidean distance; no curved map remains.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only directed successors, popularity-adjusted negatives, prefix-filtered triplets, and fixed-curvature Poincare costs/ranking at levels 2 and 3.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each valid triple and l in {2,3}, D_pos=d_c(r_i^(l),r_j^(l)), D_neg=d_c(r_i^(l),r_k^(l)); L_l=mean softplus(D_pos-D_neg+0.2). Candidate q_l=argmin_a d_c(r_i^(l),c_la)^2; straight-through quantized r feeds next residual. Total loss=L_RQVAE+0.05(L_3+0.2L_2). Note softplus argument has no inverse; all distances are computed after projecting residual vectors into the open ball.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are batch embeddings/IDs, current hard T1-T2 assignments, learned residual codebooks, and train-only sparse successor lists/popularity. Outputs are standard SID assignments and a scalar ranking loss that backpropagates to encoder/codebooks.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute geodesic distance in float32; project points to norm <=(1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp Mobius norm/atanh argument to 1-1e-6 and denominators to 1e-8; skip invalid/self triples; cap at 64 triples per rank per step; use detached hard prefix masks; maintain existing gradient clipping and monitor positive-negative margin and code usage for collapse.
END IDEA
