IDEA: successor_residual_barycenter
TITLE: Successor-consensus geodesic residual assignment at T2–T3
MOTIVATION: T3 should become predictable given the already emitted T1/T2 prefix: training items with similar directed continuation distributions should share a compact residual branch rather than being separated by embedding reconstruction alone.
NOVELTY: Unlike existing first-level successor-prototype/geodesic mechanisms, behavior-driven branch consensus is applied to residuals after subtracting the actual preceding code vectors; its codeword-dependent geodesic term directly changes T2 and T3 assignments. T1 and deterministic T4 remain untouched.
CHANGE: Precompute a sparse successor distribution for each training item, then at residual levels 2 and 3 softly summarize the successor residuals of its observed outgoing neighbors. Add the Poincare geodesic distance from each candidate residual codeword to this level-specific successor barycenter into that same level's hard assignment score.
SPECIFICATION: Let Cij be counts of adjacent directed transitions in training histories and qij=(Cij+απj)/(Σl Cil+α), with π the training successor marginal. At level s∈{2,3}, let r_i^s=z_i−Σ_{t<s}e_{k_i^t} and for each observed successor j let r_j^s be its residual using current hard assignments (stop-gradient on neighbor residuals). Compute b_i^s=log^c_0(Proj_c(Σj qij exp^c_0(r_j^s))) and assign k_i^s=argmin_k ||log^c_0(x_i^s)−log^c_0(c_sk)||² + λ_s Σj qij d_c(Proj_c(exp^c_0(r_j^s)),Proj_c(exp^c_0(c_sk)))², where x_i^s=Proj_c(exp^c_0(r_i^s)); curvature is −c, c=0.5. The second term is a successor-weighted geodesic consensus cost and is not a row-constant bias. Use assignments from the previous completed minibatch/epoch for neighbor residual lookup and refresh cached residuals once per epoch; no iterative graph traversal during a step.
COST: One preprocessing pass O(Etrain), sparse q storage, and cached neighbor residuals. Per step (about 1,023 items, 24,556 total training items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs=72,000 steps), evaluate at most M=8 outgoing neighbors × K=256 codewords × D coordinates at each of T2,T3; this is about 1,023×8×256×D×2 pairwise terms per step, vectorized and restricted to observed neighbors. Relative to 0.13 s/epoch baseline, estimate 1.15–1.35×; reject if single-epoch smoke exceeds 0.17 s. Cache/train-only sparse top-8 transitions and never add a full N×N operation.
HYPOTHESIS: High-probability successors share meaningful residual continuations, and their geodesic consensus can collapse ambiguous third-token choices conditioned on prefix while preserving reconstruction. Worth keeping only if T3 teacher-forced top-1 rises materially above 0.53 (target 0.7–0.8), both-on beats both controls, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: Sparse/low-support transitions may give noisy barycenters; stale cached residual assignments can lag; a consensus weight that is too large can erase item-specific detail or cause code collapse. Restrict to top-8 supported neighbors, stop-gradient through their residuals, and tune λ only on training-side diagnostics, not validation/test outcomes.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Smoothed directed next-item distribution qij, restricted to training items with observed outgoing transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive item pairs from training histories only; use qij=(Cij+απj)/(outcount_i+α), retain top eight observed successors, renormalize, and cache. Train split only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build counts from the Stage2 training-history partition and training item IDs; no validation/test events, histories, candidate labels, or metrics enter q, barycenter construction, or SID export. Unknown/no-outgoing items have an explicit zero consensus term.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp/log maps and squared geodesic distance d_c at fixed sectional curvature −0.5, with projection to radius (1−ε)/sqrt(c).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: At each residual assignment score for T2 and T3: map current residuals and candidate residual codewords into the ball; use geodesic distance both in successor-consensus score and item-to-codeword score. T1 remains cheap Euclidean/coarse and T4 remains deterministic collision extension.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition counts → qij → cached successor residual barycenter/neighbor set → behavior-weighted Poincare distance to each candidate residual codeword → hard T2/T3 code selection → emitted four-token SID. Either q or curved distances changed/removed, the candidate ranking and therefore SID can change.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_i^s=argmin_k[d_c(x_i^s,c_sk)^2+λ_s Σ_j qij d_c(y_j^s,c_sk)^2], s∈{2,3}, where qij is training-only successor weight and d_c is Poincare geodesic distance at curvature −0.5.
OBSERVABLES: Predict lower L1 Hard ER (or no material increase) because T1 is unchanged but residual partitions may become more behavior-consistent; gold-prefix survival should be unchanged at depth 1, rise at depths 2–3 if useful, and remain high at depth 4. Teacher-forced T3 top-1 should rise from ≤0.53 toward 0.7–0.8; T4 should remain near its current high value (roughly ≥0.9). Record distinct-token counts and SID collisions as diagnostics only. Primary outcome is unchanged-protocol Stage3 test_R@10 >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set λ_s=0 and remove q/barycenter terms, retaining identical Poincare residual item-to-codeword distance, codebooks, seeds, schedule and all other training settings.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the same q, neighbors, residual cache and λ_s but replace exp/log/projection/geodesic terms in both item and successor costs with norm/scale-matched Euclidean squared distances; use training-only scale matching.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use q from training histories and curvature −0.5 Poincare assignment/consensus at both T2 and T3, fixed T1/T4 behavior, same four-token SID and frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each s=2,3 and minibatch item i: r_i=z_i−Σ_{t<s}e_{k_i^t}; x_i=Proj_c(exp^c_0(r_i)); for j in top8(i), y_j=Proj_c(exp^c_0(stopgrad(r_j^s))); score_ik=d_c(x_i,Proj_c(exp^c_0(e_sk)))²+λ_s Σ_jqij d_c(y_j,Proj_c(exp^c_0(e_sk)))²; k_i=argmin score_ik. Update codebook/encoder with the ordinary RQ-VAE commitment/reconstruction gradients for that chosen code; retain ordinary straight-through path. Only the T2/T3 residual stage uses this score.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item encoder vector z_i, preceding hard code vectors, T2/T3 codebook vectors, cached sparse top-eight qij, cached preceding-epoch neighbor residuals, fixed c=0.5, λ2/λ3. Output: one integer code per item at each residual level, selected code vectors and the standard quantization loss. Tensorize candidate-codeword distances; no dense graph.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute geometry in float32; project every point to norm ≤(1−10⁻⁵)/sqrt(c); clamp denominator to ≥10⁻⁷ and atanh argument to [0,1−10⁻⁶]; add α=1 smoothing; require at least one observed outgoing edge or set consensus to zero; normalize top-eight weights; stop-gradient neighbor residuals; cap λ to a small train-only calibrated range; log finite values, code occupancy, argmin changes and gradient norms. Abort smoke if assignments do not change versus behavior-off or epoch time is >0.17 s.
END IDEA

IDEA: prefix_conditioned_geodesic_transport
TITLE: Prefix-conditioned successor transport for residual code selection
MOTIVATION: A code's utility at the third position depends on the first-two-code prefix; globally clustering successors can mix distinct branches. Train-only outgoing transitions can define which residual codes should be nearby specifically within the same prefix cell, making T3 nearly determined after T1/T2.
NOVELTY: This is not a global successor clustering objective, a behavior suffix, or a first-token reordering. It constructs a prefix-conditioned transition kernel and uses that kernel as a codeword-dependent geodesic transport assignment cost at T2 and especially T3; prefix partition and curved metric meet inside hard assignment itself.
CHANGE: From training transitions, estimate the distribution of destination residual codewords conditioned on the source item's current prefix. For each candidate at T2/T3, score its curved distance to the candidate's own training-derived successor-code distribution (soft geodesic transport to a small set of destination code prototypes); add this to item residual distance before argmin.
SPECIFICATION: Define prefix p_i^s=(k_i^1,…,k_i^{s−1}) from current assignments. From training-only transitions i→j, count A^s_{p,a,b} where a=k_i^s and b=k_j^s, within prefix p=p_i^s; smooth destination distribution π^s_{p,a}(b)=(A^s_{p,a,b}+απ^s_0(b))/(Σb'A^s_{p,a,b'}+α). For source i and candidate code a, let destination prototype v^s_{p,a}=argmin_v Σ_b π^s_{p,a}(b)d_c(v,c^s_b)^2 over a fixed small set (e.g. 4 weighted medoids; obtain by 4 deterministic weighted medoid updates on train counts). Assign k_i^s=argmin_a[d_c(x_i^s,c_a^s)^2+λ_s d_c(T_c(x_i^s),v^s_{p_i^s,a})²], where T_c(x)=Proj_c(exp^c_0(log^c_0(x)+δ)) and δ is a bounded trainable tangent offset shared by level (initialized zero). The candidate’s prefix-conditioned outgoing code distribution selects v; geodesic displacement makes curvature affect the selected residual code. Use s=2,3; T1 unchanged and T4 unchanged.
COST: Build sparse prefix/source/destination count tables once from training transitions; cap to at most 4 medoids per observed (level,prefix,source-code) cell. Per step cost is two vectorized Poincare distance batches per item×256 candidates at T2/T3 plus table lookup, O(B·2K·D), B≈1,023. Offline medoid construction is 4 passes over sparse counts. Expected 1.05–1.2× 0.13 s/epoch; 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs means 72,000 steps and no epoch-time graph scan. Reject if one-epoch smoke >0.17 s.
HYPOTHESIS: The observed first-two-token prefix carries enough state that conditional transition-code distributions are concentrated; selecting codes whose hyperbolic transported residual aligns with that conditional destination prototype improves prefix survival and T3 teacher-forced accuracy without harming T4. Retain only if both-on beats both controls, T3 reaches 0.7–0.8 and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 >0.065.
RISKS: Prefix cells may be sparse early and unstable; hard assignments can cause table churn; low transition support can make medoids arbitrary. Use prior smoothing and epoch-frozen count tables, update assignments/counts only between epochs, and cap the conditional term. If prefixes remain sparse, expected result is neutral or worse rather than a hidden test-time rescue.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Prefix-conditioned directed successor code distribution A^s_{p,a,b} from adjacent training item transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: For each training history, form directed adjacent item pairs i→j. Encode items with current Stage2 assignments; count each source residual code a and destination residual code b conditioned on the source prefix p at level s. Use only training histories; recompute cached counts at epoch boundaries using current hard SIDs and α-smoothed global destination prior.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Histories and transition pairs are strictly from the Stage2 training partition; validation/test histories are never encoded into counts or medoids. Freeze each epoch’s table before its optimizer steps, export SIDs using learned parameters only, and do not tune with test outcomes.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed-curvature (sectional curvature −0.5) Poincare exp/log maps, projection, and geodesic distance, including a bounded tangent displacement T_c; no Euclidean distance is substituted in the coupled assignment.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Candidate score at residual quantization levels 2 and 3. Both current item residual and candidate destination prototype are compared after the Poincare transport; curve and behavior-conditioned prototype jointly affect hard assignments at each of these levels.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition events plus current first-two-token prefix/code assignments → smoothed conditional destination-code distribution → geodesic medoid and transported candidate score → T2/T3 hard argmin → emitted SID. Removing either the conditional distribution or the curved transport/distance changes the candidate score computation and can change emitted codes.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_i^s=argmin_a{d_c(x_i^s,c_a^s)^2+λ_s d_c(T_c(x_i^s),v^s_{p_i^s,a})²}, where v^s_{p,a}=FrechetMedoid_c(π^s_{p,a}) is derived from training-only prefix-conditioned successor-code probabilities and T_c uses curvature −0.5.
OBSERVABLES: Predicted L1 Hard ER direction is flat to slightly lower versus baseline because first code assignment is fixed; depth-1 gold-prefix survival should be unchanged. Depth-2 and depth-3 gold-prefix survival should increase if conditional transitions are useful; depth-4 should remain high because T4 is unchanged. Teacher-forced T3 top-1 is the central diagnostic, expected to move above 0.53 toward 0.7–0.8; T4 should remain ≥0.9. Measure canonical L1 Hard ER using 52,855 pairs and ten fixed negatives, all depth-1–4 survival, T3/T4 top-1, and frozen-protocol Stage3 test_R@10 with target >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Replace π^s_{p,a} and v^s_{p,a} with a single global training prior/prototype independent of source item, prefix, and candidate code; retain curved map/distance, learned δ, and all schedules.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the identical train-only conditional tables/medoids and code assignments used to construct them, but replace Poincare maps, transport and geodesic distances with norm-matched Euclidean distances and linear displacement; match median score scale on training batches only.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use epoch-frozen training-only prefix-conditioned transition tables, curved medoids/transport, and coupled residual assignment at T2 and T3; all other RQ-VAE, fixed-width SID, deterministic T4 and Stage3 settings unchanged.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each s∈{2,3}, epoch e build A^s_{p,a,b} from training transitions using current assignments; smooth π^s_{p,a}. For each table cell find 4 weighted medoids v_m∈{c_b} minimizing Σ_bπ(b)min_m d_c(v_m,c_b)^2 (four deterministic Lloyd/medoid iterations). For minibatch i and candidate a, compute x_i=Proj_c(exp_0^c(r_i^s)); y_i=Proj_c(exp_0^c(log_0^c(x_i)+δ_s)); score_i(a)=d_c(x_i,Proj_c(exp_0^c(e_a^s)))²+λ_s min_m d_c(y_i,v^s_{p_i,a,m})²; emit argmin_a. Compute λ only from fixed training-scale calibration; regular RQ-VAE reconstruction/commitment training continues on selected embeddings.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: current item residual and prefix, T2/T3 codebook, epoch-frozen sparse A/π table, at most four medoids per table cell, bounded learned δ2/δ3, fixed curvature. Outputs: hard residual code at T2/T3 and its selected code vector; T1 and collision-extension T4 remain baseline outputs.
SAFEGUARDS: Float32 geometry; project to ball radius (1−10⁻⁵)/sqrt(c), clamp squared norms/denominators and atanh arguments; α≥1 prior smoothing; skip unsupported cells by using explicit smoothed global training prior; deterministic tie-breaking and medoid initialization; tables are frozen during epochs to avoid label feedback oscillation; δ norm ≤0.1; λ bounded; finite-distance/gradient checks; log occupancy, prefix support, assignment changes and wall clock. Do not run a dense item-pair graph or alter SID width.
END IDEA
