IDEA: BRad-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-ranked radial Poincare assignment: training-transition context entropy sets each item's hyperbolic radius, curvature sets the quantizer score
MOTIVATION: An RQ-VAE SID prefix is currently a purely semantic token. Nothing in the quantizer forces the coarse prefix to correlate with how generic or context-specific an item is, so the Stage3 generator must learn prefix popularity structure implicitly from short histories. If the first SID level is stratified by a measured, training-only generality statistic, the coarse prefix becomes a cheaply predictable "how generic is this item" token and later levels only refine the angle (fine semantics), which should raise prefix predictability and Stage3 Recall@10.
NOVELTY: Earlier mechanisms here put behaviour into the quantizer cost (successor mass in a Poincare Sinkhorn cost), into the codebook update (successor-weighted Poincare Frechet means), into the curvature itself (per-level curvature from a hard prefix hit rate), or into a cone/boost/Lorentz-profile/fibre variant, i.e. behaviour always modifies a weight, a curvature, a mean or a translation. Here behaviour modifies the RADIAL COORDINATE of the item point only (a rank map of a measured entropy statistic), curvature is fixed at c = 1 and therefore cannot be saturated by any protective bound, and the geometry acts in the nearest-codeword score through the factor 1/((1-|a|^2)(1-|e|^2)), which is exactly what makes radial matching dominate for far items and angular matching dominate for near items. No Sinkhorn, no Frechet update, no cone, no translation, no per-level curvature, no auxiliary loss.
CHANGE: Replace the encoder magnitude by a behaviour-derived rank radius, r_i = artanh(0.9 * rank(s_i)/(N+1)), send the item to the Poincare point p_i = exp_0(r_i * z_i/|z_i|), and choose each RQ level's codeword by argmin of the Poincare distance between exp_0(residual) and exp_0(squashed codeword) instead of the Euclidean distance, while reconstruction and commitment losses stay Euclidean on the same tangent target t_i = r_i * z_i/|z_i|.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Precompute from training sequences only: C[j,i] = directed counts of consecutive items, n_i = sum_j C[j,i], p_ji = C[j,i]/n_i, H_i = -sum_j p_ji log p_ji, s_i = (n_i*exp(H_i) + sbar)/(n_i+1) with sbar = mean{exp(H_i) : n_i >= 10}, and s_i = sbar for items never observed. (2) rank_i = rank of s_i over the N = 24556 training items (ties by id), u_i = rank_i/(N+1) in (0,1), rho_i = 0.9*u_i, r_i = artanh(rho_i) in (0, 1.4722). (3) Per step: zhat = z/max(|z|,1e-8), t = r_i*zhat, u = t; for l = 1..L: a = u*min(1, 0.9/max(|u|,1e-8)), e_lk = tanh(|m_lk|)*m_lk/|m_lk| with m_lk the level-l tangent codebook after squash min(1,0.9/|m_lk|), score s_lk = |a-e_lk|^2/((1-|a|^2)(1-|e_lk|^2)), k_l = argmin_k s_lk, u = u - m_lk; t_hat = sum_l m_lk_l; loss = |t-t_hat|^2 + beta|t - sg(t_hat)|^2 + |sg(t) - t_hat|^2 with the baseline beta; emit SID(i) = (k_1,...,k_L). (4) Curvature c = 1 is derived, not tuned: after direction normalisation |zhat| = 1, so the unit tangent scale is the encoder scale and the ball radius is 1/sqrt(c) = 1. Remaining constants are documented defaults: 0.9 boundary margin, 1 Laplace prior count, baseline beta.
COST: One extra pre-training pass over the training transition stream (O(E), sparse COO counts of a few million entries, tens of seconds on CPU, done once, cached as a length-N float vector); no extra parameters, no extra epochs, no extra forward pass. Per step at the frozen scale (batch 1024 = 24556/24, L = 3, K = 256, d = 64): 786432 distance evaluations, about 1.6e8 flops, i.e. roughly one extra elementwise pass over the similarity tensor the baseline already forms, under 1 percent of a step and under 2 percent of an epoch wall-clock.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if the level-1 prefix is genuinely radius-locked to the behaviour statistic: level-1 prefix ids nearly monotone in rho_i and the mean r_i of the items sharing a level-1 prefix separating strongly across prefixes, with the level-1 prefix becoming more predictable from the same history because it now carries a generality prior. Observable as high Spearman correlation between level-1 prefix index and mean r_i of its members, a drop in level-1 prefix conditional entropy given the previous item on training transitions (diagnostic only), and, as the judgement, Stage3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 from the single frozen run.
RISKS: Discarding encoder magnitude may cost reconstruction, since magnitude carried some frequency information that the cascade must now rebuild from radius; the hyperbolic score may let level-1 codewords collapse onto radial bands and waste angular capacity so levels 2 and 3 do all semantic work; the rank map spreads radii uniformly and may over-weight the tail; the residual clamp at 0.9 may bind at levels 2 and 3 for badly reconstructed items; and Stage3 may already model popularity from the history, in which case a popularity-stratified prefix adds nothing and recall stays flat.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Behaviour-conditioned context generality: the effective number of distinct predecessor contexts of an item, exp(H_i), from directed item-to-item transitions in the training split; because the entropy is over the predecessor distribution it is inherently popularity-adjusted.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count directed consecutive-item pairs (S_t -> S_{t+1}) over every training sequence, build sparse C, take n_i = sum_j C[j,i], p_ji = C[j,i]/n_i, H_i = -sum_j p_ji log p_ji over observed predecessors, set s_i = exp(H_i), shrink towards sbar = mean{exp(H_i) : n_i >= 10} with weight 1/(n_i+1), assign s_i = sbar to items absent from training transitions, then rank-normalise s_i over the 24556 training items and map to r_i. Computed once, offline, before Stage 2, and stored as a frozen buffer.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The statistic reads only training-split sequences; C, sbar, the ranks and r_i are frozen before any Stage 2 or Stage 3 optimisation, no validation or test interaction is touched, validation/test items never contribute counts, and items without training transitions receive the frozen prior sbar rather than any inferred value, so no test-time signal can reach the SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1 (derived from the unit-norm direction normalisation), Poincare exponential map exp_0(v) = tanh(|v|) v/|v|, and the Poincare distance in monotonically transformed squared form s(x,y) = |x-y|^2/((1-|x|^2)(1-|y|^2)), whose arcosh is the geodesic distance.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the quantizer, at every RQ level, inside the nearest-codeword assignment: the codeword is chosen by the curved score between the exponential image of the current tangent residual and the exponential image of the codeword, and the item point itself is produced by exp_0 of the behaviour-radius tangent vector.
COUPLING PATH: The behaviour rank sets the tangent norm r_i, that norm is the argument of exp_0, its image p_i enters the Poincare score against exp_0(m_lk), and the argmin over that score fixes the SID token; the reconstruction target and the residual cascade then run on the same t_i = r_i*zhat. Remove behaviour (r_i constant) and every item sits at one radius so the curved score can only separate angles; remove geometry (Euclidean score) and the radius still exists but the 1/((1-|a|^2)(1-|e|^2)) factor is gone, so radial banding no longer dominates and the codeword choice changes.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_l(i) = argmin_k s_P( exp_0( clamp(t_i - sum_{j<l} m_{j k_j(i)}, 0.9) ), exp_0( squash(m_lk) ) ) with t_i = artanh(0.9 * rank(s_i)/(N+1)) * z_i/|z_i|, where s_i is the behaviour context-entropy statistic and s_P is the fixed-curvature (c = 1) Poincare squared-distance kernel.
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 prefix ids nearly monotone in rho_i (high Spearman between prefix index and mean r_i of members); within-prefix spread of r_i shrinks versus the behaviour-off arm; level-1 conditional entropy of the prefix given the previous item drops; level-1 codebook usage concentrates in radially separated bands; reconstruction loss on the same target t compared with the geometry-off arm; and finally Stage3 test_recall@10 from one frozen run, above 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: rho_i = 0.45 for every item (median of the rank map, one radius r = artanh(0.45) = 0.4847, tangent target t_i = 0.4847*zhat_i); curvature and the Poincare score unchanged, so only the behaviour-driven radial separation is removed.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Same behaviour radii r_i and same tangent target t_i, but score is Euclidean |a-e_lk|^2 and exp_0 is the identity, so codewords are plain bounded Euclidean points and the radial-matching pressure of the curvature disappears.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run: r_i from the behaviour rank map, exp_0 into the ball, Poincare score at every level, Euclidean reconstruction on t_i.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Offline: C[j,i] += 1 per consecutive training pair; n_i = sum_j C[j,i]; p_ji = C[j,i]/n_i; H_i = -sum_j p_ji log p_ji; s_i = (n_i exp(H_i) + sbar)/(n_i+1); sbar = mean{exp(H_i) : n_i >= 10}; s_i = sbar if n_i = 0; u_i = rank(s_i)/(N+1); rho_i = 0.9 u_i; r_i = 0.5 log((1+rho_i)/(1-rho_i)). Online per step: zhat = z/max(|z|,1e-8); t = r_i*zhat; u = t; for l in 1..L: a = u*min(1, 0.9/max(|u|,1e-8)); m = m_l*min(1, 0.9/|m_l|); e = tanh(|m|)*m/|m|; s_lk = |a-e_lk|^2/((1-|a|^2)(1-|e_lk|^2)); k_l = argmin_k s_lk; u = u - m_l[k_l]; t_hat = sum_l m_l[k_l]; L_total = |t-t_hat|^2 + beta|t-sg(t_hat)|^2 + |sg(t)-t_hat|^2; SID(i) = (k_1,...,k_L).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder output z in R^d for the batch (d = 64), frozen per-item radius buffer r in R^N, L tangent codebooks m_l in R^{K x d} (L = 3, K = 256), flags behaviour_on and geometry_on. Outputs: per-item SID tuple (k_1,...,k_L), tangent reconstruction t_hat, scalar loss; the only state reaching Stage 3 is the emitted SIDs (radii frozen, codebooks read only through the SIDs).
SAFEGUARDS: Normalise direction with max(|z|,1e-8); keep rho_i in (0,0.9) so 1-rho_i^2 >= 0.19 and the Poincare denominator never vanishes (the 0.9 margin is applied to the INPUT rank map before artanh, the normalise-first rule, so no protective cap truncates the behaviour statistic: the full rank range 0..1 maps monotonically into 0..0.9 and radius 0..1.4722); clamp tangent norms with min(1, 0.9/|v|) and max(|v|,1e-8) instead of dividing by a possibly zero norm; evaluate the score via s = |a-e|^2/((1-|a|^2)(1-|e|^2)) and never via arcosh, avoiding domain errors; add 1e-12 to the denominator; keep the radius buffer requires_grad = False so no gradient drifts it into the boundary; leave items without training transitions at the frozen prior sbar.
END IDEA
