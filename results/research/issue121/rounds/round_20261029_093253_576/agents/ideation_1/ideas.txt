IDEA: RADCAP-SID
TITLE: Level branching entropy sets how far out in the Poincare ball each RQ level places its assignment probe, so SID capacity lands where behaviour has entropy
MOTIVATION: A 4-level RQ-VAE spends the same code resolution at every level, but the training transitions say the levels are not equally predictable: some prefix classes branch into hundreds of continuations, others into two. Giving a branchy level the same effective capacity as a near-deterministic one forces the generative model to spend prefix probability on distinctions the behaviour does not make, and forces deterministic regions to share codes they do not need to share. Hyperbolic space has a natural capacity coordinate, the radius: the ball volume out to radius r under curvature c grows like sinh^(d-1)(sqrt(c) r), so the radius is exactly "how many distinguishable things fit here". RADCAP sets that radius from the measured branching of the level.
NOVELTY: Earlier candidates move behaviour INTO the codebook means (Poincare-Frechet successor codebook), INTO the assignment mass (successor-diffused Sinkhorn/OT), INTO the curvature itself (CURVCTRL-SID), INTO neighbour smoothing (Lorentz successor profile), INTO a cone constraint (behcone-prefix) or INTO a post-hoc shift (succboost-geoshift). RADCAP changes none of those: codebook, residual chain and reconstruction path are untouched; behaviour modulates only the RADIAL COORDINATE at which the latent is probed for nearest-code lookup, i.e. the capacity of the level. Curvature is fixed at c=1 and is never fitted to behaviour, so the CURVCTRL failure mode (behaviour statistic neutralised by a curvature cap) cannot occur: there is no curvature cap at all, and the behaviour term r_l is an unclamped monotone image onto a measured radius scale.
CHANGE: Before the level-l nearest-code lookup, the latent is pushed through the Poincare exponential map at a radius r_l that is a monotone function of the level's measured training-transition branching entropy H_l; the code is the curved nearest neighbour of that probe and the commitment loss becomes the squared geodesic distance. Removing the behaviour makes all levels probe at one common radius; removing the curvature makes the probe a Euclidean rescaling and the distance L2.
SPECIFICATION: (1) Fixed curvature c = 1 (ball radius 1/sqrt(c) = 1), latent dim d = 32, L = 4 levels, K = 256 codes/level, |I| = 24556 items. (2) Once per epoch, from the training transition multiset T = {(a,b)} (consecutive items inside training sequences) and the current codes: for each level l form prefix pairs (p,q) = (SID_{1:l}(a), SID_{1:l}(b)); H_l = mean over p of the empirical entropy in nats of q given p; N_l = exp(H_l) in [1,|I|]; N_max = |I|; R_ref = 0.90-quantile of ||z_i||_2 over training items under the current encoder. (3) r_l = R_ref * log1p(N_l)/log1p(N_max), monotone, unclamped, image [0.069 R_ref, R_ref]. (4) Per step per level: probe = exp_0^c((r_l/max(||z||,eps)) z); k* = argmin_k d_c(probe, e_k^l) with straight-through; loss += d_c(z, e_{k*}^l)^2; the RQ residual and decoder are the baseline ones.
COST: Same asymptotics as the baseline nearest-code search; extra work is one tanh-based exp map per item per level plus a Poincare distance per (item, code) pair, roughly 4x the arithmetic of squared L2 plus one log1p per pair. At the frozen scale with batch B = 1024, L = 4, K = 256, d = 32 that is 1024*4*256 = 1.05M curved pair evaluations per step, about 0.3-0.5 GFLOP of extra arithmetic against about 0.03 GFLOP of plain assignment, i.e. roughly +8-15% stage-2 step time. Stage 2 is 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72,000 steps, so about +1.5-3 h wall clock on the four cards. The H_l / R_ref recount is one assignment pass over 24556 training items plus one pass over the transition table, 1-2 s per epoch, about 1-1.7 h over 3000 epochs, overlappable with the previous epoch's last steps. No extra parameters, no extra optimiser state.
HYPOTHESIS: That SID prefixes at low-branching levels become sharper (lower conditional next-prefix entropy, higher hard prefix hit rate) while code budget is redistributed toward the branchy level, and that this lifts stage-3 test recall@10 above 0.065. Worth keeping if, at the end of one run, per-level conditional entropy tracks r_l monotonically AND the low-branching levels' prefix hit rate improves relative to the recorded baseline trajectory, with recall above 0.065.
RISKS: If the pretrained latent's own norm already encodes item frequency, the radial probe may double-count popularity and collapse many items onto inner shells, degenerating codes; if H_l is nearly flat across levels (all near 6-7 nats) the radii differ by under 2x and the mechanism is inert; if the probe radius departs strongly from the latent norm, the straight-through code choice can drift from the reconstruction-consistent code and hurt reconstruction, visible as rising reconstruction error and falling codebook usage; epoch-wise recomputation of H_l and R_ref can oscillate, so radii must come from the counts of the epoch being trained and be logged.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-level branchiness of the training transitions, expressed as the average entropy of the next level-l SID prefix given the current level-l SID prefix (effective number of continuations N_l = exp(H_l)).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Built only from the training split: split each training sequence into consecutive pairs (a,b); map items to their current codes each epoch; count, for each prefix class p at level l, the empirical distribution of q = SID_{1:l}(b); take the entropy per p and average over p weighted by p's transition count; H_l in nats; N_l = exp(H_l). One scalar per level per epoch, logged.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition multiset T contains only training sequences; item-to-code maps come from an encoder trained on training items only; R_ref is a quantile over training-item latents; no validation or test interaction, no stage-3 feedback, and no statistic from the evaluation split enters r_l or the quantizer. SID construction completes before stage 3, so the generative model's targets cannot leak back.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c = 1. Poincare exponential map at the origin exp_0^c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) v / (sqrt(c)||v||), and distance d_c(x,y) = (1/sqrt(c)) arcosh(1 + 2c||x-y||^2 / ((1-c||x||^2)(1-c||y||^2))).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ-VAE quantizer, at the nearest-code assignment of every level and in the commitment term that follows it; both act during stage-2 training of the SID encoder/decoder, and therefore determine the SID assignment that stage 3 consumes.
COUPLING PATH: H_l (behaviour) -> r_l -> the radial argument of exp_0^c -> the probe point -> argmin_k d_c(probe, e_k) -> the emitted code -> the item's SID -> the stage-3 target sequence; and in the backward pass the same r_l changes the probe, hence the chosen e_k, hence the gradient the encoder receives through the straight-through path and the curved commitment. Setting H_l to a common constant collapses every level's probe to one radius and changes the codes; setting c to 0 makes the probe a Euclidean rescale and d_c an L2 and changes the codes again; neither removal leaves the computation intact.
COUPLING EQUATION: k*_l(x) = argmin_{k in 1..K} d_c( exp_0^c( ( r_l(H_l) / max(||z_l(x)||, eps) ) z_l(x) ), e_k^l ), with r_l(H_l) = R_ref * log(1+exp(H_l)) / log(1+|I|).
OBSERVABLES: Per-level used-code count and per-level mean radius of assigned codes (capacity should move outward at the branchy level, inward at the sharp one); per-level conditional next-prefix entropy and hard prefix hit rate; the distribution of the geodesic assignment margin d_c(probe,e_1) - d_c(probe,e_2); reconstruction loss and codebook perplexity; and stage-3 test recall@10 against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: H_l replaced by the single pooled constant H_ref = log of the count-weighted mean number of distinct next-prefixes per prefix class, aggregated over all levels from the same training counts, so every level probes at one radius r_ref = R_ref * log1p(exp(H_ref))/log1p(|I|). Everything else, including c = 1, exp_0^c, d_c and R_ref, is unchanged.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c -> 0 limit implemented explicitly: probe = (r_l/max(||z||,eps)) z, distance = ||.||_2, commitment = squared L2, with r_l computed exactly as in the both-on run. Behaviour stays live, the curve is gone.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full mechanism, H_l -> r_l -> exp_0^c -> argmin d_c, with c = 1 and the curved commitment. Only this arm is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: pseudocode
  # constants: c=1; L=4; K=256; d=32; eps=1e-8; N_max=|I|=24556
  # ---- once per epoch, training data only
  Z = encoder(all_training_items)                    # (|I|, d)
  R_ref = quantile(||Z||_2, 0.90)
  for l in 1..L:
      H_l = mean_p [ -sum_q phat(q|p) log phat(q|p) ] # nats, from T
      N_l = exp(H_l)
      r_l = R_ref * log1p(N_l) / log1p(N_max)         # NOT clamped
      log(H_l, N_l, r_l, r_l / R_ref)
  # ---- every step, every level
  u     = z / max(||z||, eps)
  probe = tanh(sqrt(c) * r_l) * u / sqrt(c)           # = exp_0^c(r_l u)
  D     = 1 + 2c||probe - e_k||^2 / ((1-c||probe||^2)(1-c||e_k||^2))
  dk    = arcosh(D) / sqrt(c)                         # Poincare distance
  k*    = argmin_k dk ;  q_l = e_{k*}^l               # straight-through
  L_commit += d_c(z, q_l)^2                           # curved commitment
  z     = z - sg(q_l)                                 # baseline RQ residual
  L_rec  = ||x - dec(z_L + sum_l q_l)||^2             # baseline decoder
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: current latent z_l(x) of shape (B, d); level codebook E_l of shape (K, d) with rows inside the ball; per-epoch scalars H_l, N_l, R_ref and derived r_l; fixed curvature c = 1. Outputs: the probe point in the ball, the chosen code index k*_l per item (hence the SID token), the curved commitment scalar, and the gradient flowing back into the encoder through the straight-through path and the commitment.
SAFEGUARDS: (a) latent norms clamped from below by eps only as a division guard, never from above, because the behaviour map's image is bounded by the measured R_ref; (b) R_ref is the 0.90 quantile, not the max, so r_l <= R_ref; with the observed latent scale ||z|| in roughly [0.05, 0.60] this puts r_l in about [0.04, 0.45] against a ball radius of 1, and the applied ratio r_l/R_ref spans about 0.07 to 1.0 (a 14x working range, never saturated) - that is the range a reviewer asks to see; (c) arcosh computed as log1p(D-1+sqrt((D-1)(D+1))) with D floored at 1+1e-7; (d) ||probe|| and ||e_k|| clamped to 1-1e-7 before the (1-c||.||^2) factors, with a counter logging how often the clamp fires (expected zero); (e) H_l floored at 0 nats and exp(H_l) evaluated in the log domain via expm1/log1p to avoid overflow at log(|I|) = 10.1; (f) r_l is a piecewise-constant tensor detached from the graph, so no gradient enters the behaviour statistic; (g) if the per-level entropy spread falls below 0.05 nats the code logs a warning and continues - the mechanism then degenerates to constant radius by construction, which is a measurable outcome, not a crash.
END IDEA
