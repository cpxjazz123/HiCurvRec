IDEA: dualramp-caprq
TITLE: Dual-ramped curvature with sign-flipped behaviour cost and a capacity-balanced deepest RQ layer
MOTIVATION: Curvature in the first RQ layer buys the first-order gain (valid_l1_hard_er 16.39 -> 2.49/3.89, positive_hit_rate 0.079 -> 0.12/0.26) but the same uniform curvature at every layer crushes the deepest codebook (l3_collision 0.549 and 0.943), blocking the 35% gate and therefore Stage3. The needed asymmetry: coarse SIDs pulled together by co-consumption so prefixes are predictable, fine SIDs pushed apart so substitutable next items stay distinguishable and the deep codebook stays occupied.
NOVELTY: Curvature is a LAYER RAMP reaching exactly zero at the deepest RQ layer and the behaviour term flips sign with depth (complement/transition attraction at layers 1-2, branch-alternative repulsion at layer 3), both inside the SAME cost matrix that drives assignment; the deepest layer also gets an explicit capacity (each codeword used ceil(B/K) times) so occupancy cannot collapse by construction. It is not cross-chart holonomy transport, not popularity-derived area-law radii, not per-codeword reach curvature at uniform intensity.
CHANGE: The residual and the codebook of layer l both pass through the same exponential map exp_0^{kappa_l} into one Poincare chart with curvature [kappa0, kappa0/2, 0]; squared Poincare distance minus a scaled per-(item,codeword) behaviour mass is the cost; the code is that cost's argmin at layers 1-2 and a capacitated transport argmax at layer 3; straight-through and tangent-space reconstruction are preserved.
SPECIFICATION: L=3 codebooks C_l in R^{256 x d} (K=256, d=encoder width, not swept); frozen kappa0=1/median_b||z_b-mean(z)||^2 from step 0; kappa_l=kappa0*(L-l)/(L-1) -> [k0,k0/2,0]; eps_l=median_{i,j}m_ij^l and tau_l=0.1*mean_{i,j}m_ij^l (per step, detached); beta=0.25; eta=1e-3; T=3 Sinkhorn iterations; 50-step behaviour warmup with tau=0; cap=ceil(B/K)=4.
Per step: r=z=Enc(x) [B=1024,d]; for l=1..3: Rt=exp0^{kappa_l}(r), Ct=exp0^{kappa_l}(C_l), m_ij=dkappa_l(Rt_i,Ct_j)^2; B_l=A_l (l<=2) or R_l (l=3); uhat=detach(clip(B_l(i,j)/(mean_j B_l(i,j)+1e-8),-3,3)); C_ij=m_ij-s_l*tau_l*uhat with s=[+1,+1,-1]; l<3: k_i=argmin_j C_ij; l=3: P=log-domain Sinkhorn(-C/eps_3, rows 1/B, cols 1/K, T=3), then capacitated greedy over ascending (C_ij,-log P_ij) with cap=4; q_i=C_l[k_i]; commit_l=0.25*dkappa_l(sg[Rt_i],Ct_{k_i})^2 if kappa_l>0 else ||sg[r_i]-C_l[k_i]||^2; r <- r+(q_i-r).detach(); z_q=sum_l q_l; L_tot=||x-Dec(z_q)||^2+sum_l[||sg[r_l]-C_l[k]||^2+beta*||r_l-sg[C_l[k]]||^2+commit_l].
COST: One run, 3000 Stage-2 epochs x 24 steps x B=1024 over 24556 training items; no extra forward pass and no new loss weight. Per step over the baseline quantizer: exp/log on B+K vectors per layer (negligible), the 3-layer curved distance at the same B*K*d FLOP count (~25M MAC), one 3-iteration Sinkhorn at layer 3 (3*B*K=0.8M) and one B*K sort (0.26M elements, ~1-3 ms GPU): roughly +3% to +6% wall-clock per step (~1.05x the frozen budget). One-off CPU precompute of sparse transition/branch tables (~0.5M entries, minutes).
HYPOTHESIS: If deep collapse (not curvature in general) is the blocker, then kappa_3=0 plus layer-3 branch repulsion plus cap=4 per codeword drops l3_collision from 0.549/0.943 to below 0.35 while valid_l1_hard_er stays <=4 and positive_hit_rate >

=0.12, keeping refinement >=2.3 bits and level2_distinct_prefixes >=4000, and Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.0638 with >0.065 the target. Falsified if l3_collision stays >0.35, or ER >4, or hit rate <0.12, or test_R@10 <=0.0638.
RISKS: Capacity balancing may force semantically poor layer-3 codes and push hard ER above 4 (main risk); shallow complement attraction may re-inflate level-1/2 collisions and undo the curvature gain; the EMA prefix table lags early so the behaviour cost is noise for hundreds of steps; sparse predecessor counts are head-item dominated so repulsion may concentrate on popular items; the B*K sort adds a small sync per step; single-seed noise of a few 1e-3 on recall could hide a small real gain.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transitions (next-item co-consumption) and user-interest branching (items sharing a predecessor set but not linked by a transition), as per-(item,codeword) masses from the Stage-2 training split only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training sequences only: N(a->b)=count of consecutive (a,b) pairs; row-stochastic w(a,b)=N(a->b)/sum_b' N(a->b). Complement mass A_l(i,j)=sum_{n in Top50(i) by w(i,.)} w(i,n)*1[pi_l(n)=j]. Branch mass: Pred(i)=top 10 predecessors by N(s->i); for pairs (i,n) with N(i->n)+N(n->i)=0, b(i,n)=sum_{s in Pred(i) cap Pred(n)} min(w(s,i),w(s,n)); keep top 16 by b, normalise so sum_n b(i,n)=1; R_l(i,j)=sum_n b(i,n)*1[pi_l(n)=j]; pi_l = stop-gradient EMA (eta=1e-3) of the model's own hard training assignments.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Both tables are built once at step 0 from the Stage-2 training split only (asserted on the split name, hard failure otherwise); pi_l is an EMA of assignments produced on training mini-batches. Validation/test interactions are never read, counted, or fed into any SID, cost, curvature, capacity or codebook; the validation split enters only post-hoc diagnostic reporting after the epoch SIDs are frozen.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature per layer kappa_l in {kappa0, kappa0/2, 0}, kappa0=1/median||z-mean||^2; the operations are exp_0^kappa(v)=tanh(sqrt(kappa)||v||)/(sqrt(kappa)||v||)*v, its inverse log map, and the Poincare distance d_kappa(u,v)=(1/sqrt(kappa))*arcosh(1+2*kappa*||u-v||^2/((1-kappa*||u||^2)(1-kappa*||v||^2))); at kappa_3=0 they reduce to identity and the Euclidean norm.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ quantization itself, at the assignment step of every layer, and through the scale-matched commit term in the codebook parameter update; the layer-3 residual is the residual of curved layers 1-2, so curvature still reaches layer 3 through the residual chain although its own metric is flat.
COUPLING PATH: One cost matrix per layer whose metric is the curved distance and whose additive term is that same layer's behaviour mass; the argmin (or capacitated transport argmax at layer 3) of that single matrix picks the codeword, the codeword sets the next residual, and the prefix statistics pi_l through which the behaviour mass is gathered are the model's own curved decisions; tau=0 changes which code wins and kappa0=0 changes the metric inside the same expression.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ij^(l) = d_{kappa_l}(exp_0^{kappa_l}(r_i), exp_0^{kappa_l}(c_j))^2 - s_l*tau_l*clip(B_l(i,j)/mean_j B_l(i,j), -3, +3), with kappa=[kappa0,kappa0/2,0], s=[+1,+1,-1], B_l=A_l for l<=2 and R_l for l=3, tau_l=0.1*mean_ij m_ij^(l).
OBSERVABLES: l3_collision below 0.35 (was 0.549/0.943/1.0); per-layer usage entropy near log 256 at layer 3 (capacity forces 4 it

ems per codeword per batch); valid_l1_hard_er <=4 and not back in the 16.39 band; positive_hit_rate >=0.12; refinement >=2.3 bits; level2_distinct_prefixes >=4000; per-layer codebook gradient norms and row-wise commit distances shift with the ramp; final Stage3 test_recall@10 above 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: tau_l=0 for all layers, i.e. the pure layer-ramped curved-metric capacity-balanced assignment (config flag exists, not trained this round).
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: kappa0=0 so kappa_l=0 everywhere, exp/log become identity and d_kappa becomes Euclidean while behaviour masses, sign schedule, capacity and straight-through path stay unchanged (flag exists, not trained).
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupled run, kappa=[kappa0,kappa0/2,0] with behaviour attraction at layers 1-2, branch repulsion at layer 3 and capacity-balanced layer 3; the only arm trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: kappa0=1/median_b||z_b-mean(z)||^2 (step 0, detached); kappa_l=kappa0*(3-l)/2; exp0^k(v)=tanh(sqrt(k)||v||)/(sqrt(k)||v||+1e-12)*v (identity if k=0); dk(u,v) as above (||u-v||^2 if k=0); m_ij^l=dk(Rt_i,Ct_j)^2; uhat=clip(B_l(i,j)/(mean_j B_l(i,j)+1e-8),-3,3).detach(); C_ij^l=m_ij^l-s_l*tau_l*uhat; k_i=argmin_j C_ij^l for l<3; for l=3: K_ij=exp(-C_ij/eps_3), log-domain Sinkhorn T=3 with row marginal 1/B and column marginal 1/K, then order pairs by (C_ij,-log P_ij) ascending and greedily assign with remaining capacity cap=ceil(B/K); q_i=C_l[k_i]; r_{l+1}=r_l+(q_i-r_l).detach(); L_tot=||x-Dec(sum_l q^{(l)})||^2+sum_l[||sg(r_l)-q^{(l)}||^2+0.25*||r_l-sg(q^{(l)})||^2+commit_l], commit_l=0.25*dk(Rt_l,Ct_{k})^2 if kappa_l>0 else ||sg(r_l)-q||^2.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latents z in R^{B x d} (B=1024), codebooks C_1..C_3 in R^{256 x d}, frozen kappa0, per-step eps_3 and tau_l, behaviour masses A_l/R_l gathered for the batch from the training-only sparse tables and the stop-gradient prefix EMA pi_l, and reconstruction target x. Outputs: hard code indices k_1,k_2,k_3 per item (the SIDs consumed by Stage 3), the tangent quantized vector z_q=sum_l C_l[k_l] for the decoder, per-layer commit values for the codebook update, and diagnostics (usage histogram, entropy, collision/prefix counts).
SAFEGUARDS: assert B>=K and cap>=1; kappa_l>=0 with an exact Euclidean branch at kappa_l=0 (limit forms) so layer 3 never divides by zero; clamp ball norm to (1-1e-5)/sqrt(kappa) so tanh saturation cannot zero the distance denominator; floor distance denominators at 1e-6; log-domain Sinkhorn with row-max subtraction and fixed 3 iterations, eps_3 floored at 1e-6; all buffer statistics detached; tau=0 for the first 50 steps and while the prefix EMA is cold; a non-finite guard replacing a non-finite cost row by the Euclidean distance row with a logged counter; deterministic stable double-key sort in the capacity greedy; behaviour-table build asserts the training split name and fails hard otherwise; no validation/test data read inside any of these paths.
END IDEA
