IDEA: strataot-curv-rq
TITLE: Stratified-curvature, behaviour-marginal hyperbolic Sinkhorn assignment for RQ-VAE Semantic IDs
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE SIDs are assigned by nearest-codeword under a flat metric, so an item and its habitual successors land in unrelated prefixes and the deepest level saturates on a few codewords, capping Stage3 next-item recall. Curvature fixed level-1 separation but crushed level 3, because deep residuals are small and sit exactly where the curved metric distorts most. The mechanism therefore needs curvature that literally decays with depth plus a spreading force at depth that is behavioural rather than geometric.
NOVELTY: None of the three prior curvature arms used a transport plan. Here the assignment itself is a doubly-stochastic entropic optimal-transport plan: the ground cost is a per-layer Poincare geodesic distance whose curvature radius is tied to that layer's remaining residual mass (so curvature decays layer by layer and level 3 is nearly Euclidean), the cost carries a second term to the Karcher mean of the item's training-successor codewords (per-pair behaviour), the column marginal is uniform (the explicit deep-layer spreading force that the collapsed arms lacked), and the straight-through path is the plan itself. No path transport (holonomy), no popularity-derived per-codeword radii (GEOCAP), no per-codeword curvature field uniform across layers (OCCURV-FIELD).
CHANGE: The layer-l RQ assignment becomes the argmax of a Sinkhorn plan on a curvature-normalised geodesic cost matrix augmented with the geodesic distance from each codeword to the hyperbolic centroid of the item's training-successor codewords; reconstruction keeps the hard codeword in the forward pass and takes encoder and codebook gradients through the plan.
SPECIFICATION: Per layer l in 1..3, per training step: build residual matrix R (B x d) from the frozen encoder/previous levels; map each residual into the Poincare ball with exp_0 under curvature kappa_l = -1/a_l^2 where a_l = q_l * S_1/S_l from last epoch's measured 90th-percentile residual norm q_l and mean remaining residual mass S_l; build cost C_ic = d_(kappa_l)(u_i,e_c)^2/a_l^2 + d_(kappa_l)(e_c,b_i)^2/a_l^2 with b_i the 3-step Karcher mean of the successor codewords taken from the previous-epoch hard-code table; compute P = Sinkhorn(C/eps_l) with eps_l = mean_i min_c C_ic, 16 log-domain iterations, marginals 1/B and 1/K; assign c_i = argmax_c P_ic; quantise with z_i^l = e_{c_i} + (sum_c P_ic e_c - sg(sum_c P_ic e_c)); next residual r_i^{l+1} = r_i^l - z_i^l; baseline reconstruction and commitment losses unchanged, no new loss term. Switches USE_BEHAVIOUR and USE_HYPERBOLIC exist and are correct; only both-on is trained.
COST: Stage 2 only. Per step (B~1024 residuals, K codewords per level, L=3, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps) the mechanism adds 3*B*K geodesic terms (about 0.79M at K=256, 3.1M at K=1024), 3 Karcher means of 8 neighbours and 16 log-domain Sinkhorn sweeps on 3 matrices of size B x K: roughly +10 to +18 percent step time and a few MB of activation memory. The successor code table is 24556 x 3 bytes; curvature statistics are one percentile per layer per epoch.
HYPOTHESIS: If level-3 collapse is caused by curvature at depth rather than by the RQ residual itself, then per-layer curvature decaying with remaining residual mass plus a balanced transport marginal must keep valid_l1_hard_er <= 4 and positive_hit_rate >= 0.12 while pushing l3_collision under 0.35, level2_distinct_prefixes above 4000 and refinement near 2.8 bits, and that configuration is what can move Stage3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 (baseline 0.0555, historical best 0.0638). Observe per-layer codeword usage entropy: it should be flat across the three layers instead of collapsing at level 3.
RISKS: RQ residual mass may not decay enough for a_l and kappa_l to separate the layers, leaving level 3 curved and collapsing again.; The epoch-lagged successor code table can oscillate or stall early while assignments are still moving.; Column-uniform Sinkhorn balancing can fight per-item identity and raise level-1 hard ER above the 4 target.; Items with no training successor drop the behaviour term and dilute the coupling.; Per-layer percentiles are noisy at the start of training before the residual scale settles.; Behavioural successor coherence can merge prefixes of consecutive items and lose discriminability the generator needs.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions (sequential co-consumption) carried per item-successor pair as integer weights w_ij, together with each successor's previous-epoch hard codeword at the same layer.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only: for each item i count directed transitions i->j inside the training sequences, keep the top M=8 successors by count as Succ(i) with weights w_ij equal to those counts. Once per epoch, record every training item's hard codeword at each layer T_l[i] from that epoch's forward pass; the next epoch consumes the previous table. No other statistic is used.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Successor lists, successor weights and the codeword table T_l come exclusively from training-split sequences and from encoder forward passes on training batches. Validation and test interactions are never read, cached or used in the codebook, the curvature radii, the transport marginals or the successor targets, so no validation or test interaction can enter SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance d_(kappa_l) with an explicit per-layer negative curvature kappa_l = -1/a_l^2 (a_l = q_l * S_1/S_l derived from measured residual scales), its exponential map exp_0 used to inject each residual into the ball, its logarithmic map log_p used inside the Karcher mean, and the same curvature used for assignment and reconstruction. |kappa_l| decreases with depth and layer 3 is nearly flat yet still evaluated through the curved map.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the layer-l RQ quantisation step of Stage 2 (assignment cost, behaviour target and reconstruction), separately at each of the L=3 residual levels, with the same curvature and the same codeword positions used for assignment and for reconstruction.
COUPLING PATH: One cost matrix per layer, C_ic = d_(kappa_l)(u_i,e_c)^2/a_l^2 + d_(kappa_l)(e_c,b_i^l)^2/a_l^2 with b_i^l the Karcher mean of the successor codewords, feeds a Sinkhorn plan whose argmax assigns the code and whose soft form carries the gradient. Removing behaviour removes b_i and changes the cost and the gradient; removing curvature changes the distances, the exp/log maps, the Karcher mean and the layer schedule; dropping either leaves a different core computation.
COUPLING EQUATION: C_ic = [d_(kappa_l)(exp_0^{kappa_l}(r_i^l), e_c)]^2 / a_l^2 + [d_(kappa_l)(e_c, Frechet_{kappa_l}({e_{T_l[j]} : j in Succ(i), w_ij}))]^2 / a_l^2 ; P = Sinkhorn(C/eps_l, rows 1/B, cols 1/K) ; c_i = argmax_c P_ic ; z_i^l = e_{c_i} + (sum_c P_ic e_c - sg(sum_c P_ic e_c)) ; where the behaviour term is the Karcher-mean successor code centroid b_i and the curved term is d_(kappa_l) with kappa_l = -1/a_l^2.
OBSERVABLES: valid_l1_hard_er (expect <= 4), positive_hit_rate (expect >= 0.12), level2_distinct_prefixes (expect > 4000), l3_collision (expect <= 0.35), refinement bits (expect ~2.8), per-layer codeword usage entropy (expect flat instead of collapsing at level 3), the transport plan's column-marginal residual, and Stage3 test_recall@10 as the sole judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: USE_BEHAVIOUR=False: b_i term deleted, cost reduces to d_(kappa_l)(u_i,e_c)^2/a_l^2 only, no successor table is read; curvature schedule and Sinkhorn unchanged. Present and correct in code, never trained.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: USE_HYPERBOLIC=False: kappa_l = 0, exp_0 and log_p become identity, d becomes Euclidean ||.||/a_l, the Karcher mean becomes the weighted arithmetic mean, and the same Sinkhorn runs on Euclidean squared distances; behaviour term unchanged. Present and correct in code, never trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The single trained candidate: per-layer decaying kappa_l, geodesic cost plus Karcher-mean behaviour term, Sinkhorn with uniform column marginal and plan-based straight-through, baseline reconstruction and commitment losses. One run, one seed, no early stopping.
SPEC EQUATIONS: (1) Per epoch, from training batches: q_l = 90th percentile of ||r_i^l||, S_l = mean_i sum_{m>=l} ||r_i^m||, a_l = q_l * S_1 / S_l, kappa_l = -1/a_l^2. (2) exp_0^{kappa}(v) = tanh(sqrt(|kappa|)||v||/2) * v / (sqrt(|kappa|)||v||). (3) d_(kappa)(p,q) = (2/sqrt(|kappa|)) * artanh( sqrt(|kappa|) * ||(-p) xor q|| / sqrt((1-|kappa||p|^2)(1-|kappa||q|^2)) ), divided by a_l for scale-free costs. (4) Karcher: p <- e_{T[j*]} with j* the most frequent successor; repeat 3 times: p <- exp_p( sum_j w_ij log_p(e_{T[j]}) / sum_j w_ij ). (5) C_ic = d_(kappa_l)(u_i,e_c)^2/a_l^2 + d_(kappa_l)(e_c,b_i)^2/a_l^2. (6) eps_l = mean_i min_c C_ic; P = 16-iteration log-domain Sinkhorn with marginals 1/B and 1/K. (7) c_i = argmax_c P_ic (lowest index on ties); z_i^l = e_{c_i} + (sum_c P_ic e_c - sg(sum_c P_ic e_c)); r_i^{l+1} = r_i^l - z_i^l. (8) Losses unchanged from the baseline RQ-VAE; no extra loss term.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: layer-l residual matrix R (B x d) from the frozen encoder and the previous levels, codebook E_l (K x d), the training-split successor lists and weights, the previous-epoch codeword table T_l, the previous-epoch scalars q_l and S_l. Outputs: hard code indices c_i, quantised vector z_i^l whose forward value equals e_{c_i}, next residual r_i^{l+1}, the plan P (detached after use), and refreshed q_l, S_l and T_l at epoch end.
SAFEGUARDS: Log-domain Sinkhorn with the cost clamped at 1e3*eps_l; eps_l floored at 1e-8 and rescaled when a row has near-equal costs; Taylor branch for exp_0 when ||v|| < 1e-6; artanh argument clamped to 1-1e-7; a_l floored at 1e-3 and |kappa_l| capped at 1e-4 so the near-Euclidean deep layer never divides by zero; q_l falls back to the running value when an epoch has fewer than 100 residuals at that layer; items with empty Succ(i) get the behaviour term dropped for that row; T_l refreshed only at epoch boundaries and always detached from the graph; successor lists and the codeword table receive no gradient.
END IDEA
