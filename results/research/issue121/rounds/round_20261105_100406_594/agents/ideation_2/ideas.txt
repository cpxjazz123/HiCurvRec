IDEA: signcurv-ladder-rq-2026
TITLE: Signed per-level curvature ladder with behaviour-oriented pair occupancy: shallow Poincare attraction, deepest-level spherical repulsion, both inside the RQ argmin
MOTIVATION: In a 3-level RQ-VAE SID the shallow levels must GROUP behaviour-adjacent items (a shared prefix is what lets the generator name the next item's neighbourhood) while the deepest level must SEPARATE behaviour-confusable items (otherwise the last token cannot disambiguate which successor actually comes next). All three previously run curvature arms used one sign and one strength at every level: they bought level-1 separation (valid_l1_hard_er 2.488-3.885 vs 16.390 baseline, positive_hit_rate 0.0794 -> 0.1222-0.2629) and then destroyed level-3 usage (l3_collision 0.549, 0.943, 1.0), so the SID was rejected by the 35% gate and Stage3 could not benefit.
NOVELTY: Not holonomy transport, not popularity-derived capacity radii, not a per-occupant curvature field of uniform strength. Here the CURVATURE SIGN ITSELF is a per-level ladder (negative shallow -> positive deepest, magnitude halving per level) and the SAME sign table orients a per-pair behaviour-occupancy term that lives inside the assignment score, so behaviour ATTRACTS on shallow levels and REPELS on the deepest level; the behaviour weight is a train-split transition log-odds thresholded at independence rather than a tuned constant, and the deep level's spreading is structural (sphere + pair repulsion) instead of a usage-balancing loss.
CHANGE: The RQ assignment at level l becomes argmin_k over a curved geodesic distance on M(kappa_l) (Poincare ball if kappa_l<0, sphere if kappa_l>0) plus sigma_l*gamma_l times the transition-log-odds mass of behaviour neighbours already occupying code k, with the exp/log maps used identically for the residual entry and the codeword positions.
SPECIFICATION: PARAMS: L=3; K=codebook size (baseline 256); d=latent width; tangent codebooks c_l in R^{K x d}; ladder s_curv=[-1,-1,+1]; sigma=s_curv; R_bar=detach(rms over the first training batch of ||z||); kappa_0=1/R_bar^2 (frozen); kappa_l=s_curv[l]*kappa_0*2^-(l-1). BEHAVIOUR (built once, train split only): n_ij=count of consecutive (i->j) transitions; n_i=out mass; T=sum n_ij; b_ij=clip(log(n_ij*T/(n_i*n_j)),0,log T)/log T. FORWARD: z=Enc(x); r_1=z; for l=1..L: u_l=exp_{kappa_l}(r_l); p_lk=exp_{kappa_l}(c_l[k]); dist_lk=d_{kappa_l}(u_l,p_lk); nb_lk=scatter_sum over j in N(i) and in the batch of b_ij*1[khat_{l,j}^{prev}=k]; score_lk=dist_lk+sigma_l*gamma_l*nb_lk; k_l=argmin_k score_lk; rhat_l=r_l+sg(c_l[k_l]-r_l); r_{l+1}=r_l-c_l[k_l]; xhat=Dec(sum_l c_l[k_l]); SID(i)=(k_1,k_2,k_3). gamma_l=frozen median of min_k dist_lk over the first forward batch. Loss = baseline RQ-VAE reconstruction + commitment on c_l[k_l]; the behaviour term exists only inside the argmin, so there is no extra loss weight to tune. Occupancy buffer khat^{prev} from the previous epoch, no_grad, refreshed once per epoch.
COST: Same number of forward and backward passes (3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 steps), same batch sizes. Extra per step: two exp/log maps and L*K closed-form geodesic-distance evaluations over d-dim vectors (tanh/atanh or tan/arccos, no iteration) plus one sparse scatter over in-batch behaviour pairs (roughly 10-30 neighbours per anchor), i.e. about +5% to +10% wall clock per step. Memory overhead is negligible: occupancy buffer 24556 x 3 x int16 = 0.15 MB plus the sparse train-item b_ij table. No extra epoch, no ablation arm, no sweep, no second seed.
HYPOTHESIS: If the level-3 collapse was caused by one-sign curvature attracting behaviour-adjacent items into the same deep code, then the sign-flipped deep level plus per-pair repulsion must move l3_collision from the observed 0.549-1.0 down to <=0.35 while keeping valid_l1_hard_er <=4, positive_hit_rate >=0.12 and level2_distinct_prefixes in the thousands, and Stage3 test_recall@10 must exceed 0.065 (baseline 0.0555, best 0.0638). It is falsified if l3_collision stays above 0.5, if valid_l1_hard_er returns to the 16.4 baseline magnitude, or if refinement bits fall below the 2.32 bits measured for OCCURV-FIELD.
RISKS: Repulsion restricted to behaviour neighbours may not de-collide the items with no in-batch neighbour, leaving l3_collision above the 35% gate.; The frozen first-batch median gamma_l can be mis-scaled if the first batch is atypical, making the behaviour term either inert or dominant at level 3.; The sphere level (kappa_3>0) bounds distances, so arccos gradients saturate near-antipodal codewords and can freeze their learning.; The previous-epoch occupancy buffer lags, so an item can be pushed into a code that the next epoch vacates, producing oscillation of level-3 usage.; Even with a clean SID, Stage3 may not convert the gain if the generator's prefix likelihood does not exploit the extra level-3 distinction.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-transition log-odds (a behaviour-conditioned pair weight), computed over consecutive items of TRAIN sequences and restricted at use time to pairs whose two endpoints are both in the current batch.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: One pass over TRAIN sequences only: n_ij = number of consecutive (i->j) transitions, n_i = sum_j n_ij, T = sum_ij n_ij; then b_ij = clip(log(n_ij*T/(n_i*n_j)), 0, log T)/log T, so 0 means 'no more than chance' (threshold at independence) and 1 means 'maximally over-represented'. Stored as a sparse train-item x train-item table, together with the per-epoch hard SID buffer khat^{prev}_{l,i} produced by forward passes over TRAIN items only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition counts, the b_ij table and the occupancy buffer are built exclusively from TRAIN sequences and TRAIN items; validation interactions are used only after an epoch to log diagnostics and never to build codes, curvatures, gamma_l, R_bar or b_ij; no test interaction of any kind enters SID construction. Cold items get zero rows in b_ij and are quantized by the curved distance alone, so validation or test co-occurrence cannot be smuggled into a prefix.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Closed-form exponential and logarithmic maps and geodesic distance of a signed-curvature manifold: Poincare ball of radius 1/sqrt(|kappa_l|) when kappa_l<0, with exp_k(x)=tanh(sqrt(|k|)||x||)/(sqrt(|k|)||x||) * x and d_k(u,v)=(1/sqrt|k|) arcosh(1+2|k||u-v|^2/((1-|k||u|^2)(1-|k||v|^2))); sphere of radius 1/sqrt(kappa_l) when kappa_l>0, with exp_k(x)=tan(sqrt(k)||x||)/(sqrt(k)||x||) * x and d_k(u,v)=(1/sqrt(k)) arccos(clamp(k<u,v>)); identity and L2 when kappa_l=0. Base magnitude kappa_0=1/R_bar^2 from the measured RMS latent norm, ladder kappa_l=s_curv[l]*kappa_0*2^-(l-1) with s_curv=[-1,-1,+1].
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the Stage-2 RQ-VAE training loop, jointly in (a) the residual entry exp_{kappa_l}(r_l), (b) the codeword position exp_{kappa_l}(c_l[k]) and its reconstruction log_{kappa_l}, and (c) the geodesic distance that forms the assignment score, at every level l=1..3 with the level-dependent sign and magnitude.
COUPLING PATH: A single scalar is the meeting point: the per-level argmin score. The curved geodesic distance defines the geometry of that scalar and the behaviour occupancy mass is added to the same scalar before the argmin, and both are oriented by the same per-level sign table sigma_l=s_curv[l]. Setting b_ij=0 leaves a pure signed-ladder curved RQ (the geometry still chooses every code); setting kappa_l=0 makes the distance and the maps Euclidean while the sigma ladder and the behaviour mass keep acting, so the code path still assigns differently. Removing either changes the actual discrete code selected at every level, not a side loss.
COUPLING EQUATION: score_{l,i,k} = d_{M(kappa_l)}( exp_{kappa_l}(r_{l,i}), exp_{kappa_l}(c_l[k]) ) + sigma_l * gamma_l * sum_{j in N(i)} b_ij * 1[khat_{l,j}^{prev} = k], with sigma_l = s_curv[l] and k_{l,i} = argmin_k score_{l,i,k}.
OBSERVABLES: l3_collision (must fall below 0.35), level2_distinct_prefixes (must stay in the thousands), valid_l1_hard_er (<=4), positive_hit_rate (>=0.12), refinement bits (>=2.3), per-level usage entropy and top-1 codeword share per level, prefix next-item predictability (conditional entropy of the true successor given each prefix length), and Stage3 test_recall@10 as the only judgement (target >0.065).
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: b_ij:=0 for all pairs, which leaves a pure signed-curvature-ladder RQ (exp/log residual entry, geodesic assignment, sphere deep level) with the behaviour mass absent. The switch exists in the code and is unit-checked, but no arm is trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: kappa_l:=0 for all l, so exp/log become identity, distances become Euclidean L2 and codewords are plain points, while sigma_l and the behaviour occupancy mass stay active; this isolates what the curvature contributes inside the same coupled score. The switch exists and is unit-checked, no arm is trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full mechanism exactly as specified (signed ladder with kappa_0=1/R_bar^2, s_curv=[-1,-1,+1], train-split b_ij, gamma_l frozen from the first batch). This is the ONE candidate that is trained, for 3000 Stage-2 epochs on four cards and then the frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: kappa_0 = 1/R_bar^2 with R_bar = detach(rms_batch(||z||)) computed once and checkpointed; kappa_l = s_curv[l]*kappa_0*2^-(l-1), s_curv=[-1,-1,+1]; b_ij = clip(log(n_ij*T/(n_i*n_j)),0,log T)/log T; gamma_l = detach(median over the first batch of min_k d_{M(kappa_l)}(exp(r_{l,i}),exp(c_l[k]))); u_l = exp_{kappa_l}(r_l); p_lk = exp_{kappa_l}(c_l[k]); score_lk = d_{M(kappa_l)}(u_l,p_lk) + sigma_l*gamma_l*sum_{j in N(i) intersect batch} b_ij*1[khat_{l,j}^{prev}=k]; k_l = argmin_k score_lk; rhat_l = r_l + sg(c_l[k_l]-r_l); r_{l+1} = r_l - c_l[k_l]; L = ||x - Dec(sum_l c_l[k_l])||^2 + beta*sum_l ||r_l - sg(c_l[k_l])||^2 with the baseline beta, and no additional weight on the behaviour term because it acts only through the argmin.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latent z (width d) for each train item; the L tangent codebooks c_l (K x d); the sparse train-split b_ij table; the previous-epoch SID buffer khat^{prev}. Outputs: one SID per item (k_1,k_2,k_3) with its prefix hierarchy, the per-level quantized tangent vectors c_l[k_l] fed to the decoder and the reconstructed sum used by the decoder, the updated codebooks, the refreshed SID buffer at the end of each epoch, and the stored constants R_bar, kappa_l and gamma_l kept in the checkpoint for exact reproduction.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp the ball radius ||u|| <= (1-1e-3)/sqrt(|kappa|) before arcosh; use the series 2*artanh(x)/x -> 2 for small sqrt(|kappa|)||x|| and 2*arctan(x)/x -> 2 for small sqrt(kappa)||x|| so the maps never evaluate 0/0; clamp the arccos argument to [-(1-1e-6), 1-1e-6]; clamp sqrt(kappa)||r|| < pi/(2*sqrt(kappa)) - 1e-3 before tan so sphere points stay inside the chart; guard the Poincare denominator (1-|kappa||u|^2) with 1e-8; freeze gamma_l and R_bar after the first forward pass and mask them from the optimizer; build the occupancy buffer and b_ij under no_grad; skip the behaviour term for anchors with no in-batch neighbour and for cold items; fall back to Euclidean assignment (kappa_l=0) if R_bar is zero or non-finite; keep the straight-through path and the commitment loss in the graph so the encoder cannot collapse to a constant as it did in the holonomy run; log per-level usage entropy every epoch so a level-3 collapse is visible without ever gating on it.
END IDEA
