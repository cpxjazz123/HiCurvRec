IDEA: poincare-frechet-successor-codebook
TITLE: Curved successor-weighted Fréchet codebook updates for the RQ-VAE residual quantizer
MOTIVATION: The SID vocabulary is not the encoder, it is the codebook. Standard RQ-VAE trains every item latent with equal weight, so codebook slots are spent on popular but behaviourally interchangeable items, and the first prefixes do not separate items that actually follow one another. Directed successor flow is the cheapest evidence of which items deserve to share a slot or a prefix, yet it currently never reaches the estimator that produces the codebook.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds placed behaviour and curvature in the assignment step (Poincaré-cost Sinkhorn, successor-fork angles, cones, shells, geodesic charts, target transport). This proposal leaves the assignment cost untouched and instead changes the estimator that produces the codebook itself: every slot is moved by a weighted Riemannian Fréchet step in the Poincaré ball, so the SID vocabulary is learned, not merely assigned, under a curved behaviour-weighted objective. No OT, no fork angle, no cone, no shell, no per-prefix curvature.
CHANGE: Replace the RQ-VAE codebook EMA/gradient update with a per-slot Poincaré Fréchet step whose per-item weights are the training-only, popularity-adjusted successor attraction of each item, and use the same weights inside the commitment term so the encoder and the codebook are pulled by one weighted geodesic objective.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed curvature kappa = -1 (documented default, never swept). Encoder emits e_i in R^d; map to the ball as z_i = exp_0^kappa( tanh(||e_i||) * e_i / (||e_i|| * sqrt(|kappa|)) ), which lands strictly inside the ball. For each residual level l = 1..3 with codebook C_l = {c_{l,k}} subset B^d: assignment a_l(i) = argmin_k d_kappa(z_i, c_{l,k}); commitment L_l = sum_i w_i * d_kappa(z_i, sg[c_{l,a(i)}])^2 plus reconstruction as in the baseline. Codebook update, per step, per level, for each slot k with S_k = {i : a(i) = k}: m_k = sum_{i in S_k} w_i * log^kappa_{c_k}(z_i) / sum_{i in S_k} w_i ; c_k <- exp^kappa_{c_k}( eta * m_k ), eta = 1. Residual r_i = log^kappa_{c_{l,a(i)}}(z_i) is fed to level l+1 as usual. Behaviour weights, training split only: count directed adjacent pairs (u -> v) inside train sessions, indeg_v = sum_u n_uv, n_v = train interaction count of v, w_v = (indeg_v + 1) / (n_v + 2) (Beta(1,1) posterior mean, in (0,1]), clip to [0.25, 4], then per-batch normalise w_hat_i = w_i / mean_batch(w). The codebook update is detached (sg), so it is an estimator, not a gradient path; the encoder still gets gradients through d_kappa and the commitment term. Self-transitions are removed before counting.
COST: One-off transition counting over train sessions, O(number of train transitions), under 2 s. Per step at the frozen scale (B = 1024, L = 3, d = 64): one extra Poincaré exp/log pair over B*L*d ≈ 2e5 floats plus one scatter-add, about 3–6% of step time, and a weighted commitment that is negligible. Across 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 steps this is roughly +5–10% Stage2 wall clock, with no extra forward or backward pass and only a detached tangent buffer of B*L*d floats in memory.
HYPOTHESIS: If successor-attracting items share codebook slots, then level-1 and level-2 prefixes become behaviourally predictive and the Stage3 generator's prefix-constrained decoding proposes better candidates, lifting test Recall@10 above 0.065. It is worth keeping only if the weighted Fréchet displacement concentrates on high-w slots, prefix→successor conditional entropy on train sequences falls relative to the unweighted codebook, and the Recall@10 gain is concentrated on high-w and high-predecessor-concentration items rather than spread uniformly.
RISKS: The Fréchet step can be unstable or collapse several slots onto one high-w region; the weights can degrade into a popularity proxy if indeg and n_v are too collinear; codebook drift can hurt reconstruction and therefore the generator's conditioning; a single eta = 1 step is only a first-order approximation of the weighted Fréchet mean and may lag; the gain may be smaller than single-run seed noise.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed successor attraction (smoothed in-degree rate) of each item, from session-level directed transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Built exclusively from the Stage2 training split. For every train session, for every consecutive pair (u -> v) with u != v, increment n_uv; then indeg_v = sum_u n_uv, n_v = number of train interactions of v, and w_v = (indeg_v + 1) / (n_v + 2); clip to [0.25, 4] and normalise to batch mean 1. The table is keyed by train item id only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: SID construction reads only train item latents, train sessions and the train item set. The transition table, the weights and the resulting codebooks are frozen when Stage2 ends; no validation interaction, no test interaction, no Stage3 decoder feedback and no validation-derived constant enters w or C. The Stage3 protocol and its split boundary are unchanged.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature kappa = -1 Poincaré ball: exponential and logarithmic maps at the slot centre, and the Poincaré distance d_kappa, used in the codebook Fréchet update, the assignment and the commitment term.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Stage2 RQ-VAE training, inside the residual quantizer's codebook update rule together with its assignment and commitment, at every one of the 3000 epochs; the frozen codebooks then define the SIDs consumed by Stage3.
COUPLING PATH: Item latent z_i → Poincaré log map at slot centre c_k → multiplied by the successor-attraction weight w_i → weighted tangent average m_k → Poincaré exp map back to the ball → the new slot c_k → next step's nearest-slot assignment and the emitted SID. Removing w_i makes the average uniform; removing curvature makes the same average a Euclidean weighted EMA. Either removal changes the slot, hence the SID assignment, in the same single pathway.
COUPLING EQUATION: c_k ← exp^kappa_{c_k}( eta * sum_{i in S_k} w_i * log^kappa_{c_k}(z_i) / sum_{i in S_k} w_i ), where w_i is the successor-attraction weight and exp/log are the kappa = -1 Poincaré maps.
OBSERVABLES: Which items share the first two prefixes; per-slot weighted tangent mean m_k and its norm; slot-centre trajectories across epochs; prefix→successor conditional entropy computed on train sequences; RQ-VAE reconstruction error; Stage3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: w_i = 1 for every i, so the update is the uniform Poincaré Fréchet mean with curvature and eta unchanged; commitment is unweighted.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp/log/d_kappa replaced by identity and squared L2, so the update becomes c_k ← c_k + eta * sum_i w_i (z_i - c_k) / sum_i w_i (weighted Euclidean EMA) with the behaviour weights kept.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full update above, successor weights inside the Poincaré Fréchet step with weighted commitment; this is the only arm actually trained in the round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: z = exp_0^kappa( tanh(||e||) * e / (||e|| * sqrt(|kappa|)) ); for l in 1..3: a = argmin_k d_kappa(z, c_{l,k}); L += w * d_kappa(z, sg[c_{l,a}])^2 (+ reconstruction); r = log^kappa_{c_{l,a}}(z); for each k: S_k = {i : a_i = k}; if sum_{i in S_k} w_i > 1e-6 then c_{l,k} = exp^kappa_{c_{l,k}}( sum_i w_i * log^kappa_{c_{l,k}}(z_i) / sum_i w_i ); z = exp^kappa_{c_{l,a}}(r).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are encoder latents e_i, the train-only transition counts n_uv, the current codebooks C_l, beta from the baseline, kappa = -1, eta = 1. Outputs are updated codebooks C_l (hence the SIDs), plus logged per-slot m_k, ||m_k|| and the number of assigned items per slot.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp z_i to radius (1 - 1e-3)/sqrt(|kappa|); clamp the log-map argument strictly inside the ball; skip slots whose weight sum or support is below 1e-6; never move dead slots; detach z and c in the codebook update; run exp/log in fp32; renormalise after each exp so ||c|| <= (1 - 1e-4)/sqrt(|kappa|); NaN/Inf guard skips the slot update and logs it; eta = 1, weight clip 4 and kappa = -1 are documented defaults, never tuned.
END IDEA
