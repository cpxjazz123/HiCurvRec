IDEA: succ_transport_shells
TITLE: Successor-transported Poincare coarse assignments
MOTIVATION: The next-item model needs a small, predictable T1/T2 prefix while retaining distinctions among items with different futures; plain semantic proximity can spend too many T2 codes on behaviorally indistinguishable items.
NOVELTY: Unlike the existing successor-geodesic coarse and successor-residual mechanisms, successor distributions enter a capacity-constrained assignment cost at T1/T2, rather than a post-hoc residual or unconstrained code geometry. It explicitly fixes the usable capacity split to 8/96/256/256.
CHANGE: Precompute smoothed outgoing-item distributions from training transitions, embed each distribution as a Poincare point, and solve a small balanced assignment over the fixed T1/T2 codebooks using a combined semantic and curved successor-distribution cost. T3/T4 retain ordinary residual quantization and deterministic collision extension.
SPECIFICATION: For each training item i, form p_i(j)=(n_ij+alpha*pi(j))/(n_i+alpha), where n_ij counts directed consecutive item pairs and pi is the training-item unigram distribution. Map the top-M coordinates of sqrt(p_i) through a learned linear map and exp_0^c to z_i in the curvature -c ball. At l=1,2 assign minibatch items to K_l prototypes by minimizing sum_i,k q_ik [||h_i-mu_k||^2/sigma_l^2 + lambda_l d_c(z_i,a_k)^2], with q row sums 1, column mass constrained to B/K_l (K=(8,96)); use 3 Sinkhorn iterations on the minibatch soft costs, then hard argmax for SID. Update prototypes and encoder with straight-through assignments. Later levels use baseline Euclidean residual quantization, K3=K4=256; collision extension remains deterministic.
COST: Transition counts and p_i are computed once from training data; with M=64 sparse coordinates this is offline O(E log M). Per training step adds two Nbatch x K_l cost matrices, exp/log maps and three Sinkhorn iterations; keep this cost bounded by only applying transport at T1/T2 and cache z_i. Expected smoke overhead <0.03 s/epoch over 0.13 s baseline; reject if measured one-epoch smoke exceeds 0.16 s. At 24 steps x 3000 epochs, extra work is 72,000 small assignment batches, no all-item pairwise distance matrix.
HYPOTHESIS: Successor-similar items occupy shared, low-branching coarse prefixes and distinct semantic content is deferred to T3. Expect L1 Hard ER to decrease or remain stable versus baseline, gold survival T1 >=.995, T2 >=.80, T3 .14-.20, T4 >=.08; teacher-forced T3 top-1 should rise above .11 and T4 remain >=.85. Worth keeping only if frozen Stage3 test_R@10 improves beyond 0.055537, with a target >0.065; ER alone is diagnostic, not the objective.
RISKS: Popularity smoothing may erase rare successors; minibatch balancing may be noisy or incompatible with globally balanced mass; optimizing a truncated successor distribution may group items with different tails. Excessive lambda may damage semantic reconstruction, while overly weak lambda recovers semantic-only clusters.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate successor distribution for each catalog item.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive item pairs (i,j) in training user sequences only, after the fixed training split; smooth with alpha=20 and training unigram pi, retain the top 64 outgoing coordinates plus an OTHER bucket. Do not use validation/test sequences.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table, smoothing prior, coordinates, and Poincare points are built solely from training interactions. Freeze them before SID export; validation/test are used only by the frozen Stage3 evaluation protocol, never for fitting, assignment, capacity selection, or tuning.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball exp_0^c and squared geodesic distance d_c^2 at fixed positive c=1 (sectional curvature -1); project norms below (1-epsilon)/sqrt(c).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: T1/T2 assignment logits, in the constrained Sinkhorn assignment during Stage2 training and final hard quantization.
COUPLING PATH: Training successor counts -> smoothed distribution -> Poincare point z_i; its geodesic distance to each code prototype contributes directly to the capacity-constrained assignment cost, changing the selected T1/T2 SID codes and their encoder gradients.
COUPLING EQUATION: C^l_ik = ||h_i-mu^l_k||^2/sigma_l^2 + lambda_l * d_{Poincare,c=1}(exp_0(z-map(p_i)), a^l_k)^2; Q^l=Sinkhorn(C^l, row_mass=1, col_mass=B/K_l); SID^l_i=argmax_k Q^l_ik.
OBSERVABLES: Log per-level unique codes and empirical occupancy; canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER with ten fixed negatives; gold-prefix survival at depths 1-4; teacher-forced T3/T4 top-1; and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Expected survival: T1 near 1.00, T2 at least .80, T3 .14-.20, T4 at least .08. Expected T3 accuracy improves; T4 stays high. Expected L1 Hard ER direction is down or neutral, not a success gate. Compare test_R@10 to .055537 and the >.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 and remove p_i/z_i, retaining semantic cost and the same K=(8,96,256,256), Sinkhorn and training schedule; tests whether successor information selects better groups.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace d_Poincare(z_i,a_k)^2 and exp/log maps with Euclidean ||u_i-b_k||^2, retaining the same successor features, lambda, capacities, and Sinkhorn; tests whether curvature matters.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use directed training successors embedded in curvature -1 Poincare space, geodesic assignment cost and Sinkhorn at K=(8,96,256,256); this is the proposed coupled run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=sum_u,t 1[x_ut=i,x_u,t+1=j] over training split; p_i=(n_i+alpha*pi)/(sum_j n_ij+alpha). u_i=W sqrt(p_i), z_i=exp_0^1(u_i). For each level l in {1,2}, C_ik=semantic_cost(i,k)+lambda_l*d_1(z_i,a_k)^2; Q=Sinkhorn(exp(-C/tau), row=1, col=B/K_l); hard SID=argmax_k Q_ik. Straight-through hard assignment updates semantic encoder and prototypes. Residual levels 3 and 4 use baseline quantizer with 256 codes; preserve deterministic collision extension and exactly four tokens.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training item sequences, frozen item embeddings h_i, sparse successor counts, codebooks, and batch size B. Outputs are four-token SIDs and Stage2 gradients; T1/T2 assignments have capacities 8 and 96, T3/T4 each 256 before deterministic collision extension.
SAFEGUARDS: alpha=20 smoothing, top-64 plus OTHER, clamp probabilities to >=1e-8, normalize sqrt(p), float32 distance calculations, clamp Poincare norms to (1-1e-5), clamp Sinkhorn logits to [-30,30], 3 iterations, stop-gradient through the cached behavior embedding map if unstable, and deterministic tie-breaking. Fixed capacities and no validation-driven tuning.
END IDEA

IDEA: successor-cone-hierarchy
TITLE: Directed successor cones for a fixed coarse-to-fine hyperbolic hierarchy
MOTIVATION: Beam search loses too many candidates at depth 2 when that level has hundreds of active codes; items with overlapping next-item neighborhoods should share a small prefix, while divergent futures should separate at the 256-way third level.
NOVELTY: This builds a hierarchy by fitting directed successor prediction cones in a negatively curved latent, then quantizes semantic item vectors against that hierarchy. It is not a successor residual, residual barycenter, or unconstrained geodesic codebook: the supervised object is a source-conditioned successor cone and the hierarchy has explicit capacities 8/96/256/256.
CHANGE: Learn a Poincare representation for each item and for each coarse parent-child code, with a source-specific direction toward its observed successors. Assign each item to one of 8 roots and one of 96 children by minimizing a geodesic cone prediction cost; assign among 256 T3 codes using the same directed cone cost plus semantic residual. Keep T4 as the baseline collision-extension token.
SPECIFICATION: For each source i, sample up to 4 positive successors j from training transition counts and 4 negatives from the training unigram, frequency^0.75. In the ball of curvature -c=-0.5, represent item i by x_i and its successor direction v_ij=log_{x_i}(x_j). For each node/code k learn anchor a_k and tangent direction r_k at a_k. Define cone cost g(i,j,k)=d_c(x_j,exp_{a_k}(r_k))^2 + beta*max(0, cos_angle(v_ij, log_{x_i}(a_k))-m)^2. Fit root and child assignments using semantic reconstruction plus sampled cone cost, with hard balanced minibatch assignment to K1=8 and K2=96. For each item choose T3 among 256 using semantic residual plus sampled successor cone cost under its T2 parent; ordinary residual reconstruction controls distortion. T4 remains baseline deterministic collision extension.
COST: Precompute sparse positive/negative successor samples from training only. Each step computes at most 8 sampled successor comparisons per item and distances to 8 root plus 96 child prototypes; do not evaluate all item pairs. Expected one-epoch smoke <=0.16 s versus 0.13 s baseline by using sampled transitions and fixed small T1/T2 matrices; if over that threshold, omit T3 cone term for the smoke candidate. At frozen scale the added per-epoch work is 24 batches x sampled geodesic distances, repeated for 3000 epochs (72,000 steps); prototype parameters are small.
HYPOTHESIS: Predictive cones align the coarse hierarchy to successor predictability: gold T1 survival >=.995 and T2 >=.80, while allowing T3 survival .14-.20 and improving T3 top-1 above .11-.16; T4 top-1 remains >=.85. L1 Hard ER should be neutral/down if behavior-compatible items group together. Keep only if Stage3 frozen test_R@10 exceeds .055537, ideally >.065, while meeting the survival profile.
RISKS: Sampled negatives can be false negatives; cone direction is noisy for items with few successors; tangent angle calculations near the ball boundary can be unstable; constrained hierarchy may trade too much semantic reconstruction for prediction. T3 cone fitting may not improve the autoregressive decoder's loss.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed source-to-successor transitions, with frequency-adjusted negative successor items.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Build positive pairs from consecutive items in training sequences only. Sample negatives using the training-only unigram raised to 0.75; exclude observed training successors. No validation/test histories, transitions, or frequencies enter.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: All pair counts, negative-sampling weights, and sampled pairs derive from the training split. Hyperbolic parameters and assignments train only against those pairs plus item embeddings; validation/test are not consulted until the fixed Stage3 evaluation, and do not influence SID construction or capacity.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz/Poincare geodesic distance, log map and exp map, with fixed curvature -0.5; tangent cone direction is computed at each source point.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Stage2 assignment objective for T1/T2 hierarchy and T3 choice under the selected T2 parent; curved operations directly affect assignment scores and gradients.
COUPLING PATH: A training-only directed edge (source, successor) defines a source tangent direction; its angular/geodesic agreement with a candidate code's learned cone enters the hard-assignment cost, selecting the coarse parent and T3 code.
COUPLING EQUATION: J(i,k)=D_sem(i,k)+lambda*d_{c=.5}(x_j,exp_{a_k}^{c}(r_k))^2+gamma*max(0,cos(v_ij,log_{x_i}^{c}(a_k))-m)^2, minimized over capacity-constrained k; v_ij=log_{x_i}^{c}(x_j), c=.5 (curvature -0.5).
OBSERVABLES: Record assignment occupancy, canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER (ten fixed negatives), gold-prefix survival at depths 1-4, teacher-forced T3/T4 accuracy, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Expected L1 ER neutral/down; survival T1 ~1.00, T2 >=.80, T3 .14-.20, T4 >=.08; T3 accuracy up, T4 stable/high. Require improvement over .055537 and target >.065 on test_R@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=gamma=0 and remove sampled edges, preserving hyperbolic semantic assignment and capacities (8,96,256,256); tests whether directed transition supervision contributes.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep identical sampled transitions, cone objective, and capacities but replace log/exp maps, geodesic distance and tangent cosine with Euclidean displacement, Euclidean distance, and cosine; tests the curved geometry contribution.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Train source-conditioned successor cones with curvature -0.5 and use the resulting geodesic cone costs in assignments at the same 8/96/256/256 capacities.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each training pair (i,j), x_i=exp_0^c(W e_i), v_ij=log_{x_i}^c(x_j). For code k, y_k=exp_{a_k}^c(r_k); cost(i,k)=semantic distortion + lambda*d_c(x_j,y_k)^2 + gamma*hinge(cos(v_ij,log_{x_i}^c(a_k))-m). Aggregate over sampled observed successors and sampled negatives using logistic pairwise ranking. T1/T2 hard assignments minimize cost under minibatch capacity 8/96; T3 minimizes parent-conditioned residual+cost over 256 codes; T4 deterministic collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training item embeddings, training-only directed transition pairs, frequency^0.75 negative sampler, and four codebooks. Outputs are four-token SIDs, cone parameters, and gradients; effective capacities are T1=8, T2=96, T3=256, T4=256 before deterministic collision extension.
SAFEGUARDS: Fixed c=.5; project Lorentz points to the valid upper sheet or Poincare norm below (1-epsilon)/sqrt(c); clamp dot products to [-1+1e-6,1-1e-6] before arccos/cosine; normalize tangent vectors with epsilon; cap per-item successors at four and negatives at four; skip items with no outgoing transition from cone loss; use float32 and finite-value checks; deterministic seeds and tie breaks. Four tokens, 3000 epochs/four cards, no early stopping or test-time tuning.
END IDEA
