IDEA: succ_mass_tree
TITLE: Successor-mass hyperbolic tree with an explicit 16/96/256 prefix budget
MOTIVATION: Next-item prediction needs broad, predictable early prefixes and should group items that are plausible directed successors, while retaining semantic discrimination at T3.
NOVELTY: Unlike flat successor-distance or post-hoc behavior penalties, train-only successor mass defines the hierarchy of a capacity-constrained quantizer: it selects the coarse partitions themselves. Curvature changes assignment scores, not merely a side loss.
CHANGE: Build a directed successor distribution from train.parquet, learn 16 T1 and 96 T2 parent cells with balanced soft assignments, and assign 256 residual T3 cells using successor-weighted hyperbolic distortion. Keep T4 solely as the existing deterministic collision-extension token.
SPECIFICATION: For train transitions (u,i_t,i_{t+1}), accumulate C_ij with unit count per adjacent pair and row-normalize P_ij=(C_ij+1e-4)/(sum_j C_ij+1e-4|V|). For item i define behavior neighbors N_i as top-32 outgoing j and symmetric incoming neighbors, with normalized weights a_ij from P_ij and its column-normalized transpose. Encode item embedding x_i to z_i; map z and learned level prototypes to the Poincare ball of curvature -c, c=1. T1/T2 assignments minimize balanced entropic transport over K=16 then 96 prototypes; initialize each next-level prototypes as residuals in the parent tangent space. At T3 choose among 256 prototypes by the joint score below and train with straight-through hard assignments plus RQ reconstruction/commitment loss. No learned or exported extra SID token.
COST: Precompute sparse successor top-32 lists once, O(E_train log 32) or linear top-k selection; storage O(32N). Per optimizer step adds sparse neighbor gathers and at most 4096x(16+96+256)x32 distance arithmetic plus two small Sinkhorn normalizations; estimated under 0.13 s/epoch target, reject if one-epoch four-card smoke exceeds 1.1x baseline (0.143 s). At frozen scale 24 steps/epoch x 3000 epochs, 72,000 steps; cost increment is 72,000 batches of those operations, no full-graph epoch pass.
HYPOTHESIS: If successor-mass parents yield genuine train-transition predictability, L1 Hard ER on the canonical 52,855 pairs/10 fixed negatives should remain near or modestly improve over baseline rather than being optimized as a proxy; gold-prefix survival should be T1 0.99-1.00, T2 0.78-0.86, T3 0.10-0.17, T4 0.07-0.10. T3 teacher-forced top-1 should exceed the 0.106-0.115 seen in 0.062 arms (target >=0.13); T4 should be >=0.55 and need not beat deterministic-extension accuracy. Keep only if frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.055537, with >=0.065 the objective.
RISKS: Successor graph may be noisy or too sparse; balanced assignments may sacrifice semantic reconstruction, while 96 parent cells may still be too many for the depth-2 beam. Fixed negative curvature may distort outliers; T3 score could be insufficiently aligned with Stage3 decoding.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate next-item transitions from user training sequences.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: On the training split only, sort each user's training events by timestamp and count adjacent target-item pairs C_ij. Row-normalize with epsilon smoothing; construct top-32 outgoing and incoming neighbors. Deduplicate repeated same-user adjacent pairs within a session if session boundaries exist; otherwise count transitions.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Read only stage0 train.parquet. No validation/test events, targets, or graph statistics are loaded. Build graph and top-k lists once before optimization; validation/test are absent from SID construction and used only under the frozen evaluation protocol.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare distance d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)⊕y||), curvature -c with c=1; exp/log maps transport residuals between tangent spaces.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Both early-level balanced assignment costs and T3 residual-code selection during RQ-VAE training and hard SID export.
COUPLING PATH: For each item, successor-neighbor codes supply behavior affinity to candidate prototypes, and that affinity reweights their Poincare-distance logits before the capacity-constrained assignments; resulting hard assignments determine residuals and later codes.
COUPLING EQUATION: s_ik = -d_c(z_i,c_k)^2/tau + lambda*sum_{j in N_i} a_ij*log(epsilon+q_jk); q_ik=Sinkhorn_rows_cols(softmax_k(s_ik)); hard_id(i)=argmax_k q_ik. For T3 replace z_i by log_{parent}(z_i) and c_k by tangent residual prototype. Thus lambda=0 removes behavior and c->0 replaces the curved distance with Euclidean distance, each changing the assignment.
OBSERVABLES: Log L1 Hard ER, gold-prefix survival at depths 1-4, teacher-forced top-1 accuracy T3/T4, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Predicted prefix survival is T1 0.99-1.00, T2 0.78-0.86, T3 0.10-0.17, T4 0.07-0.10; ER should be stable/up modestly, T3 accuracy >=0.13, and test recall >0.055537 with target >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 and remove neighbor affinity; retain Poincare assignment and exact capacities (16,96,256). Expect less transition clustering, reduced T3 accuracy and/or recall versus both-on; measure ER and all required survival/accuracy metrics.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Use squared Euclidean distances and Euclidean residual subtraction in the same logits/transport, retain successor affinities and capacities (16,96,256). If assignments and metrics are indistinguishable, the curvature is not helping; compare ER, survival, T3/T4 and recall.
ABLATION BOTH ON: lambda=0.5, c=1, Poincare distance/log-exp residuals, capacities T1=16, T2=96, T3=256; deterministic collision extension at T4. Run 3000 epochs, no early stopping, frozen Stage3.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each batch item, retrieve sparse (j,a_ij). At levels l=1,2 compute score s_ik above, with behavior affinity using the current-level neighbor assignments q_jk; Sinkhorn with row sums 1 and uniform column mass B/K, then straight-through argmax for RQ residual. At l=3 compute score on log_{p_i}(z_i) residual and 256 residual prototypes, with the same affinity. Loss = usual TIGER reconstruction + quantization/commitment losses; replace Euclidean nearest-code score by q-derived hard code. Export three raw codes and apply unchanged deterministic fourth-token collision extension.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: train-item embeddings, training-only transition top-k sparse adjacency, 16/96/256 prototypes, c and lambda. Output: trained item encoder/codebooks, three hard code IDs per item and unchanged four-token collision-extended SID.
SAFEGUARDS: Project ball vectors to radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp atanh inputs to 1-1e-6; float32 distance and Sinkhorn logits, subtract row max, clamp logits to [-30,30], add epsilon to affinity, cap gradient norm at baseline 1.0. Sinkhorn 5 iterations, sparse top-32 neighbors, no dense NxN matrix; check finite values and nonempty rows.
END IDEA

IDEA: branch_curvature_gate
TITLE: Behavior-branch gated Lorentz assignment with 24/64/256 code capacities
MOTIVATION: Users can move between distinct interests; treating all outgoing successors as one isotropic neighborhood can blur branches, so coarse prefixes should preserve dominant successor modes while leaving depth 2 highly survivable.
NOVELTY: Uses a train-only per-item transition-branch distribution to choose a point-dependent Lorentz curvature scale for the actual quantizer score. It is not a separate branch loss or a geometry layer appended to unchanged Euclidean RQ-VAE.
CHANGE: Cluster each item's outgoing successor distribution into at most four behavioral modes, then use its mode entropy to gate the curvature radius in a Lorentz hyperboloid assignment. Apply capacities 24/64/256 at the first three raw levels; fourth remains collision extension.
SPECIFICATION: For training-only transitions C_ij, derive each source item's successor distribution p_i(j) with add-one smoothing on observed outgoing neighbors. Cluster the sparse p_i vectors into M=4 modes by spherical k-means on their top-32 coordinates; assign each item a soft mode vector b_i. Define normalized entropy h_i=-sum_m b_im log(b_im+eps)/log M and curvature c_i=0.5+1.5*h_i. Map encoded item z_i to Lorentz hyperboloid H^{d}_{-c_i}; map prototypes using a fixed reference c=1 and rescale their spatial coordinates to c_i before scoring. Minimize d_L,c_i(z_i,mu_k)^2 - lambda log(eps+sum_m b_im r_mk), where r_mk is the training-set smoothed mode frequency of items assigned to prototype k. Use balanced assignment with K=24,64,256 per level; use geodesic log-map residuals between levels.
COST: Sparse distribution construction and four-mode clustering are one-time O(E_train*32*4*iterations), CPU/GPU modest. Per training step adds one Lorentz norm/dot product per batch-code pair and tiny mode-frequency lookups; 24+64+256 candidates x 32 dimensions, under 1.1x baseline expected and one-epoch smoke must be <=0.143 s (baseline 0.13). Across frozen 72,000 steps no additional per-epoch graph traversal; 3000 epochs unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: If successor mode branching predicts distinct useful next-item regions, entropy-conditioned curvature separates ambiguous multi-interest items without fragmenting coarse prefixes. Predict L1 Hard ER roughly unchanged or lower than baseline on canonical 52,855 pairs/10 fixed negatives; survival T1 0.99-1.00, T2 0.78-0.86, T3 0.10-0.17, T4 0.07-0.10. Teacher-forced T3 top-1 >=0.13 and T4 >=0.55 are desired; keep only if frozen Stage3 test_R@10 >0.055537, with target >0.065.
RISKS: Sparse outgoing distributions may not expose true interest modes; entropy may mostly measure item popularity or sequence count. Point-dependent curvature can complicate prototypes and unstable scaling; 24 T1 groups might reduce semantic fidelity.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item distribution over directed next-item successors, including branch entropy and mode membership.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From adjacent item pairs in train.parquet only, count C_ij for user-chronological consecutive training events. Smooth and normalize each source row over observed successors; form top-32 successor coordinates and cluster those distributions into four modes, retaining soft assignments b_i.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table, successor modes, entropy, and mode/prototype frequencies are computed only from training interactions. Freeze mode assignments before SID export; do not read validation/test files or use their behavior in clustering, assignment, or hyperparameter selection.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz hyperboloid distance for curvature -c_i: d_L,c(x,y)=acosh(max(1,-c< x,y>_L))/sqrt(c), with exp/log maps for residuals; c_i=0.5+1.5h_i.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Per-item-curvature Lorentz geodesic distance is the actual score in each capacity-constrained hard/soft code assignment; log maps produce residuals for subsequent levels.
COUPLING PATH: The training-transition branch entropy h_i sets c_i, changing Lorentz geodesic distances to every prototype; the same branch membership adds mode affinity to those distance logits, which feed the 24/64/256 assignment and residual pathway.
COUPLING EQUATION: score_ik= -d_L,c_i(z_i,mu_k)^2/tau + lambda*log(eps+sum_m b_im*r_mk), c_i=0.5+1.5*(-sum_m b_im log(b_im+eps)/log 4); q_i=balanced_softmax(score_i). Both b_i and the curved operator occur inside the logits that choose hard IDs.
OBSERVABLES: Required readouts are canonical L1 Hard ER (52,855 pairs, ten fixed negatives), survival at depths 1-4, teacher-forced T3/T4 top-1 accuracy and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Predict ER stable/down modestly; survival T1 0.99-1.00, T2 0.78-0.86, T3 0.10-0.17, T4 0.07-0.10; T3 accuracy >=0.13, T4 >=0.55, recall above baseline 0.055537 and ideally above 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Use b_i uniform for every item and r_mk constant, set lambda=0 and c_i=1; keep capacities 24/64/256. This removes behavior-dependent branch and curvature; as a diagnostic primary behavior-off arm retain c_i=1 Lorentz while lambda=0, separately report the full neutral control. Compare all required measurements.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Lorentz distances and log/exp residuals with Euclidean norm and subtraction, but retain b_i, branch affinities, and capacities 24/64/256. Compare assignment differences and required metrics against both-on.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only b_i, lambda=0.25, pointwise c_i=0.5+1.5h_i, Lorentz geodesic assignment, capacities T1=24, T2=64, T3=256 and unchanged deterministic T4 collision extension; train 3000 epochs and use frozen Stage3.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Construct sparse C and p_i, four-mode soft membership b_i. For each level score each candidate by the coupling equation (for later levels substitute current tangent residual); balance with 5-step Sinkhorn, hard-forward/soft-backward assignment, update ordinary RQ-VAE reconstruction and commitment losses. Set r_mk from exponentially smoothed training batch assignments, synchronized across DDP ranks. Export three hard levels plus deterministic collision token.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item embeddings, train-only user-ordered transition pairs, four-mode b_i, learned encoder/prototypes, fixed capacities and lambda. Outputs: trained RQ-VAE/codebooks and three raw item codes extended to four SID tokens.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute Lorentz inner products in float32; project spatial norm below (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c_i), clamp acosh argument to >=1+1e-6, clamp h to [0,1], normalize b_i with eps, clip logits [-30,30]. Use sparse top-32 transitions and no NxN tensor; synchronize mode counts in DDP and assert finite coordinates/distances.
END IDEA
