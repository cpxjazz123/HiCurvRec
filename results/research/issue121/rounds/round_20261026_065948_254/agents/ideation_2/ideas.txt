IDEA: flowcap_hyp_cut
TITLE: Successor-flow constrained Lorentz cuts for a coarse-to-fine SID
MOTIVATION: Likely next items need to share early prefixes often enough to survive beam pruning, without spending early-token capacity on distinctions that belong at T3.
NOVELTY: This makes the discrete assignment a capacity-constrained directed graph partition, rather than adding a successor-neighbour term to a fixed-size quantizer. Unlike existing unconstrained successor-geodesic proposals, the explicit small K1/K2 budgets are enforced by a differentiable balanced assignment, and the transition flow changes the assignment cost itself.
CHANGE: Use K1=16, K2=96, K3=256, with T4 solely deterministic collision extension; fit the first two assignments jointly to directed train-transition flow using a negative-curvature Lorentz dissimilarity, then residual-quantize within each parent at T3. Capacity constraints apply to every training-item assignment, not merely as a usage penalty.
SPECIFICATION: Let n_ij count adjacent i->j events in training users' training histories; define r_ij=(n_ij+α)/(Σ_j n_ij+α|N_i|) on observed successors and set r_i=0 if unsupported. Map encoder z_i to Lorentz point X_i=exp_o^{-κ}(z_i), curvature -κ with κ=0.5. For level l and candidate code a, let D^l_ia=d_L(X_i,C^l_a)^2 and F^l_ab=Σ_ij r_ij 1[a_i^l=a]1[a_j^l=b]. Minimize Σ_i,a P^l_ia D^l_ia + λ Σ_i,j,a,b r_ij P^l_ia P^l_jb d_L(C^l_a,C^l_b)^2, subject to row sums 1 and column sums N/K_l (balanced Sinkhorn; hard argmax at export); optimize the soft objective with straight-through assignments and baseline reconstruction/commitment losses. K2 prototypes are parent-conditioned; K3 uses the baseline Euclidean residual quantizer of width 256 within each K2 parent. Repeated runs: both-on (flow term and Lorentz distances), behavior-off (r_i term set to zero, same curvature/capacity), geometry-off (replace all Lorentz operations with matched-scale Euclidean norms, same flow/capacity).
COST: One O(E_train) count pass; retain at most 16 outgoing successors per item. The added train cost is roughly O(B*16*16 + B*96*96) transition pair terms if naively dense, so implement sparse edge minibatch evaluation O(E_batch*(K1+K2)) and streamed Sinkhorn O(B*K_l); use only the 16-code T1 and 96-code T2, and disable full pairwise code-pair expansion. Target one-epoch smoke within 0.13 s/epoch (no more than about 10% overhead); at 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs this is 73,668,000 item-step assignments per level, with cached sparse edges and no graph traversal in the loop. Reject if measured smoke exceeds baseline by >10%.
HYPOTHESIS: Directed successors have coherent, learnable coarse destinations: with K1=16 and K2=96 the gold prefix should survive depth 1 at ≥0.995 and depth 2 at ≥0.80, while T3 top-1 rises above the 0.11-0.16 range of current high-recall arms. Keep only if canonical L1 Hard ER (52,855 pairs, ten fixed negatives) moves favorably and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds the untouched 0.055537 control, ideally >0.065. Expected L1 Hard ER is lower (better pair ranking) than behavior-off; expected survival: depth1 ≳.995, depth2 ≳.80, depth3 ≈.10-.16, depth4 ≈.08-.09; T3 teacher-forced top1 increases, T4 stays near deterministic/collision-extension accuracy (about .98); Stage3 Recall@10 should increase toward and above .065.
RISKS: Balanced mass can force implausible assignments for hubs or disconnected items; joint edge costs may make optimization unstable or wash out reconstruction geometry. K2 parent-conditioned code utilization and hardening may diverge from soft Sinkhorn; if T2 is not predictable despite 96 groups, beam survival will still fail. Sparse edge cost must be profiled to meet smoke limit.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed adjacent-item successor transition frequencies, popularity-adjusted per source.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: On training split only, order each user's training events chronologically and count consecutive item pairs; use additive smoothing over observed successor support, normalize per source, and retain at most 16 successors by n_ij/sqrt((n_i+1)(n_j+1)).
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build the transition table only from training user histories before Stage2. Do not read validation/test events, labels, or sequences; export SIDs from the fixed item catalog and train-only statistics. Validation may not choose parameters and test is used only for the frozen final Stage3 measurement.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz exp map and geodesic distance d_L(X,Y)=acosh(max(1,-κ<X,Y>_L))/sqrt(κ), with fixed negative curvature -κ=-0.5.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In both the per-item code assignment cost and the successor flow cost for T1/T2, before discrete hardening; Lorentz prototypes are the actual candidates whose indices become SID prefixes.
COUPLING PATH: Train transitions set r_ij, which weights Lorentz distances between candidate prefix codes during capacity-constrained assignment; changing r or replacing Lorentz distance changes the optimal soft and hard assignments, hence exported T1/T2.
COUPLING EQUATION: J(P)=Σ_i,a P_ia d_L(X_i,C_a)^2 + λΣ_i,j,a,b r_ij P_iaP_jb d_L(C_a,C_b)^2, subject to Σ_a P_ia=1 and Σ_i P_ia=N/K_l; the behavior r_ij directly weights the curved d_L term.
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives; gold-prefix survival at depths 1,2,3,4; teacher-forced top-1 accuracy at T3/T4; and Stage3 test_R@10. Expected ER decreases, depth1 remains near 1.0, depth2 survival rises to ≥0.80, depth3 survival is roughly .10-.16 and depth4 roughly .08-.09; T3 accuracy improves and T4 remains high; Stage3 should exceed 0.055537 and target >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set r_ij=0 in the flow term (retain reconstruction, capacities K1=16/K2=96/K3=256, Lorentz geometry); this tests whether successor flow determines useful sharing.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace exp/log/Lorentz distance by matched-scale Euclidean mapping and norms (retain the same r_ij and capacities K1=16/K2=96/K3=256).
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use train-only directed r_ij, Lorentz curvature -0.5 in both item and flow costs, and capacities 16/96/256/collision-extension, with deterministic T4.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=Σ_u,t 1[i_t=i,i_{t+1}=j] over train histories; r_ij=normalized((n_ij+α)/sqrt((n_i+1)(n_j+1))) over retained top-16 successors. X_i=exp_o^{-κ}(W e_i). For l∈{1,2}, minimize J_l(P)=Σ_i,a P_ia d_L(X_i^l,C_a^l)^2 + λ_lΣ_(i,j) r_ijΣ_(a,b)P_iaP_jb d_L(C_a^l,C_b^l)^2 with row stochastic P and column sums N/K_l; Sinkhorn-soften then straight-through argmax. Set K1=16, K2=96, K3=256; perform baseline residual quantization at T3 inside selected T2 parent; assign T4 by the existing deterministic collision extension. Train with baseline decoder, reconstruction and commitment objectives plus the assignment objective. Three arms hold all capacity and training settings fixed and toggle only behavior and geometry as specified.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen Stage1 item embeddings, training-only sparse directed transition rows, trainable Lorentz item/prototype parameters, and baseline RQ-VAE decoder. Outputs are four integer SID tokens per item, where T1/T2 are capacity-constrained flow-geodesic prefixes, T3 is 256-way residual code, and T4 is deterministic collision extension.
SAFEGUARDS: Use float32/64 acosh argument clamp to ≥1+ε, project points to the Lorentz sheet after updates, clip tangent norms before exp map, clamp P and Sinkhorn kernels, and stop gradients through r. Ignore unsupported sources; cap popularity weights and per-source degree; initialize prototypes inside a bounded radius. Track finite losses and hard occupancy; if invalid values occur, abort rather than silently emit SIDs. Keep Sinkhorn iterations and edge samples fixed across arms.
END IDEA

IDEA: successor_radial_budget
TITLE: Transition-predictability radii for capacity-locked hyperbolic residual partitions
MOTIVATION: A large second-token alphabet lets the gold prefix disappear before the beam can spend its capacity on informative third-token distinctions; frequent continuation patterns should occupy compact, predictable early regions.
NOVELTY: Instead of using branching entropy to choose item shells or adding a successor barycenter to a fixed-capacity assignment, this uses directed transition predictability to allocate per-item geodesic margin within a fixed, explicitly capped T1/T2 alphabet. The same scalar behavior statistic scales the curved assignment's discrimination pressure, so it alters which codes are chosen rather than just regularizing embeddings.
CHANGE: Lock K1=24, K2=96, K3=256, and deterministic T4; compute each item's smoothed outgoing transition predictability and use it to set the radius and temperature of its Lorentz assignment at T1/T2. More predictable successors get sharper, compact coarse assignments; less predictable items get wider geodesic decision regions and are left for T3 to distinguish.
SPECIFICATION: From training counts n_ij form p(j|i), and h_i=Σ_j p(j|i)^2 (zero for unsupported i). Set τ_i=τ_min+(τ_max-τ_min)(1-h_i), and target radial coordinate ρ_i=ρ_min+(ρ_max-ρ_min)h_i. Map z_i to Poincare ball x_i=exp_0^{-c}(z_i), c=0.5; each level's code c_ka lies on the same ball. Assign a_ik=softmax_k(-[d_c(x_i,c_k)^2+μ(d_c(0,c_k)-ρ_i)^2]/τ_i), then use straight-through hard argmin and a balanced capacity projection so no more than N/K_l items use each code (K1=24,K2=96). Crucially, let each source's transition probability shape its candidate cost too: add λh_iΣ_j p(j|i)d_c(c_k,x_j)^2, evaluated on cached successor embeddings; assignment still uses the same capped alphabet. Use existing residual reconstruction at T3 with 256 codes and deterministic T4. Ablations: behavior-off sets h_i to the train-item mean and p row term to zero; geometry-off substitutes Euclidean distances/maps/norms with matched scales; both-on uses the stated Poincare path. Compare all with identical capacity 24/96/256.
COST: Precompute sparse top-8 transition neighbors in O(E_train); batch cost is O(B*(K1+K2)*8*D) for candidate-to-successor distances, plus baseline assignment and a small balanced projection. Cache embedded successors; no all-pairs item matrix. Target smoke ≤0.143 s/epoch (baseline 0.13 s); at frozen 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs there are 73,668,000 item assignments per level, with eight cached successor vectors per sampled item. If this is over 10% slower in one-epoch smoke, use only top-4 neighbors or reject.
HYPOTHESIS: Outgoing transition concentration predicts which items can be safely merged at coarse depths, while the geodesic radius makes that behavior signal geometrically operative. Expected L1 Hard ER decreases versus behavior-off; gold survival depth1 ≥.995 and depth2 ≥.80 due to 24/96 capacities, depth3 approximately .10-.16 and depth4 approximately .08-.09; T3 teacher-forced top1 improves over present high-recall arms, T4 remains around .98. Keep only if frozen Stage3 Recall@10 beats 0.055537, with >0.065 as the target; measure the specified canonical ER, survival, and teacher-forced accuracies rather than relying on SID structure counts.
RISKS: Transition concentration may reflect popularity rather than true predictability; temperature/radius could over-concentrate hubs or fragment rare items. The behavior cost can be weak relative to reconstruction, while too-large λ distorts semantic reconstruction. Fixed capacities improve prefix survival only if code assignments are actually predictable, not merely balanced.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Smoothed directed next-item distribution and its concentration per source item.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive item transitions within each user's training-only chronological sequence; form p(j|i)=(n_ij+α)/(Σ_j n_ij+α|N_i|) on observed successors, retain top eight by popularity-adjusted count, and compute h_i=Σ_j p(j|i)^2; set h_i=0 when no successor support.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The counts, smoothing support, h_i, and neighbor lists use training events only. Validation/test sequences and interactions are inaccessible during SID fitting; no validation/test-based threshold or per-item statistic is used. Final Stage3 test evaluation is frozen and occurs only after SID export.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed-curvature (-c=-0.5) Poincare exp map and geodesic distance d_c(x,y)=2/√c atanh(√c||(-x)⊕_c y||), including geodesic radius from origin.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Candidate scoring and differentiable soft assignment at T1 and T2, including the transition-conditioned candidate-to-successor term; hardening those assignments produces the actual SID prefixes.
COUPLING PATH: Training-only p(j|i), h_i set each item's temperature/radius and weight its candidate-to-successor geodesic distance inside the same Poincare assignment score; removing behavior removes item-specific score changes and Euclidean replacement changes the assignments.
COUPLING EQUATION: s_ik=[d_c(x_i,c_k)^2+μ(d_c(0,c_k)-ρ_i)^2+λh_iΣ_j p(j|i)d_c(c_k,x_j)^2]/τ_i, with a_ik∝exp(-s_ik), ρ_i=ρ_min+(ρ_max-ρ_min)h_i; the behavior h_i,p(j|i) directly weight the curved d_c terms.
OBSERVABLES: Measure L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives, gold-prefix alive ratios at depths 1-4, teacher-forced top-1 T3/T4, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Predicted ER is lower than behavior-off; survival depth1 ≥.995, depth2 ≥.80, depth3 .10-.16, depth4 .08-.09; T3 top-1 rises and T4 remains high (~.98); Stage3 exceeds control 0.055537 and should reach >0.065 to justify retention.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Replace h_i with the train-item mean, omit the item-specific p(j|i) successor sum, but preserve Poincare distances and capacities 24/96/256 plus collision T4.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Poincare exp map, geodesic distances and geodesic radius with matched-scale Euclidean map, norm and distances; retain identical train-only transition statistics and capacities 24/96/256.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use p(j|i), h_i, item-conditioned τ_i/ρ_i and candidate-to-successor Poincare distance at curvature -0.5 with capacities 24/96/256 and deterministic collision-extension T4.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=train-only adjacent-event counts; p_ij=(n_ij+α)/(n_i+α|N_i|), h_i=Σ_j p_ij². τ_i=τ_min+(τ_max-τ_min)(1-h_i); ρ_i=ρ_min+(ρ_max-ρ_min)h_i. For l=1,2 compute score s_ik=d_c(x_i^l,c_kl)^2+μ(d_c(0,c_kl)-ρ_i)^2+λh_iΣ_(j in top8(i))p_ij d_c(c_kl,x_j)^2. Softmax(-s/τ_i), apply balanced capacity projection with K1=24,K2=96, then straight-through hard assignment; use K3=256 baseline residual quantizer and T4 deterministic collision extension. Train baseline reconstruction/commitment plus assignment losses. Run three arms with identical seeds, capacities, epochs and Stage3 protocol, toggling only behavior and geometry.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen item embeddings, training-only sparse transition counts and normalized rows, a Poincare encoder/codebooks, baseline residual quantizer and decoder. Output is a four-token SID with T1/T2 chosen from fixed capacities 24/96, T3 from 256 residual codes, and T4 the existing deterministic collision extension.
SAFEGUARDS: Clip tangent vectors to stay inside radius (1-ε)/√c; use stable Möbius addition, log1p/atanh and clamp distance arguments; use float32/64 for hyperbolic ops. Clamp h_i, τ_i and ρ_i to fixed finite bounds, detach training statistics, renormalize p rows, exclude zero-support items from the neighbor sum, clip gradients and initialize codewords away from the ball boundary. Balance projection and top-8 are deterministic and fixed across ablations; monitor NaN/Inf and assignment occupancy.
END IDEA
