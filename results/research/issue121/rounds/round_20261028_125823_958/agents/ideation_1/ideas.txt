IDEA: fork-geodesic-rank
TITLE: Successor-fork rank supervision through a hyperbolic geodesic margin
MOTIVATION: A next-item code should preserve which items are interchangeable within a user's observed branching future, while separating items whose successor sets diverge; raw semantic reconstruction does not express this predictive distinction.
NOVELTY: Unlike successor co-occurrence attraction or a per-item radial shell target, this uses the *ranked overlap of successor distributions* as a pair-specific target and applies it directly to geodesic distances between the encoder representations that feed the RQ quantizer.
CHANGE: From train-only transitions, form smoothed successor distributions and sample item pairs; train the encoder to order their Poincare distances according to successor-distribution overlap, with an adaptive margin derived from the batch's distance scale. Retain the canonical reconstruction and quantization losses.
SPECIFICATION: Let n_ij be train-event transitions i→j. Define p_i(j)=(n_ij+α q(j))/(n_i+α), where q is train-only destination frequency normalized and α=1. For sampled pair (i,k), s_ik=1−JS(p_i,p_k)/log 2. Let z_i be the pre-quantization encoder output mapped to the ball, and d_c its Poincare distance, c=1/(median_batch ||h||²+ε) (fixed after an initial train-only encoder pass, clipped only to the ball-safe range). For triplets (i,k,l) ordered so s_ik>s_il, use Lfork=softplus((d_c(z_i,z_k)−d_c(z_i,z_l)+m)/τ), m=median of positive batch geodesic distances, τ=median absolute deviation of those distances (ε floor). Pair/triplet sampling is deterministic from seed 42 and train graph; take at most one triplet per anchor per step. Add Lfork with coefficient set to the ratio of baseline reconstruction-loss EMA to fork-loss EMA over the first 50 warmup epochs (stop-gradient; then freeze), avoiding a tuned weight.
COST: One-time train graph build O(E); per step O(BD) encoder-to-ball mapping and O(BD) geodesic triplet calculations for B≤4096 anchors (at most 4096 triplets), versus baseline embedding/quantization O(BD). At 24 steps/epoch × 3000 epochs = 72,000 steps, adds 72,000 bounded triplet batches; expected modest arithmetic overhead, no extra model or data pass beyond one train-only graph build and the allowed warmup-scale estimate.
HYPOTHESIS: The mechanism is worth keeping if the single frozen Stage3 run has test Recall@10 > 0.065; supporting observations are changed encoder gradients and more predictable successor ranking from SID prefixes, recorded only as diagnostics, never gates.
RISKS: Sparse transition rows make JS noisy; too many near-tied successor distributions weaken triplet signal; hyperbolic distance scale may be dominated by norm rather than angle; the automatically normalized loss can still conflict with reconstruction.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transition distributions and their pairwise Jensen–Shannon overlap.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive distinct item events per user in the Stage0 training parquet only; aggregate n_ij, destination q, and p_i once before training. No validation/test rows are read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Graph, frequency smoothing, pair sampler, scale statistics and triplets are functions only of the training parquet and train embeddings; freeze the resulting graph before fitting, and construct/export SIDs without validation or test interactions.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball distance d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)⊕_c y||), with negative curvature −c; log/exp mapping from encoder tangent output to ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The pre-quantization encoder output is mapped to the ball; the training-only pairwise successor-ranking objective differentiates through Poincare distance before ordinary RQ-VAE quantization.
COUPLING PATH: Train-only successor-distribution overlap orders sampled item pairs; that order sets which encoder representations must be geodesically nearer/farther, and the resulting gradients update the same encoder representation consumed by the quantizer.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_fork=E_{s_ik>s_il} softplus((d_c(z_i,z_k)−d_c(z_i,z_l)+m)/τ); s_ik=1−JS(p_i,p_k)/log 2. Behavior determines pair ordering; curved distance is the scored quantity.
OBSERVABLES: Encoder gradients and learned item vectors should change; SID assignments and prefix-level successor-rank predictability may change. Record these and Stage3 test Recall@10; only Recall@10 > 0.065 is success.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Keep the hyperbolic ranking computation but permute p_i across item IDs using the fixed seed, breaking the correspondence; the behavior term no longer ranks true successor overlaps.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep the exact train pairs, targets, margin and schedules, but replace d_c with Euclidean ||h_i−h_j||₂ and remove exp/log maps.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the actual train-only p_i ordering and Poincare geodesic distances as specified; this is the sole trained candidate, with no ablation arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Build p_i; per step encode h_i, map z_i=exp^c_0(h_i); draw train-derived (i,k,l), where s_ik>s_il; compute batch m,τ from detached valid distances; L=L_RQVAE+λ(t)L_fork. λ(t)=EMA(L_RQVAE)/EMA(L_fork) during epochs 1–50, then freeze at epoch 50. Geometry-off and behavior-off switches select the alternatives described above without changing any other code path.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: train-only transition counts, current item embeddings and encoder, sampled item-index triplets. Output: scalar coupled loss and gradients to encoder; standard quantizer then emits unchanged-format three-level SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp ball norm below (1−1e−5)/sqrt(c); clamp atanh argument to 1−1e−6; add ε=1e−8 to row denominators, JS logs and MAD; skip anchors without two distinct successor-overlap ranks; compute geometry/loss in float32 under mixed precision; detach scale and λ estimates; clip gradients with baseline norm 1.0.
END IDEA
IDEA: fork-radial-volume
TITLE: Successor-fanout calibrated hyperbolic radial supervision
MOTIVATION: Items that initiate many distinct future choices behave like branching hubs, while items with few futures occupy narrower predictive roles; encoding this distinction in the hierarchy could make the quantizer's coarse prefixes useful for next-item generation.
NOVELTY: This is not a shell-geometry assignment rule or an independent behavior regularizer: train-only successor fanout sets a per-item target *hyperbolic ball volume*, and inverse volume geometry directly calibrates the encoder radius the RQ-VAE quantizer receives.
CHANGE: Estimate each item's effective successor branching, map it monotonically to a target hyperbolic radius using the volume-growth law, and penalize the squared geodesic radial discrepancy between that target and the encoder point. This is a radial-only fork signal, distinct from pairwise angular/coherence or codebook assignment mechanisms.
SPECIFICATION: From train transitions define smoothed outgoing degree b_i=log(1+|{j:n_ij>0}|) and normalize u_i=(b_i−median(b))/(IQR(b)+ε), clipped to [−1,1]. For curvature −c with c fixed to 1/(median_i ||h_i||²+ε), compute hyperbolic d-dimensional ball volume V_c(r)=|S^{d−1}|∫_0^r (sinh(√c t)/√c)^{d−1}dt. Set target radius r_i=V_c^{-1}(V_c(r_0) exp(κ u_i)), where r_0=median current geodesic radius and κ=log(V_c(r_max)/V_c(r_min))/2, r_min/r_max are the 10th/90th percentiles of train-only encoder radii from the warmup pass. Use a lookup table with monotone interpolation for inverse volume. Lrad=mean_i w_i(d_c(0,z_i)−r_i)^2, with w_i=1/(1+|u_i|) so sparse extremes cannot dominate; coefficient λ=baseline reconstruction-loss EMA / Lrad EMA measured during epochs 1–50 and then frozen.
COST: One-time train graph O(E), one encoder-only warmup statistics pass O(24556D), and a small (e.g. 256-node) scalar volume lookup table. Each of 72,000 steps adds one norm/radius per item, O(BD), and scalar squared error; no pairwise matrix. Four-card, 24-step × 3000-epoch schedule unchanged; added work is negligible relative to baseline encoder and quantizer.
HYPOTHESIS: If fanout hierarchy matters to generation, placing high-fanout items at radii implied by hyperbolic volume growth will reorganize RQ coarse prefixes in a way that helps generation; keep only if the single frozen Stage3 test Recall@10 exceeds 0.065. Record radius/fanout correlation, SID changes and prefix successor predictability diagnostically, not as admission criteria.
RISKS: Degree is a blunt branching proxy and may measure exposure/popularity; the radial target may consume representational capacity or distort semantic reconstruction; inverse-volume saturation near the ball boundary requires careful bounded lookup; no repeated seed can separate effect from seed noise.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item number of distinct observed immediate successors (fanout), derived from directed train-only user transitions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: For each user's chronologically ordered Stage0 training events, count adjacent transitions i→j, then count distinct destinations j per source item i; use log1p and robust train-item median/IQR normalization. Validation/test transitions are excluded.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Fanout and all normalization statistics come only from the training parquet; warmup encoder radii use only the frozen training-item embedding matrix. No user interactions from validation/test are read for fitting, radius targets, quantization or SID export.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare origin distance d_c(0,z)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||z||), and the negative-curvature ball-volume law V_c(r)∝∫sinh(√c r)^{d−1}dr with curvature −c.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Before residual quantization, the encoder point is mapped into the Poincare ball; the training penalty matches its geodesic radius to a branching-calibrated radius obtained through inverse hyperbolic volume.
COUPLING PATH: Each item's train-only successor fanout determines its target volume/radius; the hyperbolic radial error on the same encoder point the quantizer consumes produces gradients that alter its SID assignment.
COUPLING EQUATION: u_i=robustnorm(log(1+outdegree_train(i))); r_i=V_c^{-1}(V_c(r_0)e^{κu_i}); L_rad=mean_i (1+|u_i|)^{-1}[d_c(0,z_i)−r_i]^2. The behavior signal chooses r_i and curved geometry measures the prediction error.
OBSERVABLES: Encoder norms/radii should track train fanout in hyperbolic-radius units; SID levels/prefix assignment and prefix successor predictability may change. Stage3 test Recall@10 > 0.065 is the sole keep criterion.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Preserve curvature and radial penalty, but replace u_i by a fixed seeded permutation across item IDs, destroying the fanout-to-radius mapping.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Preserve fanout targets and schedules, but use Euclidean radius ||h_i|| and a Euclidean volume law V(r)∝r^d (equivalently radius target transformed from the same volume quantile); remove ball maps and geodesic distance.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use each item's actual train-only fanout to set inverse-hyperbolic-volume radius and penalize its Poincare origin distance; this is the sole candidate run, not a trained ablation.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Build b_i and u_i from train edges. During warmup estimate c, r_0, r_min, r_max; build monotone table for V_c and inverse. At each step, z_i=exp^c_0(h_i), r_i=V_c^{-1}(V_c(r_0)e^{κu_i}), L=L_RQVAE+λ(t)mean_i(d_c(0,z_i)−r_i)^2/(1+|u_i|). Estimate λ by detached EMA loss ratio in epochs 1–50, freeze thereafter. Geometry-off replaces ball radius and V_c with Euclidean norm and Euclidean volume; behavior-off permutes u.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: train transition edge counts/fanout, encoder outputs for batch items, curvature and warmup radius statistics. Outputs: scalar radial loss/encoder gradients and ordinary RQ quantizer output SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Restrict ||z||≤(1−1e−5)/sqrt(c); clamp inverse-table query to its endpoint range; use float64 for lookup table construction and float32 interpolation/training; set ε=1e−8 for normalization and volume; use stable log-sinh evaluation; stop gradients through target radii, c, quantiles and λ; clip gradients at baseline norm 1.0; log fraction of clipped targets/radii without using it as a gate.
END IDEA
