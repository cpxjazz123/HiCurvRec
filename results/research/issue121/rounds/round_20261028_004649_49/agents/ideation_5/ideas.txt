IDEA: TRANSITION_MARGIN_FINE
TITLE: Successor-ranked hyperbolic margins for fine residual codewords
MOTIVATION: Fine-level residual tokens must retain distinctions between items with different likely successors; improving coarse-level concentration alone can erase distinctions needed to predict the next item.
NOVELTY: Unlike mechanisms that bias a coarse assignment, this applies a directed transition ranking loss to the *fine-level residual codeword scores*. It does not add a behaviour suffix to an unchanged quantizer: transition-derived positive and negative items define a curved ranking comparison that updates the fine codebook and encoder.
CHANGE: At the final residual level, compare the assigned codeword’s Poincaré distance to the source item’s residual against its distance to the residual of an observed successor, using a margin-weighted pairwise ranking loss. Use that same differentiable score to train the final-level codebook and encoder alongside the ordinary reconstruction objective.
SPECIFICATION: For training transition edge (i,j), let r_i^3 be the tangent residual before level-3 quantization and c_k^3 its codeword. Let k_i=argmin_k d_c(exp_0(r_i^3),exp_0(c_k^3))². Define s(i,j)=d_c(exp_0(r_i^3),exp_0(c_{k_i}^3))²−d_c(exp_0(r_j^3),exp_0(c_{k_i}^3))² and Ltrans=mean_edges softplus(s(i,j)). Add Ltrans to the usual level-3 quantization/reconstruction objective with coefficient 1, so the pairwise term has no tunable coupling constant. Stop gradient through k_i; gradients flow through both residuals and c^3.
COST: Precompute the directed training edges once in O(T). Each of 24 steps per epoch over 3000 epochs processes the usual batch of about 1,023 of 24,556 training items; sample one cached outgoing edge per item with at least one edge. Adds one extra final-level Poincaré distance per sampled edge (O(BD) per step) and a softplus; no extra codebook search. The extra work is approximately 72,000 × B × D coordinate contributions, modest relative to the existing item-to-codeword assignments.
HYPOTHESIS: If items sharing a likely successor should have compatible fine residual representations, the ranking term will improve successor predictability without making unrelated items share fine tokens. Judge solely by the frozen Stage3 test Recall@10; log fine-token assignment changes and successor predictability as diagnostics, not gates.
RISKS: The sampled successor may be noisy or one-to-many; using a source’s assigned codeword to rank successor residuals could overfit popular transitions or pull many successor residuals toward the same codeword.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transitions from training histories.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Build counts n_ij by counting adjacent item pairs in each training-only user sequence. For each source i with outgoing counts, sample j from p(j|i)=n_ij/Σ_l n_il; use one sampled edge per eligible source presentation, with sampling seeded from the frozen run seed.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct the edge table only from the Stage2 training split. Do not read validation or test histories, transition counts, or labels in preprocessing, training, or SID export.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Poincaré geodesic distance d_c² on a ball of fixed curvature −c, c=0.5, after exp_0 mapping tangent residuals.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The final residual-level pairwise ranking loss during Stage2 training; the same curved distances determine which final codeword is selected.
COUPLING PATH: Training edges select (i,j); their residuals are compared to the source’s selected final-level codeword using Poincaré distance; this ranking gradient updates the fine residual encoder and codebook and can change the final SID token. Removing the edge signal removes the pairwise comparison; replacing the curved distance changes its score and gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: Ltrans=E_(i,j)~p_train(j|i)[softplus(d_c²(x_i,c^3_{k_i})−d_c²(x_j,c^3_{k_i}))], where x_u=exp_0(r_u^3), k_i=argmin_k d_c²(x_i,exp_0(c_k^3)).
OBSERVABLES: Final-level code assignments and gradients should respond to observed successor edges; successor predictability of the fine prefix may change. Stage3 test Recall@10 is the outcome measure.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: In code, set the training-edge sampler to return no pairs and Ltrans to zero; retain the same curved assignment and ordinary RQ-VAE objective. This is the behavior-off comparator, not a trained arm.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: In code, replace exp_0 and d_c² in the assignment and ranking pathway with the corresponding Euclidean residuals and squared Euclidean distances; retain the same edge sampler and objective structure. This is the geometry-off comparator, not a trained arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the training-only directed edge sampler, Poincaré exp_0 and d_c² at curvature −0.5, and the stated final-level pairwise ranking loss. This is the single candidate run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: x_u=exp_0^c(r_u^3); y_k=exp_0^c(c_k^3); k_i=argmin_k d_c(x_i,y_k)^2; L=L_RQVAE+E_(i,j)~p_train(j|i) softplus(d_c(x_i,y_{k_i})²−d_c(x_j,y_{k_i})²). Use stop_gradient(k_i), not stop-gradient on x or y.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training item residuals, final-level codebook vectors, and the cached outgoing transition table. Output is the usual final-level hard code assignment plus a scalar ranking loss and its gradients.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute maps and distances in float32. Project ball points to norm at most (1−10⁻⁵)/√c; clamp atanh inputs below 1−10⁻⁵; normalize zero-count rows by excluding them from edge sampling; average over eligible pairs and use the fixed coefficient 1. Use a deterministic per-run sampler and finite-loss/gradient checks. Avoid validation-derived normalization or thresholds.
END IDEA

IDEA: BRANCH_CONDITIONED_FINE_BASIS
TITLE: Branch-conditioned hyperbolic residual frames for fine quantization
MOTIVATION: A single fine residual codebook may spend capacity on distinctions that are irrelevant to an item’s observed continuation choices, while failing to distinguish continuation branches that matter for next-item prediction.
NOVELTY: Rather than changing assignment weights or adding a successor-distance penalty, this constructs a training-only, per-item tangent frame from the spread of its outgoing successor embeddings and quantizes the item’s *fine residual in that curved frame*. The behaviour signal changes the coordinates presented to the hyperbolic fine quantizer itself.
CHANGE: Estimate a regularized covariance of each item’s training successors in the tangent space at the Poincaré origin. Whiten the item’s level-3 residual by that covariance, map the transformed residual and codewords into the fixed-curvature ball, and perform the final codeword assignment by Poincaré distance.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i, compute the weighted successor tangent mean μ_i and covariance Σ_i from training-only outgoing neighbors’ fixed Stage1 embeddings e_j, using weights p(j|i). Shrink Σ_i toward the global training covariance by the data-derived factor α_i=n_i/(n_i+D), where n_i is the outgoing transition count and D is embedding dimension. Define A_i=(Σ_i+α_i·tr(Σ_global)/D·I)^(-1/2), and z_i^3=A_i r_i^3. Quantize z_i^3 against shared tangent codewords c_k^3 using Poincaré distance after exp_0. Decode the chosen codeword in the item’s residual frame as A_i^(-1)c^3_{k_i} for residual reconstruction.
COST: One training-only preprocessing pass computes per-item counts, means and diagonal covariances in O(TD), storing O(ND) values; use diagonal covariance for feasibility. Each of the 72,000 optimizer steps applies one per-item diagonal transform and the existing final-level K-codeword Poincaré assignment (O(BD+BKD)); the transform is small relative to the existing codebook distance search. No extra training epochs or model parameters are required.
HYPOTHESIS: If successor covariance directions encode which fine distinctions matter for an item, geometry-aware whitening will change final-token assignments to preserve branch-relevant residual differences and improve Stage3 Recall@10. Record assignment changes and branch-predictability diagnostics; neither is an admission gate.
RISKS: Sparse or noisy outgoing transitions may produce unstable covariance estimates; diagonal whitening ignores correlated dimensions, and item-dependent frames may make a shared codebook’s reconstruction errors inconsistent.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item outgoing successor embedding covariance, representing training-only continuation branching.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From adjacent item pairs in the Stage2 training histories, accumulate n_ij and compute μ_i=Σ_j p(j|i)e_j and diagonal Σ_i=Σ_j p(j|i)(e_j−μ_i)². Compute Σ_global as the training-transition-weighted pooled variance; no validation/test examples are used.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The successor statistics are computed once from Stage2 training histories and fixed Stage1 item embeddings only. Validation/test interactions are not accessible to the statistics builder, RQ-VAE training, or SID exporter.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Origin exponential map and squared Poincaré geodesic distance with fixed curvature −0.5, applied after the behaviour-conditioned residual-frame transform.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The final residual quantization path: transform the level-3 residual using the training-derived frame, map it and the final codebook to the ball, then assign by geodesic distance.
COUPLING PATH: Outgoing training transitions produce Σ_i and hence A_i; A_i transforms the fine residual used as the Poincaré query point, directly changing curved codeword distances and the selected fine SID token. Without behavior A_i is identity; without hyperbolic geometry the same transformed residual is assigned by Euclidean distance.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_i=argmin_k d_c(exp_0(A_i r_i^3),exp_0(c_k^3))², with A_i=diag(Σ_i+α_i·tr(Σ_global)/D·I)^(-1/2) and α_i=n_i/(n_i+D).
OBSERVABLES: The transformed fine residuals, their curved nearest-codeword assignments, and final SID tokens should differ from the baseline where transition covariance is anisotropic; measure resulting Stage3 test Recall@10 and log branch predictability/coherence diagnostically.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: In code, set Σ_i to the global isotropic variance so A_i=I for all items; retain the same Poincaré quantizer. This is the behavior-off comparator, not a trained arm.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: In code, retain the identical training-derived A_i transforms but use squared Euclidean distance between transformed residuals and codewords for the final assignment; omit exp_0 and Poincaré distance. This is the geometry-off comparator, not a trained arm.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-derived per-item diagonal successor covariance frames and Poincaré distance at curvature −0.5 for final residual assignment. This is the single candidate run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p(j|i)=n_ij/Σ_l n_il; μ_i=Σ_j p(j|i)e_j; v_i=Σ_j p(j|i)(e_j−μ_i)²; v_g=pooled train-transition diagonal variance; α_i=n_i/(n_i+D); a_id=(v_id+α_i·mean(v_g))^(-1/2); z_i=A_i r_i^3; k_i=argmin_k d_c(exp_0^c(z_i),exp_0^c(c_k^3))². Reconstruct level-3 residual with A_i^-1 c^3_{k_i}; use the resulting quantization residual in the standard RQ-VAE objective.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are cached training transition counts, fixed Stage1 embeddings, the current level-3 residual, and final-level codebook vectors. Outputs are the final-level hard SID token, reconstructed residual in the original frame, and the ordinary quantization/reconstruction gradients.
SAFEGUARDS: Use diagonal covariance; clamp variance below by ε times the pooled training variance, with ε=10⁻⁵; compute inverse square roots and Poincaré operations in float32. The n_i/(n_i+D) shrinkage uses the observed count and embedding dimension, not a tuned coefficient. Clip transformed residual norms only to the ball-valid radius (1−10⁻⁵)/√c; for items with no outgoing transitions set A_i=I. Check finite transforms and gradients. Do not tune clamps, shrinkage, or other constants.
END IDEA
