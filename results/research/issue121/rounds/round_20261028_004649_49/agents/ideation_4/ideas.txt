IDEA: TRANSITION_GEODESIC_RESIDUAL
TITLE: Transition-preserving geodesic residual quantization
MOTIVATION: Directed next-item transitions can be lost when residual quantization spends fine-level capacity on globally similar items that are rarely substitutable in sequence context.
NOVELTY: Rather than weighting assignment to the first codebook level, train the fine residual codebooks to preserve training transition-neighbour distinctions through a directed, negatively curved residual target.
CHANGE: Build a sparse directed transition graph from training sequences; at each item’s fine-level residual, pull its reconstruction toward the hyperbolic log-map tangent of its transition-neighbour barycenter, with a per-item coefficient derived from outgoing support. This changes the fine residual target and its gradients, not only the L1 assignments.
SPECIFICATION: Let z_i be the encoder output and q_i^{<L} its detached sum of earlier quantized vectors. For outgoing transition counts n_ij, define p_ij=n_ij/sum_k n_ik and b_i=FrechetMean_{j:p_ij>0}(Exp_0(sqrt(c) z_j), weights p_ij) on B_c, using two fixed barycenter iterations. Define r_i=z_i-q_i^{<L}, u_i=Log_0(Exp_0(sqrt(c) r_i)); v_i=Log_0(b_i). At final layer choose k_i=argmin_k d_c(Exp_0(u_i), Exp_0(e_k)) and train Lfine=L_RQVAE + alpha_i ||Log_{Exp_0(u_i)}(Exp_0(e_{k_i})) - Log_{Exp_0(u_i)}(b_i)||^2, where alpha_i=min(1, sqrt(m_i)/median_{i:m_i>0}sqrt(m_i)), m_i=sum_j n_ij. Derive c as inverse median squared norm of z on the training split; alpha scale is fixed by observed support, not tuned. Behaviour-off sets alpha=0 and removes b; geometry-off replaces Exp/Log/d_c and the tangent comparison with identity/Euclidean norm; both-on uses the stated computation. No extra learned module.
COST: Sparse adjacency construction once, O(E) from training transitions. At 24 steps/epoch and 3000 epochs (=72,000 steps), per step for a batch of about 1024 items: O(B*s*d) sparse neighbour accumulation (s=bounded top neighbours retained by cumulative probability mass, without tuning), plus O(B*K_L*d) final-code distance already required by quantization and O(B*d) curved operations; two mean iterations. Expected small overhead over final-layer assignment, no extra model pass.
HYPOTHESIS: If transition neighbours need distinct fine tokens despite nearby encoder vectors, their fine residual assignments/reconstruction gradients change to preserve directional successor structure and Stage3 test Recall@10 rises above 0.065. Record fine assignment changes, successor-token predictability and test recall; diagnostics are not gates.
RISKS: Popular transitions may dominate; item embeddings may not define meaningful hyperbolic barycenters; the target can conflict with reconstruction or encode noise in sparse tails.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed consecutive-item transition counts.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count (i,j) for adjacent items in training sequences only; normalize each source row to p_ij and compute m_i. Retain neighbours in descending count until cumulative mass reaches the row's observed support fraction (all observed edges is also implementable); no validation/test sequences.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Fit counts, support median, and any sparse graph only on the Stage2 training split. Freeze graph before RQ-VAE training; validation/test interactions are never read by SID construction.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball Exp_0, Log maps and geodesic distance d_c at fixed negative curvature -c, c>0.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Final (finest) residual quantization layer, in both nearest-code assignment and the transition-directed residual target loss.
COUPLING PATH: Training transition counts determine b_i and alpha_i; b_i is mapped into the same curved residual tangent comparison used to update the fine codeword, so either removing transitions or replacing curved maps changes that layer's gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_fine(i)=d_c(x_i,c_{k_i})^2 + alpha_i ||Log_{x_i}(c_{k_i})-Log_{x_i}(b_i)||_x^2, x_i=Exp_0(sqrt(c)r_i), b_i=Frechet_c({Exp_0(sqrt(c)z_j)},p_ij).
OBSERVABLES: Fine-level code assignments and codebook gradients change selectively for high-support transition sources; measure successor-token predictability and Stage3 test Recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set alpha_i=0 and omit transition barycenters/counts; retain the curved fine-layer quantizer.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace ball Exp/Log/distance and tangent loss with Euclidean residual, Euclidean distance and Euclidean barycenter; retain the same training transition counts and alpha_i.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only transition counts, curved barycenter and curved residual assignment/target exactly as specified; this is the sole trained run.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For each training source i: m_i=sum_j n_ij; p_ij=n_ij/m_i; b_i=Frechet_c({Exp_0(sqrt(c)z_j)},p_ij); r_i=z_i-sum_{l<L}q_i^l; x_i=Exp_0(sqrt(c)r_i); k_i=argmin_k d_c(x_i,Exp_0(e_k))^2; alpha_i=min(1,sqrt(m_i)/median_{m>0}sqrt(m)); optimize L_RQVAE+alpha_i||Log_{x_i}(Exp_0(e_{k_i}))-Log_{x_i}(b_i)||_x^2. Use stop-gradient through discrete k_i and earlier q; gradients reach fine codebook and encoder.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training item sequences, encoder vectors z, previous-layer quantized sum, fine codebook, fixed c. Output: fine code index, differentiable loss/gradients, training-only sparse transition graph.
SAFEGUARDS: Project ball points to radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp acosh arguments to >=1+1e-7 and squared distances to finite range; normalize nonempty rows only; use b_i=x_i when no outgoing edges and alpha_i=0; detach neighbor z_j for barycenter targets; accumulate counts in float64 and normalize safely.
END IDEA

IDEA: BRANCH_RADIAL_FINE_CODES
TITLE: User-branch radial margins for fine-code separation
MOTIVATION: A user's training history can branch into distinct interests; flattening these branches into one item-space centroid encourages fine codes to merge items that are useful in different next-item contexts.
NOVELTY: Couples per-item branch ambiguity directly to a hyperbolic radial margin on the final residual code assignment. Unlike a behaviour suffix or separate auxiliary objective, the branch-derived radius changes the geometric distance that selects the finest token.
CHANGE: Estimate each item's successor-interest branching from training-only user sequences; use branch entropy to set a derived radial coordinate, then assign its final residual code by Poincare distance to codewords displaced to that radius along each codeword's direction. This radial geometry acts inside the argmin and its straight-through assignment gradient.
SPECIFICATION: For training item i, aggregate next-item categories by successor's fixed training-only coarse category g(j), where g is obtained once by deterministic k-means on training item embeddings (K set to the existing first-level code count); let p_i(a) be normalized counts and H_i=-sum_a p_i(a)log p_i(a). Define h_i=H_i/log(max(2,number of observed categories)), and rho_i=median(||r|| over training residuals)*sqrt(h_i), with radius scale derived from residual data. Map residual r_i to x_i=Exp_0(sqrt(c)r_i); codeword e_k is mapped to y_ik=Exp_0(sqrt(c)e_k + sqrt(c)*rho_i*e_k/(||e_k||+eps)). Select argmin_k d_c(x_i,y_ik)^2 and use selected y_ik for the final quantized reconstruction/STE. Behaviour-off sets h_i=0; geometry-off computes Euclidean distances to e_k+rho_i e_k/(||e_k||+eps); both-on uses curved displaced prototypes. c is inverse median squared residual norm.
COST: One-time successor aggregation O(number of training transitions), entropy O(E); deterministic coarse category clustering is one O(N*K*d*iterations) preprocessing pass. Per step at B≈1024 (24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs =72,000 steps), form B*K_L radial prototypes and distances: O(B*K_L*d), comparable to required final-layer code search, plus O(B*K_L) maps. No extra network pass; modest quantization overhead.
HYPOTHESIS: When the same item has successors spanning multiple interest branches, entropy-scaled radial displacement changes fine assignments enough to retain branch-specific distinctions, increasing Stage3 test Recall@10 beyond 0.065. Observe fine assignments conditioned on branch entropy, successor predictability and test recall, without gating.
RISKS: Coarse categories may be poor or overly popularity-driven; entropy may reflect noisy items rather than real interest breadth; radial shift can make an item systematically prefer a codeword regardless of residual direction.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: User-conditioned successor-interest branching summarized as successor category entropy per source item.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training sequences only, count next-item coarse categories following each item across users; normalize to p_i(a), compute entropy and normalized h_i. Coarse categories come from deterministic clustering of training item embeddings alone; category count is the existing first-level code count.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Both categories and successor counts are built exclusively from Stage2 training items/sequences. Freeze them before SID training; validation/test rows are excluded and never queried for category, entropy or assignment.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare Exp_0 and geodesic distance at fixed curvature -c; code prototypes are displaced in the tangent radial direction before mapping into the ball.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Final residual codebook prototype construction and hard nearest-code assignment during RQ-VAE training and SID encoding.
COUPLING PATH: Training-only branching entropy sets each item's radial displacement; that displaced point is compared to its residual by Poincare distance inside the fine argmin, directly changing token assignment and downstream gradient.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_i=argmin_k d_c(Exp_0(sqrt(c)r_i),Exp_0(sqrt(c)e_k+sqrt(c)rho_i e_k/(||e_k||+eps)))^2; rho_i=median_train(||r||)*sqrt(H_i/log(max(2,A_i))).
OBSERVABLES: High-branching and low-branching items may select different fine codes for comparable residual directions; measure fine token branch predictability and Stage3 test Recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set H_i=0 and rho_i=0; use ordinary curved fine code assignment.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Use the identical H_i and rho_i but replace Poincare Exp/distance with Euclidean displaced prototypes and squared Euclidean distance.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Apply entropy-derived radial displacement and Poincare geodesic argmin; only this configuration is trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p_i(a)=n_i(a)/sum_b n_i(b); H_i=-sum_a p_i(a)log(p_i(a)); h_i=H_i/log(max(2,A_i)); rho_i=median_train||r|| sqrt(h_i); x_i=Exp_0(sqrt(c)r_i); y_ik=Exp_0(sqrt(c)(e_k+rho_i e_k/(||e_k||+eps))); k_i=argmin_k d_c(x_i,y_ik)^2; q_i^L=Log_0(y_i,k_i)/sqrt(c) for residual reconstruction. Behavior-off and geometry-off switches are defined as above.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training user sequences, training item embeddings, residual vectors, K_L fine codes and curvature c. Outputs: per-item entropy/radius, fine code assignment and quantized reconstruction.
SAFEGUARDS: Use eps=machine-safe 1e-8 for direction norm; define entropy zero for empty/single-category supports and rho=0 there; clamp ball norm below (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); derive c from median residual scale; finite-clamp distance/acosh; deterministic clustering seed fixed by existing run seed.
END IDEA
