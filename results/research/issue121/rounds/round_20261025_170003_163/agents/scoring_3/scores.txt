SCOPE: transition_geodesic_encoder | IN_SCOPE | Training-only directed adjacent-item transitions are explicitly computed, and Poincare exp-map plus distance-based transition ranking updates encoder parameters before quantization. Behavior and geometry jointly shape encoder representations, so either removal changes the core training computation. The specification includes negatives, safeguards through bounded ball mapping, cost, observable encoder/SID and Recall effects, and behavior-off, geometry-off/Euclidean, and both-on comparisons.
RATIONALE: transition_geodesic_encoder | The transition ranking objective is implementable and targets a meaningful mismatch between content similarity and next-item movement; coupling is real but moderate because hyperbolic geometry only shapes the auxiliary encoder objective while quantization remains Euclidean, leaving room for the quantizer to erase its benefit. Novelty and diversity are moderate relative to other plausible encoder-geometry mechanisms. Main uncertainties are whether the ranking signal transfers beyond observed edges, how negatives and loss weighting are chosen, and whether the Euclidean quantizer preserves useful changes.

SCOPE: branch_curvature_encoder | OUT_OF_SCOPE | The training-only outgoing-transition branching statistic is explicit, and Lorentz exp/log maps are named in the encoder path. However, as specified, the curvature-dependent Lorentz Exp followed immediately by the corresponding curvature-dependent Log at the origin returns the original tangent vector u_i (up to numerical error); thus curvature does not change the core computation or couple branching to the representation. The required both-on versus geometry-off/Euclidean comparison is proposed, but this round-trip makes geometry-off effectively equivalent and does not provide a genuine curvature effect.
RATIONALE: branch_curvature_encoder | The branching-conditioned curvature concept is novel and has an implementable data statistic, but the exact map composition undermines its claimed geometric effect: the matched exp/log round trip cancels curvature rather than introducing hierarchy or volume into the vector passed to the Euclidean quantizer. This is an implementation-level issue, not merely an unfinished derivation. A non-cancelling curved operation or curved quantization path could make the idea testable; as written, downstream benefit is uncertain and coupling is weak.
SCORE: transition_tangent_warp | novelty=4 | behavior_problem_meaning=4 | coupling_strength=4 | implementability=3 | downstream_potential=4 | experimental_diversity=4
SCOPE: transition_tangent_warp | IN_SCOPE | It computes explicit training-only directed incoming and outgoing transition profiles and projects them into a bounded learned tangent displacement. Poincare exp-map, Mobius addition and log-map combine that behavior vector with the encoder output before quantization, so removing behavior removes the displacement and removing geometry changes the combination path. The proposal states ordinary RQ-VAE training, numerical bounding/unknown-profile handling, cost, expected latent/SID and Recall effects, and behavior-off, Euclidean geometry-off and both-on comparisons.
RATIONALE: transition_tangent_warp | This provides a direct, differentiable pathway from transition context through curved operations into the quantizer input, with a plausible next-item signal and a distinct experimental mechanism. It is more tightly coupled than a separate ranking loss. Implementability is somewhat reduced by sparse profile projection and the need to specify consistent curvature/map conventions and an effective Euclidean geometry-off control; behavior profiles may encode frequency or sparse noise, and the reconstruction/VQ objective may cancel the learned warp. Stage3 gains remain empirical.
