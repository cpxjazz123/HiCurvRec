IDEA: transition_geodesic_encoder
TITLE: Train the encoder in a transition-shaped Poincare latent space
MOTIVATION: Static item embeddings can put items with similar content nearby while failing to represent which items users actually move between. Training-only directed transitions provide a next-item signal without involving validation or test histories.
NOVELTY: Unlike the existing Busemann transition gate, transition behavior does not score or reweight codeword assignments; it trains the encoder representation via a hyperbolic geodesic objective. Unlike prefix-mass geometry, it acts on individual observed training transitions before quantization.
CHANGE: Map encoder outputs to a bounded Poincare ball and optimize a directed transition-ranking objective using Poincare distance, alongside the unchanged reconstruction and RQ-VAE losses. Feed the resulting encoder outputs through the existing Euclidean RQ assignment so any SID change comes from behavior-shaped encoder updates, not curved assignment.
SPECIFICATION: For each training edge (i,j), obtain encoder tangent vectors u_i,u_j; map z=exp_0^c(u), with curvature -c, and minimize softplus(m + d_c(z_i,z_j+) - d_c(z_i,z_neg)). Negatives are training-catalog items sampled uniformly or by popularity, excluding j+. Apply this loss to encoder parameters jointly with baseline RQ-VAE loss; quantizer selection remains baseline Euclidean nearest-codeword.
COST: Precompute deduplicated directed transitions from training events. At 24556 items, 24 steps/epoch, and 3000 epochs there are 72000 updates (about 1024 item examples/update); sample one positive edge and one negative per example, adding two encoder-side hyperbolic maps/distances per pair. Incremental cost is roughly one extra small-batch encoder forward for endpoints plus O(Bd) maps/distances, expected modest versus baseline, with no all-pairs computation.
HYPOTHESIS: The encoder can learn a geometry in which observed next-item edges are more locally coherent than popularity-matched non-edges while preserving content reconstruction; this should alter encoder gradients and some SIDs and, if useful, raise Stage3 test Recall@10 above 0.065. Measure against the prescribed three runs and retain only if the both-on result improves on plain baseline 0.063354 and reaches the target.
RISKS: A transition objective may overfit frequent edges, conflict with reconstruction, or make latent representations less discriminative for unseen next items. Euclidean quantization may erase the useful geometry; tuning the loss weight and popularity-matched negatives is necessary. Cost can rise if endpoint embeddings are repeatedly re-encoded rather than cached where gradients permit.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-next-item transitions from user training sequences.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training split only, count consecutive pairs (i,j) within each user's chronologically ordered training interactions; sample positive edges proportional to counts. For each positive, sample a non-positive catalog item as negative (preferably matched to the positive's log-popularity bin). No validation or test sequences are used.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct edge counts and negative-sampling distribution exclusively from the training interaction split. Item content embeddings and catalog IDs are fixed inputs; do not use validation/test transitions, histories, labels, or interaction-derived statistics when training this loss or exporting SIDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball exponential map and geodesic distance d_c at fixed c=1 (sectional curvature -1); clamp points inside radius (1-epsilon)/sqrt(c).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Encoder training objective, before unchanged residual quantization and Euclidean codeword assignment.
COUPLING PATH: A sampled training edge determines which two encoder outputs are mapped into the curved ball and compared by geodesic distance; this loss updates encoder parameters, which produce the vectors subsequently quantized into SIDs. Removing edges removes the pairwise objective; replacing the curved map/distance removes the hyperbolic computation.
COUPLING EQUATION: L = L_RQVAE + lambda * E_(i,j+,j-) [softplus(m + d_Poincare_c(exp_0^c(u_i), exp_0^c(u_j+)) - d_Poincare_c(exp_0^c(u_i), exp_0^c(u_j-)))]; the sampled directed behavior edge (i,j+) selects the positive endpoint, and d_Poincare_c is the curved term.
OBSERVABLES: Compare encoder gradient contributions and per-item encoder vectors, SID assignment changes, positive-versus-negative transition geodesic ranking accuracy, and Stage3 test Recall@10. The expected useful effect is improved ranking of held-out next items and Recall, not a required reduction in collisions or prefix statistics.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set lambda=0 and train the same architecture, schedule, seeds, and baseline RQ-VAE objective; no transition edges enter training.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep exactly the same sampled positive/negative edges and lambda, but replace exp_0^c and d_Poincare_c with identity mapping and Euclidean distance; keep architecture and other losses fixed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only directed edges, Poincare exp map and distance at c=1, and the stated joint objective; compare all three runs using the same Stage3 evaluation protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: u_k=f_theta(x_k); z_k=exp_0^c(u_k); d_c(a,b)=(2/sqrt(c))*atanh(sqrt(c)*||(-a) MobiusAdd_c b||); L_trans=mean softplus(m+d_c(z_i,z_j+)-d_c(z_i,z_j-)); L=L_RQVAE+lambda L_trans. For stable exp map use exp_0^c(u)=tanh(sqrt(c)||u||)*u/(sqrt(c)||u||), with the zero-norm limit set to u. Keep baseline quantizer and codebook distances unchanged.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen item content vectors, training-only sampled edge triples (i,j+,j-), encoder f_theta, c, m and lambda. Output is scalar training loss and its gradients to encoder parameters; after training, encoder vectors pass to baseline SID construction.
SAFEGUARDS: Use epsilon-clamped norms and atanh arguments below 1-epsilon; constrain mapped norms below (1-epsilon)/sqrt(c); use stable zero-norm limits; finite-check loss and gradients; cap gradient norm; exclude observed positive targets from negatives; fix random seeds and report multiple seeds. Start with small lambda and tune only on training-side diagnostics, not test Recall.
END IDEA
