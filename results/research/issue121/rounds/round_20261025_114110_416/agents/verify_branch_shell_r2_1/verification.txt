FINDING DETAIL: artanh clamps to 1 - 1e-10. In float32, 1 - 1e-10 rounds to exactly 1, so torch.log1p(-x) can evaluate to negative infinity. poincare_distance calls this on sqrt(c)*norm(diff); projected float32 points near the ball boundary or Mobius arithmetic rounding can yield an argument rounded to 1. The resulting infinite distance propagates into assignment and the gathered quantization loss. The trainer checks finite loss but will abort rather than train in this case. The coupled distance term is otherwise live and differentiable through the selected distance; codebook selection itself is discrete as expected.
FINDING REPRODUCTION: Evaluate artanh(torch.tensor([1.0], dtype=torch.float32)) with this implementation: the upper clamp rounds to 1.0 and the result is inf.
