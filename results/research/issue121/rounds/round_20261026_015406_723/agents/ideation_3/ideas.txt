IDEA: successor-chart-residual
TITLE: Successor-charted hyperbolic residual transport
MOTIVATION: Items with the same likely next-item neighborhood should share a predictable coarse chart, while the chart should leave a compact, behaviorally coherent residual for T2/T3 rather than forcing continuation detail into T4.
NOVELTY: Unlike assigning T1 from a successor geodesic consensus score or adding a Busemann direction to a codeword score, the transition signal defines an item-specific tangent chart in which the residual itself is quantized; it changes the coordinates and distances used at T2/T3.
CHANGE: Build each training item's smoothed successor barycenter, transport its latent to that chart on a fixed-curvature Poincare ball, and quantize the transported residual with geodesic distances at T2/T3. Keep T1 as the existing compact coarse stage; decode residual codewords back through the inverse transport before the next residual is formed.
SPECIFICATION: Let training-only P_ij=(n_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(n_i+alpha), where n_ij counts consecutive training events and pi is the training-item frequency distribution. Map frozen item embeddings z_j to x_j=exp_0^c(z_j), project to radius <(1-eps)/sqrt(c), and define b_i=FrechetMean_c({x_j;P_ij}) by 3 weighted Karcher steps. Let a_i=min(1,n_i/n0). At residual level l=2,3, let r_i be the current residual point and v_i=log_{b_i}^c(r_i); assign k=argmin_k d_c(exp_{b_i}^c(v_i), exp_{b_i}^c(q_lk))^2, with q_lk constrained to the tangent ball radius R and the usual straight-through update. Subtract the selected q in the same chart using Mobius subtraction and repeat; level 4 remains deterministic collision extension.
COST: Precompute sparse P rows and b_i once, O(T+N*3*degree*D); no sequence scans during training. Per item-step adds two log/exp maps and up to 2*K geodesic evaluations at each of T2/T3 (O(B*K*D), B≈1,023; same asymptotic assignment cost). At 24 steps/epoch and 3,000 epochs: 72,000 optimizer steps, 24,556*3,000=73,668,000 item presentations; cache lookup only per batch. Expect a modest geometry-kernel overhead, benchmark wall time against matched baseline.
HYPOTHESIS: A training successor barycenter is a useful local origin: items sharing continuations then have more similar T2/T3 residuals, reducing conditional branching without sacrificing item identity. Keep only if T3 top-1 reaches about .70, T4 is near-deterministic with improved NLL, prefix survival rises jointly, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds .065 and both ablations.
RISKS: Sparse/noisy successor rows make barycenters unstable; popular items dominate smoothed means; chart transport can over-normalize distinct items or make residual subtraction inconsistent with the baseline decoder. T1 remains the bottleneck if its compact grouping is not predictive.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed, popularity-smoothed next-item transition distribution P_ij.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count consecutive item pairs only within each user's training interaction sequence, in timestamp order; smooth outgoing counts with alpha times the training-only global item frequency pi. Drop self-pairs if the production preprocessing does so; retain no validation/test events.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct P, pi, b_i solely from training-split events and frozen training item embeddings; cache by item ID. Validation/test are used only by the existing evaluator after SIDs are frozen and cannot update counts, barycenters, codebooks, or hyperparameters.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp/log maps, weighted Karcher barycenter, Mobius subtraction and geodesic distance at fixed negative curvature -c, initially c=0.5.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Per-item tangent-chart transform and residual code assignment at T2/T3 during Stage2 quantization/training.
COUPLING PATH: Training successors determine b_i; b_i is the origin for log transport of the current latent and for exp-map reconstruction of every candidate residual codeword; geodesic candidate ranking emits T2/T3. Removing behavior sets b_i=0; replacing geometry removes the chart transport and distance.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_{i,l}=argmin_k d_{-c}(exp_{b_i}^{-c}(log_{b_i}^{-c}(r_{i,l})), exp_{b_i}^{-c}(q_{l,k}))^2, with b_i=FrechetMean_{-c}(x_j;P_{ij}); the behavior term P_{ij} sets the hyperbolic chart used by the curved term d_{-c}.
OBSERVABLES: Report canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER with ten fixed negatives, gold-prefix survival at depths 1-4 in width-20 beam, teacher-forced T3/T4 NLL and top-1, and Stage3 test_R@10. Expect ER not to worsen materially, T1 survival roughly stable (T1 untouched), T2/T3/T4 survival to improve, T3 top-1 to approach or exceed .70, T4 NLL down/top-1 up, and Recall@10 >.065. Compare all measures across the three arms.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set b_i=0 for all items and use identical Poincare residual quantizers, data, seeds and schedule; this isolates the value of the training successor chart.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Retain P_ij and chart-conditioned residual coordinate construction in a matched tangent Euclidean implementation (ordinary vector mean, subtraction/addition and squared Euclidean distance); match scale on training batches and retain codebook sizes/schedule.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use cached training-only P_ij barycenters and the specified fixed-curvature Poincare chart transport and T2/T3 geodesic assignment; train exactly 3,000 Stage2 epochs and evaluate frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: x_j=exp_0^{-c}(z_j); b_i=KarcherMean_{-c}(x_j,P_ij); v_i=log_{b_i}^{-c}(r_i); q*=argmin_k d_{-c}(exp_{b_i}^{-c}(v_i),exp_{b_i}^{-c}(q_k))^2; r_i←MobiusSub_{-c}(r_i,exp_{b_i}^{-c}(q*)). Apply at l=2,3; deterministic T4 collision extension unchanged.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen training item embeddings, sparse training transition counts, current residual, and T2/T3 codebooks; output is the selected residual token, quantized point and next residual. Cache one b_i and confidence per training item.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute geometry in float32; project norms to (1-eps)/sqrt(c), eps=1e-5; use log/exp denominator clamps, Karcher step size <=0.25 and exactly 3 iterations; normalize P with alpha>0 and minimum outgoing mass; fall back to origin for n_i=0; clip tangent norms and verify finite forward/gradients. Use identical fixed negatives and deterministic collision extension in all arms.
END IDEA

IDEA: branching-curvature-budget
TITLE: Branching-conditioned curvature budget for hierarchical quantization
MOTIVATION: A single global curvature and residual scale spend too many tokens distinguishing items with many competing continuations, while low-branching items need a compact, easily predicted prefix and a decisive tail.
NOVELTY: Existing transition methods use successor direction, geodesic consensus, or boundary potentials to choose/steer codes. This instead uses a training-only continuation branching statistic to set the curvature of an item-conditioned hyperbolic quantization metric, then lets the resulting radial hierarchy shape all four assignments; it does not append a behavior token or merely change codebook size.
CHANGE: Estimate each item's effective successor branching and use it to condition the negative curvature in a shared Lorentz/Poincare hierarchical quantizer. Low-branching items receive stronger negative curvature (more radial separation and a short predictable hierarchy); high-branching items receive flatter curvature and retain more angular/residual capacity, with a training-only regularizer preventing the four-level path from pushing all detail to T4.
SPECIFICATION: From training transitions define p_ij=(n_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(n_i+alpha), H_i=-sum_j p_ij log p_ij, and h_i=clip(H_i/log(max(2,d_i)),0,1), with h_i=1 when no evidence. Let c_i=c_min+(c_max-c_min)*(1-h_i), c_min=.15,c_max=1.0, differentiable only through latent/codebooks (c_i cached). At each level l map residual u_il to Poincare x_il=exp_0^{c_i}(u_il), assign k by argmin d_{c_i}(x_il,exp_0^{c_i}(e_lk))^2. Add a training-only continuation consistency penalty lambda*a_i*sum_j p_ij min_{k in N(k_i)} d_{c_i}(e_{l,k_i}, e_{l,k_j})^2 on the first three-level prefix representatives, where N maps successor item codes through the same current codebooks; stop-gradient through the discrete neighbors. Add a tail-balance penalty lambda_b*relu(H(T3|T1,T2)-H(T4|T1,T2,T3)-m) estimated over training batches, preventing excess ambiguity from being deferred to T4.
COST: Precompute H_i,h_i from sparse transition counts O(T+N*degree), negligible cache. Per assignment adds a per-item curvature scale and existing hyperbolic distance cost O(B*K*D); continuation regularizer adds sparse successor-code lookups O(B*degree*D) for sampled edges. Frozen scale: 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs = 72,000 steps and 73,668,000 item presentations; no extra full-catalog pass per step. Expected 5-15% wall-clock overhead depending on sparse edge sampling; measure on the four-card baseline.
HYPOTHESIS: Normalized continuation entropy predicts how much semantic branching an item actually needs. Curvature conditioned on it will reserve a small, stable prefix for low-branching behavior and use later residual tokens for genuine alternatives, improving joint beam survival rather than just shifting information to T4. Keep only if full run raises joint survival and Stage3 test_R@10 above .065, with T3/T4 metrics moving in the predicted direction versus each ablation.
RISKS: Entropy may mostly measure item popularity/data sparsity; per-item curvature can create discontinuous code boundaries, overfit rare items, or cause optimizer scale mismatch. The balance penalty may trade reconstruction accuracy for an artificial token distribution; hyperbolic effective dimension may be insufficient.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Normalized entropy/effective branching of the directed next-item distribution.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count ordered consecutive item transitions inside training user histories only; form additively smoothed p_ij using training-only item-frequency prior pi; compute normalized entropy H/log(max(2,d_i)) over observed plus smoothed successor support. Cache h_i once before Stage2.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: All pair counts, prior frequencies, entropy values, and any sampled successor edges come exclusively from training histories. Freeze cached h_i before validation or test evaluation; no interaction, metric, or target from those splits can enter curvature, assignments, or regularizer.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Item-conditioned Poincare exponential map and geodesic codeword distance with negative curvature -c_i, plus geodesic distance in the continuation consistency term.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Every residual assignment in Stage2 and the successor consistency regularizer; c_i is fixed per item from training data and applied to the latent and codebook points.
COUPLING PATH: Transition probabilities yield h_i and c_i; c_i determines the exp map and geodesic distance ranking the residual codewords, while those same selected codewords are compared along training successor edges under d_{c_i}. Removing behavior makes curvature constant and removes edge weighting; Euclidean replacement removes both curved map and distance.
COUPLING EQUATION: k_{il}=argmin_k d_{c_i}(exp_0^{c_i}(u_{il}),exp_0^{c_i}(e_{lk}))^2 + lambda*a_i*sum_j p_{ij} d_{c_i}(e_{l,k_{il}},e_{l,k_{jl}})^2, where c_i=c_min+(c_max-c_min)(1-h_i); the behavior term (p_ij,h_i) sets the curvature c_i of the curved assignment and successor score.
OBSERVABLES: Evaluate canonical 52,855-pair L1 Hard ER (ten fixed negatives), gold-prefix survival depth 1-4 at beam width 20, teacher-forced T3/T4 NLL and top-1, and Stage3 test_R@10. Predict L1 ER flat or slightly lower with 16-64 effective groups, L1 survival up, then survival retained/improved at T2-T4; T3 top-1 near/above .70, T4 NLL lower and top-1 near deterministic; test_R@10 must exceed .065. Explicitly inspect whether gains are joint rather than a T4-only information shift.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set h_i to its training-set mean and remove p_ij edge consistency, giving all items the same fixed c; retain curved quantization, architecture, schedule and codebook sizes.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep h_i, p_ij, and edge weighting but replace exp/log maps and geodesic distances with Euclidean vectors and squared Euclidean distances; use training-only scale matching for the edge term.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use per-item c_i from training-only normalized transition entropy, Poincare exp maps/geodesic assignment at all levels, and the sparse successor geodesic consistency term; run fixed 3,000 epochs and frozen Stage3 evaluation.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p_ij=(n_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(n_i+alpha); H_i=-sum p_ij log p_ij; h_i=clip(H_i/log(max(2,d_i)),0,1); c_i=cmin+(cmax-cmin)(1-h_i); x_il=exp_0^{c_i}(u_il); k_il=argmin_k d_{c_i}(x_il,exp_0^{c_i}(e_lk))^2; L=L_RQVAE+lambda*a_i*sum_j p_ij*d_{c_i}(e_{k_i},e_{k_j})^2+lambda_b*relu(H(T3|T1,T2)-H(T4|T1,T2,T3)-m).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training-only sparse successor counts/prior, cached h_i and confidence a_i, current residual vectors, and four existing codebooks. Output is four fixed-width tokens (with existing deterministic collision extension) and the standard reconstruction/commitment plus behavioral regularizer losses.
SAFEGUARDS: Smooth counts with alpha>0 and cap maximum successor support; use h_i=1 for no data and confidence a_i=min(1,n_i/n0); clamp c_i to [.15,1], project all ball norms below (1-eps)/sqrt(c_i), eps=1e-5; float32 geometry and finite-gradient checks. Sample a fixed maximum number of successors per item with deterministic training-only sampling; stop gradients through argmin and neighbor IDs. Estimate conditional entropy penalty only on adequately populated training prefixes, with pseudocounts and clipped penalty; keep seeds/schedule/negative set fixed.
END IDEA
