IDEA: successor_shell_budget
TITLE: Successor-conditioned hyperbolic prefix budget
MOTIVATION: A coarse T1 should retain almost all gold items, while T2/T3 distinguish the continuation modes within that coarse group instead of spending the first token on fine detail.
NOVELTY: Unlike a behavior-selected first-level target or item-specific shell radius, this uses a train-only successor distribution to allocate a shared hyperbolic branching budget to each T1 subtree; it changes the curved child assignment itself and explicitly penalizes behaviorally incoherent child partitions.
CHANGE: Build a smoothed successor distribution for each training item, then jointly score its candidate T1/T2 child path by hyperbolic distance plus a successor-conditioned geodesic separation term. Reserve a small number of T1 codes for broad groups and train T2/T3 to partition successors consistently inside each group; T4 remains deterministic collision extension.
SPECIFICATION: From training-only transitions estimate p(j|i) with additive smoothing. Let a_i=entropy(p(.|i))/log(max(2,|N_i|)) and let u_i be the normalized tangent-space mean of successor encoder vectors. For item i and candidate parent k, child m, set S(i,k,m)=d_c(x_i,q_k)^2+ d_c(r_i^k,c_m)^2 + λ a_i [d_c(exp_o(u_i),exp_o(c_m))^2 - d_c(exp_o(u_i),exp_o(c_alt))^2]_+; use the corresponding softmax probabilities for a differentiable sibling-separation loss against the empirical successor-child distribution. Hard argmin emits the fixed-width tokens. Allocate e.g. 32 T1 codes and leave the baseline T2/T3 codebook sizes unchanged.
COST: One preprocessing pass O(T_train) plus cached successor means/distributions; per item and candidate child, a constant number of O(D) Lorentz/Poincare distance operations. At frozen scale: about 1,023 items/step × 32 × K2 × D scored coordinate work; 24 steps/epoch × 3,000 epochs = 72,000 steps (24,556 × 3,000 = 73,668,000 item presentations). Expect approximately 1.3–1.8× baseline quantizer time depending on K2, with no added sequence traversal in the loop.
HYPOTHESIS: Training successors for an item have coherent continuation modes, and putting those modes into local hyperbolic child partitions makes T2/T3 more predictable conditional on T1. Keep only if behavior-on + curved geometry raises frozen Stage3 test_R@10 above 0.065 and beats both controls; expect T1 Hard ER lower/coarser (effective 16–64 groups), gold-prefix survival L1 near 1 and L2–L4 higher, T3/T4 NLL lower and top-1 higher (T3 around ≥0.7; T4 nearly deterministic). Measure all on the canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives and width-20 beam.
RISKS: Successor entropy can be noisy for rare items; sibling constraints may overfit frequent transitions, consume too much capacity, or make reconstruction worse. A forced coarse T1 may merely move errors to T2 unless continuation modes really cluster.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed next-item transition distribution for each item, derived exclusively from training sequences.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count adjacent (i,j) pairs in the Stage2 training split only; p(j|i)=(n_ij+α)/(Σ_j n_ij+α|N_i|), retaining top M successors and an OTHER bucket. Compute entropy and normalized successor-embedding tangent mean once from frozen encoder item vectors; no validation/test sequence is read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Cache is keyed only by training item IDs and training adjacent pairs, generated before Stage2 fitting; validation/test interactions are inaccessible to the preprocessing function. Unseen items use the training global prior and fixed fallback vector.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz-hyperboloid exponential map and geodesic distance d_κ at fixed negative curvature -κ, κ=0.5; candidate sibling codes are compared by squared geodesic distance.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: The T1-to-T2 residual assignment and behavior-conditioned child-partition training objective; T3 uses the same curved residual assignment, while export preserves deterministic T4 collision extension.
COUPLING PATH: Train transitions determine each item's successor tangent direction and continuation uncertainty; these set the geodesic child-partition score inside each candidate T1 subtree, which selects T2/T3 tokens and backpropagates through the same curved distances.
COUPLING EQUATION: S_beh(i,k,m)=d_κ(x_i,q_k)^2+d_κ(r_i^k,c_m)^2+λ a_i d_κ(exp_o(u_i),c_m)^2; minimize S_beh and add L_sib=λ_b Σ_i a_i Σ_{j~p(.|i)}[d_κ(c_{z_i},c_{z_j})-δ]_+^2, where z_i is the assigned child and the successor weighting uses p(j|i).
OBSERVABLES: Report canonical L1 Hard ER on 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives, width-20 gold-prefix survival at depths 1–4, teacher-forced T3/T4 NLL and top-1, and Stage3 test_R@10. Predicted direction: coarser/lower L1 ER, L1 survival ↑ toward 1, survival at each later depth ↑, T3/T4 NLL ↓ and accuracy ↑, test_R@10 ↑ toward >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Replace a_i and u_i by their training-item mean / zero direction, preserving code sizes, curvature, optimizer and all other settings; this removes successor-conditioned child scores.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace every Lorentz exp/distance in the score and sibling term with matched Euclidean norm and keep the same behavior statistics, temperatures and coefficients.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use training-only successor statistics in the Lorentz score and sibling objective at fixed κ=0.5, with identical four-token export and Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: For curvature -κ, map tangent v by exp_o(v)=(cosh(√κ||v||)/√κ, sinh(√κ||v||)v/||v||); d_κ(X,Y)=acosh(max(1,-κ<X,Y>_L))/√κ. For each residual level l, choose z_il=argmin_m d_κ(r_il,c_lm)^2 + λ_l a_i d_κ(U_i,c_lm)^2, where U_i=exp_o(u_i), and form residual via Lorentz log map at selected parent. Train sibling loss using soft assignments and train-only successor weights; straight-through hard assignments feed baseline reconstruction/commitment losses. Exact adapted-path formula: S(i,k,m)=d_κ(X_i,Q_k)^2+d_κ(Log_{Q_k}X_i,C_m)^2+λ a_i d_κ(U_i,C_m)^2; minimize over k,m and use sequential residual levels thereafter.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are encoder item vector x_i, existing codebooks, train-only p(j|i), a_i and u_i; outputs are four fixed-width SID tokens, quantized vectors and differentiable code-assignment loss.
SAFEGUARDS: Float32/64 Lorentz arithmetic; clamp acosh argument to 1+ε, normalize zero tangent with ε, project vectors to future hyperboloid, clip tangent norms and gradients, cap λ and a_i, smooth rare-item counts, deterministic tie-breaking, and keep T4 collision extension unchanged. Cache statistics once; use soft assignments only for training loss, hard IDs for export.
END IDEA

IDEA: curved_transition_residual
TITLE: Behavior-directed Lorentz residual transport
MOTIVATION: Fine residual tokens should encode the next-item options that remain after a broad prefix is known; ordinary residual subtraction can leave continuation evidence arbitrarily scattered across T3 and T4.
NOVELTY: Rather than adding a successor direction to an unchanged Euclidean residual or steering a selected anchor with a Poincare tangent correction, this makes the residual itself a Lorentzian transition-conditioned geodesic displacement and trains the child code to decode that displacement.
CHANGE: At each item, construct a train-only successor barycenter in the Lorentz tangent space, transport it to the current residual anchor, and subtract a bounded fraction through an exponential map before T2/T3 quantization. A small train-only successor reconstruction term makes the transported residual encode continuation modes early, rather than merely appending a behavior token.
SPECIFICATION: For training item i with successor weights p(j|i), compute μ_i=normalize(Σ_j p(j|i) Log_o(X_j)) in the tangent space. Given current residual point R_i^l and parent code Q_i^{l-1}, parallel-transport μ_i from origin to Q_i^{l-1}, then set R'_i=Exp_{Q_i^{l-1}}(Log_{Q_i^{l-1}}(R_i^l)-η g_i PT(μ_i)); quantize R'_i to the next code by Lorentz distance. Train a decoder from the selected T2/T3 code to μ_i with a clipped tangent-space cosine loss; fixed T1 coarse grouping (32 effective groups), T4 unchanged.
COST: O(T_train) preprocessing, then one log/parallel-transport/exp per item and residual level, plus a small code-to-tangent prediction head. At frozen scale this is 24 steps/epoch × 3,000 = 72,000 steps and 24,556 × 3,000 = 73,668,000 item presentations; added transport is O(D) per item at T2/T3, roughly 1.2–1.5× baseline Stage2 time and no additional sequence scans.
HYPOTHESIS: A broad prefix leaves continuation signal in the residual, and subtracting its transported geodesic component makes T2/T3 codes align to predictable successor modes. Expect L1 Hard ER ↓ (coarser 16–64 effective groups), L1 survival ↑ near 1, gold-prefix survival at L2–L4 ↑, T3/T4 NLL ↓ and top-1 ↑ (T3 target ≈0.7+, T4 near deterministic), and Stage3 test_R@10 ↑ above 0.065; retain only on measured improvement over both controls.
RISKS: The successor mean may average incompatible modes and erase useful item identity; transport can distort residual norms or destabilize code usage. A behavior decoder could improve its auxiliary objective without improving held-out next-item prediction.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Empirical directed next-item distribution p(j|i) and its weighted successor embedding mean, from Stage2 training interactions only.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count within-session adjacent transitions in the training split, smooth counts with α=0.1 and cap to top 32 successors plus OTHER; map successor item vectors through the encoder snapshot and average their origin-log vectors weighted by p(j|i). No target labels from validation/test are included.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition table and barycenters are generated exclusively from the training split and training item embeddings, then frozen/cached. Validation/test events cannot update counts, barycenters, assignments, or hyperparameters; test is used only once by the frozen Stage3 evaluator.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz log/exp maps, parallel transport and geodesic quantization on curvature -κ, fixed κ=0.5.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Residual update between T1 and T2 and between T2 and T3, before hard nearest-code assignment; the transported behavior changes the residual point that produces the tokens.
COUPLING PATH: The training successor barycenter is parallel-transported into the item's parent tangent space, subtracted by a bounded geodesic step from its residual, and that altered point is assigned to the child codebook and decoded toward the same successor barycenter.
COUPLING EQUATION: R'_i=Exp_{Q_i}(Log_{Q_i}(R_i)-η g_i PT_{o→Q_i}(μ_i)); z_i=argmin_m d_κ(R'_i,C_m)^2; L=L_RQVAE+β g_i(1-cos(Log_o(C_{z_i}),μ_i)), with μ_i=Σ_j p(j|i)Log_o(X_j).
OBSERVABLES: Track canonical L1 Hard ER (52,855 pairs, ten fixed negatives), width-20 gold-prefix survival L1–L4, teacher-forced NLL/top-1 for T3/T4, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Desired pattern: L1 ER modestly lower/coarser; survival L1 ≈1 and each subsequent depth higher than baseline; T3/T4 NLL lower and accuracy higher; Stage3 Recall@10 >0.065 and above both ablations.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set μ_i=0 and g_i=0 in transport and auxiliary prediction, retaining Lorentz residual quantization and the same learned parameter count.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Log/Exp, parallel transport and d_κ with Euclidean residual subtraction, identity transport and Euclidean distance; retain the same p(j|i), η, g_i and behavior decoder.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the train-only successor barycenter, Lorentz transport and geodesic residual quantization as specified, under the fixed 3,000-epoch Stage2 and frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Let X_i=Exp_o(x_i), μ_i=Σ_j p(j|i)Log_o(X_j). At level l with anchor Q_i^l, R_i^l=Log_{Q_i^l}(X_i^{l residual}); R'_i=Exp_{Q_i^l}(R_i^l-η sigmoid(a_i)PT_{o→Q_i^l}(μ_i)); z_i^l=argmin_m d_κ(Exp_{Q_i^l}(R'_i),C_m^l)^2. Add L_beh=β sigmoid(a_i)(1-cos(Log_o(C_{z_i}^l),μ_i)); use straight-through hard z and standard RQ-VAE losses, then form the next residual using the baseline code subtraction in tangent coordinates. Apply at l=2,3; deterministic collision extension defines T4.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are encoder item vectors, parent code anchors, residual codebooks and cached train-only successor distributions/means. Outputs are T2/T3 hard tokens, reconstructed residual vectors, and RQ-VAE plus behavior prediction losses.
SAFEGUARDS: Use stable Lorentz log/exp and parallel transport implementations; project to future sheet, clamp acosh and norm denominators, cap η≤0.1 and gradient norms, normalize μ with ε and set it to zero for insufficient counts, clip sigmoid gates, use float32/64, deterministic tie breaks, and retain baseline collision extension. Monitor reconstruction and code occupancy to detect collapse.
END IDEA
