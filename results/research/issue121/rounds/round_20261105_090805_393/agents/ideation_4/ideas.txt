IDEA: parentcurv-rq-2026
TITLE: PARENTCURV-RQ - parent-conditioned, residual/occupancy/branching-scaled curvature field for the RQ semantic-ID quantizer
MOTIVATION: GEOCAP-RQ fixed level-1 separation but collapsed level 3 (l3_collision 0.549 > 0.35) because the size of a capacity cell was a function of the item's own demand/popularity, so a few crowded high-demand cells swallowed the deep codewords and the level-3 residual was never split. What is needed is a geometric quantity that decides how finely a deep cell is cut from the residual that is actually still unquantized inside that cell, and from how diversely its residents behave - not from how popular they are.
NOVELTY: Not an additive per-codeword radial offset on a fixed metric (GEOCAP), not a two-leg parallel transport between charts (holonomy arm), not a standalone hyperbolic auxiliary loss. Curvature itself is the conditioning variable: a measured (not trained) scalar kappa_hat per (level, parent cell) drives the Poincare exponential map of BOTH the residual and the codeword of that level, so the same codeword sits at a different hyperbolic radius depending on which parent cell the item came from, and the residual/codeword comparison becomes direction-dominated exactly where that parent's level-l residual is rich, branchy and crowded. Level 1 is provably run at unit curvature in level-RMS units, so the mechanism has no free curvature constant to tune.
CHANGE: Each level's residual is normalised by the measured level RMS and quantised in a Poincare ball of curvature kappa_hat_{l,m} in [0.25,4], where m is

the item's parent codeword at level l-1 and kappa_hat is the clipped geometric mean of three measured ratios (residual energy of that parent, successor-branching entropy of that parent, occupancy of that parent); the code index is the geodesic argmin and the commitment term is the squared geodesic distance, while the straight-through reconstruction path is byte-identical to the baseline.
SPECIFICATION: Stage-2 only. Levels l=1..3, codebook E_l in R^{K_l x d}, r_i^(1)=encoder(h_i). Detached bias-corrected EMA (decay 0.99) after each optimizer step: S_l = E||r_i^(l)||^2; per parent code m of level l-1: n_{l,m}, S_{l,m}=E[||r^(l)||^2 | parent=m], h_{l,m}=successor branching entropy; hbar_l, nbar_l = means over occupied parents. Per item and level: u_i = r_i^(l)/(sqrt(S_l)+1e-8); kappa_hat_{l,m} = clip( (S_{l,m}/S_l)^0.5 * ((h_{l,m}+1e-3)/(hbar_l+1e-3))^0.5 * ((n_{l,m}+1)/(nbar_l+1))^0.5, 0.25, 4 ). rad_c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) * v / (sqrt(c)||v||) (v itself if c<1e-6); ztilde_i = rad_c(u_i); ctilde_{l,k} = rad_c(E_l[k]/(sqrt(S_l)+1e-8)); delta_ik = arcosh(1 + 2c||ztilde_i (-) ctilde_k||^2 / ((1-c|ztilde_i|^2)(1-c|ctilde_k|^2))); k_i = argmin_k delta_ik. Commitment term at level l becomes lambda_commit * delta_{i,k_i}^2 (replaces the Euclidean commitment, same lambda_commit from the baseline config). Reconstruction untouched: r_q^(l)=E_l[k_i]; r^(l+1) = r^(l) + (r_q^(l) - r^(l)).detach(); x_q = h + (sum_l r_q^(l) - h).detach() - the STE line is preserved verbatim. Parent m for level 1 is the global code 0, so n=nbar, S=S_1, h=hbar_1 and kappa_hat_{1,0}=1 exactly.
COST: One extra BxK geodesic distance per level (B=1024, K_l=256, d=32), implemented in the Lorentz model as one BxK GEMM per level, so about +3% to +8% step time and no extra forward pass for any statistic (all EMAs are accumulated from the batches already being trained). At the frozen scale (24556 training items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs = 72k steps) that is roughly +0.1 to +0.3 GPU-hour per card, memory +O(B*K) per level for the distance matrix (about 1 MB per level at B=1024, K=256 in fp32). No change to the number of epochs, no early stopping.
HYPOTHESIS: If curvature conditioned on the parent's own residual/occupancy/branching is the right second-order fix, then at level 3 the curvature field is non-degenerate (IQR of kappa_hat_{3,m} over occupied parents > 1.5x) and the crowded/rich parents get kappa_hat close to 4, their level-3 assignment becomes direction-dominated and spreads over materially more distinct codewords, so l3_collision falls below the 0.35 Stage-3 gate while level-1 separation does not regress to baseline (valid_l1_hard_er stays below ~8, valid_l1_positive_hit_rate >= 0.11), and Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065 (baseline 0.0555, prior best 0.0638). It is falsified in one run if l3_collision stays >= 0.35, or level-1 hard ER returns to ~16, or test_R@10 <= 0.065.
RISKS: ["Level 1 is also curved (kappa_hat=1 by construction), so the first-order separation that GEOCAP reached through radial offsets may not be reproduced and l1_hard_er may drift back toward the baseline 16.39.", "The curved commitment term scales gradients by d(delta^2)/dE, which grows near the ball boundary; without the standardisation safeguard it can destabilise the codebook early in training.", "The EMA statistics of S_{l,m}, n_{l,m} and h_{l,m} lag the codebook, so kappa_hat can oscillate between epochs and make deep assignments flip.", "Successor branching entropy is a coarse proxy

for residual diversity; if branchy parents are not the residual-rich ones the behaviour factor adds noise instead of sharpening level 3.", "If the level-3 residual is genuinely low-dimensional, no curvature can spread the collisions and the collision gate fails for a reason the mechanism cannot address.", "Early in training S_l is still tiny, so the Euclidean degeneracy branch and the clip lower bound may dominate for the first few hundred epochs and lock the deep levels into one geometry."]
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-parent-code successor branching entropy of the directed item-transition graph (a behaviour-conditioned per-code measure of how diversely the residents of a parent semantic cell are consumed next), together with the parent's occupancy.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only: for every training user sequence and every consecutive pair (v_t, v_{t+1}) with v_t != v_{t+1}, increment N[v_t, v_{t+1}] once per user (deduplicated per user so heavy users do not dominate); sparse CSR over the 24556 training items. From the current (detached) assignment snapshot, residents I_{l,m} = items whose level-(l-1) code is m; successor mass s_{m,j} = sum_{i in I} N[i,j]; p_{m,j} = s_{m,j}/sum_j s_{m,j}; aggregate successors into level-1 prefix classes c: q_{m,c} = sum_{j: l1code(j)=c} p_{m,j}; h_{l,m} = H(q)/log K_1 in [0,1]; hbar_l = mean over occupied parents; n_{l,m} = |I_{l,m}|. All three are EMAs updated inside the normal training batches (the sample already yields both the item's code and its next item, so no extra pass).
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Transitions, occupancies and branching entropies are built exclusively from training-split sequences and from item embeddings learned on the training split; the assignment snapshot is computed on training items only. No validation or test interaction is ever read while constructing the codes, so a validation/test item's identity, popularity or neighbours cannot influence its own or any other item's SID; the validation set is used only for the recorded diagnostics and the test set only for the final Stage-3 recall.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball exponential map at the origin, rad_c(v) = tanh(sqrt(c)||v||) v/(sqrt(c)||v||), with curvature -c, c = kappa_hat in [0.25,4] measured per (level, parent cell) in level-RMS units, plus the Mobius difference and the arcosh geodesic distance in the same ball of curvature c (optionally evaluated in the equivalent Lorentz model, d = arcosh(-c <z,c>_L)/sqrt(c), which is the same number).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ quantisation of Stage-2 training at every one of the three levels - both in the assignment (geodesic argmin used to emit the semantic-ID digit) and in the commitment term that produces the gradient on the codebook and (through the assignment) on the encoder. It is not in the decoder and it is not a separate auxiliary loss.
COUPLING PATH: training-only successor-branching entropy h_{l,m} -> kappa_hat_{l,m} -> curvature of the ball in which the level-l residual and the level-l codeword are both exp-mapped -> geodesic argmin that emits code k_i and geodesic commitment gradient -> codebook/encoder update -> next epoch's parent occupancy and residual statistics. Switch the behaviour factor off and kappa_hat loses its dependence on h; switch curvature off and the same pathway becomes the Euclidean nearest-neighbour + squared-error commitment. Either change alters k_i, the gradient on E_l and hence the emitted SIDs, so the coupling is the core com

putation and not an add-on.
COUPLING EQUATION: kappa_hat_{l,m} = clip( (S_{l,m}/S_l)^{1/2} * ((h_{l,m}+eps_h)/(hbar_l+eps_h))^{1/2} * ((n_{l,m}+1)/(nbar_l+1))^{1/2}, 1/4, 4 ), and k_i^{(l)} = argmin_k arcosh( 1 + 2 kappa_hat_{l,m} || ztilde_i (-)_{kappa_hat} ctilde_{l,k} ||^2 / ((1 - kappa_hat |ztilde_i|^2)(1 - kappa_hat |ctilde_{l,k}|^2)) ) with ztilde_i = Exp_0^{kappa_hat}( r_i^{(l)} / sqrt(S_l) ), ctilde_{l,k} = Exp_0^{kappa_hat}( E_l[k] / sqrt(S_l) ). Here h_{l,m}/hbar_l is the behaviour term and kappa_hat is the curved term; the behaviour term acts only through the curvature of the geodesic distance, and the geodesic distance acts only through the behaviour-conditioned curvature.
OBSERVABLES: (a) the distribution of kappa_hat_{3,m} over occupied parent cells (report median, IQR, mass at the clip bounds) - it must vary, otherwise the coupling is inert; (b) rounded l3_collision must fall below 0.35 (GEOCAP 0.549) while level-2 distinct prefixes stay near or above 4236 and l3_refinement_gain_bits stays near or above 2.82; (c) valid_l1_hard_er must stay far below the baseline 16.390 and valid_l1_positive_hit_rate at or above 0.11 so the first-order separation is not lost; (d) usage entropy and the number of used codewords per level; (e) Stage-3 test_recall@10 must exceed 0.065 versus baseline 0.0555 and prior best 0.0638. All diagnostics are recorded, and only (e) is the judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: `use_branching=True` -> False sets h_{l,m} := hbar_l for every parent, so the middle factor is identically 1 and kappa_hat^{beh-off}_{l,m} = clip((S_{l,m}/S_l)^{1/2}((n_{l,m}+1)/(nbar_l+1))^{1/2}, 0.25, 4); the geodesic machinery is unchanged. This arm exists in the code and is never trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: `curvature_mode='euclidean'` forces kappa_hat := 1e-6 for every (l,m), so rad_c(v)=v, ctilde/z-tilde are the plain scaled vectors, the arcosh branch is replaced by delta = 2||u - E_l[k]/sqrt(S_l)||, and the commitment term is the ordinary squared Euclidean distance: the same code, the same STE, the same statistics, only flat. This arm exists in the code and is never trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: `curvature_mode='poincare'` and `use_branching=True`: kappa_hat_{l,m} = clip((S_{l,m}/S_l)^{1/2} ((h_{l,m}+eps_h)/(hbar_l+eps_h))^{1/2} ((n_{l,m}+1)/(nbar_l+1))^{1/2}, 0.25, 4) with the geodesic argmin and the geodesic commitment term. This is the single official run of the round, trained once on the frozen 3000-epoch Stage-2 budget and the frozen Stage-3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: "u = r^(l) / (sqrt(S_l) + 1e-8)  # S_l = EMA of E||r^(l)||^2
kappa_hat = clip( (S_{l,m}/S_l)**0.5 * ((h_{l,m}+1e-3)/(hbar_l+1e-3))**0.5 * ((n_{l,m}+1)/(nbar_l+1))**0.5, 0.25, 4.0 )
def rad(c, v):  n = norm(v);  return v if c < 1e-6 else tanh(sqrt(c)*n)/(sqrt(c)*max(n,1e-8)) * v
zt = rad(kappa_hat, u);  ct = rad(kappa_hat, E_l / (sqrt(S_l)+1e-8))
num = 2*kappa_hat*sqnorm(mobius_sub(zt, ct, kappa_hat))
den = (1 - kappa_hat*sqnorm(zt)) * (1 - kappa_hat*sqnorm(ct))
delta = arcosh(clamp(1 + num/den, min=1+1e-7));  k_i = argmin_k delta[k]
L_commit_l = lambda_commit * delta[k_i]**2        # replaces the Euclidean commitment, same lambda_commit
r_q = E_l[k_i];  r_next = r + (r_q - r).detach()  # STE preserved verbatim
after step: EMA-update S_l, S_{l,m}, n_{l,m}, h_{l,m} with decay 0.99, bias corrected"
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder embeddings h_i (B x d), the three codebooks E_l (K_l x d, K_l = 256 as in the baseline), the detached

parent code m(i) from level l-1, the detached EMA statistics S_l, S_{l,m}, n_{l,m}, h_{l,m}, and the frozen training-only transition matrix N (CSR, 24556 x 24556). Outputs: the level-l code index k_i^(l) for every item (i.e. the three semantic-ID digits), the straight-through quantised residuals r_q^(l) feeding the unchanged reconstruction path, the curved commitment losses L_commit_l and their gradients on E_l and on the encoder, and the refreshed detached EMAs used by the next step.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp the arcosh argument to >= 1+1e-7 and the output to 1e4; clamp the codebook loss per element (gradient clip 1.0) and standardise delta^2 by its detached batch median before weighting so the curved term has the same scale as the Euclidean one at every epoch; use max(||v||,1e-8) in rad_c and branch to the identity when kappa_hat < 1e-6 (Euclidean limit) so no 0/0 appears; recompute (1 - c|z|^2) with float64-free but clamped arguments at >= 1e-6, since |rad_c(v)| < 1/sqrt(c) strictly; require |ztilde| <= (1-1e-6)/sqrt(kappa_hat) after the map and rescale if violated; EMPTY cells (n_{l,m}=0) inherit kappa_hat = 1 instead of dividing; Laplace smoothing 1e-3 on h; bias-corrected EMAs and a detached copy of all statistics so no gradient flows through the curvature; drop the h factor at the very first epoch (hbar_l undefined) and use kappa_hat=1 there; gradient-norm watchdog that falls back to Euclidean only if a NaN/Inf appears (a numeric guard, not an ablation arm).
END IDEA
