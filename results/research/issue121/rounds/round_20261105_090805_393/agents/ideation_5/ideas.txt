IDEA: OCCURVE-RQ
TITLE: Occupancy-inverse hyperbolic cell elasticity: behaviour-weighted, level-specific cell radii from the hyperbolic area law replace popularity-sized capacity cells
MOTIVATION: GEOCAP-RQ sized each level-l codeword cell by item popularity (demand -> bigger cell). That is the wrong sign for rate-distortion: a quantizer must shrink cells where the residual density is high. At level 1 the residual mass is spread, so GEOCAP accidentally helped (valid_l1_hard_er 3.885 vs 16.390 baseline, l1 positive hit 0.1222 vs 0.0794). At level 3 the residual mass concentrates in a few cells, GEOCAP inflates exactly those cells, and the deep code degenerates: l3_collision 0.549 broke the 35% Stage-3 gate, so Stage-3 recall was never measured. The recommender problem is therefore: keep the level-1 separation gain while making deep SID prefixes finer where the level's own residual occupancy is high, so that SID prefixes stay behaviour-discriminative instead of collapsing to a few hub blobs.
NOVELTY: Distinct from GEOCAP-RQ (popularity-sized, positive feedback, per-codeword offset added to a Poincare distance), from the holonomy two-leg transport (cross-chart, rejected), and from BRad-RQ (behaviour-rank on a radial coordinate). Here there is no popularity term at all and no additive offset: each codeword k at level l receives a geodesic cell RADIUS that is an inverse function of that level's behaviour-weighted occupancy, and the radius is computed by INVERTING the hyperbolic area law of the SAME curvature that defines the assignment distance. The curvature is trainable and appears three times in one chain (area law, exp/log chart map, geodesic distance); the exponent is derived from the area law instead of being a tuned power; and the occupancy feedback is negative (homeostatic), the opposite sign of everything tried so far.
CHANGE: Each level-l codeword gets rho_{l,k} = R_ref(c_l) * g_{c_l}(m_l/(m_l+N_{l,k})) where N_{l,k} is that level's behaviour-weighted hard-assignment mass from the previous epoch; the assignment cost is the Poincare distance divided by rho_{l,k}, and the reconstructed residual is log_0 of the SAME codeword position, so STE, the chart and the codeword positions are untouched.
SPECIFICATION: Precompute once from Stage-2 TRAIN sequences only: successor entropy H_i for every item, btilde_i = clip(H_i/mean(H) - 1, -1, 3) (btilde_i = 0 for items with < 5 training transitions). Buffers per level l in {1,2,3}: trainable kappa_l (init 0, c_l = exp(kappa_l) clamped to [1e-3, 8]), trainable tangent codebook T_l in R^{K x d} (init N(0, 10^-2)), stop-grad buffers N_l in R^K (init uniform), s_l scalar (init 1, EMA of RMS level-l residual norm, stop-grad). Per step (batch of item encoder vectors z): for l=1..3: v = exp_0^{c_l}(r/s_l); C = exp_0^{c_l}(T_l); cost_{i,k} = d_P^{c_l}(v_i, C_k)/rho_{l,k}; chat = argmin_k cost; ehat = s_l * log_0^{c_l}(C_{chat}); r = r - ehat; x_q += ehat. x_q = z + (x_q - z).detach() (single STE, unchanged). Loss unchanged (reconstruction + codebook/commitment). Once per epoch, after the epoch's last step, one no-grad forward over all 24556 train items recomputes chat_l(i) and N_{l,k} = sum_{i: chat_l(i)=k} clip(1+btilde_i, 0.05, 4) and s_l. No extra losses, no tuned weights, no curvature term beside a behaviour term: only the one cost equation.
COST: +4 to +6 percent wall clock at the frozen scale (24556 train items, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs). Per step: batch about 1023 items, 3 levels, K codewords per level, width d: geodesic pair cost is about 10 flops per (item, codeword) pair, so B*K*L ~ 7.9e5 pairs ~ 8 MFLOP, cheaper than the baseline's B*K*L*d ~ 25 MFLOP Euclidean distance term; the exp_0/log_0 chart maps add B*L*d ~ 1e5 flops. The only real overhead is one extra no-grad encoder forward pass over 24556 items per epoch, about 1/24 of a training epoch (about 4 percent). Behaviour preprocessing is a single sparse pass over the train transition counts (seconds, once). Memory: +3*K*d floats for T_l, +3*K floats for N_l, 24556 floats for btilde.
HYPOTHESIS: If the inverse-occupancy hyperbolic radius is the right capacity rule, then in the single run: (i) l3_collision drops below the 0.35 Stage-3 gate while l3_refinement_gain_bits stays >= 2.8, (ii) level-1 separation does not regress (valid_l1_hard_er stays near 4, i.e. well below the 16.39 baseline, and valid_l1_positive_hit_rate >= 0.12), and (iii) Stage3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065 (beating the 0.0555 baseline and the 0.0638 historical best). If l3_collision stays above 0.35 or l1 ER returns to baseline magnitude, the mechanism is falsified.
RISKS: Inverse-occupancy radii may over-sharpen level-1 cells for very popular codes and push level-1 ER back up; the floor g>=0.05 and the negative feedback are the only guards, and they are not tuned.; The occupancy buffer is frozen per epoch, so the homeostatic loop can oscillate between epochs (code shuts, refills, shuts); EMA with a fixed documented default 0.5 is the only damping and is not swept.; Cold or rare items have degenerate successor entropy; the neutral fallback (btilde = 0 below 5 transitions) may wash out the behaviour signal exactly where SIDs are hardest.; Trainable curvature can drift to the lower clamp c -> 1e-3, which makes the run Euclidean in disguise and removes the geometric part of the coupling; the run has no geometric-off arm, so this can only be seen in the logged c_l trajectory, not repaired.; A single run and a single seed mean the l3_collision/recall margins must be large enough to survive seed noise; a marginal pass is not evidence.; Recomputing N_l every epoch depends on the previous epoch's hard assignments, so epoch 0 uses uniform radii; early codebook specialisation can lock in an unlucky partition.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only user-interest branching: the Shannon entropy H_i of item i's directed successor distribution over training sequences, used as a per-item weight that is aggregated into a per-code, per-level occupancy mass.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 TRAIN split only: scan each training sequence with a forward window of 3 and count directed transitions (i -> j), giving a sparse row-normalised matrix Q (24556 x 24556, one sparse pass, done once before training, frozen into a buffer). H_i = -sum_j Q_ij log Q_ij, normalised so mean(H) = 1 over warm items, btilde_i = clip(H_i/mean(H) - 1, -1, 3); items with fewer than 5 outgoing training transitions get btilde_i = 0. btilde is a frozen 24556-vector read by the epoch-level occupancy update N_{l,k} = sum over train items assigned to (l,k) of clip(1+btilde_i, 0.05, 4).
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Q and btilde are built once, before any training, from the Stage-2 train split's sequences only. Validation and test sessions are never tokenised into the scan, the frozen buffer is never recomputed from any other split, and N_l and s_l are recomputed only from the train items' own forward passes. Therefore no validation or test interaction can influence a radius, a codeword position, a curvature, or a SID; the Stage-3 evaluation reads the finished SIDs only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball model with trainable per-level curvature c_l = exp(kappa_l) in [1e-3, 8] (init c_l = 1): Riemannian distance d_P^c(u,v) = (1/sqrt(c)) * arcosh(1 + 2c||u-v||^2 / ((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||v||^2))), exponential map exp_0^c(t) = tanh(sqrt(c)||t||)/(sqrt(c)||t||) * t, logarithmic map log_0^c(p) = artanh(sqrt(c)||p||)/(sqrt(c)||p||) * p, and the hyperbolic ball-area law V_c(rho) proportional to (cosh(sqrt(c) rho) - 1)/c which is inverted to turn a volume fraction into a radius.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In the quantizer only, at every residual-quantization level l = 1,2,3: the residual is mapped into the ball with exp_0^{c_l}, the codebook lives in the ball (tangent-space parameterisation T_l -> exp_0^{c_l}(T_l)), the assignment cost is the geodesic distance divided by an area-law radius of the same curvature, and the chosen codeword is decoded back with log_0^{c_l} with the same c_l. Nothing else in the model is curved.
COUPLING PATH: One chain, no side terms: item training-transition entropy btilde -> per-code behaviour-weighted occupancy N_{l,k}(btilde) -> volume fraction p_{l,k} = m_l/(m_l + N_{l,k}) -> hyperbolic area-law inversion with curvature c_l -> cell radius rho_{l,k} -> denominator of the geodesic assignment cost d_P^{c_l}(v, C_k)/rho_{l,k} -> hard code chat_l -> decoded residual and codeword update. Removing btilde collapses N_{l,k} to a plain count and therefore changes p and rho and the partition; removing c_l (c -> 0) changes the same radius through the Euclidean power law and the distance through d_E, and the partition again.
COUPLING EQUATION: cost_{i,k}^{(l)} = d_P^{c_l}(exp_0^{c_l}(r_i^{(l)}/s_l), exp_0^{c_l}(T_{l,k})) / [ R_ref(c_l) * g_{c_l}( m_l / (m_l + N_{l,k}(btilde)) ) ], with N_{l,k}(btilde) = sum_{i': chat_l(i')=k} clip(1 + btilde_{i'}, 0.05, 4), m_l = (1/K) sum_k N_{l,k}, R_ref(c_l) = (1/sqrt(c_l)) artanh(0.95 sqrt(c_l)), g_c(p) = arcosh(1 + p(cosh(sqrt(c) R_ref) - 1)) / (sqrt(c) R_ref). The curved term d_P^{c_l} and the behaviour term btilde meet inside the single denominator rho_{l,k}.
OBSERVABLES: Level-wise SID diagnostics logged every epoch and at the end: level-1 hard ER, level-1 positive hit rate, level-2 distinct prefixes, l3_refinement_gain_bits, l3_collision (the Stage-3 admission gate), prefix-behaviour predictability (accuracy of a frozen small probe predicting the level-3 code from the level-1..2 prefix plus the behaviour context vector), and the logged trajectories of c_l and of the entropy of rho_{l,k} across codes per level; finally Stage3 test_recall@10, the only judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: btilde identically 0 in the occupancy update, so N_{l,k} is a plain assignment count and rho_{l,k} depends only on geometry and occupancy; code exists and is exercised by unit checks, but no such arm is trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c_l -> 0 in the same equation: cost becomes Euclidean ||r/s_l - T_{l,k}|| / rho^E_{l,k} with the Euclidean volume law rho^E_{l,k} = R_ref^E * p_{l,k}^{1/d} and R_ref^E = s_l, so the same behaviour-weighted occupancy drives the same kind of radius with no curvature; code exists, not trained.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The single trained run: behaviour-weighted occupancy inside the hyperbolic area-law radius, the geodesic assignment, the same codeword positions for assignment and reconstruction, and the unchanged straight-through estimator x_q = z + (x_q - z).detach().
SPEC EQUATIONS: exp_0^c(t) = tanh(sqrt(c)||t||)/(sqrt(c)||t||) * t (limit t as ||t||->0); log_0^c(p) = artanh(sqrt(c)||p||)/(sqrt(c)||p||) * p; d_P^c(u,v) = (1/sqrt(c)) arcosh(1 + 2c||u-v||^2 / max((1-c||u||^2)(1-c||v||^2), 1e-9)); C_l = exp_0^{c_l}(T_l); v_l = exp_0^{c_l}(r_l/s_l); p_{l,k} = m_l/(m_l + N_{l,k}); g_c(p) = arcosh(1 + p(cosh(artanh(0.95)) - 1)) / artanh(0.95) with sqrt(c_l) R_ref(c_l) = artanh(0.95) = 1.83178; rho_{l,k} = R_ref(c_l) * clip(g_{c_l}(p_{l,k}), 0.05, 0.98); cost_{i,k} = d_P^{c_l}(v_{l,i}, C_{l,k}) / rho_{l,k}; chat_{l,i} = argmin_k cost_{i,k}; ehat_{l,i} = s_l * log_0^{c_l}(C_{l,chat}); r_{l+1,i} = r_{l,i} - ehat_{l,i}; x_q = sum_l ehat_l; x_q = z + (x_q - z).detach(); per-epoch update (no grad): N_{l,k} = sum_i clip(1+btilde_i,0.05,4) * 1[chat_{l,i}=k], s_l <- 0.5 s_l + 0.5 RMS(r_l). Parameter signature: per level l: kappa_l (1 scalar), T_l (K x d), frozen N_l (K), frozen s_l (1); shared frozen btilde (24556); the recursion is identical for l = 1,2,3 with d and K unchanged from the baseline RQ-VAE.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder latent z (batch x d) for each train/eval item; frozen btilde (24556); the per-level state (kappa_l, T_l, N_l, s_l). Outputs: per-item hard code tuple (chat_1, chat_2, chat_3) that becomes the Semantic ID, the quantized vector x_q used by the generative model's decoder, and per-epoch statistics (N_l, s_l, c_l, entropy of rho_{l,k}).
SAFEGUARDS: (1) Straight-through estimator kept exactly as x_q = z + (x_q - z).detach(), applied once, so the encoder cannot collapse. (2) Codebook parameterised in the tangent space at the origin and clamped, ||T_{l,k}|| <= 6/sqrt(c_l), and c_l clamped to [1e-3, 8] through kappa_l, so no point leaves the ball and no artanh diverges; Euclidean norms c||p||^2 are kept below 1 by the 0.95 boundary fraction. (3) arcosh argument clamped to >= 1 + 1e-7 and the Poincare denominator clamped to >= 1e-9 against NaN/Inf. (4) Radius clipped to [0.05, 0.98] * R_ref so no cell is zero or degenerate, and empty codes legitimately get the maximum radius and refill (self-healing, negative feedback). (5) Laplace-style floor on the occupancy weight, clip(1+btilde, 0.05, 4), keeps weights strictly positive and bounded, and items with fewer than 5 training transitions get btilde = 0. (6) N_l and s_l are stop-gradient buffers rebuilt once per epoch from train items only, with the documented default EMA 0.5 damping s_l; epoch 0 uses uniform N_l and p = 0.5. (7) Assignment and reconstruction use the identical codeword position C_l = exp_0^{c_l}(T_l) and the identical c_l, so no cross-chart comparison is possible. (8) Loss, optimiser, schedule, epoch budget (3000) and the Stage-3 protocol are unchanged; no auxiliary loss, no coupling weight to tune.
END IDEA
