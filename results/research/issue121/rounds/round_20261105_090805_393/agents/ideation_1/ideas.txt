IDEA: OCCURV-FIELD-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour reach per occupant becomes each codeword's own negative curvature, on a residual-renormalised Poincare chart, so deep-level codes spread instead of collapsing.
MOTIVATION: The previous capacity-cell mechanism (GEOCAP-RQ) won at level 1 (valid_l1_hard_er 3.885 vs 16.390, positive_hit_rate 0.1222 vs 0.0794) but its cell sizes were driven by item popularity, so at level 3 a few high-demand codewords absorbed most items (l3_collision 0.549 > 0.35 gate) and Stage 3 never ran. The real problem is that the quantity that decides how much room a codeword gets must respond to that level's residual occupancy and to behaviour, not to item popularity: a codeword should only be allowed to keep a large cell if the items it holds actually carry behavioural reach beyond their share.
NOVELTY: The curvature is not a global constant, not a per-item radius and not a transport between charts: it is a per-(level,codeword) NEGATIVE CURVATURE FIELD produced by a closed-loop, occupancy-normalised behavioural statistic (behavioural reach per occupant divided by the level mean), and every codeword is scored with its own curvature inside one shared coordinate chart (x = exp_0(z), c = exp_0(e), same tangent system, nothing is moved to another chart). The curvature enters assignment, the commitment gradient and the codebook pull simultaneously, and it is detached (a measured control variable), so behaviour shapes the geometry rather than being added as a side loss. It differs from GEOCAP (popularity-sized radii, one curvature), from BRad-RQ (behaviour-scaled radius) and from the rejected holonomy arm (cross-chart transport).
CHANGE: Each level's residual is renormalised by its own measured RMS to a fixed shell of the unit Poincare ball (giving level 3 the same capacity as level 1), and each codeword k at level l is scored with the Poincare distance of curvature -kappa_{l,k}, where kappa_{l,k} is raised when the codeword's behavioural reach per occupant falls below its level mean, which inflates that codeword's distances and makes the hoarding codewords shed items at every level, deepest included.
SPECIFICATION: L=3 levels, K=256, width d=32. r_1=h (encoder), r_{l+1}=r_l-q_l. Measured scale s_l = EMA of RMS(r_l) (detached, train split). z_l=(c*/s_l)*r_l with c*=0.9, clamped ||z||<=c*. x_l=exp_0(z_l)=tanh(||z||)z/||z||. Codewords e_{l,k} in R^d (trainable), c_{l,k}=exp_0(e_{l,k}). Distance D_{l,k}=(1/sqrt(kappa_{l,k}))*acosh(1+2*kappa*||x-c||^2/((1-kappa||x||^2)(1-kappa||c||^2))). kappa_max=0.9/tanh^2(c*)=1.754; kappa_{l,k}=(kappa_max/sqrt(e))*clip(rho_{l,k}^{-1}, e^{-1/2}, e^{1/2}), so kappa in [0.6453,1.754], kappa0=kappa_max/sqrt(e)=1.0638. rho_{l,k}=phi_{l,k}/mean_k(phi_{l,k}), phi_{l,k}=mean over items assigned to (l,k) of b_i/pop_i, b_i=sum_j C_ij/sqrt(pop_i*pop_j) from TRAIN transitions. Assignment k*=argmin_k D_{l,k}; decode r_hat_l=(s_l/c*)*log_0(c

_{l,k*})=(s_l/c*)*e_{l,k*}; STE x_q=x+(x_q-x).detach(). Loss = frozen reconstruction + codebook loss + frozen commitment coefficient times sum_l D_{l,k*}(r_l)^2.
COST: Relative to the frozen Stage-2 baseline: +3 to +5% wall clock, no extra epoch, no extra forward pass. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, 24 steps/epoch => about 1023 items/step, 3000 epochs): 3*1023*256 = 786k curvature-specific distance evaluations (each about 20 flops, incl. one acosh), 768 curvature table entries recomputed once per step (detached), and one scatter-add of 3*1023 hard assignments into occ/phi accumulators; roughly 15-20 MFLOP/step on top of the encoder/decoder. Memory +768+3 floats. One-off transition table build: O(#train transitions) over a window of 5, minutes on CPU, done once before epoch 0.
HYPOTHESIS: If equality of level capacity comes from residual renormalisation and anti-hoarding comes from the per-codeword curvature field, then level 1 keeps its GEOCAP-level separation (valid_l1_hard_er well below the 16.390 baseline, positive_hit_rate near 0.12) while level 3 spreads (l3_collision below 0.35, level-3 assignment entropy and distinct triples up), and Stage 3 test_recall@10 exceeds 0.065; the mechanism is worth keeping if the single full run shows both the non-regressed level-1 numbers and the level-3 spread together, since either alone was already seen without a recall gain.
RISKS: Detached EMA curvature lags assignment drift and can oscillate (mitigated by the narrow bounded range and the epoch-horizon EMA); cross-curvature comparison is scale-biased so high-kappa codewords can be starved permanently (bounded by kappa_max and by log-clipping rho); s_l for level 3 may be tiny, so renormalisation amplifies noise and saturates the clamp (numeric floor s_l >= 0.01*s_1); sparse items (pop=1) make b_i/pop_i noisy; acosh argument can overflow or fall below 1; the extra curvature term in the commitment loss could dominate the reconstruction term if the frozen coefficient is large.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted directed transition reach of an item, consumed per (level, codeword) as behavioural reach per occupant relative to the level mean.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From TRAIN split sequences only (user histories ordered by time). For each position t and each j in (t, t+5] of the same user history, increment C_{u_t,u_j}; pop_i = training occurrence count (floored at 1). b_i = sum_j C_ij / sqrt(pop_i*pop_j) (popularity-adjusted reach of item i to its successors). Everything is a static table built once before Stage 2 and read-only during training; no validation or test interaction is ever read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: C, pop and b are built exclusively from Stage-2 training-split interactions before the first epoch and are frozen; the codeword statistics occ and phi are accumulators over training batches only; no validation or test item ever contributes a transition, an occupancy or a curvature value, and SID construction therefore cannot see held-out interactions. The curvature field is a within-training measured control variable, and the Stage-3 protocol sees only the frozen SIDs.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare ball of measured per-(level,codeword) negative curvature kappa_{l,k} in [kappa_max/e, kappa_max] with kappa_max = 0.9/tanh^2(0.9) = 1.754 (unit-ball coordinates, curvature -kappa_{l,k}), used through exp_0, log_0 and the curvature-scaled geodesic distance acosh(1 + 2k||x-c||^2/((1-k||x||^2)(1-k||c||^2)))/sqrt(k).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Insid

e the RQ quantiser, at every level: the residual is exp-mapped, the codeword is exp-mapped in the same chart, the assignment argmin uses the curvature-scaled geodesic distance, the decode uses log_0 of that same codeword point, and the commitment/codebook terms are the squared geodesic distances; curvature acts on assignment, on the codebook parameter update and on the encoder gradient.
COUPLING PATH: One pathway: train-split transitions -> b_i/pop_i -> per-codeword reach-per-occupant phi_{l,k} (with occupancy in the denominator) -> rho_{l,k} -> curvature kappa_{l,k} -> geodesic distance D_{l,k} -> argmin assignment k*, commitment loss gradient and codebook pull -> encoder and codeword positions next step. Removing behaviour sets rho=1 and loses the per-codeword curvature structure (all codewords identical curvature); removing curvature removes the metric in which rho is expressed, so the behaviour term no longer inflates any distance. Neither can be deleted without changing k*, the gradients and therefore the SIDs.
COUPLING EQUATION: kappa_{l,k} = (kappa_max/sqrt(e)) * clip( rho_{l,k}^{-1}, e^{-1/2}, e^{1/2} ),  rho_{l,k} = ( (1/occ_{l,k}) * sum_{i:a_{l,i}=k} b_i/pop_i ) / ( (1/K) * sum_{k'} (1/occ_{l,k'}) * sum_{i:a_{l,i}=k'} b_i/pop_i ),  D_{l,k}(r) = kappa_{l,k}^{-1/2} * acosh( 1 + 2*kappa_{l,k}*||x_l - c_{l,k}||^2 / ((1-kappa_{l,k}||x_l||^2)(1-kappa_{l,k}||c_{l,k}||^2)) ),  x_l = exp_0(z_l), z_l = (c*/s_l)*r_l, c_{l,k} = exp_0(e_{l,k}).
OBSERVABLES: (a) per-level assignment entropy and level-3 distinct prefixes / l3_collision (expect l3_collision far below 0.549 and below 0.35, distinct prefixes up from 4236); (b) the number of codewords whose kappa sits at the upper bound (the shedding set) and their occupancy over epochs; (c) level-1 diagnostics must stay at GEOCAP order (valid_l1_hard_er near 3.9, positive_hit_rate near 0.12), not baseline order; (d) gradient norm of the encoder through the geodesic commitment term should differ in sign structure per level (measured), and prefix behaviour predictability (given prefix, entropy of the next code) should fall; (e) the judgement: Stage3 test_recall@10 > 0.065 vs baseline 0.0555.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: b_i/pop_i is replaced by the constant 1, so rho_{l,k}=1 for every occupied codeword (empty codewords also get rho=1), kappa_{l,k}=kappa0=1.0638 uniformly, and the geometry remains fully hyperbolic with the same renormalisation and STE; this isolates the behaviour contribution and is coded by one switch BEHAV_OFF=True (not trained in this round).
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: exp_0/log_0 become identity and D becomes the Euclidean-weighted distance D^E_{l,k}=||z_l - e_{l,k}||*rho_{l,k}^{-1/2}, with c_{l,k}=e_{l,k}, the same STE and the same behaviour table; this isolates curvature and is coded by GEOM_OFF=True (not trained in this round).
ABLATION BOTH ON: Full mechanism of this block: renormalised residual shell + per-codeword curvature field from behaviour reach per occupant + Poincare geodesic assignment/commitment + unchanged STE. This is the single run this round performs.
SPEC EQUATIONS: 
# once, before epoch 0 (train split only)
pop_i = count of i in train; C_ij = window-5 directed co-occurrence counts
b_i = sum_j C_ij / sqrt(max(pop_i,1)*max(pop_j,1))
# per step, detached state
for l in 1..3:
    s_l <- (1-m)*s_l + m*sqrt(mean_i ||r_{l,i}||^2),  m = 1/24, init from batch 1, s_l >= 0.01*s_1
    z_{l,i} = (0.9/s_l)*r_{l,i}; z <- z*min(1, 0.9/||z||)
    x_{l,i} = tanh(||z||)*z/||z||

# exp_0
    e_{l,k} <- e_{l,k}*min(1, 0.9/||e_{l,k}||); c = tanh(||e||)*e/||e||   # exp_0
    # curvature field (detached, from previous step's accumulators)
    phi_{l,k} = (1/max(occ_{l,k},eps)) * sum_{i:a=k} b_i/max(pop_i,1)
    rho_{l,k} = phi_{l,k} / mean_k'(phi_{l,k'})
    kappa_{l,k} = (1.754/sqrt(e)) * clip(rho_{l,k}^{-1}, e^{-1/2}, e^{1/2})
    u2 = ||x - c||^2; ax = clamp(1-kappa*||x||^2, 0.05, 1); ac = clamp(1-kappa*||c||^2, 0.05, 1)
    D_{l,k} = (1/sqrt(kappa)) * acosh(max(1 + 2*kappa*u2/(ax*ac), 1+1e-6))
    k*_l = argmin_k D_{l,k}
    q_l = (s_l/0.9) * log_0(c_{l,k*_l}) = (s_l/0.9) * e_{l,k*}      # same point as the distance
    r_{l+1} = r_l - q_l
    occ_{l,k} <- EMA(EMA_horizon 1 epoch) of hard counts over (l,k); a_{l,i} = k*_l
# reconstruction, STE preserved
x_q = x + (Decode(q_1+q_2+q_3) - x).detach()
L = L_rec(x, x_q) + L_code + beta_frozen * sum_l D_{l,k*_l}(r_l)^2
L_code = sum_l [ D_{l,k*}(sg(r_l))^2 pull on c_{l,k*} + D_{l,k*}(sg(c_{l,k*}))^2 encoder commitment ]
# switches: BEHAV_OFF -> b_i/pop_i := 1 ; GEOM_OFF -> exp/log := identity, D := ||z-e||*rho^{-1/2}
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Input: per level the residual r_l (B x 32), codeword tangent table e_l (256 x 32), detached state {s_l, occ_l (256), phi_l (256)}, static behaviour table {b_i, pop_i} (24556), and switches (BEHAV_OFF, GEOM_OFF). Output: k*_l (B int), q_l (B x 32) as the same-chart decode of the assigned codeword, D_{l,k*} (B), the curvature vector kappa_l (256), the updated detached state, and the per-level prefix codes used to build SIDs. Pure function of its inputs at each step; the only cross-step state is the EMA state.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp ||z||<=0.9 and ||e||<=0.9 before exp_0 so kappa_max*tanh^2(0.9)=0.9 leaves a >=0.10 denominator margin; clamp both ball denominators to >=0.05; acosh argument floored at 1+1e-6 and capped at 1e6; safe norms sqrt(||.||^2+1e-8) to avoid 0/0 at the origin; s_l floored at 0.01*s_1 and capped at 10*s_1; pop_i floored at 1; occ floored at eps=1e-6 with empty codewords set to rho=1; kappa computed with detach() and clip so no gradient flows through a measured statistic and no rho can explode; kappa hard-bounded in [0.6453,1.754] so no distance can diverge; behaviour table built from the train split only and frozen before epoch 0; straight-through x_q = x + (x_q - x).detach() is never replaced, so encoder gradients never vanish through the quantiser; assignment and reconstruction read the same e_{l,k} parameter (distance point c=exp_0(e), decode log_0(c)=e), so the codebook cannot be optimised to two different positions.
END IDEA
