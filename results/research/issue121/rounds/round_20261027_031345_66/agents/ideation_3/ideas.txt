IDEA: curved_follow_margin
TITLE: Curved hard-negative margins on first-token assignment
MOTIVATION: Prefix regions should capture items that genuinely co-occur in users' training histories while excluding embedding-near items that do not co-occur; positive-only cohesion cannot enforce that distinction.
NOVELTY: Train a sparse assignment-level ranking objective over fixed training co-consumption positives and their co-consumption-near nonpositive hard negatives. Unlike behavior-conditioned distance offsets or a separate embedding loss, the Poincare distances between the candidate's actual first-level code assignment and positive/negative item representatives define the ranking gradient and can change the emitted first token.
CHANGE: At each sampled co-consumption edge, compare the item's assigned first-level code's curved distance to a positive-context representative against its distance to a hard negative representative; add a scale-matched margin to the assignment objective, while retaining the fixed four-token alphabet.
SPECIFICATION: From train-only user-item histories, build sparse symmetric cosine co-consumption rows s_ij. For each anchor i sample positive j with s_ij>0 and negative n among top-20 s_in neighbors with no co-consumption edge (exclude known positives). Let z_i be encoder output and a_{i1} its hard/straight-through first-level quantized point. Let h_j=exp_0^c(W z_j), with c=0.1 fixed. Define L_i=softplus((d_c(a_{i1},h_j)^2-d_c(a_{i1},h_n)^2+m)/tau); minimize L_RQVAE + lambda L_i. Use a detached per-batch median of baseline reconstruction scale to initialize lambda so lambda*L_i starts at about 1e-4; fix lambda thereafter. Sample at most one (j,n) pair per anchor in each step.
COST: Preprocessing O(nnz co-consumption) once; cache top-20 sparse neighbors. Per step with B anchors, one sampled pair each, and embedding width D: O(BD) extra maps/distances, versus O(BKD) standard quantizer scoring; at frozen scale 24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs = 72,000 steps and at most 72,000*B pair comparisons (one positive and one negative per anchor). Expected small training overhead; no change in codebook size or export.
HYPOTHESIS: Hard negatives expose false neighborhood attraction and the curved margin improves prefix-level discrimination rather than merely increasing positive similarity. Keep only if both-on beats each control and Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065; report the required metrics, especially positive-minus-negative gap and gold-prefix survival.
RISKS: Co-consumption zeros can be unobserved positives; top neighbors may be popularity artifacts; ranking gradients through discrete assignments may be weak; margin may distort reconstruction or concentrate first-token occupancy.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only item-item co-consumption cosine, used as positive evidence and to mine hard negatives.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: For each training user, form binary item sets from that user's training interactions; accumulate item co-occurrence counts, normalize each sparse row to cosine similarity, and freeze. For each anchor, choose a positive from observed co-consumers and a hard negative from its top-20 cosine neighbors excluding observed edges and self.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct counts solely from the Stage2 training split, freeze before optimization, and never consult validation/test sequences or future interactions; held-out recommendation evaluation is downstream only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Squared Poincare geodesic distance d_c at fixed sectional curvature -c=-0.1, using exp_0 and Mobius subtraction.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Pairwise ranking score from encoder item points to the actual first-level assigned code point during Stage2 training; the ranking gradient updates encoder and first-level codebook.
COUPLING PATH: Train co-consumption rows select the positive and hard negative; their item representations and the item's assigned code are compared by Poincare distances; the resulting ranking gradient changes first-token assignment and codebook.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_i=softplus((d_{-0.1}(a_{i1},h_j)^2-d_{-0.1}(a_{i1},h_n)^2+m)/tau), where j is a train co-consumer and n is a train co-consumption-near non-edge.
OBSERVABLES: The margin gap should rise on train-held-out-within-train sampled positive/negative pairs, cooccur_overlap_L3 and adjacency_L3 should improve, and first-token/gold-prefix survival may shift; report L1 Hard ER, positive-minus-negative gap, extension alphabet, all required depths, and Stage3 test_R@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Shuffle the item-to-row association of the frozen co-consumption matrix before pair mining, preserving its values and sampler; keep geometry and all weights fixed.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace every d_c term with squared Euclidean distance in the same ranking equation; keep pairs, sampling, weights, maps and initialization fixed.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use the correctly indexed frozen training co-consumption signal and Poincare distance at -0.1, with unchanged four-token/export and frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: C_ij=count of users whose training item sets contain both i,j; s_ij=C_ij/sqrt((C_ii+eps)(C_jj+eps)). For each i sample j from {j:s_ij>0}, n from top20_k(s_ik) with C_ik=0. x_i=exp_0^c(z_i), a_i=exp_0^c(first_quantized_euclidean_latent_i), h_j=exp_0^c(W z_j). d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c)*atanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x) MobiusPlus y||). Add lambda*mean_i softplus((d_c(a_i,h_j)^2-d_c(a_i,h_n)^2+m)/tau) to baseline loss; lambda calibrated once using training batches to make its initial weighted magnitude approximately 1e-4.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen train split item co-occurrence CSR, item IDs, encoder latents, first-level assignments/codebook, c,m,tau,lambda. Output is scalar ranking loss and gradients to encoder/first codebook; standard quantizer exports the unchanged four-token SID.
SAFEGUARDS: Add eps to counts/norms; exclude self and all known positives from negatives; if no valid negative skip anchor. Project Poincare points to norm <=(1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), clamp atanh arguments below 1-1e-5, compute in float32, clip gradients, and log unweighted/weighted loss and gradient norms. Train-only fixed lambda calibration; no per-step dense N-by-N matrix.
END IDEA

IDEA: incoming_context_barycenter
TITLE: Incoming-context hyperbolic barycenter assignment with rejection pressure
MOTIVATION: A next item reached from many distinct prior regions is difficult to tokenize; its first-level region should inherit the concentrated incoming context that best explains its observed predecessors, without collapsing merely similar but behaviorally wrong items together.
NOVELTY: Use each candidate first-token prototype as a hyperbolic destination and a train-only incoming predecessor-context barycenter as its target, while a co-consumption-hard negative destination supplies explicit rejection pressure. This is not a successor-consensus term added to Euclidean assignment: predecessor concentration and the negative contrast jointly alter the actual curved candidate assignment score.
CHANGE: Precompute each item's sparse predecessor-region distribution from training transitions, represent the distribution by a weighted Poincare Fréchet barycenter of predecessor first-token prototypes, and include positive-versus-hard-negative geodesic contrast in the first-token candidate score. Optimize sampled candidate comparisons only; no new extension tokens.
SPECIFICATION: Build directed counts n_{pi} from adjacent training events. During a minibatch, get the current soft first-token probabilities q_p over K prototypes and compute for target i a cached sparse weighted prototype barycenter b_i=FrechetMean_{p:n_pi>0}(prototype[p], normalized n_pi). For each target, select positive target-side context i and hard negative item r with high training co-consumption cosine but no transition into i. Score candidate code k by S(i,k)=d_c(v_k,b_i)^2 - eta*d_c(v_k,b_r)^2, where b_r is r's incoming-context barycenter; use softmax(-S/tau) for straight-through assignment, plus standard reconstruction/VQ losses. Downweight targets with diffuse predecessor-region distribution using g_i=1-H(p(region|i))/log(max(2,K)), clipped [0,1], so the actual score is S_g=g_i*S+(1-g_i)*S_base-curved; compute g from train counts and current predecessor assignments detached. Curved distance and behavior both enter each candidate score.
COST: One sparse transition/count pass and per-item top predecessor cache. Per step sample B targets and at most one hard negative each; barycenter evaluation can use at most 8 cached predecessor representatives, costing O(16BD+BKD) with 8-step Karcher iterations, versus O(BKD) baseline. At frozen scale 72,000 optimizer steps, this is at most 72,000*B*16D extra barycenter coordinate updates plus the existing candidate scoring; cache final detached barycenters per epoch to reduce to O(BKD+BD) while refreshing on 24 steps/epoch. Expected moderate overhead, fixed four tokens.
HYPOTHESIS: Concentrated incoming context is a useful supervision target: items whose predecessors come from fewer distinct regions should be pulled toward their incoming hyperbolic barycenter, while co-consumption-close non-transition items are pushed away. Keep only if concentration-stratified gold-prefix survival and the discrimination gap improve without harming diffuse-target recall, and both-on Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065 and both controls.
RISKS: Early assignments make predecessor regions noisy; cached barycenters can stale; entropy gating can suppress useful learning for rare items; incoming context may not align with next-item ranking, and hard negatives can include unobserved transitions.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed incoming transition context and co-consumption-similar non-transition hard negatives, both derived from training interactions.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Within each training user's chronological training events, count adjacent predecessor->target pairs n_pi. Normalize incoming predecessor counts per target, aggregate predecessor code-region probabilities into p(region|i), and compute normalized entropy. Separately compute binary-user-set co-consumption cosine and choose a top-20 neighbor r with no observed predecessor edge into i; freeze the sparse counts and negative candidate lists.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Use only training users/events and training timestamps when building n and co-consumption; no validation/test interactions, future events, or prediction-time context enter barycenters or SIDs. Current predecessor assignments are model states trained only on those frozen edges.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare Frechet/Karcher weighted barycenter and squared Poincare geodesic distance at fixed curvature -0.1; barycenter iterations use log maps to the current iterate and exp map back.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First-level code candidate score and soft assignment in Stage2; the incoming-context barycenter and negative-context barycenter are directly compared to candidate prototypes before argmin/softmax.
COUPLING PATH: Train transitions determine predecessor-region weights and concentration gate; co-consumption identifies a hard negative; both barycenters are formed in the Poincare ball and their geodesic distances change each first-token candidate's assignment probability.
COUPLING EQUATION: S(i,k)=g_i[d_{-0.1}(v_k,b_i)^2-eta*d_{-0.1}(v_k,b_{r(i)})^2]+(1-g_i)d_{-0.1}(v_k,z_i)^2, with b_i=FrechetMean_{p}(n_{pi}/n_i, v_{a_p}) and r(i) selected by train co-consumption similarity subject to n_{r,i}=0.
OBSERVABLES: Expect target assignment concentration by incoming-region entropy bin, higher positive-minus-negative gap, improved cooccur_overlap_L3 and adjacency_L3, and changes in L1 Hard ER and gold-prefix survival depths 1-4. Report extension alphabet and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 alongside all required measures.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Shuffle item-to-row association of the frozen transition and co-consumption signals, recompute cached barycenters/gates/negative lists under that shuffled association, and keep the hyperbolic scorer and coefficients fixed.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Frechet barycenters by weighted Euclidean means and all geodesic distances by squared Euclidean distances, with same behavior, cache size, coefficients, candidate sampler and initialization.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use correctly indexed train-only incoming transitions and co-consumption hard negatives with Poincare barycenters/distances at -0.1; no extra collision alphabet or Stage3 change.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p_i(p)=n_{pi}/(sum_q n_{qi}+eps); q_p(k)=softmax(-d_c(v_k,z_p)^2/tau_q); r_i(k)=sum_p p_i(p)*stopgrad(q_p(k)); H_i=-sum_k r_i(k)log(r_i(k)+eps)/log(max(2,K)); g_i=clip(1-H_i,0,1). For top M=8 predecessors, b_i initialized at highest-weight prototype and repeat 8 times: u=sum_p p_i(p)log_{b}(v_{a_p}); b=exp_b(u), renormalizing weights over retained predecessors. Select r(i)=argmax cooc_cos(i,r) subject to no train edge r->i. Compute S(i,k) as in COUPLING EQUATION; q_i=softmax(-S(i,:)/tau), use straight-through argmax; add standard RQ-VAE reconstruction and commitment/codebook losses. Compute negative b_r identically. A detach assignment probabilities in barycenter construction each epoch; gradients flow through candidate distances and assignment only.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are train-only sparse directed transition counts, train co-consumption nearest-neighbor lists, item IDs, first-token prototypes/assignments, encoder latents, c=-0.1, M=8, Karcher iterations=8, eta and temperature. Outputs are per-item cached behavior barycenters/gates, first-token candidate scores/probabilities, and unchanged downstream residual tokens.
SAFEGUARDS: Smooth incoming counts; fall back to encoder point for no predecessors; renormalize top-M weights; clamp ball norms below (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), stable log/exp maps and float32, stop gradients through stale cached context, refresh cache once per epoch, mask invalid negatives and skip empty cases. Clip gradients and bound eta/g_i to [0,1]. Keep all sparse data structures train-only; never materialize dense item-item matrices.
END IDEA
