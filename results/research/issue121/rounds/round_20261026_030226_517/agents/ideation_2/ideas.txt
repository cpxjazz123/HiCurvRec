IDEA: branch_lorentz_prefix
TITLE: Branch-conditioned Lorentz prefix closure with popularity-ordered collision tails
MOTIVATION: Items that share a useful first-two-token context should have a train-behaviour-predictable third token, while the first level must retain broad coverage and collisions should not create a large unpredictable fourth-token alphabet.
NOVELTY: Unlike a transition-attraction term on item pairs, behaviour sets a per-item Lorentz radial coordinate and the quantizer jointly assigns to curved prefix representatives; unlike a generic prefix penalty, closure is computed only over the first-three-token representatives and the fourth token is separately and deterministically ordered by train successor popularity.
CHANGE: Build a fixed-curvature Lorentz assignment whose radial coordinate is the item's training successor branching entropy; add a closure loss over items with the same first-two hard codes that makes their third-code representatives and behaviour-weighted continuation barycentre agree. Keep the fourth code as a deterministic rank of training successor popularity within each exact three-token collision set.
SPECIFICATION: Let b_i be the normalized successor entropy below. At each residual quantization level, map item residual y_i to Lorentz point x_i=exp^L_o([s*y_i, a*(b_i-mean(b))]), with curvature -c, c=1 and clipped a; assign to code c_k minimizing d_L(x_i,mu_k)^2. For batch items sharing hard prefix (q1,q2), let m_g be the Lorentz Frechet mean (2 fixed Karcher steps) of their third-level targets x_i and let p_g be the normalized behaviour-weighted mean of their third-level prototypes; Lclose=mean_g d_L(m_g,p_g)^2, only for levels 1-3. Optimize reconstruction + commitment + 0.1 Lclose. For every exact three-code collision group G, sort items by descending train-only successor count n_i (ties ascending item ID); assign fourth token rank modulo M=32, with deterministic fixed-width token encoding; do not train or penalize level 4 as part of Lclose.
COST: 3000 epochs, 24 steps/epoch, four cards unchanged. Cache b_i; each step adds O(batch*d) Lorentz maps and at most two Karcher iterations per represented (q1,q2) group, typically <2% wall time; offline tail sorting O(N log N) once. No N-by-codebook all-pairs behaviour matrix.
HYPOTHESIS: The branch entropy radial coordinate separates broad successor items from narrow branches and the closure makes T3 predictable conditional on (T1,T2), while popularity rank makes T4 nearly deterministic. Keep only if both-on raises T3 and T4 teacher-forced top-1 and gold-prefix survival at depths 3-4 versus each control, and Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.065. Expected L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives: slight increase or neutral (not an objective); reject if it rises sharply with worse depth-1 survival.
RISKS: Entropy may encode popularity rather than useful branching; small prefix groups yield noisy Frechet means; closure could collapse T3 codes or hurt reconstruction; deterministic modulo tail rank may collide if a group exceeds 32.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item entropy of directed train successor distribution.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Named function successor_entropy_by_item(train_split, n_items) returns float tensor b[N]. Count each adjacent (source,target) item pair only within training users' training sequences to obtain C_ij; p_ij=C_ij/sum_j C_ij, b_i=-sum_j p_ij log(p_ij+1e-12)/log(max(2, number_of_nonzero_successors_i)); zero for no outgoing edge. No validation/test split is passed.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: SID vectors, code assignments, b_i, and extension ordering use only training item features and training interactions. Validation/test interactions cannot be arguments to successor_entropy_by_item; item-ID tie-breaking is fixed and interaction-independent.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz exp map and Lorentz geodesic distance d_L at fixed negative curvature -1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Each residual level's code assignment and the level-3 prefix closure during Stage 2; fourth token remains deterministic post-assignment extension.
COUPLING PATH: b_i sets the Lorentz radial coordinate of the point that is compared geodesically with candidate codes; that hard assignment determines first-three prefixes consumed by closure and extension groups. Removing b or replacing d_L changes that assignment path; tail ordering additionally uses training successor counts.
COUPLING EQUATION: score(i,k)=d_L(exp^L_o([s*y_i,a*(b_i-mean(b))]),mu_k)^2; L=L_RQ+lambda*mean_g d_L(m_g(b),p_g(b))^2, with g defined by hard (q1,q2) over levels 1-3 only.
OBSERVABLES: Record L1 Hard ER (52,855 canonical pairs, ten fixed negatives), survival depths 1/2/3/4, teacher-forced T3/T4 top-1, T4 NLL/alphabet, and frozen-protocol Stage3 test_R@10. Prediction: ER neutral/slightly worse; survival depth 1 roughly neutral, depths 3-4 improve; T3 rises materially above 0.34 toward 0.7, T4 toward 0.977; Stage3 target >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set b_i=its train-item mean for every item and use no successor-popularity ordering (tie-break item ID); retain Lorentz assignment and closure. Predict no behavioural radial separation, worse T3/T4 and depth-3/4 survival than both-on; ER may remain comparable.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Replace Lorentz map/distance/Karcher mean with matched Euclidean identity-map, squared Euclidean distance and arithmetic means, keeping exact b_i, closure, optimizer, and popularity-ranked tail. Predict less useful branch hierarchy and lower depth-3/4 survival/T3 than both-on; measure ER independently.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use actual train b_i, Lorentz curvature -1 throughout assignment and closure, and popularity-ranked deterministic tail. Success criterion is Stage3 test_R@10 >0.065 and both T3/T4 and depth-3/4 survival exceed both ablations.
SPEC EQUATIONS: C_ij=count_train(i->j); b_i=H(C_i*)/log(max(2,deg_i)). x_i=exp^L_o([s*y_i,a*(b_i-mean b)]); q_l(i)=argmin_k d_L(x_i^l,mu_lk)^2. For each g=(q1,q2), m_g=Frechet_L({x_i^3:i in g}), p_g=normalize_L(sum_i(1+b_i)mu^3_{q3(i)}); Lclose=mean_g d_L(m_g,p_g)^2. Sort each exact (q1,q2,q3) item group by (-n_i,item_id), set q4=rank mod 32. Here n_i=sum_j C_ij. No q4 member enters Lclose or the prefix representative calculation.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are Stage-2 item vectors/residuals, training interaction split, four codebooks and fixed SID width. Output is four integer tokens per item plus RQ-VAE parameters and logged diagnostics. Behaviour function input is train split and item count; output is float32 [N].
SAFEGUARDS: Use float64 for entropy sums then float32 output; clamp entropy [0,1]; clamp Lorentz tangent norm before exp map; project points/prototypes onto valid future sheet with <x,x>_L=-1/c and x0>0; clamp acosh argument to >=1+1e-7; normalize Frechet updates, stop update for singleton/empty groups; gradient-clip closure and keep lambda fixed. Fixed tie breaks; modulo tail is explicit and audited for maximum group size.
END IDEA

IDEA: successor_tail_transport
TITLE: Behaviour-directed curved residual transport for decisive third codes
MOTIVATION: A coarse first code can preserve many gold prefixes yet leave the third decision effectively 256-way; the third residual should encode which training successor branch an item's two-token context supports, and the collision extension should exploit a small deterministic popularity order.
NOVELTY: The behaviour signal is not used as a pairwise attraction or suffix: it selects a per-item tangent transport direction before curved third-level quantization. The direction is estimated from the training successor distribution's codebook barycentre, so it changes the actual residual presented to the third-level hyperbolic quantizer. The fourth token is independently prescribed as a train-popularity rank within exact first-three collisions.
CHANGE: At level 3, transport the item's Euclidean residual into the tangent space at the current Lorentz prefix representative, subtract a bounded successor-distribution barycentre direction, then exp-map and choose the nearest third-level code by Lorentz distance. Keep levels 1-2 normal RQ and use the same three-prefix-only continuation closure and popularity-ranked deterministic extension.
SPECIFICATION: Learn/initialize Lorentz prototypes mu_k^3 for third code. For each item, compute z_i=log^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(exp^L_o(s*y_i)); compute v_i=sum_j p_ij log^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(mu^3_{q3(j)}) where successor target j's current third code is detached and p_ij=C_ij/(sum_j C_ij+eps). Then r_i=clip_norm(z_i-eta*stopgrad(v_i),R), x_i^3=exp^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(r_i), q3=argmin_k d_L(x_i^3,mu_k^3)^2. Use Lclose over only first-three prefix representatives: within each hard (q1,q2), penalize squared Lorentz distance between each item's third representative and the group's behaviour-weighted Frechet mean, with weight (1+b_i). Tail q4 sorts exact three-token collision members by descending n_i, then item ID, assigning rank mod 32.
COST: 24 updates/epoch for 3000 epochs, four cards; sparse successor rows cached and at most 16 sampled outgoing edges per item/step. Extra log/exp maps and distance only for third code, O(batch*(16+d*256)) with code distances already required; estimated 3-6% wall time. One-time sort O(N log N); no added full pairwise graph tensor.
HYPOTHESIS: The transport subtracts the item's likely-successor branch direction from its third residual, making q3 a stable context/branch discriminator instead of a fresh arbitrary code choice; popularity rank makes T4 predictable. Require both-on T3 accuracy and depth-3/4 survival to exceed controls and Stage3 test_R@10 >0.065. L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives is expected unchanged or modestly worse; a large ER gain with no survival/Recall gain is not success.
RISKS: Successor-target assignments are nonstationary and may oscillate; high-outdegree rows need sampling; transport can erase useful content residual; groups with >32 items alias in q4; train successor labels may be sparse for cold items.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed train successor probability row p_ij plus outgoing count n_i; normalized row entropy b_i.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Named function successor_profile_by_item(train_split,n_items) returns (CSR C[N,N], b[N], n[N]) from consecutive item pairs in training sequences only; p_ij=C_ij/(sum_j C_ij+1e-12), b_i normalized entropy, n_i=sum_j C_ij. Cache CSR rows and sample at most 16 nonzero successors with probabilities renormalized for minibatch computation.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The profile function accepts only the training split; it is called once before Stage 2. Successor targets and popularity ranks are built from train interactions only. No validation or test interaction, metric, or sequence is read during SID learning, ordering, or hyperparameter choice.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz log map at the (q1,q2) prefix representative, tangent-vector subtraction, exp map, and Lorentz geodesic nearest-code assignment, curvature -1.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Third residual formation and level-3 code selection within Stage 2, plus a level-1-to-3 prefix-only continuation regularizer; no fourth-level quantization/penalty.
COUPLING PATH: C_ij determines p_ij and the successor-code barycentre v_i; v_i is subtracted from the tangent residual before exp^L and geodesic argmin chooses q3. Thus behaviour changes the input point and geometry changes the selected q3; disabling either breaks that same path.
COUPLING EQUATION: x_i^3=exp^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(clip(z_i-eta*sum_j p_ij log^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(mu^3_{q3(j)}),R)); q3(i)=argmin_k d_L(x_i^3,mu_k^3)^2, where p_ij is the named train successor signal and log/exp/d_L are curvature -1 operators.
OBSERVABLES: Log canonical L1 Hard ER (52,855 pairs, ten fixed negatives), gold-prefix survival at each depth, teacher-forced top-1 T3/T4 and T4 NLL/alphabet, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Expected L1 ER roughly unchanged/slight increase; depth 1-2 neutral, depth 3-4 higher; T3 top-1 moves from the 0.34 region toward 0.7 and T4 toward 0.977; Stage3 should cross 0.065 if the gains translate to beam coverage.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Set p_ij to a uniform distribution over items for each source (and disable popularity ordering, using item-ID tie-break); retain all curved operators, closure, and matched training. Predict loss of branch-directed residual signal and lower T3/depth-3 survival and T4 predictability than both-on.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Use tangent identity coordinates, ordinary vector subtraction and squared Euclidean third-code distance; keep the exact observed C_ij/p_ij, popularity ordering, closure terms, and parameter budget. This is a matched Euclidean assignment, not removal of behaviour. Predict weaker T3 conditional separation and lower depth-3/4 survival than both-on; compare ER and Stage3 equally.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use actual train successor profiles, curved tangent residual transport at curvature -1, three-token-prefix closure only, and train-popularity rank extension. Keep only if both top-1 tail positions and depths 3-4 improve over both ablations and frozen Stage3 test_R@10 >0.065.
SPEC EQUATIONS: p_ij=C_ij/(n_i+eps); b_i=H(p_i)/log(max(2,nnz(C_i))). z_i=log^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(exp^L_o(s*y_i)); v_i=sum_j p_ij log^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(mu^3_{q3(j)}), with target codes detached; x_i^3=exp^L_{mu_{q1,q2}}(clip(z_i-eta*v_i,R)); q3=argmin_k d_L(x_i^3,mu^3_k)^2. For g=(q1,q2), m_g=Frechet_L({mu^3_{q3(i)}:i in g}, weights 1+b_i); Lclose=mean_{i} d_L(mu^3_{q3(i)},m_g)^2, where g and members use only first three positions, never q4. q4 ranks members of each exact (q1,q2,q3) collision by (-n_i,item_id) mod 32.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are frozen item vectors, Stage-2 train split, current first-two codes and Lorentz representatives, third codebook, and RQ-VAE optimizer state. Outputs are third-code assignments/updates and four-token SIDs; the behavior function returns sparse successor profiles, entropy, and counts.
SAFEGUARDS: Curvature fixed at -1; use stable Lorentz exp/log formulas with inner-product clamps and valid-sheet projection; cap successor row sampling to 16 with renormalized p; define empty rows v_i=0 and b_i=0; detach q3 targets in v_i to prevent moving-target gradient loops; clip tangent norm to R and gradient norm; clamp Frechet weights and skip singleton groups; deterministic item-ID ties and modulo-32 extension; preserve separate level-4 generation and exclude it from all prefix losses.
END IDEA
