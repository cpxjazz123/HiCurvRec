IDEA: holonomy-path-behaviour-rq
TITLE: Behaviour-chosen two-leg parallel transport (curvature holonomy) inside the RQ residual recursion
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE Semantic IDs are built by a purely reconstructive residual walk, so deeper tokens split items by residual geometry, not by what users actually do next; behaviour-only prefix cues help (+0.012~0.014) but every curvature term tried so far was add-on and measured inert.
NOVELTY: Unlike successor-frame warp, branch-geodesic rank, fork-angle transport, cohesion prefix ranking, co-occurrence Frechet, tangent quantization, region argmax, interest-cohort parents, Poincare-Frechet/VQ codebooks, EQVOTE-HYP, HORO, statecell margin, anisotropic Poincare, CURVCTRL, LORENTZ profile smoothing, behcone, succboost-geoshift and BRad-RQ, no codebook statistic, rank, mean, vote or per-level curvature is touched. The curved operation is a parallel-transport composition that carries code tangents o->m->a instead of o->a, where m is the behaviour successor anchor of the already-decided prefix. Because hyperbolic transport is path dependent (holonomy = triangle area) and Euclidean transport is the identity, the mechanism's entire effect is exactly the curvature; curvature-off reproduces baseline RQ bit for bit, so the geometry switch cannot be inert.
CHANGE: The code vectors and the residual used at each RQ level are expressed in a frame reached by transporting through the prefix's training-only successor anchor, so the residual direction seen by the quantizer is rotated by the holonomy of (o, m, a); the token at levels 2..L is chosen in that transported frame.
SPECIFICATION: Lorentz/unit-hyperboloid model with curvature -1 in units z~ = z/s, s = RMS of encoder outputs measured once on the first training minibatch. Tangents are (D+1)-vectors, points on <u,u>_L = -1. Per level: m = exp_o(v_pi) from the anchor table (prefix pi already known), w_k = PT_{m->a}(PT_{o->m}(theta_hat_{l,k})), k_l = argmin_k <r - w_k, r - w_k>_L, e = r - w_{k_l}, a <- exp_a(w_{k_l}), r <- PT_{a_old->a}(e). Anchor table: every 25 epochs, no-grad forward over the 24556 training items gives each item's prefix, then v_pi = sum_j t_pi(j) z~_j with t_pi the row-normalised training transition mass out of the prefix cohort, norm-clipped to the measured 99th percentile of ||z~_j||; prefixes with fewer than 5 items or no outgoing mass fall back to v_pi = 0 (m = o).
COST: +6 to 10 percent wall clock over baseline Stage 2; no new parameters (same L x K x D codebook tensor), table is at most (L-1)*K*D floats, per step the transports are two rank-1 updates on a K x D block per item (about 100 MFLOP extra per step at batch 1023, L=3, K=256, D=32), and the anchor refresh is 120 no-grad full-passes over 24556 items (about 1.3 percent of the 3000-epoch budget). Stage 3 unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: Keeping the idea requires that the transports change the realized partition and that the change helps: level 2..3 token flip rate against the behaviour-off forward pass is well above zero and concentrated on items with small top-2 hyperbolic distance gaps, prefix-conditional successor entropy falls relative to behaviour-off, and Stage 3 test recall@10 exceeds 0.065 (above the 0.0525-0.0540 behaviour band and the 0.0638 history best).
RISKS: holonomy angles are O(1) rad and may over-rotate level 2..3 partitions and hurt reconstruction; the encoder/anchor table co-evolution may collapse all anchors toward the same point, making the path degenerate and the coupling

inert; the successor anchor may be dominated by globally popular items, adding no prefix-specific direction; extra autograd through exp/log/transport could slow or destabilise training; single-run seed noise is comparable to the expected gain.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-item transition mass from training sequences, aggregated over the cohort of items that currently share a prefix.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage 2 training split only: a sparse row-normalised transition matrix T (row i = next-item counts after i, symmetric-free, no self loops). Every 25 epochs, a no-grad encoder pass over all training items yields current prefixes pi(i) and normalised codes z~_i; for each prefix pi at level l-1, t_pi(j) = sum_{i in S_pi} T_ij / sum_{i in S_pi} sum_{j'} T_ij', and v_pi = clip(sum_j t_pi(j) z~_j). Table entries are frozen between refreshes and detached from autograd.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: T is built only from training-split sequences and z~ only from training-split items encoded by the Stage 2 encoder; validation and test interactions are never read during Stage 2, the table is rebuilt only inside Stage 2, and Stage 3 consumes the frozen SIDs as before. SID construction for any item depends on statistics of the training cohort, never on that item's own held-out continuation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Unit-hyperboloid (Lorentz) model, fixed negative curvature K = -1 in z~ units (c = 1/s^2 in raw encoder units, s measured, not tuned): parallel transport PT_{u->w}(v) = v + <w,v>_L/(1-<u,w>_L) (u+w), composed as PT_{m->a} o PT_{o->m}; the holonomy of the triangle (o,m,a) equals its area.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the RQ residual recursion itself, at every level l >= 2, in the frame in which the nearest-code assignment and the residual are computed (not a loss term, not a post-hoc re-ranking of codes).
COUPLING PATH: prefix tokens -> anchor-table lookup m -> two-leg transport -> the code frame w_k and residual frame r used by the argmin -> token k_l -> next prefix -> Stage 3 SID. Removing the behaviour removes m (m = o, direct path); removing the curvature makes both transports the identity, so w_k = theta_k and r is plain-subtracted; either removal changes the argmin and hence the tokens.
COUPLING EQUATION: w_{l,k} = PT_{m_pi -> a_{l-1}}( PT_{o -> m_pi}( theta_hat_{l,k} ) ), m_pi = exp_o( sum_j t_pi(j) z~_j ), k_l = argmin_k <r_{l-1} - w_{l,k}, r_{l-1} - w_{l,k}>_L.
OBSERVABLES: level-1 tokens identical to behaviour-off by construction; level 2..3 token flip rate against the behaviour-off forward pass at the same checkpoint; hyperbolic top-2 distance gap of flipped items (should be small); prefix-conditional successor entropy; code-norm invariance check ||w_k||_{g(a)} = ||theta_k||_{g(o)}; Stage 3 test_recall@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: m_pi := o for all levels, i.e. w_k = PT_{o->a}(theta_hat_{l,k}) and the anchor table is never consulted - a curvature-only transport RQ with zero behaviour input.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: PT := identity and exp/log := identity (plain additive residual recursion), so w_k = theta_{l,k} and r_{l} = r_{l-1} - theta_{l,k_l}: exactly the baseline Euclidean RQ, in which m has no effect on any computed value.
ABLATION BOTH ON: table-built anchors plus two-leg transport, i.e. the trained candidate.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Parameters theta_{l,k} in R^D (l=1..L, k=1..K, same tensor and init as the baseline codebook), lift theta_hat_{l,k} = (0, theta_{l,k}) in R^{D+1}; s scalar = RMS(Z_0) on batch 0, frozen. Minkowski <x,y>_L =

-x_0 y_0 + x_{1:}.y_{1:}. o = (1,0..0). log_o(u) = arcosh(u_0) u_{1:}/||u_{1:}||_, exp_o(v) = (cosh||v||, sinh||v|| v/||v||). exp_u(v) = cosh||v||_L u + sinh||v||_L v/||v||_L (T_u). log_u(w) = d (w + <u,w>_L u)/||w + <u,w>_L u||, d = arcosh(-<u,w>_L). PT_{u->w}(v) = v + <w,v>_L/(1-<u,w>_L) (u+w). Forward: r = Z/s (lifted (0,r)), a = o. For l=1..L: v_pi = M[prefix] (0 if l=1, use_behaviour off, or missing); m = exp_o(v_pi); W[b,k] = PT_{m_b->a_b}(PT_{o->m_b}(theta_hat_{l,k})); d2[b,k] = <r_b - W[b,k], r_b - W[b,k]>_L; k_l = argmin_k d2[b,k] (lowest index wins); e = r_b - W[b,k_l] projected to T_{a_b} via e <- e + <e,a_b>_L a_b; a_b <- exp_{a_b}(W[b,k_l]); r_b <- PT_{a_b,old -> a_b}(e); token[b,l] = k_l. Reconstruction input x_hat = s * log_o(a_b) (D-dim) to the unchanged decoder. Loss = baseline reconstruction + baseline codebook term sum_l <sg(r_{l-1}) - W[b,k_l], sg(r_{l-1}) - W[b,k_l]>_L + beta * baseline commitment, beta unchanged. Anchors: v_pi = clip_{||.||<=Q99(||Z~||)}( sum_j t_pi(j) Z~[j] ), refreshed every 25 epochs.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: encoder batch Z in R^{BxD} (or the SID assignment pass over items), trainable codebook theta in R^{LxKxD}, scale s, anchor table M (dict prefix tuple -> R^D), switches use_behaviour and use_curvature. Outputs: token matrix tok in {1..K}^{BxL}, reconstructed vector x_hat in R^{BxD}, per-level (r, W_{k_l}) pairs for the VQ losses, and the transported walk point a in R^{Bx(D+1)}.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp arcosh arguments to >= 1+1e-6; compute the Lorentz inner products and the denominators 1-<u,w>_L (>= 2 always) in float64; re-project a to <a,a>_L = -1 after every exp and every transport; project tangents to T_a; clip ||theta_{l,k}|| to Q99(||Z~||)/L (measured once, frozen) and clip the anchor tangent norm to Q99(||Z~||); prefixes with < 5 items or zero outgoing mass use m = o; deterministic lowest-index tie-break in the argmin; anchor table built no-grad and stored detached, refreshed only between epochs; log (never gate on) the median anchor norm and the flip rate so a degenerate path is visible.
END IDEA
