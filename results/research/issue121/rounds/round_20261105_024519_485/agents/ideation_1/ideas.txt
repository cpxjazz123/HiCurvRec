IDEA: HYPVOLCAP-RQ
TITLE: Demand-sized geodesic cells: hyperbolic volume-capacity assignment for the RQ-VAE Semantic ID partition
MOTIVATION: Current SIDs are cut by a Euclidean nearest-centroid partition that ignores how much next-item traffic each code actually has to carry: a few codes absorb most of the popular-item mass while other codes are near-dead, so the level-1/level-2 prefixes that the frozen Stage-3 generative model beams over are capacity-mismatched. Behaviour-only prefix fixes land at ~0.0525-0.0540 and the previous curvature arms added nothing because they moved centroids or added a penalty term, never the partition itself.
NOVELTY: Not a codebook/centroid variant (codebook update and the Euclidean residual stay baseline), not per-item radial ranking, not per-level curvature control, not Fréchet / horosphere / tangent / transport / anisotropic / region-argmax. The geometric object is the VOLUME of a geodesic ball at constant curvature -1: each code's bias in a Klein-model power diagram is the geodesic radius whose hyperbolic volume equals that code's training-only traffic share. Curvature thus acts as a capacity law (exponential growth) that a flat r^d rule cannot reproduce, and behaviour demand and curvature meet inside a single assignment equation.
CHANGE: Each RQ level's assignment replaces squared-Euclidean argmin by s_ik = (1 - <p_i,q_k>)/sqrt(1-||q_k||^2) - cosh(rho_k), where p and q are Poincaré images of the encoder output and of the codebook row under curvature -1, and rho_k is the geodesic radius inverted from the code's traffic share pi_k through the exact hyperbolic ball-volume recursion.
SPECIFICATION: Per level l: sigma_l = 0.99-quantile of ||z_i|| on one no-grad full pass at the end of epoch 1; rho_max = max_i ||z_i||/sigma_l clamped to [1e-3, 8]; p_i = tanh(||u_i||) u_i/||u_i|| and q_k = tanh(||v_k||) v_k/||v_k|| with u_i = z_i/sigma_l, v_k = c_k/sigma_l; item traffic tau_i precomputed once from training transitions; pi_k = Laplace-smoothed share of tau under the held assignment a_hat; rho_k = V^{-1}(pi_k V(rho_max)) by 24-step bisection with V = W_{d-1}, W_0 = rho, W_1 = cosh rho - 1, W_n = (sinh^{n-1}rho cosh rho)/n - ((n-1)/n) W_{n-2}; assignment a_i = argmin_k s_ik; pi and rho refreshed every T = 8 steps (one extra no-grad forward); residual, codebook update, loss and optimiser unchanged.
COST: 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72000 steps on four cards, one single run. Per step and per level the curved score replaces the K x d distance computation already done (same FLOPs); the added work is an O(N) scatter for m_k, K bisections of at most 24 cheap volume evaluations each, and one extra no-grad assignment forward on 1 step in 8. Estimated +6 to +10 percent wall clock on the frozen budget, i.e. the same GPU-day envelope plus under two hours.
HYPOTHESIS: If the curvature-capacity coupling is real, Stage-3 test_recall@10 must exceed the behaviour-prefix band 0.0525-0.0540 by more than seed noise and approach or pass 0.065, and this must coincide with a large level-1 SID change rate against the Euclidean partition, a fall in dead codes and a lower level-1 prefix conditional entropy. If 30 percent or more of items change SIDs while test_recall@10 stays below 0.055, curvature moved the partition but the partition was not the bottleneck, and the idea is falsified in one run.
RISKS: Demand-sized cells can overload head items and hurt mid-tail recall that beam search already covered; Rich-get-richer feedback between pi_k and the assignment can collapse the partition onto a few codes (mitigated by Laplace smoothing and a per-item share floor); The tanh squash can make the curved score numerically close to Euclidean and dilute the curvature effect; The frozen Stage-3 model may be insensitive to the re-partition; Single-run seed noise of about +-0.003 may be comparable to a real but small gain; Refresh lag (T = 8) may oscillate and leave an inconsistent partition at the final epoch
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Popularity-adjusted reverse-transition traffic of each item: how much training-sequence flow arrives at an item, after debiasing every source by the breadth of its outgoing choices.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From Stage-2 training sequences only: A_ij = number of consecutive pairs (s_t, s_{t+1}) = (i, j); outdeg_j = sum_i A_ji; tau_i = sum_j A_ji / (1 + outdeg_j); tau normalised to sum to 1 over the 24556 training items; cached once before training and never recomputed from validation or test sequences.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: tau is built only from the training-sequence transition matrix, sigma_l and rho_max only from a no-grad pass over training-item encoder outputs, and pi_k only from training assignments; the SID pipeline never reads validation or test interactions, and the diagnostics (coherence, adjacency, transfer predictability) are computed from training statistics and are never used as an admission gate.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincaré-ball geodesic distance at fixed negative curvature phi = 1 in normalised ball coordinates (raw curvature -sigma_l^{-2}), used through cosh(d) = (1 - <p,q>)/(sqrt(1-||p||^2) sqrt(1-||q||^2)), together with the exact geodesic-ball volume V(rho) of curvature -1 (exponential growth).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage-2 training, inside the RQ quantizer's assignment step at every residual level, i.e. the step that decides which code each residual is snapped to and therefore which SID digit the item receives; it is not a loss term and not a post-hoc regulariser.
COUPLING PATH: tau_i -> pi_k (share of traffic under the held assignment) -> rho_k = V^{-1}(pi_k V(rho_max)) -> the same Klein/power score that carries the curvature factor 1/sqrt(1-||q_k||^2) -> argmin_k -> code a_i -> SID prefix. GEO=0 replaces the hyperbolic distance and the hyperbolic capacity radius rho_k by its flat form rho_max pi_k^{1/d}, so the partition changes; BEH=0 sets pi_k = 1/K, so every rho_k becomes rho_max, the behaviour term disappears and the curved term degenerates to a constant bias, so the partition changes again.
COUPLING EQUATION: a_i = argmin_k [ (1 - <p_i, q_k>) / sqrt(1 - ||q_k||^2) - cosh(rho_k) ], with rho_k = V^{-1}( pi_k V(rho_max) ), pi_k = (m_k + taubar)/(sum_k' m_k' + K taubar), m_k = sum_i tau_i 1[a_hat_i = k], taubar = mean_i tau_i.
OBSERVABLES: Level-1/2/3 SID change rate versus the Euclidean partition; dead-code count per level and code-usage entropy; level-1 prefix conditional entropy and prefix hit rate; head/mid/tail recall split; Stage-3 test_recall@10 (the judgement); coherence, adjacency and transfer-predictability diagnostics recorded but never gating.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEH=0 exists in code: pi_k = 1/K for all k, hence rho_k = rho_max and the score reduces to hyperbolic nearest-centre with a constant bias (pure geometry, no traffic). Switchable, never trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: GEO=0 exists in code: points are used raw (p_i = u_i, q_k = v_k), the score is ||u_i - v_k||^2 - (rho_k^E)^2 with rho_k^E = rho_max pi_k^{1/d}, i.e. flat r^d capacity carrying the same behaviour shares. Switchable, never trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: GEO=1 and BEH=1: hyperbolic score with traffic-sized geodesic radii. This is the single trained arm of the round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Frozen config: L residual levels, K codes per level, latent dim d, phi = 1, N = 24556, 24 steps per epoch. (1) Measured scales, once at the end of epoch 1 on a no-grad full pass: sigma_l = Quantile_0.99(||z_i^l||_2); rho_max = clamp(max_i ||z_i^l||_2 / sigma_l, 1e-3, 8). (2) Exp map: u_i = z_i^l / sigma_l; p_i = tanh(||u_i||) * u_i / max(||u_i||, 1e-12); v_k = c_k^l / sigma_l; q_k = tanh(||v_k||) * v_k / max(||v_k||, 1e-12). (3) Traffic: A_ij from train consecutive pairs; outdeg_j = sum_i A_ji; tau_i = sum_j A_ji / (1 + outdeg_j); tau <- tau / sum tau. (4) Demand: m_k = sum_i tau_i * 1[a_hat_i = k]; taubar = (1/N) sum_i tau_i; pi_k = (m_k + taubar)/(sum_k' m_k' + K taubar), with pi_k floored at taubar/(sum m + K taubar). (5) Volume: W_0(rho) = rho; W_1(rho) = cosh rho - 1; W_n(rho) = (sinh^{n-1} rho * cosh rho)/n - ((n-1)/n) * W_{n-2}(rho) for n >= 2; V(rho) = W_{d-1}(rho); rho_k = bisect on [0, rho_max] solving V(rho) = pi_k * V(rho_max), 24 iterations, tolerance 1e-6, V monotone. (6) Score and assignment: s_ik = (1 - <p_i, q_k>)/sqrt(max(1 - ||q_k||^2, 1e-6)) - cosh(rho_k); a_i = argmin_k s_ik with ties to the smallest k. (7) Refresh: if step mod T == 0 with T = 8 = floor(24/3), run one extra no-grad forward with the current rho, recompute a_hat, then (4) and (5); rho is held over the next T steps and stored as a buffer, not a parameter. (8) Everything else baseline: Euclidean residual z_i^{l+1} = z_i^l - c_{a_i}^l, baseline codebook update over assigned members, baseline RQ-VAE loss and optimiser; no gradient flows through rho_k, pi_k or sigma_l. (9) Switches GEO and BEH are config booleans read once at run start, as specified in the three ablation fields.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs per level and step: z^l in R^{B x d} (B <= 24556 encoder outputs or residuals), codebook c^l in R^{K x d}, tau in R^{N}, scalars sigma_l and rho_max, buffers a_hat in {1..K}^N and rho in R^K. Outputs: a in {1..K}^B integer SID digit per item, pi in R^K, rho in R^K, and the same quantized vector and Euclidean residual the baseline quantizer produces.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp ||p||, ||q|| <= 1 - 1e-6 and use max(1 - ||q_k||^2, 1e-6) in the denominator; clamp rho to [0, 8] so cosh rho <= 1.5e3 and no overflow (subtract the per-item score max before exponentiating if ever needed); bisection always bracketed by [0, rho_max] where V is monotone with V(0) = 0; floor pi_k at one item's traffic share so no code gets a zero-volume cell; empty codes keep their previous centroid and are re-seeded at the farthest-assigned item; Laplace smoothing taubar stops one hot code from taking all capacity; a_hat and rho are computed under no_grad so no double backward or graph reuse; if fewer than two codes are non-empty at a level, fall back to uniform pi at that level and log it; the switches and the refresh period T = 8 are fixed config constants and are never tuned in this round.
END IDEA
