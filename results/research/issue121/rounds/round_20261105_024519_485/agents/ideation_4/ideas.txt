IDEA: GROMPULL-RQ
TITLE: Fractional-geodesic-budget Gromov pull inside the RQ level argmin: behaviour-relative transition mass sets an admissible hyperbolic separation between two items' partial-code points
MOTIVATION: Baseline RQ-VAE decides every SID level by Euclidean nearest-residual, so the prefix hierarchy the Stage-3 generator must learn is whatever the latent geometry happens to make close; how users actually move (which item deterministically follows which) is never expressed as prefix structure. Euclidean space also has no graded, unbounded notion of "shared prefix depth", so a behaviour signal cannot be turned into a prefix quantity there.
NOVELTY: Not a per-item or per-level radial target (BRad-RQ, CURVCTRL), not a Fréchet codebook, not transport/tangent/cone/rank. The new object is a FRACTIONAL GEODESIC BUDGET m_ij^l = (1-q_ij)*(rho_i+rho_j): the relative mass q_ij of a training transition licenses a hyperbolic separation of at most that fraction of the maximal separation in curvature -1, and this budget enters the argmin that PRODUCES the SID, not a side loss.
CHANGE: Each level's code is chosen by argmin of one score = hyperbolic squared distance from the candidate partial-code point to the curved target point, PLUS a behaviour-weighted one-sided hinge on the hyperbolic distance between the item's candidate partial-code point and its training successor's partial-code point (two-sweep per batch: sweep 1 freezes reference codes, sweep 2 re-assigns).
SPECIFICATION: lambda = RMS encoder norm measured at init; c=1; exp_0(v)=tanh(|v|/2)v/|v|; one-sided hinge with budget (1-q)(rho_i+rho_j); shortlist of T=16 candidates; sweep-1 detached references; SID=(khat_1..khat_L).
COST: encoder cost unchanged; +1 extra quantizer pass per step and ~3.9e5 hyperbolic distance evaluations per step at batch 1023, L=3, T=16, |B|<=8 (about +20-25% quantizer FLOPs, est. +20-30% Stage-2 wall clock).
HYPOTHESIS: curvature turns behaviour mass into depth-graded prefix sharing; observable as positive rank correlation between q_ij and shared-prefix count above the flat-metric reference, and test_R@10 above the 0.0525-0.0540 behaviour-only band, targeting >0.065.
RISKS: SID collapse under pull; sparse tail transitions; boundary gradient blow-up; sweep-1/sweep-2 inconsistency; curvature again adding nothing measurable.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: directed training-split transitions i->next(i), kept as per-item successor set B_i with relative mass q_ij.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: counts from training-split item sequences only (window 1), keep successors with count>=2 covering >=0.9 of row mass, cap 8, q_ij=p_ij/max_k p_ik, row-normalised.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: T built once from the Stage-2 training sequences only; no validation/test interaction, session or item statistic enters; matrix frozen and hashed before epoch 0.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: exp_0/log at the origin of the Poincare ball of fixed negative curvature c=1, Poincare distance d_P, and the induced Gromov-product/geodesic separation scale; no trainable curvature.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: inside the RQ residual quantizer, in the per-level code-selection argmin and in the training loss term of the chosen code.
COUPLING PATH: q_ij -> budget m_ij -> hinge h_ij(k) -> score J_i(k) -> argmin -> SID. Removing behaviour zeroes the hinge (score becomes a monotone reparametrisation of Euclidean nearest-residual); removing curvature rep

laces d_P and the (rho_i+rho_j) budget by their flat analogues, which are not depth-graded.
COUPLING EQUATION: J_l(i,k) = d_P(P_i^l(k), T_i^l)^2 + sum_{j in B_i} qtilde_ij * max(0, d_P(P_i^l(k), P_j^l)^2 - [(1-q_ij)(rho_i^l(k)+R_j^l)]^2).
OBSERVABLES: shared-prefix count of top-mass transition pairs, rank correlation between q_ij and shared-prefix count, per-level code-usage entropy, per-level radius distribution, collision/validity counts, Stage-3 test_R@10.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: B_i set empty for every item; hinge removed; same curvature, same two sweeps, same everything else.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c->0 flat analogue: exp_0 replaced by v -> v/lambda, d_P by Euclidean, budget kept as (1-q)(||.||+||.||)/lambda.
ABLATION BOTH ON: full coupled run above; this is the only arm trained.
SPEC EQUATIONS: full recursion as in the block below.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: per batch: z_i, codebooks, T -> codes khat_l(i), loss increment.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp |v|/2<=8 before tanh, clamp |P|<=1-1e-6, stable arccosh via log1p, no-grad references, symmetric-free directed handling, deterministic shortlist and tie-breaks.
END IDEA
