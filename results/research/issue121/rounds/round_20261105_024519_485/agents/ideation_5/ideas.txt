IDEA: MOEBIUS-MASS-RQ
TITLE: Moebius-composed residual quantization with a hyperbolic shell-volume behaviour-mass prior
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE Semantic IDs come from a Euclidean greedy residual chain whose codebook cells are wildly uneven in the behaviour mass they carry: some tokens absorb most of the training transitions (hard for Stage3 to predict), others absorb almost none (wasted beam-search steps). Behaviour-only prefix changes reached about +0.013 Recall, while curvature-only distance changes reached nothing, because a monotone reparametrisation of Euclidean distance cannot change an argmin. This idea makes curvature change the discrete token decisions themselves and makes behaviour mass per token equal in curved-volume terms.
NOVELTY: Not a distance, a regulariser, a centroid rule, a radius/entropy assignment, a transport/warp, a cohort parent, a per-level feedback curvature, a barycentre or a tangent quantizer. Curvature enters as (i) NON-COMMUTATIVE COMPOSITION of the residual chain (the level-l candidate point is P_{l-1} (+) c_hat_k, so the level-l metric depends on the prefix and curvature stops being a monotone reparametrisation of the Euclidean residual once codeword radii differ - exactly the invariant that killed the earlier curved arms) and (ii) a HYPERBOLIC SHELL-VOLUME divisor sinh^2(rho)/rho^2 on a training-only behaviour-mass prior, inside the same argmax.
CHANGE: The Euclidean residual argmin is replaced by one scored argmax over the Moebius composition distance to the running prefix point plus the log of a behaviour prefix mass divided by the hyperbolic shell volume at that codeword's radius. Geometry and behaviour meet in a single scalar score per (item, level, codeword); dropping either removes half of that score.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed L=3, K codewords, width d, c=1 (unit-free via the measured scale s), beta_c=0.25 (inherited, unchanged). Trainable tangent codebooks U in R^{L,K,d}. Measured: s = EMA(0.99, detached) batch RMS of ||z|| over training items. Poincare tools (c=1): exp0(v)=tanh(||v||)v/||v||; log0(p)=artanh(||p||)p/||p||; p(+)q=((1+2<p,q>+||q||^2)p+(1-||p||^2)q)/(1+2<p,q>+||p||^2||q||^2); dP(p,q)=2artanh(||(-p)(+)q||). Behaviour: m_i = 1+log1p(outdeg_i) on training sequences; epoch-end detached per-level prefix mass n[l,k]; prior[l,k]=(n[l,k]+1/K)/2. Shell volume g[l,k]=sinh(rho)^2/rho^2 with Vhat normalised to mean 1. Per batch: v=z/s, p=exp0(v), P<-0; for l=1..3: Q_k=P(+)c_hat_k, score_k = -dP(p,Q_k)^2/(2s^2)+log(prior_k/Vhat_k), k*=argmax (straight-through into U), P<-Q_{k*}. Reconstruct v_hat=log0(P), loss = mean||v-v_hat||^2 + beta_c*mean||r_l/s - U[l,k*]||^2 with curved residual r_l=log0((-P_{l-1})(+)p).
COST: One run (3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72,000 steps, four cards) plus frozen Stage3. Per step at B=1024, K=256, L=3: 786,432 Moebius+distance evaluations, about 40 MFLOP and a 1024x256x3 score tensor, expected 8-12% of Stage2 step time; epoch-end n update is one pass over 24,556 train items; Stage3 unchanged (same three-token format), no extra inference pass.
HYPOTHESIS: The single coupled run must flip tokens versus the geometry-off rule (recorded diagnostic, expected 10-30% at level 1), raise the normalised entropy of n[l,:] toward log K, and lift Stage3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 versus the official 0.0410 baseline and the 0.0525-0.0540 behaviour band.
RISKS: The prior can dominate early and starve semantically useful codewords; the volume divisor can push heavy items onto origin-near codewords

and collapse capacity; artanh/Mobius saturation can produce NaN near the boundary; the straight-through composition lengthens the reconstruction gradient path and can slow convergence on the frozen budget; one seed may land in a bad basin; mass balance may not translate into Stage3 predictability.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only directed item-transition mass m_i = 1+log1p(outdeg_i), aggregated per RQ level into the prefix mass vector n[l,k].
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Count directed pairs (item -> next item) inside training-split sequences to get outdeg_i; at every Stage2 epoch end, encode each training item, take its current token at each level, accumulate m_i into n[l, token_l(i)], renormalise to sum 1, detached. No validation or test sequence is read.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: SID construction sees only training sequences (outdeg) and training items (encoder forward filling n). Validation/test interactions never enter m_i, n, s or the frozen assignment; n and s are frozen before the final encoding pass, which uses only an item's own features plus training-derived statistics.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c=1 in the Poincare ball (unit-free after dividing the latent by s): Mobius addition composing the codeword chain, the induced Poincare distance dP(p,q)=2artanh(||(-p)(+)q||), and the shell-volume factor sinh(sqrt(c)rho)^2/(c rho^2) at each codeword radius.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside Stage2 quantization at every RQ level: the candidate point is a Moebius composition of the chosen prefix with the codeword, the score uses the Poincare distance to that composition, and the codeword prior is divided by the shell volume at the codeword's radius. Encoder and decoder untouched.
COUPLING PATH: One scalar score per (item, level, codeword) carries both terms; the argmax emits the token, which re-anchors the next level's prefix point P_l (changing the curved distance) and rebuilds next epoch's n table (changing the prior). Removing behaviour sets prior/Vhat to 1; removing curvature sets c->0 so exp0=id, (+)=+, dP=||.||, Vhat=1.
COUPLING EQUATION: k*_l(i) = argmax_k [ -dP( p_i , P_{l-1}(i) (+) c_hat_{l,k} )^2/(2 s^2) + log( prior_{l,k}^{behaviour} / Vhat_{l,k}^{hyperbolic}(c) ) ], prior_{l,k}=(n_{l,k}+1/K)/2, Vhat normalised shell volume, P_l = P_{l-1}(+)c_hat_{l,k*}.
OBSERVABLES: Per-level token flip rate versus the geometry-off rule; normalised entropy of n[l,:]; distribution of codeword radii and per-prefix behaviour mass; fraction of items outside the ball-safe radius (must be 0); Stage3 prefix-transfer diagnostics recorded only; and Stage3 test_recall@10 as the judgement.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: prior/Vhat := 1, i.e. pure curved-composition nearest neighbour with Mobius composition and dP unchanged; n still computed but unused; flag BEHAVIOUR_ON=False, not trained this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c->0 exact limit (exp0=id, (+)=+, dP=||.||, Vhat=1), reducing the model exactly to a baseline Euclidean RQ-VAE carrying the behaviour prefix prior; flag GEOMETRY_ON=False, not trained this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: The full coupled run, BEHAVIOUR_ON=True and GEOMETRY_ON=True, c=1, prior divided by the shell volume; the only trained arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS: c_hat=exp0_c(U); rho=(2/sqrt(c))artanh(sqrt(c)||c_hat||); g=sinh(sqrt(c)rho)^2/(c rho^2) (series 1+c rho^2/3 for rho<1e-3); Vhat=g/mean_k(g); prior=(n+1/K)/2 with sum_k prior=1; v=E(x)/s; p=exp0_c(v); P_0=0; for l=1..L: Q_{l,k}=P_{l-1}(+)c_hat_{l,k} with A=1+2c<P,q>+c^2||q||^2, B=1-c||P||^2, C=1+2

c<P,q>+c^2||P||^2||q||^2 (C clamped >= eps); dist2=(4/c)artanh(sqrt(c)||(-p)(+)Q||)^2 (arg clamped <= 1-eps); score=-dist2/(2s^2)+log prior-log Vhat; k*=argmax score; P_l=Q_{l,k*} with straight-through (forward value Q, gradient into U[l,k*] and P_{l-1}); v_hat=log0_c(P_L); r_l=log0_c((-P_{l-1})(+)p) detached; Loss=mean||v-v_hat||^2+beta_c*mean||r_l/s-U[l,k*]||^2; epoch end: zero n, accumulate m_i into n[l,token_l(i)] over training items, renormalise.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: item batch x (B x d_x), U (L x K x d), scalar s, n (L x K), scalar c, switches BEHAVIOUR_ON / GEOMETRY_ON. Outputs: token ids t (B x L) int, prefix points P_l (B x d) on the ball, v_hat / z_hat, scalar loss, epoch-end n update; encoding-only call returns t and P_L.
SAFEGUARDS: divide by clamped s so ||v|| <= 8 and no point reaches the boundary; clamp artanh argument <= 1-1e-6 and Mobius denominator >= 1e-6; series for g near rho=0; init ||U|| ~ 0.1 so all c_hat start near the origin; add-one smoothing and normalised Vhat keep log terms finite and O(1) and handle empty codewords; on any non-finite score/distance/loss the batch falls back to the Euclidean branch and is logged; n is detached, renormalised, never back-propagated; s frozen at Stage2 end for a deterministic encoding pass; both switches are single flags read at the top of the score function so the off-paths are the exact stated limits.
END IDEA
