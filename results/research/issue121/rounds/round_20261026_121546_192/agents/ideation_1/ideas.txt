IDEA: successor-prefix-geodesic
TITLE: Successor-pair contrastive learning on the actual two-level quantized prefix
MOTIVATION: Content reconstruction can preserve item identity while leaving next-item-compatible items in unrelated coarse branches; train-only adjacent transitions should shape the partition directly.
NOVELTY: Unlike assignment-cost reweighting and prototype/residual successor heuristics already present, this adds a pairwise objective over the actual first-two-level quantized representation and updates both encoder and codebooks.
CHANGE: Map the cumulative T1 and T2 quantized sums to a Poincare ball, and use a symmetric transition contrastive loss there. Use train-only branching entropy to set negative curvature, not as a post-hoc assignment multiplier.
SPECIFICATION: For each item i, aggregate directed adjacent training transition counts n_ij. Sample one positive j proportional to n_ij^.75 and M=8 non-neighbor negatives k. Let q_i^(2)=e_i1+e_i2 (straight-through quantized cumulative sum); u_i=exp_0(q_i^(2)); minimize InfoNCE over -d_{c_i}(u_i,u_j)/tau against negatives, with reciprocal direction when observed. c_i=clip(c0*(1+H_i/log(1+deg_i)),cmin,cmax), c0=1, cmin=.25,cmax=2; H_i is smoothed outgoing entropy. Add lambda=0.05 times loss to unchanged RQ-VAE objective. Capacity remains baseline K=(256,256,256,256), four tokens and deterministic collision extension; no capacity change.
COST: Precompute sparse train transition rows once. At each step batch size B, 9B positive/negative geodesic pairs and backward; O(9Bd) plus baseline quantization, expected <0.13 s/epoch smoke target using vectorized batched distances. Frozen scale: 24 steps/epoch x 3000 = 72,000 steps; about 648B pair distances per epoch and 1,944,000B across training (B is global batch), negligible versus encoder if fused/vectorized; verify one-epoch timing.
HYPOTHESIS: If directed transitions induce a useful coarse partition, L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives should rise, gold-prefix survival should remain >.95 at depth1 and >.8 at depth2, and Stage3 test_R@10 should exceed baseline .055537 and ideally .065. Record T1..T4 teacher-forced top-1: T1/T2 should improve or remain stable, deeper positions may trade off slightly; measure each rather than assuming.
RISKS: Noisy or one-way transitions may collapse popular hubs; the T2 cumulative vector can be dominated by Euclidean residual scale; pair sampling may underrepresent low-frequency items; hyperbolic distances can saturate near the boundary.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed immediate successor item pairs with count-weighted sampling.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training split only, sort each user's training events by timestamp and count consecutive distinct item pairs (i,j); smooth counts by +1 only on observed outgoing edges, normalize row probabilities, and compute entropy H_i. Never use validation/test rows.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Build pair table and H_i solely from the training interaction file; map only training item IDs, freeze table before Stage2; validation/test events are unavailable to SID trainer and never enter negative exclusions or statistics.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exp map and geodesic distance d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c) atanh(sqrt(c)||(-x)⊕_c y||), with negative sectional curvature -c_i determined by train-only H_i.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Contrastive loss on straight-through T1+T2 cumulative quantized vectors during every RQ-VAE minibatch; gradients pass through exp map and quantized straight-through path to encoder and T1/T2 codebooks.
COUPLING PATH: Training successor pair sampling selects (i,j); the actual first-two-level quantized vectors for both are mapped into curvature c_i and their geodesic distance enters the positive logit, directly changing encoder/codebook gradients and therefore assignments.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_pair(i,j,N)= -log[ exp(-d_{c_i}(u_i,u_j)/tau)/(exp(-d_{c_i}(u_i,u_j)/tau)+Σ_{k∈N_i}exp(-d_{c_i}(u_i,u_k)/tau)) ]; u_i=exp_0(e_i1+e_i2), c_i=clip(c0(1+H_i/log(1+deg_i)),cmin,cmax).
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives (predicted upward); gold-prefix survival depths 1,2,3,4 (up, up, roughly unchanged/down slightly, potentially down); teacher-forced top-1 accuracy T1,T2,T3,T4 (T1/T2 up or stable, T3/T4 monitored); Stage3 test_R@10 expected >.055537, target >.065. Also verify nonzero encoder gradient on real smoke batch and changed T1/T2 assignments.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Shuffle positive successor destinations within the training item set, preserve batch/degree and curvature computation, and train identically; tests whether meaningful transition pairs matter.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep identical pairs, code path and curvature-derived scalar but replace Poincare geodesic distance with Euclidean norm on the same vectors; tests metric necessity.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Real directed training successor pairs with H_i-derived negative curvature and Poincare geodesic contrastive loss.
SPEC EQUATIONS: q_i2=ST(e_i1+e_i2); u_i=exp_0^c_i(q_i2); s(i,j)=-d_c_i(u_i,u_j)/tau; L_b=-log softmax([s(i,j+),s(i,k1-),...,s(i,k8-)])_0. L=L_RQVAE+0.05*mean(L_b). K=(256,256,256,256). Train-only H_i=-Σ_j p_ij log p_ij.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: batch item IDs, encoder embeddings, four-level codebook outputs, frozen sparse training successor table and per-item H_i. Output: scalar augmented loss and normal four-token SID assignments.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute distances in fp32; clamp ball norms to (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c), clamp atanh argument to 1-1e-6; use stable logsumexp; exclude self and observed adjacent items from negatives; fallback to Euclidean-safe zero loss for rows lacking positive; clip curvature and loss gradients; ensure ST path gives nonzero encoder gradient at initialization.
END IDEA

IDEA: cohort-target-lorentz-prefix
TITLE: Shared-successor cohort contrast in Lorentz space with prefix-balanced sampling
MOTIVATION: Items consumed as next targets by the same user-history cohorts are substitutable recommendation outcomes even when exact directed pair counts are sparse; putting these outcomes in common coarse prefixes should keep generative next-token prediction economical.
NOVELTY: This uses destination-side cohort overlap rather than the already explored anchor-successor residual/prototype/assignment mechanisms, and applies contrastive gradients to the quantized T1+T2 representation itself in Lorentz geometry.
CHANGE: Construct positive pairs from distinct items that are next targets of overlapping training context cohorts, then optimize a Lorentz geodesic supervised contrastive loss on their actual cumulative two-level quantized vectors. Keep baseline codebook capacities K=(256,256,256,256), so only partition learning changes.
SPECIFICATION: For each training event at t, define context cohort g as the unordered set of up to the previous 3 item IDs in that user's training history. Accumulate m_ig=1 when i is the next target after context g; for item i choose positive j≠i with Jaccard-weighted shared cohort A_ij=Σ_g min(m_ig,m_jg)/Σ_g max(m_ig,m_jg) >0. Sample negatives with disjoint cohorts. Let global train branching B=mean_g log(1+number distinct next items after g); curvature c=clip(0.5+B,0.25,2). Map q_i=e_i1+e_i2 by Lorentz exp at origin and minimize logsumexp geodesic contrastive loss, lambda=.05. No reweighting of any code assignment cost.
COST: Build inverted cohort lists once over training events (linear in events times context length); per minibatch sample one positive/eight negatives and evaluate 9B Lorentz distances, O(9Bd). At frozen scale 24 steps/epoch x 3000=72,000 updates; 648B pair distances per epoch and 1,944,000B total. Sparse lists need O(number train events) memory; smoke target remains near 0.13 s/epoch with vectorized kernels.
HYPOTHESIS: Shared context predicts target substitutability and therefore helps prefix-token prediction; L1 Hard ER should increase, gold-prefix survival target >.95 depth1/>.8 depth2 and be measured through depth4, T1/T2 teacher-forced top-1 should improve without a damaging T3/T4 loss, and Stage3 test_R@10 should beat .055537 toward .065.
RISKS: Context-window overlap could encode popularity rather than intent; context order loss may make false positives; large cohort lists are expensive; globally fixed curvature may inadequately fit local branching; top-1 improvements may not translate to beam Recall@10.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only shared-context next-target overlap, defining positive items that follow the same three-item context cohorts.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: Within each user's training history sorted by timestamp, emit (previous up-to-three item set, next item); build sparse item-to-context incidence sets. Positive sampling uses weighted Jaccard overlap; negatives have zero overlap. All counts derive from training split only.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Context cohorts are emitted only from training histories. Do not concatenate validation/test events to construct contexts; do not use validation/test targets to label positives, negatives, branching B, or select parameters. Stage3 test remains evaluation only.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz hyperboloid exponential map and geodesic distance with curvature -c: d_L(x,y)=acosh(max(1,-c⟨x,y⟩_L))/sqrt(c).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Pairwise supervised contrastive term over the straight-through T1+T2 cumulative quantized vectors during Stage2; gradients reach encoder and first two codebooks, not a detached auxiliary embedding.
COUPLING PATH: Shared-context incidence chooses each positive/negative pair; each pair's same T1+T2 quantized vectors are mapped to the Lorentz hyperboloid, and their Lorentz geodesic logits drive gradients that alter coarse assignments.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_cohort(i)= -log[exp(-d_L(z_i,z_j+)/τ)/(exp(-d_L(z_i,z_j+)/τ)+Σ_k exp(-d_L(z_i,z_k-)/τ))], where A_ij=Σ_g min(m_ig,m_jg)/Σ_g max(m_ig,m_jg), j+ sampled ∝ A_ij, z_i=exp^L_0(e_i1+e_i2), c=clip(.5+B,.25,2).
OBSERVABLES: Predict upward L1 Hard ER (canonical 52,855 pairs/ten fixed negatives); gold-prefix survival upward at depths 1 and 2 (targets >.95 and >.8), depth3/4 must be reported; teacher-forced top-1 T1/T2 upward or stable and T3/T4 tracked; test_R@10 should exceed .055537 and target .065. Inspect actual T1/T2 item assignments and real-batch encoder gradient.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Randomly permute item-to-context incidence while retaining context sizes and global B, producing non-semantic positives; same Lorentz loss/training budget.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Preserve pair selection and all parameters but calculate logits with Euclidean distance on q_i; only geometric operator changes.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Actual training context-overlap positives/zero-overlap negatives and Lorentz geodesic contrast on the two-level quantized representation.
SPEC EQUATIONS: m_ig∈{0,1}; A_ij=Σ_g min(m_ig,m_jg)/max(1,Σ_g max(m_ig,m_jg)); p(j|i)=A_ij/Σ_l A_il. q_i2=ST(e_i1+e_i2); z_i=exp^L_0(q_i2;c). L=L_RQVAE+λ mean_i[-s(i,j+)+logsumexp(s(i,j+),s(i,k-))], s=-d_L/τ, λ=.05. Capacity K=(256,256,256,256).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs are training item IDs, timestamped training-only user sequences, batch item IDs, encoder outputs and T1/T2 quantized embeddings. Outputs are augmented scalar loss, updated encoder/codebooks, and baseline-format four-token SIDs.
SAFEGUARDS: Cap each context incidence list at deterministic max 256 targets for sampling, but retain sparse overlap computation; require at least one positive or omit that anchor's contrastive term; exclude self/positive from negatives; compute Lorentz inner products in fp32 and clamp acosh argument ≥1+1e-6; normalize/project points to valid upper sheet, cap tangent norm and curvature; use stable logsumexp and gradient clipping; smoke-test nonzero encoder gradient and timing.
END IDEA
