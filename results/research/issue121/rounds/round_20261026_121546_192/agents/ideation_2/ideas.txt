IDEA: branch_scaled_transition_nce
TITLE: Branch-scaled directed transition contrast on the quantizer's curved prefix
MOTIVATION: Items that lead to the same next-step choices should share cheap early SID prefixes, while an item's outgoing alternatives should not be collapsed indiscriminately; this targets gold-prefix survival and next-item recall rather than bucket balance.
NOVELTY: Unlike successor-weighted assignment or successor residual prototypes, this trains the actual quantized prefix representation with a pairwise geodesic contrastive gradient. Curvature is set by observed training-only branching and changes the contrastive geometry itself.
CHANGE: Build positives from directed adjacent training events, encode both endpoints through the same RQ-VAE and first-two-level quantizer, and minimize sampled geodesic InfoNCE on their soft quantized prefix points. Use fixed capacities [256, 256, 256, 256] and retain the deterministic collision-extension token; do not alter assignment costs or capacities.
SPECIFICATION: For item i, let y_i^(1:2) be the differentiable soft prefix point formed from its encoder output and the first two codebooks (details below). Estimate b_i=H(next|i) from training transitions, and set shared curvature c=clip(c0/(1+mean_i b_i), 0.05, 2). For a directed training pair (i,j), L_i=-log[ exp(-d_c(y_i,y_j)/tau) / (exp(-d_c(y_i,y_j)/tau)+sum_n exp(-d_c(y_i,y_n)/tau)) ]. Minimize reconstruction + quantization + lambda L; sample 1 positive and 8 negatives/item in each step. Negatives are training items with no observed directed adjacency to anchor i.
COST: One extra encoder-side pairwise term with 9 distance evaluations per sampled anchor; use one sampled anchor minibatch (not all 24,556 squared pairs). Expected under 0.13 s/epoch baseline if pair gathering is precomputed and vectorized; conservatively budget +5–10% epoch time, about 0.137–0.143 s/epoch, or +21–39 minutes across 3,000 epochs. Four cards; no additional codebook capacity.
HYPOTHESIS: Worth keeping only if behavior-on curved training creates behaviorally coherent early prefixes and Stage3 test_R@10 exceeds 0.055537, ideally >0.065. Predict L1 Hard ER decreases (more behaviorally useful shared prefixes), gold-prefix survival rises at depths 1 and 2 to >0.95 and >0.80 respectively, with depths 3–4 neutral or mildly lower from early-prefix prioritization; teacher-forced T1/T2 accuracy rises or holds and T3/T4 holds or slightly falls; test_R@10 rises. Log all these against baseline and required ablations; ER direction is a hypothesis, not an admission criterion.
RISKS: Directed adjacency may be sparse/noisy and sampled non-neighbours can be false negatives; large lambda may sacrifice item reconstruction or cause prefix collapse. Branch entropy may estimate popularity/context mixture poorly. Soft prefix gradients can blur hard assignments, so inspect hard-ID metrics and gradient norms.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item-to-next-item transitions in training histories, with per-source successor entropy for curvature.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From Stage2 training split only, count adjacent (i,j) events per user history; aggregate counts n_ij and n_i, retain observed pairs as positives, and compute b_i=-sum_j p(j|i)log p(j|i), p(j|i)=n_ij/n_i. Exclude self-transition if it is an artifact of preprocessing. Freeze this table before Stage2 optimization.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Construct transition counts only from the Stage2 training histories; validation/test histories are never loaded by this table builder or objective. Stage3 test is used only for the frozen final evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance d_c at negative curvature -c, c=clip(c0/(1+mean training successor entropy), 0.05, 2.0); use Mobius addition and the standard arcosh distance.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Contrastive loss on the same curved soft-quantized two-level prefix representation that supplies T1/T2 hard code assignments, during Stage2 training.
COUPLING PATH: Training adjacency chooses positive/negative pairs and training branching sets c; geodesic loss on shared encoder plus first-two-codebook prefix points backpropagates through soft assignments to encoder and both codebooks, changing the partition.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_pair(i,j,N)=-log { exp[-d_c(y_i^(1:2),y_j^(1:2))/tau] / [exp(-d_c(y_i^(1:2),y_j^(1:2))/tau)+sum_{n in N}exp(-d_c(y_i^(1:2),y_n^(1:2))/tau)] }; (i,j) comes from training successor edges, c=c0/(1+mean b_i).
OBSERVABLES: Report L1 Hard ER on canonical 52,855 pairs with ten fixed negatives, gold-prefix survival at depths 1–4, teacher-forced top-1 T1–T4 accuracy, encoder/T1/T2-codebook gradient norms, and frozen Stage3 test_R@10. Expected direction: ER down; survival at depths 1–2 up, depths 3–4 neutral/slightly down; T1/T2 accuracy up or stable, T3/T4 stable or slightly down; R@10 up versus 0.055537, target >0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Shuffle positive successor destinations among training items while preserving pair count and curvature calculation; contrastive pathway remains, but transition identity signal is destroyed. Compare all observables.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep identical pairs, sampling, curvature-derived scale and optimization, but replace d_c with Euclidean distance between the same prefix vectors. Compare all observables.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use true training-directed pairs and Poincare geodesic distance with branching-derived c; fixed four-token capacity and frozen protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: At each assignment, soft weights a_lk=softmax(-||u_l-e_lk||^2/s) for l=1,2 (use existing hard forward assignments with soft backward/straight-through); define y_i=Proj_c(e_i + sum_k a_1k q_1k + sum_k a_2k q_2k), where encoder e_i and q_lk are mapped consistently into the ball. Evaluate d_c(y_i,y_j) using Poincare distance. L_total=L_RQ-VAE+lambda*mean_i L_i. Do not detach y or codebooks. Keep hard argmin assignments and deterministic extension for emitted SIDs.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: minibatch item IDs, their training successor pair(s), sampled non-adjacent negatives, encoder outputs, first-two-level codebooks, frozen training counts/entropies. Output: scalar pair loss and gradients to encoder and first two codebooks; normal hard four-token SID output is unchanged in format.
SAFEGUARDS: Project all ball points to norm <=(1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp arcosh argument to >=1+1e-7 and distance denominator/norms; compute distances and logsumexp in float32; clip loss gradients; cap c and temperature, exclude invalid/unseen edges, sample negatives without replacement where possible, and verify nonzero encoder and both codebook gradients on a real smoke batch.
END IDEA

IDEA: cohort_successor_curved_nce
TITLE: Shared-destination cohort contrast in a train-branching hyperbolic prefix space
MOTIVATION: A next-item model benefits when items that serve the same successor cohort become nearby at early SID levels, even if their own content differs; this directly reshapes which items share prefixes instead of changing level occupancy.
NOVELTY: This uses a target-cohort relation (two distinct sources pointing to the same training successor set) rather than direct successor prototype attraction. The cohort relation defines contrastive pairs, while a branching-conditioned Poincare geodesic on the quantizer's shared prefix representation supplies the optimizing metric; it is not a separate behavior loss or assignment reweighting.
CHANGE: Derive source-item positive pairs from overlap of their training successor distributions, then optimize a symmetric geodesic contrastive loss on their differentiable first-two-level quantized prefix points. Keep the four codebooks at the baseline capacity [256, 256, 256, 256], with the existing deterministic collision-extension token.
SPECIFICATION: Let P_i(j)=n_ij/n_i from training adjacent transitions. Define s_ij=cosine(P_i,P_j) over the union of observed successors, retaining top-one positive j per anchor when s_ij>=0.2. Define branching B as mean training successor entropy and c=clip(c0*(1+B)/(1+B_ref),0.05,2). For positive pair (i,j), L_i=-log exp(-d_c(y_i,y_j)/tau) / sum_{k in {j}+N_i} exp(-d_c(y_i,y_k)/tau), where negatives N_i have s_ik=0 and are training items. Minimize this with standard RQ-VAE losses.
COST: Precompute sparse successor distributions and top-positive lists once; per epoch compute 1 positive and 8 negative Poincare distances per anchor using the existing minibatch prefix path. Estimated +5–10% over 0.13 s/epoch (0.137–0.143 s/epoch; +21–39 minutes for 3,000 epochs), assuming sparse overlap preprocessing is excluded from epoch time. Memory is sparse edge counts plus one positive per item; capacity unchanged.
HYPOTHESIS: Shared successor cohorts indicate functional interchangeability useful for next-item prediction. Keep only if the full run improves frozen test_R@10 above 0.055537 toward >0.065, accompanied by higher depth-1/2 gold-prefix survival (>0.95/>0.80) and nonzero gradients into encoder and T1/T2. Predicted L1 Hard ER decreases; survival depth 1/2 rises, depths 3/4 stable or slightly lower; teacher-forced top-1 T1/T2 rises or holds while T3/T4 holds or slightly decreases. Validate these directions empirically in behavior-off and Euclidean controls.
RISKS: Common popular successors can make unrelated sources appear similar; sparse overlap misses meaningful cohorts; the 0.2 threshold may require tuning on training-only statistics. This can over-cluster common intents, harm reconstruction, or make hard prefixes disagree with soft contrastive geometry.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Training-only co-consumption of next-item distributions: similarity between source items based on their observed directed successor sets.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From Stage2 training histories only, count directed adjacent transitions n_ij. Form sparse row-normalized P_i(j), then compute cosine overlap of rows on shared successor IDs. Select one highest-overlap positive per source above a fixed 0.2 threshold; negatives are items with zero successor overlap. No validation/test events are inputs.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: All transition matrices, row supports, overlap scores, and pair lists are generated from the Stage2 training split and frozen before training. No validation or test interaction participates in pair mining, curvature, SID construction, or tuning; test is reserved for final Stage3 measurement.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare-ball geodesic distance at negative curvature -c, where c is explicitly scaled by mean training successor branching entropy and clipped to [0.05,2].
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Pairwise contrastive term during Stage2, on the differentiable soft-prefix vectors derived from the same encoder and first two RQ-VAE codebooks used for hard T1/T2 assignment.
COUPLING PATH: Training successor-row overlap chooses the positive cohort pairs and training branching determines geodesic curvature; the resulting geodesic NCE gradients flow through the shared soft-quantized prefix and its first two codebooks, changing hard item neighborhoods and prefixes.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_i=-log {exp[-d_c(y_i,y_j)/tau] / sum_{k in {j}+N_i}exp[-d_c(y_i,y_k)/tau]}, with positive j selected by cosine(P_i,P_j) and c=clip(c0*(1+B)/(1+B_ref),0.05,2).
OBSERVABLES: Measure L1 Hard ER (canonical 52,855 pairs, ten fixed negatives), gold-prefix survival depths 1–4, teacher-forced top-1 accuracy T1–T4, encoder/T1/T2 codebook gradient norms, and Stage3 test_R@10. Hypothesis: ER decreases; survival at depths 1 and 2 increases past 0.95 and 0.80; depth 3/4 stays similar or declines slightly; T1/T2 accuracy holds/improves, T3/T4 holds; test_R@10 increases over 0.055537 with >0.065 as the target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Permute successor-row supports across source item IDs before overlap mining, preserving sparsity/counts approximately but destroying source-cohort identity; keep geodesic and training-derived curvature procedure. Compare all required measurements.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Keep mined pairs and all sampling fixed, replace Poincare d_c with Euclidean distance of identical prefix vectors. Compare all measurements.
ABLATION BOTH ON: True training successor-row overlap pairs and branching-scaled Poincare geodesic contrastive loss; baseline capacities and frozen 3000-epoch/four-card Stage2 plus frozen Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Build sparse P_i(j)=n_ij/(sum_r n_ir). For each anchor, j=argmax_{k!=i} cosine(P_i,P_k) if score>=0.2; N_i are eight sampled k with cosine=0. For curvature use B=mean_i[-sum_j P_i(j)log(P_i(j))], c=clip(c0*(1+B)/(1+B_ref),0.05,2). Let a_l=softmax(-dist(u,q_l)^2/s) for l=1,2, with straight-through hard forward/soft backward; y_i=Proj_c(e_i+sum_l sum_m a_lm q_lm). Optimize L_RQ-VAE+lambda*mean_i L_i with d_c(y_i,y_k). Hard quantizer output and collision extension remain as baseline.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training-only sparse transition counts, source minibatch IDs and cohort positive/negative IDs, encoder vectors, first-two-level codebook vectors. Outputs: scalar contrastive term, gradients into shared encoder and both codebooks, and unchanged-format hard four-position SID assignment.
SAFEGUARDS: Sparse computation only; discard empty successor rows and self-pairs; threshold positive similarity and sample only zero-overlap negatives; clip curvature and temperature; project points strictly inside radius (1-1e-5)/sqrt(c); clamp Poincare arcosh inputs and norms, calculate distance/logsumexp in float32, gradient-clip, and check real-batch encoder plus both early-codebook gradients are nonzero. Monitor positive-pair count and popularity concentration; do not use validation/test to adjust threshold.
END IDEA
