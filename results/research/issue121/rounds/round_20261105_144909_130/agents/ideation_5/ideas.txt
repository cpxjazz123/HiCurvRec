IDEA: GEOMARGIN-L1-RQ
TITLE: Behaviour-shifted hyperbolic Voronoi bisectors for the level-1 RQ-VAE Semantic ID: a training-only transition-demand margin added to the Poincare distance, with a scale-derived weight
MOTIVATION: The level-1 codeword decides the first SID token and hence the whole prefix, yet the baseline selects it by Euclidean nearest-anchor. Two items whose successors are interchangeable but whose embeddings sit on opposite sides of a bisector therefore get different prefixes. Behavioural co-transition information exists at training time but never enters the assignment, and the partition that induces the SID tree is flat although the tree it induces is hierarchical.
NOVELTY: Prior arms moved curvature per code (OCCURV-FIELD), per stratum with Sinkhorn (strataot-curv-rq), radially per branch (brancurv-l1-rq), or along a parent/anchor ladder. Here the curvature is one fixed c and behaviour enters as a displacement of the level-1 geodesic Voronoi bisectors measured in hyperbolic distance units: behaviour-shifted half-spaces, not a per-code curvature field, not Sinkhorn, not radial coordinates, not capacity units, not cross-graph transfer. Extra distinctness: the behaviour term is zero-mean so behaviour-off is exactly the baseline argmin, and the margin weight is derived from the measured distance scale rather than chosen.
CHANGE: The level-1 residual is lifted into the Poincare ball and compared to lifted baseline anchors with d_c, and the level-1 argmin subtracts a frozen zero-mean log-demand margin whose maximum equals 10% of the typical level-1 geodesic coding distance; levels 2-3, all losses and the straight-through estimator are unchanged.
SPECIFICATION: dims: M = baseline latent dim (32), K1 = baseline level-1 codebook size (256), 3 levels as baseline. Forward: b = encoder(x); level-1 residual r = b; s = RMS of ||b|| from a frozen training pre-pass; u = exp_0^c(r/s); q_k = exp_0^c(a_k) with a_k the baseline level-1 codebook (same parameter tensor, no new quantizer parameters); D_k = d_c(u,q_k); z_k = frozen zero-mean log-demand of code k; w = theta * Dbar / rms_k(z_k), theta = 0.1 documented default; k1 = argmin_k (D_k - w*z_k) with ties to the smallest index; reconstruction and gradients exactly as baseline with the selected a_{k1}. z, w, s are buffers with requires_grad=False. Levels 2-3 identical to baseline (Euclidean, same residual recursion, commitment and codebook losses). gradient_rules: dL/da_{k1} and dL/db are the baseline straight-through expressions because the selection index is a constant in the graph; no gradient enters z, w or s; d(d_c)/du is autodiffed through the Mobius map and arctanh. training_integration: one frozen pre-pass over training data computes s, z and Dbar from the baseline level-1 assignments and the training-split transitions; these are stored as buffers, then the unchanged two-stage protocol runs with only the level-1 argmin modified. conventions copied verbatim from HYPERBOLIC_GEOMETRY_CONTRACT: ball curvature kappa=-c, c>0, R_c=1/sqrt(c), d_c(x,y)=(2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x)(+)y||), exp_0^c(v)=tanh(sqrt(c)||v||)*v/(sqrt(c)||v||), the Euclidean tangent-coordinate norm is not the Riemannian norm.
COST: One-time pre-pass: about 24556 latents plus |training transitions| ~ O(1e6) level-1 lookups, seconds to under a minute, <0.5% of the run. Per step at the frozen scale (24556 training items, batch about 1024, 24 steps per epoch, 3000 epochs = 72000 steps): one exp map per sample (M ops), K1 = 256 exp maps for the anchors (cached per step), and 256 Mobius additions plus arctanh per sample, roughly 1.7e8 flops per step, i.e. +3-6% of the quantizer block and about +1-3% of step wall clock; zero extra parameters, no extra optimiser state, under 50 MB extra memory; about +2% total wall clock per run.
HYPOTHESIS: If transition demand carries prefix-predictive information that the Euclidean level-1 partition ignores, the demand-scaled geodesic margin reallocates a modest fraction (order 5-15%) of level-1 assignments toward transition-central codes and lifts Stage3 test recall@10 above 0.065 (baseline 0.0555, best so far 0.0638). It would be observed as (i) a level-1 usage-histogram shift positively correlated with z_k, (ii) higher conditional log-likelihood of the next SID token given the level-1 prefix on held-out prefixes, (iii) the recall gain. If the histogram does not move, the margin is too small relative to the geodesic distance spread and the hypothesis fails in the single run.
RISKS: c and theta=0.1 are fixed (no sweep permitted), so the margin may be too weak to change any argmin, or strong enough to collapse level-1 usage onto hub codes; artanh saturation near the ball boundary makes d_c numerically stiff and can let geometry noise dominate the margin; degree-normalised demand may be too smooth to differentiate codes, especially if level-1 usage is already saturated and no headroom exists; the frozen pre-pass assignments can become stale relative to the moving encoder late in training; a real gain in prefix predictability may still be too small to push Stage3 recall above 0.065 under single-seed noise
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed training-set item-to-item transition mass projected onto level-1 codewords (the behaviour demand of each level-1 code), popularity-adjusted by the successor degree.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the TRAINING split only: for every observed transition (i -> j) in the training sequences, take the level-1 assignment k1(j) produced by the frozen pre-pass encoder; accumulate demand_k = sum over (i->j) of deg(j)^(-1/2) * 1[k1(j)=k]; divide by a scalar Z so that mean_k demand_k = 1; then z_k = log(1+demand_k) - mean_m log(1+demand_m). One pass, frozen as a buffer, never back-propagated.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: The transition graph, the successor degrees, s and Dbar are computed exclusively from the training split. Validation and test interactions are never read, never counted and never used to fit any buffer; z, s and w are frozen before Stage 2 training starts, so no optimisation step can see validation or test signal, and SID construction uses no test-time popularity or co-occurrence statistic.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature kappa=-c with c>0 (c=1 default, so R_c=1); the operations are exp_0^c, Mobius addition, and the Poincare distance d_c(x,y)=(2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x)(+)y||).
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside the level-1 assignment step of the RQ-VAE quantizer, during Stage 2 training and unchanged at inference: the distance that decides the first SID token.
COUPLING PATH: One argmin, k1 = argmin_k [ d_c(u, exp_0^c(a_k)) - w*z_k ] with u = exp_0^c(r/s). The behaviour term z_k is added to the geodesic distance inside the same scalar comparison, so removing behaviour collapses the comparison to baseline nearest-anchor, and removing curvature replaces d_c by the flat distance and also changes the scale of the same margin because w is derived from Dbar; the selected index and therefore the straight-through gradient path change in both ablations.
COUPLING EQUATION: k1 = argmin_k [ (2/sqrt(c))*artanh(sqrt(c)*|| (-exp_0^c(r/s)) (+) exp_0^c(a_k) ||) - w * ( log(1+demand_k) - mean_m log(1+demand_m) ) ] with w = 0.1 * mean_k d_c / rms_k(z_k)
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 codeword usage histogram and its correlation with z_k; mean level-1 geodesic residual radius; fraction of samples whose level-1 index differs from the plain nearest-anchor choice; prefix conditional log-likelihood of the next SID token given the level-1 prefix on held-out prefixes; Stage3 test recall@10 (judgement only).
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: z_k := 0 for all k (equivalently demand_k := 1 and off_k := 0), so the level-1 argmin is exactly the pure geodesic nearest-anchor; the switch exists in code as BEHAVIOUR_ON=False and is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: c := 0 path: exp_0^c becomes the identity, d_c becomes 2*||u-q_k|| (Euclidean), and Dbar is recomputed in the same flat units so w stays scale-consistent, everything else identical; the switch exists in code as GEOMETRY_ON=False and is not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Behaviour margin and curvature both active, which is the single run of this round.
SPEC EQUATIONS: s = sqrt(mean_{x in T} ||b(x)||^2) u = tanh(sqrt(c)*||r/s||) * (r/s) / (sqrt(c)*||r/s||), with u = 0 when ||r/s|| = 0 q_k = exp_0^c(a_k) M(x,y) = ((1+2c<x,y>+c||y||^2) x + (1-c||x||^2) y) / (1+2c<x,y>+c^2||x||^2||y||^2) D_k = (2/sqrt(c))*artanh( clip( sqrt(c)*||M(-u,q_k)||, 0, 1-1e-6 ) ) demand_k = (1/Z) * sum_{(i->j) in T} deg(j)^(-1/2) * 1[k1(j)=k], Z such that mean_k demand_k = 1 z_k = log1p(demand_k) - mean_m log1p(demand_m) w = theta * Dbar / rms(z), theta = 0.1 k1 = argmin_k (D_k - w*z_k), ties to the smallest index r2 = r - sg(a_{k1}) ; levels 2-3 recursion, commitment and codebook losses unchanged from baseline STE: gradient to a_{k1} and to r as baseline, exactly zero gradient to z, w, s
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: batch embeddings b (B x M), baseline level-1 codebook a (K1 x M), curvature c, frozen buffers s (scalar), z (K1), w (scalar), and the unchanged levels 2-3. Outputs: level-1 index k1 (B,), the quantized level-1 vector a_{k1} (B x M) feeding the unchanged residual recursion, plus per-sample D and margin as diagnostics. float32 in training, float64 only in the frozen pre-pass.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp the radial argument sqrt(c)||v|| <= 10 before tanh; clamp the arctanh argument to 1-1e-6; clamp ||a_k|| <= 0.999*R_c and ||u|| <= 0.999*R_c; handle u == q_k with D = 0 and a 1e-9 floor under the square root and arctanh; z is zero-mean and RMS-normalised so w is finite, with rms(z) < 1e-8 mapping to w = 0; deterministic tie-break to the smallest index for reproducibility; demand is positive degree-normalised mass and log1p is used, so no log of a non-positive value; frozen buffers are stored outside the optimiser and asserted to have requires_grad=False; before training, assert that the geometry-off path reproduces 2*||u-q|| exactly and that the behaviour-off path reproduces the baseline nearest-anchor indices on a fixed batch.
END IDEA
