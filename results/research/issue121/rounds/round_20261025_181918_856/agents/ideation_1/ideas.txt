IDEA: successor_entropy_curvature
TITLE: Successor-entropy-conditioned curvature for prefix assignment
MOTIVATION: Items with a single reliable continuation and items that branch into several plausible continuations need different quantization geometry: one shared global curvature can either over-compress branching items or fragment predictable chains.
NOVELTY: Existing candidates use a transition direction/Busemann score, a behavior-driven latent warp, branch-dependent radius, or behavior-edge contrastive loss. This instead makes training-only successor uncertainty determine the negative curvature used in the actual first-stage codeword ranking; no behavior suffix or separate auxiliary objective is added.
CHANGE: Estimate each item's smoothed next-item entropy from training transitions, map it to a bounded item-specific curvature magnitude, and use that curvature in the Poincare geodesic distance between its encoded point and each first-level codeword. The selected first SID token and quantizer gradients therefore depend jointly on branching behavior and curved geometry.
SPECIFICATION: Count adjacent transitions n_ij within train users' chronologically ordered train events. Let q_ij=(n_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(n_i+alpha), with pi the train next-item frequency, and H_i=-sum_j q_ij log(q_ij+eps)/log(max(2,N)). Set c_i=clamp(c_min+(c_max-c_min)*H_i, c_min,c_max), e.g. c_min=0.1,c_max=1.0. For encoder z_i and codeword a_k, map x_i=exp^{c_i}_0(z_i), y_ik=exp^{c_i}_0(a_k), assign k*=argmin_k d_{c_i}(x_i,y_ik)^2, where d_c(x,y)=2/sqrt(c)*atanh(sqrt(c)*||(-x)⊕_c y||), and train standard reconstruction/VQ commitment losses through the chosen curved quantizer. Use the same c_i for all candidates of item i; later residual levels may remain baseline. Pre-score evidence probe on train only: compare held-out-within-train next-item top-k predictability and branching-bin prefix purity for high- versus low-H items; the hypothesis requires high-H items to have more distinct useful continuation prefixes and expects high-H bin prefix successor coverage to rise under this assignment.
COST: One O(E_train) transition-count preprocessing pass and O(N) entropy/cache storage. Per quantizer comparison cost is still O(B*K*D), with per-item curvature scalar/map overhead O(B*K*D) and no pair sampling; expect a small constant-factor increase over baseline assignment. At frozen scale, N=24,556 items, 24 steps/epoch, 3,000 epochs = 72,000 optimizer steps and 73,668,000 item presentations; cache lookup adds one scalar per item and curvature-specific distance adds approximately one O(D) computation per item-codeword pair, or about 73,668,000*K*D coordinate operations over all presentations. Measure wall time against matched arms.
HYPOTHESIS: Train-only successor entropy is a useful proxy for the local branching structure the tokenizer should preserve. Required pre-score statistic is the entropy-stratified next-item top-k coverage/prefix predictability computed by holding out a portion of training transitions (never validation/test); expect higher-entropy items to show greater continuation diversity, and the coupled model to improve their train-held-out successor coverage without reducing low-entropy chain predictability. Keep only if both-on improves Stage3 test Recall@10 over both controls and exceeds 0.065.
RISKS: Entropy may mostly encode sparse-count noise or popularity; smoothed q may flatten real branches. Item-specific curvature can create inconsistent distance scales across items, and per-item curvature might shift assignment without useful semantic grouping. Curvature bounds, count shrinkage, and a training-only matched-distance-scale calibration are needed.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Per-item uncertainty of the directed next-item distribution (successor branching entropy).
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From training split only, sort each user's training events by timestamp and count adjacent directed pairs i->j without crossing user/split boundaries. Build smoothed q_ij from those counts and train-only next-item marginal pi_j; compute normalized entropy H_i. Cache and freeze the result before SID construction; no validation or test event participates.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: Only train user sequences, train item frequencies, and the frozen item embedding/ID inventory are inputs to n_ij, pi, q_ij, and H_i. Hyperparameters and curvature bounds are fixed or selected using train-only diagnostics; validation/test interactions are inaccessible to preprocessing, fitting, and SID assignment, and are used only for the prescribed downstream evaluation.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Poincare exponential map and geodesic distance with item-conditioned fixed negative curvature -c_i, with c_i bounded in [0.1,1.0].
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: First residual quantizer's candidate-codeword assignment and corresponding straight-through/commitment path; the emitted first SID token changes directly.
COUPLING PATH: Train-only outgoing transition counts -> normalized successor entropy H_i -> curvature c_i -> curvature-specific exp maps and geodesic distances for item i against every first-level codeword -> argmin assignment and quantizer gradients -> SID prefix.
COUPLING EQUATION: c_i=clip(c_min+(c_max-c_min)H_i,c_min,c_max); k_i=argmin_k d_{c_i}(exp^{c_i}_0(z_i),exp^{c_i}_0(a_k))^2. Here H_i is the behavior term and d_{c_i}/exp^{c_i}_0 are the curved terms; setting H_i to a constant removes behavior-conditioned geometry, while replacing d_c and maps with Euclidean distance/identity removes curvature.
OBSERVABLES: First-level assignment flips and gradient magnitudes should vary with train-entropy bins; high-branching items should gain train-held-out successor coverage/prefix predictability, with no unacceptable reconstruction degradation. Compare final Stage3 test Recall@10 against behavior-off, geometry-off, and both-on runs.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: Replace all H_i with the train-set mean H_bar while keeping the same hyperbolic machinery, initialization, schedule, and other settings. This removes item-specific behavioral conditioning while retaining a single global curved quantizer.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: Retain each actual training-derived H_i and c_i cache but replace exp/log and d_{c_i} assignment with identity maps and squared Euclidean distance; keep all other settings identical.
ABLATION BOTH ON: Use cached training-only H_i to set bounded c_i and perform the specified Poincare mapping/geodesic first-level assignment; evaluate the resulting SIDs with the unchanged Stage3 protocol.
SPEC EQUATIONS: n_ij=sum_{u,t in train} 1[x_{u,t}=i and x_{u,t+1}=j]; pi_j=(sum_i n_ij+eps)/(sum_{i,j}n_ij+N*eps); q_ij=(n_ij+alpha*pi_j)/(sum_l n_il+alpha); H_i= -sum_j q_ij log(q_ij+eps)/log(max(2,N)); c_i=clip(c_min+(c_max-c_min)H_i,c_min,c_max). For each z_i,a_k: x=exp_0^{c_i}(z_i), y=exp_0^{c_i}(a_k), k*=argmin_k [2/sqrt(c_i) atanh(sqrt(c_i)||(-x)⊕_{c_i}y||)]^2. Compute standard RQ-VAE reconstruction and commitment/codebook losses using selected code; gradients pass through maps and curved distance. Treat zero-count items via smoothed q and the same formula.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: frozen item embeddings, item IDs, train-only user-ordered interaction sequences, encoder output z_i, first-level codebook vectors a_k, alpha, epsilon, and fixed c_min/c_max. Outputs: cached per-item H_i and c_i, first-stage assignment indices, quantized vectors, losses, and final RQ-VAE SID tokens.
SAFEGUARDS: Compute geometry in float32; clamp Poincare norms to (1-eps_ball)/sqrt(c_i), with eps_ball around 1e-5; clamp atanh arguments below 1-eps_ball and to nonnegative values; use eps-smoothed probabilities and a bounded log; shrink low-count item distributions toward pi; normalize entropy to [0,1] and clamp c_i to configured bounds. Use stable Mobius addition, gradient clipping, deterministic cached counts, finite-value/gradient tests, and a train-only calibration to keep median curved assignment distance near the baseline scale. For the geometry-off control, use identity/squared Euclidean distance and match score scale on training batches only.
END IDEA
