IDEA: behcone-prefix-rq
TITLE: Behaviour-entropy Poincare entailment cones as a differentiable surrogate for the first-level prefix score
MOTIVATION: Issue #126 showed that the hard hyperbolic prefix score Q_H ranks with downstream test_R@10 but left open whether a differentiable version can be optimised and whether that optimisation reaches recall. The first RQ-VAE token is the coarse cluster the generator must commit to first; if the level-1 codebook is organised so that a prefix's observed successors fall inside its own curved "future cone", the first token becomes behaviourally predictive instead of only reconstruction-optimal.
NOVELTY: The earlier rounds used successor mass inside Sinkhorn, successor-weighted Fréchet codebook updates, curved residuals, geodesic charts, Lorentz cones and branch curvature. This proposal is not a cone quantizer and not a mass-weighted assignment: it keeps the RQ-VAE quantizer unchanged and adds a behaviour-conditioned aperture to a Poincare entailment cone on the level-1 codewords, so that the aperture itself is a measured function of the training-only prefix-transition entropy (no swept constant), and the cone penalty is the differentiable proxy for the hard Q_H of Issue #126. Prefix-to-prefix pairs (not item pairs) make the whole penalty act on the codebook only.
CHANGE: Build a directed level-1 prefix transition matrix from training-split item transitions, turn each row into a successor distribution, and add to the RQ-VAE objective a hyperbolic entailment-cone penalty that pushes each observed successor prefix into the cone rooted at the source prefix, with half-aperture alpha_k = arctan(exp(H_k/log(K+1) - 1)) taken from that row's entropy.
SPECIFICATION: See SPEC EQUATIONS. Inputs: level-1 codebook C in R^{K x d} (K codewords, d dim), training-split transition counts N_ij, a curvature c=1 Poincare ball. Precompute once: M_kk' = sum_{i in k, j in k'} N_ij with k = prefix1(i), k' = prefix1(j) from the current codeword assignment, so M is refreshed every 100 epochs (cheap, 24.6k items). Per step: sample up to 256 source prefixes with >=20 transitions, take their top-8 successors by M mass, evaluate the cone penalty, backprop into C. No encoder or decoder change.
COST: One extra pass every 100 epochs over 24556 items to refresh M (O(#transitions), seconds). Per step at the frozen scale (24556 items, batch ~1023, 24 steps/epoch, 3000 epochs): at most 256 source prefixes x 8 successors = 2048 Poincare log/angle evaluations of dimension d plus one scalar entropy per source row; this is under 1% of the RQ-VAE forward+backward flops and adds roughly 2-4% wall clock. 72000 steps total; no extra model, no extra encoder forward pass for successor items.
HYPOTHESIS: If the hard Q_H-to-recall relation is causal and not a small-sample artefact, then a differentiable cone proxy that is optimised during Stage 2 should (i) raise cone containment and lower the level-1 successor rank of held-out training transitions, (ii) raise the Issue #126 hard Q_H evaluated on the resulting SID, and (iii) lift Stage 3 test_recall@10 above 0.065 with the same single run. If Q_H only correlates because both are driven by item popularity, Q_H will move but recall will not.
RISKS: The cone penalty may collapse many prefixes toward the boundary or make level-1 codes redundant, hurting reconstruction and prefix counts; behaviour sparsity (first-level prefixes with few transitions) can make entropy and aperture noisy; the aperture could be gamed by pushing entropy down instead of structuring the geometry (mitigated by stopping the gradient through alpha); the effect may be too weak at level 1 alone.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Directed item transitions aggregated to first-level prefix transitions, plus the entropy of each prefix's successor distribution.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training split only. For each consecutive pair (u_t, u_{t+1}) in each training sequence (optionally with gap <= 3), increment N[u_t, u_{t+1}]. At refresh time assign every training item i its current level-1 codeword k(i) = argmin_k d_P(enc(i), c_k), accumulate M[k,k'] += N[i,j], row-normalise q_k, keep the top K=8 successors by mass plus an "other" bucket, and compute H_k = -sum q log q over that distribution (log base e). Rows with fewer than 20 total transitions are masked.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: N is built only from Stage-2 training sequences; validation and test interactions are never read. Prefix assignments k(i) use only training items' encoder outputs. The successor table is frozen per refresh block and never uses a validation/test target, so no validation or test interaction can influence a codeword position or an aperture.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Fixed negative curvature c=1 Poincare ball: Riemannian log map log_p(x), hyperbolic angle between tangent vectors at p, and a geodesic entailment cone (angular aperture plus radial-depth hinge). No trainable curvature, no Lorentz model.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage-2 RQ-VAE training, as an auxiliary term on the level-1 codebook (and optionally the level-2 prefix points), inside the same backward pass as the reconstruction and commitment losses, before the codebook is frozen for Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: behaviour -> prefix successor matrix M -> row entropy H_k -> aperture alpha_k and successor set S_k -> Poincare cone penalty on (p_k, p_k') -> gradient on level-1 codewords -> new SIDs. Removing behaviour removes alpha_k and S_k; removing geometry removes log_p and the hyperbolic angle. Either change alters the gradient on c_{1,k}, hence the assignment and the SID.
COUPLING EQUATION: L_cone = mean_k mean_{k' in S_k} [ relu( theta_{p_k}(p_{k'}) - alpha_k ) + relu(||p_k|| - ||p_{k'}||) ], with alpha_k = arctan(exp(H_k/log(K+1) - 1)) and theta_{p_k}(p_{k'}) = arccos( <log_{p_k}(p_{k'}), p_k> / (||log_{p_k}(p_{k'})|| ||p_k||) ).
OBSERVABLES: Cone containment rate of held-out training prefix transitions; level-1 branch entropy of the learned SID versus the data; successor rank of the true next prefix; the Issue #126 hard Q_H on the new SID; reconstruction loss and codebook usage; and the single-run Stage 3 test_recall@10 against the 0.065 target.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: alpha_k is replaced by the constant pi/4 for every k and S_k is replaced by K uniformly sampled other prefixes (same cardinality, no transition statistics); the geometry term is unchanged. The switch exists in code and is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: the same behaviour-conditioned S_k and alpha_k are used, but theta is the Euclidean angle between p_{k'} - p_k and p_k, and the depth hinge uses Euclidean norms; no log map, no ball. The switch exists in code and is not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: the full coupling above, behaviour-conditioned aperture and successor set evaluated with the Poincare log map and hyperbolic angle. This is the single trained candidate.
SPEC EQUATIONS: (1) Poincare log map, c=1: log_p(x) = (2/(1-||p||^2)) * artanh(||(-p) (+) x||) * ((-p)(+)x)/||(-p)(+)x||, with Moebius addition (+). (2) prefix point p_k = exp_0(c_{1,k}) = tanh(||c_{1,k}||) * c_{1,k}/||c_{1,k}||. (3) theta as in COUPLING EQUATION. (4) alpha_k = arctan(exp(H_k/log(K+1) - 1)), stop-gradient through H_k. (5) total loss L = L_rec + L_commit + L_codebook + lambda_b * L_cone, with lambda_b fixed once on the first batch after the encoder warm-up as lambda_b = stopgrad(L_rec)/stopgrad(L_cone), clipped to [0.01, 10], then frozen for all 3000 epochs. (6) pseudocode: every 100 epochs recompute k(i) and M; each step, sample prefixes, compute p_k, log_{p_k}(p_k'), theta, alpha, L_cone, add to the existing backward.
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: level-1 codebook rows, the current item-to-prefix assignment, the training-only transition matrix, K=8, curvature c=1, batch. Outputs: a scalar L_cone and its gradient with respect to the level-1 (and optionally level-2) codebook rows; no change to the encoder output shape or to the Stage-3 interface.
SAFEGUARDS: clamp all ball points to norm <= 1 - 1e-3 before log/artanh; if ||p|| < 1e-4 use the Euclidean limit theta = arccos(<x-p,p>/(||x-p|| ||p||)); if ||log_p(x)|| < 1e-8 skip the pair; mask prefixes with fewer than 20 transitions; drop self-pairs k=k' before the depth hinge; stop the gradient through H_k and through the assignment k(i); if L_cone is non-finite or zero, skip the term for that step and log it; refresh M only from training sequences and reuse the previous M if the refresh is degenerate.
END IDEA
