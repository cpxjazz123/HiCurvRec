IDEA: LORENTZ-SUCCPROFILE-SMOOTH-RQ
TITLE: SIDs quantised from the Lorentzian centre of mass of an item's successor-profile twins
MOTIVATION: Two items can be behaviourally interchangeable — their next-item distributions match — while sitting far apart in content space, so a Euclidean RQ-VAE hands them unrelated level-1 prefixes and the Stage-3 generator cannot use "this is an X-intent item" as a cheap first token. Issue #126 measured that the hard hyperbolic first-level prefix score Q_H ranks with downstream test_R@10 (Spearman +0.821, small sample), which says the level-1 partition, not the deeper digits, is where recall is decided. The unaddressed gap: the point that actually enters the quantiser is a single item's content vector, never a behaviour-consensus position, so behaviourally equivalent items cannot be pulled into one cell at the level that matters.
NOVELTY: The earlier rounds occupied (i) soft/OT assignment mass, (ii) codebook centroid updates (Fréchet/Karcher), (iii) curvature as a function of branching, (iv) residual transport and geodesic charts, (v) a post-hoc prefix-rank proxy. None of them changed *which point is quantised*. Here the behaviour signal is moved upstream into the geometry of the quantiser input: the item's hyperboloid point is replaced by its Lorentzian weighted midpoint over a successor-PROFILE-equivalence neighbourhood, and the same curvature that forms that midpoint also defines the assignment metric and the residual chain. It also differs from a GNN feature-smoothing trick: the smoothing is not preprocessing of features, it is the exp/log/pseudosphere arithmetic that generates the residual chain, and the codebooks live on the same hyperboloid. The behaviour relation is successor-profile similarity (Hellinger/cosine of row-normalised transition rows), not raw directed edges — raw edges, which every earlier round used, conflate "co-occurs with" with "behaves alike".
CHANGE: Lift the encoder output to the hyperboloid, replace it by a 50/50 Minkowski convex combination with its K successor-profile twins, re-project onto the hyperboloid, and run the entire residual-quantisation chain (Lorentzian-distance assignment, log_0 residual) on that smoothed point.
SPECIFICATION: kappa = 1 (documented default: one unit of curvature in latent units), alpha = 0.5 (documented default: equal trust between own content and behaviour twins), K = round(median positive out-degree) measured from the training transition matrix, twins from cosine similarity of sqrt of row-normalised training transition profiles, whole graph frozen before Stage 2; full equations in SPEC EQUATIONS.
COST: Frozen scale, B = ceil(24556/24) = 1023 items per step, L levels, C codes per level, latent d = 32, K neighbours. Smoothing gather/combine B*K*(d+1) ≈ 1023*8*33 ≈ 2.7e5 MAC per step ≈ 1% of the existing B*L*C*d ≈ 1023*3*256*32 ≈ 2.5e7 MAC assignment. The Lorentzian assignment replaces the Euclidean one at the same order (B*L*C*(d+1) vs B*L*C*d), so step overhead is under 2% of the quantiser and under 1% of a full Stage-2 step; the epoch-start table refresh is one no-gradient encoder pass (24 batches) = +4.2% encoder forward per epoch. One-off graph build (sparse P·Pᵀ on a 24556×24556 transition matrix, ~5e5 nnz) is 1–3 min CPU, a few hundred MB, once. Over 3000 epochs x 24 steps = 72k steps this is baseline + ~5% wall clock, no memory growth.
HYPOTHESIS: If behaviourally interchangeable items should share the level-1 token, then smoothing the quantiser input along successor-profile edges will (a) raise the share of successor-linked pairs that share a level-1 code, (b) raise the issue-#126 Q_H recomputed on the new SIDs, and (c) push the single frozen Stage-3 test_recall@10 above 0.065. Observed through the three diagnostics plus the one test run; if Q_H rises but recall does not, the level-1 partition is not the binding constraint.
RISKS: (a) Smoothing can wash out content identity, raising reconstruction error until the SID carries too little item-specific information and Stage 3 loses precision at rank 10; (b) the transition graph is sparse and popularity-skewed, so twin sets are noisy and hub items dominate the consensus; (c) alpha = 0.5 must be right on the first attempt because no sweep is permitted; (d) per-epoch staleness of the neighbour table lets the smoothed point lag the encoder; (e) K = median out-degree can be large, in which case the twin set is diluted.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Successor-profile equivalence: the Hellinger/cosine similarity between the row-normalised directed-transition distributions of two items.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the training split only, count directed gap-1 item transitions T_ij over the training sequences of the 24556 training items; row-normalise P_i· = T_i· / Σ_j T_ij (zero rows stay zero); s_ij = ⟨√P_i·, √P_j·⟩ / (‖√P_i·‖‖√P_j·‖) clipped to [0,1], with s_ii = 0; keep the top-K per item, K = round(median_i outdeg_i); w_ij = s_ij / Σ_{j'∈N_i} s_ij'. Built once, frozen, no gradient, no parameters.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: T is counted only from training-split interactions over the 24556 training items. The twin table, K and w are frozen and hashed before Stage 2 starts, and the hash is asserted on every epoch; validation and test interactions are never read by this module and no statistic of the graph is fitted on any held-out split. Codebook, curvature and smoothing are trained only through Stage-2 reconstruction on training items.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: Lorentz/hyperboloid model L^d_kappa = {x ∈ R^{d+1} : ⟨x,x⟩_L = −1/kappa, x_0 > 0} with fixed negative curvature −kappa, kappa = 1: exp_0, log_0, pseudosphere re-projection, and the Lorentzian distance d_H(x,y) = (1/√kappa)·arcosh(−kappa⟨x,y⟩_L); the smoothing is a Minkowski-ambient convex combination followed by re-projection, which is the exact Lorentzian weighted midpoint for two points.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: In Stage-2 quantisation and at the encoder→quantiser interface: the lift, the behaviour consensus point, the codebook parameterisation, the assignment cost and the residual chain are all on the hyperboloid; the decoder maps back with log_0.
COUPLING PATH: training transitions → successor-profile twins and weights w → Minkowski combination of the item's and its twins' hyperboloid points → re-projected point → log_0 → level-1 residual chain → Lorentzian argmin → SID digits 1..L → Stage-3 sequence. Behaviour off (alpha = 0, w = 0) feeds the raw item point into the same curved chain; geometry off (kappa → 0) keeps the same twins but replaces the consensus and every assignment with Euclidean ones. Both switches change the SID assignment itself, not an accessory loss.
COUPLING EQUATION: x̂_i = proj_kappa( (1−alpha)·exp_0^kappa(z_i) + alpha·Σ_{j∈N_i} w_ij·exp_0^kappa(z_j) ), and q_1(i) = argmin_k d_H( exp_0^kappa(r_1(i)), e_{1,k} )² with r_1(i) = log_0^kappa(x̂_i); w_ij is the behaviour term (successor-profile mass) and exp_0/log_0/d_H are the curved terms, in the same expression.
OBSERVABLES: Level-1 prefix purity against the dominant-successor label; share of successor-linked item pairs sharing a level-1 code; the issue-#126 hard hyperbolic prefix score Q_H recomputed on the new SIDs; per-level codebook usage entropy; the Stage-2 reconstruction curve; and the single Stage-3 test_recall@10 against 0.065.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: alpha = 0 and w ≡ 0 in the consensus step so x̂_i = exp_0^kappa(z_i); Lorentzian chain, codebooks, curvature untouched. Code exists and is correct; not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: kappa → 0: identity lift, Euclidean consensus x̂_i = (1−alpha)z_i + alpha·Σ_j w_ij z_j over the same twins, L2 assignment, linear residual r_{l+1} = r_l − e_{l,q_l}. Code exists and is correct; not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: alpha = 0.5, w from successor profiles, kappa = 1, full Lorentzian chain — this is the single trained arm.
SPEC EQUATIONS:
  ⟨x,y⟩_L = −x_0 y_0 + Σ_{i=1..d} x_i y_i ;  proj_kappa(y) = y / √(−kappa⟨y,y⟩_L), defined when ⟨y,y⟩_L < 0
  exp_0^kappa(v) = ( cosh(√kappa‖v‖), sinh(√kappa‖v‖)·v / (√kappa‖v‖) ), with v embedded with leading 0
  log_0^kappa(x) = arcosh(√kappa·x_0) · x_{1:} / (√kappa‖x_{1:}‖)
  d_H(x,y)² = (1/kappa)·arcosh( clamp(−kappa⟨x,y⟩_L, 1+1e-7, ∞) )²
  behaviour: T_ij = # directed gap-1 transitions i→j in training sequences; P_i· = T_i· / Σ_j T_ij
             s_ij = cos(√P_i·, √P_j·) clipped to [0,1], s_ii = 0
             N_i = topK_j s_ij, K = round(median_i outdeg_i), w_ij = s_ij / Σ_{j'∈N_i} s_ij'
  consensus: X_i = proj_kappa( 0.5·exp_0^kappa(z_i) + 0.5·Σ_{j∈N_i} w_ij·M_j ), M_j = detached hyperboloid point of j from the epoch-start table; if Σ_j w_ij = 0 then X_i = exp_0^kappa(z_i)
  chain: r_1 = log_0^kappa(X_i); for l = 1..L: p_l = exp_0^kappa(r_l); q_l = argmin_k d_H(p_l, e_{l,k})²; r_{l+1} = r_l − log_0^kappa(e_{l,q_l})
  reconstruction: ẑ_i = Σ_{l=1..L} log_0^kappa(e_{l,q_l}); loss = ‖ẑ_i − z_i‖² plus the baseline RQ-VAE straight-through commitment and codebook terms, unchanged
  epoch refresh: after each epoch, for all 24556 items, M_i ← proj_kappa(exp_0^kappa(encoder(x_i))) with no gradient, 24 batches
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: In — item content features x_i, the frozen K-NN successor-profile graph (indices and weights), encoder parameters, codebook parameters, kappa, alpha, L, C. Out — for every item its L SID digits, its quantised tangent vector ẑ_i, and gradients to the encoder, the codebooks and (through the assignment) the consensus point.
SAFEGUARDS: (1) clamp the arcosh argument to ≥ 1+1e-7 and the tangent norm to atanh(1−1e-5)/√kappa before every exp so no point reaches the boundary; (2) re-project onto the hyperboloid after every addition or multiplication — the future timelike cone is convex, so a convex combination of valid points stays timelike and the projection is always defined, but assert ⟨y,y⟩_L ≤ −1e-6 and fall back to the self point otherwise; (3) zero and renormalise rows with Σ_j w_ij = 0 or NaN similarity; (4) drop exact-duplicate twins so an item cannot average with itself; (5) evaluate the arcosh branch in float64 to avoid gradient blow-up near coincidence; (6) initialise the codebooks by lifting the baseline Euclidean codebook through exp_0 so step 1 matches baseline geometry; (7) keep the behaviour graph in a frozen buffer and assert its snapshot hash matches the pre-Stage-2 value (leak guard).
END IDEA
