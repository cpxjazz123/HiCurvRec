IDEA: succboost-geoshift
TITLE: Behavioural-diffuseness geodesic pre-shift of item latents before a Poincare residual quantiser, so level-1 prefixes fill with behaviourally predictable items.
MOTIVATION: RQ-VAE level-1 prefixes are the token the generative recommender emits first, and a level-1 cell that mixes a niche item with a hub is untrainable: the cell's successor distribution is smeared by the hub, so the first token carries little information. Issue #126 established that the hard hyperbolic first-level prefix score Q_H ranks with downstream test_R@10 (Spearman +0.821 on seven SIDs), which says the geometric separation of the level-1 partition is the quantity worth changing - yet all seven SIDs shared one radial layout. Here the radial layout itself is set by a training-only behaviour statistic, so level-1 capacity is spent on items whose continuation is predictable and fine-level capacity on items whose continuation is diffuse.
NOVELTY: Earlier rounds moved behaviour into the assignment mass (successor-diffused Poincare Sinkhorn), into the codebook update (successor-weighted curved Fréchet means), into per-branch and per-level curvature, into tangent/gyro targets, into Lorentz cones, into the prefix score itself, into geodesic charts and into code repair. None changed WHERE an item sits along the ball's radial geodesic as a function of behaviour. This is a per-item curved displacement (an explicit exp map along the item's own radial geodesic) applied to the latent before any assignment exists; the hyperbolic quantiser is only the substrate, and the new object is a deterministic behaviour-conditioned geodesic displacement field - not a loss term, not a codebook rule, not an assignment weight.
CHANGE: Each item gets a frozen scalar beta_i = gamma*(cbar - c_i) from the concentration of its training successor distribution; every training step moves the latent along its own radial geodesic by hyperbolic distance beta_i, z'_i = exp_{z_i}(beta_i * rhat_i), and only z'_i is quantised by the Poincare residual chain. Capacity redistributes across levels: concentrated items stay near the origin where level-1 codes live, diffuse items are pushed into the exponentially large outer region and must be resolved by levels 2-3.
SPECIFICATION: Fixed curvature K = -1/R^2 with R = 0.9 (documented default, never tuned). Encoder head z = R*u/(1+||u||) so ||z|| < R with no tanh saturation. Offline once, before epoch 1: directed transition counts T[i,j] from consecutive items inside training sessions only; Laplace-smoothed successor distribution p_i(j) = (T[i,j]+1)/(sum_j T[i,j] + |Succ(i)|); concentration c_i = 1 - H(p_i)/log|Succ(i)| in [0,1]; cbar, sigma = mean and std of c_i over items with at least one successor; gamma = clip(1/sigma, 0.5, 8); beta_i = clip(gamma*(cbar - c_i), -1, 1); items with no successor row get beta_i = 0. Per step: rhat_i = z_i/max(||z_i||,1e-8); z'_i = z_i (+) (R*tanh(beta_i/(2R)) * rhat_i) with Möbius addition at curvature -1/R^2; clamp ||z'_i|| into [1e-3 R, 0.999 R]; then the usual three-level chain r_1 = z'_i, k_l = argmin_k d_P(r_l, c_{l,k}), r_{l+1} = r_l (+) (-c_{l,k_l}), zhat = c_1k1 (+) c_2k2 (+) c_3k3, SID(i) = (k1,k2,k3). Loss, optimiser, learning rate, ordering, epoch count and the Stage-3 protocol are unchanged; only the quantiser's input changes. Switches: BEHAVIOUR=off sets beta_i = 0; GEOMETRY=off replaces the exp map by Euclidean dilation z_i*exp(beta_i) and d_P by squared Euclidean. Both switches exist and are unit-tested; no arm is trained with them off this round.
COST: One frozen run, one seed, no sweep. Per step the batch is 24556/24 ≈ 1023 items: 1023 exp maps (elementwise, O(d)) plus 3 × 1023 × 256 ≈ 7.9e5 Poincare distances instead of squared Euclidean ones, so the only added transcendental is arcosh on a kernel that is a small fraction of encoder/decoder cost: roughly +4 to +6 percent step time and under 5 percent of wall clock over 3000 epochs × 24 steps = 72k steps, plus a one-off beta precompute of a few seconds over the training transition pairs. Memory adds one frozen float and one unit vector per item plus one latent buffer.
HYPOTHESIS: It is worth keeping only if the encoder does not cancel the shift and the shift buys prefix sharpness. Concretely, the post-training rank correlation between beta_i and hyperbolic radius artanh(||z_i||/R) must stay clearly positive (target > 0.3; near zero means the encoder inverted the displacement and the mechanism is inert), and level-1 successor purity (share of training transitions whose endpoints share the level-1 code) must rise together with Stage-3 test_recall@10 above 0.065. If radius tracks behaviour but recall is flat, the reallocation is real but the generator cannot exploit it.
RISKS: The encoder controls z and can compensate the shift by pulling high-beta items inward, cancelling the effect (that is exactly why the radius-beta correlation is recorded). Pushing diffuse items outward can crowd the outer shell, inflate level-2/3 collisions and starve the mid radius, giving dead level-1 codes and a level-1 usage-entropy collapse. arcosh is numerically hostile near the boundary. Laplace-smoothed concentration is noisy for low-degree items, and sigma dominated by the many one-successor items makes gamma small and the shift weak. Finally a better prefix partition need not survive the frozen Stage-3 generator, which may simply re-learn the SID marginal.
BEHAVIOR SIGNAL: Successor entropy concentration of each item's directed training transition distribution (how diffuse versus predictable its continuation is), normalised by its successor-set size.
BEHAVIOR COMPUTATION: From the Stage-2 training split only. Walk each training session in order, drop immediate repeats of the same item, and increment T[i,j] for every consecutive pair (i then j). Row-normalise with Laplace smoothing as above, compute H(p_i) and divide by log|Succ(i)| to obtain c_i in [0,1]. Compute cbar, sigma, gamma once and freeze beta for all 24556 items before the first epoch as an item-indexed lookup table. Items never appearing as a transition source get beta = 0.
LEAKAGE BOUNDARY: T is built exclusively from interactions in the Stage-2 training split that the RQ-VAE already trains on; validation and test interactions are never read while constructing SIDs, and beta is frozen before epoch 1, so no validation- or test-derived statistic can influence a code assignment. Vocabulary and beta indices come from the training split; unseen items keep beta = 0 and are quantised as in the baseline. Stage 3 consumes the frozen SIDs and feeds nothing back into beta or the quantiser.
HYPERBOLIC OPERATOR: The Poincare exponential map at z along its radial unit geodesic, exp_z(beta*rhat) = z (+) (R*tanh(beta/(2R)) * rhat), at fixed negative curvature K = -1/R^2 with R = 0.9; the substrate uses the Poincare distance d_P(x,y) = R*arcosh(1 + 2R^2||x-y||^2 / ((R^2-||x||^2)(R^2-||y||^2))) and Möbius addition at the same curvature.
HYPERBOLIC LOCATION: Inside training, on the RQ-VAE forward path between the encoder head and the first residual level; also inside quantisation, because assignment, residual chain and reconstruction all use curved distances at the same curvature. It is not a side loss and does not touch Stage 3.
COUPLING PATH: beta_i (behaviour, frozen per item) sets the geodesic step length; the exp map (geometry) turns that length into a new position z'_i; the Poincare distance from z'_i to the level-1 codebook produces k1 and hence the SID. With geometry off the same beta becomes a scalar dilation and yields different codes; with behaviour off the geometry is the identity shift and yields different codes; neither factor can be dropped without changing k1, k2, k3.
COUPLING EQUATION: k1(i) = argmin_k d_P( exp_{z_i}( beta_i * rhat_i ), c_{1,k} ), with beta_i = clip(gamma*(cbar - c_i), -1, 1), gamma = clip(1/sigma_c, 0.5, 8), rhat_i = z_i/||z_i||, and d_P the curvature -1/R^2 Poincare distance. Removing the behaviour term sets beta_i = 0 so the exp map is the identity; removing the curved term replaces the exp map by a Euclidean dilation and d_P by Euclidean distance.
OBSERVABLES: (1) Spearman correlation between beta_i and post-training hyperbolic radius artanh(||z_i||/R), the direct signature of the coupling; (2) level-1 successor purity and the same for levels 2 and 3; (3) number of distinct level-1 codes used and level-1 usage entropy, to show capacity has not collapsed; (4) per-item SID flip rate between consecutive checkpoints; (5) Stage-3 test_recall@10. All diagnostics are recorded, none is an admission gate.
ABLATION BEHAVIOR OFF: BEHAVIOUR=off sets beta_i = 0 for all items, so the exp map is the identity and the run reduces to the plain curved three-level RQ-VAE; the switch exists in code and is unit-tested, and this arm is not trained in this round.
ABLATION GEOMETRY OFF: GEOMETRY=off keeps beta_i but replaces the curved operations by Euclidean ones: z'_i = z_i*exp(beta_i), squared Euclidean assignment, additive residual chain, Euclidean commitment; the switch exists in code and is unit-tested, and this arm is not trained in this round.
ABLATION BOTH ON: BEHAVIOUR=on and GEOMETRY=on, i.e. the single frozen run reported for this candidate: exp_{z_i}(beta_i*rhat_i) followed by Poincare assignment, Möbius residuals and Poincare reconstruction, 3000 Stage-2 epochs on four cards, frozen Stage-3 protocol, no early stopping, no sweep, no seed repetition.
SPEC EQUATIONS: Precompute (training split only): T[i,j] += 1 for consecutive (i,j) with i != j; p_i(j) = (T[i,j]+1)/(sum_j T[i,j] + |Succ(i)|); H_i = -sum_j p_i(j) log p_i(j); c_i = 1 - H_i/log|Succ(i)| for |Succ(i)| >= 2, c_i = 1 for |Succ(i)| = 1, else undefined; cbar, sigma over finite c_i; gamma = clip(1/sigma, 0.5, 8); beta_i = 0 if c_i undefined else clip(gamma*(cbar - c_i), -1, 1). Every step: u = encoder(x); z = R*u/(1+||u||); rhat = z/max(||z||,1e-8); step = R*tanh(beta_i/(2R)); z' = mobius_add(z, step*rhat); if ||z'|| > 0.999R: z' *= 0.999R/||z'||; if ||z'|| < 1e-3R: z' *= 1e-3R/max(||z'||,1e-12); r = z'; for l in 1..3: k_l = argmin_k d_P(r, c[l][k]); r = mobius_add(r, -c[l][k_l]); codes.append(k_l); zhat = mobius_add(mobius_add(c[1][k1], c[2][k2]), c[3][k3]); loss = reconstruction(decoder(zhat), x) + lambda_c * sum_l d_P(r_l, c[l][k_l])**2 / R**2 + codebook terms exactly as baseline with lambda_c and lambda_e unchanged; backprop through d_P and through z', with beta held constant (stop-gradient on the statistics).
SPEC INPUTS OUTPUTS: Inputs: training item sequences (Stage-2 split only) for beta; per step the encoder latent u_i, the frozen beta_i, the three 256-code ball codebooks and the curvature radius R. Outputs: the beta lookup table (24556 floats), displaced latents z'_i, SID triples (k1,k2,k3) per item, and the loss and gradients that update encoder and codebooks. The operator is deterministic given (z, beta, R) and returns a point of the same dimension strictly inside the ball.
SAFEGUARDS: Clamp the arcosh argument to >= 1+1e-7; clamp both radius denominators R^2-||x||^2 to >= 1e-6 before dividing; clamp ||z|| into [1e-3 R, 0.999 R] after the encoder and after the shift so no point reaches the boundary; beta bounded by one curvature-radius unit and gamma bounded to [0.5, 8] so a degenerate sigma cannot explode the shift; fall back to z' = z when ||z|| < 1e-8 (rhat undefined); beta = 0 for items without a successor row, stored with requires_grad=False and no gradient into the statistics; curvature fixed at K = -1/R^2 with R = 0.9, so there is no curvature sweep; assert every step that max ||z'|| < R and that no NaN enters d_P.
END IDEA
