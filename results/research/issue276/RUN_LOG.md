## Frozen splits / protocol decisions (before any fitting)
- Unit of analysis: SID content (sha256 of `item_sids.json`), not mechanism family; families are audit metadata only.
- Folds: 5 folds by SID-group (near-dup groups merged by exact duplicate SID content and by identical item→triple mapping); seeded assignment, stored as `FOLDS.json` before model fitting. Recall-band stratification attempted; declared if too few positives.
- Target: `recall@10` from the unified Stage3 snapshot (per-SID aggregate of protocol-consistent records).
- Success criterion (pre-registered): a formula/feature set is "usable" only if its OOF MAE is **strictly better than the strongest control** with a SID-group bootstrap CI excluding 0 on the paired MAE difference **and** its OOF Spearman is positive and ≥ the best single feature's OOF Spearman. Otherwise the honest verdict is negative.
