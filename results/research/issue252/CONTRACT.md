## Phase2 frozen training estimator — 2026-11-24, before production changes

The completed original-path profile (run `issue252_baseline_profile_3415496`, ranks3415592–3415595) measured one real1024-item/rank step at202.74–202.75s. Its dominant repeated work was152s ego assembly,24–25s GNN dispatch and88–89s whole-catalogue forward (nested timings, not additive); rank0 streamed1,307,338 target rows twice. This is not an epoch timing. The following estimator is chosen from computational structure, before any formal run or recall observation; it is not hyperparameter search.

### Exact optimizations (not approximations)

Keep both complete induced one-hop/two-hop egos, every node/edge and the existing shared two-layer directional GNN. Persist only static local sparse operators under `assets/`, with full visible-graph ancestry. Incoming operator has `A_in[target,source]=1/in_degree(target)` and outgoing operator has `A_out[source,target]=1/out_degree(source)`, zero rows for empty neighborhoods. Each layer is exactly `tanh(W_self h + b_self + W_in A_in h + W_out A_out h)`, followed by the same full subgraph mean. Static operators may be packed once and gathered into block-diagonal batches; trainable activations are recomputed on every call. No graph construction inside the candidate and no frozen GNN output cache.

For distance matrices use bounded native pairwise norm kernels instead of materializing `[items,codes,32]` or ambient33 broadcast differences. Hyperbolic radial/angular log identities, common scale, five curvatures, spherical chord-angle atan2, coincidence/saturation behavior and public broadcasting contract remain unchanged. All float64 output/gradient comparisons use existing independent Lorentz/ambient references (absolute1e-9, relative1e-8 away from nondifferentiable ties); float32 tolerance is1e-5 absolute/1e-4 relative. Controlled non-tied hard IDs must agree; near-tie numerical ordering is diagnosed, not silently asserted bit-identical.

### Declared stochastic structural training (approximate, not the full objective)

Let C be all frozen valid centres, T_m the complete stored valid triple occurrences for centre m (duplicates count), and R_m its entire original visible component, including m,b,c. Existing complete targets/graph are unchanged. Each optimizer step on rank r uses one centre from a seed42 permutation, index `(global_step*world_size+r) mod |C|`; successive cycles reuse the complete centre order. Thus every centre is visited once per complete world-interleaved cycle, not permanently skipped. Draw `min(32,|T_m|)` occurrences uniformly without replacement from the existing memory-mapped table and `min(32,|R_m|)` reference IDs uniformly without replacement from the FULL component.32 is the frozen latent/GNN width, not a tuned count. RNG derives solely from seed42, global step and rank; sampling state/coverage is recorded each epoch. A centre with no valid row is excluded exactly as before; empty components or nonfinite targets fail, never impute zero.

Form unique global IDs I from all sampled triple endpoints and references, then pad uniformly without replacement from the complete catalogue to at least256 IDs, the minimum feasible population for the existing floor1/256-book assignment. Sort I by global ID. Compute fresh full-ego GNN gates, encoder and actual three-level quantization on I with `balanced=False`, using the same real hard assignment/floor and the same-cost soft backward rule; do not replace it with argmin or a straight-through bypass. There are no additional learnable parameters. Reconstruction still uses the unchanged full real training loader, original per-rank1024/global4096 batch, actual configured Sinkhorn layers, optimizer/scheduler and losses.

For each sampled triple t=(m,b,c), `k_hat = mean_{a sampled from R_m}[d(q_a,q_m)^2-(d(q_a,q_b)^2+d(q_a,q_c)^2)/2] + d(q_b,q_c)^2/4`, where d² is the fixed equal-expert mean and cannot read learned gate weights. Structural loss is the mean sampled Huber(k_hat/target_scale,target/target_scale), delta1. It enters EVERY actual optimizer step, without the old full-partition scaling multiplier. DDP's mean gradient supplies the mean across rank samples. Lambda is frozen once from the initial actual reconstruction-vs-THIS-estimator encoder-gradient norms; no optimizer step during calibration and no weight adjustment thereafter.

For externally fixed q, uniform reference sampling makes k_hat unbiased and uniform triple sampling estimates the centre mean when all references are used; finite-population enumeration will verify these facts. **The executed training objective is biased relative to the original full-catalogue objective:** Huber after finite reference means is nonlinear; hard assignment/floor/global normalization uses finite auxiliary I instead of the whole catalogue; q itself depends on sampled I. No claim of unbiased full-loss gradients is made. Endpoint/reference/filler gradients remain connected through the same-cost soft probabilities, residual quantization, gates and GNN; no detached learned teacher, no stale parameter output. Record centre/triple/reference coverage and actual update counts, not a fictional full-table pass each epoch.

Full inference/export uses the entire ordered catalogue, full egos and the unchanged actual hard rule. Final hard-q and continuous-z curvature evaluation still visits all28,391,948 valid occurrences and all legal references using the independent evaluator. The exact full objective remains executable for checks; stochastic training metrics are labelled estimates. Initial scale, calibrated lambda, sampling seed/schedule, source/assets and actual coverage bind the checkpoint. All structural sampling/recomputation, optimizer and communication costs are inside the continuous performance window; initialization and final export are separately timed.
