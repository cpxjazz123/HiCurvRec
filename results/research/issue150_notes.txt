Issue #150 implementation record

TDD, in the order it happened:
1. tests/test_struct_prior.py was written before model/struct_prior.py existed; the
   run failed at import (the module was missing). A first version asserted the
   column marginal equals pi at the run's own epsilon; that failed (the plan is
   sharp there, and the plan is row-normalised, so its columns carry B*pi, not pi)
   -- see issue150_marginal_regime.txt. The test was corrected to the two regimes
   that are actually true: exact at a smooth epsilon, ordering-and-tolerance at the
   frozen one, and the uniform special case is pinned against the baseline's own
   sinkhorn to 1e-9.
2. tests/test_prior_entrypoints.py pins the export path (the last level's token
   comes from the structured plan) and the checkpoint binding (another prior, or a
   missing identity, must raise).
3. A synthetic two-epoch run of the real main() exercised every wiring point:
   epoch 1 uniform marginal, epoch 2 the structural one, the three terms with
   coverage counts, the bucket partition, the export.

Measured property of the definitions, recorded before any training:
quartile strata force rho ~ 1/4 (measured [0.24927, 0.24825, 0.24601, 0.25647],
relative spread 4.19%), so with equal-size buckets the target marginal pi is
within 2.59% relative of uniform. The marginal's structural content is therefore
bounded by that spread by construction; the rest of the mechanism (bucket
composition, L_tri, L_fork, L_prior) is what can act. This is reported, not
repaired, because changing G, rho or the stratification after seeing it is
forbidden by the issue's own risk rules.
