Issue #150 implementation record

Design decision taken before the single run, after the controller's review of
commit 304385ce (their measurement: quantile strata make the marginal change
0.39% of tokens at the frozen epsilon, i.e. nearly inert):

  strata are now THRESHOLDS on tau, not quartiles: tau = 0 is its own layer, then
  (0, 1/3], (1/3, 2/3], (2/3, 1]. The frozen prior is
    rho = [0.24080, 0.25102, 0.37722, 0.13097]  (max/min 2.88)
    layer sizes [6118, 6379, 9582, 3327] of 24556
    table SHA-256 ca4f07f840fa1383195f233a5134c4bad7e4fe84a0bd0ebd3125ced16768df60
  and the target marginal now deviates from uniform by up to 50.9% relative. The
  same token-change measurement the review used returns 77.3% on a real 1024x256
  cost matrix at epsilon = 0.003 with 50 iterations (uniform vs structural plan).
  Reason, alternatives and their rejection are in math_spec.json decision_log.

Everything else is unchanged from the reviewed commit: the structured Sinkhorn is
the baseline's iteration with the column target replaced, the buckets stay equal
sized, epoch 1 uses the uniform marginal, and the three terms run on the batch's
own triangles and edges.

   the run failed at import. A first version of the column-marginal test asserted

   (columns carry B*pi): see issue150_marginal_regime.txt. The test now pins the
   uniform special case against the baseline's own sinkhorn to 1e-9, the exact
   marginal in the smooth regime, and the bucket ordering at the frozen epsilon.
2. tests/test_prior_entrypoints.py pins the export path (the last level's token
   comes from the structured plan) and the checkpoint binding (another prior, or a
   missing identity, must raise).
3. A synthetic two-epoch main() exercised epoch-1 uniform -> epoch-2 structural
   marginal, the bucket partition, the three terms and the export.
4. The real entry point ran one epoch on the real data (results/research/
   issue150_smoke.txt): batch-induced coverage 2597 triangles and 8784 edges per
   step, L_tri 0.9687, L_fork 0.00066, L_prior 0.1815, epoch-1 bucket mass
   [8118, 7296, 5781, 3361] against targets [6118, 6095, 6040, 6303].
