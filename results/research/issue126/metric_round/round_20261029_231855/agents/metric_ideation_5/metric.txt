METRIC: hyp_prefix_gibbs_enrichment_k10
TITLE: Resolution-matched hyperbolic Gibbs-mass enrichment of each item's own SID cell, with the item excluded from its own neighbourhood.
FORMULA: Let N be the number of exported items, L the number of levels, c_l(j) the integer code of item j at level l, and B_l(j) = {j' : c_1..l(j') = c_1..l(j)} its level-l prefix cell. Let K_l = the number of distinct level-l prefixes. Fix K = 10 (the Recall@10 cut) and set l* = max{ l in 1..L : K_l <= N/K }; if no level satisfies this, l* = 1. The frozen reference supplies source vectors s_u and target vectors t_j in the open Poincare ball D^d of curvature -1, plus its own softmax temperature tau > 0. For every item i with at least one training positive, let P_i be the set of training users who interacted with i, and define q_i = argmin_{q in D^d} sum_{u in P_i} d_H(q, s_u)^2, the hyperbolic Karcher/Frechet barycentre, computed by the Poincare exp/log fixed-point iteration. Here d_H(q,t) = arcosh(1 + 2||q-t||^2 / ((1-||q||^2)(1-||t||^2))) is the Poincare distance; the iteration uses Moebius addition and exp_q/log_q with curvature -1. Put w_i(j) = exp(-d_H(q_i, t_j)/tau) for j != i. Let C_i = B_l*(i) minus {i} and c_i = |C_i|. If c_i = 0 set E_i = 0; otherwise set W_i = sum_{j in C_i} w_i(j), Z_i = sum_{j != i} w_i(j), and E_i = ln( W_i / ( (c_i/(N-1)) * Z_i ) ). The metric is M = (1/|A|) sum_{i in A} E_i, where A is the set of items with at least one training positive. M is one scalar; larger is better.
INPUTS: (1) the exported SID table: one integer code per item per level; (2) the frozen Issue #126 reference: source vector per training user, target vector per item, and its softmax temperature tau; (3) the TRAINING split positives only, used to form P_i and q_i. No validation or test interaction, no recall, and no fitted quantity is read.
GEOMETRY: All operations are in the Poincare ball of fixed curvature -1. The query point q_i is a geodesic Frechet/Karcher barycentre of the training users' source representations (Poincare exp/log and Moebius addition). Every weight w_i(j) is a negative Poincare geodesic distance d_H(q_i,t_j) passed through the reference's temperature. Curvature enters both in the barycentre (a Euclidean mean of s_u would be a different point and would change every q_i) and in every distance and weight. The size-matched null is a probability-space ratio, not a Euclidean distance.
WHY IT SHOULD TRACK RECALL: For each item i the number asks whether the level of the SID tree whose codebook is matched to the Recall@10 resolution puts i together with the items the frozen reference says actually surround i's training audience. W_i/Z_i is the fraction of the reference's full temperatured negative mass that falls inside i's own cell; c_i/(N-1) is exactly the fraction a size-matched random cell would capture. Their log-ratio is a pointwise mutual information: zero for a random, collapsed or singleton cell, positive when the cell carries more of the reference's neighbourhood than its size deserves, and negative when it is anti-aligned. It adds over Q_H because Q_H uses one anchor source representation and a mean over ten negatives, whereas this uses (a) a hyperbolic barycentre of the whole training audience of the item, which averages out per-user noise and captures co-consumption, (b) the whole temperatured negative distribution through Z_i instead of a ten-negative mean, and (c) the table's own codebook size to evaluate at the level that can actually hold ten candidates, rather than at level 1 only. The explicit size-matched denominator is what the plain path likelihood lacked: it removes the collapse reward.
COMPUTE: At most N = 24587 anchors, each requiring distances to the other N-1 targets and one Karcher barycentre over its training users: about 6.0e8 Poincare distances. With precomputed squared target norms, blocked float32 arrays, and log-sum-exp normalisation this is a single pass over an N-by-N distance matrix, roughly 40-60 s on one CPU core with NumPy and less on a GPU; extra memory is O(N). The Karcher iterations are O(sum_i |P_i| d), negligible.
RISKS: tau must be the reference's own temperature; if tau is tiny the weights collapse to a nearest-neighbour indicator and the estimator becomes noisy, and if tau is huge all weights flatten and M tends to 0. The Poincare ball must be clipped to radius 1-1e-7. l* is 1 for most 256-code tables, so the number may be dominated by level 1; deeper levels are used only when their codebook still satisfies K_l <= N/10. Items with very few training positives have noisy audience barycentres. The null assumes a cell is a uniform random subset of the other N-1 items; a table whose cell membership correlates with reference mass in a way unrelated to retrieval could still score high.
FAILURE MODE: Worst on an adversarial anti-aligned hash at the chosen resolution: items grouped into level-l* cells independently of their reference neighbourhoods, so W_i/((c_i/(N-1)) Z_i) is far below 1 for most anchors and M is strongly negative. The fully collapsed one-cell table scores exactly 0, and any table that becomes item-identifying at or above the chosen level also scores 0 because singletons contribute nothing; the metric therefore deliberately refuses to reward collapse or fragmentation. It under-rates a table whose only geometric splits occur below the N/K codebook budget, since l* never goes there.
END METRIC
